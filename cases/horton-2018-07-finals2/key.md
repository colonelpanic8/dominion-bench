# Key: Horton Summer 2018 finals 2: Forge, Peddler, Fortune Teller, Bandit Fort

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, Summer 2018 Tournament Summary](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-summer-2018-tournament-summary/):
  the designer's write-up of a 3-player finals kingdom, with limited 3P playtesting.

## Expert summary
Bandit Fort undercuts most of what you'd want to do, and the only "draw" is Jack of All Trades,
which isn't really draw. Horton sees three paths. First, thin with Moneylender, gain as many
Peddlers as possible, then Forge them into Provinces. Second, Farming Villages with Fortune
Tellers. Third, economy from Candlestick Makers, which he's fairly sure is the worst. Jack
could fit any of them with care. He'd open Moneylender/Candlestick Maker, gain lots of
Hamlets, drain the Peddlers and get Forge at the first chance. He bets on Forge/Peddler, but
thinks Fortune Teller's attack could be very disruptive.
