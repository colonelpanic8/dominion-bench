# Key: U-Con 2018 Set 3: Colonies, Dominate, Ghost Town, Replace, Taxman

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion: Michigan Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-michigan-tournament-summary/),
  2018-11-12. Horton played this 3-player preliminary game at U-Con and won.
  He didn't design it. The post lists only nine Kingdom cards.

## Expert summary
Horton saw a board where everything is possible: trashing is a little slow,
but Colonies and Dominate leave plenty of time to build a strong deck. With
Ghost Town the only village pile in a 3-player game, he judged the split
decisive: he opened Replace and started buying Ghost Towns on turn 2, ended
with 8 of the 10, and felt he couldn't lose after that. Taxman was his best
Copper trasher and also upgraded Gold to Platinum. An opponent took early
Colonies, so Horton had to watch the piles (Messenger can gain across piles),
but that opponent was then tempted by Mountain Pass VP. Horton Dominated
about three times and ended the game on piles once he was safely ahead.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert + result) Builds a drawing deck aimed at Dominate and Colonies rather than early greening; the long game favors building.
- **R2** [core] (single expert + result) Ghost Town is the only village, so winning the Ghost Town split is a priority (Horton took 8 of 10).
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert + result) Opens Replace and uses it to gain Ghost Towns early.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert + result) Taxman is the best Copper trasher here and also turns Gold into Platinum.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert + result) Payload is Dominate several times, then ending on piles when ahead.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Watch three-pile risk; Messenger lets players gain extra cards into piles.

## Traps
- **T1** (result) An early Colony before the deck is built: the opponent who did this fell behind the builder.
- **T2** (result) Chasing Mountain Pass VP tokens with debt while the opponent is still building.
