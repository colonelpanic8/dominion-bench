# Key: KotW 10/3/2021: Ghost Ship, Border Village, Treasure Trove, Displace

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/q0zyqw/), 2021-10-03, 8 comments.
  willowhelmiam outlined an engine; flaquito_ reported synergies from games against a
  bot; MadtownLems commented on Menagerie. Several comments are rules questions.

## Expert summary
willowhelmiam: a traditional engine. Open Remake/Silver or Ghost Ship/Patrician, take
Ghost Ship with the first $5, then Border Village at $6 alongside Ghost Ships,
Menageries and Patrician/Emporium. Treasure Trove and Displace help in the endgame,
and Farmers' Market supplies +Buy. MadtownLems points out Menagerie defends against
Ghost Ship (a hand of distinct cards draws 3); willowhelmiam agrees but wouldn't skip
Ghost Ship. flaquito_: Treasure Trove gains Golds, which Displace into Provinces,
and because Trove keeps adding Golds, Provinces can be Displaced into Border Village
and engine pieces. Remake clears Trove's Coppers and any Curses bought for Defiled
Shrine VP. The Farmers' Market/Defiled Shrine synergy seemed weak.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Builds an engine with Ghost Ship as the attack and Border Village, Menagerie and Patrician/Emporium as parts. (willowhelmiam, flaquito_)
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Treasure Trove + Displace: Golds Displaced into Provinces, Provinces into Border Village and engine pieces. (flaquito_, bot games)
- **R3** [supporting] (majority) Menagerie partly defends against Ghost Ship, but Ghost Ship is still worth taking. (MadtownLems, willowhelmiam)
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Remake trims Treasure Trove's Coppers and Defiled Shrine Curses; it is also a possible opener (Remake/Silver). (flaquito_, willowhelmiam)
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Farmers' Market for +Buy. (willowhelmiam)

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Building around Farmers' Market gathering for Defiled Shrine. "that doesn't seem very strong" (flaquito_)

- None substantive.
