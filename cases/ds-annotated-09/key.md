# Key: DS Annotated Game #9: Remake, Menagerie, Hunting Party, Horn of Plenty

  2012-01-20: DG (a top engine player) annotates his one-point loss to olneyce.

## Expert summary
No attacks, so a race for Provinces. DG's plan, which olneyce also followed:
open Silver/Remake; Remake trashes Estates into Menageries (and Coppers); add
Pawns, Menageries and Hunting Parties so the deck cycles and draws itself; once
the deck draws reliably, add Horns of Plenty, which gain good cards each turn
when many different cards are in play; finally trash the Horns for Provinces in
one decisive turn. Variety matters: Menagerie wants no duplicates in hand and
Horn of Plenty wants about eight different cards to reach a Province. Keep
Treasures few, and don't add Golem alongside Remake. DG frames it as contract,
control, then expand. He lost 25–24 on turn 11 after greening on turn 10; he says
he should have built one more turn, which would also have threatened a three-pile
ending (Menagerie empty, Horn of Plenty and Hunting Party low).

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus of both players) Engine: Remake trashes the starting cards (Estates into Menageries), Menagerie and Hunting Party draw the deck, Pawn adds Buys.
- **R2** [core] (single expert + result) Horn of Plenty is the payload once the deck draws itself: gain good cards each turn, then trash Horns for Provinces.
- **R3** [core] (single expert) Builds for variety: avoid duplicates (Menagerie) and collect about eight different cards so Horn of Plenty reaches Province.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Opens Silver/Remake, aiming at Hunting Party with the next $5.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Keep Treasures few; skip Golem with Remake.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert + result) In the engine mirror, don't green early: build a further turn and watch for a three-pile ending.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert + result) Greening a turn early in the engine mirror (DG's turn-10 Provinces lost the game by 1 VP).
- **T2** (single expert) Golem in a Remake deck.

## Disagreements
- olneyce added an early Gold and bought more components each turn, while DG
  thinned faster; both were viable and the game was decided by one point.
