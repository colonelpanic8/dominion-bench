# Key: DS Kingdom Analysis #1: Butcher, Inn, Border Village, Messenger, Peddler

## Sources
- [Kingdom Analysis #1](https://dominionstrategy.com/2019/01/03/kingdom-analysis-1/),
  Dominion Strategy blog team, 2019-01-03. The team wrote first impressions of a
  random kingdom, then playtested the ideas (several testers) and reported what
  worked. No named authors; the blog's editors are experienced players.

## Expert summary
First impressions were that the board was weak: nothing increases hand size, so
no draw engine, and Butcher money (Estates into Silvers, Golds into Provinces)
looked like the default. Testing found Butcher money takes the majority of points
in about 15–17 turns, and adding Vampire only slowed it. A Messenger opening
(Messenger gaining a Silver, plus a Silver) reliably hits $5–$6 and its
deck-discard cycles Butchers back faster. The key discovery was a **golden
deck**: with two Messengers, two or more Butchers, two Border Villages and one or
two Peddlers, gaining Inn at the end of a shuffle sets up a perfect next hand.
Each turn after that: play Border Village, Butcher a Peddler into a Province,
play the other Peddler/Border Village, play Messenger to discard the deck, then
use the two Buys to take a Peddler (costing $0 with four Actions in play) and a
new Inn to repeat. Coffers stockpiled by Butcher fund extra Provinces at the end.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert + playtest) Builds around Butcher trashing Peddlers (and Golds) into Provinces, not around draw; notes the board has no real hand-size increase. "There is no way to increase hand size" (blog team)
- **R2** [core] (single expert + playtest) Finds the Inn loop: gaining Inn shuffles Border Village, Butcher, Peddler(s) and Messenger back into the deck, Messenger's discard plus a bought Inn repeats it every turn. "this board has a very potent and reliable golden deck" (blog team)
- **R3** [core] (single expert + playtest) Uses Peddler as Butcher food and as a free buy: with several Actions in play Peddler costs $0, and Butcher turns its $8 printed cost into a Province.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert + playtest) Opens Messenger (gaining a Silver) plus Silver to hit $5–$6 on the next shuffle.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Component counts: two Messengers, at least two Butchers, two Border Villages, one or two Peddlers; $6 hands buy Border Village and gain a Butcher with it.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Uses Messenger's put-deck-into-discard to cycle new Butchers and components into play sooner.
- **R7** [supporting] (single expert) Finishes with a setup turn that topdecks Border Village, two Butchers and two Peddlers for two Provinces, plus Butcher's banked Coffers for an extra Province.
- **R8** [supporting] (playtest) Benchmarks against Butcher money, which takes most of the points in roughly 15–17 turns.

## Traps
- **T1** (playtest) Adding Vampire to Butcher money: it delays the first Butcher by a shuffle and Bat's trashing starts late, so it slows the deck down.
- **T2** (single expert) Planning a village/draw engine: nothing here increases hand size.
- **T3** (single expert) Relying on Talisman for multi-Peddler buys: described as riskier and needing luck; Silver is fine.

## Disagreements
- None recorded. The initial impression (Butcher money, maybe with Vampire) was
  revised by the team's own testing.

## Grader notes
- The golden deck is a specific find. An analysis that sees Butcher-into-Province
  with Peddler and Border Village but not the Inn/Messenger loop deserves partial
  credit on R2.
- Sacrifice, Cobbler and Mint are not discussed in the source. Treat claims about
  them as beyond-key extras.
- Colonies are not stated; the article plays for Provinces.
