# Key: Making Luck ep. 117, kingdom 4: Way of the Frog, Cursed Village, Leprechaun, Monument, Swamp Hag

## Sources
- [Making Luck ep. 117 "Rapid-Fire Kingdom Analysis 2"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_6QemY1KJtc&t=1830s),
  2022-09-03, 30:30-39:40. Adam Horton and Wandering Winder, first look, no playtesting.

## Expert summary
Both hosts build. Moat alone can't hold off Swamp Hag and Barbarian, and
there's nothing to do with money. Hideout trashes, though it hands you Curses
for Estates. Cursed Village looks good: it draws a lot and works with Way of
the Frog. Way of the Frog is unusually relevant here. It lets you topdeck a
Moat after using it, or frog a Leprechaun so it's back for the next turn.
With many Actions in the deck, a second Leprechaun is plausible, played
normally when it hits 7 cards in play and frogged otherwise. +Buy is scarce
(only Pouch and Tracker's Forest's Gift), so Leprechaun's Wishes and Monument
are the extra payload. A Moat/Frog/Magpie/Leprechaun money deck was floated
and rejected. Piazza isn't impressive, partly because it can flip a
Leprechaun at the wrong time.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Builds a deck that plays many Actions (engine), not money; the attacks (Swamp Hag, Barbarian) make money/Moat unattractive.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Way of the Frog is a significant part of the plan here, e.g. frogging Moat or Leprechaun to keep them for next turn. "This is like the Way of the Frog board." (a host)
- **R3** [supporting] (consensus) Cursed Village is a good village/draw here (despite its Hex).
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Leprechaun is payload in an action-dense deck (Wishes; a second copy frogged when it won't trigger).
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus) Hideout is the trashing; Estates are harder to clear because Hideout gives a Curse for them.
- **R6** [supporting] (consensus) Notes the scarce +Buy (Pouch, Forest's Gift) caps the payload, making Monument and Wishes relevant.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Money with Moat-as-Frog, Magpie and Leprechaun, playing 4-card hands all game.
- **T2** (single expert) Piazza as a priority: it isn't impressive here and can reveal Leprechaun when you don't want it.

## Disagreements
- Monument: one host wants several (about three per turn eventually) and
  thinks it can make up a 5-3 Province deficit; the other doubts Monument
  points ever decide the game. They agree the decision comes about 12 turns in.
