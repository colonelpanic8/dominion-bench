# Key: Horton Galactic 2022 Finals 3: Gamble/Bridge megaturn, Vault, Bandit Fort

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion Galactic Championship Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-galactic-championship-summary/),
  Gen Con, 2022-08-04 to 06. Horton designed and playtested this kingdom for
  the later stages of a 2-player tournament; it was played twice. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/16Yun1MG09amRq-PlPe6ilxC3UZL9snIfgrAYmLn5Ovo).

## Expert summary
Bandit Fort stifles most money decks here, except perhaps lots of Vaults with
few Golds. The designed deck is a Gamble/Bridge megaturn: thin hard with
Chapel, get one Altar, a Villain or two and a few Vaults, then add Bridges
fast with Stonemason (helped by Bridge's cost reduction) and Altar. A turn:
play Vault, discard your whole hand, and start buying Gamble repeatedly,
using Coffers saved from earlier turns (Villain) to play through your whole
deck with Gamble. Unattacked, it empties Provinces on turn 12-13 while
playing Villain on most turns after the first 6-7. In the tournament, both
games had one Gamble/Bridge player against a money-density deck, and each
side won once. The Gamble/Bridge win used Bandit to gain Golds as its "draw"
instead of Villain. Horton weighs that variant: it needs fewer Vaults and
less thinness and can spend Coffers in the midgame, but it can't play Actions
from its starting hand unless the opponent keeps playing Vault, may struggle
if the opponent stops Vaulting and floods Gold, and its many Golds cost VP to
Bandit Fort. His gut says Villain beats Bandit in the megaturn deck.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Gamble + Bridge megaturn is the intended top deck: Gamble repeatedly with stacked Bridge cost reduction and +Buys.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Vault discards the starting hand to begin Gambling (Vault also gives the money).
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Thin aggressively with Chapel.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Stonemason (and Altar) gain Bridges cheaply.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Villain banks Coffers for the megaturn and attacks Vault-based money decks.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Bandit Fort makes Silver/Gold money weak; a Vault-heavy, low-Gold money deck is the only decent money option.
- **R7** [supporting] (designer) Expected clock: Provinces empty about turn 12-13 if unattacked.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Plain Silver/Gold money: Bandit Fort's -2 VP per Silver and Gold cripples it.

## Disagreements
- Villain vs. Bandit as the megaturn deck's support: the Bandit version won a tournament game, but Horton suspects Villain is better. The money-density deck also won one game.

## Grader notes
- Gamble discards the top card and lets you play it if it's an Action or
  Treasure, so an emptied hand and a thin deck turn each Gamble buy into a
  play (my note on why Vault matters).
