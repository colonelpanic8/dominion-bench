# Key: Horton Spring 2022 Finals 2: Pirate Ship, King's Court, Keep, Trade

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion: Spring 2022 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-spring-2022-tournament-summary/),
  tournament 2022-04-09. Horton designed and playtested this kingdom (first
  for Winter 2019) for the 2-player finals bracket. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Yf8ZZyyavqIiKeg_rTFl1x-KuDldt_1dtOoN7c4Pc0s)
  (the sheet adds Ironmonger, which the blog list omits).

## Expert summary
Inspired by a ladder game where Pirate Ship was actually good. Two
conditions make it work: you can play enough Pirate Ships to destroy the
opponent's Treasures, and Pirate Ship is the only sustainable virtual money.
Sacrifice, Beggar, Mining Village and Bandit Camp look like virtual money but
are temporary and can't sustain a deck under heavy Pirate Ship attacks. In a
Pirate Ship mirror, whoever gets more Pirate Ship tokens usually has a big
edge, so start playing Ships as early as possible and trash your own
Treasures quickly once you see the opponent buying Ships. Horton notes a
stalemate is theoretically possible.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Pirate Ship is the key card: it's the only sustainable virtual money, and enough Ship plays destroy the opponent's Treasures.
- **R2** [supporting] (designer) In a mirror, the player with more Pirate Ship tokens is usually far ahead; start playing Ships early.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Trash your own Treasures quickly once the opponent is buying Ships.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Sacrifice, Beggar, Mining Village and Bandit Camp give only temporary money.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Relying on Treasure economy (Silver, Gold, Spoils, Ducat) against Pirate Ships.
- **T2** (designer) Treating Sacrifice/Beggar/Mining Village/Bandit Camp as sustainable money.
