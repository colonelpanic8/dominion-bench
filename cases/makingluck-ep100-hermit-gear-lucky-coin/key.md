# Key: Making Luck ep. 100, kingdom 6: Hermit, Gear, Soothsayer, Conspirator, Fool (Lucky Coin)

## Sources
- [Making Luck ep. 100 "Rapid-Fire Kingdom Analysis"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RQk58SzbZd0&t=3210s),
  2020-02-28, 53:30-60:20. Adam Horton and Wandering Winder, first look, no playtesting.

## Expert summary
Draw is thin and wonky. Squire "isn't draw", and the real draw is Crypt and
Madman. Hermit is the only thinning, and Coppers can only be removed
temporarily via Crypt, so a Conspirator engine would take too many hoops.
Both hosts choose a money-centred deck: one Hermit, a few Gears, a
Soothsayer, plus the Silvers from Lucky Coin, which feed money far better
than they feed Conspirators. Take Madman at some point, and buy City Gate on
the Madman turn since Hermit's skip-buy turn makes it free otherwise. Fool
is weak. Open Hermit/Gear (or Hermit/Silver); they settle on Gear because
Lucky Coin already provides Silvers. One host would go money even without
Lucky Coin; the other says it would then be close. Hermit/Squire for a
Basilica VP was floated and rejected.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Plays a money-centred deck (Hermit, Gear, Soothsayer, Lucky Coin) rather than a Conspirator engine.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Opens Hermit plus Gear (Silver acceptable).
- **R3** [supporting] (consensus) Lucky Coin's Silver gains push toward money; they don't help a Conspirator deck.
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Notes the thin draw (Crypt, Madman; Squire isn't draw) and the lack of Copper trashing.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Takes Madman later and buys City Gate on that turn.
- **R6** [supporting] (consensus) Gets a Soothsayer as the attack.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) A Conspirator engine with Squires and Crypt: too many hoops, too slow.
- **T2** (single expert) Fool: weak in general and here.
- **T3** (consensus) Opening Hermit/Squire for the Basilica point.

## Disagreements
- Without Lucky Coin: one host would still go money; the other calls it close.

## Grader notes
- Clear consensus; most of the weight is on R1-R2.
