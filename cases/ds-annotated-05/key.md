# Key: DS Annotated Game #5: King's Court, Pawn, Monument, Quarry, Bishop

## Sources
- [Annotated Game #5](https://dominionstrategy.com/2011/03/15/annotated-game-5/)
  ([preview](https://dominionstrategy.com/2011/03/11/annotated-game-5-preview/)),
  2011-03-15: theory (blog founder) annotates his loss to DG.

## Expert summary
theory thought the board was boring and King's Court risky in a Province game, so
he opened Bishop/Silver for Bishop money with Mining Villages. DG opened
Pawn/Quarry and built a King's Court deck fed by cheap Actions: Pawns and
Monuments, with Quarry making King's Courts and Pawns cheap and Pawn's +Buy
buying several at once. theory's Bishops let DG trash his own Coppers for free,
thinning the King's Court deck. From turn 13 DG chained King's Courts on Pawns and
Monuments for $21+ turns and multiple Provinces, winning 76–45. theory's lessons:
recognize which deck grows faster; the deck that ramps should delay greening and
end with mega-turns, while the other must race and not prolong the game (no extra
Bishops, no lone King's Court).

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (result + single expert) King's Court is the build-around: KC on Pawn and Monument, with Quarry and Pawn's +Buy to collect several King's Courts, produces mega-turns. "a textbook example of how to play for the 'mega-turn'" (theory)
- **R2** [core] (single expert) The ramping KC deck should delay greening and finish with a few huge turns; a non-ramping deck must race.
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert + result) Bishop's drawback: each Bishop lets the opponent trash a card free, which feeds a thin King's Court deck.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Masquerade is a counter to a thin King's Court deck (forces it to pass a good card).
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Monument gives VP tokens as the KC deck's payload alongside Provinces.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert + result) Bishop money with many Bishops: it thins the opponent and can't keep pace with a King's Court mega-turn deck.
- **T2** (single expert) A lone King's Court (or $7 on KC) in a deck without cheap Actions to multiply; Gold or Province is better.
