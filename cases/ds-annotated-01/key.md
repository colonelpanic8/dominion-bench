## Expert summary
theory names Mountebank the defining card: with no trashing it is close to
unstoppable, so the first goal is $5. He opens Warehouse/Silver, intending to use
Warehouse to cycle and replay Mountebank, and calls Warehouse the hidden crux of
the set. He rates the Reactions (Secret Chamber, Watchtower) as poor defence,
Saboteur and Smugglers as weak, Caravan as only slightly better than the $3s
here, and Harem as good once Golds are in. He thinks the City/Caravan/Peddler
mega-turn is too slow without trashing or Colonies, and prefers Gold to early
City. yaron opened Silver/Caravan and spent his $5–6s on Cities; theory
won 33–27 after building a Duchy buffer, since there is no +Buy. Geronimoo's
simulations found Mountebank money far ahead of plain Big Money, Warehouse/Silver
only marginally ahead of Caravan/Silver, and that theory could have ignored the
Cities entirely.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert + simulation + result) Mountebank is the key card; with no trashing it dominates, so prioritize reaching $5 for it. "The defining card in this game is going to be Mountebank." (theory)
- **R2** [core] (single expert + simulation) Plays a money deck around Mountebank (Gold, then Provinces), not a City/Caravan/Peddler engine, because there is no trashing and no Colony.
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert + simulation) Warehouse supports the Mountebank deck by cycling through junk to replay Mountebank; opens Warehouse/Silver (or Mountebank on 5/2).
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Secret Chamber and Watchtower are weak defence against Mountebank; Watchtower's draw is useless without villages.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Saboteur and Smugglers are weak here; Harem (Farm) is good once Golds are in the deck; Caravan is a modest $4.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert + result) With no +Buy or Remodel-style gainers, get a points buffer (Duchies) before the final Province split.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert + simulation) Rushing Cities with early $5–$6 instead of Gold or a second Mountebank. Geronimoo's bots show the City rush stalls (Cities do nothing until a pile empties), and that the Mountebank player wins more by ignoring Cities (70% to 89%).
- **T2** (single expert) Saboteur as a terminal in a Copper/Curse-filled deck.

## Disagreements
- Opening: a commenter argued Warehouse/Silver only worked through a lucky shuffle;
  Geronimoo's first simulation favored Caravan/Mountebank (51–44), and after
  tuning (Gold before more Mountebanks) Warehouse won "by a nose". Either opening
  is acceptable; Silver/Silver beat Caravan/Silver for plain Caravan money.

## Grader notes
- 2011, first-edition cards (Secret Chamber, Saboteur, Mountebank; Farm was Harem).
- Credit a Mountebank-money plan with Warehouse or Caravan support for R1–R3.
- Modern players might value City more once the Curse pile is sure to empty; the
  sources' simulations say not, but that is the only evidence. (My note.)
