# Key: Making Luck ep. 24 (ep. 23 kingdom): Trade Route, Archive, Den of Sin, Market Square, Gladiator, Colony

## Sources
- [Making Luck ep. 24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OFFsp4LoT7o&t=198s),
  2018-05-15, about 3:18-17:12. Adam Horton (a world champion) and Jake,
  after playing several games. They also discuss a plan Wandering Winder (a
  top player) posted, which Adam tried twice.

## Expert summary
Trade Route is the opener. It's the only way to thin Estates, and Colonies
give every reason to trash, even though the Trade Route mat rarely gets
past $2. Each trash also lets Market Square in hand gain a Gold. The deck
plays a Trade Route nearly every turn (open one, get a second soon), adds
Market Squares, then Archives for deck control (so the two Trade Routes
stop colliding), then Dens of Sin, which turn Market Square Golds and
discards into big hands. It can reach double-Colony turns. Gladiator/Fortune
barely matters: with no village and no terminal space nobody wants
Gladiators, so Fortune never comes into reach in time. Gladiators are at
most Trade Route food. Wedding is weak as an opener. Castles don't matter,
since there aren't enough points next to Colony. If you don't get a Trade
Route, Haggler is strong. Wandering Winder's alternative, a Haggler + many
Archives money deck with some Weddings and no Trade Route, went 1-1 in
Adam's hands. Jake thinks it's competitive but less reliable because it
keeps its Estates. Adam thinks it's close, and better with one Trade Route.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Opens Trade Route: the only Estate trasher, and Colony games reward thinning.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) The core deck is Trade Route + Market Square (Golds from trashing) + Archive, then Den of Sin, aiming at double-Colony turns.
- **R3** [core] (consensus) Gladiator/Fortune is a non-factor: no village or terminal space for Gladiators, so nobody can clear them to reach Fortune in time.
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Gets a second Trade Route early (opening or turns 3-4) to play one nearly every turn.
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus) Takes about two or three Archives, then switches to Dens of Sin for the larger hand-size boost; with a double-Trade Route opening, a few more Archives.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Opens Trade Route/Market Square (Adam), keeping the chance of $5 for an early Archive.
- **R7** [supporting] (consensus) Wedding isn't a good opener; its Debt can wreck turn 3.
- **R8** [supporting] (consensus) Castles are insignificant with Colonies in the game.
- **R9** [supporting] (result) Fool was fine but didn't change the power level much; a Haggler/Archive money deck (no Trade Route) went 1-1 against the main deck.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Buying Gladiators to set up Fortune.
- **T2** (consensus) Opening Wedding with Trade Route.
- **T3** (consensus) Building around Castles.

## Disagreements
- Opening: Adam prefers Trade Route/Market Square; Jake first opened Trade
  Route/Fool, then came to like double Trade Route (followed by Market
  Square, Archives, then Dens of Sin). Both treat these as close.
- Haggler/Archive money (Wandering Winder's plan): Jake thinks the Trade
  Route deck usually wins because the money deck keeps its Estates and can
  choke; Adam thinks win rates are close and the money deck improves with
  one Trade Route.

## Grader notes
- Armory, Horse Traders and Travelling Fair weren't seriously discussed
  (Travelling Fair only in a joke).
- Credit any opening that includes Trade Route; the second card is open.
