# Key: KotW 3/14/2021: Diplomat, Goons, Steward

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/m5drvd/), 2021-03-14, 8 comments.
  Nearly all substance is PathfindingLibrary (strong regular) in one-line replies,
  prompted by IDontLikeBeingRight and Snip3.

## Expert summary
PathfindingLibrary reads the board as built to make Diplomat a power card, with
Goons as the payload ("a Goons powered exit") and Duke and Colony changing nothing.
He would open double Steward: Salvager's strength is trashing while hitting $5, but
the $5s are low priority, and Steward's weakness (leaving you with $2) doesn't matter
because the $2s here are fine. Horse Traders makes no difference.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert) Diplomat is the key card (draw that gives +2 Actions in a small hand). "This kingdom looks designed to make Diplomat a power card." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Goons is the payload (VP tokens per buy plus the discard attack); Duke and Colony make no difference to the plan. (IDontLikeBeingRight, confirmed by PathfindingLibrary)
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Opens Steward/Steward. "Double Steward." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) The $2 cards are good enough that Steward's $2 hands are fine, and $5s are low priority early.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Opening Salvager (its strength, hitting $5 while trashing, matters little here). (PathfindingLibrary)
- **T2** (single expert) Building around Duke or Colony.
- **T3** (single expert) Horse Traders. "you could play without Horse Traders and it would make literally no difference." (PathfindingLibrary)
