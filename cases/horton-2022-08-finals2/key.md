# Key: Horton Galactic 2022 Finals 2: Tactician, Pathfinding, Royal Carriage, Priest

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion Galactic Championship Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-galactic-championship-summary/),
  Gen Con, 2022-08-04 to 06. Horton designed and playtested this kingdom for
  the later stages of a 2-player tournament. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/16Yun1MG09amRq-PlPe6ilxC3UZL9snIfgrAYmLn5Ovo).

## Expert summary
The intended deck puts the Pathfinding (+1 Card) token on Tactician, then uses
Royal Carriage, called after a Tactician is played, to replay Tacticians, so
several Duration Tacticians resolve in one turn. Nothing else on the board
increases hand size, and Priest's explosive payload justifies the hoops. In
the one tournament game, neither player found the multiple-Tactician deck;
both played a more standard double-Tactician deck with Pathfinding and
Candlestick Maker as payload.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Tactician is the only real draw; the deck aims to resolve multiple Tacticians per turn.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Pathfinding on Tactician (Tactician needs a card in hand to discard, which the +1 Card supplies).
- **R3** [core] (designer) Royal Carriage replays Tactician, multiplying its next-turn draw.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Priest is the payload (trash for growing +$).

## Traps
- **T1** (result) Settling for a standard double-Tactician deck with Candlestick Maker payload; both finalists did and missed the designed deck.
