# Key: Horton 2026-09 Semifinals Kingdom 2: Ronin, Tireless Souk, Village Green, Armory

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "September 2026 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/september-2026-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, September 2026. Horton designed and playtested this
  kingdom for the semifinals. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1E0IUYK9fU2KXZmANMprklRV0caaVj6CqO3ScusjCOvQ).

## Expert summary
Ronin is the only draw, so the deck is built around it: Horton says Ronin
turns draw-to-X, normally a weak archetype, into something good even with
little support, and this board has plenty of support. The hard part is how to
build. Using the tournament's deck-stacking rule, he likes turn 1 Armory, then
using that Armory to gain a Transmogrify while buying an Importer on turn 2,
and on turn 3 Transmogrifying the remaining Estate into a Coin of the Realm.
From there the draws decide; often he takes a Ronin with the first $5 rather
than rushing Souks. The Souks are Tireless, so they go on top of the deck at
end of turn, after the new hand is drawn; you want them in your starting hand,
which needs a duration Village Green in play to help. In the tournament the
Tireless timing was played incorrectly, and Horton is banning Tireless from
future events as a result.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Builds a Ronin draw-to-X deck; Ronin is the only draw and the core of the deck.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Opening sequence: Armory on turn 1, Armory gains Transmogrify and buys Importer on turn 2, Transmogrify an Estate into Coin of the Realm on turn 3.
- **R3** [core] (designer) Tireless Souks land on top of the deck after you draw your next hand, so they are drawn later, not in the starting hand; a duration Village Green helps get them played.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Often takes Ronin with the first $5 rather than buying Souks straight away.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Coin of the Realm and Village Green supply the Actions the Ronin deck needs.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer + result) Treating Tireless as putting Souk on top before drawing the next hand (so it starts every hand). Horton says this is wrong and was how it was misplayed in the tournament.
- **T2** (designer) Rushing Souks with every $5 from the start instead of getting Ronin.

## Grader notes
- The opening relies on the tournament's deck-stacking rule (Armory on turn 1
  means a stacked 4/3 split). Credit an equivalent Armory/Transmogrify/Importer
  start.
- Grader's note (mine): Souk wants a small hand, so Ronin and Souk pull in
  different directions; an analysis that handles the order of play (Souk
  before Ronin, or Souk late in a turn) deserves credit as an extra.
