# Key: KotW 9/11/2022: Collection, Experiment, Tiara, Raze

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/xc05mq/), 2022-09-12.
  willowhelmiam (regular) gave the plan; SignError supported it; a deleted
  user argued for Artificer/Scholar; OverCryptographer169 questioned Silos.

## Expert summary
Collection + Experiment is a combo: each Experiment gain also gains a second
Experiment, and with Collections in play every Action gained is +1 VP. Since
Experiment returns itself to the Supply when played, the same pile can be
re-bought every turn. willowhelmiam's target deck is about 10 Experiments,
5 Collections and 5 Tiaras, re-buying the Experiment pile each turn; Tiara
topdecks gains and doubles Collection. Opening Raze/Silver, then Silos and a
second Raze, then Collections, Tiaras and Experiments. Lackeys (and later
Port) can supplement draw. SignError agreed Experiment and Lackeys are better
draw than Scholar, and the game goes to whoever gets more Collections.
Expensive gains (Forge, Grand Market, Artificer, Scholar) are distractions.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Collection + Experiment: gain many Experiments per turn with Collections in play for VP; Experiments return to the Supply to be re-gained. "Collection+Experiment is a combo." (willowhelmiam)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Winning the Collection (and Tiara) split decides the game. "The game goes to whoever gets the most Collections" (SignError)
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Tiara for +Buy, topdecking gains, and playing Collection twice.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Opens Raze/Silver; Raze trashes Estates.
- **R5** [supporting] (majority) Lackeys as supplementary draw; Port as extra Actions later.

## Traps
- **T1** (majority) Artificer/Scholar as the main plan. "Every artificer or scholar you buy is a collection you don't." (willowhelmiam)
- **T2** (single player) Gaining $6+ cards (Forge, Grand Market, Platinum) instead of Collection pieces. "No cards >$5 are worth gaining here." (deleted user)

## Disagreements
- A deleted user defended Artificer gaining and topdecking Scholar and
  dismissed the importance of splits; willowhelmiam and SignError disagreed.
- Silos: willowhelmiam takes it early to find Raze; OverCryptographer169
  said Tiara topdecking makes it nearly useless; willowhelmiam conceded Tiara
  might be better.

## Grader notes
