## Expert summary
The goal was to make Tide Pools good without Guide. The starting point is
Outpost: play all your Tide Pools and then an Outpost, and simply forfeit the
Outpost turn. The Tide Pools' discard penalty lands on the 3-card Outpost turn,
and you shuffle all your Tide Pools back into the deck for your functional
turns. Contract, the only village, adds a twist: on the Outpost turn you can
discard your hand to Tide Pools and then play the Action you Contracted, so the
Outpost turn becomes functional too. Horton added other things you can do at
the start of a turn before discarding: Enlarge, Save, and in a way Bishop (with
several Contracts, Bishop something before discarding, then draw). Tide Pools
ends up quite good, and he believes the semifinal was won by the player with
more Tide Pools.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer + result) Tide Pools is the main draw and is good here; take many.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Outpost absorbs Tide Pools' next-turn discard: play Tide Pools then Outpost so the discard hits the short Outpost turn.
- **R3** [core] (designer) Contract sets aside an Action to play at the start of the next turn, so even the Outpost turn can do something after discarding to Tide Pools.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Start-of-turn effects before the Tide Pools discard (Enlarge's second half, Save's set-aside card, Contracted Bishop) squeeze value from those turns.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Contract is the only village (and a Liaison feeding Crafters' Guild).
