# Key: Horton 2025-03 Third-place Kingdom: Biding Time, Overlord, Kitsune, Bridge, Aristocrat

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "March 2025 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/march-2025-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, March 2025. Horton designed and playtested this
  kingdom for the third-place match. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/12E1dAbWrWfyzVCSUhEPvG0ut0CSurOst9er15uUWjeY).

## Expert summary
Like the finals board, this one rewards giving up turns to save for a huge
turn. Gang of Pickpockets (discard to 4 at start of turn unless you spend a
Favor) and Biding Time (the hand you set aside returns at start of turn) are
both start-of-turn effects, so you can resolve Gang of Pickpockets first. With
a 5/2 opening available, Kitsune/Overlord is clear, apart from possible
tactical Overlord-as-Menagerie plays, and the Curses should go out fast. Early
turns are simple: junk quickly and pick up another Overlord or two. Once Biding
Time triggers, Coppers should rarely be played: Biding Time is your deck
control, and paying $1 to effectively thin a Copper is often worth it. Next you
mostly play only Overlords and Kitsunes; once Curses run out, Overlord as
Marauder has value (Spoils is efficient payload and Ruins slow opponents). Wait
until most of the deck is held in hand via Biding Time, add one Aristocrat and
several Bridges (likely with a 2-3 Bridge turn to pick up the rest of the
payload), then pass turns until you can take six or more Provinces in a turn.
Overlords can be Aristocrats for more Actions or Bridges for more payload.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Opens Kitsune/Overlord (5/2) and junks quickly with Curses.
- **R2** [core] (designer) After Biding Time triggers, uses it as deck control: hold cards across turns and rarely play Coppers.
- **R3** [core] (designer) Final plan: hold most of the deck in hand, add one Aristocrat and several Bridges, then take a turn with six or more Provinces.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Gang of Pickpockets and Biding Time both trigger at start of turn and you choose the order, so you can discard to Pickpockets before the held hand returns.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Picks up another Overlord or two early; Overlords flex as Kitsune, Marauder, Aristocrat or Bridge as needed.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) After Curses run out, Overlord as Marauder for Spoils and Ruins.
- **R7** [supporting] (designer) May pass turns while building up to the big turn.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Playing Coppers routinely once Biding Time is active.
