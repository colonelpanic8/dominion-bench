# Key: KotW 5/8/2022: Lookout, Vassal, Witch, Herald

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/uldlkr/), 2022-05-08.
  SignError gave a detailed plan that skipped Witch; PathfindingLibrary,
  GonffzCheeze and petnarwhal (regulars) disagreed and gave the opening.

## Expert summary
Open Vassal/Lookout and make the first $5 a Witch; skipping Witch is "VERY
losing" (GonffzCheeze). SignError's otherwise sensible outline wants an
Action-dense deck for Vassal and Herald: trash Estates with Lookout, later
Stonemason Lookouts into Candlestick Makers, then add Vassals, Heralds and
Throne Rooms. He avoids Tactician (it clashes with Herald) and prefers Vassal
to Treasury. His proposal to skip Witch was rejected by three commenters.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Buys Witch, as the first $5. "First $5 should be Witch." (GonffzCheeze; PathfindingLibrary and petnarwhal agree)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Opens Vassal/Lookout. "Vassal/Lookout" (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Builds an Action-dense deck so Vassal and Herald hit Actions; Throne Room fits.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Lookout trashes Estates early; Stonemason can later trash a Lookout to gain two cheaper cards (e.g. Candlestick Makers).
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Watches for three-pile endings involving Stonemason/Candlestick Maker and the Action piles.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Skipping Witch because its terminal +2 Cards is awkward. "Skipping Witch is VERY losing" (GonffzCheeze)
- **T2** (single expert) Tactician alongside Herald (SignError: they don't play well together).

## Disagreements
- SignError would buy Witch only when the opponent isn't trashing or near the
  end; the others want it first. He kept that view in an edit.

## Grader notes
- Thin, but the opening and Witch priority are clear. Advisor and Treasury
  were dismissed only by SignError.
