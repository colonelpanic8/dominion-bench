# Key: 2026 Online Championship final, game 3: Horn of Plenty, Overlord, Upgrade, Scrounge

## Sources
- [Lemonspawn and Sharur, neutral commentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3oj4O-lLaH4), 38:30-1:13:00.
  Quotes are attributed to "commentator". Spectators sillypotter (a former champion) and markusin
  commented in chat, noted where used.
- Result, read from the end screen: agoo03 won 43-30, 12 turns each (yurikamome was player 1).
  agoo03 finished with 8 Duchies, 3 Provinces and 1 Estate, gained in one big Horn turn. His deck
  also held 4 Overlord, 3 Bandit Camp, 3 Council Room, Walled Village, Inn, Patrol, Upgrade, 2
  Spoils; all his Horns trashed themselves. yurikamome had 5 Provinces.

## Expert summary
Everyone read this as a Horn of Plenty megaturn board ("big Horn of Plenty turn", sillypotter in
chat). Horn gains a card costing up to $1 per differently named card in play and trashes itself if
that card is a Victory card, so the plan is a deck of many differently named $5 Actions (Upgrade,
Council Room, Bandit Camp, Patrol, Inn, Walled Village, Overlord) plus enough Horns to take most of
the Provinces and Duchies in one turn. The commentators compared it to Bridge: about seven Horns
is a lock, since seven Horn gains plus a bought Province empties the pile, and six is usually
enough. agoo03 opened Upgrade/Overlord, added Overlords and $5s, then took eight Horns, which
both commentators called overkill, and won with one huge greening turn. yurikamome opened Forager,
which the commentators disliked because it couldn't reach $5, and never had enough Horns to
answer.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) The plan is a Horn of Plenty megaturn: build up Horns and play them all in one turn to gain Provinces and Duchies. agoo03 won with 8 Duchies and 3 Provinces gained in one turn.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Horn needs variety: first add several differently named $5 Actions (Upgrade, Council Room, Bandit Camp, Patrol, Inn, Walled Village, Overlord), then Horns. "Five five five five five. All fives all the time." (commentator)
- **R3** [supporting] (majority) Count Horns like Bridges: six is usually enough, seven is a lock, more is overkill that weakens the deck. "seven is the number because it's kind of like Bridge" (commentator)
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Open Silver/Scrounge or Upgrade/Overlord, aiming at $5s. "Silver Scrounge is generically pretty good." (commentator, echoing sillypotter)
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus) One Bandit Camp is enough; more add Spoils and cards to draw, and Walled Village is the better extra village.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Scrounge's second mode (take an Estate from the trash to gain a $5) adds cards when you need them, but don't add Estates back without a reason.
- **R7** [supporting] (single expert) Keep adding draw (Patrol, Council Room, Inn) alongside Horns so the deck can play them all; agoo03's deck "cannot draw" was the one risk the commentators saw.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Opening Forager: early on it only trashes an Estate and the opening misses $5. yurikamome opened it and lost.
- **T2** (consensus) Buying Horns past seven instead of draw or Inn. "having eight is overkill, and your deck sucks." (commentator) agoo03 did it and still won, so this is about efficiency, not the result.

## Disagreements
- Overlord: one commentator thought it too expensive (8 Debt) to buy many; the other liked
  it because Horns can gain cards later and Overlord can be any $5 you need. agoo03 had four.
- Six or seven Horns: one commentator would stop at six; the other said seven, with six as
  probably winning.
- Yurikamome's comeback options (Scrounge for Estates or Duchies, or a pile-out) were floated and
  then judged infeasible.
