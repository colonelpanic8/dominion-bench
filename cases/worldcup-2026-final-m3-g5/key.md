# Key: 2026 World Cup final, Minionpawn vs yurikamome, game 5: Recruiter, Overlord, Mining Road, Seize the Day

## Sources
- [JNails with drsteelhammer, neutral commentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hY-_wubT4nw), 1:41:30-2:22:00.
  Captions don't mark speakers, so quotes are "commentator".
- Match: Dominion World Cup 2026 final, match 3. Minionpawn (player 1) dominated, but yurikamome
  won with a last-turn pile-out.

## Expert summary
A straightforward engine board: draw from Pilgrim, villages from Recruiter (Villagers) and Harbor
Village, trashing from the Goat Heirloom (from Pixie) and Scrap, +Buy from Mining Road and Scrap.
The commentators expected a Treasure-based payload, since Mining Road gains Treasures and plays
them at once. Herbalist was the only card they'd skip, and Coin of the Realm mattered little with
Recruiter's Villagers. Overlord can be played as any of these. Minionpawn opened Pixie/Recruiter,
got River's Gift (a strong early Boon) and added Overlords;
yurikamome opened Anvil/Overlord on 3/4 and kept drawing Estates. Minionpawn built a big lead and, by the commentators' count, should have bought Seize the Day while his deck was at
its peak and taken enough Provinces to stay out of pile-out range. Instead he left a Harbor
Village/Estate pile-out open. yurikamome bought Seize the Day and Estates and, on the extra
turn, found exactly six gains to empty the piles and win; the commentators called it "a bit
of a throw".

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Builds an engine: Recruiter and Harbor Village for Actions, Pilgrim for draw, Overlord as a flexible copy, Goat and Scrap (and Recruiter on Estates) for trashing.
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Payload is Treasure-based via Mining Road, which gains Treasures and plays them at once; +Buy comes from Mining Road and Scrap.
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Herbalist is the one card not to gain; Coin of the Realm is low priority because Recruiter gives Villagers.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Buy Seize the Day when the deck is at its strongest, before greening.
- **R5** [core] (single expert + result) When far ahead, take enough VP that the opponent can't win on a pile-out (here Harbor Village plus Estates, helped by their own Seize the Day). "I agree that this is a pretty big oversight." (commentator)
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Early Pixie Boons matter; the commentators named Swamp's Gift and Sea's Gift as good early pulls, and River's Gift helped Minionpawn.

## Traps
- **T1** (result + single expert) Coasting with a big lead while a cheap pile-out remains open to the opponent.
- **T2** (single expert) Herbalist: it does little here.

## Disagreements
- None on the plan. The commentators split mildly on whether yurikamome should take a Silver or
  nothing from Scrap during the comeback (buys, not money, were the constraint).

## Grader notes
- Anvil and Stampede were barely discussed (my note).
- The result was decided by an endgame oversight, so weight R1-R2 and R5 over who won.
