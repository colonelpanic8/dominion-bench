# Key: KotW 20 Jan 2025: Bridge, Cathedral, Acting Troupe, Hunting Grounds

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/1i5bw3m/), 2025-01-19.
  Nadenkend440 designed the kingdom and explained the intended plans;
  EphesosX argued for money; bnoel12345 reported a win against the app AI.
- [4-player game thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/1i9zzse/), 2025-01-25.
  ddDeath_666 won a 4-player game on this kingdom; SignError commented.

## Expert summary
No villages apart from Acting Troupe's Villagers, which run out. EphesosX
reads it as Big Money with a few terminals: Cathedral for trashing without
spending Actions, Spices for +Buy, Silver/Gold, and Hunting Grounds as the
terminal draw. He judged a Bridge megaturn hard to set up (several Bridges,
Acting Troupes and a way to draw them, and the risk of Cathedral forcing you
to trash a Bridge), and noted Mountain Pass favors the money player who can
bid higher. The designer intended a Cathedral/Acting Troupe/Hunting Grounds/
Bridge engine (7-9 Villagers for the megaturn), with Importer to speed the
build. bnoel12345 beat the AI with Acting Troupes, Bridges and Spices for 3
Colonies, ending on Black Cat, Acting Troupe and Spices piles (with $3+ of
reduction those cards are free). In the 4-player game, the winner skipped
Cathedral and stacked Black Cats by trashing Coppers with Tanuki; SignError
said Cathedral is weaker than usual here because other trashing is strong and
Masquerade punishes over-trashing.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Acting Troupe is the only source of +Actions (Villagers), so terminal space is limited and the engine has a cap.
- **R2** [core] (majority) Spices provides +Buy and Coffers; it appears in both the money and Bridge plans.
- **R3** [supporting] (majority) Cathedral as trashing that costs no Action, with the risk of trashing a good card when the hand is all good cards.
- **R4** [supporting] (single player + result vs. AI) Bridge cost reduction makes Acting Troupe, Black Cat and Spices free, so a Bridge turn can end the game on piles.
- **R5** [supporting] (single player) Hunting Grounds as the main terminal draw.
- **R6** [supporting] (result, 4-player) Tanuki trashing Coppers into Black Cats as an attacking alternative to Cathedral.

## Traps
- **T1** (single player) Duke/Duchy in a Colony game; the setter conceded it is probably not enticing. (bnoel12345, Nadenkend440)

## Disagreements
- Money with Hunting Grounds (EphesosX) vs. a Bridge engine (the designer,
  bnoel12345's AI win). Nobody played a strong opponent.
- Cathedral: EphesosX and bnoel12345 used it; SignError and ddDeath_666's
  4-player result say it is weaker here than usual.

## Grader notes
