# Key: KotW 3/7/2021: Seer, Highway, Silk Merchant, Salt the Earth

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/m09x04/), 2021-03-07, 8 comments.
  treelorf and PathfindingLibrary (strong regulars) gave the plan in short replies;
  ahicks58 gave openings; halhag reported a 6-player game (not 2-player).

## Expert summary
treelorf notes that Salt the Earth on Feodum (each Feodum trashed from the Supply
gives you 3 Silvers) is usually a dominant plan, but doesn't see it beating the
Seer/Highway/Silk Merchant deck here. PathfindingLibrary answers the +Buy question
with Forager, a few Ducats and a few Silk Merchants, and corrects the opening from
Forager/Salt the Earth to Forager/Silk Merchant. treelorf adds that Silk Merchant
works half as a village through its Villagers. PathfindingLibrary dismisses Rebuild.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Main plan is a Seer/Highway/Silk Merchant deck, not the Salt the Earth/Feodum rush. "Here though, I can't really see that beating the silk/seer/highway stuff" (treelorf)
- **R2** [core] (single expert) +Buy comes from Forager, a few Ducats and a few Silk Merchants. (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Opens Forager/Silk Merchant. (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Silk Merchant's Villagers make it partly a village. "Silk merchant is 50% terminal, and 50% a village" (treelorf)
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Names Salt the Earth + Feodum (3 Silvers per Feodum trashed) as the board's notable alternative. (treelorf)
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Notes that Highway can push cards below Seer's $2-$4 range (e.g. Squire). (ahicks58)

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Rebuild. "I think the point of this kingdom is just to make fun of Rebuild for being a bad card." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **T2** (single expert) Opening Forager with Salt the Earth on a Feodum; PathfindingLibrary replaced it with Forager/Silk Merchant.

## Disagreements
- In halhag's 6-player game, Salt the Earth on Feodum once plus an early Road Network
  won with a Province most turns from turn 6-7. That doesn't settle 2-player.

## Grader notes
- Thin thread; nothing was tested in 2-player. Credit a well-argued Seer/Highway
  build even if it differs in detail.
- Enchantress, Jack of All Trades and Road Network were barely discussed.
