# Key: KotW 27 Mar 2023: Mining Village, Magnate, Cutthroat, Maroon

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/123eh7q/), 2023-03-27.
  Curebob and willowhelmiam gave detailed plans; Triumph44 and Dame-Da-Nae
  debated Maroon. No games reported.

## Expert summary
Both main voices build a deck that draws itself while keeping plenty of
Treasure: Mining Village is the vital village (Curebob calls the split
important), Magnate draws a card per Treasure in hand, and Loot (from
Cutthroat's attack trigger) and Gold make Magnate stronger. willowhelmiam
opens Maroon/Silver or Maroon/Grotto, takes Magnate and Acting Troupe on
turns 3-4 and a Cutthroat as the next $5, then Mining Villages, Magnates and
a second Maroon; +Buy comes from Animal Fair and Loot. Curebob instead sees
Maroon as slow and terminal, prefers Mining Villages at $4, and likes
Soothsayer (Gold gains feed Cutthroat Loot, and Cursing makes Monkey draw);
willowhelmiam warned that Soothsayer's Gold also triggers the opponent's
Cutthroats. Grotto can hide junk and Provinces.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Mining Village is the village; the split matters. "Mining Village is vital here for terminal space" (Curebob)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Magnate draw with a Treasure-rich deck (Loot, Gold), so don't strip Treasures out.
- **R3** [supporting] (majority) Cutthroat for Loot (+Buy and money) and as an attack.
- **R4** [supporting] (majority) Grotto to set aside junk or green so it doesn't clog draws.
- **R5** [supporting] (majority) Animal Fair (bought by trashing an Action) as +Buy, especially once piles empty.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Opens Maroon/Silver or Maroon/Grotto; Maroon trashes without losing tempo.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Soothsayer: its Gold gain triggers the opponent's Cutthroats. (willowhelmiam)

## Disagreements
- Maroon: willowhelmiam and Dame-Da-Nae rate it strong; Curebob calls it slow
  and terminal; Triumph44 prefers Grotto over Maroon here, with maybe one
  Maroon for Curses.
- Soothsayer and Monkey: Curebob likes them; willowhelmiam doesn't.

## Grader notes
- No consensus on trashing. Windfall and Rush were mentioned only for
  Rush + Acting Troupe timing.
