# Key: 2026 World Cup final, Minionpawn vs yurikamome, game 1: Orchard, Sickness, Odysseys, Sentry

## Sources
- [JNails with drsteelhammer, neutral commentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hY-_wubT4nw), 0:00-41:30.
  Both are top players. Captions don't mark speakers, so quotes are "commentator".
- Match: Dominion World Cup 2026 final, USA A vs Japan A, match 3 (yurikamome won 4-2).
  Minionpawn was player 1; yurikamome (5/2) won this game.

## Expert summary
No village, so no real engine. Distant Shore gives some non-terminal draw but adds Estates, and
trashing is plentiful (Sentry, River Shrine, Goatherd, Rogue). The commentators expected decks of
cantrips (Sentry, Old Map, Secret Passage) because Orchard pays 4 VP for each Action you own three
or more copies of. Courtyard has the Nearby trait (+Buy when gained), so a late $6 buys three
Courtyards for Orchard points. Goatherd looked good: the opponent's River Shrine often trashes
several cards early, and once Sickness (the Prophecy) activates, players take Curses and trash
them, so Goatherd is rarely dead. On 5/2 open Sentry (yurikamome did); on 4/3, River Shrine with
Goatherd. One commentator disliked the Odysseys pile in principle but expected it to matter here: Voyage
gives a limited extra turn, Sunken Treasure gains an Action you don't have in play (often a
Sentry or Secret Passage, or Distant Shore), and who controls the pile's rotation decides access
to Distant Shore. Minionpawn won the Old Map split 3-1 (worth Orchard points) and built an
Abundance mega-turn; the commentators thought he should have bought Sunken Treasure instead of
cheap cards bought only to trigger Abundances. yurikamome had the only Distant Shore and Sunken
Treasure for a while and won by a clear margin as the piles ran out (Estates empty, Curses
and Courtyards low).

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) No village: plays a good-stuff/cantrip deck, not a drawing engine. "There's nothing that you're building to here, I don't think." (commentator)
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Orchard drives scoring: collect three or more copies of several Actions (Sentry, Old Map, Secret Passage, Courtyard); Nearby Courtyards are cheap late Orchard points.
- **R3** [supporting] (consensus + result) Sentry is the 5/2 opening.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Goatherd is strong here because opponents trash often (River Shrine, Sentry, Curses from Sickness).
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Sunken Treasure is good: most turns leave Actions unplayed, so it gains useful Actions, including Distant Shore.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Sickness turns on via River Shrine's Sun; once active, each turn you take a Curse onto your deck or discard three cards, which feeds trashing and pile counts.
- **R7** [supporting] (result) Estates (from Distant Shore and buys), Curses and Courtyards are the likely three piles.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) A late River Shrine (terminal trasher) when Goatherd would be the natural add; the commentators couldn't see the reason except speeding up Sickness.
- **T2** (single expert) Spending buys on cheap cards just to trigger stored Abundances instead of Sunken Treasure. "Look at this hand and tell me that distant shore would not be better as a sunken treasure." (commentator)

## Disagreements
- Whether Ducat is better than nothing here: the commentators were unsure.
- The commentators thought Minionpawn's Old Map lead (3-1) didn't make up for yurikamome's Sentry.
