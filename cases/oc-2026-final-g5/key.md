# Key: 2026 Online Championship final, game 5: Sentinel, Tools, Caravan, Villa, Innovation

## Sources
- [Lemonspawn and Sharur, neutral commentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3oj4O-lLaH4), 1:35:00-2:03:00.
  Quotes are attributed to "commentator".
- Result, read from the end screen: a 33-33 tie after 12 turns each (yurikamome was player 1).
  The tie gave agoo03 the half point he needed for the title. yurikamome had 5 Provinces and
  1 Duchy; agoo03 had 3 Provinces and 5 Duchies. Both decks held 1 Sentinel, 3 Caravan, 3 Villa,
  3 Vault and 1 Silver, with 3-5 Lost City, 3-4 Tools and 3-4 Gold. Neither bought Innovation;
  Band of Misfits, Junk Dealer, Lighthouse and Harbor Village don't appear in either final deck.

## Expert summary
The commentators named Sentinel as the trasher and Sentinel/Caravan as the opening, with Borrow
for a Band of Misfits (played as a second Sentinel) as an alternative; both players opened
Sentinel/Caravan. Tools is the payload engine: it gains a copy of anything in play, so Tools
gains Caravans, Lost Cities, Villas and Golds. Draw comes from Caravan and Lost City, with Vault
turning extra draw into money; they thought one or two Vaults were enough and that Lost City
should have been taken aggressively, enough to pile it. Their main criticism was Innovation:
with Villa returning you to the Action phase, Innovating a Caravan or Lost City gained in the Buy
phase keeps the turn going, and they argued both players underplayed it. Late on yurikamome was
$2 short of a triple-Province turn, which they said Innovation would have fixed. The game tied
and the match went to agoo03.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) Open Sentinel/Caravan: Sentinel is the trasher, Caravan makes sure you see it. "Sentinel Caravan is my open" (commentator). Both players opened it.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Tools is the main payload and gainer here: use it to copy Caravans, Lost Cities, Villas and Gold in play. "Totally want tools." (commentator)
- **R3** [core] (consensus) Draw comes from Caravan and Lost City; take Lost Cities aggressively, several in a turn, despite giving the opponent a card. "I think lost city should have piled earlier." (commentator)
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Innovation is worth its $6 here: Villa returns you to the Action phase, so a gained Caravan or Lost City can be played the same turn. The commentators called it underplayed and said it would have won yurikamome the game.
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus) Keep Vaults to one or two; more add little payload. "Villa Vault is like Market" (commentator)
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Borrow for Band of Misfits in the opening, played as Sentinel, is a reasonable alternative to a second $3-$4 card.
- **R7** [supporting] (consensus) Villa supplies +Buy and the gain-and-play trick; you need a few.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Band of Misfits after the opening: "I don't think Band of Misfits is doing ... like there's any real reason to get" it later.
- **T2** (consensus) Taking many Vaults instead of Lost Cities (both players took three).
- **T3** (single expert) Junk Dealer as the trasher instead of Sentinel. "Junk Dealer, which you could borrow for. But I don't think I would." (commentator)

## Disagreements
- Innovation's size: one commentator said the game can be won without it but getting it is better;
  the other thought it a significant edge. Neither player bought it.
- Gold: one commentator thought a turn-five Gold was premature, wanting Innovation first; the
  other didn't mind it.

## Grader notes
- yurikamome played under a running clock in the late game, which the commentators said made the
  fancier lines harder. The tie says little about which deck was better built.
- Lighthouse and Harbor Village were not discussed; credit plausible uses as extras.
