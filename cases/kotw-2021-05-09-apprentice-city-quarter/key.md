# Key: KotW 5/9/2021: Vineyard, University, City Quarter, Masquerade

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/n8wp2l/), 2021-05-09, 8 comments.
  EphesosX gave the only plan; treelorf (strong regular) added an Advance note.
  Nobody tested it.

## Expert summary
EphesosX: Vineyard beats Duke even with Count and Annex, so build an Action engine
and score with Vineyards. Open Masquerade/Potion, then University to gain Actions
(Counts). City Quarter provides draw and Actions, and Storeroom provides +Buy and
cycles non-Action cards out of hand before City Quarter. Get 2-3 Potions for many
Universities and the Vineyards. Skip Duke unless needed (or after winning the Duchy
split 6-2), but contest Duchies if the opponent goes Duke. Expect a three-pile ending,
likely Duchy, Vineyard and Count. treelorf reminds that Advance can trash Necropolis
(the Shelters are in use) for a good Action.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert) Vineyard (Action-dense engine) is the main VP, ahead of Duke. "Vineyard > Duke, even with Count and Annex" (EphesosX)
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Opens Masquerade/Potion, then University to gain Actions; 2-3 Potions overall.
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) City Quarter for draw and Actions; Storeroom for +Buy and to cycle non-Actions before City Quarter.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Advance trashes Necropolis for a better Action. "Dont forget about advance/necropolis!" (treelorf)
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Expects a three-pile ending (Duchy, Vineyard, Count) and contests Duchies if the opponent goes Duke.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Duke as the main plan. "I'd probably skip Duke unless I really need it" (EphesosX)
