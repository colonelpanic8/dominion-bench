# Key: KotW 2/7/2021: Groundskeeper, Mill, Crop Rotation, Silk Road

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/lf3v6o/), 2021-02-07, 12 comments.
  willowhelmiam gave a build line; cyanraichu and PotusChrist reported bot games;
  PathfindingLibrary (strong regular) commented on Cemetery. No game against a human.

## Expert summary
A green-heavy, alt-VP board that will end on piles. The thread agrees on
Groundskeepers (+1 VP per Victory gain) as the main scorer, Crop Rotation as early as
possible (discard a Victory card each turn for +2 Cards), Mills (VP, Silk Road count
and economy), and Baron/Contraband for +Buy. Silk Road, Gardens, Farm (Harem) and
Cemetery all add VP. Crossroads is the draw, but it won't draw the whole deck.
PathfindingLibrary wants at least one Cemetery trashing turn, because Crossroads draw is
finicky and you need to find Groundskeepers every turn. Colony/Platinum are a
distraction here.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Plays a green-heavy alt-VP deck (Mill, Cemetery, Farm, Silk Road, Gardens) and expects a pile ending. "The board will always pile" (cyanraichu)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Groundskeeper is the main VP engine: take many and play them on gaining turns. (cyanraichu, willowhelmiam, PathfindingLibrary)
- **R3** [core] (majority) Buys Crop Rotation as soon as possible. (willowhelmiam, cyanraichu)
- **R4** [supporting] (majority) Values Mill highly: VP, a Victory card for Silk Road and Crop Rotation, and $ when discarding.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Uses Cemetery's on-gain trashing at least once to thin. "I would aim to get a cemetery trash off, maybe multiple." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R6** [supporting] (majority) +Buy from Baron, and Contraband (cyanraichu would take it over Gold here).
- **R7** [supporting] (single expert) A few Crossroads (3-4) as draw; the deck won't draw itself fully.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Going for Platinum/Colony. "Interesting that Platinum/Colony is included because you will never touch them." (cyanraichu)

## Disagreements

  "a couple"; both say it matters more against a human who greens.
- PotusChrist won a bot game without using Cemetery's trashing at all; PathfindingLibrary
  still wants it.
