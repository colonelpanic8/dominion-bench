## Expert summary
Sickness (the Prophecy, triggered by Rustic Village's Sun tokens) is coming:
every turn each player must gain a Curse onto their deck or discard 3 cards,
and you need a plan for it. Horton says the trashing here is too slow to keep
up with the Curses, but Ronin, which he calls the best card for making
draw-to-X decks consistent, solves the problem; other tools help too. By the
endgame the deck is a big pile with almost everything in it, but it works well.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Plans for Sickness before it triggers; it will arrive because Rustic Village is the Omen.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Ronin (draw to 7, playable from the deck as a Shadow) is the core draw and the answer to Sickness.
- **R3** [core] (designer) The available trashing is too slow to clear the Curses, so the plan is to build a big deck that functions despite them rather than to trash them all.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) The endgame deck is large and contains most of the kingdom, yet still functions.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Relying on trashing to keep up with Sickness Curses.
- **T2** (designer) Ignoring Sickness until it triggers.
