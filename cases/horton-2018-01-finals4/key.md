# Key: Horton Winter 2018 finals 4: Villa, Library, Crown, Bank, Seaway

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, Winter 2018 Tournament Summary](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-winter-2018-tournament-summary/):
  the designer's write-up of the kingdom slated for the final game (Colonies).

## Expert summary
Everyone at the tournament went for the shiny distraction: Monastery, Grand Market and Den of
Sin, with Jack/Changeling support, racing for Grand Markets until piles ran out. Horton built
the board around Villa with draw-to-X (Library, Jack of All Trades). Enter your Buy phase,
spend Treasures, gain a Villa, which returns you to your Action phase, then draw again with
Library. Repeat this many times, put Seaway's +1 Buy on Villa, and finish by Crowning Banks
worth about $30 each. Scheme and Library make it very consistent: in playtesting he could
empty the Colonies by turn 9, sometimes turn 8. It's strong enough that Grand Market, Den of
Sin and Monastery, normally powerhouses, don't fit. Inheritance and Changeling give other
ways to get Villa's on-gain effect, so Villa denial isn't decisive (he usually needed only 5
Villas). His opening is Jack/Scheme, hoping to hit $5 for an early Library; buy Scheme on any
sub-$5 hand and add draw fast. You can buy the Bank mid-turn, then gain a Villa and draw it.
Seaway can be bought mid-turn, and Changelings in the Night phase can gain a Villa to return
to the turn.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Villa megaturn: gain Villa in the Buy phase to return to Actions and draw again with Library/Jack, repeated many times.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Payload is Crowned Banks (about $30 each) to buy Colonies; aims to end on Colonies by turn 8-9.
- **R3** [core] (designer) Scheme and Library give the consistency; opens Jack/Scheme and buys Schemes on sub-$5 hands.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Seaway on Villa for +Buy with each Villa gain.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Inheritance and Changeling are alternative ways to trigger Villa's on-gain if Villas run low.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Gets an early Library ASAP.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer + result) Monastery/Grand Market/Den of Sin deck: everyone at the tournament built it, and Horton says it is far weaker than the Villa deck. "Don't worry, you'll probably have one or two Grand Markets by the time I empty the Colonies" (Adam Horton, kingdom name)
- **T2** (designer) Racing for Villas to deny them: Inheritance and Changeling make denial ineffective.

## Disagreements
- None recorded; no tournament player built the intended deck.
