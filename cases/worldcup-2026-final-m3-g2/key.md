# Key: 2026 World Cup final, Minionpawn vs yurikamome, game 2: Wharf, Transmogrify, Hideout, Ironworks

## Sources
- [JNails with drsteelhammer, neutral commentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hY-_wubT4nw), 41:30-1:01:30.
  Captions don't mark speakers, so quotes are "commentator". Chat (Lemonspawn, silly potter)
  is quoted by the commentators.
- Match: Dominion World Cup 2026 final, match 3. yurikamome (player 1) won.

## Expert summary
A board with everything an engine needs: draw (Wharf, Storyteller, Apprentice, Imps from Devil's
Workshop), villages (Mountain Village, Hideout), trashing (Transmogrify, Apprentice, Hideout) and
+Buy (Wharf, Farmers' Market). The commentators' plan: an Ironworks or two, some villages, a
stack of Wharves and a few Storytellers. Swamp Hag looked weak here. Transmogrify/Silver seemed
automatic: Estates are the first thing to trash, Transmogrify is good at it and Hideout is not,
and Transmogrify on turn 3 often still hits $5 on turn 4. Ironworks/Silver was the backup.
Both finalists opened Hideout/Silver instead, which the commentators called surprising and wrong,
and they kept saying the players didn't respect Transmogrify (e.g. using it at the start of turn
to turn a spare Hideout into the Wharf you need). Ironworks rewards a thin deck with overdraw:
gain Ironworks, draw them, gain more. The number of Wharves was their proxy for who was ahead;
Minionpawn was stuck on one Wharf for a while. Farmers' Market was questioned (Lemonspawn:
Ironworks is better; Wharf already gives +Buy). yurikamome got more Wharves and won.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Builds a draw engine: Wharf as the main draw, Mountain Village/Hideout as villages, +Buy from Wharf.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Opens Transmogrify/Silver (Ironworks/Silver as the backup), because trashing Estates comes first and Transmogrify does it well. "I think transmogriphy silver just seemed pretty automatic." (commentator)
- **R3** [supporting] (consensus + result) Wharf count is the best proxy for who's ahead; prioritize Wharves at $5.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Ironworks scales with overdraw in a thin deck: gain Ironworks or Silvers mid-turn and draw them.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Transmogrify can turn a spent Hideout or Silver into a $5 (a Wharf) at the start of turn.
- **R6** [supporting] (consensus) Swamp Hag is weak on this board.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Opening Hideout over Transmogrify: Hideout trashes Estates badly and only trashes one card. "Everybody else hated that except the two people playing the game." (commentator)
- **T2** (single expert + chat) Farmers' Market when Ironworks or Wharf covers its role.

## Disagreements
- Both finalists opened Hideout, against the commentators and chat, and the winner was one of
  them; the opening claim rests on the commentators' reasoning, not on the result.
