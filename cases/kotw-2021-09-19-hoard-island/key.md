# Key: KotW 9/19/2021: Mountebank, King's Court, Lighthouse, Silk Merchant

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/prjaz5/), 2021-09-19, 8 comments.
  NovaRogue gave the only plan; PathfindingLibrary and treelorf (strong regulars)
  corrected it in one-liners.

## Expert summary
NovaRogue: trashing is weak, so junk the opponent with Mountebanks, multiplied by
King's Court when possible, and defend with Lighthouse. PathfindingLibrary's point on
King's Court-Mountebank: "you should have 2 Lighthouses by the time they have a King's
Court." treelorf rejected NovaRogue's 3/4 opening (Research/Smugglers): a 4/3 or 3/4
that doesn't include Silk Merchant is "madness". On Guildhall, treelorf: very good in
Mountebank games (Mountebank hands out Coppers), but usually not a reason to skip
Mountebank.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Mountebank junking (with King's Court) is central; trashing is weak. (NovaRogue; treelorf: don't skip Mountebank)
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Lighthouse as defense, two of them before the opponent has King's Court. (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Opens with Silk Merchant on 4/3 or 3/4. (treelorf)
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Guildhall is good in a Mountebank game but doesn't replace taking Mountebank. "Guildhall is very good in MB kingdoms, but usually not reason enough to skip MB" (treelorf)

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) A 4/3 or 3/4 opening without Silk Merchant, e.g. Research/Smugglers. "Suggesting an open on 4/3 or 3/4 that doesn't involve silk merchant? Madness" (treelorf)

## Disagreements
- UBKUBK suggested Mountebank could be skippable when the opponent has Lighthouses and
  Guildhall; treelorf's general view is otherwise.

## Grader notes
- Thin. Hoard, Island, Salvager and Seer weren't discussed.
- Mountebank is a first-edition card.
