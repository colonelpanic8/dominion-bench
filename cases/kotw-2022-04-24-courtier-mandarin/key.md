# Key: KotW 4/24/2022: Courtier, Mandarin, Village Green, Gamble

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/ub77bx/), 2022-04-24.
  PathfindingLibrary (strong regular) argued for a Courtier/Mandarin combo
  and reported clocking it at $118 with 8 Buys on turn 11 uncontested;
  Interesting_Dare_640 argued a Steward/Displace/Gamble plan.

## Expert summary
PathfindingLibrary called this "a very strong kingdom for Mandarin". His plan
is Courtier + Mandarin: gain Golds with Courtier, then use Mandarin's on-gain
(put all Treasures in play back on your deck) to replay the same Golds turn
after turn ("Put all the Golds in your deck and then play them 5 times per
turn"). Mandarin's play also topdecks a card from hand: he topdecks Village
Green each turn so Courtiers can reveal it (Action, Duration, Reaction: three
types, three choices). Playing Village Green itself "does nothing". +Buy is
still needed. He judged Displace "much too slow" and "not economical", and
both commenters agreed Cardinal is useless. Interesting_Dare_640 preferred
thinning with Steward, Tunnel and Village Green with Gamble, and Displace
Golds into Provinces; he did not test it.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert + result) Courtier + Mandarin is the main engine/payload. "Courtier Mandarin combo." (PathfindingLibrary; "$118 with 8 buys on turn 11 uncontested")
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Mandarin's on-gain topdecks the Golds in play so they are replayed every turn; Courtier gains the Golds.
- **R3** [core] (single expert) Courtier reveals a multi-type card (Village Green: three types), topdecked each turn with Mandarin's play. "Topdeck it each turn with Mandarin for Courtiers." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Still needs +Buy (from Courtier choices, Barge or Gamble) to use the money.
- **R5** [supporting] (single player) Thins early with Steward.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Displace as the main gainer/payload. "Displace is not economical." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **T2** (consensus) Cardinal. "Agree cardinal is useless." (PathfindingLibrary, after Interesting_Dare_640)

## Disagreements
- Interesting_Dare_640's Displace/Gamble plan (Displace Tunnel into Duchy and
  back for Golds, Silk Road alt VP) against PathfindingLibrary's Courtier/
  Mandarin combo. Neither posted a game.

## Grader notes
- Uses first-edition Mandarin (on-gain topdecks Treasures in play). The
  combo works only with that wording.
- The $118 figure is an uncontested solo clock; treat it as evidence the
  combo is fast, not as a target.
- Lurker, Silk Road, Tunnel and Way of the Goat were barely discussed.
