# Key: Making Luck ep. 117, kingdom 1: Jack of All Trades, Margrave, Hoard, Familiar

## Sources
- [Making Luck ep. 117 "Rapid-Fire Kingdom Analysis 2"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_6QemY1KJtc&t=541s),
  2022-09-03, 9:01-14:10. Adam Horton and Wandering Winder (both top players)
  look at a randomly rolled kingdom for the first time, without playtesting.

## Expert summary
Both hosts see zero villages and few non-terminals (Familiar, a couple of
Treasures, parts of the Clashes pile), so they want a money deck built around
Jack of All Trades, "relatively close to Jack money". One or two more terminals
or Treasures come on top: a Margrave or Hoard at $5, maybe Catacombs or Capital.
Jack also stands up well to the board's attacks (Familiar, Margrave), Archer
excepted. Remake came up as a way to answer Familiar's Curses. They settle on
opening Jack and adding Remake only if the opponent commits to Potion/Familiar.
Rabble and Temple are each the third-best of their kind and get skipped, and
Battle Plan isn't wanted. The rest depends on what the opponent does.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Notes there is no village, so this is a money deck (few terminals), not an engine.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Jack of All Trades is the central card: plays Jack money and opens Jack. "Inclined to play this as something relatively close to Jack money." (a host)
- **R3** [supporting] (consensus) Adds a $5 terminal or Treasure on top: Margrave or Hoard (or Catacombs) rather than a second draw engine piece; Margrave's discard attack is strong against the opponent's money deck.
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Remake is a reactive pick to deal with Curses if the opponent goes Familiar; it's not the opener when nobody has bought Potion.
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus) Doesn't commit to Potion/Familiar.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Picks attacks according to the opponent, e.g. takes an Archer once the opponent uncovers Archers, and values Margrave less when the opponent has 3 Jacks.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Rabble and Temple: each is the weakest of three similar cards here (draw: Catacombs, Margrave, Rabble; trashers: Jack, Remake, Temple).
- **T2** (consensus) Building an engine around Clashes (Battle Plan, Warlord) with no real villages.

## Disagreements
- Capital: one host is fairly warm on it as a late money-deck pickup; the other isn't warm on it at all.
- Catacombs vs Margrave: one host prefers Catacombs and Capital to Margrave; the other values Margrave's attack more.

## Grader notes
- A short first-look discussion. Credit a clear "Jack/money with one or two
  $5 terminals" plan fully, whichever $5 it picks.
- (My note) Hoard pairs naturally with greening in a money deck; credit it as
  a reasonable extra.
