# Key: 2025 Online Championship semifinal, game 5: Port, Inventor, Altar, Tactician

## Sources
- [Lemonspawn's Dominion Videos](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e2dIV5xzXTo), 1:38:00-1:51:30.
  Commentary by Lemonspawn and Stef (a former champion), both strong players,
  with tracer's chat comments. jonts went first. VRSmith won on her turn 7 by
  emptying the Port, Inventor and Altar piles while ahead 6-0, taking the match
  4-1. Final decks read from the end-of-game screen.

## Expert summary
Pre-game, the commentators thought Port looked better than Rustic Village
and that Sickness might not slow the game much if decks gain control fast.
Chat pointed out Toil with Tactician (Toil plays an Action during the Buy
phase, so Tactician can be played after buying), which a commentator called
a strong synergy. One commentator would open Inventor/Silver. jonts got Altar
on turn 2 and looked ahead; the commentators then advised loading up on Ports
and Inventors, with a second Tactician, because "inventors is going to
outscale anything you do with all the other cards". They criticised Gold Mine
(Rustic Village was "way better") and a second Buried Treasure.

VRSmith won the Port split (10 of 12) and built Inventors, Altars and
Council Rooms. On turn 7 she chained Inventors (each makes every card cost $1
less for the turn) with Ports for Actions and Council Rooms for draw, so
Altar and Inventor gains kept getting cheaper; she took a Province and
emptied the Inventor and Altar piles, ending the game on three piles. Her
final deck: 10 Ports, 8 Inventors, 8 Altars, 3 Council Rooms, a Tactician.
The commentators had expected a Province turn and were surprised by the
pile-out; afterwards they said the early Altar "was just not as exciting as we
thought".

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) Port is the village, better than Rustic Village; take the Port split. VRSmith got 10 of the 12.
- **R2** [core] (consensus + result) Inventor is the scaling payload: stacked Inventors cut costs so each later gain is bigger. "inventors is going to outscale anything you do" (commentator)
- **R3** [core] (result) The game can end very fast on piles: Port (gains two at a time), Inventor and Altar emptied on turn 7. An analysis should see a quick three-pile threat with these gainers.
- **R4** [supporting] (result) Altar plus Inventor cost reduction gains Altars, Inventors and Ports cheaply; Altar also trashes the starting cards.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Toil with Tactician is a strong synergy.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Council Room provides the draw for a big Inventor turn; tracer suggested piling Council Room as an alternative finish.
- **R7** [supporting] (consensus) Gold Mine is weak here; Rustic Village was way better.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus + result) Valuing an early Altar alone as decisive; jonts got one on turn 2 and still lost by turn 7.
- **T2** (consensus) Gold Mine; Gold and Debt don't fit a gain-and-play Inventor deck.

## Disagreements
- Opening: Inventor/Silver (one commentator) vs early Altar (jonts). Nobody worked out a full plan pre-game.

## Grader notes
- The commentary never laid out the turn-7 pile-out before it happened; R3 is from the result. My view: Port's double gain and Inventor's stacking reduction make a three-pile with Altar or Inventor plausible from card text, so credit an analysis that spots the threat even if it names different piles.
- Treasure Trove, Herb Gatherer (Augurs) and Way of the Mule were barely discussed; Sickness was expected to matter less than it looks.
