# Key: Horton Summer 2018 finals 4: Festival, Journeyman, Gardens, Silk Road

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, Summer 2018 Tournament Summary](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-summer-2018-tournament-summary/):
  the designer's write-up of a 3-player finals kingdom.

## Expert summary
Horton sees two decks. One is a slog with Bureaucrat, Silk Road, Gardens and Tunnel, with
Festival for support. The other is a higher-payload deck with Festival and Journeyman for
draw, supported by Artisan and Chapel, which can also use Shanty Town and Tunnel with Secret
Passage's help, and Mission. He thinks the Journeyman deck is probably best when uncontested,
and it's closer if two players go for it and a third plays Bureaucrat. Adapting to opponents
matters, which is harder if you don't open Chapel. He'd stack a 5/2 for Festival on turn 1
and probably Chapel on turn 2, because Festival is good in every deck here, then aim for $6
for Artisan. He'd build the draw deck unless too few Festivals made it a losing proposition,
and then pivot toward Silk Roads rather than Gardens.
