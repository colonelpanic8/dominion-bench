# Key: Horton 2025-10 Designed Kingdom 2: Wharf, Crystal Ball, Way of the Turtle, Plan, Colonies

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "October 2025 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/october-2025-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, October 2025. Horton designed and playtested this
  kingdom for the bracket. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1i-xyLMNrixItE8fQi91r3od4uZAdHhdTSi86wCznmnw)
  (5/2 openings disallowed).

## Expert summary
There is no village, so Wharf might look weak, but Crystal Ball (which can play
Actions off the top of your deck in the Buy phase) and Way of the Turtle (set
the card aside to play next turn) easily cover all the village needs. Horton
calls a Plan/Oasis opening obvious. After that he wants one or two more Oases
and all of the Crystal Balls, probably with a Pirate as the first $5 to help
gain them. The deck comes together nicely and is fun to play.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Wharf is the draw despite no village, because Crystal Ball and Way of the Turtle provide the village effect.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Opens Plan/Oasis.
- **R3** [core] (designer) Takes all of the Crystal Balls.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Adds one or two more Oases after the opening.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Pirate as the first $5 helps gain Crystal Balls (Treasures).

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Dismissing Wharf because there is no village.
