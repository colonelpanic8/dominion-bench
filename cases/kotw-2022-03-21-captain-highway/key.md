# Key: KotW 3/20/2022: Captain, Highway, Envoy, Artisan

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/tiz0j9/), 2022-03-21.
  PathfindingLibrary (strong regular) gave a one-line build order; SignError
  and quantumhovercraft explained Captain/Highway interactions.

## Expert summary
PathfindingLibrary's build: open Envoy/Silver, get two Captains, then an
Artisan, then the rest of the Captains. Captain plays an Action from the
Supply costing up to $4, so Highway cost reduction widens its reach: with one
Highway in play Captain can play $5 cards, with two Highways it can play
Artisan, and with three those Artisans can gain Provinces. Captaining a
Highway does not reduce costs, because Highway counts copies in play.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert) Captain is the key card; buy many of them. "Envoy/Silver. 2 Captains. Artisan. The rest of the Captains." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Opens Envoy/Silver.
- **R3** [supporting] (majority) Highways in play let Captain play pricier Supply cards: $5s with one, Artisan with two. "with 2 Highways in play, you can use Captain to play Artisan." (SignError)
- **R4** [supporting] (single player) With three Highways, Captained or played Artisans can gain Provinces. (quantumhovercraft)
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Captaining a Highway in the Supply does not reduce costs; Highway counts copies in play.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Expecting Captain playing a Highway from the Supply to lower costs. "Highway cares about the number in play, not the number of plays." (PathfindingLibrary)

## Disagreements

## Grader notes
