# Key: Horton Winter 2019 Table 4: Scholar, Scepter, Star Chart, Capitalism

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion: Winter 2019 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/repost-dominion-winter-2019-tournament-summary/),
  tournament 2019-01-12. Horton designed and playtested this kingdom for a
  2-player tournament (played in the Swiss rounds). [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1__ePuWrM5XpCsVuLMIA3dmGWHKNOoZV5auC1ObWGbbk).

## Expert summary
The core deck has a very short Action phase: play one Scholar (discard hand,
draw 7). In the Buy phase play all your Treasures, then play Scepter
replaying Scholar to draw a fresh 7, and repeat. Star Chart makes sure you
draw Scholar every turn, which is essential. Capitalism opens payload beyond
basic Treasures. Legionary punishes the distractions: Laboratory, Warehouse,
Tunnel, and a Grand Market/Conspirator deck can be built, but they have
reliability problems under Legionary (Fortune Teller then Legionary is
brutal) and there's no trashing. The Scholar deck doesn't care about
Legionary, and Scepter can be payload or draw as needed. It can use Tunnel
if needed, and still faces decisions about playing Coppers, since it needs a
few Grand Markets in play for Scepter to copy for +Buy (the only +Buy). Many
players went for the distractions; nobody fully committed to the Scholar
plan.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Scholar played in the Action phase, then Scepter replays Scholar in the Buy phase after Treasures are played, to redraw a full hand repeatedly.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Star Chart topdecks Scholar so it starts every turn.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) The Scholar deck shrugs off Legionary because it discards and redraws.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Grand Market is the only +Buy; Scepter copying Grand Market is part of the payload, so keep a few.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Capitalism adds payload options (Action cards with +$ become Treasures).
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Legionary plus Fortune Teller attack decks that lack draw-after-attack.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer + result) Laboratory/Warehouse/Tunnel-based decks: distractions that are unreliable against Legionary.
- **T2** (designer) A Grand Market/Conspirator deck with no trashing: workable but unreliable under Legionary.

## Disagreements
- None recorded; nobody played the designed line in the tournament, so there's no result.

## Grader notes
- Scepter replays an Action played this turn that is still in play; Scholar
  played in the Action phase is still in play during the Buy phase. That is
  card-text support for R1 (my note).
- Fortune Teller is first edition.
