# Key: KotW 8/15/2021: Chapel, Inventor, Mastermind, Sanctuary

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/p5a169/), 2021-08-15, 8 comments.
  PathfindingLibrary (strong regular) gave the plan in one line; quantumhovercraft and
  theRealVim sketched the finished deck; others discussed openings.

## Expert summary
PathfindingLibrary: Chapel into Inventor/Mastermind "looks fast", and Peasant may be
skippable. Mastermind replays an Action three times next turn, so on Inventor it
gains three cards and cuts costs by $3. quantumhovercraft suggests a deck of
Sanctuary, two Masterminds and three Inventors all but empties the Provinces, and
theRealVim agrees you needn't chase Museum. cyanraichu notes that several otherwise
powerful cards aren't worth buying here.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert) Chapel trashing into an Inventor + Mastermind deck. "Chapel into Inventor/Mastermind looks fast." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R2** [core] (majority) A very small deck (around Sanctuary, 2 Masterminds, 3 Inventors) can gain most of the Provinces through stacked Inventor cost reduction. (quantumhovercraft, theRealVim)
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Peasant may be skippable. (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R4** [supporting] (majority) Several strong cards on the board (and Museum) are unnecessary for this plan. (cyanraichu, theRealVim)

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Building toward Museum. "You probably don't even need to get hung up on Museum doing it this way." (theRealVim)

## Disagreements
- Openings: Chapel/Peasant on any split (mountebanker), Sanctuary/Peasant (MadtownLems),
  and sumrex8 hinted at a 5/2 without Chapel.

## Grader notes
- Untested; Colony status is unknown (see case.json).
- Grand Market, Bounty Hunter, Diplomat, Archive, Mill and Tomb weren't discussed.
