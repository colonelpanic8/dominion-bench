# Key: Horton Galactic 2022 Finals 1: Rats, Tomb, Seaway, Cultist, Miser

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion Galactic Championship Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-galactic-championship-summary/),
  Gen Con, 2022-08-04 to 06. Horton designed and playtested this kingdom for
  the later stages of a 48-entry 2-player tournament. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/16Yun1MG09amRq-PlPe6ilxC3UZL9snIfgrAYmLn5Ovo).

## Expert summary
The design aim: decks full of junk (Cultist, Witch, Swindler, Barbarian), where
Rats, the only trashing, plus Tomb improves your deck enough to decide the
game. Tomb gives VP per trash, Rats trashing Cultists triggers Cultist's
on-trash draw, and the pile control from Rats (especially with Seaway's +Buy
token on Rats) should win even with more junk than the opponent. Horton's
playtests had a direct Seaway/Rats deck aiming to three-pile Coppers,
Curses and Rats, which wasn't fast enough to beat a Miser deck using Hunting
Lodge. But those tests predated Ruins in the Supply (Cultist adds them), and
Ruins let the Seaway/Rats deck end the game fast, with enough points, before
anything else gets going. One tournament game was a Seaway/Rats mirror;
Horton found it interesting but not what he intended.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Rats is the only trashing; with Tomb each trash scores VP, so Rats is a core payload/control card.
- **R2** [core] (designer + result) Seaway on Rats with a pile-out plan (Coppers, Curses, Rats, and Ruins) is fast and strong here, faster than Horton intended.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Trashing Cultists with Rats draws cards (Cultist's on-trash) and scores Tomb VP.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Junkers make everyone's deck bad; accept junk and fight with pile control and Tomb points.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) A Miser deck with Hunting Lodge was the playtest answer to Seaway/Rats before Ruins were added.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Assuming a clean, thin deck is achievable; the board is a junk slog by design.

## Disagreements
- Horton's intended Rats/Tomb control deck vs. the "degenerate" Seaway/Rats pile-out, which the Ruins made stronger than he wanted. He doesn't say the Miser deck still beats it.

## Grader notes
- Ruins count as a Supply pile for the three-pile ending; that's why Cultist
  speeds the pile-out (my note).
- No winner reported for the mirror game.
