# Key: KotW 9/12/2021: Butcher, City Quarter, Night Watchman, Tunnel

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/pn49ed/), 2021-09-12.
  geoffreyward144 reported a solo playtest; PathfindingLibrary, a strong regular,
  gave the opening and turn-3 line; SignError and willowhelmiam argued alternatives.

## Expert summary
The best-supported plan is a City Quarter engine opened Butcher/Night Watchman.
Night Watchman sets up the next hand: it guarantees Butcher on turn 3 and later
topdecks City Quarter so it is drawn with Actions to reveal. Butcher turns
Cursed Gold into a second Butcher early, City Quarter is bought nearly every
turn, and Coffers are saved for Butcher rather than spent. Payload is Gold from
discarding Tunnels (via Embassy and Night Watchman), Butchered into Provinces.
The solo playtest took 8 Provinces by turn 11 with two Embassies and two
Tunnels. Merchant Guild was argued down: Actions are scarce and there is little
cheap to buy.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert + result) Builds a City Quarter engine rather than money. "I got 8 provinces turn 11 playing this by myself." (geoffreyward144)
- **R2** [core] (single expert + result) Night Watchman topdecks City Quarter (and sets up the next hand) so City Quarter draws well. "Using Night Watchmen to topdeck a City Quarter every hand is very good." (geoffreyward144)
- **R3** [core] (consensus) Payload is Gold gained from discarding Tunnel, turned into Provinces with Butcher. "The payload is gold from Tunnel Butchered into Provs" (geoffreyward144)
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert + result) Opens Butcher/Night Watchman. "Butcher/Night Watchman opening if possible." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Butchers Cursed Gold into a second Butcher on turn 3 and saves Coffers for later Butcher plays. "Cursed Gold into second Butcher. Buy City Quarter. Don't spend any Coffers." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Night Watchman controls the reshuffle (discarding exactly 2 gets a clean shuffle with Butcher on turn 4).
- **R7** [supporting] (majority) Embassy discards Tunnels for Gold; one or two Embassies and one or two Tunnels suffice.
- **R8** [supporting] (single expert) A few Faithful Hounds help: Butchered from Estates or Curses and returned to hand when discarded. "1-2 Faithful Hounds is good." (PathfindingLibrary)

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Merchant Guild as main payload. "No Merchant Guilds. Action is too limited." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **T2** (single expert) Fool's Gold in the City Quarter deck: stop cards that don't reveal as Actions.

## Disagreements
- SignError and Mirendana2 preferred a money deck (Nomad Camp/Tunnel/Night Watchman,
  then Provinces), with Ritual on Golds and Tower offsetting Curses. Nobody reported
  testing it against the engine.
- willowhelmiam wanted Merchant Guild payload; PathfindingLibrary disagreed.

## Grader notes
- The engine claim rests on one solo playtest plus PathfindingLibrary's line.
  A careful money analysis that names the Tunnel/Butcher payload deserves partial
  credit on R1.
- Ritual and Tower interactions (Ritual on Gold, Tower counting Curses once the
  pile empties) were mentioned only in passing; credit them as beyond-key extras.
