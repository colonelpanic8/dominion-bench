# Key: 2026 World Cup final, Minionpawn vs yurikamome, game 6: Bridge, Herald, Cage, Outpost, Colonies

## Sources
- [JNails with drsteelhammer, neutral commentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hY-_wubT4nw), 2:22:30-2:39:00.
  Captions don't mark speakers, so quotes are "commentator".
- Match: Dominion World Cup 2026 final, match 3. yurikamome (player 1) won, taking the match 4-2.

## Expert summary
The commentators read this as a Bridge mega-turn board. Thinning is plentiful (Investment, Cage,
Island, Maelstrom), but the only draw is Herald, so get very thin so that Heralds reliably hit
Actions, then add many Bridges. Colony is relevant, and Outpost is worth it: an extra turn is
almost a +Buy, since you can buy more on it. Cage looked good because the deck can score in one
or two turns: setting cards aside until you gain a Victory card keeps junk out for the whole
build. One commentator's proposed opening was Walled Village/Cage: Cage wants a cantrip beside it, and
Walled Village still topdecks itself when played via Way of the Seal. Both players opened
Investment/Cage. Way of the Seal on a Bridge lets you topdeck a gain (e.g. a Cage) to fix the
next hand. Minionpawn had a disastrous start (neither opener played on turns 3-4, then an
Investment/Cage collision) and his Heralds kept missing even after he won the Herald split.
yurikamome had a nearly full deck in hand by turn 9, used Outpost turns, and finished with Walled
Villages topdecked by Way of the Seal to guarantee the win. Guildmaster with Circle of Witches
(cursing) was dismissed as far too slow.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) Plan is a thin Bridge deck with Herald as the only draw, aiming at a mega-turn of Colonies/Provinces.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Thin hard first (Investment, Cage, Island, Maelstrom) so Heralds hit Actions consistently.
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert + result) Outpost is worth buying: the extra turn works like an extra Buy.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Cage is good here: it parks junk until the scoring turn, and scoring happens in only one or two turns.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Walled Village (topdecks itself) and Way of the Seal (topdeck a gain) help set up reliable turns.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert + result) Colony is the main VP target, and the Walled Village pile is a pile-out route for the finish.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Guildmaster for Circle of Witches Curses: Favors build too slowly and each play gives only one Curse. "The guild master I think is just way too slow." (commentator)
- **T2** (single expert) Treating Island as free points mid-build: gaining a Victory card pops your Cage and returns the junk to hand.

## Disagreements

  (chat also floated Bridge/Cage).
