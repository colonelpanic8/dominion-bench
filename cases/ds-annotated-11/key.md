# Key: DS Annotated Game #11: Ambassador, Warehouse, Tunnel, Apprentice, Conspirator (Colonies)

## Sources
- [Annotated Game #11](https://dominionstrategy.com/2012/04/23/annotated-game-11/)
  ([preview](https://dominionstrategy.com/2012/04/20/annotated-game-11-preview/)),
  2012-04-23: theory (blog founder) annotates his loss to Captain_Frisk, including
  a post-game test of an alternative line.

## Expert summary
Ambassador "sits on" the game: both players pass Estates and Coppers back and
forth, so the opening is long and the best engine is one you can build while
slogging through it. There is no +Buy and no +Actions, which caps any deck at one
Colony or Province a turn. theory names Warehouse the true key card because it
enables Tunnel (discard for Golds), Conspirator, Apprentice and even Counting
House. He sees three lines: Ambassador into Warehouse/Conspirator/Scheme;
Ambassador into Warehouse/Tunnel/Apprentice, where Apprentice trashes Golds to
draw and Warehouse discards Tunnels to regain Golds; and Warehouse/Counting House
with Bank, using the opponent's Ambassadored Coppers (swingy, found in a game the
next day). With 5/2, theory opened Ambassador and took the Conspirator line;
Captain_Frisk opened Apprentice and took the Tunnel line, thinned faster, reached
Colonies, and won. theory concludes his engine was too slow and had a lower peak.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert) Ambassador dominates the opening and slows the game; both players should take it, and plan an engine that builds through the junk exchange.
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Warehouse is the key enabler (Tunnel, Conspirator, Apprentice, Counting House).
- **R3** [core] (result + single expert) Warehouse/Tunnel/Apprentice into Golds and Colonies beat the Conspirator/Scheme Province line.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) No +Buy and no +Actions cap payload at one big card per turn.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) On 5/2 open Apprentice (it's hard to afford later) or Ambassador.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert, post-game test) Warehouse/Counting House plus Bank, fed by the opponent's Ambassadors, is a viable but swingy third line.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert + result) The Ambassador/Conspirator/Scheme engine racing for Provinces: too slow and too low a ceiling against a Colony deck.

## Disagreements
- Scrying Pool: theory calls it probably a distraction, but in the game Captain_Frisk's
  Scrying Pools drew more than a Laboratory once his deck was thin.
