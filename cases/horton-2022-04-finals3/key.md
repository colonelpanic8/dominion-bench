# Key: Horton Spring 2022 Finals 3: Mint, Crown, Rabble, Way of the Rat, Sheepdog

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion: Spring 2022 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-spring-2022-tournament-summary/),
  tournament 2022-04-09. Horton designed and playtested this kingdom for the
  2-player finals bracket. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Yf8ZZyyavqIiKeg_rTFl1x-KuDldt_1dtOoN7c4Pc0s).

## Expert summary
Built around Menagerie synergies on top of Crown and Mint: Way of the Rat and
Sheepdog take them further. Payload is Animal Fair and Improve. Because of
Rabble attacks and Crown being the only village, you must build a lot and use
every tool to get as many Crowns as possible. Horton's intended timing (from
his sheet note and the post): use Havens to set up a Mint later on, and keep
the Havens afterward as a way to set aside Estates since there's no Estate
trashing; then Mint/Crown/Rat synergy, Rabble for draw, and Animal Fair with
Improve as end payload. In the finals game the eventual champion opened Mint
and planned to Animal Fair the Haven; Horton thinks this left him behind, but
he won with two explosive turns after a lucky last shuffle.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Must build heavily: Rabble attacks and Crown as the only village make Crown count critical.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Mint thins Treasures; Haven sets up the Mint turn.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Way of the Rat (gain copies of Crown, Mint and other key cards) and Sheepdog are the key Menagerie synergies.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Haven stays useful later as pseudo-thinning of Estates (no Estate trashing on the board).
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Payload is Animal Fair and Improve.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Rabble is the draw.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer, contested by result) Opening Mint immediately: Horton thinks it falls behind the Haven-into-Mint plan, though the player who did it won.

## Disagreements
- The winner's Mint opening against Horton's preferred later Mint; the game was decided by a lucky last shuffle.

## Grader notes
