# Key: KotW 2/28/2021: Groom, Cemetery, Catapult, Battlefield

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/lv2jj2/), 2021-02-28, 11 comments.
  presumably_alterable, willowhelmiam and pancake_stacker12 debated the opening;
  cyanraichu commented on piles; halhag reported a 6-player game (not 2-player).

## Expert summary
The agreed core is Groom gaining Cemetery: Groom gains a Victory card for +1 Card +1
Action, Cemetery trashes up to 4 cards from hand when gained, and Battlefield pays
2 VP for each Victory gain. What goes with it is disputed. presumably_alterable wants
Stockpile on 4/3; willowhelmiam prefers Patrician, which can't hurt if Groom misses
the shuffle; pancake_stacker12 (highest-voted) favors Groom/Catapult, arguing the
handsize attack matters with mediocre draw and that Catapult punishes the opponent's
Groom-Cemetery turn. cyanraichu expects quick piles (Cemetery, Patrician/Emporium,
maybe Stockpile) and still wants Bounty Hunter.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Groom gaining Cemetery for thinning, with Battlefield VP on each gain. "I feel like you can't ignore grooming cemeteries for thinning + battlefield points here." (presumably_alterable)
- **R2** [supporting] (single expert) Catapult is worth taking, in the opening or soon after, as a handsize attack (Groom gains Silvers to fuel it). (pancake_stacker12)
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Expects a fast pile ending on Cemetery, Patrician/Emporium or Stockpile. (cyanraichu)
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Picks up Bounty Hunter. (cyanraichu)
- **R5** [supporting] (majority) Battlefield points shape the game: Victory gains (Cemetery, Castles) score 2 VP each while the Battlefield tokens last.
