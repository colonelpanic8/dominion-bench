# Key: KotW 7/4/2021: Lurker, Coven, Ironworks, Mill

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/oe16xm/), 2021-07-04, 9 comments.
  avocadro (the KotW host) explained the kingdom's construction; EphesosX and
  doinscottystuff gave plans; PathfindingLibrary (strong regular) refined them;
  Hasselhof88 and willowhelmiam argued for Rebuild.

## Expert summary
avocadro built the board from average-power cards and swapped out Hunting Grounds to
avoid the Lurker/Hunting Grounds combo. EphesosX's plan A is Lurker trashing Squire
from the Supply (Squire gains an Attack when trashed) to gain Coven, plus Ironworks
gaining Mill. PathfindingLibrary agreed that's the right direction, but said the
consequence matters most: the Squire and Mill piles will empty fast and Lurker and
Ironworks make a third pile easy, so the game is short and Curses sit in a deck for
one shuffle at most. He thinks buying Coven for $5 is as bad as usual, 1-2 Ironworks
is enough, and Rebuild is playable once you have Ironworks.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Lurker trashes Squire from the Supply to gain Coven, instead of buying Coven. (EphesosX, PathfindingLibrary)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Ironworks gains Mills (+card and VP). "Ironworking Mill is awesome!" (doinscottystuff; EphesosX, PathfindingLibrary agree)
- **R3** [core] (single expert) Expects a fast three-pile ending (Squire, Mill, and a third pile easily emptied with Lurker/Ironworks). (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) 1-2 Ironworks is enough. (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Rebuild is playable once Ironworks is in the deck, since few $4-5 cards compete. (PathfindingLibrary; Hasselhof88 and willowhelmiam rate it higher)

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Buying Coven for $5. "Buying Coven for $5 here would be about as bad as usual." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **T2** (single expert) Planning around trashing Curses or a long game; "The Curses will be in the deck for one shuffle at max." (PathfindingLibrary)

## Disagreements
- Nobles: EphesosX calls it high priority as the only draw; Hasselhof88 wants to win the
  Nobles split via Rebuild; PathfindingLibrary sees "no reason to care about winning the
  Nobles split."
- Rebuild: willowhelmiam thinks nothing beats it; PathfindingLibrary sees it as a side option.
