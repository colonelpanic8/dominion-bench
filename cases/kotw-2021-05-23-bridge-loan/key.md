# Key: KotW 5/23/2021: Steward, Nobles, Invest, Wayfarer, Bridge

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/njqjto/), 2021-05-23, 13 comments.
  willowhelmiam and Madmanquail gave engine plans; PathfindingLibrary (strong regular)
  gave the opening and Invest lines; EphesosX and BigSpoonFullOfSnark on Monument.

## Expert summary
A Bridge engine: Nobles (Investing in Nobles) as the village, draw from Nobles and
Wayfarer, and Bridges added once the deck draws itself, for a big Bridge turn.
PathfindingLibrary opens Steward/Steward, with Upgrade on 2/5 (Steward if Upgrade was
Invested). Going first on 3/4 he also uses Invest against the opponent's opener:
Steward + Invest in Upgrade against a Lurker opener, Steward + Invest in Steward
against Bridge or Monument openers. He and Madmanquail rate Wayfarer highly.
EphesosX called Monument irrelevant with Bridge in play.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Bridge engine, ending in a multi-Bridge turn. (willowhelmiam, Madmanquail, EphesosX)
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Opens Steward/Steward. "I prefer Steward/Steward." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Uses Invest tactically on the opponent's key opener (Upgrade vs Lurker, Steward vs Bridge/Monument), so you draw 2 whenever they gain it. (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R4** [supporting] (majority) Nobles is the village; Invest in Nobles. (willowhelmiam, Madmanquail)
- **R5** [supporting] (majority) Wayfarer is strong draw here. "Wayfarer is strong." (PathfindingLibrary; Madmanquail wants 2)

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Monument as payload (e.g. Monument/Loan opening). "Monument is pretty irrelevant with Bridge in play." (EphesosX)
- **T2** (single expert) Loan, Lurker and Vault as main pieces; willowhelmiam called them too slow.

## Disagreements
- Openings: Steward/Steward (PathfindingLibrary), Steward with Bridge or Upgrade
  (willowhelmiam), no two terminals (Madmanquail), Monument/Loan (BigSpoonFullOfSnark).
- willowhelmiam doubted Wayfarer; PathfindingLibrary disagreed.
