## Expert summary
The hosts admit their preview was "flat wrong". The tempting deck (Altar as
the only trashing, turning Estates into Wishing Wells or Schemes, Patrician
and Wishing Well for draw, then many Grand Markets) is far too slow: with no
village, no real draw and only Altar to thin, a Grand Market needs three
Silvers or Golds to collide, which that deck doesn't want to buy. Its best
draws don't beat the average draws of money. Money is also the higher point
ceiling here, because Treasure Trove and Palace together make each Silver
about a Duchy: Troves gain Golds and Coppers, so every bought Silver
completes another Palace set. The winning deck is Big Money with Treasure
Trove: buy Troves over Gold (about four, maybe a fifth), Silver at lower
amounts, then Provinces. Jake prefers a Scavenger/Smugglers opening
(Scavenger topdecks Trove or Gold; Smugglers gains extra Silvers). Warehouse
helps cycle past Coppers to Golds, but every Warehouse is a Silver you
didn't buy, so take few.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Plays a money deck, not an Altar/Grand Market (or Patrician/Wishing Well) build. "Really you just play big money here." (a host)
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Treasure Trove is the key money card: several copies (about four), bought over Gold.
- **R3** [core] (consensus) Sees the Treasure Trove + Palace interaction: Trove's Gold and Copper gains make each Silver complete a Palace set, so Silver is worth about 3 VP and money has the higher point ceiling.
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Explains why the Grand Market deck is slow: no village or real draw, Altar as the only thinning, and Grand Market needs three Silvers/Golds to buy.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Opens Scavenger/Smugglers (Smugglers for extra Silvers, Scavenger to topdeck Troves and Golds); WanderingWinder suggested Smugglers/Silver.
- **R6** [supporting] (majority) At most one or a few Warehouses, weighed against Silvers worth Palace points.
- **R7** [supporting] (result) In the hosts' games, Big Money with Treasure Trove beat the Grand Market deck by a wide margin; one host lost to a bot with double his score even after replaying Wishing Well guesses.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Altar thinning into a Grand Market/Scheme/Wishing Well deck. It was the hosts' own preview plan and they called it garbage once played.
- **T2** (consensus) Judging by card quality (Grand Market and Altar are "good cards") instead of counting how fast the deck comes online against money.
- **T3** (single expert) Many Warehouses in place of Silvers.

## Disagreements
- Opening: Jake prefers Scavenger/Smugglers; WanderingWinder Smugglers/Silver;
  in the preview a host proposed Scavenger/Silver.
- Warehouse count: Adam's first instinct was "a Warehouse or three";
  WanderingWinder thinks about one is right in a mirror.

## Grader notes
- The core of this case is rejecting the Grand Market build and seeing that
  Silver is worth points via Palace. An analysis that recommends Altar into
  Grand Markets as the main plan should score low even if its money details
  are good.
- Bank, Patrician/Emporium and Scheme went mostly undiscussed for the money
  deck. (My note) A Bank or two in the Trove money deck is plausible.
