# Key: Horton Summer 2017 finals game 2: Ambassador, Soothsayer, Donate, Wall

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, Summer 2017 Tournament Summary](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-summer-2017-tournament-summary/):
  the designer's write-up of a 3-player finals kingdom, with what he saw in the tournament games.

## Expert summary
The opening phase revolves around Soothsayer, Ambassador, Donate and Wall. Soothsayer gains
Golds and junks while Curses last; once they run out, Ambassador keeps opponents' decks
from getting too good. There is no +Buy, so too many Golds are wasted. At the tournament,
games mostly went: once the Curses ran out, Donate a second or third time, add a terminal
draw card and green, hoping it was enough. The champion kept Native Villages to hide green
from Rabble attacks. Horton argues there is a deck one level beyond: draw most of the deck
with Villages and Rabbles, Artificer to gain Pillage to the top of the deck, discard a
Province to a second Artificer and put it on the Native Village mat, play Pillage and
Ambassadors, then Library to draw the Spoils, and buy a Province. It stays thin while
greening and attacks with three different attacks every turn, so he doubts opposing decks
can buy Provinces under it. Wall punishes bloated decks, so a deck that keeps building can
shut opponents down. Nobody built this deck at the tournament.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Early game is Soothsayer for Golds and Curses, with Ambassador to keep junking after the Curses run out.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Donate is the way to clean up after the junking phase (the tournament players Donated two or three times).
- **R3** [core] (designer) The strongest deck keeps building past that point: Villages/Rabbles for draw, with repeated Rabble, Pillage and Ambassador attacks every turn, while staying thin.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Native Village mats green cards (bought Provinces, via an Artificer discard) to stay thin and to beat Rabble.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Artificer gains Pillage onto the deck; Library draws the Spoils.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Wall makes bloated decks lose points, which rewards the thin, attacking deck.
- **R7** [supporting] (designer) No +Buy, so don't overload on Gold.
- **R8** [supporting] (result) Native Villages to hide VP from opposing Rabbles helped the tournament champion.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Greening straight after the Curses run out with only a terminal draw card, when an opponent keeps attacking: Horton thinks those decks can't buy Provinces under sustained attacks.
- **T2** (designer) Buying many Golds from Soothsayer: there's no +Buy to use them.

## Disagreements
- The tournament players' plan (Donate, add terminal draw, green) won games; Horton's stronger
  deck was never tested against it in play.

## Grader notes
- 3-player game. Horton's deck is untested in tournament play; give R3 partial credit to an
  analysis that keeps attacking with Rabble/Ambassador without the full Artificer/Pillage line.
- Mine: Mining Village can be trashed on the final turn for Wall points, which Horton mentions
  as an option; credit as an extra.
