# Key: Making Luck ep. 16 (ep. 15 kingdom): City Quarter, Remake, Lighthouse, Rabble, Soothsayer

## Sources
- [Making Luck ep. 16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7wIa56lgSA&t=278s),
  2018-03-13, about 4:38-19:58. Adam Horton (a world champion) and Jake,
  after each played several games on the board.

## Expert summary
Build a deck that draws everything, keep a Lighthouse in play every turn to
block the attacks, and punish an opponent who lacks Lighthouse coverage with
Soothsayer and several Rabbles a turn. Remake is the key thinner and gainer:
open Remake/Lighthouse on 4/3, or Count/Lighthouse on 5/2. The first $5s
buy Rabble. Soothsayer comes once you can expect to draw the Gold it gains.
Invest only in deck control until you overdraw your deck, then use Remake to
turn spare cards into payload: Estates or Lighthouses into Silvers, Silvers
into Nomad Camps (rather than spending buys on them), Ports into Duchies
late. The target turn is two Provinces with Nomad Camp's +Buy. One Province
a turn is too little and three too slow, and taking one Province plus a
draw card sets up a double next turn. Games ended on Provinces, with Ports,
Lighthouses and sometimes Curses running low and Duchy dancing at the end.
Ill-Gotten Gains is weak: its Curses are easily trashed. Villages come from
Port or City Quarter, and the hosts disagree how much City Quarter is
needed.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Builds a draw-everything deck rather than money.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Keeps Lighthouse in play every turn as defense; attacks hard with Rabble (several per turn) and Soothsayer if the opponent lacks coverage.
- **R3** [core] (consensus) Remake is the key card for thinning and gaining; winning the Remake split matters. "You have to win the remake split." (a host)
- **R4** [core] (consensus) Opens Remake/Lighthouse on 4/3, Count/Lighthouse on 5/2.
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus) Buys Rabble with the first $5s; adds Soothsayer once its Gold can be drawn the same turn.
- **R6** [supporting] (consensus) Gets Nomad Camps by Remaking Lighthouses/Estates into Silvers and Silvers into Nomad Camps.
- **R7** [supporting] (consensus) Builds toward two Provinces a turn, not one or three; Province plus a draw card sets up the double.
- **R8** [supporting] (consensus) Port (two cards per buy) provides villages and Remake fodder; late, Remake turns Ports or Remakes into Duchies.
- **R9** [supporting] (result) Games always ended on Provinces, though Ports and Lighthouses got low; Curses ran out only when a player lacked Lighthouse coverage.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Ill-Gotten Gains: the Curses are easily trashed, and Remaking it into Gold is worse than buying Soothsayer.
- **T2** (single expert) Mid-game Count: good only as an opener; Remake does the thinning better.
- **T3** (consensus) Greening at one Province a turn too early, or overbuilding for triple-Province turns.

## Disagreements
- City Quarter: Jake thinks most winning decks here use it. Adam thinks it's
  often a pitfall: Rabble draws about as much, and City Quarter's Debt can
  stop you from threatening a double Province in the endgame. Jake says the
  no-City-Quarter deck needs to win the Port split decisively; Adam thinks
  about six Ports is enough.
- Prince: one host Princed a Remake every game (about five) and won them,
  saying whoever gets Remake on Prince first usually wins; the other bought
  Prince once and regretted it.
- Count after the opening: Jake thinks its Duchy gain or +$3 may have a
  place; Adam doesn't.

## Grader notes
- With Shelters (a possibility the hosts mention, since Count is Dark Ages),
  they'd Remake Shelters into Lighthouses, open City Quarter and lean on
  Necropolis. This case has no Shelters; don't credit Shelter-based lines.
- Prince isn't settled: credit an analysis that Princes Remake as a plausible
  line, but don't require it.
