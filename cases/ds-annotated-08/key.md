## Expert summary
The dominant interaction is Vault into Grand Market: Grand Market can't be bought
with Copper in play, but Vault discards Coppers for $ instead, so a Vault turn
reliably buys a Grand Market. That makes Vault a must-have $5, worth opening
Feast to gain it. For the $3, theory picks Wishing Well over Silver: later the
average card is worth more than $2, Wishing Well cycles to Vault faster, and money
mostly comes from Grand Markets and Vault. Both players saw Vault/Grand Market;
Blooki opened Feast/Silver. theory played Vault 7 times to Blooki's 4, built a
Grand Market lead, then ended on piles (Labs, then Great Halls) and won 30–21.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus of both players + result) Vault to buy Grand Markets: Vault discards Coppers for $ so they never enter play. "The dominant combo in this game is Vault->Grand Market." (theory)
- **R2** [core] (single expert + result) Gets Vault early (Feast/Wishing Well opening here) and builds toward a Grand Market/Lab/Throne Room action deck.
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert + result) Prefers Wishing Well (non-terminal cycling) to Silver; Silvers block drawing Vault.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert + result) With a Grand Market lead and non-terminal Actions, end the game on piles (Labs, Great Halls) rather than Provinces.
- **R5** [supporting] (result) Throne Room on Grand Market beats Throne Room on Lab.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert + result) Buying Silvers in this deck: they rarely add buying power and stop you reaching Vault.
