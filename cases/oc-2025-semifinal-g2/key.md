# Key: 2025 Online Championship semifinal, game 2: Hunting Grounds, Remodel, Jester, City

## Sources
- [Lemonspawn's Dominion Videos](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e2dIV5xzXTo), 27:00-53:00.
  Commentary by Lemonspawn and Stef (a former champion), both strong players,
  with chat comments from strong players. VRSmith went first and won on piles
  in a pile-out turn. Kingdom read from the video; the ending is read from the
  screen and spectator chat.

## Expert summary
The commentators opened with Moneylender (Crucible also possible) and
disliked Remodel as an opener. For the other buy, Dungeon on $3 beat using
Asceticism on Estates: chat noted that trashing one Estate guarantees playing
Moneylender on turn 3 or 4, but Dungeon almost guarantees it too and stays a
good card. Both players opened Dungeon alongside Moneylender. Seaway's +Buy was not
needed early; later, Seaway on Dungeon (or Moneylender) supplies +Buy.

From there they wanted Cities as the village, Jester as payload ("Jester seems
like really good payload especially when there's still cities on the board"),
and Hunting Grounds as draw once enough Cities support it. Terminal space is
tight. One commentator would not buy Gold, preferring to Remodel something
into Gold, and both wanted more Cities and Jesters.

VRSmith built Cities, Jester, Hunting Grounds and several Remodels, then
ended the game: Remodeling Hunting Grounds gained 3 Estates each time, which
emptied the Estates pile (and powered up City); with City and, by the
commentators' reckoning, Dungeon as the other piles, VRSmith three-piled
while ahead. jonts struggled: he Remodeled his Jester for a mid-turn City, then
bottom-decked his other Cities.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) Opens Moneylender, with Dungeon on $3; both players did. "Regardless of the four, this three is going to be a dungeon, right?" (commentator)
- **R2** [core] (consensus) City is the village and will empty; empty piles make City draw more and give +Buy and +$.
- **R3** [core] (result) Remodel + Hunting Grounds: trashing Hunting Grounds gains 3 Estates (or a Duchy), which both scores and empties the Estates pile for a three-pile ending. VRSmith won this way.
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Jester is the main payload and also pushes the City pile down.
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus) Hunting Grounds is the draw, added once there are enough Cities to play it with the other terminals.
- **R6** [supporting] (consensus) Seaway's +Buy comes later, placed on Dungeon or Moneylender, not in the opening.
- **R7** [supporting] (single expert) Crucible can trash the extra Moneylender or Remodel, or Remodel can turn Moneylender into Hunting Grounds, once Coppers are gone.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Opening Remodel.
- **T2** (single expert) Buying Gold; Remodel into Gold if you want it. "I would not have bought gold." (commentator)
- **T3** (consensus) Asceticism on Estates instead of buying a Dungeon on $3.

## Disagreements
- Whether to Crucible away the Remodel or keep it; one commentator wanted the terminal space for Jester, the other wanted to keep Remodel.

## Grader notes
- The pre-game read didn't predict the Remodel/Hunting Grounds/Estates ending; it emerged in play and chat. Give full credit on R3 only to an analysis that sees Hunting Grounds' on-trash Estates as both VP and a pile driver.
- My note (not the sources'): Feodum, Crystal Ball and Castles barely came up; credit reasoned arguments about Feodum or Castles as beyond-key.
