# Key: KotW 5/22/2022: Ill-Gotten Gains, Banquet, Keep, Trader

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/uvnrsy/), 2022-05-23.
  EphesosX proposed an Ill-Gotten Gains Duchy rush; SignError, petnarwhal
  and Interesting_Dare_640 extended it. Interesting_Dare_640 sketched an
  engine alternative but would play money. No games reported.

## Expert summary
The thread's plan is an Ill-Gotten Gains (IGG) Duchy rush: buy IGGs to
Curse the opponent, gain Coppers with IGG and Banquet, and turn Coppers or
Estates into Silvers with Trader. Keep rewards winning the Treasure splits:
Copper, Silver and IGG majorities are 15 VP, plus a Talisman or Crown
majority. Crown should be added once the IGGs are gone, then Gold and maybe
Platinum for Keep before Duchies. Trader also defends against the Curses (exchange a gained
Curse for Silver), which can stall the Curse pile; the rusher can answer by
buying a Curse, Catapulting, or gaining an IGG and Trading it into Silver to
rebalance the piles. Trader with Banquet was called "a particularly stupid
combo". An engine (Catapult, Worker's Village, City Quarter) was mentioned
but no one argued it was better.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Ill-Gotten Gains rush: Curse the opponent, then Duchies, speeding the piles. "the simple IGG Duchy rush, with Banquet speeding things up" (EphesosX)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Keep scoring: contest Treasure majorities (Copper, Silver, IGG, and others such as Crown or Talisman) worth 5 VP each.
- **R3** [supporting] (majority) Trader turns gained Coppers or junk into Silvers, and its Reaction swaps a gained Curse for a Silver.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Banquet gains two Coppers plus a $5 (IGG), feeding Trader and Keep.
- **R5** [supporting] (single player) Adds Crown (and Gold/Platinum) for extra Keep majorities after the IGGs run out. (SignError, petnarwhal)
- **R6** [supporting] (single player) Opens Silver/Silver or Silver/Trader. (Interesting_Dare_640)

## Traps
- None stated by the sources.

## Disagreements
- EphesosX called the IGG rush "probably not the best strategy", while
  Interesting_Dare_640 would "just play money" over an engine. Nobody tested
  a Catapult/City Quarter engine against it.
- How to end when the opponent defends with Trader: buy the Curse yourself,
  end on Talisman, or gain IGG and Trade it away.

## Grader notes
- Uses first-edition IGG and Talisman. SignError's Talisman musings concern
  hypothetical errata and aren't part of the plan.
- Thin and untested; an analysis arguing a Colony engine instead can earn
  credit on R2-R3 for spotting Keep and Trader, but misses R1.
