# Key: Horton 2024-10 Table 2: Merchant Camp, Tireless Scavenger, Baths

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "October 2024 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/october-2024-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, October 2024. A randomly generated kingdom, filtered
  by Horton for the Swiss rounds. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1UwplRNQfziRzY95Ey7zI9-hDjvWqNh99xISpgMjrhRo).

## Expert summary
A perfectly reasonable deck exists with draw, trashing and +Buy, almost
everything but an attack. But you can also build a kind of golden deck with
Merchant Camp and Tireless Scavengers: both return to the top of your deck, so
you replay them every turn. It's a bit more complicated than putting three
Scavengers in the deck, because they land on top at end of turn, so you need
more real cards in the deck before you can buy a Province every turn. Still,
you can consistently buy a Province every turn from as early as turn 9, which
puts a very aggressive clock on any other strategy. Only one player tried this
deck and couldn't make it consistent enough to win.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Identifies the golden-deck line: Merchant Camp (topdecks itself) plus Tireless Scavengers (return to deck top) give a fixed strong hand each turn.
- **R2** [core] (designer) That deck can buy a Province every turn from about turn 9, an aggressive clock for any other plan.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Tireless Scavengers go on top at end of turn, after the new hand is drawn, so the deck needs enough other cards for the setup to work.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) A conventional deck with draw, trashing and +Buy is also available.

## Disagreements
- Horton's claim is from his own testing; the one player who tried the golden
  deck in the tournament couldn't make it consistent and lost. No one argued
  the conventional deck is better.

## Grader notes
- The board is random, not designed around this line, so Horton's claim is a
  single expert's playtesting. Credit an analysis that finds the Merchant
  Camp/Scavenger lock-in idea, even if it prefers the conventional deck with
  reasons.
- Baths (VP when you end a turn without gaining) isn't discussed.
