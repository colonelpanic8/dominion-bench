# Key: KotW 30 Sep 2024: Saboteur, Bureaucrat, Masquerade, Lurker

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/1fsgmj4/), 2024-09-29.
  EphesosX and Rachelisapoopy discussed attack and pin plans; Chorby-Short,
  the kingdom's setter, dropped hints; kieranmillar (mod) answered a rules
  question. No games reported.

## Expert summary
No +Buy, and a lot of attacks. EphesosX's plan: thin with Masquerade, then
use Lurker, Mining Village, Watchtower and City Quarter for Actions, draw and
gains until you can play several Saboteurs a turn and stop the opponent from
functioning. Rachelisapoopy: Lurkers look good with Mining Village and no +Buy,
and even one Saboteur combos with Lurker. Both discussed a pin: Masquerade passes the opponent a green card,
then several Bureaucrats force Victory cards onto their deck, with Watchtower
trashing your own gained Silvers. EphesosX judged the pin slow to set up and
"win-more", and noted Lurker can regain Mining Villages trashed for +$2
mid-turn. The setter hinted it can be done with a single Victory card
(EphesosX guessed: Saboteur a passed Great Hall and Lurker it back) and that
Watchtower can guarantee City Quarters in your starting hand. Masquerade's
pass is not a gain, so Watchtower can't respond to it.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Builds an Action engine on Mining Village, City Quarter and Lurker, thinned by Masquerade.
- **R2** [core] (majority) Attack-heavy payload: multiple Saboteurs per turn to cripple the opponent, recovered or recycled with Lurker.
- **R3** [supporting] (majority) Masquerade + Bureaucrat pin (pass Victory cards, then Bureaucrat them onto the opponent's deck), recognized as possible but slow.
- **R4** [supporting] (single player) Watchtower trashes or topdecks your own gains (Bureaucrat's Silvers, City Quarters).
- **R5** [supporting] (single player) Lurker regains key Actions from the trash (Mining Village after its self-trash, Great Hall after Saboteur).

## Traps
- **T1** (single player) Expecting Watchtower to react to a card passed by Masquerade; passing is not gaining. (kieranmillar)

## Disagreements
- How practical the pin is: Rachelisapoopy thought it works; EphesosX said
  it needs winning the Lurker, City Quarter and Saboteur splits and risks a
  three-pile first; the setter insisted a lighter version exists.

## Grader notes
- Speculative thread built around a designed pin; credit analyses that spot
  the Masquerade/Bureaucrat lock or Saboteur + Lurker.
- Spy, Thief, Great Hall and Saboteur are first-edition cards.
