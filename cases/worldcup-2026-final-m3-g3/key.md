# Key: 2026 World Cup final, Minionpawn vs yurikamome, game 3: Collection, Berserker, Riverboat, Sailor

## Sources
- [JNails with drsteelhammer, neutral commentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hY-_wubT4nw), 1:01:30-1:21:30.
  Captions don't mark speakers, so quotes are "commentator". Chat (tracer, Fircoal) is quoted
  by the commentators.
- Match: Dominion World Cup 2026 final, match 3. Minionpawn (player 1) won.

## Expert summary
Riverboat's set-aside card is Livery, so each Riverboat plays a Livery next turn: +$3 and a
Horse for each card costing $4 or more you gain that turn. The only village-like card is Sailor,
which plays Durations (more Sailors, Riverboats) when you gain them, so the deck is
terminal-limited. Still, the commentators called it a (sad) Collection board: get thin with Sailors, draw a
little with Advisor, and play a Riverboat every turn. With Collection and Riverboat in play,
gaining a Berserker is worth about 4 VP: +1 VP for the Berserker, a Horse, Berserker's own gain
of a cheaper card for another +1 VP, and another Horse. Openings: Ferry on Sailor (making Sailors
cheap) with Sailor/Sailor or Sailor/Riverboat were all fine, Sailor/Silver too, but not Gladiator
(terminal). Both players used Ferry on Sailor and opened with Sailors. A commentator warned that the player
who builds too long (many Collections, many Advisors) can lose to one who takes a few Collections,
clicks Berserker every turn, keeps a points lead and piles out. Mid-game, Berserker gaining a
Sailor that is played at once lets you play a second Berserker; the commentators thought
Minionpawn should have used that trick more and bought several Riverboats on one turn. Minionpawn
won on Collection/Berserker points, with Advisor emptied and Berserkers piling.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert + result) Payload is Collection: gaining Actions (especially Berserkers, which gain a second card) with Collection in play scores VP every turn.
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Riverboat is effectively a Livery each turn here: gains costing $4+ come with Horses, which are free draw.
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert + result) Sailor is the only "village": it plays gained Durations (Sailor, Riverboat) and thins; Ferry on Sailor makes them cheap.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert + result) Don't overbuild: start scoring with Berserker gains and Collection early, and watch the pile-out (Berserker, Advisor, Sailor/Riverboat).
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Berserker gaining a Sailor (played on gain) frees an Action to play another Berserker.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Advisor gives some draw.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Opening Gladiator: it's terminal in a terminal-starved deck.
- **T2** (single expert) Building a slow Collection/Advisor engine while the opponent scores every turn with Berserkers and piles out first.
- **T3** (consensus) Fortune Hunter and Cutpurse are filler; take them only to trigger $4 gains when nothing else is left.
