# Key: Horton 2026-04 Third Place Kingdom: Invasion, Laboratory, Develop, Sacrifice, Alchemist

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "April 2026 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/april-2026-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, April 2026. Horton designed and playtested this
  kingdom for the third-place match. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1pc0bdGvuPpApfFOfVEA6zX3njAqS_RO_JlPebAMSK5k).

## Expert summary
The board is about Invasion ($10 Event: play an Attack from hand, gain a
Duchy, gain an Action onto your deck, gain a Loot and play it), which rarely
has so many of its parts matter. Loot is the only +Buy, which looks risky, but
in playtesting Horton never felt short of +Buy even when his first two Loots
lacked it: the Loots without +Buy are good here (they are all good Develop
food), and the first few $10+ turns usually buy Invasion anyway. Sacrifice and
Develop handle the Duchies Invasion gives, keeping deck control, and you
probably already own them by then. Alchemist is a second stack of
Laboratories that Invasion can gain without putting a Potion in your deck.
Vault/Tunnel is partly a distraction but also an efficient way to build
payload, which he sometimes used depending on his early Develops.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Invasion is the central payload: build toward $10+ turns and buy Invasion repeatedly.
- **R2** [supporting] (designer) Unusually, every part of Invasion is relevant here: the Attack it plays (the kingdom has Clerk and Blockade), the Duchy, the Action gained onto your deck and the Loot.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Loot is the only +Buy, but that is acceptable; early $10 turns buy Invasion anyway.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Sacrifice and Develop trash the Duchies from Invasion (Sacrifice for VP and keeping control), and Loots are good Develop targets.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Alchemist can be gained with Invasion without owning a Potion, giving a second Laboratory pile.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Laboratory is the main draw.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Treating Vault/Tunnel as the main plan; Horton put them in partly as a distraction, though they can be useful payload.
- **T2** (designer) Worrying that the lack of Kingdom +Buy makes the board a one-Province-per-turn money game.

## Disagreements
- None recorded.

## Grader notes
- Horton gives no explicit opening. Credit an opening that gets Develop or
  Sacrifice plus an attack or economy toward Laboratories.
- Grader's note (mine): Sacrifice on a Duchy gives +2 VP, so trashing Invasion
  Duchies keeps most of their value while cleaning the deck.
