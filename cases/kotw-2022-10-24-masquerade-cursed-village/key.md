# Key: KotW 10/23/2022: Masquerade, Ghost Ship, Vampire, Cursed Village

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/ybz1nk/), 2022-10-24.
  willowhelmiam and HRoll (regulars) gave plans; Educational_Mine_334 gave a
  Coven/Cursed Village outline; DJMoShekkels questioned an opening.

## Expert summary
No +Buy on the board; extra gains come from Vampire and Populate. Cursed
Village (+2 Actions, draw to 6) is the best draw, so the long-term deck is
Cursed Village with Scheme topdecking pieces each turn. Both willowhelmiam
and HRoll open Masquerade/Silver. willowhelmiam ranks $5 gains Ghost Ship
first ("Definitely take ghost at first opportunity"), then Vampire, Cursed
Village and Coven, and expects a sloggy game because Ghost Ship followed by
Masquerade is a brutal attack. HRoll takes Vampire as his first $5, expects
to shrug off Ghost Ship once Scheme + Cursed Village is running, and aims for
$10 (Populate or Province), maybe Populating once for more Cursed Villages
and Covens. Educational_Mine_334 wanted early Covens and Vampire/nothing on
5/2.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Opens Masquerade/Silver. (willowhelmiam, HRoll)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Cursed Village is the main draw, paired with Scheme to topdeck key cards. "setting up the Scheme + Cursed Village thing every turn" (HRoll)
- **R3** [core] (majority) Vampire early for gains, since there is no +Buy.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Ghost Ship as the first $5; Ghost Ship then Masquerade is a harsh attack combination.
- **R5** [supporting] (majority) Populate as an alternative to Province for multiple gains.
- **R6** [supporting] (single player) Coven to Curse the opponent early. (Educational_Mine_334)

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Building around Exorcist; too slow. (willowhelmiam)
- **T2** (single expert) Changeling, mostly bad. (willowhelmiam)

## Disagreements
- First $5: Ghost Ship (willowhelmiam) vs. Vampire (HRoll).
- How much Ghost Ship matters long-term: willowhelmiam expects a slog;
  HRoll says it becomes useless against Scheme + Cursed Village.

## Grader notes
- Ghost Ship is first edition.
- Way of the Ox (+2 Actions) was mentioned only to say Black Cat isn't dead;
  using it for Actions is a reasonable beyond-key point.
