# Key: Horton Winter 2019 Table 3: Exploration, Canal, Inventor, Engineer, Lackeys

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion: Winter 2019 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/repost-dominion-winter-2019-tournament-summary/),
  tournament 2019-01-12. Horton designed and playtested this kingdom for a
  2-player tournament (played in the Swiss rounds). [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1__ePuWrM5XpCsVuLMIA3dmGWHKNOoZV5auC1ObWGbbk).

## Expert summary
Horton designed it so opening Exploration is good, the board is puzzly, and
Villagers are saved and managed carefully. His best build after much tuning:
open Steward/Exploration. Get one Patron while trashing with Steward, then 3
Inventors and 3-4 Engineers, using gainers for these and often skipping buys
to stockpile Villagers and Coffers, so you can buy Canal (which still earns
the Exploration bonus) and ideally one Catacombs. Then spend one turn adding
as many Lackeys as possible to reach about 15 Villagers. On the next turn
spend them on Lackeys and Catacombs to draw everything (overdrawing lets you
play gained cards from earlier Inventors), play three Inventors so Provinces
cost $4, and trash Engineers to gain two Provinces each. He could empty the
Provinces by turn 12 consistently. The champion was the only one seen
building it (with variations) and took six Provinces in one turn.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer + result) The plan is a single megaturn that takes most Provinces, built by saving Villagers and Coffers.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Opens Steward/Exploration.
- **R3** [core] (designer) Inventors (about 3) cut Province cost so Engineers, trashed for their second gain, take two Provinces each.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Lackeys provide the Villagers; one turn of gaining many Lackeys stockpiles about 15 Villagers.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Canal (bought on a no-gain turn, keeping Exploration's bonus) and Catacombs support the megaturn.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) One Patron early, while Steward trashes.
- **R7** [supporting] (designer) Expected clock: empties Provinces around turn 12.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Courtier (even with Patron): nice but doesn't speed the megaturn.
- **T2** (designer) Fairgrounds: mostly a distraction.
- **T3** (designer) Haven: usually not worth a buy.
- **T4** (designer) Opening Remodel: Exploration is the better opener and skipping Remodel was part of the most efficient build.

## Disagreements
- None recorded.

## Grader notes
- Exploration gives a Villager and Coffers when you buy it and gain no cards
  in your Buy phase (current wording) or buy no cards (2019 wording); either
  way the plan uses Exploration and Canal on turns where nothing else is gained.
- Credit any megaturn plan built on Inventor cost reduction plus Engineer even
  if the counts differ.
