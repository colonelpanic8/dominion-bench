# Key: Making Luck ep. 117, kingdom 2: Steward, Wandering Minstrel, Tiara, Museum (Colony)

## Sources
- [Making Luck ep. 117 "Rapid-Fire Kingdom Analysis 2"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_6QemY1KJtc&t=855s),
  2022-09-03, 14:15-20:00. Adam Horton and Wandering Winder, first look, no playtesting.

## Expert summary
Six villages and essentially one useful terminal: Steward. Steward is both the
trashing and the only serviceable draw, so the plan is to get lots of Stewards
and build a deck that draws itself. Even without Colonies the hosts would
build. Open Steward/Steward. Wandering Minstrel is very good here. The first
$5 is one Relic (or Bandit Camp when you need more villages). Tiara supplies
the +Buy and gets much better with Platinum. Merchant Camp and Fortress are
almost never bought, and Crystal Ball is weak because Steward trashes better
and the deck wants Platinums, not Peddler-style cantrips. Museum is a minor
bonus: Colonies are so many points that variety scoring matters only in a
close game.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Builds a deck-drawing deck (engine), not money, even with Colonies.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Steward is the key card (the trashing and the draw); buys many Stewards. "Get all the Stewards." (a host)
- **R3** [core] (consensus) Opens Steward/Steward on 4/3.
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Wandering Minstrel is very good here and is picked up early.
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus) Tiara for +Buy, strong with Platinum; payload is Platinum/Colony money.
- **R6** [supporting] (consensus) One Relic (attack) as an early $5; Bandit Camp only when short of villages.
- **R7** [supporting] (single expert) Museum matters only in a close game; Colonies dwarf it.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Merchant Camp and Fortress as real deck components (at most a late buy for Museum points with spare $3-4).
- **T2** (consensus) A Way of the Mole draw-to-X plan with Merchant Camps: "Seems incredibly bad." (a host)
- **T3** (consensus) Crystal Ball as a main card: Steward trashes better and the deck wants Platinums.

## Disagreements
- Minor: one host might open Crystal Ball on 5/2; the other would still try to open Steward.
- One host leans on having one of each village type for Museum; the other dismisses Museum.
