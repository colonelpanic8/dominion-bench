# Key: 2025 Online Championship semifinal, game 1: Black Market, Capital City, Research

## Sources
- [Lemonspawn's Dominion Videos](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e2dIV5xzXTo), 0:00-27:00.
  Commentary by Lemonspawn and Stef (a former champion), both strong players;
  tracer's chat comments are quoted on stream. jonts went first; VRSmith won,
  11-1 when jonts resigned on turn 12, and took the match 4-1. Kingdom read from
  the video.

## Expert summary
Both commentators would open Research and Black Market, and both players did.
The kingdom "basically has everything", so the job is to pick the strongest
cards. Capital City was their favourite $5 ("Capital City is so strong");
Laboratory, Wheelwright or Pilgrim from the Black Market were also fine early.
The commentators agreed Credit is worth using in general but not here, since
nothing in this kingdom is worth the debt; Credit is for cards like Altar or
Trading Post.
They wanted a second source of economy besides Research.

The game was decided by Black Market offers and draws. VRSmith bought Bridge
and First Mate from the Black Market and built Capital City/First Mate turns
("First Mate is synergizing with Capital City and Black Market"); jonts picked up
Militia but had, in tracer's words, too many non-functional turns, and the
commentators questioned a Silver buy. VRSmith's Black Market also found
Margrave, Royal Galley and Siren.
Afterwards they said it was "hard to say where it went wrong", and that it
snowballed from VRSmith's better turns 3-4.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) Opens Research/Black Market. "I think I would probably open with the black market and research here." (commentator)
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Capital City is the best $5 to build around; get several.
- **R3** [supporting] (consensus) Credit is not worth it here: there is no single card worth taking on debt for early.
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Black Market is both a gainer of strong off-board cards and payload; buy useful offers (Bridge, First Mate, draw) rather than holding out.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) First Mate works with Capital City and Black Market, refilling the hand after playing several copies.
- **R6** [supporting] (consensus) Research needs a second economy source; Buried Treasure has synergy with Capital City.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Buying Silver in this deck; the commentators called it questionable.
- **T2** (consensus) Using Credit on an ordinary early card.

## Disagreements
- Laboratory vs Pilgrim/Wheelwright vs another Capital City as an early $5; the commentators were split and called all reasonable.

## Grader notes
- Black Market makes this game noisy: the Black Market deck contents aren't in the brief. Grade the opening, Capital City and the handling of Credit; don't penalize an analysis for not predicting Bridge or First Mate.
- Feodum, Mystic, Wild Hunt, Mapmaker and Road Network barely came up.
