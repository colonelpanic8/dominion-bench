# Key: KotW 1/17/2021: Magpie, Mastermind, Guildhall, Invest

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/kzqppx/), 2021-01-17, 10 comments.
  PathfindingLibrary (strong regular) endorsed pancake_stacker12's Mastermind/Cardinal
  take; treelorf and willowhelmiam argued Invest makes Magpie a trap and favored a
  Guildhall money deck; Madmanquail and PurpleAlbatross2931 proposed Magpie money.
  Nobody reported a game.

## Expert summary
No consensus. Two lines carry the most weight. First, Invest punishes the obvious
Magpie plan: Magpie gains copies of itself, and if the opponent has Invested in
Magpie, every gain draws them 2 cards, so treelorf called Magpie a trap and
willowhelmiam named it the best Invest target. Second, PathfindingLibrary found a
Mastermind/Cardinal attack plan more convincing than Magpie money: whoever gets
Mastermind-on-Cardinal going first gains a large edge, and Cardinal exiling Magpies
cripples a Magpie deck. willowhelmiam's alternative is moneyish with Guildhall:
Priest opening, Guildhall on the first $5, one Kiln, then Silver/Gold/Platinum and
Colonies.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert) Recognizes that Invest counters Magpie: each Magpie gain draws an Investing opponent 2 cards, so a Magpie-centered plan is risky. "Magpie is a trap!" (treelorf; willowhelmiam agrees Magpie is a good Invest target)
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Treats Cardinal as a real attack here, especially multiplied by Mastermind, and notes it exiles Magpies. "whoever gets that going first ending up with a huge advantage" (pancake_stacker12, endorsed by PathfindingLibrary)
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Opens with Priest (Priest/Raze or Priest/Silver) for trashing. Given by presumably_alterable and willowhelmiam.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Guildhall is a strong Project here and supports a money-leaning plan. (willowhelmiam; treelorf mostly agrees)
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Accounts for Colony/Platinum in the payload (willowhelmiam's money plan ends on Platinum and Colonies).

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Building around Magpie without addressing the opponent's Invest. "you buy one, your opponent invests the pile and now it's just tons of free draw for them" (treelorf)

## Disagreements
- Magpie money (Madmanquail: open Raze/Magpie, win the Magpie split, then money;
  "Big money strat" from blink-or-else) vs Mastermind/Cardinal (pancake_stacker12,
  PathfindingLibrary, presumably_alterable) vs Guildhall money (willowhelmiam, treelorf).
- Madmanquail called Mastermind too draw-dependent, with tripling Magpie its only payoff;
  PathfindingLibrary sided with the Mastermind/Cardinal view.

## Grader notes

  effect on Magpie and the Cardinal attack; don't penalize a choice between Mastermind
  and Guildhall money if it is argued from card text.
