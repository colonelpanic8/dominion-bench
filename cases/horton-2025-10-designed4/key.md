# Key: Horton 2025-10 Designed Kingdom 4: Horn of Plenty, Enlightenment, Storyteller, Magnate

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "October 2025 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/october-2025-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, October 2025. Horton designed and playtested this
  kingdom for the bracket. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1i-xyLMNrixItE8fQi91r3od4uZAdHhdTSi86wCznmnw).

## Expert summary
There seems to be no village, but once Enlightenment (Mountain Shrine's
Prophecy) is active, Treasures played in the Action phase become cantrips
(+1 Card +1 Action), and with Storyteller that gives plenty of Actions to use
a Magnate or two as draw. That enables a Horn of Plenty megaturn, which might
be possible without it but would take far longer. You don't really need to
trash Coppers, since they eventually become cantrips anyway, and a cute
interaction: if a Copper is in the trash, Mountain Shrine draws cards, because
Copper counts as an Action once Enlightenment is active.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) The payload is a Horn of Plenty megaturn.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Enlightenment makes Treasures into Actions that act as cantrips in the Action phase, so with Storyteller there are enough Actions to play Magnates for draw.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Copper thinning is not essential, since Coppers become cantrips under Enlightenment.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) With a Copper in the trash, Mountain Shrine's "+2 Cards if an Action is in the trash" turns on under Enlightenment.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Mountain Shrine is the Omen that triggers Enlightenment, so it is part of the build.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Concluding there's no village, so no draw deck is possible.
- **T2** (designer) Spending effort trashing all Coppers.
