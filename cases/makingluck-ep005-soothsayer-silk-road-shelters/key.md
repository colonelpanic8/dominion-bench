# Key: Making Luck ep. 5 review: Soothsayer, Mountebank, Ill-Gotten Gains, Cutpurse, Silk Road (Shelters)

## Sources
- [Making Luck ep. 5 "Pre-game Analysis"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S5T1Yzwtjcw&t=53s), 2016-04-10,
  0:53-20:06. Adam Horton and Wandering Winder review the kingdom from ep. 4
  after playing many games against each other (Wandering Winder won most),
  plus listener-suggested variants.

## Expert summary
A junking slog with no trashing. Both hosts open Silver/Cutpurse on 4/3. On
5/2, Mountebank on turn 1 or 2 is clearly best: the extra $2 buys another
$5 early. Otherwise Soothsayer is the cursor. Adam came in planning a
Mountebank before Soothsayer and was "shot down pretty hard": Gold is very
good in this long game, and the Copper from Mountebank isn't a real cost.
The typical deck takes two Soothsayers, then mostly Ill-Gotten Gains while
Curses remain (sometimes Gold instead, early on), Cutpurse support, and
Contraband as the +Buy. Contraband works with Silk Road: if Province is
denied, you take two Silk Roads or Silk Road plus Duchy. The game doesn't end
on Provinces, so the winner tends to take one to three Provinces plus many
Duchies and Silk Roads. Getting the cursor played a shuffle earlier (5/2,
first player, Cutpurse hits) is a big edge. City was awful in every test.
Quarry is bad next to Cutpurse. A pure Ill-Gotten Gains rush won only with
a lucky 8-2 Curse split.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) A cursing slog: Soothsayer is the main cursor; two Soothsayers rather than Mountebank first (except on 5/2).
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Opening: Silver/Cutpurse on 4/3; Mountebank on 5/2 ("I would go Mountebank. And I think it's not close.", a host).
- **R3** [core] (majority + result) Greens early on Duchies and Silk Roads rather than focusing on Provinces; expects the game to end on piles, with 1-3 Provinces each.
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Ill-Gotten Gains is the usual $5-7 buy after the Soothsayers while Curses remain; Gold sometimes, early.
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus) Contraband is good here (+Buy, Silk Road gives alternatives when Province is named); better than Cache.
- **R6** [supporting] (consensus) Tempo of the first cursor play matters a lot (5/2, turn order, Cutpurse delaying the opponent's $5).

## Traps
- **T1** (result) City: "pretty awful" in testing, even with Cutpurse support.
- **T2** (consensus) Quarry over Cutpurse.
- **T3** (result) Ill-Gotten Gains rush: won only through an unusual 8-2 Curse split.
- **T4** (single expert) Mountebank before Soothsayer on a 4/3.

## Disagreements
- Greening speed: Wandering Winder greened earlier and more on Duchies/Silk
  Roads and won more. Adam isn't fully convinced, noting the player with more
  Provinces usually won when there was a difference.
- Ill-Gotten Gains over Gold: one host says over 90% of the time, the other 70-80%.

## Grader notes
- Two-player only; the hosts note this kingdom is miserable with 3+ players.
- With Estates instead of Shelters, the hosts would green even more aggressively (Silk Roads worth more).
