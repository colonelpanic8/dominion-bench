# Key: Horton 2025-03 Semifinal Kingdom 2: Divine Wind, Witch, Mountain Shrine, Save

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "March 2025 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/march-2025-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, March 2025. Horton designed this kingdom, and the
  hidden Divine Wind replacement kingdom, for the semifinals.
  [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/12E1dAbWrWfyzVCSUhEPvG0ut0CSurOst9er15uUWjeY).

## Expert summary
Divine Wind replaces all ten Kingdom piles once Mountain Shrine's Sun tokens
are gone. The replacement kingdom was prepared in advance and hidden from the
players. (For the grader only: it was Worker's Village, Village, Mining
Village, Bazaar, Fishing Village, Fortress, Wandering Minstrel, Port, Mountain
Village, Walled Village, plus the Event Dominate.) The main kingdom has no
+Buy apart from Save's, and the hidden kingdom does. Horton says the fact that
it was ten villages is mostly irrelevant trolling: if you expect better
villages than Acting Troupe after the switch, you may prioritize the terminal
draw available in the main kingdom, which both semifinalists did to some
extent. He also thinks grabbing Fool's Golds on low-money turns from the main
kingdom has value. His intent was that players use the replacement villages'
extra effects to build turns that hit Dominate once deck control is achieved,
making the best of an awkward situation.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Plans around Divine Wind replacing the Kingdom mid-game: what you buy before the switch should still be useful after it.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Prioritizes terminal draw from the main kingdom (Witch, Hunting Grounds, Swashbuckler), expecting villages to arrive later.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Picks up Fool's Golds on low-money turns, since Treasures survive the switch and stack well.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) The main kingdom lacks +Buy (only Save gives it), which shapes payload before the switch.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer + result) Both semifinalists prioritized terminal draw to some extent.
