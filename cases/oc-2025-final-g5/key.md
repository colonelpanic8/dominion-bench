# Key: 2025 Online Championship final, game 5: Governor, Possession, Ninja, Banquet

## Sources
- [JNails and jonts, neutral commentary, part 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AWDONuwsN74), 17:00-38:00.
  One commentator laid out the plan confidently; the other said he didn't know how the board
  works. Cited as "commentator".
- [VRSmith's own commentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kWZyqrCn-ws), 1:35:30-2:01:00.
- Match: VRSmith (player 2) won 39-17 with six Provinces; Minionpawn resigned on turn 16.

## Expert summary
Governor plus Possession dominates: Governor's draw option gives every player cards, so you
play Governors, then Possession, and take the opponent's extra turn with their enlarged hand,
gaining what they would gain. The commentator's summary was one Possession and as many Governors
as you can get, and the Governor split (5-3 to VRSmith) decided the game. Gold is dangerous
because the possessing player can use it, so trash Golds quickly, e.g. Governor's remodel mode
turns Gold into Province. VRSmith opened Banquet for Souk (Souk's on-gain trashes the Estates,
and Souk is fine against Ninja's handsize attack), added Governors, a Potion and Possession,
remodeled Golds into Provinces, and used Ninja's attack on himself while possessing his opponent
to leave a junk hand for the return Possession. Minionpawn opened Ninja, trashed it and a
Governor with Procession, and fell behind on Governors.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert + result) Governor + Possession is the plan: play Governors for draw (which also fills the opponent's hand), then Possession. "Get one possession and as many governors as you can." (commentator)
- **R2** [core] (single expert + result) The Governor split decides the game; prioritize gaining Governors and don't trash them.
- **R3** [supporting] (consensus + result) Open Banquet for Souk rather than Silver: Souk trashes two Estates on gain, and its hand-size penalty is softened by Ninja's attack. "A silver over banker souk just seems completely wrong to me." (commentator)
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus + result) Keep Golds out of your deck, because a Possessing opponent can use them; Governor's remodel turns Gold into Province. "The golds kind of have to get trashed right away." (VRSmith)
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Procession removes Souks/Ninja and gains a card costing $1 more, but it is more useful to a possessing opponent than to you; VRSmith trashed his.
- **R6** [supporting] (result) Ninja's discard attack combines with Possession: discard yourself down to junk before the opponent can possess your hand.
- **R7** [supporting] (single expert) Rice is a useful dummy card: you can Governor-remodel it into a Province on your turn, while the opponent gains little from it.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Opening Silver instead of Banquet for Souk.
- **T2** (single expert) Trashing your own Governors with Procession to hit $6 for Possession sooner; a commentator thought keeping the Governor was better.
- **T3** (single expert) Crystal Ball as a village; VRSmith judged it not very good here.

## Disagreements
- One commentator thought Ninja stays useful as a card the opponent can't play on your turn;
  VRSmith thought Ninja was nearly worthless once Possession was in play.

## Grader notes

  VRSmith's own reasoning, and the result agrees.
