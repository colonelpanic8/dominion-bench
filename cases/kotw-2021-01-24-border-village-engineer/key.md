# Key: KotW 1/24/2021: Shepherd, Triumph, Tunnel, Engineer

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/l4hela/), 2021-01-24, 11 comments.
  nave_the_muser proposed the Shepherd plan and openings; treelorf (strong regular) gave
  the most concrete endgame; PathfindingLibrary (strong regular) added Inn/Faithful Hound
  and capped Tunnel at one; cyanraichu and PurpleAlbatross2931 agreed.

## Expert summary
Everyone agreed this is a Shepherd board: a deck that stays green feeds Shepherd's
draw (discard Victory cards for +2 Cards each), Pasture counts too, and Groundskeepers
score on every Victory gain. The finish is a big gaining turn followed by Triumphs
(+1 VP per card gained that turn). Tunnel is useful but limited to one: Golds clog a
deck that wants to stay green, and you don't take Gold on every discard. treelorf
would delay Tunnels, even until the go-off turn (Engineer gains Tunnels, Shepherd
discards them for Gold). He also wants to discard Shepherds with Inn or Horse Traders
so a shuffle full of green and Shepherds triggers each turn. Horse Traders supplies +Buy.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Main plan is a Shepherd deck that keeps Victory cards as draw fuel. "This is a super obvious Shepherd game right?" (nave_the_muser)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Endgame/payload: one turn gaining many cards, then buying multiple Triumphs, with Groundskeepers adding VP per Victory gain. "gain a million things in one turn and then buy a bunch of triumphs" (treelorf)
- **R3** [core] (majority) Only about one Tunnel; avoid flooding the deck with Gold. "Yes, one tunnel is enough" (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Inn and Faithful Hound fit the deck (PathfindingLibrary); Inn or Horse Traders can discard Shepherds to set up the next shuffle (treelorf).
- **R5** [supporting] (majority) Groundskeepers are a main VP source alongside the green.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Horse Traders provide +Buy; you have only as many extra Buys as Horse Traders (cyanraichu, nave_the_muser).
- **R7** [supporting] (single expert) Engineer is part of the plan: opening Shepherd/Engineer on 4/3 (nave_the_muser), and late Engineer gains of Tunnels for the go-off turn (treelorf).

## Traps
- **T1** (majority) Taking many Tunnels and Golds. "you don't want to be constantly triggering its effect or you'll get clogged up with golds" (cyanraichu)

## Disagreements
- nave_the_muser would open Tunnel/Shepherd on 3/4; treelorf advised against opening Tunnel.

## Grader notes
- Keep, Border Village, Remake and Necromancer weren't discussed beyond a rules note
  (Necromancer can play Engineer from the trash, but trashing it there does nothing).
  Credit sensible use of them as beyond-key extras.
- Horse Traders is a first-edition card.
