# Key: 2025 Online Championship final, game 3: Joust, Haunted Woods, Innkeeper, Baths

## Sources
- [JNails and jonts, neutral commentary, part 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MhlRn5rrlbU), 41:00-65:00.
  Captions are machine-garbled ("domain" is Demesne, "Carl from home" is Housecarl), so speakers are cited as "commentator".
- [VRSmith's own commentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kWZyqrCn-ws), 47:30-76:00.
- Match: Minionpawn (player 1) beat VRSmith 37-25 (5 Provinces, Demesne, vs 3 Provinces).

## Expert summary
A board with no trashing and almost no villages, so the commentators expected a fairly simple
Province game decided by Joust's Rewards: whoever lines up Joust with a Province first and most
often gets the strong Rewards. Everyone preferred opening Innkeeper/Silver to Envoy/Silver:
Innkeeper is non-terminal, cycles to line up Joust with Province, and suits Haunted Woods, while
Envoy is a terminal that lets the opponent discard your best card. The planned deck was a couple
of Haunted Woods, Jousts, Innkeepers, then Provinces; among Rewards the villages and +Buy
(Demesne, Courser, Coronet) mattered most. VRSmith followed that plan but had poor Joust/Province
alignment, took Renown as his first Reward, and went a long time without a second Haunted Woods.
Minionpawn opened Envoy/Silver, bought Gold to reach Joust, then took Demesne, Coronet and
Housecarl and won with a big final turn. The commentators thought Renown and the missing second
Haunted Woods cost VRSmith a game he had been leading.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) Joust and its Rewards are the centerpiece: get Joust, a Province, and line them up early.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Open Innkeeper/Silver (not Envoy/Silver): Innkeeper sifts to line up Joust with Province and works with Haunted Woods' extra cards. "I've really been impressed by these inkeepers." (VRSmith)
- **R3** [supporting] (consensus) Haunted Woods is the best terminal: duration draw plus an attack that bites on a board without trashing; two of them alternate turns.
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus + result) With no Kingdom villages, the village and +Buy Rewards (Demesne, Courser, Coronet) are the priority; Demesne was rated highly. "I think the domain is actually quite good." (commentator)
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) This is a Province race with limited +Buy; three Innkeepers is about the useful maximum, and Chariot Race is filler.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Witch's Hut's Curses are good but it is awkward to play (needs two Actions to discard), so it isn't a priority early.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Opening Envoy: a terminal with no villages that lets the opponent discard your best card. VRSmith felt "vindicated" about this, though Minionpawn won anyway.
- **T2** (single expert) Taking Renown as the first Reward; a commentator judged it the choice that cost VRSmith the game.

## Disagreements
- The Envoy opening and an early Gold buy were criticized by all three voices, yet Minionpawn
  won; later the commentators conceded Envoy plus Housecarl/Demesne did good work once villages arrived.
- A commentator listed Housecarl among the Rewards you don't need; Minionpawn's final turn used it.
