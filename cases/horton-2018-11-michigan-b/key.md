# Key: U-Con 2018 Set B: Colonies, Bonfire, Tragic Hero, Horn of Plenty, Mandarin

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion: Michigan Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-michigan-tournament-summary/),
  2018-11-12. Horton played this 3-player semifinal at U-Con and won cleanly
  with 6 Colonies. He didn't design the board.

## Expert summary
Horton spotted the Mandarin/Horn of Plenty trick (Mandarin's on-gain puts
your Treasures back on your deck), possibly boosted by Fortune, but rejected
it because Militia hard-counters it. Without Colonies he'd have been tempted
by a slower Jack/Horn of Plenty/Gardens deck, but judged Colonies too many
points for that. His build: Jack of All Trades and Bonfire to get thin, then
one big turn with a Ghost (Cemetery's Haunted Mirror) in play that turned a
few Silvers into bigger Treasures and added several Tragic Heroes. It was
fast (no opponent opened Jack), and he won with 6 Colonies.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert + result) Plays for Colonies: thin deck with Treasure payload, not a Gardens slog.
- **R2** [core] (single expert + result) Thins quickly with Jack of All Trades and Bonfire.
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert + result) Tragic Hero gains Treasures (and gives +Buy), set up for a big turn with a Ghost from Haunted Mirror in play.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Names the Mandarin + Horn of Plenty (and Fortune) loop and notes Militia counters it.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Jack of All Trades is a strong opener here.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Committing to the Mandarin/Horn of Plenty loop with Militia on the board.
- **T2** (single expert) A Jack/Horn/Gardens slog when Colonies are available.

## Disagreements
- None recorded.
