# Key: Horton Winter 2018 finals 3: Menagerie, Feodum, Raid, Shepherd

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, Winter 2018 Tournament Summary](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-winter-2018-tournament-summary/):
  the designer's write-up of a 2-player finals kingdom, with a tournament player executing the line.

## Expert summary
A Menagerie deck is easy to build (Secret Cave pairs well with it), but Capital is the only
+Buy, so scoring is the problem. Feodum is supported by Ironworks and Cobbler, but only Raid
gains Silver. Horton's answer: the Menagerie build-up is fast enough that you should do it,
spamming Cobblers, Ironworks and other unique cards. Then switch the deck's purpose to
Shepherd as the main draw. Overdraw enough to gain a Feodum or two in a turn, Sacrifice them
for Silvers, draw those, and Raid several times, so the deck suddenly holds about 18 Silvers.
After that, gain Feoda and Silver every turn with Cobbler, Ironworks and Raid, and the Silver
pile can empty in three or four turns for a huge score. He opens Sacrifice/Silver, does
better trashing only Coppers to Sacrifice and keeping the Estates, and usually spent
all three Wishes from Magic Lamp on Menageries. At the tournament, Adam Hopkins did this and
ended with 36 Silvers and Feoda worth 12 VP each.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer + result) Feodum + mass Silver is the payload: Raid (several plays per turn), Sacrifice on Feodum, and Cobbler/Ironworks gains, emptying Silvers.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Builds up with Menagerie and lots of unique cheap gains (Cobbler, Ironworks) first.
- **R3** [core] (designer) Transitions to Shepherd as the main draw once the deck holds green.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Notes Capital is the only +Buy, so normal Province scoring is weak.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Opens Sacrifice/Silver; trashes Coppers rather than Estates.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Secret Cave's Magic Lamp Wishes go mainly to Menageries.
- **R7** [supporting] (designer) Feodum is gained and Sacrificed for Silvers (trashing Feodum gives 3 Silvers).

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Building a Menagerie deck that tries to score with Provinces: with Capital as the only +Buy it can't score enough.
