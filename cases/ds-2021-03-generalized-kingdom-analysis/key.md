# Key: DS Generalized Kingdom Analysis: Tomb, Watchtower, Masterpiece, Pooka

## Sources
- [Generalized Kingdom Analysis and Some Ramblings About Teaching](https://dominionstrategy.com/2021/03/27/generalized-kingdom-analysis-and-some-ramblings-about-teaching/),
  2021-03-27: terracubist, a coach in the Dominion Discord's #coaching lessons with
  about a year of competitive play, walks through a procedural pre-game analysis
  of Kingdom 1. Only the worked example is used.

## Expert summary
Working backwards from scoring: the ways to score are Provinces, Tomb, Monument
and Curses (from Desperation and Cursed Gold). Monument is hard to use because
terminal space is tight; Tomb looks very good because Watchtower and Pooka trash.
Watchtower trashes the Curses that Desperation and Cursed Gold give, turning
their economy into free points, and it trashes the Silvers from Masterpiece
overpays, so overpaying converts $ into Tomb VP more efficiently than Provinces
(e.g. $8 for 6 VP, $9 for 7 VP) until the Silvers run out. Payload is Watchtower,
Pooka (trash Coppers for VP and draw) and Scepter to replay Pooka. Get Watchtower
as soon as possible, then Pooka; avoid taking a Curse without Watchtower in hand.
Curses will surely run out, and Silvers and Masterpiece might. No strong attacks
or combos disturb the plan.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert) Tomb is the main VP source: every trash is 1 VP, and the board has several trashers (Watchtower, Pooka).
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Watchtower trashing gained cards is the engine of points: Curses from Desperation/Cursed Gold and Silvers from Masterpiece overpay become Tomb VP while keeping the economy.
- **R3** [core] (single expert) Masterpiece overpay into Watchtower is more efficient than buying Provinces early.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Opens to get Watchtower as soon as possible, then Pooka; Scepter replays Pooka.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Monument is hard to use because terminal space is limited; other terminals compete with Pooka.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Curses will run out; Silvers and Masterpiece might.
- **R7** [supporting] (single expert) Only take a Curse (Desperation, Cursed Gold) with Watchtower in hand to trash it.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) A Province-focused plan that ignores Tomb and Watchtower.
- **T2** (single expert) Monument as a main payload here.
