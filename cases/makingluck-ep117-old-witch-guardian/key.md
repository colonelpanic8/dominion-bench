# Key: Making Luck ep. 117, kingdom 7: Old Witch, Guardian, Scepter, Philosopher's Stone, Gardens

## Sources
- [Making Luck ep. 117 "Rapid-Fire Kingdom Analysis 2"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_6QemY1KJtc&t=3290s),
  2022-09-03, 54:45-58:50. Adam Horton and Wandering Winder, first look, no playtesting.

## Expert summary
Philosopher's Stone with Gardens is tempting but "really, really doesn't"
work here. The only villages are Gamble and Scepter, and both are awkward for
playing several different terminals. Old Witch "may just be too good", so the
board becomes a junking slog: Guardian/Old Witch on a 4/3, then Scepter (to
replay Old Witch), Silver, Gold and Provinces, maybe a second Old Witch once
the deck is big. Without Old Witch the hosts would build Laboratory/Shepherd
with a Sleigh (Horses for Shepherd, Scepter replaying Sleigh, Guardian
coverage), but with Old Witch on the board that is overbuilding for one
Province per turn. Gardens is at best a cheap Duchy in the bloated endgame.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Old Witch is the key card; opens Guardian/Old Witch on 4/3 (or otherwise gets Old Witch ASAP).
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Plays a junked money/slog deck (Old Witch, Scepter, Silver/Gold, Provinces) rather than a Laboratory/Shepherd engine.
- **R3** [supporting] (consensus) Guardian is valued as defense against the opposing Old Witch.
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Scepter is the useful "village" here, mainly to replay Old Witch.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Gardens is only a cheap Duchy late, if decks end near 30 cards.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Philosopher's Stone/Gardens: "It's so bad here." (a host)
- **T2** (consensus) A Laboratory/Shepherd/Sleigh draw engine that always draws the deck: overbuilding against Old Witch.

## Disagreements
- None of substance.
