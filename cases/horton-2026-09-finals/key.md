# Key: Horton 2026-09 Finals Kingdom: Hostelry, Triumph, Footpad, Weaver, River Shrine

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "September 2026 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/september-2026-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, September 2026. Horton designed and playtested this
  kingdom for the final. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1E0IUYK9fU2KXZmANMprklRV0caaVj6CqO3ScusjCOvQ).

## Expert summary
Horton calls this board far more complex than usual. Two ideas compete. The
secondary one is Change, Alchemist, Stonemason and Vineyard: Change can turn an
Estate into an Alchemist for one Debt, and Stonemason on an Alchemist gains two
Vineyards, so the Potion cards are reachable without buying a Potion. He mostly
saw this as the fallback if the main deck is mirrored.

The main idea is Hostelry plus Triumph, which he says is strong almost by
itself and is supercharged here. Open Exploration, then River Shrine. Thin,
pick up gainers (Weaver, Change) and keep collecting Exploration tokens on
River Shrine turns, until you can add an Artificer or two (Witch's Hut also
works, slightly worse). Get a couple of Hostelries early for Horses and at
least two or three Weavers. Once River Shrine's Prophecy (Flourishing Trade)
has fired, cards cost $1 less and Action plays can be used as Buys. After
about six turns, take one enormous turn: discard Weavers to play them, gaining
Weavers and Hostelries, discarding Treasures to Hostelry for Horses, and with
Footpad drawing a card for each gain in the Action phase, draw the deck four
or five times while piling up the turn's gain count. Late in the turn, Weavers
gain Silvers you draw immediately, and unused Action plays act as Buys for
several Triumphs. In testing he bought five Triumphs on turn 7 worth about 55
VP tokens, uncontested. Hostelries and Estates can run out, so Vineyard is a
respectable alternative source of points. The tournament winner found "the
thing to do" here and won the final.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Main plan: one huge turn of mass gaining that ends with several Triumphs, each worth the number of cards gained that turn.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Hostelry gains Horses by discarding Treasures, and Footpad's "+1 Card when you gain a card in an Action phase" lets you draw what you gain, so the deck can be drawn repeatedly in one turn.
- **R3** [core] (designer) Opens Exploration, then River Shrine; collects Exploration tokens on River Shrine turns (no gain in the Buy phase).
- **R4** [core] (designer) Flourishing Trade (River Shrine's Prophecy) cuts costs by $1 and turns spare Action plays into Buys, which is what buys the Triumphs.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Weaver is central: discarded Weavers (by Artificer, Hostelry) play themselves to gain Weavers, Hostelries or Silvers; wants at least 2-3 Weavers.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Artificer (one or two) discards for gains; Witch's Hut works but is slightly worse.
- **R7** [supporting] (designer) Change can gain Alchemist from an Estate without buying a Potion, and Stonemason on Alchemist gains two Vineyards; this is the backup plan, mainly for a mirror.
- **R8** [supporting] (designer) Vineyard is a fallback source of points once Hostelries and Estates run low.
- **R9** [supporting] (designer) The megaturn deck comes together in about six turns.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Buying Potions to access Alchemist and Vineyard; Horton calls the Potions a distraction since Change and Stonemason reach them.
- **T2** (designer) Building the Change/Alchemist/Vineyard deck as the main plan when the Hostelry/Triumph deck is uncontested.
