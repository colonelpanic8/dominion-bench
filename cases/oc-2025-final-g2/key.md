# Key: 2025 Online Championship final, game 2: Remodel, Grotto, Peddler, Foray

## Sources
- [JNails and jonts, neutral commentary, part 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MhlRn5rrlbU), 16:00-41:00.
  Captions are machine-garbled ("4a" is Foray), so speakers are cited as "commentator".
- [VRSmith's own commentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kWZyqrCn-ws), 18:30-47:30.
- Match: VRSmith (player 1) won 30-6 with five Provinces; Minionpawn resigned on turn 12.

## Expert summary
No kingdom +Buy except Fortune, Foray's Loots and Gladiator, but plenty of cheap useful cards.
Everyone agreed the core is Grotto for draw and consistency, Farmhands as the village, Peddler
as payload, and Remodel. Peddler costs $8 printed, so late in the game Remodel turns Peddlers
(and Provinces) into Provinces, giving a small deck strong endgame control without many Buys.
Foray is bought to gain Loots when you can discard three different cards. Two Grottos setting
aside four cards each turn let the deck draw itself. VRSmith opened Remodel/Amulet, added
Enchantress, Grotto, Farmhands and Peddlers, took Loots with Foray, started on Provinces early
(Grotto can set them aside), and bought extra Remodels so he could end the game safely.
Minionpawn opened Monastery/Amulet on 5/2, which both commentators disliked: Monastery trashed
almost nothing, and they would have opened Grotto.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) Grotto is a key card: two Grottos setting aside four cards each turn make the deck draw itself; open Grotto (on 5/2 too). "I think I would simply choose grotto in 2-5." (commentator)
- **R2** [core] (consensus + result) Remodel is strong: Estates into Farmhands early, and late Peddlers (printed cost $8) and Provinces into Provinces for endgame reach.
- **R3** [core] (consensus + result) Peddler is the main payload, cheap with Actions in play; Farmhands provides the extra Actions.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert + result) Several Remodels let a two-Buy deck end the game quickly once ahead; VRSmith bought a second and third Remodel instead of risking a double-Province line.
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus) Foray gains Loots for +Buy and money once you can discard three different cards; Loots like Sextant and Figurehead were welcome.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Greening fairly early is fine because Grotto sets Provinces aside, so they hurt the deck little. "Grotto kind of encourages early greening." (VRSmith)
- **R7** [supporting] (single expert) Way of the Squirrel on surplus Grottos or terminals adds next-turn draw.
- **R8** [supporting] (consensus) The Sword Loot's discard attack is weaker here because Guardian blocks it.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus + result) Opening Monastery. "The opening with the monastery was simply very bad." (commentator)
- **T2** (single expert) Buying Enchantress for its attack; a commentator thought the attack does too little to matter, valuing it as delayed draw.

## Disagreements
- Opening on 4/3: the commentators leaned Grotto/Remodel; VRSmith opened Remodel/Amulet and added
  Grotto later. Amulet was considered fine by both.
- Commentators criticized a VRSmith turn that skipped setting aside cards with Grotto and a turn
  without Foray; VRSmith also called one of his own Silver gains "indefensible". Small errors, not plan.
