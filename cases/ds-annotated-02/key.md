# Key: DS Annotated Game #2: Apprentice, Tactician, Mine, Baron (Colonies)

## Sources
- [Annotated Game #2](https://dominionstrategy.com/2011/02/14/annotated-game-2/)
  ([preview](https://dominionstrategy.com/2011/02/11/annotated-game-2-preview-2/)),
  2011-02-14: theory (blog founder) annotates his loss to Mean Mr Mustard.

## Expert summary
theory found the board dull: Explorer is bad with Colonies, Woodcutter and Shanty
Town weak, and the Secret Chamber/Tactician combo tops out near $9. He opened
Baron/Silver and played money with Mine (Golds into Platinums), Tactician and
Embargo. Mean Mr Mustard opened Silver/Silver and built Apprentice money:
Apprentices plus Golds, trashing Estates, Coppers and Silvers, and later Golds and
even his own Colonies to draw his whole deck and buy another Colony. He took four
Colonies by turn 15 and won 39–23 in 18 turns. theory's conclusion: he
misjudged the pace. Apprentice works as a game-end accelerator, trashing Colonies
out of the Supply so the opponent can't catch up, and a slow Mine/Platinum deck
can't compete with it.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (result) Apprentice money (Silvers, Golds, several Apprentices) is the strongest line shown: it reached Colonies by turn 12 and took four by turn 15.
- **R2** [core] (single expert + result) Apprentice accelerates the ending: trashing Golds and Colonies from hand draws the deck and drains the Colony pile, so a fast lead can't be caught. "it just can't compete when game-end-accelerators like Apprentice are present" (theory)
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Expects a short game despite Colonies, so plans to win the race for the 8 Colonies rather than build slowly.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Explorer, Woodcutter and Shanty Town are weak here.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert + result) A slow Mine/Platinum money deck with Tactician that expects a long Colony game. theory: it "might have worked if there were 30 Colonies in the supply".
- **T2** (result) Embargoing the Platinum pile when the opponent isn't going for Platinum: it only blocked theory's own Platinums.

## Disagreements
- theory wondered whether a Secret Chamber/Tactician Province rush would have
  forced the Apprentice player to stop trashing Colonies. Untested.
