# Key: Making Luck ep. 37 review: Groundskeeper, Chariot Race, Inheritance, Duke

## Sources
- [Making Luck ep. 37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uLiQMGBkZ98&t=300s),
  2018-08-15, 5:00-16:57. Adam Horton and Jake review the kingdom shown at
  the end of ep. 36, after games with both candidate decks and mirrors. The
  episode's topic is boards with "no X", and this board has no draw.

## Expert summary
Two decks were candidates: Duchy/Duke and a thin VP-token deck with
Groundskeeper and Chariot Race. The token deck "definitely" comes out ahead.
With no draw, cantrips are the only way to function, and here the cantrips
are what score VP tokens. The build: a turn-1 $4 is Nomad Camp, and the
first $5 is Capital, which gives a high chance of $7 on turn 3 or 4 for an
early Inheritance of Chariot Race (Estates become Chariot Races, so no need
to trash them). Thin Coppers aggressively with Lookout (at least two),
since Chariot Race and Groundskeeper only work once Coppers are gone. Keep
the Capital until it buys an Expand (the second $7 after Inheritance).
Expand trashes the Capital, turns Nomad Camps and spare Lookouts into
Groundskeepers or more Expands, and turns Province into Province for
Groundskeeper points. Stocking Groundskeepers is delayed scoring: it costs
no reliability, and once greening starts it can overturn a points lead,
especially as Duchy/Duke can't end the game quickly. Ironworks is strong
after Inheritance, since it gains Estates that act as Actions. In mirrors
the Groundskeeper split decides the game, and University is the fastest
way to gain Groundskeepers.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Plays the thin VP-token deck (Groundskeeper + Chariot Race, Lookout thinning) over Duchy/Duke.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Inheritance on Chariot Race early, so the Estates become Chariot Races; Capital as the first $5 to reach $7 on turn 3-4.
- **R3** [core] (consensus) Thins Coppers aggressively (Lookout, at least two), because there's no draw and the token cantrips need a thin deck.
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Nomad Camp on a turn-1 $4.
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus) Expand as soon as possible (keeping Capital to afford it): trashes Capital, upgrades Nomad Camps and spare Lookouts, turns Province into Province with Groundskeepers in play.
- **R6** [supporting] (consensus) Groundskeepers bought early act as stored points without hurting reliability; Duchy/Duke has little control over when the game ends.
- **R7** [supporting] (consensus) The Groundskeeper split decides mirrors; University gains Groundskeepers fastest.
- **R8** [supporting] (single expert) In a mirror, get a couple of Lookouts before the first Potion.
- **R9** [supporting] (result) In the hosts' games the Groundskeeper/Chariot Race deck beat Duchy/Duke ("Not kind of, it definitely comes out ahead").

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Duchy/Duke as the main plan: competitive only without Groundskeeper or Chariot Race.
- **T2** (single expert) Early Capital plus Inheritance in a Duchy/Duke deck: one host tried it and Capital was a poor card for that deck afterwards; Silvers then Inheritance on Ironworks worked better.
- **T3** (single expert) Trashing the Capital with Lookout before it has bought the Expand.

## Disagreements
- University: one host buys a Potion mainly when contested on Groundskeepers;
  the other says it's "not close" to get University even against Duchy/Duke,
  as the fastest way to take all the Groundskeepers.
- Lookouts before the first Potion in a mirror is one host's view.

## Grader notes
- Charm was "okay"; Duke matters only as the rival plan.
- An analysis that recognizes Inheritance on Chariot Race (Estates that
  score tokens) and the no-draw, cantrip-and-thin deck shape deserves most
  of the credit even if it misses Expand's role.
