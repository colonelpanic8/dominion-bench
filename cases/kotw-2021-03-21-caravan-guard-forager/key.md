# Key: KotW 3/21/2021: Page, Witch, Horn of Plenty, Star Chart

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/mafwvz/), 2021-03-21, 8 comments.
  willowhelmiam gave the only full plan; PathfindingLibrary and treelorf (strong
  regulars) corrected its opening; sparant76 defended Hideout.

## Expert summary
willowhelmiam: race the Page line to Champion, which makes you immune to Witch and
gives +Action on every Action played, then build a Horn of Plenty megaturn. Gain Horn
of Plenty from Hero; add Ironmongers and Witches until the opponent has Champion, then
Rabbles until the deck draws itself, then Horns until you can gain the Provinces.
PathfindingLibrary changed the opening to Page/Hideout then Star Chart/Forager: Star
Chart is only good in the opening when the other buy is a card you'd rather see on
turn 3 than turn 4. treelorf agreed.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert) Pushes Page toward Champion early, as Witch defense and +Actions. "Champion as fast as possible here to defend against witch" (willowhelmiam)
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Payload is Horn of Plenty (gained with Hero), using many differently named cards in play to gain Provinces.
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Plays Witch (and Ironmonger) early, before the opponent's Champion makes Witch useless against them.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Rabble as draw once Champion supplies Actions.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Opens Page/Hideout, then Star Chart/Forager on turns 3-4. (PathfindingLibrary; treelorf agrees on the Star Chart rule)

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Opening Star Chart beside a card that doesn't cycle the deck (e.g. Page/Star Chart). "Star Chart is usually only good in the opening if your other buy is a card you'd rather see T3 than T4." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **T2** (single expert) Caravan Guard and Loan. (willowhelmiam: "too weak to be worth considering")

## Disagreements
- Hideout: willowhelmiam called it too weak; PathfindingLibrary opened it; sparant76
  argued it has tempo advantages over Forager.

## Grader notes
- The plan is one commenter's and wasn't tested. Credit other coherent answers to
  Witch (e.g. trashing Curses with Forager) as beyond-key.
- Loan is a first-edition card.
