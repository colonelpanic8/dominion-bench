## Expert summary
The focus is Triumph, which has many enablers here: Talisman with lots of
Buys, Canal, Bandit Camp (Spoils), even Watchtower, plus the ability to draw a
lot. There is counterplay: denying Estates is a real option if the opponent
commits too hard to Triumph. Winning usually means building a flexible deck
(able to gain and to trash) and watching the opponent closely. Horton likes
a Baron/Forager opening, using Fishing Village and Watchtower as draw for a
while, then adding Council Rooms.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Triumph is the main payload, fed by many gains per turn.
- **R2** [supporting] (designer) Enablers: Talisman (+Buys gaining copies), Canal, Bandit Camp, Watchtower.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Opens Baron/Forager.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Early draw from Fishing Village and Watchtower, then Council Rooms.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Watches the Estate pile: denying Estates counters an opponent overcommitted to Triumph.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Keeps options to both gain and trash, adapting to the opponent.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Committing so hard to Triumph that the opponent can deny Estates.
