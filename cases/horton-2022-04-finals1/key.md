# Key: Horton Spring 2022 Finals 1: Urchin, Fortress, Save, Barracks

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion: Spring 2022 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-spring-2022-tournament-summary/),
  tournament 2022-04-09. Horton designed and playtested this kingdom (first
  for Winter 2019, whose finals were snowed out) for the 2-player finals
  bracket. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Yf8ZZyyavqIiKeg_rTFl1x-KuDldt_1dtOoN7c4Pc0s).

## Expert summary
The kingdom tests what happens when Mercenary/Fortress is the only draw.
Making Urchin playable took work: Save is the only thing that stops most
games being decided by turn 4 on Urchin collision, and since Mercenary's
discard attack still hits a Save deck hard, Horton added reliability tools:
Horse Traders, Barracks, Cobbler and Scheme. Efficient payload comes from
Fool's Gold, Market and Fortune (Gladiator's pile). The Mercenary split
(how many Urchins become Mercenaries) is important, and Throne Room is there
to take pressure off it. Familiar is a trap card, good only as win-more
against an opponent who doesn't thin or forgets Save exists. Horton calls the
result a brutal slugfest that snowballs only for a player who misses the
right resource at the right time.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Urchin/Mercenary is central: the Mercenary split matters, and Mercenary/Fortress is the only real draw.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Save is the defense against Urchin collision, but alone isn't enough against Mercenary's attack.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Reliability tools: Horse Traders, Barracks, Cobbler, Scheme.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Payload: Fool's Gold, Market, Fortune.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Throne Room helps when short on Mercenaries (e.g. Throne a Mercenary/Urchin).
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Expects a long, grinding game (slugfest) rather than a fast engine.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Familiar: a trap, only win-more against a non-thinning opponent.
