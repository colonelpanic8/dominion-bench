# Key: 2026 Online Championship final, game 1: Vineyard, Litter, Border Guard, Jester

## Sources
- [Lemonspawn and Sharur, neutral commentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3oj4O-lLaH4), 0:00-17:00.
  The stream joined during turn 5, so there is no pre-game read, and one commentator kept dropping
  out. Quotes are attributed to "commentator". The players, agoo03 and yurikamome, are two of the
  strongest players in the world.
- Result, read from the end screen: yurikamome (player 1) won 35-24 on piles at turn 14. Vineyard,
  Border Guard and Market Square were empty. yurikamome scored 25 VP from 5 Vineyards, with 15
  Action cards (4 Border Guard, 5 Market Square, 3 Litter, 2 Jester, 1 Patrol), plus 1 Province
  and 4 Estates. agoo03 had 3 Vineyards, 1 Province and 3 Estates.

## Expert summary
Both players set aside their Estates with two Cages each, then filled their decks with cheap
Actions (Border Guard, Market Square, with Seaway bought for Market Square), Litter for draw, two Jesters each and
Potions for Vineyards. The game turned into a race for Vineyards that ended on three piles
(Vineyard, Border Guard, Market Square) by turn 14. The more Action-dense deck took the Vineyard
split and won. The commentators called Litter the key card, since it is the only village and also
draws, and Jester the payload. They expected a longer game with more draw and were surprised both
players greened as early as they did, but called yurikamome the winner once he started taking the
Vineyards. Whether Cage was worth it, and whether Border Guard plus Tunnel for Golds was the better
payload, stayed open.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (result) Vineyards are the main source of VP, so the deck should be dense in cheap Actions (Border Guard, Market Square, Litter) with Potions to buy Vineyards. The winner's 5 Vineyards were worth 5 VP each.
- **R2** [core] (result + commentator) The game ends on three piles: Vineyard plus the cheap Action piles drain fast with Market Square and Seaway +Buys. "Well, I guess the piles are going to get low fairly quick." (commentator)
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Litter is the key card, as the only village and also draw; take many. "Litters are really good. You still want like all the litters." (commentator)
- **R4** [supporting] (commentators + result) Jester is the payload attack; both players took two early.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) For extra draw while greening, Patrol is better than Council Room: it pulls Victory cards out of the way and doesn't help the opponent. "Probably patrol just better than council room." (commentator)
- **R6** [supporting] (result) Cage is used early to set aside the Estates; both players bought two and set aside all their Estates.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Council Room as the main draw card in a deck that is greening.

## Disagreements
- Cage: one commentator doubted it mattered with this much draw and wouldn't have bought a second;
  the other argued Litter wants a thinner deck rather than drawing Coppers.
- Payload: one commentator expected Border Guard plus Tunnel to dig for Golds; the other noted
  Patrol puts Tunnels into hand instead of letting them be discarded. Neither player bought Tunnel.
- Timing: the commentators wanted more draw (more Litters, Patrol) before greening. Both players
  greened early, and the early greener with more Actions won.
