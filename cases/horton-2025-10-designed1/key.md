## Expert summary
Dominate (gain a Province, +9 VP, for $14) gives a big incentive to build, but
building takes some hoop-jumping. Horton packed in synergies for each stage of
the build that compete with each other, so the right choice depends on your
draws; he wanted it to feel tactical. A key question is whether to build
without villages or use Taskmaster. You can play many Falconers, and even
Mapmakers, without Taskmasters: Swap a card and gain a Falconer to hand, then
play that Falconer at once by reacting to its own gain (Falconer is a
two-type card), and with lots of Swaps you can cycle in and out of temporary
cards like Flag Bearer, Mapmaker or Cemetery, Coven, and even Advisor,
depending on how thin you are. If you overbuild deck control, buying two King's
Caches and gaining three Silvers with Falconer gives a quick shot of payload.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Builds toward Dominate; the payload incentive justifies a real build.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Swap gaining Falconer to hand lets Falconer react to its own gain and be played immediately, so Falconers can chain without villages.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Considers both a village-less Swap/Falconer build and a Taskmaster build, choosing by draws.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Swap can return and re-gain temporary cards (Flag Bearer, Mapmaker, Cemetery, Coven, Advisor) as the deck's needs change.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) King's Cache (two copies) plus Falconer gaining Silvers is a quick payload boost once deck control is sufficient.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Nearby Swap gives +Buy on each Swap gain.
