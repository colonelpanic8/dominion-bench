# Key: Making Luck ep. 15 (ep. 14 kingdom): Highway, Secret Cave (Magic Lamp), Leprechaun, Necromancer, Monastery

## Sources
- [Making Luck ep. 15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YmsUlqovf7U&t=297s),
  2018-03-06, about 4:57-22:10. Adam Horton (a world champion) and Jake,
  after playing several games with each other; Adam also played many games
  against the client's bot and published videos of them.

## Expert summary
Before playing, Jake expected Leprechaun/Relic Big Money to win, since there
is no real draw and no +Buy. Adam wanted the "shenanigans" Wish deck, and
after play both agree it is quite a bit better than Big Money, though hard
to pilot. The idea: with two Highways in play, Province costs $6, so each
Wish gains a Province to hand. Magic Lamp (from Secret Cave) is easy enough
to trigger on this board, giving three Wishes, and Leprechaun can add
Wishes when you have exactly 7 cards in play. Necromancer is the flexible
support: Zombie Mason thins and gains, Zombie Apprentice gives a little draw.
The dream of Zombie Mason turning Peddlers into Provinces doesn't really
work, and Zombie Mason risks hitting your own Magic Lamp. Spend Wishes on
Provinces when Highways allow; a Wish on a Highway is right when it lets
Leprechaun gain a Wish or turns a weak turn into a Province buy. Leprechaun's
Golds get in the way until the Highways are there. Relic slows the Wish deck
but it still beats Big Money consistently. Open Monastery almost always,
with Necromancer on either 4/3 or 5/2 (getting thin early beats opening
Highway). A turn-1 Summon on Necromancer was a mistake.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Plans the Wish deck: two Highways in play make Province cost $6, so Wishes gain Provinces.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Magic Lamp (6+ singleton cards in play, three Wishes) is a central, achievable payoff here; Leprechaun's exactly-7-cards Wish adds to it.
- **R3** [core] (consensus) Rejects Leprechaun/Relic Big Money: the Wish deck beats it despite Relic.
- **R4** [core] (consensus) Opens Monastery (about 9 times in 10), with Necromancer on both 4/3 and 5/2; thinning early beats opening Highway.
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus) Necromancer is the flexible support card: Zombie Mason for thinning and gaining, Zombie Apprentice for some draw.
- **R6** [supporting] (consensus) Wishes go mostly to Provinces; wishing for a Highway is right when it enables a Leprechaun Wish or turns a weak turn into a Province.
- **R7** [supporting] (single expert) Leprechaun's Golds are a hindrance until enough Highways are in place to turn Wishes into Provinces.
- **R8** [supporting] (single expert) Secret Caves early help spike $6, and later serve as Zombie Apprentice fodder.
- **R9** [supporting] (single expert) Buying Border Village instead of Highway brings a free cheaper card (e.g. Highway), and the extra Actions feed Zombie Apprentice for draw.
- **R10** [supporting] (result) The Wish deck consistently beat Big Money and the bot once Adam had it working; Jake won a game in which he wished for Highways to keep the Highway split.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Leprechaun/Relic Big Money as the main plan (Jake's pre-game pick, which play disproved).
- **T2** (single expert) Building around Zombie Mason trashing Peddlers into Provinces: there's no draw to set it up reliably, and Zombie Mason can hit your Magic Lamp.
- **T3** (single expert) Summon on Necromancer turn 1: the Zombies are weak and it causes a bad shuffle.
- **T4** (single expert) Opening Highway instead of thinning.
- **T5** (single expert) Counting on Necromancer playing a trashed Highway for cost reduction: it isn't left in play, so it doesn't reduce costs.

## Disagreements
- Highway count: Jake treats Highways as a premium and sometimes wished for
  them to win the split; Adam says only two in play are needed and the split
  isn't worth prioritizing.
- Whether a contested Wish mirror is much harder to pilot (Jake) or not (Adam).
- Zombie Mason on Peddlers: Jake sees little downside as a tactic; Adam
  thinks if it's even possible you've overbuilt.

## Grader notes
- Golem and Mandarin weren't discussed beyond dismissing Golem as real
  draw, and Relic only as Big Money's attack. Credit an analysis that skips
  them.
- Don't require exact Wish counts; the core is "Highway + Wish = Province,
  Magic Lamp is live, beat money".
