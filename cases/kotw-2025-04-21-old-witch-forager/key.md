# Key: KotW 21 Apr 2025: Old Witch, Fortress, Forager, Squire

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/1k48ci5/), 2025-04-21.
  Three detailed analyses: EphesosX, willowhelmiam and presumably_alterable.
  bnoel12345 raised the Curse-pile ending. No games reported.

## Expert summary
A "pretty traditional village-smithy engine with fortress and old witch"
(willowhelmiam). Fortress is the village and Old Witch the draw and junker.
EphesosX's line: Forager trashes Squire to gain an Old Witch (Squire's
on-trash gains an Attack), buy more Squires and Fortresses for Actions and
more Foragers for money and +Buy, aiming for 2 Provinces a turn ($16), e.g.
4 Foragers, 3 Silvers, 3 Squires, a Fortress and 4 Old Witches. Squire and
Curses can run out fast, so the game may end on piles: green earlier so the
opponent can't end it while ahead, or threaten to end it yourself. willowhelmiam
opens Silver/Forager, takes Old Witch as the first $5 and Old Witches and
Fortresses until drawing the deck, with payload options of Merchants plus
Silvers, Squire Silvers doubled by Counterfeit, or Foragers trashing
Fortresses (Fortress returns to hand). presumably_alterable opens
Storeroom/Silver for an early $5 (Counterfeit, Old Witch).

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Builds an engine with Fortress for Actions and Old Witch for draw and Cursing.
- **R2** [core] (majority) Forager is central: thinning, +Buy and money, and trashing Squires into Old Witches or Fortresses (which return to hand). "Trash Squire with Forager to gain Old Witch" (EphesosX)
- **R3** [supporting] (majority) Payload aims at two or more Provinces per turn using Forager/Silver/Merchant/Counterfeit money.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) The game likely ends on piles (Curses, Squires); green earlier or keep the threat to end. (EphesosX)
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Opens Silver/Forager, with Old Witch as the first $5. (willowhelmiam)

## Traps
- **T1** (majority) Rats. "Rats is worthless." (willowhelmiam; EphesosX: "not that useful")
- **T2** (majority) Laboratory over Old Witch or Fortress. (willowhelmiam, EphesosX)
- **T3** (single expert) Storeroom, useful at most late in a Duchy dance. (willowhelmiam; EphesosX agrees it is unnecessary)

## Disagreements
- Opening: Silver/Forager (willowhelmiam), Forager early for faster thinning
  (EphesosX), Storeroom/Silver to hit $5 (presumably_alterable).
- Counterfeit: worth rushing for presumably_alterable; EphesosX would rather
  have another Forager.
- Buying Old Witch with $5 vs. getting it by trashing Squires.
