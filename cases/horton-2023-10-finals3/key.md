# Key: Horton 2023-10 Finals Kingdom 3: Monument, Tireless First Mate, Monkey

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "October 2023 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/october-2023-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, 2023-10-07. Horton designed this kingdom for the
  finals; his sheet notes it got less playtesting than the first two.
  [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/17d822tX5MEwbZLUAaWKnAbqEaaxWVIHy-pzWNZHiW9M).

## Expert summary
A deck here can play a lot of Monuments very quickly. Deck control is weak:
First Mate is the only village and also a major part of the draw. But the deck
can be very consistent while still building aggressively toward payload,
thanks to the synergy between Tireless First Mates (they return to the top of
your deck) and Monkey (draws when the opponent gains, and at the start of your
next turn), which ensures you start each hand with at least one First Mate and
potentially all of them. There are cute tricks with Treasure Map and
City-State, but Horton doubts they measure up to a deck playing many Monuments
every turn.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Payload is many Monuments per turn (VP tokens plus $).
- **R2** [core] (designer) Tireless First Mate is the village and much of the draw; First Mate plays all same-named Actions from hand (e.g. all Monuments) then draws to 6.
- **R3** [core] (designer) Monkey's start-of-turn draw picks up the Tireless First Mates from the top of the deck, so every hand starts with First Mates.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) The deck can stay consistent while building payload aggressively.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Treasure Map or City-State tricks as the main plan; Horton doubts they match the Monument deck.

## Disagreements
- None recorded.

## Grader notes
- Grader's note (mine): Tireless puts First Mate on top at end of turn, after
  the next hand is drawn, so it is drawn next turn by Monkey's start-of-turn
  card or by First Mate's own draw-to-6; credit analyses that get this timing
  right.
- Lower playtesting by the designer's own account.
