# Key: KotW 06 Jan 2025: Quartermaster, Siren, Sleigh, Sheepdog

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/1huj2ez/), 2025-01-05.
  EphesosX gave combos and a plan; bnoel12345 (who submitted the kingdom)
  listed intended synergies and reported games against the app's Hard AI;
  Curebob explained the Sleigh/Siren rule. No strong-player playtest.

## Expert summary
EphesosX's plan: a Sheepdog engine fed by Quartermasters, using Toil and
Trail for Actions; two Sirens on alternating turns (draw to 8, Curse the
opponent), with Sleigh moving each gained Siren to hand so it dodges its own
on-gain trashing; trash-for-benefit (he wrote "Replace", meaning Enlarge) for
Curses and to trash Trail (which plays when trashed) into a $6; payload from
Buried Treasure and Gold. He soured on Buried Treasure (playable only every
other turn; fewer than 5 copies) and avoided relying on Landing Party, since
Buried Treasure, which keeps re-topdecking it, will run out. Key tricks:
Quartermaster or Enlarge gains at the start of turn trigger Sheepdog; Cheap
Buried Treasure ($4) can be gained by Quartermaster and plays itself on gain;
Cheap Buried Treasure allows a 4/6 opening. The setter added: one
Quartermaster out means Berserker always plays on gain; Siren's draw to 8
beats Berserker's discard attack; Stonemason trashing Trail gains two $3s and
plays Trail; Sleigh can put a gained Sheepdog in hand so it reacts to its own
gain. Games tend to end on piles, since there are so many gainers.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Quartermaster as the engine's gainer, triggering Sheepdog at the start of the turn and gaining Cheap Buried Treasure (which plays on gain).
- **R2** [core] (majority) Sleigh puts a gained Siren into hand, so Siren's on-gain trash fails; Siren then Curses and draws to 8. "Sleigh can let you dodge Siren's trashing effect (but Quartermaster cannot)." (EphesosX)
- **R3** [supporting] (majority) Sheepdog draw triggered by gains during the turn (Quartermaster, Enlarge, Stonemason, Berserker).
- **R4** [supporting] (majority) Trail as a free play when trashed (Enlarge, Stonemason) or discarded; Toil for extra Actions.
- **R5** [supporting] (majority) Watch the three-pile ending; many gainers make piles likely. "making sure your opponent can't turn around a 3-pile" (EphesosX)
- **R6** [supporting] (single player) Siren's draw to 8 counters Berserker's handsize attack.

## Traps
- **T1** (single player) Building around Landing Party with Buried Treasure, which will run out. (EphesosX)
- **T2** (single player) Playing Berserker against a Siren deck: it lets the opponent cycle bad cards before drawing to 8. (EphesosX)

## Disagreements
- Buried Treasure's value: EphesosX first liked it, then thought it weaker
  than it looks. The setter's AI games used Quartermaster to gain one Buried
  Treasure per turn to keep topdecking Landing Parties.

## Grader notes
- EphesosX's mentions of "Replace" refer to a card not on this board; read
  them as Enlarge. The setter's later spoiler comments concern other
  kingdoms and are not part of this key.
- Tested only against the AI.
