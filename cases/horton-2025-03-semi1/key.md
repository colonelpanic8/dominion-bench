# Key: Horton 2025-03 Semifinal Kingdom 1: Way of the Mouse (Black Market), Cemetery, Devil's Workshop

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "March 2025 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/march-2025-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, March 2025. Horton designed and playtested this
  kingdom for the semifinals. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/12E1dAbWrWfyzVCSUhEPvG0ut0CSurOst9er15uUWjeY).

## Expert summary
The trick is that Way of the Mouse's card is Black Market, but the Supply has
no Action cards. You start with a Necropolis (Shelters), and Night cards in the
Supply give Imps (Devil's Workshop) and Will-o'-Wisps (Exorcist), so you can
play Way of the Mouse as Black Market from the start. Once you buy an Action
from the Black Market deck you have much more access to it. The Black Market
deck is mostly homogeneous: almost any three cards from it include a draw card
or two, mostly terminal draw, so you can plan around what you will get and
when. Horton would open with a $5 hand, using Necropolis as Black Market, and
try to get a big draw card to set up a Cemetery turn, then a Devil's Workshop
to enable it. After that he loads up on Coins of the Realm and Black Market
cards, balancing draw against payload (more Black Markets). Finding a trasher
in the Black Market deck changes things only slightly. In hindsight he would
cut Fairgrounds, because the game ran very long.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Way of the Mouse turns any Action (starting with Necropolis) into a Black Market play, which is the only access to Action cards.
- **R2** [core] (designer) The Black Market deck is reliable: it nearly always offers draw, mostly terminal, so plan to buy draw from it.
- **R3** [core] (designer) Opens a $5 hand, playing Necropolis as Black Market to buy a big draw card.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Sets up a Cemetery turn (trash up to 4 cards on gain), enabled by Devil's Workshop.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Then adds Coins of the Realm (villages) and Black Market cards, balancing draw against payload (more Black Market plays).
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Imps and Will-o'-Wisps from the Night cards are Actions that can also be played as Black Market.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Fairgrounds as a focus; Horton thinks it mostly makes the game drag.
