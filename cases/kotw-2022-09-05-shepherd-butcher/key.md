# Key: KotW 9/4/2022: Shepherd, Butcher, Peasant, Ranger

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/x6228u/), 2022-09-05.
  willowhelmiam (regular) gave a detailed Shepherd plan; a deleted user and
  MysticalLasagna (3-player game report) favored Rangers;
  Prestigious-Elk1137 suggested Herald; SignError commented on Herald.

## Expert summary
willowhelmiam's plan: open Butcher/Peasant (using the Baker Coffers if
needed); Butcher the Lucky Coin (Fool's Heirloom) into a Shepherd, using Save
if needed, and buy a second Peasant; then more Shepherds and maybe
Stonemasons. Once the deck is drawn, Save a Shepherd every turn, keep enough
green to discard to Shepherd, put Lost Arts on Butcher, upgrade Peasants to
Disciples, and Butcher Coppers and Stonemasons into Estates and Duchies. He
considered a Ranger line (Estates into Rangers, Lost Arts on Ranger) but
judged Shepherd better. A deleted user replied that Soldier attacks (from
Peasant) hit the Shepherd deck hard, so you want Rangers. MysticalLasagna
reported winning a 3-player game going heavy on Rangers (Lost Arts on Ranger,
Teacher's +1 Card token on Shepherd), ending with 3-Province turns.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Butcher is central payload: trashes junk into better cards, later Coppers/Estates into green. "Butcher is strong enough payload that I don't want extra Soldiers." (willowhelmiam)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Peasant for the Traveller line (Disciple/Teacher) and +Buy; opens Butcher/Peasant. (willowhelmiam, Prestigious-Elk1137)
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Shepherd draw with Save to keep a Shepherd each turn; keep some green to discard to it.
- **R4** [supporting] (majority + result) Ranger draw with Lost Arts on Ranger as the competing plan; won a 3-player game.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Butcher the Lucky Coin into a Shepherd early.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Fool. "Fool is just bad here" (willowhelmiam)
- **T2** (single expert) Herald: not enough Action density, and it can force a bad Stonemason play. (willowhelmiam, SignError)

## Disagreements
- Shepherd draw (willowhelmiam) vs. Ranger draw (deleted user,
  MysticalLasagna's 3-player win). Neither was tested head-to-head in
  2-player.
- Prestigious-Elk1137 wanted Heralds via Butcher and Stonemason;
  willowhelmiam and SignError disagreed.

## Grader notes
- Give R3 or R4 credit for either draw plan if it is argued from the card
  text; the thread doesn't settle which is better.
- Cobbler, Guide and Baker were rated as weaker options by willowhelmiam.
