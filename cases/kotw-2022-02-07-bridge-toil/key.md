# Key: KotW 2/6/2022: Bridge, Toil, Doctor, Animal Fair

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/smc54p/), 2022-02-07.
  PathfindingLibrary (strong regular) posted a full solo log taking all 8
  Provinces on turn 9 from a 5/2. willowhelmiam and SignError agreed on the
  Bridge megaturn; SignError proposed a Duplicate variant.

## Expert summary
A Toil engine building to a Bridge megaturn. Toil ($2, +1 Buy, play an Action
from hand) is the +Action source, so every Toil buy plays another card. The
log: turn 1 Doctor overpaid by $2 (trashing an Estate and a Copper), turn 2
Doctor names Copper, then Encampment. Encampment is trashed to buy Animal
Fair at no $ cost; Animal Fairs give +$4 to fund Toils. Sheepdog (and Way of
the Owl on Sheepdog or Merchant Guild) draws when cards are gained, Merchant
Guild adds Coffers, and Bridges pile up. On turn 9 the deck played 7+ Bridges
and bought all 8 Provinces. willowhelmiam's outline was the same: thin with
Doctor, draw with Encampment, build to a Bridge megaturn.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) Builds toward a Bridge megaturn that buys most or all Provinces in one turn. "Playing it out, 8 Provinces takes 9 turns from 5/2." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R2** [core] (single expert + result) Toil is the engine's +Action source: buy Toil repeatedly to play Actions from hand. "Not everyday do you see a Toil engine, and a fast one at that." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R3** [core] (majority) Thins early with Doctor, including Doctor's overpay.
- **R4** [supporting] (result) Animal Fair bought by trashing an Action (Encampment, later Doctor), giving +$4 to fund Toil and Bridges.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert + result) Sheepdog draws on gains, made stronger with Way of the Owl. "Sheepdog will be very strong in that deck." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R6** [supporting] (result) Merchant Guild for Coffers to bank toward the megaturn.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Relying on Duplicate on a Province instead of more Bridges. "Playing 7 Bridges seems easier than playing a bunch of Duplicates" (PathfindingLibrary)

## Disagreements
- SignError saw Duplicate + Bridge as more flexible and useful if the Bridge
  split goes 5/5; PathfindingLibrary preferred more Bridges and SignError
  agreed.
- SignError worried Encampment would be contested, leaving Actions scarce,
  with Animal Fair, Toil and Way of the Owl as backups.

## Grader notes
- Toil costs $2 and is not reduced by Bridge (PathfindingLibrary); credit
  analyses that get this right.
- Courtier, Miser and Royal Blacksmith were not discussed.
