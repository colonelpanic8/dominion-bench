# Key: KotW 10/16/2022: Library, Patron, Triumph, Catapult

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/y5w82j/), 2022-10-17.
  Four detailed but differing plans: Triumph44, Curebob, willowhelmiam and
  SapphosFriend. No games reported.

## Expert summary
All four agree Library is the only real draw (draw to 7), so the deck is a
Library engine with non-drawing Actions and disappearing money, and Patron is
payload (revealing it to Ironmonger or Wandering Minstrel gives Coffers;
Library only "looks", so it doesn't). Three of the four want Triumph (VP per
card gained this turn) as the main score, fueled by Squire's +2 Buys and
Silver gains, Rocks, or buying Coppers; Triumph44 warns that skipping Triumph
lets the opponent take several big ones. Curebob instead wants Charm for
double gains and early Provinces. Openings split: Urchin/Urchin, Urchin/
Catapult, Catapult/Catapult or Catapult/Squire; Mercenary (from Urchin) or
Catapult does the thinning. A 2/5 is weak (SapphosFriend).

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Library is the key draw card; the deck uses non-drawing Actions (Squire, Patron, Minstrel) around it. "The Library split is important here as it is the only real draw." (Triumph44)
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Patron is payload, with Coffers when revealed by Ironmonger or Wandering Minstrel (not Library).
- **R3** [core] (majority) Triumph is a major VP source, using Squire +Buys and cheap gains. "I want to ... use Squire's +Buy and Silver-gaining to get big Triumphs." (willowhelmiam)
- **R4** [supporting] (majority) Thinning from Catapult and/or Urchin's Mercenary.
- **R5** [supporting] (majority) Wandering Minstrel to reveal Patrons and provide Actions.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Opens Catapult/Urchin on 4/3 and avoids a second Urchin. (SapphosFriend)

## Traps
- **T1** (majority) Charm as a main +Buy source: it is a Treasure that weakens Library. (willowhelmiam; Triumph44 would take at most one)
- **T2** (single expert) Expecting Library to trigger Patron's Coffers. "Library is 'look at', Patron needs to be revealed" (Triumph44)

## Disagreements
- Triumph plan (Triumph44, willowhelmiam, SapphosFriend) vs. early
  Provinces with Charm double-gains (Curebob).
- Openings differ across all four commenters.

## Grader notes
- Arena (VP for discarding an Action at the start of your Buy phase) was
  mentioned only as a use for Catapult; Poacher and Hideout were rated as
  side options.
