# Key: Horton 2026-04 Semifinals Kingdom 2: Procession, Rats, Advance, Way of the Monkey

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "April 2026 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/april-2026-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, April 2026. Horton designed and playtested this
  kingdom for the semifinals. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1pc0bdGvuPpApfFOfVEA6zX3njAqS_RO_JlPebAMSK5k).

## Expert summary
Procession plus Rats is a solid core here: Advance gets rid of extra Rats
(trash an Action to gain one costing up to $6), and Way of the Monkey makes it
easy to control how many Rats you have. In playtesting Rope wasn't even
needed. Procession can change the whole composition of a deck in one turn, and
there are many tools for pivoting between kinds of deck control and payload:
Seer with a Silver flood from Amulet, draw-to-X with Library, and a Rats
pile-out. In the semifinal, one player drew their deck and had a big turn with
piles low but couldn't find a win; they lost the next turn, and post-game
discussion found a forced win had been available via a Rats pile-out.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Procession plus Rats is the core of the deck. (By card text, Procession plays Rats twice, gaining Rats and trashing cards, then trashes it to gain a $5 Action.)
- **R2** [core] (designer) Rats count is controlled with Advance (trash a Rats to gain a better Action) and Way of the Monkey (play Rats for +Buy +$1 instead of gaining another).
- **R3** [core] (designer + result) The Rats pile-out (emptying Rats as one of three piles) is a real win condition; it was a missed forced win in the semifinal.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Procession lets the deck pivot quickly between forms of deck control and payload.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Viable pivots include Seer with Silvers from Amulet, and draw-to-X with Library.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Rope isn't necessary.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Avoiding Rats out of fear of bloat; Advance and Way of the Monkey keep them manageable.
- **T2** (designer) Investing in Rope.
- **T3** (result) Going for a big turn without checking for a pile-out win when piles are low.
