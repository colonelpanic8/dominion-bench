# Key: 2025 Online Championship final, game 6: Hunting Party, Patron, Grotto, Silos

## Sources
- [JNails and jonts, neutral commentary, part 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AWDONuwsN74), 40:30-51:30.
  Cited as "commentator".
- [VRSmith's own commentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kWZyqrCn-ws), 2:05:00-2:17:30.
- Match: VRSmith (player 1) bought four Provinces in one turn and Minionpawn conceded; this won
  the championship.

## Expert summary
Both commentators and VRSmith went straight to Hunting Party + Patron: Hunting Party reveals your
hand and then reveals cards from your deck, and every Patron revealed in the Action phase gives a
Coffers, so a deck of a few Patrons and Hunting Parties makes lots of money and draws reliably.
Margrave is the main +Buy (Buried Treasure the other), Patron's Villagers cover the terminals, and
Grotto plus the Silos project make the deck consistent. Open Grotto/Patron, take Silos, a second
Grotto and Margrave as the first $5, then buy Hunting Parties and Patrons. The deck is
consistent enough to green early and take several Provinces per turn. VRSmith did exactly that
and quadrupled Provinces; Minionpawn opened Patron/Silver without Grotto and fell behind at once,
which the commentators called the losing decision.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) Hunting Party + Patron is the core: Hunting Party's reveals trigger Patron for Coffers, giving big money and reliable draw. "Patron hunting party. That is very strong." (VRSmith)
- **R2** [core] (consensus + result) Open Grotto/Patron; Grotto (with Silos) makes the deck consistent. "On boards you want grotto, you almost always open grotto." (commentator)
- **R3** [core] (consensus + result) Margrave is the +Buy (first $5), with Buried Treasure as extra +Buy; the deck aims for two to four Provinces a turn.
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus + result) Silos is worth buying early: it turns Coppers into draw and helps line up Patrons and Hunting Parties.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) The deck is consistent enough to start greening early; it builds money faster than Buys.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Patron's Villagers pay for terminals like Margrave; play Margrave before the last Patron so drawn cantrips can still be played.
- **R7** [supporting] (single expert) Keep the deck lean of off-plan cards: extra Silvers or more than two Buried Treasures make Hunting Party miss Patrons.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus + result) Opening Patron/Silver without Grotto.
- **T2** (single expert) Rushing Hideout trashing; VRSmith expected never to buy one and didn't.
