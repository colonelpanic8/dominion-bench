# Key: KotW 4/4/2021: Priest, Recruiter, Ill-Gotten Gains, Goatherd

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/mke8mx/), 2021-04-04, 10 comments.
  treelorf (strong regular) and Snip3 (bot game) favored a Priest/Recruiter trashing
  deck; halhag reported a 6-player game where Ill-Gotten Gains won.

## Expert summary
For 2-player, treelorf and Snip3 agree on a trashing-heavy deck around Priest
(+$2 per card trashed after it) and Recruiter (Villagers per trashed card's cost),
opened Priest/Recruiter. Desperation (gain a Curse for +$2 and +Buy) reaches the $5
for Recruiter, and with this much trashing a Curse is almost friendly fodder.
Snip3's bot game ended on turn 12 using Priest, Recruiter and Develop to turn Horses
(from Supplies) into Hostelry or Flag Bearer. He found gains the bottleneck and
suggests Spice Merchant for +Buy. treelorf flags Goatherd: it draws you cards per
card the opponent trashed last turn, so a heavy trasher feeds the other player's
Goatherds.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Builds a trashing deck with Priest as payload and Recruiter for Villagers. (treelorf, Snip3)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Opens Priest/Recruiter, using Desperation to afford the $5. "Priest/recruiter sounds great to me." (treelorf)
- **R3** [supporting] (majority) Desperation's Curse is acceptable or useful as trash fodder. "with priest around the curses are borderline friendly." (treelorf)
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Gains are the bottleneck; Develop and Supplies' Horses turn into Hostelry or Flag Bearer. (Snip3, result vs a bot: ended turn 12)
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Goatherd punishes heavy trashing by the opponent, so plans must react to each other. (treelorf)
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Spice Merchant for +Buy, or Spice Merchant/Recruiter as an alternative opening. (Snip3, treelorf)

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Ill-Gotten Gains with Duchies in 2-player. "IGG looks really really bad to me here." (treelorf)

## Disagreements
- halhag's 6-player game was won by two players going Ill-Gotten Gains then Duchies
  (four piles needed, game over in 13 turns). treelorf agrees 6-player is different.

## Grader notes
- No 2-player human game was reported.
- Ill-Gotten Gains is a first-edition card.
