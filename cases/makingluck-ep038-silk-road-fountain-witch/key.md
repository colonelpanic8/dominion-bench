## Expert summary
This board is a slog. Baker's starting Coffers plus Cursed Gold let every
player open with $5, and that $5 is almost always Witch. With $7, a
Stonemason overpaid for Witch and Baker is good. Otherwise Witch/Temple is
decent despite collisions. The tempting draw deck (Shanty Town and Pooka,
Stonemason for extra gains) is bad: Pooka needs a Shanty Town and a
Treasure to trash in the same hand, the draw isn't sustainable, and
there's little worth drawing into once Curses arrive. Copper is one of the
better cards in the deck, and you keep Coppers because Fountain's 15 VP
is decisive in games ending around 30-35 points. Point ceilings are low,
so small points matter a lot: buy an Action you don't really want to take
Colonnade VP (which also denies them), or a Temple for its pile VP. Silk
Road is the big scorer, because Provinces are hard to empty and the game
runs long. Stonemason gains two Victory cards (Silk Roads, Tunnels,
Estates) to feed it, the Silk Road split is decisive, and the game usually
ends on Curses, Silk Roads and a third pile, not Provinces.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Plays a slog, not a draw deck; rejects building around Shanty Town + Pooka draw.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Opens Witch, using Baker's Coffers and Cursed Gold to reach $5.
- **R3** [core] (consensus) Keeps Coppers (adds them if needed) to secure Fountain's 15 VP, which is close to decisive.
- **R4** [core] (consensus) Silk Road is the main alt-VP; the Silk Road split is decisive and the game tends to end on piles, not Provinces.
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus) Small points matter on a low-ceiling board: Colonnade VP (taking them also denies the opponent) and Temple pile VP justify otherwise unwanted buys.
- **R6** [supporting] (consensus) Stonemason gains pairs of Victory cards (Silk Road, Tunnel, Estate) for Silk Road, and its overpay can open Witch + Baker on $7.
- **R7** [supporting] (consensus) The second opening card on a $4 is Temple (Witch/Temple), accepting the collision risk.
- **R8** [supporting] (result) In the hosts' games Provinces never ran out (except against a bot), final scores were around 30-35, Colonnade VP ran out about every game, and taking six Silk Roads felt decisive.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) A thin, drawing deck with Shanty Town/Pooka: Adam calls Shanty Town "a bad card", and Adam said on the forum that he never came close to winning against a bot that way.
- **T2** (consensus) Trashing Coppers and giving up Fountain.
- **T3** (single expert) Playing Cursed Gold automatically every time.

## Disagreements
- Cursed Gold: most forum voices (including WanderingWinder) said play it
  essentially always while Witches are flying; one host found that often
  harmful and plays it only after thinking it through.
- Temple: the hosts found Witch/Temple decent; WanderingWinder's
  pre-review gut was that Temple isn't very good here (he hadn't played).
- Opening on $4/$5 without $7: WanderingWinder preferred Witch/Silver
  (maybe Witch/Baker) and Mandarin later; the hosts used Temple.
