# Key: Making Luck ep. 100, kingdom 4: Donate, Treasure Map, Grand Market, Witch, Castles

## Sources
- [Making Luck ep. 100 "Rapid-Fire Kingdom Analysis"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RQk58SzbZd0&t=2066s),
  2020-02-28, 34:26-41:10. Adam Horton and Wandering Winder, first look, no playtesting.

## Expert summary
Donate is extremely powerful and organizes the game. There's no village but
plenty of non-terminals (Grand Market, Seer, Cartographer). The line the
hosts work out: on 4/3, open Treasure Map/Improve, Donate on turn 3 (or 5),
keeping the Treasure Maps so they collide immediately for four Golds. Then
pay off the debt and buy Grand Markets. A 5/2 is worse here because it can't
set this up. Witch shouldn't be ignored just because Donate exists. Donate
is the only way to remove Curses, inefficient until there are many, and
Curses cost points. So a Witch comes after a couple of Grand Markets, as an
economic attack that forces the opponent to spend on Donate. Seer looks
overrated (few $2-4 cards to draw), and Cartographer may beat it. Farmers'
Market is out for lack of terminal space. Castles vs Provinces needn't be
decided early, but having both means building big enough not to lose the
Castles race after taking Provinces.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Donate is the central card; plans an early Donate turn.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) The concrete line: Treasure Map/Improve opening (4/3), Donate around turn 3, Treasure Maps colliding into Golds, then Grand Markets. "Treasure Map, Improve, Donate turn three." (a host)
- **R3** [supporting] (consensus) Grand Market as the main purchase after the Golds.
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Still buys a Witch (after some Grand Markets); Curses matter because only Donate removes them.
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus) Builds big enough to handle both Castles and Provinces as point sources; doesn't decide which early.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) 5/2 is a bad opening on this board for the Donate line.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Farmers' Market: no terminal space for it.
- **T2** (single expert) Seer as the workhorse draw: overrated here.
- **T3** (consensus) Ignoring Witch because Donate can trash Curses.

## Disagreements
- Seer vs Cartographer: one host first preferred Seer, then both leaned to Cartographer or neither.
