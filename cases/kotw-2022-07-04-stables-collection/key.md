# Key: KotW 7/3/2022: Stables, Collection, Charlatan, Forager

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/vquj7b/), 2022-07-04.
  PathfindingLibrary (strong regular) gave a one-line plan; SignError gave
  the only full plan; several others suggested Forager/Tiara/Fool's Gold or
  Forager/Market Square decks without detail.

## Expert summary
PathfindingLibrary: "Use Stables draw to play many Collections." SignError
filled this in: take Charlatan early (it junks faster than Forager can
trash; on 5/2 open Charlatan and nothing). Open Forager/Silver or
Silver/Silver to hit the important $5s. After Charlatan, $5s are Stables
(preferred) and Collection. Scheme topdecks Stables (more Schemes can topdeck
themselves), but don't overdo Actions, since Stables needs Treasures to
discard. Collection gains Actions for VP (Scheme, or Market Square/Worker's
Village with spare $). Avoid Squire until the endgame (stop card), Fool's
Gold and Market Square's Gold gains (self-junking); Tiara can help once there
are a few Collections.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Stables is the draw that powers the deck (discard Treasures to draw). "Use Stables draw to play many Collections." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Collection is the main VP source, scoring each Action gained while in play.
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Charlatan early; it junks faster than Forager trashes. On 5/2, Charlatan/nothing. "Charlatan junks faster than Forager can trash." (SignError)
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Scheme topdecks Stables so it is drawn reliably.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Collection's gains are cheap Actions (Scheme, Market Square, Worker's Village) for VP.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Keeps enough Treasure in deck for Stables to discard.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Fool's Gold and Market Square's Gold gains junk the deck here. (SignError)
- **T2** (single expert) Squire before the endgame (a stop card). (SignError)
- **T3** (single expert) Buying a Curse to trash for Forager. "Buying a curse here sounds awful." (GonffzCheeze)
