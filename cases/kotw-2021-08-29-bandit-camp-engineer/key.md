# Key: KotW 8/29/2021: Rats, Engineer, Horn of Plenty, Way of the Horse

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/pe6q13/), 2021-08-29, 8 comments.
  PathfindingLibrary (strong regular) gave the opening and goal; Qaanol gave the
  fullest plan; DoggyDoggy_What_Now reported bot games; others added short takes.

## Expert summary
Rats are the trasher, and Way of the Horse fixes their usual problem: once thinning
is done, play Rats as a Horse (+2 Cards +1 Action) and return them to the pile.
PathfindingLibrary calls it a very fast kingdom, opens Rats/Engineer if possible, and
names a Horn of Plenty engine as the goal. Qaanol opens Rats + Engineer on 4/3 and
5/2 (Pixie + Rats on 2/5 and 3/4), uses Engineer for Pixies and Stockpiles, then
villages and Islands, and finishes with Horn of Plenty into Tragic Hero. PitchforkJoe
adds that Pixie as a Horse can fill a hand so Tragic Hero becomes Platinum (Colonies
are in). DoggyDoggy_What_Now doubted a Horn engine in so thin a deck and ended games
on turns 12-13 against a bot with Bandit Camps, Stockpile and Spoils.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Rats as the trasher, returned to the pile with Way of the Horse once thinning is done. "Rats are phenomenal here. They trash early, then become horses." (Qaanol)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Opens Rats/Engineer when the split allows. (PathfindingLibrary, Qaanol)
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Goal is a Horn of Plenty engine with many different cards in play. "End goal is Horn engine." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R4** [supporting] (majority) Engineer gains cheap pieces (Pixies, Stockpiles, villages). (Qaanol, PitchforkJoe)
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Tragic Hero in a big hand trashes itself for Platinum. (PitchforkJoe, Qaanol)
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Wolf Den (-3 VP per card you have exactly one of) makes singletons such as Tragic Hero, Rats, Spoils, Pixie and Engineer risky. (WildcatKid)
- **R7** [supporting] (majority) Expects a fast game. (PathfindingLibrary; DoggyDoggy_What_Now: ends on turns 12-13 vs a bot)
