# Key: KotW 06 Feb 2023: Goatherd, Wizards, Tomb, Cultist

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/10v1oit/), 2023-02-06.
  Curebob gave the longest analysis; zoppytops, Interesting_Dare_640 and
  ahicks58 gave openings; NovaRogue suggested Cultist money. No games.

## Expert summary
A trashing-heavy engine scored partly by Tomb (+1 VP per card trashed). The
common opening is Student/Goatherd (or two Goatherds), with up to 3-4
Goatherds and a couple of Students later; Student trashing a Treasure gives a
Favor and topdecks itself, and trashing Jewelled Egg also gains a Loot.
Shaman puts trashed cards back into circulation, so you want more trashers
anyway. Animal Fair can be bought by trashing an Action (even a Ruins), and
City-state lets you spend Favors to play gained Actions immediately (e.g.
Conjurer, Animal Fair). Conjurer is "the real prize" (Interesting_Dare_640).
Curebob also highlighted Lich + Voyage: skip the Voyage turn for Lich, so
each pair works like four Laboratories. He rated Cultist weak here because of
the trashing, Tomb and limited terminal space.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Heavy trashing with Goatherd and Student, scoring with Tomb. "Just trash trash trash" (Interesting_Dare_640)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Opens Student/Goatherd (or Goatherd/Goatherd) on 4/3.
- **R3** [supporting] (majority) Wizards pile: Conjurer is a key gainer, with City-state Favors to play gained Actions immediately.
- **R4** [supporting] (single player) Lich + Voyage: use the Voyage extra turn as the turn Lich skips.
- **R5** [supporting] (majority) Jewelled Egg trashed for Loot; Animal Fair bought by trashing Actions (including Ruins).
- **R6** [supporting] (single player) Shaman returns cards from the trash, so keep trashing to stay clean. (Curebob)

## Traps
- **T1** (single player) Leaning on Cultist: trashing and Tomb blunt the Ruins. "I feel like this is one of the weakest boards for it" (Curebob)

## Disagreements
- NovaRogue suggested Big Money with Cultists for draw and Loot payload,
  opening Cultist/Shaman on 5/2; others favored a trashing engine.
- ahicks58 suggested Rush + Shaman on 4/3 to trash an Estate that the
  opponent must regain with Shaman before their shuffle.
