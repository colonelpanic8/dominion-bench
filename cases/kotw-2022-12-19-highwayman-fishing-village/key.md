# Key: KotW 12/18/2022: Highwayman, Fishing Village, Lookout, Desert Guides

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/zpgzgg/), 2022-12-19.
  willowhelmiam (regular) gave the plan; GonffzCheeze (regular) disputed
  parts of it; Charlie_Yu suggested Bishop/Silver.

## Expert summary
A Colony engine: Lookout thins, Fishing Village is the village, Highwayman
the draw, Ducat or Grand Market the +Buy; build to double Colony.
willowhelmiam opens Lookout/Courier (or Courier/Silver, or Double Ducat to hit
$6 deterministically) and then takes Fishing Villages, Innovation and
Highwaymen, adding Ducat or Grand Market the shuffle before drawing the deck
and Platinums afterwards. GonffzCheeze agreed on the core but insisted on
Marquis (plays well with Fishing Village, Grand Market, Lookout, Courier),
Grand Market, a Bishop for supplementary scoring, and Desert Guides for
consistency; willowhelmiam conceded one or two Marquis over extra Highwaymen.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Builds a draw-the-deck Colony engine. "I wanna draw my deck here and build to double colony or more." (willowhelmiam)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Fishing Village for Actions, Highwayman as draw/attack, Lookout for thinning.
- **R3** [supporting] (majority) Includes Grand Market; Desert Guides helps. "You are absolutely fitting a GM into this deck." (GonffzCheeze)
- **R4** [supporting] (majority) One or two Marquis. (GonffzCheeze; willowhelmiam partly agreed)
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Opens Lookout/Courier; Innovation early.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Platinums once the deck draws itself.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Opening Bishop/Silver to hit $6. "Bishop doesn't help you hit $6" (willowhelmiam)
- **T2** (single expert) Acting Troupe; outclassed by Fishing Village. (willowhelmiam)

## Disagreements
- Bishop for late scoring: GonffzCheeze yes, willowhelmiam no (few gains to
  trash).
- Desert Guides' value with Highwayman: GonffzCheeze high, willowhelmiam low.

## Grader notes
- No playtests. Guildmaster went essentially undiscussed.
