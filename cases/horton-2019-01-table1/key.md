# Key: Horton Winter 2019 Table 1: Sewers, Embargo, Tragic Hero, Horn of Plenty

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion: Winter 2019 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/repost-dominion-winter-2019-tournament-summary/),
  tournament 2019-01-12. Horton, a top in-person player, designed and
  playtested this kingdom for a 2-player tournament (played in the Swiss
  rounds). [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1__ePuWrM5XpCsVuLMIA3dmGWHKNOoZV5auC1ObWGbbk).

## Expert summary
The design idea: weak draw, Sewers as the only thinning, and the jankiest
possible ways to trigger it. You can trash a Hovel when gaining a Humble
Castle, buy Embargo or Treasure Map and trash them when played, or use Tragic
Hero's or Horn of Plenty's self-trashing, and each trash lets Sewers trash
another card. University with Horn of Plenty lets a player who uses this
trashing sidestep the likely Embargo spam (gaining instead of buying). The
deck eventually builds to a big turn of trashing Tragic Heroes for Horns of
Plenty, which then gain Provinces. In play, Marlene (5th overall) built this
core and won crushingly, though she began greening early enough that she
took only two or three Provinces in one turn.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Sewers is the only real thinning; the plan finds trash triggers to fire it (Hovel via Humble Castle, Embargo and Treasure Map trashing themselves, Tragic Hero, Horn of Plenty).
- **R2** [core] (designer + result) Builds to a big turn where Tragic Heroes trash themselves to gain Horns of Plenty (Treasures), and Horns gain Provinces.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) University gains Horn of Plenty (and other cards) without buying, which avoids Embargo tokens on bought piles.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Expects Embargo spam; Embargo is also a cheap one-shot that triggers Sewers.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) The board's draw is weak by design, so the deck relies on Sewers thinning rather than big draw.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Assuming there's no trashing and playing pure money: Sewers plus self-trashing cards provide it.

## Disagreements
- None recorded. Horton thought the winner could have scored more by greening later.
