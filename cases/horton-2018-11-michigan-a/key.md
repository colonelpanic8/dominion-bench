# Key: U-Con 2018 Set A: Colonies, Chariot Race, Wall, Triumph, Counting House

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion: Michigan Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-michigan-tournament-summary/),
  2018-11-12. Horton played this 3-player semifinal at U-Con. He didn't
  design it. He reports his VP tokens but not his placement.

## Expert summary
Some players talked up a Counting House/Storeroom deck. Horton calls it
madness: it needs a huge deck with many copies of key cards and lots of
Secret Passages, all to buy a couple of Colonies. He instead built a Chariot
Race deck for VP tokens: Secret Passage topdecks the same expensive card so
Chariot Race reveals it every time, villages (Settlers/Bustling Village) give
room for Goons, and Monastery keeps the deck reasonably thin. He bought many
Settlers, Chariot Races and Secret Passages but got only one Bustling
Village, so Goons did less than planned. He finished with almost 50 VP
tokens and lost only 10 VP to Wall.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert) Chariot Race spam for VP tokens is the main payload.
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Secret Passage topdecks an expensive card so Chariot Races win their reveals.
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Settlers/Bustling Village as villages, with Goons as extra VP payload.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Monastery as the thinning that keeps the deck workable.
- **R5** [supporting] (result) Wall costs relatively little (Horton lost about 10 VP to it) against ~50 VP tokens.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Counting House/Storeroom combo: needs a huge deck and many copies for a payload of a couple of Colonies.

## Disagreements
- Other players in the event favored Counting House/Storeroom; Horton disagreed.

## Grader notes
- No result is stated for this game, so treat the Chariot Race plan as one
  expert's choice. A Goons-and-Colony engine that names Chariot Race tricks
  deserves partial credit on R1.
- 3-player game.
