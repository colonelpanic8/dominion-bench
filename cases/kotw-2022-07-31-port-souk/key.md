# Key: KotW 7/31/2022: Plan, Port, Souk, Magpie

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/wd2dib/), 2022-07-31.
  willowhelmiam compared two detailed plans; SignError checked the odds and
  reported a game with Plan + Magpie; IlstrawberrySeed gave a full Port/Souk
  line; johnelectric named the Storeroom/Souk payload.

## Expert summary
Plan puts the Trashing token on a pile; each gain from it lets you trash a
card from hand. Port comes in pairs when bought, so Plan on Port trashes two
cards per Port buy. The favored line opens Silver + Plan (on Port): Silver
with two Coppers on turn 3/4 is about 88% (SignError), buying Port to trash
the rest. Then Gear, Ports and Souks; Storeroom (and Gear setting aside
green) shrinks the hand so Souk pays well; Souk's +Buy helps double
Provinces from around turn 12. The alternative is Plan on Magpie with
Cauldron (curse with the third Action gained), but early Magpie timing is
messy because it often forces an early reshuffle; SignError would take it
only after trashing Overgrown Estate on turn 2, and otherwise prefers the
Port line as more reliable. Nomads, Raze, Vagrant, Squire and Travelling
Fair were rated weak.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Plan (Event) on Port: each Port buy gains two Ports, so it trashes two cards. "Silver + Plan on Port is more reliable" (SignError)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Opens Silver + Plan on 4/3. (willowhelmiam, SignError, IlstrawberrySeed)
- **R3** [core] (majority) Payload is Souk with a small hand: Storeroom (and Gear) discard or set aside cards so Souk gives more. "use Storeroom to discard down to only a Souk" (johnelectric)
- **R4** [supporting] (majority) Plan on Magpie (with Cauldron) is the main alternative; it is less reliable because of early shuffle timing.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Trashing priority with Shelters: Hovel, Overgrown Estate, then Copper; keep Necropolis as a village for Souk turns.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Gear sets aside green cards late so they don't shrink Souk.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Nomads. "Nomads just sucks in both strats." (willowhelmiam)
- **T2** (single expert) Travelling Fair, since +Buy is available elsewhere. (willowhelmiam)
- **T3** (single expert) Souk in the Magpie deck, where Magpie grows the hand. (willowhelmiam)

## Disagreements
- willowhelmiam leaned Port but called it close; Curses from a Magpie/
  Cauldron opponent are the Port deck's risk. IlstrawberrySeed's 2/5 advice
  is to take the opposite $5 to the opponent's plan.
- Multiplayer: willowhelmiam thought Magpie gets better; SignError said it
  depends on how players split.
