# Key: KotW 8/7/2022: Sea Witch, Trail, Weaver, Warehouse

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/witniz/), 2022-08-07.
  Three fairly detailed plans from regulars (SignError, willowhelmiam,
  GrimoireM) that disagree; TheFunfighter and others commented. No games.

## Expert summary
The board has discard-reaction pieces: Trail and Weaver play when discarded
outside Clean-up, so Warehouse discarding a Trail nets +1 Action (Trail is
played for free). SignError at first prioritized winning the Trail split
(Weaver gains Trails and Warehouses), then softened to opening Salvager/
Warehouse with Weaver on turn 3. willowhelmiam wants a deck-drawing engine
led by Sea Witch: Salvager/Silver to hit $5, Sea Witches first, Sailor and
Trail/Weaver on $4s, building to double Province plus Salvaging a Province.
GrimoireM thinks trashing will be slow, so winning the Curse race matters
more than Trails; he opens Weaver/Warehouse (Crossroads/Sea Witch on 2/5)
and expects a faster ending, with Salvager trashing Sea Witches for green
once Curses and Trails run. All three call Berserker weak, and all warn that
Trail and Curse piles make three-pile endings likely.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Sea Witch matters early: winning the Curse race is a priority. (willowhelmiam, GrimoireM)
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Trail/Weaver discard synergies: Warehouse (and the other discarders) discarding Trail or Weaver plays them, giving net +Actions. "Warehouse + Trail is a good source of draw and especially +Actions" (SignError)
- **R3** [supporting] (consensus) Watch for a three-pile ending on Trail, Curses and another pile.
- **R4** [supporting] (majority) Salvager provides +Buy and late payload (e.g. Salvaging a Province or Sea Witch).
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Sailor thins Coppers and Curses.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Berserker: its discard attack feeds the opponent's Trails and Weavers. "Berserker is really bad unless you get the right alignment for it." (GrimoireM)
- **T2** (single expert) Crossroads is unnecessary given Trail, unless you lose the Trail split badly. (willowhelmiam)

## Disagreements
- Engine (willowhelmiam) vs. faster pile-out ending (GrimoireM).
- How much the Trail split matters: SignError first said it was key, then
  backed off; GrimoireM ranks it below the Curse race.
- Openings: Salvager/Warehouse (SignError), Salvager/Silver (willowhelmiam),
  Weaver/Warehouse (GrimoireM).

## Grader notes
- No consensus on the plan; grade R1-R2 as the stable core.
- Wharf and Guard Dog were barely discussed; Guard Dog plays when an opponent
  attacks, a reasonable beyond-key point.
