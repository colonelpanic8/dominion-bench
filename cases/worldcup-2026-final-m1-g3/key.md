# Key: 2026 World Cup final, Fircoal vs kojii, game 3: Horn of Plenty, Mining Road, Apothecary, Wishing Well

## Sources
- [JNails with Paolo99, neutral commentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_2At4CXlgQA), 52:30-1:15:30.
  Captions don't mark speakers, so unnamed quotes are "commentator".
- Match: Dominion World Cup 2026 final, match 1. kojii was player 1; Fircoal (5/2) won on
  Provinces.

## Expert summary
JNails expected Apothecary to be even better than in game 2 because it combos with Wishing Well:
Apothecary shows you the top four cards, so you know what to wish for. His suggested opening on
3/4 was Scrap (to kill Estates) with a Potion, then a few Apothecaries, building towards Horns of
Plenty, with Apothecary and Wishing Well as the draw. kojii did roughly that: Scrap, then
Wishing Well and Potion. Fircoal's 5/2 looked worse to the commentators (no $5 worth opening):
he took Journeyman (good for hitting $6 by skipping Estates), then Scrap, Litter and Barracks.
Barracks is a village at the start of every turn for a deck full of terminal draw
(Wayfarer, Journeyman). JNails's main strategic point: in Mining Road/Horn games, gain Mining Road
before the first Horn. Mining Road plays a gained Treasure, so a Mining Road that gains a Horn
does everything a Horn does plus $2 and a Buy. kojii ended up with six of the nine Horns
but too little draw and too few villages, and dudded several turns. Fircoal's Wayfarer/Litter
deck drew more reliably and won; JNails noted he could have ended it sooner by piling Litter and
Wayfarer.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert + result) Horn of Plenty is the main payload: gain cards (eventually Provinces, once you have 8+ differently named cards) from a drawing deck.
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Gains Mining Road before the first Horn: Mining Road can gain a Horn and play it at once, plus $2 and +Buy. "I would say that's typically true." (JNails, asked whether Mining Road comes first)
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Apothecary and Wishing Well work together (Apothecary reveals the top cards, so Wishing Well can name a hit); Scrap plus Potion is a natural opening.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert + result) Wayfarer and Journeyman are terminal draw and need villages: Litter, and Barracks for a guaranteed Action each turn.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert + result) Deck control matters more than Horn count: kojii had six of nine Horns and lost to dud turns.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Scrap trashes Estates early and can trash a Horn for a Card or Horse to dig for a full turn, then rebuy the Horn.
- **R7** [supporting] (single expert) Litter and Wayfarer can be piled to end the game when ahead.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Buying Horn of Plenty before having a Mining Road.
- **T2** (single expert) Adding more Mining Roads late instead of villages; JNails said the extra Mining Road mostly raised the chance of a dud, and Litter was better.
- **T3** (single expert + result) Piling up terminal draw (Wayfarers) without enough villages.

## Disagreements
- Fircoal's Journeyman opening on 5/2: the commentators said it looked worse than Scrap with a
  Potion or Wishing Well, but it worked out and Fircoal won.
- How to end: JNails thought Fircoal should have piled Litter/Wayfarer instead of taking a
  seventh Province (both won here).
