# Key: Making Luck ep. 100, kingdom 1: Witch, Embassy, Cargo Ship, Fortress, Pathfinding

## Sources
- [Making Luck ep. 100 "Rapid-Fire Kingdom Analysis"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RQk58SzbZd0&t=928s),
  2020-02-28, 15:28-21:50. Adam Horton and Wandering Winder, first look, no playtesting.

## Expert summary
There is no trashing, and there is Witch, so Witch is "priority numero uno":
the Curses can't be removed once given. Open Witch on 5/2. On 4/3, open
Cargo Ship/Silver: Cargo Ship either sets aside the Witch you buy with it
for next turn or helps you reach $5 for it. Both expect about two Witches.
They split on the follow-up. One host would add Fortresses and Heralds
(non-terminal and village-ish) and buy Pathfinding to put +1 Card on
Fortress. The other worries that after the Curse split nothing lets you catch
up, and prefers a soft-terminal Embassy money deck: Embassy doesn't mind
other terminals, though it does like discarding the junk. The hosts stress
that only the opening has to be decided now. Storeroom/Philosopher's Stone
gets a mention as "much less bad than usual", but Embassy is better.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Witch is the top priority because there's no trashing; plans to get Witch ASAP and likely two.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Opening: Witch on 5/2; Cargo Ship/Silver on 4/3 to get Witch played sooner. "I'm pretty sold on the cargo ship." (a host)
- **R3** [supporting] (consensus) Notes there's no trashing for Curses (Curses stick).
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Follows up with Embassy and Treasures (money), since there's no way to catch up after the Curse split.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Alternatively adds Fortress/Herald and Pathfinding (+1 Card token on Fortress) after the Witches.
- **R6** [supporting] (consensus) Doesn't commit to the later plan on turn 1; only the opening is decided now.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Buying Raid every turn.
- **T2** (consensus) Ignoring Witch for a Philosopher's Stone/Storeroom or other non-attacking plan.

## Disagreements
- Embassy money (one host) vs Fortress/Herald with Pathfinding (the other) after the Witches.
