# Key: KotW 12/12/2021: Worker's Village, Patrol, Grand Market, Invest

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/rf58en/), 2021-12-12, 15 comments.
  willowhelmiam, SignError and GonffzCheeze gave plans; no top player or game report.

## Expert summary
The commenters agree on a "traditional engine": Worker's Village as village and +Buy,
Patrol as draw, Grand Market as +Buy payload, and Way of the Goat for thinning.
Black Cat is the attack. GonffzCheeze and SignError stress the endgame: Black Cat
reacts when the opponent gains a Victory card, so topdeck or hold Black Cats when they
green, and Worker's Village leaves room for more. Invest on the opponent's key pile
(Worker's Village, Grand Market) is annoying for them, but timing matters: Invest in
Grand Market once they can afford it, yet before they Invest in it. willowhelmiam
uses Invest to pick up key cards cheaply. SignError adds Cellar after the first Black
Cat, since trashing is weak and terminal space limited.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Builds an engine of Worker's Village, Patrol and Grand Market, thinned with Way of the Goat. "WV's a village, Patrol's draw, GM is +buy, WoTGoat is thinning, traditional engine here." (willowhelmiam)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Black Cat is the attack, with its main punch when the opponent greens; keep Cats ready (topdecked, in hand) in the endgame. (GonffzCheeze, SignError, loge212)
- **R3** [supporting] (majority) Uses Invest on the opponent's key pile (Worker's Village or Grand Market), timing it before they Invest it. (GonffzCheeze, SignError)
- **R4** [supporting] (majority) Cellar has a role in a weak-trashing deck (sifting, finding Black Cats). (SignError, GonffzCheeze)
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Cardinal, Cartographer, Trader and Gardens are worse than the alternatives. (willowhelmiam)

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Gardens over Provinces. (willowhelmiam: "Gardens just seems worse than going for provinces")

## Disagreements
- Opening: Silver/Black Cat (willowhelmiam) vs Invest in Worker's Village plus Cellar
  as Way of the Goat on 4/3, or Cartographer/Cellar on 5/2 (SignError, who doesn't want
  a $5 on turns 3-4).
- Silvers: willowhelmiam takes a few early; GonffzCheeze wants few.
- Animal Fair: willowhelmiam calls it bad (terminals should be Patrols); SignError
  gets one by trashing an unused Action, then uses it toward Grand Market.

## Grader notes
- No strong player or test. Credit a plan built around the R1 pieces and Black Cat timing.
