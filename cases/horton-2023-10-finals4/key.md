# Key: Horton 2023-10 Finals Kingdom 4: City Quarter, Shepherd, Camel Train, Odysseys, no trashing

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "October 2023 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/october-2023-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, 2023-10-07. Horton designed this kingdom for the
  finals; his sheet notes it got less playtesting than the first two.
  [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/17d822tX5MEwbZLUAaWKnAbqEaaxWVIHy-pzWNZHiW9M).

## Expert summary
There is no trashing, and the draw is mostly Shepherd and City Quarter, which
both struggle here: Shepherd works well only alongside other draw, and City
Quarter wants high Action density. But you can gain enough of them with Camel
Train and Sunken Treasure (from the Odysseys pile), plus King's Cache if
needed, that it all works. Go heavy on gains, because the only +Buy is Animal
Fair. Once the City Quarters are empty the turns become very explosive, and
the deck is more consistent than you'd expect with zero trashing. The pieces
are weak and don't have great synergy, but you can stuff the deck with so many
Action cards that it works anyway. His sheet adds that emptying City Quarters
fast with Corsair slows opponents, and that Distant Shores and Estates help
make up VP.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Builds a draw deck around City Quarter and Shepherd despite no trashing, by flooding the deck with Action cards.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Gains are the build engine: Camel Train (Exile a card from the Supply each play; gaining a copy later discards all Exiled copies into your deck) and Sunken Treasure (gains an Action), plus King's Cache if needed; go heavy on gains.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Animal Fair is the only +Buy; empty piles (City Quarter) add Buys and make turns explosive.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Emptying the City Quarters quickly, with Corsair slowing opponents, is part of the plan.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) The deck is more consistent than expected without trashing.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Distant Shores and Estates help make up VP.

## Traps
