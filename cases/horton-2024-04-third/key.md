# Key: Horton 2024-04 Third Place Kingdom: Capitalism, Magnate, Inspiring Gladiator, Church

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "April 2024 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/april-2024-tournament-summary/),

## Expert summary
Two ideas around Capitalism (Actions with +$ in their text are also
Treasures). First, make Magnate (+1 Card per Treasure in hand) better by
turning most of the deck into Treasures. Second, play most of the turn in the
Buy phase with Capitalism as the main village, while still working deck
control in. Bazaar is present and you can build a decent deck with it, but in
playtesting you do much better by skipping it. Oasis and Courier let you play
Action cards from hand that Capitalism doesn't make Treasures (Church,
Menagerie, more Magnates). Inspiring Gladiators, which are Treasures under
Capitalism, and even Inspiring Fortunes help, and Silver Mine can gain whatever
card you need. The result is a deck full of good cards with big turns.
Menagerie gives crunchy decisions: hold extra Coppers to power Magnate, or
play them to switch Menagerie on. Treasure play order matters a lot.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Capitalism is the key: it makes +$ Actions into Treasures, so Magnate draws heavily and Capitalism acts as the village (cards played in the Buy phase need no Actions).
- **R2** [core] (designer) Magnate is the main draw, boosted by a Treasure-heavy hand under Capitalism.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Oasis and Courier (Treasures under Capitalism) play non-Treasure Actions such as Church, Menagerie and Magnate from hand.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Inspiring Gladiators (and Fortunes) let you play an extra Action after them.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Silver Mine gains the specific card you need.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Treasure play order and holding Coppers (Magnate vs Menagerie) are key tactical decisions.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Building a Bazaar deck; Horton found skipping Bazaar does much better.
