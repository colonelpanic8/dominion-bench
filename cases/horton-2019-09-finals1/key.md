# Key: Horton Fall 2019 Finals 1: Hermit/Sewers, Bridge, Diplomat, Outpost

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion: Fall 2019 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-fall-2019-tournament-summary/),
  tournament 2019-09-21. Horton designed and playtested this kingdom for the
  2-player finals bracket. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ViQF-GpoQYWCvz7H-Mr_4xKip7v59aUu_A2K9Z_nUd0).

## Expert summary
The intended deck plays many Bridges, with a few odd ways to enable it.
Whatever you build, the opening revolves around Hermit and Sewers: with
Sewers bought, one Hermit play can trash an Estate (and a Copper via
Sewers), gain another Hermit, and when the played Hermit trashes itself for
a Madman (no buy that turn), Sewers trashes another Copper. That thins very
fast and lets you add payload quickly. Three paths follow. (1) Madman/Bridge
megaturn, re-gaining Hermits from the trash with Rogue: finicky, slow and
exposed to a contested Bridge pile; two players who went for it lost because
their big turn wasn't enough. (2) Diplomat as the village (Horton's pick for
the strongest): Diplomat only gives +2 Actions with 5 or fewer cards in hand,
so enable it via an Outpost turn (doing the big things there), a deck of 5 or
fewer cards, or an opponent's Attack (Diplomat's reaction). With enough
Diplomats and Bridges this makes big turns, and Outpost was wanted anyway.
(3) A Treasure Trove money flood with the duration draw, buying Provinces the
old way.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Opening revolves around Hermit + Sewers: each Hermit play trashes several cards and Hermit trashing itself for Madman triggers Sewers again.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Bridge is the main payload (cost reduction plus +Buy, played many times).
- **R3** [core] (designer) Diplomat as the village, enabled by small hand size: on an Outpost turn, in a very thin deck, or after an opponent's Attack.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Outpost is valuable: it gives the 3-card turn that turns Diplomat into a village (at the cost of the other Outpost synergies such as Caravan and Hireling).
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Rogue can re-gain Hermits from the trash for more Madmen.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Bridge split can be contested, which hurts Bridge-dependent plans.
- **R7** [supporting] (designer) A Treasure Trove money/duration-draw deck is a viable third option.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer + result) Pure Madman-fueled Bridge megaturn: two players tried it and lost; the big turn wasn't enough.

## Disagreements
- Horton thinks the Diplomat deck is likely best but doesn't report a game won with it.

## Grader notes
- Played with the 2019 Hermit (trashes itself for Madman), stated in the
