# Key: 2025 Online Championship semifinal, game 4: Old Witch, King's Court, Coven, Avoid

## Sources
- [Lemonspawn's Dominion Videos](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e2dIV5xzXTo), 1:19:30-1:38:00.
  Commentary by Lemonspawn and Stef (a former champion), both strong players,
  with tracer's chat comments. VRSmith went first and won 35-23 when the
  Provinces ran out on turn 18. Kingdom and score read from the video.

## Expert summary
The commentators saw a curse slog. Old Witch was the opening $5. Kintsugi on
its own was judged poor: with no +Buy it uses a whole turn to trash a single
card. Avoid (+1 Buy, and you choose up to 3 cards from your next shuffle to
put in the discard pile) was the better event: use it to keep Old Witch in the
shuffle and junk out, so it is played as often as possible. Tracer said
"Avoid and trash sounds good". King's Court comes later, on Old Witch or
Coven. Ronin is good in junky decks, since a Shadow card can be played from
the deck.

jonts used double Avoid and Kintsugi, then King's Court with Old Witch and
Coven. A Sculptor buy surprised the commentators, who came round to thinking
it is not very good here. VRSmith's deck accumulated many Silvers alongside
Coven and Ronin. In the end, they said, VRSmith correctly judged that
her Silver-heavy deck would hold up longer, while jonts's money turns still
depended on King's Court colliding with the right cards. She steered into a
Duchy dance, and the game ended on Provinces in her favour.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Open Old Witch; this is a junking game and Curses will flow both ways.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Avoid is the useful event: +Buy, and it keeps Curses and junk out of the next shuffle so Old Witch is played often.
- **R3** [supporting] (consensus) Kintsugi alone is slow: one trash per turn and no +Buy.
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) King's Court is the mid-game target, on Old Witch or Coven.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Ronin is good in junked decks, since Shadows can be played from the deck.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert + result) In the endgame a deck with many Silvers holds up better than one that needs King's Court to line up; VRSmith played for a long game with Duchies and won.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Sculptor; the commentators came round to thinking it is not very good here.
- **T2** (consensus) Relying on Kintsugi as the main trashing without +Buy.

## Disagreements
- One commentator first suggested Kintsugi on Copper and Estate to guarantee $5; jonts's Avoid line looked better to them.
- Monkey: one commentator dislikes playing with it; its value here wasn't settled.

## Grader notes
- The middle of the game got little commentary; R6 rests on one post-game remark plus the result.
- Witch's Hut, Carnival, Warehouse and Corsair were hardly discussed; credit reasoned use of Corsair against a Silver-heavy opponent, or Warehouse for discarding Curses, as beyond-key.
