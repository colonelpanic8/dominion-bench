# Key: U-Con 2018 Final: Summon Young Witch, Procession, Raze bane, Colonies

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion: Michigan Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-michigan-tournament-summary/),
  2018-11-12. Horton won this 3-player tournament final at U-Con, going first.
  He didn't design it and credits first-player advantage.

## Expert summary
Horton calls the board Procession madness for anyone who gets control of
their deck. Going first, he used Summon on Young Witch: an early junking lead
makes it much harder for opponents to keep their decks under control, even
with Watchtower and Raze (the Bane) as defenses. He bought many Razes and
trashed them aggressively so he could play Young Witch as often as possible,
and took no Curses until the last turn. Opponents spent several turns
cleaning up while he ran Procession chains, gained lots of cards and kept
cursing. Near the end, with Curses low, he three-piled and took Duchies (he
bought a Curse to end on piles).

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert + result) An early junking lead with Young Witch is decisive; Summon (Young Witch on turn 1-2) is a strong opening.
- **R2** [supporting] (single expert + result) Raze is both the Bane (blocks Young Witch) and cheap thinning: buy several and trash them aggressively.
- **R3** [core] (single expert + result) Procession chains are the engine and gainer once the deck is under control.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert + result) Ends the game on three piles while ahead (Curses low, plus Duchies).
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Watchtower and Raze are the defenses against Young Witch.

## Traps
- **T1** (result) Ignoring the junking race: opponents who fell behind on Curses spent turns cleaning up and lost.

## Disagreements
- None recorded.

## Grader notes
- Horton notes a large first-player advantage; an analysis that says the
  Summon/Young Witch line is weaker going second is reasonable.
- The 2018 Procession, Prince and Band of Misfits differ from current errata
  (stated in the brief). An opponent's Band of Misfits/Necromancer loop
  through the trash was a highlight but not a winning plan.
- 3-player game.
