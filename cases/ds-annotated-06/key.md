# Key: DS Annotated Game #6: Wharf, Envoy, Bridge, Thief

## Sources
- [Annotated Game #6](https://dominionstrategy.com/2011/03/23/guest-article-annotated-game-6/)
  ([preview](https://dominionstrategy.com/2011/03/21/annotated-game-6-preview/)),
  2011-03-23: Geronimoo (author of the first Dominion simulator) annotates his
  narrow loss to crduemling, then simulates both strategies.

## Expert summary
An unexciting money board. Bridge has no +Actions to support it, so it's barely
better than Silver; Workshop is bad without Gardens; Wharf, Envoy and Harem (Farm)
suit Big Money. Militia conflicts with Wharf and Envoy, the attacks are weak so
Watchtower isn't needed, and the trashers are too slow for a sub-20-turn money
game (Loan is terrible in Big Money per simulation). Plan: open Envoy/Silver (or
Wharf on 5/2), get up to three Wharves or two Wharves and an Envoy, money, then
Provinces and Harems. The opponent opened Thief and won by 2 after stealing
Harems and Geronimoo bought a Thief himself; simulations put the Wharf strategy
at 61% to Thief's 35%, rising further if the Wharf player skips Harems against
Thief and delays Duchies.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert + simulation) Wharf/Envoy Big Money is the plan; there is no engine (Bridge lacks villages).
- **R2** [core] (simulation) Opens Envoy/Silver or Wharf on 5/2, adds about 2–3 Wharves (or Wharves plus Envoy), then Gold, Provinces and Harems.
- **R3** [supporting] (simulation) Militia/Silver is an equally good opening (same win rate as Envoy/Silver in the bots), though it clashes with Wharf draws.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert + simulation) Thief is weak in 2-player; Wharf money beats it.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert + simulation) Lookout and Loan are too slow for this money game.
- **R6** [supporting] (simulation) Against a Thief player, skip Harems (they get stolen) and green a little later.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert + simulation) Thief, including buying one to steal back Harems (Geronimoo's own mistake).
- **T2** (simulation) Watchtower to counter Militia: it conflicts with Wharf and lost about 2/3 of simulated games.
- **T3** (single expert) Building around Bridge or Workshop with no villages.

## Disagreements
- None among the sources; the actual game went to the Thief player by 2 VP, which
  Geronimoo attributes to luck and his own Thief buy.

## Grader notes
- 2011, first-edition cards (Loan, Thief; Farm was Harem).
- Simulator results are reliable here: both bots are one-Province-per-turn money
  decks (see context/card-power.md on simulators).
