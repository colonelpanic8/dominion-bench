# Key: 2026 World Cup final, Fircoal vs kojii, game 2: Relic, Apothecary, Advisor, Pursue

## Sources
- [JNails with Paolo99, neutral commentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_2At4CXlgQA), 41:40-52:30.
  Captions don't mark speakers, so unnamed quotes are "commentator".
- Match: Dominion World Cup 2026 final, match 1. kojii (player 1) won; Fircoal resigned early.

## Expert summary
The commentators read this as a money board with no strong money cards: they saw no Kingdom
+Buy and no terminal draw or payload that Big Money wants, so complex plans have little value
when you gain one card per turn. Monkey is weaker than usual (it draws extra only when the
opponent gains cards). Relic is the clear $5, and in a money game two or three Relics are fine,
because you want to find one most turns even though the -1 Card token doesn't stack. JNails
called Apothecary a solid maybe; the co-commentator and chat doubted it. They expected Advisor
to be poor (you can't gain enough of them to power through a greening deck, and you need heavy
trashing first) and recalled simulations showing Goatherd is bad in money games. Captain was
expected to be played as Goatherd early to trash Estates, then as Advisor or Merchant.

Fircoal opened Relic with Pursue (topdecking Relic), then added Monkey, Advisors and a Swap.
kojii took Potion, three Apothecaries, Monkey and a Captain (played as Goatherd to trash
Estates), and pulled ahead fast. JNails then asked who had been bad-mouthing Apothecary and
"expect[ed] a written apology"; Fircoal resigned. Chat felt kojii's early draws were lucky.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Reads the board as money-oriented: few gains per turn and no strong engine, so build a Treasure deck with a few good Actions. "This is a big money kingdom without any particularly good big money cards." (commentator)
- **R2** [core] (single expert + result) Apothecary (with a Potion or two) is a real option for the money deck: it draws Coppers and arranges the top of the deck. kojii's three Apothecaries won.
- **R3** [supporting] (consensus) Relic is the best $5; a money deck wants two or three Relics, not one.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Captain is played as Goatherd early to trash Estates, then as Advisor or Merchant later.
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus) Monkey is weak here: it rarely draws more than a card or two without opponent gains, though it's the only terminal draw.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus + result) Building around Advisor: it needs heavy trashing and many copies, which a one-gain-per-turn board can't support. "I think advisor is going to be pretty sad." (commentator)
- **T2** (single expert) Goatherd as a main card in a money deck; JNails cited simulations showing a low win rate.
- **T3** (single expert) Swap: JNails couldn't see a useful target in Fircoal's deck.

## Disagreements
- Apothecary: JNails thought it plausible; Paolo99 and chat expected it to be bad. The game sided
  with JNails, but on one short game with lucky early draws.
- Whether to buy a second Relic early: JNails would have; Fircoal took Advisor and Monkey.

## Grader notes
- The commentators said there was no +Buy, but Pursue gives +1 Buy for $2, and Gardens is in the
  Kingdom; neither was discussed. Credit an analysis that weighs Gardens or uses Pursue to
  topdeck Relic (Fircoal did), without penalizing analyses that ignore them (my note).
- Single short game: weight R1 and R3 over the result.
