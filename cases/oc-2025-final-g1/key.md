# Key: 2025 Online Championship final, game 1: Orchard, Populate, Necromancer, Sculptor

## Sources
- [JNails and jonts, neutral commentary, part 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MhlRn5rrlbU), 0:00-16:00.
  Both are top league players; the captions are machine-garbled, so speakers are cited as "commentator".
- [VRSmith's own commentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kWZyqrCn-ws), 0:00-18:00. VRSmith
  (two-time champion) lost this game and explains why.
- Match: Minionpawn (player 1) beat VRSmith 37-18.

## Expert summary
The decisive payload is Populate (gain one card from each Action Supply pile) scored by Orchard
(4 VP per differently named Action you have 3+ copies of). Eight Action piles are on the board
(Gondola and Pendant are Treasures), so repeated Populates are worth up to about 32 VP from
Orchard alone, and the commentators flagged this before the opening. Minionpawn played for $10
early: Gondola opening, two Sculptors (gaining Gondolas and Mountain Villages to hand for
Villagers), Encampments and lots of Mountain Villages, and bought Populate twice before VRSmith
bought any, then a third time. VRSmith opened Necromancer/Silver aiming to trash with the Zombies
and Rats, bought Gold and Library, and fell far behind; afterwards he said he had the wrong idea
about the board and that spiking Populate mattered more than trashing and draw. The commentators
had preferred a Necromancer opening and doubted the third Populate, but called the game for
Minionpawn from the second Populate on.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) Populate is the main payload, because Orchard rewards owning 3+ copies of many different Actions; repeated Populates are worth far more than Provinces. "I think just spiking populate was more important." (VRSmith)
- **R2** [core] (single expert + result) Plays for early $10 hands and buys Populate early and repeatedly, rather than building a long trashing engine first.
- **R3** [supporting] (consensus) Sculptor is strong: it gains Gondola (a Treasure, so +1 Villager, and Gondola's on-gain plays an Action) or Mountain Village to hand.
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Mountain Village/Encampment are the village and draw; Sculptor and Populate let you collect three or more copies of several Actions for Orchard.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Necromancer turns the trashed Zombies into trashing/gaining (Zombie Mason), and a trashed Encampment becomes a free village every turn via Necromancer.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Rats is awkward to start: the first Rats is junk, and it is better if the opponent trashes one so your Necromancer can play it.
- **R7** [supporting] (single expert) With no Province race from +Buy, counts Orchard sets: each Populate adds one copy of every Action pile, including Plunder once Encampments run out.

## Traps
- **T1** (result + single expert) A slow trashing build (Necromancer/Zombie Mason, Rats, Zombie Apprentice) that delays Populate. VRSmith: "I think I just had like the totally wrong idea about this board."
- **T2** (single expert) Buying Gold here instead of Populate pieces or Plunder; a commentator was openly sarcastic about VRSmith's Gold, and VRSmith noted Plunder is much better than Gold on this board.

## Disagreements
- Opening: the commentators and VRSmith preferred Necromancer (or Rats) on $4; Minionpawn's Gondola
  open looked weak to them but fed early Sculptor/$10 turns and won.
- A commentator thought a third Populate was hard to justify against buying a Province;
  Minionpawn took it anyway, and the margin (37-18) suggests it was fine.
