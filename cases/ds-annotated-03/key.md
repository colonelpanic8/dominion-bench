# Key: DS Annotated Game #3: Hoard, Island, Trade Route, Tactician (Colonies)

## Sources
- [Annotated Game #3](https://dominionstrategy.com/2011/02/21/annotated-game-3/)
  ([preview](https://dominionstrategy.com/2011/02/18/annotated-game-2-preview/)),
  2011-02-21: theory (blog founder) annotates his win over Dekker (then #2 on
  Isotropic).

## Expert summary
theory sees three interactions: Hoard with Island and Harem (Farm), where the
Victory cards Hoard rewards are themselves useful, so Hoard's Golds don't come
with dead cards; Trade Route, strong because there are many Victory piles (Island,
Harem, Colony) and Hoard pushes players to buy them; and Forge on Tactician turns.
Mining Village is a good enabler as an early one-shot Grand Market. Feast, Moat
and Talisman are weak. He opened Mining Village/Trade Route, bought Hoard at $6
and piled Islands and Harems, adding Tactician and Forge. Dekker opened
Talisman/Silver, took two Silvers with Talisman, and later a second Forge that he
never played. theory won 68–66 on a large Island advantage.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert + result) Hoard plus Island/Harem: buying Victory cards that are also useful (Island sets aside junk, Harem is money) earns Golds without clogging the deck.
- **R2** [core] (single expert + result) Trade Route is strong because many Victory piles (Island, Harem, Colony) will be gained; buy it early, while it still has trashing targets and you're not yet buying VP.
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Mining Village is the best early enabler (an early one-shot +Buy +$2).
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Tactician and Forge are good support; Forge converts junk on big Tactician turns.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Feast, Moat and Talisman are weak here.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Kingdom Victory cards change the endgame math: lesser VP (Islands, Duchies) can outweigh a Colony lead.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Opening Talisman and using it on Silvers: it can't copy Islands and leaves too many Silvers for a Gold/Colony game.
- **T2** (single expert + result) A second Forge late: Dekker never played it; a Province would have sealed his lead.
