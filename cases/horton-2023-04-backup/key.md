# Key: Horton 2023-04 Backup Finals Kingdom: Highway, Silk Merchant, Way of the Horse, Search

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion: Spring 2023 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-spring-2023-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, 2023-04-15. Horton designed and playtested this
  kingdom; it was used for the tournament's final game. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1bUdgaAKlS6lsndwvGKeABDdWVf9etjaLFIMcRVTcgqU).

## Expert summary
Inspired by a Highway/Silk Merchant megaturn, with Silk Merchant as the only
village and Way of the Horse to keep the deck from being over-terminal. A
Highway megaturn is possible but tricky because there is no trashing, so draw
comes from Way of the Horse and Imps (Devil's Workshop). The trick is to bank
Villagers by gaining Silk Merchants and returning them to the pile with Horse
(each gain gives +1 Coffers and +1 Villager, and returning them lets you
gain them again), while contesting Highways. Once you have 3-5 Villagers and the Highways are gone, take one setup
turn buying 3-4 Silk Merchants, then pop off the next turn for the remaining
Provinces or pile cards. Searches and which Loots you get change the math.
Other points: the Flag (Flag Bearer) is very important because draw is tight,
and you can Horse the Flag Bearer; Tide Pools is a trap as always; finding time
and terminal space for Searches is a crunchy call that helps late.

The final was won by Search spam: the champion contested Highways but mainly
bought Search every turn until they ran out, collected many Loots (Horsing a
Search to re-buy it), and greened right away. Horton says this sounds bad but
he has heard enough success stories that he wants to test it more.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) The intended plan is a Highway megaturn, enabled by Villagers banked from Silk Merchants.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Bank Villagers by gaining Silk Merchants and returning them with Way of the Horse.
- **R3** [core] (designer) Contest the Highways.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) With 3-5 Villagers and Highways gone, take a setup turn buying 3-4 Silk Merchants, then the megaturn.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Draw is tight (Way of the Horse and Imps only; no trashing), which makes the Flag very valuable; Horse the Flag Bearer.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer + result) Search/Loot is a significant factor; the tournament final was won by Search spam into early greening.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Tide Pools ("a trap as always").

## Disagreements
- Horton's designed line (Highway megaturn) vs the Search-spam strategy that
  won the final; he is unsure which is better and wants more testing. Give
