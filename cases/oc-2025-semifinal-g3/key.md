# Key: 2025 Online Championship semifinal, game 3: Highwayman, Outpost, King's Cache, Way of the Sheep

## Sources
- [Lemonspawn's Dominion Videos](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e2dIV5xzXTo), 53:00-1:19:00.
  Commentary by Lemonspawn and Stef (a former champion), both strong players,
  with tracer's chat comments read on stream. jonts went first and won with a
  big final turn, pulling the match to 2-1. Kingdom read from the video.

## Expert summary
jonts had a $5/$2 split. The commentators liked Highwayman on the $5 ("I would [be]
leaning heavily to the Highwayman"), noted Port with Way of the Sheep as a
decent alternative that hits price points, and chose Silver for the $2-$3 slot.
Their roadmap was patience: keep buying Highwayman, Capital City and Port
until the deck draws itself, and only then add Outpost, King's Cache and
Wealthy Village ("I think this is a good board for being patient"). They
would skip Gold, wait to buy Outpost until the deck has control, and use
King's Cache on the best Loots, ideally ones with +Buy, Hammer, Puzzle Box or
Prize Goat; Figurehead and Doubloons are the worst. Way of the Sheep turns
Highwayman into +$2 on a turn when you no longer need draw.

Both players bought an Outpost around turns 8-10, each time with the
commentators arguing for one more Capital City or Highwayman first ("no, it
should not be outpost"); Outpost's 3-card hand also sits awkwardly with
Highwayman's draw. jonts kept adding Highwaymen and Capital Cities, got King's
Cache and a Puzzle Box Loot, and finished with a turn of several Highwaymen
played as Way of the Sheep plus King's Cache on Loots. VRSmith's key Loot
gain was a Figurehead, which "pretty much sealed it".

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) Highwayman is the key $5: draw next turn plus an attack that hurts early. jonts opened it and won.
- **R2** [core] (consensus + result) Build the draw first (Highwayman, Capital City, Port) and only then add Outpost, King's Cache and Wealthy Village.
- **R3** [core] (consensus) Skip Gold; King's Cache is the Treasure payload. "gold is so much worse" (commentator)
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Wealthy Village's Loot gain matters; +Buy Loots, Hammer, Puzzle Box and Prize Goat are the best King's Cache targets.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Port with Way of the Sheep is a reasonable opening that hits price points.
- **R6** [supporting] (result) Way of the Sheep on Highwaymen converts spare draw cards into +$2 each on the payoff turn.
- **R7** [supporting] (single expert) Settlers and Capital City work well together.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Buying Outpost before the deck can draw itself; its 3-card hand clashes with Highwayman draw until the deck has control.
- **T2** (consensus) Buying Gold instead of building or King's Cache.
- **T3** (single expert) Taking a Loot without +Buy as the King's Cache target; Figurehead ended VRSmith's chances.

## Disagreements
- When exactly to buy Outpost: both commentators said later, but one noted many players buy it early and sometimes it works.
- One commentator disliked a Bishop buy in this game; tracer liked it.
