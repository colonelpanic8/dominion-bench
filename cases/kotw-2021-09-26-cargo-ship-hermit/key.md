# Key: KotW 9/26/2021: Rebuild, Patron, Cargo Ship, Hermit

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/pw53xr/), 2021-09-26, 12 comments.
  willowhelmiam argued for Rebuild in detail; treelorf and PathfindingLibrary (strong
  regulars) insisted on Hermit; SignError explored Scheme/Hermit; mountebanker tested a
  Rebuild variant.

## Expert summary
Split thread. willowhelmiam: plain Rebuild, supported by Patron (Coffers when revealed,
which Rebuild does), Cargo Ship, Silos and Crop Rotation. Open Cargo Ship/Patron, take
2-3 Rebuilds, Crop Rotation ASAP, then judge Duchies against more Rebuilds; ignore
Hermit, Jack, Mystic, Research, Silk Road and Squire. treelorf replied that a plan
without Hermit "is almost certainly wrong". When willowhelmiam answered that Hermit's
Estate trashing conflicts with Rebuild, PathfindingLibrary said that conflict is "a
case against Rebuild." mountebanker tested a variant (trash Estates and deliberately
whiff Rebuild to farm Patron Coffers) and it was "crushed" by the normal Rebuild deck
opening Patron/Cargo Ship. SignError noted that Scheme can keep Hermit from being lost
when it becomes a Madman.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert) Treats Hermit as a key card rather than dismissing it. "If your game plan doesn't involve hermit here it's almost certainly wrong" (treelorf; PathfindingLibrary agrees)
- **R2** [core] (single expert + result) Recognizes Rebuild, supported by Patron, Cargo Ship and Crop Rotation, as a serious candidate plan. (willowhelmiam; mountebanker's test favored standard Rebuild with a Patron/Cargo Ship opening over his variant)
- **R3** [supporting] (majority) Names the tension: Hermit trashes the Estates that Rebuild turns into Duchies and Provinces, so a plan must choose. (willowhelmiam, PathfindingLibrary)
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Rebuild revealing Patrons gives Coffers; Crop Rotation turns green cards into draw. (willowhelmiam, jppbkm)
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Scheme topdecks Hermit before it is trashed, so you can get a Madman and keep the Hermit. (SignError, citing the Dominion Strategy wiki)

## Traps
- **T1** (result) Farming Patron Coffers by deliberately missing with Rebuild. "this gets crushed by normal rebuild strat with patron and cargo ship open." (mountebanker)

## Disagreements
- Rebuild (willowhelmiam) vs a Hermit-based plan (treelorf, PathfindingLibrary). Neither
  side spelled out the Hermit plan, and nobody tested the two against each other.
