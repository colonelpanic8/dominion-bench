# Key: KotW 1/9/2022: Priest, Bishop, Cultist, Worker's Village

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/s06n50/), 2022-01-10.
  PathfindingLibrary (strong regular) named the plan and the pile risk;
  SignError and willowhelmiam debated Cultist and the three-pile ending;
  SignError added a Mission/Pilgrimage/Bishop idea. No playtests.

## Expert summary
PathfindingLibrary's plan is a Priest megaturn: thin hard, then trash many
cards in one turn with Priest's +$2-per-trash. He warned that if both players
build, the game can end on three piles: Worker's Village, Ruins (from
Cultist) and Estates. He also argued that skipping the Cultist/village race
just loses: the Ruins end up in your deck and the opponent outbuilds you.
SignError suggested opening Church/Priest for early trashing, and saw Mission
plus Pilgrimage (gaining Catacombs and Gold to feed Bishop) as explosive.
willowhelmiam doubted Worker's Village would run out given how strong the
trashing is and how few terminals the deck needs.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert) Builds a thin engine aiming at a Priest megaturn (many trashes for +$2 each). "Ideally a Priest megaturn" (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Strong early trashing: opens Church/Priest or similar. "Open Church and Priest for early trashing" (SignError)
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Names the three-pile risk on Worker's Village, Ruins and Estates when both players build.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Contests Cultist and Worker's Village rather than ceding them; ignoring the village pile leaves you junked and outbuilt. "If you ignore the village pile, you just lose the normal way." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Bishop as VP payload, fed by Pilgrimage gains (Catacombs, Gold), with Mission giving an extra turn to use them.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Leaving the Cultist/Worker's Village race to the opponent and only trashing and piling Estates. PathfindingLibrary rejected it.

## Disagreements
- Whether Worker's Village actually empties: PathfindingLibrary expects it,
  willowhelmiam doubts it because few terminals are needed.
- SignError thought a Cultist player gives the opponent a three-pile route
  (Ruins plus Estates); PathfindingLibrary replied the Ruins are then in the
  victim's deck, so it's still a loss for them.

## Grader notes
- Thin thread. Credit any analysis that sees Priest as the payload engine
  with strong trashing (Church, Priest, Monastery, Exorcist, Sanctuary) and
  watches the piles. The Mission/Pilgrimage line is one commenter's idea.
- Relic's −1 Card token and Exorcist's Spirits went unmentioned; treat
  sensible use of them as beyond-key.
