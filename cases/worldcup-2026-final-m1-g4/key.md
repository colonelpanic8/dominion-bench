## Sources

  Captions don't mark speakers, so unnamed quotes are "commentator". Chat (Lemonspawn,

- Match: Dominion World Cup 2026 final, match 1. kojii (player 1) won with a large points lead.

## Expert summary
Only a short read. Research is the only trashing; Island can set cards aside; there are no
villages and the only draw is non-terminal (Hunter, Research), so the board looked moneyish.
JNails would "probably buy Guildhall" though it didn't look great, and couldn't see the
Treasure-gaining synergy Lemonspawn hinted at (Jewelled Egg makes Loots). Band of Misfits can no
longer play Duration cards, which shuts it off from what JNails called the best card here
(presumably Research, the only Duration Action). Both players opened Sage/Research. kojii added Herb Gatherer to control the Augurs pile; the commentators and
Minionpawn found it odd because it is terminal with no villages. Fircoal took an Acolyte, which
whiffed because Acolyte can't trash Treasures. Loots from Jewelled Egg mattered: Staff is the
best Loot here because villages are missing, then Sword, Hammer, Figurehead and Prize Goat.
Research can trash a Loot mid-to-late game to draw a lot. kojii pulled ahead early, gained
Island for easy points, took an early Province, and won.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) No villages and only non-terminal draw (Hunter, Research), so this is a money-leaning deck with a few good Actions, not a terminal-heavy engine.
- **R2** [supporting] (single expert + result) Research is the trasher (and draw); Sage/Research is a reasonable opening, and both players chose it.
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Jewelled Egg's Loots are valuable payload; Staff (a village) is the best Loot here, then Sword, Hammer, Figurehead.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Island is an easy VP gain (from a Hammer or a spare $4) once scoring starts.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Guildhall is probably worth buying for Coffers from Treasure gains (Loots, Silvers).
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Researching a $7 Loot (or a Gold) sets aside that many cards for a big next turn; it's a mid-to-late-game trick.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Counting on Band of Misfits to copy Research: it can't play Duration cards under current rules.
- **T2** (consensus) Herb Gatherer as a terminal in a deck with no villages. "Herb gatherer i would just rather be a silver" (Minionpawn, chat)

## Disagreements
- Lemonspawn (chat) saw value in Guildhall; JNails would buy it but wasn't sure why it was good.
