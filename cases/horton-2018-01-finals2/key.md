# Key: Horton Winter 2018 finals 2: Counting House, Minion, Council Room, Travelling Fair

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, Winter 2018 Tournament Summary](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-winter-2018-tournament-summary/):
  the designer's write-up of a 2-player finals kingdom ("I wish for a Counting House"); played once.

## Expert summary
Horton built three competing decks into the board. The Counting House/Travelling Fair combo
is present along with its hard counter, Minion, but Minion has thin support (Royal Carriage,
Travelling Fair, Chapel), builds slowly and greens poorly. After playtesting, the combo and
Minion decks came out close. Any other deck can't splash Minion, because it would attack
itself as much as the opponent. Council Room is there to complicate the combo deck, which
doesn't want extra cards, and it turns out to be the most powerful deck. Its enablers are
Coin of the Realm (excellent), Royal Carriage, and Storeroom for reliability, so a very large
deck stays reliable. Topdeck Council Rooms and Royal Carriages with Travelling Fair each turn,
draw piles of Coppers, keep buying Coppers with spare buys even after the Fountain points are
locked in, and use Leprechaun's Wishes (easy to trigger with Reserves) once you hold 15-20
Coppers and a Storeroom. Stacking Travelling Fair/Chapel to trash three Estates on turn 2
helps the Minion deck but makes the Council Room deck worse. His opening would be Council
Room/Coin of the Realm, or Storeroom/Silver.

- **R3** [core] (designer) The Council Room deck draws many Coppers every turn and keeps buying Coppers with spare buys (even after securing Fountain), staying reliable via Storeroom.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Coin of the Realm and Royal Carriage are key support; Travelling Fair topdecks Council Rooms/Royal Carriages.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Leprechaun is easy to activate with Reserves; its Wishes are used late.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Opens Council Room/Coin of the Realm (or Storeroom/Silver).
- **R7** [supporting] (designer) Minion counters the Counting House combo, and the two decks are close.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Stacking the opening for a turn-2 Chapel/Travelling Fair Estate trash in the Council Room deck: it helps Minion decks, not this one.
- **T2** (designer) Splashing Minion into a non-Minion deck: it attacks you as much as the opponent.
