# Key: KotW 11/20/2022: Destrier, Sleigh, Village Green, Stampede

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/z0m182/), 2022-11-21.
  Short comments from Interesting_Dare_640, SignError, WildcatKid,
  mrcelophane and UluruMonster. No full plan or game report.

## Expert summary
Destrier costs $1 less per card gained this turn, so the theme is cheap gains
to make Destriers cheap. Interesting_Dare_640: open Remake to turn Estates
into useful $3s, then build a simple "two villages plus two Horse gainers"
engine with Sleigh, gaining many Horses, and pick up Destriers. Way of the
Monkey gives +Buy on any Action. SignError doubted Stampede (needs 5 or fewer
cards in play) and preferred lowering Destrier's cost with Sleigh, Stonemason
and Remake; UluruMonster said playing an Action or two as Monkey and buying
Stampede can still get two or three cheap Destriers. Village Green discard
synergies stand out (Oasis especially, and Hunting Lodge), and Village Green
also defends against Urchin's discard attack.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Cheap Destriers via many gains in a turn (Sleigh Horses, Stonemason, Remake, Stampede). "seems like a clear cheese-gains-for-destriers Menagerie board." (Interesting_Dare_640)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Village Green discard synergies, especially with Oasis and Hunting Lodge. "Oasis is especially nice." (WildcatKid)
- **R3** [supporting] (majority) Way of the Monkey supplies +Buy on any Action.
- **R4** [supporting] (single player) Opens Remake to turn Estates into useful $3s.
- **R5** [supporting] (single player) Sleigh as a Horse gainer in a basic villages-plus-Horse-gainers engine.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Relying on Stampede: hard to have the money and Buys for Stampede plus Destriers with 5 or fewer cards in play. (SignError; UluruMonster disagreed)

## Disagreements
- Stampede's usefulness: SignError skeptical, UluruMonster thinks Monkey
  plays make it work.

## Grader notes
- Very thin; no one specified payload or an ending. Credit coherent engine
  plans that use the cheap-Destrier idea.
