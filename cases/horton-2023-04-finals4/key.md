# Key: Horton 2023-04 Finals Kingdom 4: Priest, March, Beggar, Siren, Colonies

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion: Spring 2023 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-spring-2023-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, 2023-04-15. Horton designed this kingdom for the
  finals; it received a lot of playtesting. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1bUdgaAKlS6lsndwvGKeABDdWVf9etjaLFIMcRVTcgqU).

## Expert summary
The kingdom makes you use Beggar and March as draw. They are poor draw, but
Priest shenanigans make you desperate enough for draw (and you don't mind
Beggar's Coppers, which Priest trashes for money) that they are worth it. Siren
and Witch's Hut also draw, but you get much stronger using March and Beggar
too. Actions and Buys are plentiful, so the more Priests you play per turn,
the faster you gain control and the more payload you have. A trick: with two or
more Priests in play, buy a Siren and let it trash itself (its on-gain) for $4
or more, converting extra Buys into money while running a pile.

His opening: Priest/March (don't use Coastal Haven yet), then on the next
shuffle a Worker's Village and a Sycophant (don't be tempted by a nice $5 hand;
you need the Worker's Village), and if possible Haven a card on turn 4. Then
get a Siren and March it over the next few turns, a second Sycophant into
Siren, and more Villages and Priests. Spend Favors freely to line things up,
since once you get a Siren out every turn, Favors are nearly useless. If
junked heavily, March Priests or Sirens often to keep control. Priest can
eventually trash junk, but playing Beggar to gain junk to hand is much better,
so reach that point quickly.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Priest is the payload engine: trash cards for +$2 each after it; more Priests per turn means more control and more money.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Beggar (Coppers to hand, fodder for Priest) and March (replay an Action from the discard pile) are worth taking as draw-like support.
- **R3** [core] (designer) Opens Priest/March, without using Coastal Haven on the opening turns.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Next shuffle: Worker's Village and Sycophant, even over a $5 card; Coastal Haven a card on turn 4 if possible.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Gets a Siren and Marches it; a second Sycophant into Siren; then Villages and Priests.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Spend Favors liberally early to line things up; they matter less once a Siren plays every turn.
- **R7** [supporting] (designer) Trick: with 2+ Priests in play, buy a Siren and let it trash itself for $4+ while emptying a pile.
- **R8** [supporting] (designer) Under heavy junking, March Priests or Sirens to keep control.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Buying a $5 card instead of the planned Worker's Village on turns 3-4.
- **T2** (designer) Relying on Siren and Witch's Hut alone for draw, skipping March and Beggar.
- **T3** (designer) Hoarding Favors.
