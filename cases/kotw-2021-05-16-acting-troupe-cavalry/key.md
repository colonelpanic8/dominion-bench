# Key: KotW 5/16/2021: Ironworks, Courtier, Village Green, Cavalry

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/ne7oyd/), 2021-05-16, 12 comments.
  EphesosX gave a detailed plan; PathfindingLibrary and treelorf (strong regulars)
  accepted its core and rejected its greening timing and Pageant.

## Expert summary
EphesosX: only Ironworks and Courtier get you more than one card per turn, so use
both. Open Ironworks/Silver and Ironworks more Ironworks, then Village Green and
Courtier. Courtier revealing Village Green (three types, so three choices) is
"insanely good", and Courtier's Gold plus Swashbuckler's Treasure Chest replace buying
Gold. Way of the Goat trashing is slow and costs Actions. Once you have 3-4 Village
Greens, Ironworks Cavalry for draw. PathfindingLibrary corrected two points: Ironworks
on Cavalry also gives +Buy, so Courtier isn't the only route to double Province; and
build much bigger before greening. treelorf agreed this is a board for building
massive. PathfindingLibrary also wouldn't buy Pageant.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Ironworks drives the build: open Ironworks/Silver and use Ironworks to gain more $4 pieces. (EphesosX; unchallenged)
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Courtier revealing Village Green is the key payload interaction. "Courtier Village Green is just insanely good" (EphesosX)
- **R3** [core] (majority) Builds big before greening; single Provinces in the midgame are a mistake. "Build much bigger than that." (PathfindingLibrary; treelorf agrees)
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Ironworks gaining Cavalry gives +Buy (and draw). "You can Ironworks a Cavalry for +Buy." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Doesn't buy Gold; gets Gold from Courtier and Treasure Chest. (EphesosX)
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Way of the Goat trashing is slow; use it rarely. (EphesosX)

## Traps
- **T1** (majority) Greening whenever $8 appears. "Early Province is actually an excellent way to destroy an engine." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **T2** (single expert) Building the plan around Pageant's Coffers. "It is at best a marginal buy" (PathfindingLibrary)

## Disagreements
- EphesosX argued the lead plus contested piles (Village Green, Cavalry, Ironworks)
  lets you threaten a three-pile and green earlier; PathfindingLibrary and treelorf disagreed.

## Grader notes
- No game reported. Minion, Patron, Acting Troupe and Wishing Well got only passing mentions.
