# Key: 2026 World Cup final, Fircoal vs kojii, game 1: Kitsune, Stables, Rope, Progress

## Sources
- [JNails with Paolo99, neutral commentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_2At4CXlgQA), 0:00-41:40.
  JNails is a top US player (on the USA A team in this final); Paolo99 joins at about 7:00.
  Captions don't mark speakers, so unnamed quotes are "commentator".
- Match: Dominion World Cup 2026 final, USA A vs Japan A, match 1 (six games, ended 3-3).
  Fircoal (player 1) won this game in a long pile-race ending.

## Expert summary
JNails reads the board as a Stables deck. Draw is Stables, plus Imps if you use Exorcist;
villages (Rustic Village, Wealthy Village, Kitsune) matter little because there are few
terminals to play. Trashing is plentiful (Rope, Church, Exorcist). +Buy comes from Rope (only
every other turn) and from the Loots that Wealthy Village gains, which he called the better
source. Kitsune is worth getting early for its Curses: there is enough trashing that Curses
won't cripple anyone, but each one still wastes a draw, and Progress makes it worse. Once five
Omens (Kitsune, Rustic Village, Tea House) have been played, every gain is topdecked,
including Curses. Paolo99's plan was to take as many Ropes as possible, gain lots of Stables
(Ironworks helps), then shift into Wealthy Villages. Both players opened Rope and Church and
picked up Kitsunes; Fircoal added Ironworks for Ropes. JNails thought Exorcist was underused:
turning Silvers or spent Kitsunes into Imps gives Lab-like draw in a deck with spare Actions.
Progress shapes the ending: a turn that buys several Provinces topdecks them and duds next turn,
so either win in one turn or pair green with Stables. Annex works as a free Duchy on the final
turn. Rope, Stables and Church ran low, and the game was decided by pile and Annex counting.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert + co-commentator) Stables is the main draw. Gain many Stables, with Ironworks if needed, and keep enough Treasures for it to discard.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Rope is the key $4: thinning and +Buy. Get as many Ropes as you can (Ironworks gains them). "You definitely want all the ropes as much as you can get" (commentator)
- **R3** [core] (single expert + result) Kitsune is worth taking early for its Curses even with plenty of trashing on the board, and both players did so.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Progress plus Kitsune is nasty: once Progress is active, Curses are gained onto the deck and drawn next turn.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Villages matter little (few terminals). Wealthy Village is valued for its Loot (+$3 and +Buy), and Stables is the better $5 gain until you can reliably play three Treasures.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Exorcist is underrated here: trashing Silvers or spent Kitsunes into Imps gives Lab-like draw. "Quite the underrated card." (JNails)
- **R7** [supporting] (single expert) Too many Silvers hurt; after the Curses are gone, Kitsune is roughly two extra Silvers, so it's not a priority.
- **R8** [supporting] (single expert) Endgame under Progress: topdecked Provinces dud the next turn, so win in one turn or buy Province plus Stables. Annex is a free Duchy on the final turn.
- **R9** [supporting] (result) Rope, Stables and Church run low, so a pile-out (with Estates and Annex) is a live ending.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Gaining an extra Estate in the hope of drawing it while Progress is active: it goes onto the deck and you draw it at once. JNails thought Fircoal's Estate gain was a mistake.
- **T2** (single expert) Treating Kitsune as a priority after the Curses run out; it clogs the deck as Silver-like cards.

## Disagreements
- Opening: JNails would have opened Church/Exorcist and listed Rope only third among the $4s;
  both players opened Rope with Church.
- Paolo99 doubted Tea House; JNails thought it could replace Wealthy Village once you have enough
  +Buy, but called it low priority.
