# Key: Making Luck ep. 117/118: Emissary, League of Bankers, Amulet, Coin of the Realm, Wild Hunt, Odysseys

## Expert summary
First look: build a lot. Militia matters, and there's draw (Emissary, Wild
Hunt), villages (Coin of the Realm, judged better than Native Village) and
payload (Expand, Candlestick Maker, Wild Hunt points). Both opened double
Amulet to thin fast, picking up Coins of the Realm on the Amulet hands. They
preferred Wild Hunt for draw, dismissed League of Bankers as unreachable,
and doubted the Odysseys.

After testing, the big finding is that Emissary beats Wild Hunt "way, way,
way". Once you draw your deck, you gain a card and play Emissary to draw it
(it shuffles, so it triggers), often twice a turn, and a third time when
Militia or Old Map put something in the discard pile. Each trigger gives 2
Favors. By turns 10-12 a deck can hold about 12 Favors, so League of Bankers
pays +$3 or more at the start of every Buy phase, growing fast. Old Map turned
out useful as a non-terminal way to set up Emissary. Voyage turns improve
because the League money needs no cards played, and Expand works with Voyage.
Being non-terminal also matters, so you don't need four Coins of the Realm
in play. Take an Emissary over trashing a Copper a bit earlier than instinct
says, though not over trashing an Estate. Militia was wanted but not a
priority. Native Village, Bureaucrat and Desperation went essentially unused.
Candlestick Maker came late for +Buy. Amulet luck on turns 3-4 decided only
one tested game, but small early edges snowball.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (result) Emissary is the main draw, not Wild Hunt, and builds to repeated Emissary triggers per turn (gain a card, play Emissary to draw it).
- **R2** [core] (result) League of Bankers becomes major payload via Emissary's Favors (about 12 Favors by turns 10-12 means +$3 or more every turn).
- **R3** [core] (consensus) Opens Amulet/Amulet and thins fast, picking up Coins of the Realm early.
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Coin of the Realm is the village, preferred over Native Village.
- **R5** [supporting] (result) Old Map (Odysseys) or Militia helps trigger Emissary by putting cards in the discard pile; Voyage benefits from League money.
- **R6** [supporting] (consensus) Expand is part of the payload; Candlestick Maker for +Buy later.
- **R7** [supporting] (result) Militia is good but not a priority over drawing cards.

## Traps
- **T1** (result) Treating League of Bankers as irrelevant because Emissary is the only Liaison. This was the hosts' own pre-game error.
- **T2** (result) Wild Hunt as the main draw.
- **T3** (result) Native Village, Bureaucrat and Desperation went essentially unused in testing.

## Disagreements
- Pre-game, one host floated Emissary/League of Bankers and wanted to lean
  into Expand. The other stuck with Wild Hunt and one Expand. Testing sided
  with Emissary.
- How aggressively to trash vs buy Emissaries early is "a complex decision" depending on deck size.

## Grader notes
- The tested result overturned both hosts' first impressions. An analysis
  that finds the Emissary/League engine deserves high marks. One that picks
  Wild Hunt gets partial credit on R3-R4 only.
