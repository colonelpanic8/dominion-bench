# Key: Horton Winter 2018 table 1: Farmland, Secret Passage, Vagrant, Ritual

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, Winter 2018 Tournament Summary](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-winter-2018-tournament-summary/):
  the designer's write-up of a fifth designed kingdom, used in the Swiss rounds rather than the
  finals, so many players saw it.

## Expert summary
Horton designed this board to be fun rather than to have one answer. There is no real draw
apart from Imp (from Devil's Workshop) and no village apart from Necropolis, but many cards
work well together. He describes two packages. First, Secret Passage and Forum make Vassal
and Vagrant good: set up a card with Secret Passage, play Vagrant, then filter with another
Secret Passage or Forum, and stack Vassals onto non-terminals. Second, the Shelters can't
really be trashed, but Farmland (trashing Hovel and Overgrown Estate with one buy) and Rats
get value from them, and Farmland also works as a narrow Province-buying Silver or a good
Ritual target. Forum pulls toward greening early, while Ritual rewards building first and
then scoring big. His opening is Moneylender/Market Square from a 4/3, happy to gain an early
Gold if they collide. He'd skip early Silver and go next for Devil's Workshop and lots of
Secret Passages.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Notes there is no real draw (only Imp) and no village beyond Necropolis, so this isn't a draw engine board.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Secret Passage (with Forum) sets up Vagrant and Vassal; Secret Passage is bought in bulk.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Opens Moneylender/Market Square on 4/3; the collision trashes a Copper and gains a Gold.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Devil's Workshop is an early priority after the opening.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Farmland and Rats get value from the Shelters, which can't otherwise be removed (one Farmland can turn Hovel and Overgrown Estate into something useful).
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Ritual (on Farmland and other cards) rewards building, which pulls against Forum's early greening.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Prioritizing early Silver: Horton says it isn't important here.

## Disagreements
- Players tried many different things at the tournament; Horton gives no result.

## Grader notes
- Designed for fun; many plans are reasonable. Grade on recognizing the no-draw structure and the
  Secret Passage/Vagrant/Vassal and Shelter/Farmland/Rats packages.
- Farmland and Forum are pre-errata here (on-buy). Under current wording Farmland triggers on
  gain; the analysis may use either.
