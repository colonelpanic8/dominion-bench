# Key: DS Annotated Game #7: University, Wharf, Bazaar, Transmute, Lookout

## Expert summary
Kemps notes the attacks (Cutpurse) are mild, Wharf is a top $5 and is helped by
Bazaar and University for +Actions, and the board has +Buy (Wharf) and
trashing-for-VP (Transmute), so the game can swing late. He opened Silver/Potion
for University and built a large action deck (University, Wharf, Bazaar,
Conspirator, Lookout). spazard1 opened Cutpurse/Lookout, added Trading Post,
Bazaars, a Wharf and Gold, thinned hard and bought Provinces earlier. Kemps won
40–33 (3 Provinces, 7 Duchies) because Transmute, which he hadn't planned on,
turned Estates into Gold and later Universities into Duchies. He judged Ironworks
and Scrying Pool dead here (Scrying Pool clashes with Lookout), and says the
thin Lookout deck got worse late, e.g. trashing a Bazaar.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert + result) Wharf is the key $5, supported by Bazaar and University for Actions.
- **R2** [core] (result + single expert) Transmute matters: Estates into Gold early and Action cards into Duchies late give a Duchy lunge that won the game.
- **R3** [supporting] (result) Lookout/Trading Post thinning builds a fast, thin money-ish deck that takes Provinces early.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Cutpurse is a weak attack; the game won't be decided by attacks.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Plan the ending: +Buy from Wharf and Transmute's Duchies make late swings possible, so end it quickly if ahead.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Ironworks and Scrying Pool: dead cards here (Scrying Pool anti-synergizes with Lookout).
- **T2** (single expert) Adding terminal Wharves without enough villages: Kemps' collision cost him a turn.

## Disagreements
- Kemps thought his larger deck had the edge over the thin Lookout deck; the
  editor (theory) wasn't sure, crediting the Duchy lunge instead.
- Kemps says Alchemy shouldn't be ignored, but his University opening isn't shown
  to be better than skipping Potion.
