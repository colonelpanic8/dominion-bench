# Key: Horton Summer 2017 finals game 3: Mandarin, Fortune, Dominate

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, Summer 2017 Tournament Summary](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-summer-2017-tournament-summary/):
  the designer's write-up of a 3-player finals kingdom with a playtested "golden deck".

## Expert summary
There is no thinning and the only draw is Diplomat, so an engine here is unreliable: without
heavy overbuilding Horton's best one fired every other turn. Dominate is the reason to build
at all, and the natural deck is Warehouse/Diplomat/Throne Room with Bakers, trying to hit
$14 often, with Fortune helping. Mine is very good: Baker lets you open it with another good
card, and with no thinning, upgrading Treasure is what you want. At the tournament players
built decks that Dominated a few times, and whoever Dominated most won. The hidden best line,
which nobody found, is a golden deck: with Fortune, Gold, Gold, Gold and Copper in hand, buy
Dominate and a Mandarin (Fortune gives the +Buy). Gaining Mandarin topdecks those five
Treasures, so you Dominate every turn, with the option to double Province to end the game. Mine, Warehouse/Diplomat, Gladiator
(Gold when gaining Fortune) and Throne Room assemble it. Horton set it up by turn 8 in
playtesting and nearly always by turn 10, and it beat every engine he tried.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Identifies the Mandarin golden deck: Fortune + 3 Gold + Copper, buy Dominate plus a Mandarin, and Mandarin topdecks the Treasures so you Dominate every turn.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Notes that the draw is weak (Diplomat only) and there's no thinning, so a conventional engine is unreliable.
- **R3** [core] (designer) Dominate is the payload worth building toward.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Mine is strong here: Baker's Coffers let you open it, and upgrading Treasure matters with no thinning.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Warehouse/Diplomat is the core that finds the five Treasures; Throne Room helps.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Gladiator helps by giving a Gold when you gain the Fortune.
- **R7** [supporting] (designer) The golden deck can also double-Province to end the game, and was set up by turns 8-10 in playtesting.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) A big Action engine (Warehouse/Diplomat/Throne/Baker) as the main plan: it fires about every other turn and lost to the golden deck in playtesting.

## Disagreements
- At the tournament nobody found the golden deck; whoever Dominated most won (result).
