# Key: 2026 Online Championship final, game 4: Flag Bearer, Ritual, Jester, Crop Rotation

## Sources
- [Lemonspawn and Sharur, neutral commentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3oj4O-lLaH4), 1:15:30-1:32:00.
  Quotes are attributed to "commentator".
- Result, read from the end screen: agoo03 (player 1) won 45-32 when the Provinces ran out, 19
  turns to 18. agoo03 had 6 Provinces, 3 Estates, 8 VP tokens from Ritual and 2 Curses, with
  12 Coppers, Silver, Gold, Jester, 2 Ghost Town, 2 Flag Bearer and Tactician. yurikamome had
  2 Provinces, 3 Duchies, 3 Estates, 12 VP tokens and 4 Curses, with 10 Coppers, 3 Jester,
  2 Ghost Town, 2 Flag Bearer and 2 Tactician. Neither bought Laboratory, Alley, Aristocrat or
  First Mate.

## Expert summary
The commentators expected a draw deck of Alleys, Ghost Towns, Aristocrats and Laboratories, with
Jester as the attack, Jewelled Egg or Aristocrat for +Buy, and no way to thin. They also predicted
Ritual on Flag Bearer: Ritual trashes the Flag Bearer for 4 VP, and trashing it retakes the Flag.
The players went straight there. yurikamome bought Flag Bearer on turn 1, agoo03 took the Flag
back, both bought Crop Rotation, and the game became a long fight over the Flag. Each Ritual
added a Curse, Jesters spread Coppers and Curses, and neither deck ever built. The commentators
called it degenerate: contesting the Flag is often correct, but it makes both decks worse, and
if one player had ignored Flag Bearer both would probably have built. agoo03 came out ahead on
money and Provinces.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) Ritual on Flag Bearer is the main combination: 4 VP per Ritual, and trashing the Flag Bearer takes the Flag back. Both players did it repeatedly.
- **R2** [core] (result + commentator) Expect a fight over the Flag that slows both decks; contesting is often right, but it costs real tempo. "it's correct to contest the flag in a lot of cases." (commentator)
- **R3** [supporting] (consensus) The build, if anyone builds: Laboratory, Alley, Ghost Town and Aristocrat for draw, with no trashing available. "I think that you're going to get a bunch of alleys, ghost towns, aristocrats, and labs." (commentator)
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Jester is the attack, junking with Curses and Coppers (and handing out Flag Bearers).
- **R5** [supporting] (commentator + result) Crop Rotation is a good buy in a green-heavy slog. Both players bought it early. "Crop rotation sounds amazing." (commentator)
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Jewelled Egg is the cheap +Buy; Ritualing it gives a Loot, but adds a Curse as well.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Letting the Flag war run indefinitely instead of switching to building (Laboratories) once the decks are bad. "I think I would stop flagging at some point and just start buying labs" (commentator)

## Disagreements
- Whether to fight for the Flag at all: the commentators would let the opponent keep it for a while
  and build; both players fought from turn 1.
- First Mate with Tactician, or First Mate playing several Flag Bearers, was raised by spectators;
  the commentators said spending a gain on First Mate was a significant cost here. Neither player
  bought one.

## Grader notes
- The commentators' build line was never tested in this game, so treat R3 as a reasonable read,
  not a proven plan. A good analysis should name the Flag Bearer/Ritual interaction (R1) and
  discuss the cost of fighting over it.
