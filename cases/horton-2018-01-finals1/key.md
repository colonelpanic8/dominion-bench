# Key: Horton Winter 2018 finals 1: Sea Hag, Tragic Hero, Rogue, Exorcist

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, Winter 2018 Tournament Summary](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-winter-2018-tournament-summary/):
  the designer's write-up of a 2-player finals kingdom; it was played twice.

## Expert summary
Horton usually thinks Sea Hag can be ignored in 2-player games, but not here: you can deal
with the Curses, yet the attack slows the opponent enough that you need to play it. Exorcist
can later turn Sea Hag into an Imp. +Buy is scarce: Tragic Hero can't give its +Buy and stay
alive with Actions left, and Armory-gaining Silvers just to trash them to Spice Merchant
feels bad. So the intended trick is Rogue gaining Tragic Heroes back from the trash, with a
Ghost (from Exorcist) playing a Tragic Hero twice each turn for two Buys while it dies again.
Its death can gain a Plunder you draw the same turn. Key splits are Encampment/Plunder, the
Curses and Fairgrounds. His opening is a 5/2 with Ball (gaining Sea Hag and Exorcist), then
paying off the -$1 token on turn 2. Without stacking he'd open Sea Hag/Silver and get
Exorcist and Spice Merchant soon. In the two games played, one player went for Sea Hag and
the other didn't, and the player who took all the Curses lost.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer + result) Plays Sea Hag: the junking is worth it here even though the board has answers. "this is not one of them" (Adam Horton, on boards where Sea Hag can be ignored)
- **R2** [core] (designer) Notes +Buy is scarce and Tragic Hero's +Buy comes at the cost of the card.
- **R3** [core] (designer) Rogue regains Tragic Heroes from the trash, and an Exorcist Ghost plays Tragic Hero twice for two Buys; Tragic Hero's gain can be a Plunder.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Exorcist turns Sea Hag (later) or junk into Spirits such as Imp/Ghost.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Opening: 5/2 with Ball to gain Sea Hag and Exorcist, or Sea Hag/Silver on 4/3, then Exorcist and Spice Merchant early.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Contests the key splits: Encampment/Plunder, Curses and Fairgrounds.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer + result) Ignoring Sea Hag: the player who took all the Curses lost.
- **T2** (designer) Armory-gaining Silvers to trash to Spice Merchant for +Buy: Horton calls it bad.

## Disagreements
- Horton says the opening is extremely tough to figure out; his Ball opening is a preference.
