# Key: KotW 5/30/2021: Remodel, Magpie, Watchtower, Mountebank

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/novrbd/), 2021-05-30, 8 comments.
  Doomburrito, EphesosX and Hasselhof88 discussed; no known top player and no game report.

## Expert summary
The three commenters agree on remodeling Estates into Magpies, playing King's Court
on Magpie for draw, and taking Mountebank. EphesosX and Hasselhof88 rate Watchtower
highly because of the gains and junk: it trashes the Coppers from Banquet and from
Mountebank, and topdecks Remodeled cards (EphesosX also notes topdecking to trigger
Coin of the Realm, then drawing to 6). Hasselhof88 calls it "a great watchtower
board": lots of gains, Curses and no +Buy. He opens Watchtower/Remodel on 4/3 or
Coin of the Realm/Mountebank on 5/2, remodels Coppers into Coin of the Realm, and
ends with King's Court/Artisan for Duchies and then Triumph. EphesosX adds Triumph
after a King's Court-Magpie turn that gains 3-4 Magpies.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Remodels Estates into Magpies; Magpie (with King's Court) is the main draw. (all three)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Watchtower is strong here: it trashes Banquet's Coppers and Mountebank junk and topdecks gains. "Definitely a great watchtower board." (Hasselhof88; EphesosX agrees)
- **R3** [supporting] (majority) Takes Mountebank early. "I wouldn't underestimate a really early Mountebank." (EphesosX)
- **R4** [supporting] (majority) Triumph as late VP after turns with many gains (King's Court-Magpie, Artisan for Duchies).
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Opens Watchtower/Remodel on 4/3; Coin of the Realm/Mountebank on 5/2. (Hasselhof88)
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Banquet gets an early $5 attack while Watchtower handles its Coppers. (EphesosX, Doomburrito)

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Chariot Race as a main card; Hasselhof88 thought it too slow.

## Disagreements
- Doomburrito doubted Banquet/Watchtower beyond an early attack; EphesosX and
  Hasselhof88 valued them.
- How many Watchtowers (Hasselhof88: 1-2).
