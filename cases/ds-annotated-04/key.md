## Expert summary
A plain board: limited trashing (Trading Post), Witch, good money cards (Envoy,
Bank), no +Buy and no end-game accelerators, Native Village the only +Actions.
Captain_Frisk wants Witch as early as possible and opens Warehouse/Quarry (as
first player) to hit $5 and find Witch often. He adds Trading Post early for
Estates, buys Silver over a second Witch to keep enough money, and greens into a
fast Province race. yaron ignored Witch for Envoy/Bank money with Trading Post
for Curses; he took five Curses and lost 37–21 in 15 turns. Both players agreed
afterwards that ignoring Witch was the mistake.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert + result) Witch is the priority $5 and must be taken; ignoring it for money loses. "Beware of the Witch." (Captain_Frisk)
- **R2** [core] (single expert + result) Witch plus money: few Actions, a fast game (about 15 turns), expect a 4/4 or 5/3 Province split since there's no +Buy.
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Opens with cards that hit $5 on turns 3–4 (Warehouse/Quarry here, or Silver) to get Witch immediately.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Trading Post only pays if bought early, to clear Estates.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Don't overbuy Actions: prefer money (Silver, Gold, Bank) over a second Witch or extra terminals.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Watchtower is a bit better than usual as Witch defence, but still not a priority.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus of both players + result) Envoy/Bank money that ignores Witch and relies on Trading Post to clear Curses.
- **T2** (single expert) An early Province before the deck has a couple of Gold-equivalents.

## Disagreements
- Preview commenters raised Warehouse/Counting House, which neither player tried.
