# Key: Horton Galactic 2022 Finals 4 (championship final): Bank, Crystal Ball, Catapult, Canal

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion Galactic Championship Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-galactic-championship-summary/),
  Gen Con, 2022-08-04 to 06. Horton designed and playtested this kingdom; it
  was used for the recorded tournament final. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/16Yun1MG09amRq-PlPe6ilxC3UZL9snIfgrAYmLn5Ovo)
  (the sheet adds Keep, which the blog post omits).

## Expert summary
Bank is great here with many enablers, but the only draw-like card is Haven
and Catapult attacks hand size, so the game is about squeezing value from
every card into your Banks. Transmogrify and Market Square gain Banks;
Crystal Ball thins you quickly, then works like a cantrip with lots of money
in the Buy phase; Secret Passage helps make Banks the last Treasures played;
Astrolabe and Coin of the Realm add Treasures (easy to gain with Canal or
Transmogrify from spare Coppers or Curses); Haven carries cards from earlier
turns into bigger turns. Horton was pleased with how the final played out
but gives no result.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Bank is the main payload; play as many Treasures before Banks as possible.
- **R2** [core] (designer) There's essentially no draw (only Haven), so value per card matters most.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Transmogrify and Market Square gain Banks.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Crystal Ball thins early, then acts as a money cantrip in the Buy phase.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Secret Passage helps line up Banks to be played last.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Astrolabe and Coin of the Realm as extra Treasures, gained cheaply via Canal or Transmogrify.
- **R7** [supporting] (designer) Haven saves cards for a bigger next turn.
- **R8** [supporting] (designer) Catapult is the attack to account for.
