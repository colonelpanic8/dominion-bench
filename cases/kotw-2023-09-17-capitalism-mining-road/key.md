# Key: KotW 18 Sep 2023: Capitalism, Prosper, Mining Road, King's Cache

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/16ld1je/), 2023-09-17.
  EphesosX submitted the kingdom and described its intended combo;
  lechatonnoir gave a general plan; Curebob added Fortune Hunter; gkraigher
  argued an Ironworks rush. No games reported.

## Expert summary
The submitter's key interaction: buy Capitalism (Actions with +$ count as
Treasures during your turns), get enough money to buy Prosper with a Mining
Road in play, and use Mining Road ("when you gain a Treasure, you may play
it") to play another Mining Road gained by Prosper, which plays another
Treasure, for +1 Buy and +$2 each time and possibly a second Prosper. Not
infinite, since each Mining Road's ability works once per turn, but about 26
gains in one turn. lechatonnoir's plan: trash minimally with Priest, rush
Capitalism, then King's Cache, then spam Prospers. Minion's discard-and-
redraw lets you see more than 5 cards; Pendant adds money once you own many
different Treasures; Fortune Hunter can play King's Cache or Minion (now
Treasures) from the deck. gkraigher instead called Ironworks the best card
and a 5/2 bad, opening Ironworks/Silver.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Capitalism + Prosper + Mining Road: Mining Road plays Treasures as they are gained, chaining into more Buys and money. "gaining 26 cards in a single turn is pretty good." (EphesosX)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Buys Capitalism early, then King's Cache, then Prospers. "rush to capitalism, then rush to KC, then spam prospers" (lechatonnoir)
- **R3** [supporting] (single player) Minion's discard-and-draw-4 as a way to see more cards each turn.
- **R4** [supporting] (single player) Pendant grows with many differently named Treasures (from Prosper and Capitalism).
- **R5** [supporting] (single player) Fortune Hunter plays Treasures (King's Cache, Capitalism-Actions) from the top of the deck.
- **R6** [supporting] (single player) Priest for light trashing. (lechatonnoir)

## Traps
- **T1** (majority) Improve on King's Cache to gain Provinces: King's Cache is not an Action, so Improve can't target it. (The_Constant_Liar, lechatonnoir)
- **T2** (single player) Swindler as a way to trash your own cards for Priest: the opponent trashes their own card. (lechatonnoir)

## Disagreements
- gkraigher: Ironworks/Silver opening and a "rush strategy"; nobody else
  engaged with it.
- Whether to Prosper for every Treasure or skip some (Flag Bearer,
  Ironworks).

## Grader notes
- The combo is the submitter's design intent, untested in posted games.
- Under Capitalism, Flag Bearer, Ironworks, Priest, Swindler, Minion and
  Mining Road (all with +$ in their text) count as Treasures during your
  turn, so Prosper can gain them.
