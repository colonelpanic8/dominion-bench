## Sources

  Cincinnati tournament, September 2026. Horton, a top player, designed and
  playtested this kingdom for the semifinals and says he playtested this
  event's kingdoms more than usual. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1E0IUYK9fU2KXZmANMprklRV0caaVj6CqO3ScusjCOvQ).

## Expert summary
Horton built this board so that Transmute, usually a weak card, has a lot
going for it. With Kitsune giving out Curses, Transmute is the only way to
trash cards from your deck, and because it is Reckless it returns to its pile
when discarded from play, so it doesn't clog the deck. The central early
decision is about Panic: once Kitsune's Sun tokens run out, Panic returns
played Treasures to their piles, so players must choose between using
Transmute on Coppers (each trashed Treasure gains more Transmutes) and simply
letting Panic strip the Coppers out. After that strange deck-control phase,
Transmute's synergy with Lurker gives a tactical endgame with many options,
and Highway looms as a threat. He gives no game report.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Transmute is a key card here despite its usual weakness: it is the only way to trash from your own deck against Kitsune's Curses, and Reckless sends it back to the Supply so it doesn't stay in the deck.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Identifies the Panic decision: once Panic is active, played Treasures leave the deck, so trashing Coppers with Transmute to gain more Transmutes competes with just letting Panic remove them.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Kitsune is the Omen that drives Panic and the source of Curses, so Panic arriving is inevitable and must be planned for.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Lurker synergizes with Transmute in the endgame (e.g. moving Action cards between Supply and trash), creating many tactical options.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Highway is a looming late threat (cost reduction, especially with Panic's +Buys), so piles and endgame timing need watching.

## Traps

## Grader notes
- Horton describes ideas, not a full line. Credit analyses that plan around
  Panic arriving (Treasures returning to their piles, +2 Buys per Treasure
  played) and use Reckless Transmute and Lurker coherently.
- My reading (grader's, not the source's): Reckless makes each Transmute
  play trash two cards, gaining a Gold per Victory card, a Duchy per Action
  and a Transmute per Treasure. Lurker gaining Actions from the trash to feed
  Transmute for Duchies is presumably part of the endgame. Credit these as
  reasonable extras.
