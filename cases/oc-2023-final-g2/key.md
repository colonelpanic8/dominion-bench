# Key: 2023 Online Championship final, game 2: Witch, Graverobber, Patrician, Taskmaster

## Sources
- [Dominionstreams broadcast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dj3S4ncnupE), 6:55-12:30.
  Commentary by xyrix and drsteelhammer, both long-time top players. agoo03 was
  first player; syamii4 resigned around turn 14. Kingdom and deck details read
  from the video.

## Expert summary
The commentators saw plenty of engine pieces (Walled Village, Mountain Village,
Patrician, Forge, Taskmaster) but little in the way of gaining: only
Graverobber. They dismissed the Tunnel tricks (an opponent's Pillage or a
Doctor overpay can discard it for Gold) as unreliable. Doctor appeared in the
openings, and syamii4 fell behind early on draws the commentators called
unlucky ("the doctor lock was brutal"). Both players built with Walled Villages and Witches, and the
Curse pile was empty by turn 13. agoo03 added Forge and two Graverobbers, which
the commentators read as setting up Emporium gains (2 VP each with 5+ Actions
in play). They agreed that Graverobber makes Pillage repeatable, since it can
regain Pillage from the trash. syamii4 resigned well behind.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (result) Witch is the key attack; both finalists played Witch with Walled Village, and the Curses ran out by turn 13.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) The board has engine pieces but weak gaining: Graverobber is the only gainer, which limits payload.
- **R3** [supporting] (consensus) Graverobber + Pillage: Graverobber regains the trashed Pillage, so Pillage can be played over and over. "you have a grave robber right so you can get it back every time" (commentator)
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert + result) Graverobbers plus Forge and Patrician set up gaining Emporiums with 5+ Actions in play for VP.
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus) Walled Village is a good buy here (commentators would buy it at $4); Taskmaster is pleasant if the deck can keep hitting $5.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Building around Tunnel: it gains Gold only if discarded outside Clean-up, which here needs the opponent's Pillage or a Doctor overpay. "neither of these really seem very good to me" (commentator)

## Disagreements
- A commentator was tempted by Banquet; nobody followed it up.

## Grader notes
- Witch is barely mentioned in the commentary; R1 rests on what both players did (frames show both playing Witches and an empty Curse pile). My view: on a board with only one trasher-ish gainer (Graverobber), Forge and Doctor as the junk answers, Witch is the obvious priority, and an analysis that misses it should not score well.
- syamii4 lost mainly to an early bad draw, so don't read the result as evidence against their build.
