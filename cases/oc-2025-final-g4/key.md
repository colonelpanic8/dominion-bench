## Sources
- [JNails and jonts, neutral commentary, part 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AWDONuwsN74), 0:00-17:00.
  Speakers alternate quickly in the captions, so they are cited as "commentator".
- [VRSmith's own commentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kWZyqrCn-ws), 1:17:00-1:35:30.
- Match: VRSmith (player 1) won 9-7 on piles on turn 11.

## Expert summary
No trashing, but villages and draw, and $5 is the key price. Trappers' Lodge lets you spend
Favors (5 each, thanks to Importer) to put gained cards on your deck, which shapes the opening:
topdeck a Silver on turn 1 to guarantee $5, then topdeck a Barge, or open Importer. The
commentators' plan was Barges and Bazaars until you can buy Invasion ($10: gain a Duchy, an
Action onto your deck and a Loot to play), which both they and VRSmith named the payload. Bandit
Fort makes each Silver and Gold worth -2 VP, so keep Treasure low and expect low scores. Tools
copies anything in play, including itself, so Tools and Barge piles drain and a three-pile
ending comes fast. VRSmith did the Silver-then-Barge topdeck opening, added First Mate and Vault
earlier than the commentators liked, plus Bazaars and Imps, hit Invasion, and ended the game on
piles with Tools gaining Tools while ahead by two. The commentators doubted Druid and Devil's
Workshop.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) Invasion is the main payload; building to $10 hands matters. "I think invasion is definitely going to be like the long-term payload." (VRSmith)
- **R2** [core] (consensus + result) Barge (draw and +Buy) and Bazaar (village) are the core of the deck.
- **R3** [supporting] (consensus + result) Uses Trappers' Lodge Favors to topdeck early gains: Silver on turn 1 to guarantee $5, then Barge (or open Importer for a guaranteed $5 gain).
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Bandit Fort makes Silver and Gold cost points: keep few of them, and expect a low-scoring game where Duchies and Estates matter.
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus + result) Tools can gain Tools and drains piles; tracks the three-pile ending, which decided this game.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert + result) First Mate with Vault (discard everything, draw back to six) is a usable later-game draw package; one Vault also turns Estates into $.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Druid: commentators thought it was probably worse than nothing with Barge already giving +Buy.
- **T2** (single expert) Devil's Workshop for Gold: "it's just a laboratory", and Gold is -2 VP under Bandit Fort.
- **T3** (single expert) Buying an untopdecked Silver on turn 1; a commentator noted Importer or a topdecked Silver guarantees the $5.

## Disagreements
- The commentators called VRSmith's early First Mate a mistake (Bazaar first) and said First Mate
  and Vault belonged around turns 11-12; VRSmith used First Mate/Vault from the midgame and won.
- Commentators thought VRSmith's final Invasion buy was unnecessary and risky (a bad Loot could
  have cost the lead); the game-ending line through Tools gaining Tools worked.
