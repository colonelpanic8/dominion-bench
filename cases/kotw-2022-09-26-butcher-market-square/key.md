# Key: KotW 9/25/2022: Butcher, Market Square, Forager, Way of the Owl

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/xo3kuj/), 2022-09-26.
  GonffzCheeze (regular) and several casual commenters backed a Butcher/
  Market Square/Forager deck; willowhelmiam argued Storeroom or Horn of Plenty
  payload; presumably_alterable and SignError added the Owl/Demand line.

## Expert summary
The majority deck thins with Forager, gets Gold from Market Square reacting
to trashes, and uses Butcher both to trash and to turn Golds into Provinces,
with Fishing Village for Actions and Way of the Owl (draw until 6 in hand,
mostly on Horses from Supplies) for draw. GonffzCheeze: "This is a Butcher
board. Everything else is just supporting it." willowhelmiam agreed on
Forager thinning and Owl draw-to-6 with Fishing Villages and Supplies, but
wanted Storeroom payload or a Horn of Plenty megaturn depending on the game.
GonffzCheeze replied it is not a Horn board and greening comes fast. With
Shelters, Necropolis can be played as Owl early, and Demand can gain a
Forager onto the deck.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Butcher is the main payload, trashing Golds (from Market Square) into Provinces. "can butcher golds into provinces!" (NovaRogue)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Market Square gains Gold when your cards are trashed by Forager or Butcher.
- **R3** [core] (majority) Forager thins early.
- **R4** [supporting] (majority) Way of the Owl is the draw (draw to 6), played mostly on Horses from Supplies; Fishing Village for Actions.
- **R5** [supporting] (single player) Necropolis played as Owl early, and Demand gains a Forager to the deck. (presumably_alterable, SignError)
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Opens Forager/Forager or Butcher/Supplies (willowhelmiam) or Butcher/Forager (sgaf).

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) A Horn of Plenty megaturn plan. "No way this is a HoP board." (GonffzCheeze)
- **T2** (single expert) Baker, probably not needed. (GonffzCheeze)

## Disagreements
- willowhelmiam: Butcher payload does little before greening and needs
  terminal space; Storeroom or Horn may be better depending on splits.
  GonffzCheeze: greening comes fast and Butcher is the board.

## Grader notes
- Thin and untested. Merchant and Farming Village (first edition) went
  undiscussed.
