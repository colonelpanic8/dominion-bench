# Key: KotW 7/18/2021: Alms, Cemetery, Ranger, Rebuild, Dungeon

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/on7ees/), 2021-07-18, 8 comments.
  mountebanker gave an opening and engine plan; treelorf (strong regular) assessed the
  board's payload; saapato, cyanraichu and loge212 added short takes.

## Expert summary
Agreed: Alms gaining Cemetery is very good thinning (Alms gains a card up to $4 when
you have no Treasures in play, and Cemetery trashes up to 4 cards when gained), and
Village plus Ranger give strong deck control. The open question is payload. treelorf
sees "strong deck control ... but no payload" and expects an engine using Dungeon and
Rebuild as payload and pile control; buying Treasures one at a time is too painful.
mountebanker thinks there is enough to build for double Provinces rather than
Rebuild/Dungeon. He opens Ranger with Alms for Cemetery (trashing 2 Coppers and 2
Estates) on 4/3, hopes for an early Ghost on the second shuffle, and reads the
kingdom's theme as Pilgrimage and Ranger setting up each other via the Journey token.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Alms gaining Cemetery as the main thinning. "Alms cemetery is very good trashing." (mountebanker; loge212, treelorf agree)
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Notes the board is short of payload despite strong deck control, and takes Rebuild/Dungeon payload or pile control seriously. (treelorf)
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Opens Ranger + Alms (for Cemetery) on 4/3. (mountebanker)
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Ranger and Pilgrimage interact through the Journey token. (mountebanker)
- **R5** [supporting] (majority) Village + Ranger as the draw engine. (treelorf, saapato)

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Building economy by buying Treasures one at a time. "Buying treasures up one at a time seems too painful" (treelorf)

## Disagreements
- Engine to double Province (mountebanker, saapato with Witches) vs Rebuild/Dungeon-
  flavored payload (treelorf, cyanraichu).

## Grader notes
- Thin and untested. Witch, Library, Cobbler and Fortress were barely discussed;
  credit a Witch-based attack as beyond-key.
