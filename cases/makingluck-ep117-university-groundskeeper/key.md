# Key: Making Luck ep. 117, kingdom 6: University, Groundskeeper, Collection, Highwayman, Goatherd

## Sources
- [Making Luck ep. 117 "Rapid-Fire Kingdom Analysis 2"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_6QemY1KJtc&t=2859s),
  2022-09-03, 47:40-54:30. Adam Horton and Wandering Winder, first look, no playtesting.

## Expert summary
University is the centerpiece. Both hosts open Potion, one specifying
Potion/Goatherd, to get Universities, which provide villages and gain
Actions. Goatherd is the only trashing and matters because the draw is tight.
The big points come from Groundskeeper and Collection, though not much from
the two together. Groundskeeper is the easier source with University gains
(gain Groundskeepers, then gain Victory cards for extra VP). A Pirate money
deck is dismissed because of those points and Highwayman. One host plans
one or two Highwaymen and then Witch's Hut, and the other agrees. In a mirror
the Groundskeeper split matters. Pirate can also gain Potions and Collections
and so offers some control over the University pile. Cartographer won't be
bought. Cardinal may matter late once piles are low.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Opens Potion (with Goatherd) to get University early; University is the key card.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Scores mainly with Groundskeeper (and Collection), not just Provinces; contests the Groundskeeper split.
- **R3** [supporting] (consensus) Goatherd is the only trashing and is worth having because draw is tight.
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Rejects a Pirate money deck because of the alternative-VP payoff and the attacks.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) One or two Highwaymen, then Witch's Hut, as the draw/attack terminals.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Pirate as a later money source that can also gain Potions or Collections.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Cartographer: "You know what I think won't come up here? Cartographer." (a host)
- **T2** (consensus) Trying to play Collection early to score off University gains: the timing doesn't work.

## Disagreements
- One host would prioritize the Groundskeeper split over money in a mirror;
  the other would take a Pirate or two first.
- Apothecary before Universities: one host considered one; the other came
  around to skipping it because the Potion often lines up with too little money.

## Grader notes
- Credit any plan that opens Potion for University and scores with
  Groundskeeper; the Pirate and Apothecary details are secondary.
