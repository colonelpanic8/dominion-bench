# Key: Making Luck ep. 117, kingdom 3: Horn of Plenty, Highway, Royal Blacksmith, Gang of Pickpockets

## Sources
- [Making Luck ep. 117 "Rapid-Fire Kingdom Analysis 2"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_6QemY1KJtc&t=1260s),
  2022-09-03, 21:00-30:20. Adam Horton and Wandering Winder, first look, no playtesting.

## Expert summary
The hosts build big rather than play money. Horn of Plenty with Highway looks
"pretty nuts" as payload. There is draw in Royal Blacksmith with Blessed
Village (and Broker as a backup village), though the thinning is slow. One
host argues money can't keep up with the attacks (Gang of Pickpockets'
discard-to-4, Clerk, Taxman) and the extra points from Tunnel and Conquest,
and keeps Storeroom/Tunnel/Conquest as a fallback. After going back and forth
between Clerk, Taxman, Transmogrify and Broker, both settle on opening
Broker, probably with a Silver. Clerk loses value because Gang of Pickpockets
already shrinks hands. Taxman comes a little later to clear Coppers, which
helps Royal Blacksmith, and with Highways in play Taxman can turn Coppers
into Horns of Plenty. Transmogrify and Highway turn out not to synergize. A
few Royal Blacksmiths and three or so villages are enough, since Highway lets
the deck stay thin. Fleet is a minor tool against a pile-out in a Horn
mirror. Whoever cashes in Horns for points first probably wins.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Builds a draw deck (Royal Blacksmith + Blessed Village/Broker), not money. "I'm not interested in doing money here." (a host)
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Horn of Plenty is the main payload, helped by Highway cost reduction.
- **R3** [core] (consensus) Opens Broker (likely Broker/Silver).
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Gang of Pickpockets weakens Clerk's (and Taxman's) attack, so Clerk isn't the opener.
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus) Gets one Taxman after a while to trash Coppers; with Highways in play, Taxman can gain Horns of Plenty from Coppers.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) The attacks plus Tunnel and Conquest points mean money can't keep up; Storeroom/Tunnel/Conquest is a fallback if Horns don't come together.
- **R7** [supporting] (consensus) Fleet matters only as protection against a pile-out late in a Horn mirror.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Opening Tunnel/Tunnel against Gang of Pickpockets (raised as a joke).
- **T2** (consensus) Counting on Highway + Transmogrify synergy: Highway must be in play when Transmogrify is called, which this board can't arrange.

## Disagreements
- Highway: one host wants several (thin deck, Taxman-into-Horn trick); the
  other is unsure, since Highway cuts against buying Royal Blacksmiths.
- Early on one host wanted to open Taxman or Transmogrify; both converged on Broker.

## Grader notes
