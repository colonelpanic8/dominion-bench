## Sources

  Captions don't mark speakers, so unnamed quotes are "commentator".
- Match: Dominion World Cup 2026 final, match 1. kojii (player 1) won to tie the match 3-3.

## Expert summary
There was no real pre-game read; the commentators judged the plays as they came. Opening: with
Pouch's +Buy, Amass ($2: gain an Action costing up to $5 if you have no Actions in play) can be
bought twice on a $4 hand, so the $4 hand matters. kojii opened Village Green, Maroon and
Mastermind; Fircoal opened just Maroon and Mastermind, which JNails called greedy. It paid off when
Mastermind on turn 3 set up a triple Maroon on turn 4. Mastermind is the key card: triple-playing
Maroon, Wayfarer or another Mastermind. The main payload is Invasion at $10: it gains a Duchy, an
Action onto your deck and a Loot that you play, which usually pays back about $3 and often a Buy.
Repeated Invasions empty the Duchies and threaten a pile-out with Wayfarer and Mastermind.
Village Green is the village, and discard effects (Raze, an opponent's Sword) play it for free.
JNails thought Militia was poor against Mastermind draw and Village Green, and that Barbarian
competes with Mastermind at $5. Broker is a strong late card: trash Duchies or a spare Mastermind
for money or draw on the last turns. kojii triple-played a Mastermind to set up a huge next turn
while keeping a points lead, then won with Invasions, Brokers and Farmland gains for points.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) Mastermind is the key engine card; triple-playing Maroon, Wayfarer or another Mastermind drives the deck.
- **R2** [core] (consensus + result) Invasion at $10 is the main payload: Duchy plus an Action plus a Loot per buy, and it naturally threatens a Duchy pile-out.
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Amass with Pouch's +Buy gains two $5 Actions on a $4 opening hand.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert + result) Maroon (trash for +2 Cards per type) is the trashing and draw; Mastermind on Maroon trashes three cards early.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Village Green is the village; discards from Raze or the opponent's Sword play it for free.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Broker is a strong endgame card, trashing Duchies, Provinces or spare Masterminds for money or draw.
- **R7** [supporting] (result) Farmland converts cards into VP on the final turns.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Militia: weak against Mastermind draw, and Village Green counters it.
- **T2** (single expert) Barbarian: at $5 it competes with Mastermind, which is better wherever you'd want Barbarian.
