# Key: 2023 Online Championship final, game 4: Livery, Ranger, Upgrade, Way of the Ox

## Sources
- [Dominionstreams broadcast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dj3S4ncnupE), 38:20-48:00.
  Commentary by xyrix and drsteelhammer, both long-time top players. agoo03 was
  first player and won 44-8 on turn 12 (syamii4 resigned), clinching the 2023
  championship. Kingdom and final decks read from the video.

## Expert summary
The commentators read a rich engine board: Actions are plentiful because
Way of the Ox turns any Action into +2 Actions, and there is draw (Ranger,
Horses, Rabble), trashing (Lookout, Upgrade), Outpost and two attacks. Livery
was the card they kept coming back to: with Livery in play every $4+ gain also
gains a Horse, so it is money, draw and (via Way of the Ox) a village at once
("I would just keep clicking on Livery"). Ranger gives +Buy and a big draw
every other play; Upgrade turns Horses ($3) into $4 cards such as Rangers,
each of which brings another Horse with Livery in play. One commentator would
have opened Upgrade; syamii4 opened Livery and agoo03 Ranger.

agoo03 built five Liveries and six Rangers, put Lost Arts' +Action token on
Ranger, played an Outpost turn with Rangers, gained 22 Horses, and finished
with triple-Province and Fairgrounds turns. syamii4's build (Swamp Shacks,
Hunting Party, fewer Liveries) never got going. Swamp Shacks drew comment:
"a great card in general, I don't think it's that great here".

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) Livery is the key card: it is payload and, through Horses, draw; buy several. agoo03 had five.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Way of the Ox supplies Actions, so terminal space is not the constraint; Horses and Rangers can be played as +2 Actions when needed.
- **R3** [core] (consensus + result) +Buy from Ranger plus Livery turns every $4+ buy or gain into an extra Horse; Ranger is the main +Buy and draw.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert + result) Upgrade Horses into $4 cards (Rangers) with Livery in play for more Horses. "upgrading horses is definitely a way you want to go" (commentator)
- **R5** [supporting] (result) Lost Arts on Ranger; Outpost turns are strong once Rangers are in the deck.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Opening Upgrade or Livery/Ranger; a commentator preferred Upgrade, and both players' openings were defensible.
- **R7** [supporting] (result) Fairgrounds is worthwhile VP in this varied deck alongside Provinces.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Swamp Shacks as the village here; Way of the Ox already gives Actions.
- **T2** (single expert) Hunting Party/Big Money with the attacks: the commentators joked that against it they could "click on whatever I want".

## Disagreements
- Whether Ranger is worth buying when you can Upgrade into it; one commentator thought it was still a really good card.
- Opening Livery vs Upgrade vs Ranger; no strong consensus.

## Grader notes
- Rabble and Idol (Curses on even plays) were mentioned only as present; neither finalist made them central. Credit a reasoned case for Idol junking as beyond-key, not as a miss.
- The margin reflects good draws for agoo03 too; grade the plan, not the 44-8.
