# Key: KotW 10/17/2021: Hermit, Scrying Pool, Contraband, Hunting Grounds

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/qab4vv/), 2021-10-17, 21 comments.
  willowhelmiam and Most_Attitude_9153 gave plans; PathfindingLibrary (strong regular)
  gave the opening and argued with willowhelmiam at length about Contraband and
  greening; iamjacobsparticus floated Big Money.

## Expert summary
All three planners open Hermit/Potion. Hermit thins the Shelters and Estates and turns
into a Madman on turns without buys, and Potion gets Scrying Pool. Draw comes from
Scrying Pools, Hunting Grounds and Fortress. The only +Buy is Contraband, which lets
the opponent name a card you can't buy that turn. With two VP sources (Province and
Vineyard), you take whichever wasn't named. PathfindingLibrary plays about 2
Contrabands and adapts to what is named; he would name Scrying Pool first whenever the
opponent plays Contraband, until late. One or two Hunting Grounds from a single
Contraband turn is plenty. He dismissed Big Money ("does not look remotely playable")
and told willowhelmiam that having your deck in hand is not by itself a signal to green.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Opens Hermit/Potion. (willowhelmiam, Most_Attitude_9153, PathfindingLibrary)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Builds a Scrying Pool engine, with Hunting Grounds and Fortress as further draw and Hermit thinning. (willowhelmiam, Most_Attitude_9153)
- **R3** [core] (consensus) Contraband is the +Buy; plays around the named card by switching between Province and Vineyard. "Play with ~2 Contrabands, and adapt strategy to what the opponent names." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) When the opponent plays Contraband, names Scrying Pool until late in the game. (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) One or two Hunting Grounds is enough. "Get 1 or 2 on a single Contraband turn and that is plenty." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Big Money is not viable here. (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R7** [supporting] (single expert) Madman from Hermit helps hit high price points early. (willowhelmiam)

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Golem. "Golem is bad." (willowhelmiam: worse than Fortress and Hunting Grounds)
- **T2** (single expert) Quarry, since there is little +Buy to use its discount. (willowhelmiam)
- **T3** (single expert) Greening as soon as the deck is drawn. "Your deck being in hand is not an indication to start greening." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **T4** (single expert) Hermit/Hunting Grounds Big Money. "Big money does not look remotely playable" (PathfindingLibrary)

## Disagreements
- Contraband count: about 2 (PathfindingLibrary) vs 1, never two plays per turn (willowhelmiam).
- Monument: Most_Attitude_9153 wanted an engine playing 2-3 Monuments a turn with
  Vineyards as backup; willowhelmiam called Monument weak; PathfindingLibrary implied
  Fortress, Monument and Golem shouldn't be written off.
