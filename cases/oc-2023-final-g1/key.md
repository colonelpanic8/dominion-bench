# Key: 2023 Online Championship final, game 1: Rebuild, Scavenger, Gamble, Merchant Guild

## Sources
- [Dominionstreams broadcast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dj3S4ncnupE), 0:00-6:55.
  Commentary by xyrix and drsteelhammer, both long-time top players. The match is
  the 2023 Dominion Online Championship final, syamii4 (first player) vs agoo03,
  which agoo03 won 4-0. Kingdom read from the video.

## Expert summary
The commentators' first read was that Rebuild is the plan, with Gamble as the
only other real direction ("you could play Rebuild ... there's Gamble but not
much else for the engine"). agoo03 played Rebuild, had two by turn 4, and used
Scavenger to put a Rebuild back on top of the deck: the commentators called
Scavenger/Rebuild "quite an old school combo" and said it "can go extremely
fast". Gamble also played Rebuild off the top of the deck. There is no real
thinning (Trader is slow), so a deck that wants to draw or Gamble through
itself builds slowly. syamii4 tried Gamble with Merchant Guilds, then tried to
switch into Rebuild, which the commentators called a difficult transition.
agoo03 drained the Duchies, used Seize the Day, and won around turn 10, 25-13,
with "almost half" the VP in the kingdom.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) Rebuild is the main plan: Rebuild turns Estates into Duchies and Duchies into Provinces, and the game is short. agoo03 won with it by about turn 10.
- **R2** [core] (single expert + result) Scavenger sets up Rebuild: put the deck into the discard pile and topdeck a Rebuild, so it is played every turn. "the scavenger rebuild is quite an old school combo" (commentator)
- **R3** [supporting] (result) Get a second Rebuild early; agoo03 had two by turn 4, and Rebuild mirrors are decided by who drains the Duchies and Provinces first.
- **R4** [supporting] (result) Gamble can play a topdecked Rebuild as an extra Rebuild play (with +Buy), and Scavenger can stack it for Gamble.
- **R5** [supporting] (result) Seize the Day is worth buying in a short Rebuild game; agoo03 used it near the end.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) There is no real thinning: Trader is slow, and only tolerable in a Gamble deck.

## Traps
- **T1** (result) A Gamble/Merchant Guild deck as the main plan without real thinning: it produced Gamble chains "ending with zero money" (commentator) and lost badly.
- **T2** (single expert + result) Switching into Rebuild late after the opponent already has two; the commentators called it a difficult transition, and it was.

## Disagreements
- One commentator called the Gamble plan "quite skill expressive"; nobody argued it beats Rebuild here.

## Grader notes
- The commentary is short; it doesn't discuss Haven, Night Watchman, Harvest or Figurine. My reading (not the sources'): Night Watchman and Haven also set up Rebuild, and an analysis that builds a Rebuild deck around any reliable way to play Rebuild every turn deserves credit on R2.
- Opening details weren't discussed; don't grade the opening beyond "gets Rebuild fast".
