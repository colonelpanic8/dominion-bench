# Key: KotW 10/31/2021: Pirate Ship, Capitalism, Lurker, Lookout

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/qk2sma/), 2021-10-31, 13 comments.
  EphesosX and SignError gave engine plans; PathfindingLibrary (strong regular) replied
  only "Pirate Ship"; several commenters argued about Capitalism.

## Expert summary
Short and conflicting. EphesosX: trash everything with double Lookout, take 4-6
Lurkers with Minion for draw, Expand Lackeys, get +Buy from Courtier and maybe Pawn,
and force a quick three-pile with Lurker and Expand once ahead. PathfindingLibrary's
whole comment was "Pirate Ship". The Capitalism exchange added that, during the
attacker's turns, Capitalism makes the opponent's +$ Actions (Courtier, Minion, Pawn)
Treasures for Pirate Ship to trash. GonffzCheeze called Capitalism + Pirate Ship the
best combo; presumably_alterable would take a couple of Sculptors first (gaining
Pirate Ships to hand) to win the split. willowhelmiam noted Capitalism + Minion (Minion
playable in the Buy phase). SignError flagged Courtier and Pawn as "Pirate food".

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert) Pirate Ship is a central card here. "Pirate Ship" (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Capitalism is strong, and with Pirate Ship it turns the opponent's +$ Actions into trashable Treasures. (GonffzCheeze, presumably_alterable, ghostyface; TheFunfighter retracted his objection)
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Sculptor gains Pirate Ships to win the split. (presumably_alterable)
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Avoids relying on +$ Actions (Courtier, Pawn) that Pirate Ship can hit; against Pirate Ship, trash your own Coppers fast. (SignError)
- **R5** [supporting] (majority) Lookout/Lurker trashing, and Lurker plus Expand make a quick three-pile possible when ahead. (EphesosX, SignError)
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Capitalism + Minion: Minion can be played in the Buy phase. (willowhelmiam)
