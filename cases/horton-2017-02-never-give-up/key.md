# Key: Horton "Never Give Up": Urchin, Possession, Council Room

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, Dominion: Never Give Up](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-never-give-up/):
  one online game (2017) with Horton's pre-game plan and turn-by-turn commentary. The opponent
  is unnamed.

## Expert summary
Before the game Horton worried about two things. Urchin is the only real trashing, and with
Council Room support you can play several Possessions a turn, so a Possession mirror would
likely be degenerate. Shanty Town/Council Room draw is also unreliable. His plan was to thin
fast with a double Urchin opening, contest Shanty Towns, and build a draw deck that plays 2-3
Possessions a turn, then trash his other useful cards so his deck only plays Possessions. In
the game the opponent built money instead (Golds, Forums, Mercenaries) and took a large lead
while Horton fell behind. Horton notes his own mistakes: a Merchant Guild where a second
Council Room was better, forgetting Hermit would turn into a Madman when he bought nothing,
and trashing his Urchin and Mercenary too early. He also judges the opponent inefficient,
since an earlier Council Room would have made double Provinces possible turns sooner. With
six Provinces, the opponent's deck was full of stop cards and liabilities when Possessed.
Once Horton played Possession into a perfect hand, he could deny the opponent's turns, and
the opponent resigned.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert) Identifies Possession, played several times a turn with Shanty Town/Council Room draw, as the board's dominant threat.
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Opens double Urchin for fast thinning (Urchin is the only real trasher).
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Contests the Shanty Town pile as the village for the draw deck.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Prefers Council Rooms over Merchant Guild for this deck.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Notes Shanty Town/Council Room draw is unreliable.
- **R6** [supporting] (result) A money deck with Golds and Forums led for most of the game; Possession turned it around once the opponent's deck was greened and Possessed.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Merchant Guild in the Possession deck: Horton calls it a mistake that cost a turn or two.
- **T2** (single expert) Trashing your attack and trasher (Urchin, Mercenary) too early to make the deck Possession-only.

## Disagreements
- The opponent's money deck (Gold, Forum, Mercenary) nearly won, and Horton only came back
  after the opponent's mistakes. The game doesn't settle whether Possession beats strong money.

## Grader notes
- Quality C: one game, many mistakes on both sides. Credit analyses that identify Possession
  and plan the Urchin/Shanty Town/Council Room build; a strong money plan is defensible.
- Pre-errata Hermit and Possession rules apply; first-edition Pearl Diver and Explorer.
