# Key: Horton Summer 2018 finals 3: Mandarin, Capital, Legionary, Donate

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, Summer 2018 Tournament Summary](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-summer-2018-tournament-summary/):
  the designer's write-up of a 3-player finals kingdom (Colonies). He is openly unsure about it.

## Expert summary
The board is built around the Mandarin/Capital combo, which Legionary counters hard. To beat
Legionary you need to draw a lot and have many Action cards. City Quarter is hard to get,
so Horton thinks Moat is pretty good here. He expects Capital tricks to be the best payload.
Crown and Mandarin probably still work, but Mandarin doesn't fit the bigger decks unless you
include something with Crown or Night Watchman, and he had hoped Crown would enable Capital
better than Mandarin. He finds the opening hard. Charm or Butcher with a Moat both have a
case, maybe with an Ironworks or Mandarin before Donating, and the build depends on how fast
you want Legionary and what opponents do.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Names Mandarin/Capital as the combo and Legionary as its hard counter.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Expects Capital-based payload in a deck that draws a lot with many Actions.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Moat is good here (City Quarter is hard to get, and Legionary is around).
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Donate after a few opening gains (Charm or Butcher with Moat, maybe Ironworks or Mandarin).
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Legionary is worth getting, with the timing set by the opponents.

- None identified by the source.

## Disagreements
- Horton is unsure whether Crown or Mandarin is the better Capital enabler.
