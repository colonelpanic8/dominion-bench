# Key: Making Luck ep. 17 (ep. 16 kingdom): Duke, Altar, Market Square, Develop, Remodel, Ritual

## Sources
- [Making Luck ep. 17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pn_K22OGgzQ&t=344s),
  2018-03-20, about 5:44-18:40. Adam Horton (a world champion) and Jake,
  after many games, including a recorded session of about three hours
  between Adam and Wandering Winder (another top player).

## Expert summary
The board revolves around Duke/Duchy. Uncontested, Duke/Duchy crushes
whatever the other player does. It has a lot of support: Market Square
floods Golds, Altar and Jester gain $5 cards (Duchies), and Remodel turns
Silvers into Duchies. The rival plan is a Remodel/Market Square deck with
some Peddlers: gain Golds, buy or Remodel into Provinces, and occasionally
Ritual a Peddler for a big VP splash. It is competitive, but only if it
contests Duchies early (with Altar, later remodeled or Ritualed away). Each
player has to read the other and respond: whoever ignores Duchies loses to
an uncontested Duke player, and the Duke player who isn't pressured early
gets time for a second Altar and lots of Silver. Develop/Remodel on 4/3 is
the best opening by a lot. 5/2 is worse here, since you lack a trasher after
the first shuffle. A pure Duke player can open Develop/Silver. Ritual plus
Peddler is anti-synergistic (Peddler's cost drops with Actions in play), but
Ritualing a Peddler for 6-8 VP is still a good tactical play. Trashing the
Curses Ritual gives happened less than expected, because Remodel and
Develop are poor Curse trashers. Contraband and Treasure Map are bad.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Duke/Duchy is the central plan or threat; an uncontested Duke player crushes.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Whoever doesn't go Duke must contest Duchies early, not just snipe two or three late.
- **R3** [core] (consensus) Names the support for Duke: Market Square's Gold gains, Altar and Jester gaining $5s, Remodel Silver into Duchy.
- **R4** [core] (consensus) Opens Develop/Remodel on 4/3; 5/2 is worse here. "I think it's the best opening by a lot." (a host)
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus) The alternative is Remodel + Market Square + Peddlers into Provinces, keeping an Altar to fight for Duchies.
- **R6** [supporting] (consensus) Ritual/Peddler anti-synergy: the full 8 VP needs no Actions played that turn, but Ritualing a Peddler for about 6 VP is still good as a tactic, not a whole strategy.
- **R7** [supporting] (consensus) Remodel and Develop are poor Curse trashers, so Ritual's Curses mostly stay.
- **R8** [supporting] (single expert) A pure Duke player opens Develop/Silver, takes two Altars and maybe a Jester, and only one or two Market Squares (Developed into where possible).
- **R9** [supporting] (single expert) In the Province deck, Peddler beats Gold (Ritual fodder, Remodel targets).
- **R10** [supporting] (result) Across the recorded games the board produced real play and counterplay; the outcome turned on reading which plan the opponent chose and responding.
- **R11** [supporting] (single expert) Market Squares set the ceiling for the Province deck, so buying extras to deny an opponent who has few is a tactic.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Contraband: the opponent just bans Province or Duchy.
- **T2** (consensus) Treasure Map: "clearly garbo" (a host), despite a cute Market Square interaction.
- **T3** (consensus) Ignoring Duchies while the opponent goes Duke.
- **T4** (consensus) Making Ritual on Peddler the whole strategy.

## Disagreements
- How many Market Squares a Duke deck wants: one host would barely use them,
  the other wants one or two for the Gold gains.
- Develop/Remodel vs Develop/Silver for a committed Duke player.

## Grader notes
- Aqueduct and Merchant were barely discussed. (My note) Aqueduct pays
  VP when you gain a Victory card after gaining Golds or Silvers, so Market
  Square Golds plus Duchy gains feed it; treat remarks on this as plausible
  extras.
- Jester was called a good opener and good for Duke; credit it as a
  reasonable alternative.
