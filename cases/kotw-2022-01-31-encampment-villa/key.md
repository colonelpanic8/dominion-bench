# Key: KotW 1/30/2022: Encampment, Villa, Way of the Mouse (Ambassador)

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/sgpu2q/), 2022-01-31.
  SignError and willowhelmiam (regulars) gave openings; archlon reported
  several games against the Lord Rattington bot with a final turn. No
  strong-player playtest.

## Expert summary
Way of the Mouse borrows Ambassador, so any Action can be played as an
Ambassador that returns Estates (or Coppers) to the Supply and gives them to
the opponent. Commenters saw this "Ambassador war" as the deciding factor.
The agreed opening is Encampment/Villa: Villa goes to hand when gained, so it
can be played immediately as an Ambassador on turn 1 or 2 to send an Estate
over. After that, buy Encampments for draw and Actions and keep winning the
Ambassador exchange. archlon's games used Villa plus Gladiators to reach Gold
by turn 3-4 (so Encampments return), then drew the deck, used Overlord as
Hunting Party, put Pathfinding on Villa, and reached many Buys and about $50
(from Fortune) by turns 12-15.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Way of the Mouse (Ambassador) is central: play Actions as Ambassador to return junk and hand it to the opponent; winning this exchange decides the game. "trying to win the ambassador war. That's probably the real deciding factor" (willowhelmiam)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Opens Encampment/Villa (SignError's later edit; willowhelmiam).
- **R3** [core] (majority) Villa is gained to hand, so it can be played as an Ambassador on the turn it is bought. "Villa can be immediately played on turn 1 or 2 to Ambassador the Estate." (SignError)
- **R4** [supporting] (majority) Builds an engine on Encampment draw, with a Gold or Plunder available so Encampments return.
- **R5** [supporting] (result, vs. bot) Gladiator/Fortune economy: early Gold via Villa plus Gladiators, late Fortune for big money and many Buys.
- **R6** [supporting] (single player) Pathfinding on Villa; one or two Overlords mostly played as Hunting Party.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Too many Villas: there is no real trashing apart from Ambassador, so extra Villas clog. "You don't want to go too overboard with the Villas." (SignError)

## Disagreements
- SignError first suggested Gladiator/Villa, then preferred Encampment on 3/4
  as more flexible. On 5/2 he suggested buying Encampment turn 1, then Villa
  turn 2 and another Encampment.

## Grader notes
- archlon's results are against a bot that ignores Way of the Mouse; treat
  his payload numbers as weak evidence.
- Tournament (first edition) and Prizes were mentioned only in passing
  (archlon found Diadem unusually good). Wharf, Outpost and Conspirator were
  not discussed.
