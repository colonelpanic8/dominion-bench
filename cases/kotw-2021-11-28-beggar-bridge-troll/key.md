# Key: KotW 11/28/2021: Bridge Troll, Donate, Engineer, Villa

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/r4j4o4/), 2021-11-28, 10 comments.
  PathfindingLibrary (strong regular) gave turn-by-turn opening lines; SignError explained
  them; GonffzCheeze named the payload; NohatCoder and hazeknight described an
  Engineer/Villa/Destrier line, hazeknight from a game.

## Expert summary
Bridge Troll is the payload: its cost reduction (and +Buy) makes Engineer's gains
better and Destriers cheap. PathfindingLibrary's 4/3 line: Silver, then Donate
trashing 7 cards (down to Silver and three Coppers, $5), pay off the Debt, Bridge
Troll, then Bridge Troll + Engineer. On 2/5: Engineer, Donate 7, gain 2 Silvers
(Engineer gains one and trashes itself for another), Bridge Troll, Bridge Troll +
Engineer. Asked why Silver over Engineer, SignError said paying Donate's Debt and
reaching $5 for Bridge Troll fast matters more than early gains. PathfindingLibrary
agreed Engineer-first is "usually a good line" but said getting Bridge Troll in play
early is what matters here. NohatCoder's alternative opens double Engineer, trashing
Engineers for Villas to buy many Destriers; hazeknight did that in a game and gained
6 Destriers in one turn.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Bridge Troll is the payload, with Engineer and Destrier benefiting from its cost reduction. "BT payload. Engineer looks great with cost reduction." (GonffzCheeze)
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Donates very early to trash down to a tiny deck (Silver/Donate opening on 4/3, then pay off the Debt). (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R3** [core] (single expert) Prioritizes getting Bridge Troll in play early over early Engineer gains. "Getting Bridge Troll in play early is important here though." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) 2/5 line: Engineer, Donate, gain two Silvers, Bridge Troll. (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R5** [supporting] (majority) Destriers become cheap under Bridge Troll; Villa via Engineer adds Actions and Buys for mass Destrier gains. (GonffzCheeze, NohatCoder; hazeknight result: 6 Destriers in a turn)

## Traps

## Disagreements
- NohatCoder opens double Engineer and was unsure when to Donate; PathfindingLibrary's
  lines Donate on turn 2. hazeknight Donated on turn 4-5 in his game.
