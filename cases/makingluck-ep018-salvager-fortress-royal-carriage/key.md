# Key: Making Luck ep. 18 (ep. 17 kingdom): Salvager, Fortress, Royal Carriage, Swindler, Defiled Shrine

## Sources
- [Making Luck ep. 18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fw-q_ZywLNM&t=355s),
  2018-04-03, about 5:55-16:10. Adam Horton (a world champion) and Jake,
  after many live-streamed games against each other. They refer back to
  their pre-game read at the end of ep. 17.
- Ep. 17.5, a bonus episode, also mentions this kingdom ("just play
  Fortress Big Money"), but it is an April Fools' parody and is ignored.

## Expert summary
Salvager is by far the most important card. It is the only thinner, it
undoes Swindler's damage, and later it is payload: the long-term goal is a
deck that draws itself and Salvages Fortresses over and over (Fortress
returns to hand when trashed, so each Salvage is +$ and +Buy for free).
Thin first: don't Salvage a Fortress early when an Estate or Copper could
go instead, though stop thinning Coppers before you can't afford what you
need. Open Salvager/Swindler on 4/3, and Salvager/nothing on 5/2. Jake
preferred Margrave on 5/2 before playing and came round to Salvager. The
deck is about playing Salvager as often as possible. Royal Carriage is the
most effective way to do that: each Carriage is like another Salvager play.
Scavenger and Guide help find it, and Gear less so. Get a Salvager, then
lots of Royal Carriages until you can draw most of your deck by adding a
Margrave and a Catacombs or two. Swindler is a legitimate tactical attack.
It hurts a deck that has thinned to few Coppers or turns Royal Carriages
into Duchies, while Fortress in a thin deck is a real defense against it.
Defiled Shrine's VP was usually worth a Curse when you had a spare Buy and
5-7 VP were waiting; the tokens drained from every pile except Beggar.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Salvager is the key card: the only thinner, and later payload; play it as often as possible.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) The end-state deck draws itself and repeatedly Salvages Fortresses (Fortress returns to hand when trashed) for money and Buys.
- **R3** [core] (consensus) Opens Salvager/Swindler on 4/3 and Salvager/nothing on 5/2. "You do just bite the bullet and get Salvager or nothing." (Jake)
- **R4** [core] (consensus) Royal Carriage is the best way to replay Salvager; builds Salvager then many Royal Carriages.
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus) Adds a Margrave and Catacombs for draw once the Carriages are in place.
- **R6** [supporting] (consensus) Scavenger and Guide also help find Salvager; Gear is weaker for this.
- **R7** [supporting] (consensus) Thin Estates and Coppers before Salvaging Fortresses, but keep enough money to buy what you need.
- **R8** [supporting] (consensus) Swindler is a legitimate tactical attack here; Fortress in a thin deck and Salvager blunt it.
- **R9** [supporting] (consensus) Defiled Shrine: with a spare Buy, buying a Curse for about 5-7 VP is worth it; the VP is finite, so deny it to the opponent and use the lead to steer the ending.
- **R10** [supporting] (result) In their games the Defiled Shrine tokens consistently drained from every pile except Beggar, and Swindler set players back by hitting the Coppers of a thinned deck or turning Royal Carriages into Duchies.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Opening Margrave (or anything but Salvager) on 5/2: Jake's pre-game pick, which play disproved.
- **T2** (consensus) Salvaging a Fortress early while junk is still in hand.
- **T3** (consensus) Gear as the main way to find Salvager.

## Disagreements
- Pre-game, the 5/2 opening (Salvager/nothing vs Margrave); settled for
  Salvager after play.
