# Key: Horton 2026-04 Finals Kingdom: Governor, Flagship, Tactician, Way of the Owl, Leprechaun

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "April 2026 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/april-2026-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, April 2026. Horton designed and playtested this
  kingdom for the final. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1pc0bdGvuPpApfFOfVEA6zX3njAqS_RO_JlPebAMSK5k)
  (comment: "Multiple tacticians are worth building around").

## Expert summary
Horton designed three ideas into this board. First, Throne Room and Flagship
on Leprechaun can gain many Wishes in one turn. Second, the payload is
explosive with several Throne Rooms, while Friendly Governors trickle into the
deck (discard one in Clean-up to gain another). Third, Throne Rooms and Way of
the Owl let you play several Tacticians in one turn. Because Forager can be
huge payload, the extra draw from Tacticians both makes the deck consistent and
frees Governor from draw duty so it can gain Golds and remodel. In the final
neither player went for Tactician, which he found quite good in testing and
had designed the kingdom around.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Multiple Tacticians per turn are worth building: Throne Room doubles a Tactician, and Way of the Owl (draw to 6) refills after a Tactician discards, giving big start-of-turn draw.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Throne Room or Flagship replaying Leprechaun can gain several Wishes in one turn (Leprechaun gives a Wish with exactly 7 cards in play).
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Friendly Governors flow into the deck over time (discard one in Clean-up, gain another).
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Payload is explosive with multiple Throne Rooms; Forager (with a growing trash) can be large payload.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) With Tactician providing draw, Governor can be used for Gold gains and remodeling instead of the draw mode.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer + result) Skipping Tactician; both finalists did, and Horton thinks it is a key part of the best deck.
