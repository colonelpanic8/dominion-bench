## Expert summary
Horton wanted a crunchy board around Frigate and Wall. Junking with Old Witch
and good gainers in Jack of All Trades and Tunnel push against Wall (−1 VP per
card beyond 15), and keeping Frigate coverage at all times can really hurt the
opponent. The player who best prioritizes and times these things, according to
what the opponent does, has a big edge. His sheet adds that Frigate matters a
lot, but you still want to build a lot, and which draw works depends on how
well the opponent covers you with Frigate.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Frigate is a big deal: aim to play a Frigate every turn (coverage), since it punishes the opponent's draw.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Still build a lot despite Frigate; choose draw that works under the opponent's Frigate pressure.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Wall penalizes large decks, which conflicts with Old Witch junking and gainers (Jack of All Trades, Tunnel); weigh deck size.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Plan adapts to what the opponent does (their Frigate coverage, junking).
