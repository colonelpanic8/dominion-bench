# Key: 2023 Online Championship final, game 3: Alchemist, Contract, Crucible, Battlefield

## Sources
- [Dominionstreams broadcast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dj3S4ncnupE), 12:30-38:20.
  Commentary by xyrix and drsteelhammer, both long-time top players. syamii4 was
  first player; agoo03 won a close game on the last turn (35 VP vs roughly
  31-34). Kingdom and deck details read from the video.

## Expert summary
The commentators saw a board with lots of good Treasures but weak draw and
weak gaining. They raised Alchemist as the draw worth paying a Potion for, and
expected both players to build for a while rather than rush Gardens, even with
Battlefield, because there is no real Workshop ("if there was like a workshop
then I think you should consider that"). Terminal space is scarce: the only
village-like effect is Contract, which plays an Action from hand next turn, so
terminals have to be chosen carefully. A suggested opening was Maroon,
Importer and Silver; Market plus a Potion soon after made sense to them.

Both players went Alchemist. agoo03 won the Alchemist split (six to four) and
the Battlefield split, and used Crucible with Architects' Guild: each gain
spent 2 Favors on a cheaper non-Victory card, often another Crucible or
Market, and Crucible trashed spare Treasures and Actions for money. Bandit's
Gold gains fed Crucible too. syamii4 went heavier on Importers and Maroons, a
"go big" plan the commentators didn't trust. agoo03 greened first with
Provinces, Duchies and Battlefield VP; syamii4's final turn with Province and
Gardens came up just short.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) Alchemist (with Potion) is the main draw; both finalists built around it and the Alchemist split decided the game.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Draw and gaining are weak, so build a moderate deck rather than rushing Gardens; there is no good Workshop for a Gardens rush.
- **R3** [core] (single expert + result) Crucible combines with Architects' Guild and Bandit: Favors turn each gain into an extra cheaper card, and Crucible cashes in Golds, Markets and spare cards. "The Crucible was really nice here" (commentator)
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Terminal space is scarce; Contract is the only way to play an extra Action, so be selective about terminals.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Opening Maroon/Importer/Silver, then Market and a Potion.
- **R6** [supporting] (result) Battlefield VP rewards greening first; agoo03 took most of it.
- **R7** [supporting] (single expert) Save Favors for Architects' Guild gains, which convert $5 gains into extra cards later (e.g. Province plus a $5).
- **R8** [supporting] (result) Gardens is late cheap VP with +Buy from Charm and Market, not the main plan.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus + result) Rushing Gardens with Battlefield: no Workshop-style gainer makes it work.
- **T2** (result) Loading up on Importers and Maroons to "go big" while the opponent wins the Alchemist split; syamii4 did this and fell behind.

## Disagreements
- The commentators weren't sure Charm beats Market as an early $5; one called either defensible.
- They also weren't sure Contract is better than just playing Bandit every turn; one said the Favors do "some heavy lifting".

## Grader notes
- The game was close (decided on the last turn), so weight R2-R4 and the Alchemist read over the margin.
- Credit Secret Passage (Friendly) as a non-terminal option if justified; the commentators mentioned it only for reliability.
