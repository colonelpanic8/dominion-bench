# Key: KotW 10/10/2021: Sculptor, Cemetery, Vassal, Citadel, Exploration

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/q5jhvz/), 2021-10-10, 9 comments.
  Triumph44 and willowhelmiam gave independent, matching plans; SignError proposed a
  Ducat/Coffers plan and played both plans against each other himself.

## Expert summary
Two independent plans agree. Sculptor gains Cemetery to hand (Cemetery trashes up to 4
cards when gained), plus Priest, gives heavy thinning. In the thin deck, Vassals and
Throne Rooms, gained with Sculptor, provide the economy, with no real draw on the
board. Exploration gives Coffers and Villagers on turns you don't buy a card, and
Citadel (replays the first Action each turn) is the big buy. Triumph44 opens
Exploration/Vassal on 4/3; willowhelmiam opens Sculptor or Vassal/Priest. Both skip
chasing the Ghost from Haunted Mirror. SignError's playtest (both sides 3/4, Vassal
went first): the Vassal deck was swingy, came alive on turn 10 with Citadel ("did not
disappoint") and double-Provinced. The Ducat deck (Ducat trashing Coppers, Priest,
Sculptor-Cemetery, Exploration, saved Coffers and Ducat Buys) was steadier and won
38-36. He judged both better than money.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Sculptor gaining Cemetery to hand is the main thinning (with Priest). (Triumph44, willowhelmiam, SignError)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Economy comes from Vassals and Throne Rooms in a thinned deck; there is no real draw. (Triumph44, willowhelmiam)
- **R3** [core] (majority + result) Citadel is a key buy at the first $8. "I've never had Citadel in a random game, and it did not disappoint." (SignError)
- **R4** [supporting] (majority) Exploration early for Villagers/Coffers. (Triumph44, willowhelmiam)
- **R5** [supporting] (result) A Ducat/Coffers plan (Ducat trashing Coppers, Priest, Exploration, saved Coffers for a multi-Province turn) is competitive and steadier: it won 38-36 in SignError's solo test.
- **R6** [supporting] (majority) Doesn't chase the Ghost from Haunted Mirror. (Triumph44, willowhelmiam)

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Artisan, Scepter, Vampire and Necromancer (unless something good is in the trash). "Artisan, Ducat, Necro, Scepter, and Vamp are really bad here." (willowhelmiam; SignError's test contradicts him on Ducat only)

## Disagreements
- Ducat: willowhelmiam called it bad; SignError's Ducat plan won his test.
- Openings: Exploration/Vassal (Triumph44), Sculptor or Vassal/Priest (willowhelmiam),
  Ducat/Priest (SignError). Vassal/Priest risked a terminal collision in SignError's test.

## Grader notes
- The playtest is one person playing both sides; treat the 38-36 margin as noise.
  Credit either Vassal/Citadel or Ducat/Coffers if built on Sculptor-Cemetery thinning.
