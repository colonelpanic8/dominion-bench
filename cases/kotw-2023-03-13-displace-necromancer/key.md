# Key: KotW 13 Mar 2023: Displace, Necromancer, Acting Troupe, Destrier

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/11q4h69/), 2023-03-13.
  ppltn and Curebob gave detailed plans; willowhelmiam summarized the
  engine; pigmelons23 clarified the Necromancer rule. No games reported.

## Expert summary
Displace is the key card: the first $5 target, used to exile junk while
gaining better cards (it exiles rather than trashes). Necromancer playing an
Acting Troupe from the trash gives +4 Villagers every time (Acting Troupe's
"trash this" fails, so it stays there), a reusable village. Destriers score
2 VP each through Obelisk and give draw; Displace can turn a Province into a
Destrier and back, discarding Exiled copies when you gain one. ppltn's line:
Fisherman/Silver (or Envoy) opening, Displace first, an Acting Troupe in the
second shuffle, then Displace into Necromancer, Wild Hunt on $5, Envoy or
Necromancer on $4, Fisherman or Acting Troupe on $3 or less; once drawing the
deck, a second Displace and Provinces, Displacing between Provinces and
Destriers. Curebob: Displace Coppers into Fishermen, Estates into Acting
Troupes, multiple Displaces, and as many Destriers as possible.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Displace is the first $5 and the main gainer/thinner. "First 5 is Displace, as it's the only good form of thrashing." (ppltn)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Necromancer + Acting Troupe in the trash is a repeatable village (+4 Villagers each play). "Necromancer on Acting Troupe is an amazing village." (ppltn)
- **R3** [core] (majority) Destriers score via Obelisk; get many of them, gaining them cheaply after other gains.
- **R4** [supporting] (majority) Displace swaps Provinces and Destriers late, releasing Exiled copies.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Envoy or Fisherman as non-terminal draw/economy; Wild Hunt on $5s once drawing.
- **R6** [supporting] (single player) Displace Coppers into Fishermen and Estates into Acting Troupes.

## Traps
- **T1** (majority) Treating Displace as trashing for on-trash effects (e.g. Flag Bearer); it exiles. (FerretChrist, NovaRogue)

## Disagreements
- Opening: Fisherman/Silver or Envoy (ppltn), Acting Troupe/Envoy
  (nuggins), Monument/Silver to rush Displace (Dame-Da-Nae).
- ppltn also mentioned an all-in Wild Hunt plan if the opponent greens too
  early.
