# Key: Horton 2023-04 Finals Kingdom 1: Order of Masons, Inheritance, Settlers, Gardens

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion: Spring 2023 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-spring-2023-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, 2023-04-15. Horton designed and playtested this
  kingdom for the finals. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1bUdgaAKlS6lsndwvGKeABDdWVf9etjaLFIMcRVTcgqU)
  (comment: a real deck with Settlers/Bustling Village as the only draw and no
  trashing, using Order of Masons).

## Expert summary
There is no trashing, but Order of Masons (when shuffling, spend Favors to put
up to 2 cards per Favor into your discard pile) acts as effective trashing.
Inheritance and Settlers let you draw your Coppers for free, with possible
Storeroom synergy. There are several ways to hit an early Inheritance ($7), not
all equal, and what to Inherit is not obvious. After a lot of playtesting
Horton likes a Baron/Grotto opening: it nearly guarantees $7 by turn 5 at the
latest and gives a clear path to Inheriting Settlers. On a rough draw,
Inheriting Throne Room can be good, but he usually prefers Settlers. Opening
Capital also gets there but leaves a worse deck. From then on, collect as many
Favors as possible, since they are your trashing for a while. Once you can
start a turn with all your Coppers in the discard pile, you stop drawing bad
cards and can build fast and add payload with Throne Room/Artisan tricks. The
deck is very resilient to greening, so Gardens can catch up against an
opponent who leads on Provinces, since there's no great way to drain the
Provinces to close out. It was played twice in the finals and he thinks the
better strategy won both times.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Order of Masons is the "trashing": spend Favors when shuffling to keep Coppers (and junk) out of the new deck.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Opens Baron/Grotto to reach $7 for Inheritance by turn 5.
- **R3** [core] (designer) Inherits Settlers (Estates become Settlers, pulling Coppers from the discard pile as needed); Throne Room is an alternative on a rough draw.
- **R4** [core] (designer) Collects as many Favors as possible to fuel Order of Masons.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Once a turn can start with all Coppers in the discard pile, the deck builds fast; payload via Throne Room/Artisan.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Gardens is a catch-up option against a Province lead, because the deck greens well and Provinces are hard to drain quickly.
- **R7** [supporting] (designer) Storeroom synergizes with the Settlers/Copper setup.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Opening Capital to reach Inheritance; it gets there but leaves a worse deck.
- **T2** (designer) Inheriting something other than Settlers by default (Throne Room only on a rough draw).
