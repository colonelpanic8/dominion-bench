# Key: DS The Value of Building: Mastermind, Artisan, Young Witch, Cutpurse

## Sources
- [The Value of Building](https://dominionstrategy.com/2021/03/15/the-value-of-building/),
  2021-03-15: topher1588 analyzes his game on this board against Stef, one of the
  top-rated players on Dominion Online, describing several plausible decks and how
  a bigger build beats each.

## Expert summary
The board has no +Buy, but Cathedral trashes while still buying and Mastermind
(three plays of an Action next turn) is the strongest card. Journeyman money
(Exploration/Cathedral, Silvers, Journeymen, Golds) gets 5–6 Provinces quickly
but then can't keep hitting $8 and is outlasted by a deck with Artisans adding
villages, Journeymen and late Duchies. A light engine with a couple of Artisans
greening early is crushed by Mastermind on Cutpurse, a discard attack that stacks
every turn; the Mastermind player is less hurt because his Masterminds draw at the
start of his turn and Cathedral trashes the Coppers. topher's actual deck used
Artisan to gain Masterminds and topdeck Journeyman, starting turns with two
Masterminds in play; he greened first (emptying Duchies, 5 of 8) instead of
buying the last Mastermind. Stef built one more turn, started his final turn with
six Masterminds and won; he could also have used Mastermind on Artisan to gain
three Young Witches and Masterminded them for about ten Curses. The lesson:
build bigger, because more building lets you use payload you notice late, like
the Curse pile.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert + result) Builds a Mastermind engine: Artisan gains Masterminds and topdecks Journeyman; aim for several Masterminds in play at the start of each turn.
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Mastermind on Cutpurse is a stacking discard attack that wrecks a lightly built opponent; the Mastermind player is less affected.
- **R3** [core] (single expert) Curses as payload: Mastermind on Artisan for three Young Witches, then Mastermind the Witches to hand out most of the Curse pile.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Opens Exploration/Cathedral (Coffers and Villagers from Exploration; Cathedral trashes each turn and Artisan can topdeck Coppers around it).
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Mastermind on Artisan gains several Duchies/Provinces a turn despite no +Buy; Duchy and Estate piles become pile-control tools.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert + result) Build one turn longer than feels necessary; the deck with more Masterminds controls the ending.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Journeyman money with no engine: it stalls after 5–6 Provinces.
- **T2** (single expert) A small Artisan engine that greens early: punished by Mastermind/Cutpurse.
- **T3** (single expert + result) Greening first instead of taking the last Mastermind (topher's actual mistake).
