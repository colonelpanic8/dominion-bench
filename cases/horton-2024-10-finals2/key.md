# Key: Horton 2024-10 Finals Table 2: Pious Procession, Outpost, Artisan, Way of the Squirrel

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "October 2024 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/october-2024-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, October 2024. Horton made this finals kingdom but says
  health issues meant less playtesting than usual and less of his usual
  balancing. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1UwplRNQfziRzY95Ey7zI9-hDjvWqNh99xISpgMjrhRo).

## Expert summary
Procession's errata (it no longer plays Durations) hurts here, since half the
board is Durations, but Horton still thinks Procession is very good. All the
draw comes at the start of your turn or the end of the previous one (Fishing
Village, Siren, Longship, Way of the Squirrel), which makes Outpost extremely
good but makes gain-and-play with Procession harder. Gaining to hand with
Artisan is clearly the way to feed Procession; Treasury after Procession gains
doesn't seem as strong. While building, focus on as much deck control as
possible so you reliably get a Province on both your main turn and your
Outpost turn. If you get ahead on junking (Siren) and thinning (Pious
Procession, Church), you can add many Duchy gains with Artisan on top.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Outpost is extremely good because all the draw happens at the start of turn or end of the previous turn.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Procession is still very good despite not working on Durations; Pious adds trashing when gaining Processions.
- **R3** [core] (designer) Artisan gaining to hand is the way to set up Procession plays (gain an Action and Procession it the same turn).
- **R4** [core] (designer) Build target: enough deck control to buy a Province on both the normal turn and the Outpost turn.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) If ahead on junking and thinning, add Duchy gains with Artisan.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Treasury as the Procession follow-up is weaker than Artisan.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Planning to Procession Duration cards (Fishing Village, Outpost, Siren...); the errata forbids it.
