# Key: Horton 2023-04 Finals Kingdom 2: Bridge Troll, Sailor, Fawning Hermit, City-State

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion: Spring 2023 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-spring-2023-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, 2023-04-15. Horton designed and playtested this
  kingdom for the finals. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1bUdgaAKlS6lsndwvGKeABDdWVf9etjaLFIMcRVTcgqU).

## Expert summary
Two merged ideas. First, Sailor is the main village for playing Bridge Trolls
(Sailor plays a Duration you gain that turn). Second, an endgame trick: reduce
Province's cost to $3 with Bridge Trolls (using the awkward villages
available), have a pile of Favors ready, then buy a Province. Fawning makes you
gain a Hermit when you gain a Province; City-State lets you spend 2 Favors to
play that Hermit immediately, and the Hermit gains another Province (costing
$3 or less), which gains another Hermit, and so on while Favors last and until
the Provinces are gone. Then buy Duchies with every Buy, since they cost $0.
Horton likens it to assembling Exodia: get Favors, grab Sailors and some
gainers meanwhile, then over two turns gain Bridge Trolls and play them with
Sailor; once it's all assembled you just win. With enough Favors you can skip
Sailor, but Sailor speeds you up. It was played once in the finals, and the
winner went for many Bridge Trolls.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Finisher: Bridge Trolls reduce Province to $3 or less; buy a Province, Fawning gains a Hermit, City-State (2 Favors) plays it, and Hermit gains another Province, repeating until Favors or Provinces run out.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Stockpile Favors for the finisher (Liaisons: Wizards, Bauble).
- **R3** [core] (designer) Sailor is the village that plays Bridge Trolls the turn they are gained.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Once Provinces are gone, extra Buys take Duchies at $0.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Gain Bridge Trolls over two turns and play them with Sailor to set up the cost reduction.
- **R6** [supporting] (result) The finals winner went for many Bridge Trolls.
