# Key: KotW 07 Aug 2023: Sycophant, Fortress, Woodworkers' Guild, Tomb

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/15k26pd/), 2023-08-06.
  helmster123 proposed the opening; Snip3 and presumably_alterable agreed.
- [Snip3's end-of-game screenshot](https://i.imgur.com/jAIkeNP.jpg): an
  unrated game against the Lord Rattington bot, 59 VP in 13 turns (Snip3
  later said 12 turns was easy). Final deck: 10 Grand Markets, 5 King's
  Courts, 5 City Quarters, 2 Specialists, Fortress, Procession, Barbarian,
  Royal Blacksmith, 7 Provinces and 11 VP of tokens.

## Expert summary
Woodworkers' Guild lets you spend a Favor at the start of your Buy phase to
trash an Action from hand and gain any Action card, so cheap Actions become
King's Courts, City Quarters and Grand Markets, with +1 VP from Tomb per
trash. Open Sycophant/Fortress: Sycophant gives Favors when gained and when
trashed, and Fortress returns to hand when trashed, so it can be trashed to
the Guild over and over. Trash the openers next shuffle for Grand Markets and
other expensive Actions. Procession on Fortress plays it twice, returns it to
hand and gains a $5 Action (e.g. Specialist), also scoring Tomb VP. Snip3's
game shows the result: a Grand Market/King's Court/City Quarter deck taking 7
Provinces by turn 13. One player felt there was "only one strategy to really
play".

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) Opens Sycophant/Fortress and uses Woodworkers' Guild to trash them into expensive Actions. "Open sycophant + fortress and trash them next shuffle for grand markets or other stuff?" (helmster123)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Fortress returns to hand when trashed, so it can be repeatedly trashed to the Guild (and Procession) for gains and Tomb VP.
- **R3** [core] (result) Targets of the Guild: Grand Market, King's Court and City Quarter form the end deck.
- **R4** [supporting] (single player) Procession on Fortress to gain Specialists and score. (presumably_alterable)
- **R5** [supporting] (majority) Sycophant's Favors on gain and trash fuel the Guild; about 5 Favors early is enough.
- **R6** [supporting] (result) Tomb adds meaningful VP (11 in the reported game).

## Traps
- **T1** (single player) Hoarding Favors at the expense of Fortress: "you'll want the fortress in your deck more than favors 6-9" (Snip3, on opening double Sycophant)

## Disagreements
- Opening Sycophant/Sycophant was floated; Snip3 preferred keeping Fortress.

## Grader notes
- The result is against a weak bot, but the build rate (Grand Markets and
  King's Courts from turn 3-4) is the point.
- Rogue, Barbarian and Royal Blacksmith were not discussed.
