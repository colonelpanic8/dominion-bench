# Key: DS Annotated Game #10: Young Witch, Masquerade, Festival, Highway, Venture

## Sources
- [Annotated Game #10](https://dominionstrategy.com/2012/02/17/annotated-game-10-geronimoo-vs-wanderingwinder/),
  2012-02-17: Geronimoo (simulator author) annotates his game against
  WanderingWinder; they were #1 and #2 on Isotropic. The game ended at turn 14
  when Geronimoo's laptop crashed.

## Expert summary
No Colonies, so Geronimoo leans to money unless an engine clearly beats it. Young
Witch is the cursing attack (he calls it underrated, skippable only against a
strong Bane), but the Bane is Swindler, which almost makes skipping it right, and
Masquerade softly counters it. His simulations after the game show the openings
(Masquerade, Young Witch, Swindler, Silver combinations) beat each other in a
rock-paper-scissors loop. WanderingWinder opened Masquerade and played Masquerade
money with Ventures, greening early. Geronimoo went Young Witch plus a Festival
and Watchtower draw engine (Watchtower draws to 6 after Festivals) aiming for a
Highway mega-turn. WanderingWinder led 3 Provinces to 0 by turn 13 despite the
Curses, and Geronimoo didn't like his chances; the game was never finished.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (result, unfinished) Masquerade/Venture money is strong and simple here; it took a 3–0 Province lead against Young Witch plus an engine. "This strategy is very strong and easy to play." (Geronimoo)
- **R2** [supporting] (single expert) Young Witch is worth considering, but a Swindler Bane and Masquerade weaken it.
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert + simulation) The opening is a rock-paper-scissors among Masquerade, Young Witch and Swindler; respond to the opponent's opening.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) If engining: Festival plus Watchtower (draw to 6) with Highways for a mega-turn; Native Village is better than Crossroads here.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Passing a Silver to a money opponent with Masquerade.
- **T2** (single expert) Crossroads as the village over Native Village in the Festival/Watchtower deck.

## Disagreements
- The two players took opposite plans and the game didn't finish, so the board's
  best plan is unsettled.
