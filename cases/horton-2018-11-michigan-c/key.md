# Key: U-Con 2018 Set C: Enchantress, Transmogrify, Ritual, Labyrinth

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion: Michigan Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-michigan-tournament-summary/),
  2018-11-12. Horton played this 3-player semifinal at U-Con, going last,
  and finished a close 2nd. He didn't design it.

## Expert summary
Horton liked the board's tricks. He opened Transmogrify; seeing opponents buy
Enchantress, he avoided adding more Action cards that Enchantress would
blank and bought a Silver. An opponent drew Transmogrify and Enchantress
together on turn 3 and then had an Enchantress in play almost every turn,
which forced Horton to pick up extra copies of the Actions he wanted before
he could do much. He recovered to a close second by Ritualing a few Golds and
Transmogrifying Peddlers into Provinces. He believes going last and the
opponent's turn-3 draw decided it.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert + result) Enchantress is a strong attack here; keeping it in play every turn can cripple Action-reliant opponents.
- **R2** [supporting] (single expert + result) Transmogrify is a good opener and later turns Peddlers into Provinces.
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Against Enchantress, a deck needs spare copies of its key Actions (or non-Action economy) to function.
- **R4** [supporting] (result) Ritual on Golds is good late payload.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Adding Actions that do nothing while opponents' Enchantresses blank your first Action each turn.

## Disagreements
- None recorded.
