# Key: KotW 4/11/2021: Wayfarer, Sea Hag, Fishing Village, Way of the Turtle

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/mp8f7s/), 2021-04-11, 9 comments.
  Madmanquail gave a full plan; Doomburrito and PathfindingLibrary (strong regular)
  disputed its opening; mountebanker proposed a Sea Hag/Fishing Village opening.

## Expert summary
An engine board with no trashing. Wayfarer is the draw (its cost equals the last
other card gained this turn), Fishing Village and Festival the villages, and Way of the
Turtle adds consistency. Sea Hag makes the opponent gain a Curse, which costs $0, so
Wayfarer costs $0 for the rest of that turn. Madmanquail's target deck: 3-4 Wayfarers,
2-3 Fishing Villages, a Hunting Lodge, Sea Hag and 1-2 Festivals, with Silvers for
economy, aiming at double Provinces in a fast game. His opening (Bargain for Hunting
Lodge plus Silver) was rejected: Doomburrito would take Sea Hag first,
PathfindingLibrary wouldn't get a $5 at all, and mountebanker opens Sea Hag/Fishing
Village.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Builds an engine with Wayfarer as draw and Fishing Village/Festival as villages, aiming for double Province turns. (Madmanquail; others argue only the opening)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Sea Hag's Curse makes Wayfarer cost $0 that turn. "the main benefit is that it lets you buy wayfarer for $0" (Madmanquail; mountebanker agrees)
- **R3** [supporting] (majority) Gets Sea Hag early (opening Sea Hag/Fishing Village, or Sea Hag before Hunting Lodge). (mountebanker, Doomburrito)
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Way of the Turtle improves consistency. (Madmanquail)
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Economy comes from Silvers, given no trashing. (Madmanquail)

## Traps
- **T1** (majority) Opening Bargain on Hunting Lodge, or any $5. "not get a $5" (PathfindingLibrary)

## Disagreements
- On 5/2 mountebanker would open Sea Hag/Embargo "and feel sad"; no one proposed better.

## Grader notes
- Thin and untested. Beggar, Gatekeeper, Harbinger, Merchant and Embargo weren't discussed.
- Embargo and Sea Hag are first-edition cards.
