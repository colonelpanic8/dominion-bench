# Key: Horton 2024-10 Table 1: Cavalry, Feodum, Necromancer, Mining Road, Vineyard

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "October 2024 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/october-2024-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, October 2024. A randomly generated kingdom, filtered
  by Horton for the Swiss rounds; played four times. He reports what happened
  rather than a plan. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1UwplRNQfziRzY95Ey7zI9-hDjvWqNh99xISpgMjrhRo).

## Expert summary
Draw is limited: Cavalry's Horses are the only sustainable draw, and Zombie
Apprentice (via Necromancer) can be used only once per turn. But it stops being
limited if someone trashes a Horse, so the contents of the trash decided much
of what each game could do. Mining Road was important because it allows a
quick pivot into Vineyards. The Necromancer and Cavalry piles ran low in every
game. Some games were draw-light while others had plenty of Horses in the
trash. Mountain Pass mattered too: in one game the Provinces were untouched when
piles were low, and on the last turn, when Vineyards emptied, the opponent
could bid 40 Debt first to guarantee 8 VP, so Mountain Pass was a liability for
the player ending the game. Many games went to time.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer + result) Draw is limited to Cavalry/Horses and once-per-turn Zombie Apprentice, unless Horses end up in the trash for Necromancer.
- **R2** [core] (designer + result) Necromancer's value depends on the trash; trashed Horses (and other Actions) make it real draw.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer + result) Mining Road enables a quick pivot into Vineyards (Potion-cost alt VP).
- **R4** [supporting] (result) Necromancer and Cavalry piles ran low in every game; expect pile pressure.
- **R5** [supporting] (result) Mountain Pass can matter late: if no Province has been gained, gaining the first one on the game's last turn lets the opponent bid the full 40 Debt, which costs nothing then, and take the 8 VP.
