# Key: KotW 1/16/2022: Artificer, Cargo Ship, Flag Bearer, Replace

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/s5qamq/), 2022-01-17.
  PathfindingLibrary (strong regular) gave the opening and a sample turn 3-6
  sequence; GonffzCheeze ran solo games and confirmed the opening; SignError
  agreed it is the best 4/3.

## Expert summary
Open Cargo Ship and the Exploration project on 4/3. The first priority is
playing Artificer; Cargo Ship sets aside an Artificer gained on turn 3 so it
is in hand on turn 4, and Exploration (Coffers and Villagers on turns you buy
nothing) is already paying off when the Artificer turns start. Artificer then
gains more Artificers and Flag Bearers onto the deck, Cargo Ship holds a
Sheepdog, and Way of the Butterfly upgrades Flag Bearer into Replace while
the gain triggers Sheepdog. Estates are left for Replace later rather than
Razed early. "Every card in this kingdom could see play."

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert + result) Opens Cargo Ship/Exploration on 4/3. "Cargo Ship/Exploration open." (PathfindingLibrary; GonffzCheeze's solos: "Clear CS/Explo.")
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Getting Artificer into play early is the first priority, e.g. turn 3 Cargo Ship sets aside a gained Artificer. "First priority is playing Artificer" (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Exploration's Coffers/Villagers support the early Artificer turns (turns that buy nothing still build).
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Artificer gains Artificers and Flag Bearers; Way of the Butterfly turns Flag Bearer ($4) into Replace ($5).
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Sheepdog is triggered by gains during the turn (set aside on Cargo Ship, then reacting to a Butterfly gain).
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Estates are dealt with later by Replace, not by an early Raze.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Opening Raze/Cargo Ship to trash Estates. "Estates will be killed later by Replace." (PathfindingLibrary)

## Disagreements
- None recorded. SignError asked about 5/2 openings; nobody answered.

## Grader notes

  with Sheepdog on Cargo Ship, T6 Butterfly Flag Bearer into Replace) is an
  example, not a fixed script.
