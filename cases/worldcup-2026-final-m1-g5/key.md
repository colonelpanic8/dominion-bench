# Key: 2026 World Cup final, Fircoal vs kojii, game 5: Souk, Credit, Innovation, Maroon, Search

## Sources
- [JNails with Paolo99, neutral commentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_2At4CXlgQA), 1:34:30-1:48:40.
  Captions don't mark speakers, so unnamed quotes are "commentator". Chat (Lemonspawn,
  Minionpawn) is quoted by the commentators.
- Match: Dominion World Cup 2026 final, match 1. Fircoal (player 1, 5/2) won to go up 3-2.

## Expert summary
The commentators liked Souk and Credit from the start. Credit gains an Action or Treasure costing
up to $8 for Debt equal to its cost, so a 5/2 can effectively open with two $5s. Souk's on-gain
trashes two cards, so Credit for Souk on turn 1 or 2 trashes two Estates at once; Lemonspawn
would have opened Junk Dealer/Souk "100%". Villages are scarce: Innovation (play an Action when you
gain it) works as one, and Prince only finds non-terminal targets in Imps from Devil's Workshop.
JNails kept wanting Devil's Workshop for Imps and liked Dismantle (turn surplus Souks into a
Silver plus a Gold); neither player took Devil's Workshop. He also explained that under the
current Debt rules you can pay Debt mid-turn, so Souk's +$7 can be spent on Debt before its
"-$1 per card" penalty applies.

Fircoal's 5/2 got him both Junk Dealer and Souk in the opening (Credit covering one of them),
added Artisan and Innovation, then used Credit to buy
Prince and set aside Maroon. He filled his deck with Searches (each gains a Loot when a pile
empties) and Loots, then used Maroon (+2 Cards per type) on multi-type Loots such as Endless
Chalice to draw his deck. kojii bought a Province early, which JNails thought was premature;
Fircoal caught up from 12 points behind and won.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) Souk is a key early buy: its on-gain trashes two cards, and Credit (or a 5/2) gets it on turn 1-2. "I would have opened JD/Souk 100%" (Lemonspawn, chat)
- **R2** [core] (single expert + result) Credit is strong: it gains a $5-$8 Action or Treasure early for Debt, e.g. Souk on the opening or Prince later.
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Villages are scarce; Innovation acts as one (play the Action you gain), and Prince wants a non-terminal target.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Devil's Workshop gaining Imps would give Prince a good target and add draw.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Dismantle turns spare Souks into Silver plus Gold.
- **R6** [supporting] (result) Maroon is the big draw here: trashing multi-type cards (Loots like Endless Chalice, Werewolf) for +2 Cards per type; Prince on Maroon makes it every turn.
- **R7** [supporting] (result) Search feeds the deck with Loots once piles start emptying.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Greening early before the deck can draw itself; JNails questioned kojii's early Province.
- **T2** (single expert) Artisan as a main $6 here; the commentators preferred Souk or Devil's Workshop, though Fircoal used Artisan with Innovation.

## Disagreements
- Fircoal's Artisan and Maroon buys puzzled JNails at the time; they worked once Credit put Maroon
  under Prince.
- JNails and chat disagreed on whether Endless Chalice should be trashed to Maroon or played.
