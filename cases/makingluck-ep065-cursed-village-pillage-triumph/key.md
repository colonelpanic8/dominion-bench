# Key: Making Luck ep. 65 review: Cursed Village, Storeroom, Pillage, Triumph

## Sources
- [Making Luck ep. 65](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ujg9djeeQQE&t=169s),
  2019-02-26, 2:49-18:30. Jake and Wandering Winder review the kingdom shown
  at the end of ep. 64 (an episode on Cursed Village and Lost City). Jake
  played many games against Adam Horton; Wandering Winder played only bots
  and gives his own read.

## Expert summary
The deck draws with villages: Cursed Village and Lost City are close, and
both end up in the deck, but Cursed Village is clearly better early and won
out overall. The board supports a draw-to-X plan: Storeroom (Jake and Adam
ran many, discarding four or more cards each), Forager (non-terminal, and
it thins cards out of hand before Cursed Village draws), and to a lesser
degree Plaza and Gear. After three or four Cursed Villages, switch toward
Lost City, sooner if a pile-out is coming. Pillage is far more important
than its reputation. The deck draws well enough to play the Spoils, so
Pillage is money-positive, its gains add to Triumph, and its discard hits
hard because a hand with one village is crippled. A player who starts
Pillaging first can chain several free turns. Turns were all-or-nothing,
$18 and many Buys or about $3. Triumph is the best point source (Vineyard,
stressed in the preview, mattered less). The best Triumphs were 7-8 VP. The
endgame turns on whether to run out the Estates with Triumphs or move to
Provinces and Vineyards, and on how close the piles are.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Builds a village/draw deck around Cursed Village (better than Lost City early), adding Lost Cities later.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Triumph is the main point source, fed by big multi-gain turns (Storeroom/Forager +Buys, Spoils gains).
- **R3** [core] (consensus) Pillage is important: money-positive once the Spoils can be drawn, extra gains for Triumph, and its discard often strips the opponent's only village, giving the Pillager free turns.
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Storeroom (and Forager) support Cursed Village's draw-to-6: discard or trash cards out of hand, then refill.
- **R5** [supporting] (majority) Forager as the trasher, non-terminal so it fits beside Storerooms; opening includes Forager (Forager/Gear or Forager/Silver per the players who tested).
- **R6** [supporting] (consensus) Vineyard is secondary to Triumph; Provinces/Vineyards are the fallback once Triumph and Estates are spent.
- **R7** [supporting] (result) In Jake and Adam's games, one player usually came out far ahead: whoever got the Pillage loop going took free turns and most of the Triumph points; the biggest Triumphs were about 7-8 VP.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Writing off Pillage as a weak card here.
- **T2** (consensus) Treating Vineyard as the main point source (the hosts' preview read).
- **T3** (single expert) Forager/Forager opening: in Jake's games it lost out because it didn't hit $5 early enough.

## Disagreements
- Opening: Jake liked Forager/Gear (Gear can set aside cards to reach $5),
  close with Forager/Silver; Adam (as reported) leaned Forager/Forager or
  Forager/Silver; Wandering Winder prefers Gear/Silver, a Cursed Village on
  turn 3-4, and Foragers later.
- Triumph timing: Wandering Winder would take Triumphs earlier and run out
  the Estates; Jake thinks you can't go too fast, since Storeroom lets the
  opponent carry some green and switch to Provinces or Vineyards.
