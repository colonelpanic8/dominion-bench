# Key: Making Luck ep. 100, kingdom 2: Shepherd, Den of Sin, Stonemason, Silk Road, Cathedral

## Sources
- [Making Luck ep. 100 "Rapid-Fire Kingdom Analysis"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RQk58SzbZd0&t=1314s),
  2020-02-28, 21:54-30:00. Adam Horton and Wandering Winder, first look, no playtesting.

## Expert summary
No village, so the deck draws with its non-terminals (Archive, Den of Sin,
Shepherd) and plays one terminal per turn, which limits payload. Den of Sin
and Shepherd fit together: start-of-turn draw lines Shepherd up with Victory
cards to discard. Scouting Party also helps Shepherd. Stonemason is the
natural terminal: overpay to gain $5s, and occasionally trash. Payload is
modest (Wild Hunt, Swashbuckler, Remodel Gold into Province), but Silk Road,
Feodum and Pasture score outside Provinces, so the game may run long. Their
big split is Cathedral. One host would skip it: only six Coppers to trash, a
long game, and Shepherd wants green cards. The other ends up "very sure" of
opening Cathedral, arguing a Shepherd draw deck wants its Coppers gone fast,
and Cathedral's tempo is what wins.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Notes there's no village, so draw comes from non-terminals (Archive, Den of Sin, Shepherd) and only one terminal per turn.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Shepherd is a central draw card here, supported by Den of Sin (and Scouting Party) to line it up with green cards.
- **R3** [supporting] (consensus) Stonemason is the main terminal (overpay for $5 gains).
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Alternative VP (Silk Road, Feodum, Pasture) is part of the payload; expects a long game.
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus) Archive is weaker than Den of Sin here because strong trashing (Cathedral) is available.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Takes Cathedral early; a Shepherd draw deck wants Coppers gone fast.

## Traps
