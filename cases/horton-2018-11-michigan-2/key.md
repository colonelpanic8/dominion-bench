# Key: U-Con 2018 Set 2: Bandit Fort, Beggar, King's Court, Pooka

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion: Michigan Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-michigan-tournament-summary/),
  2018-11-12. Horton played this 3-player preliminary game at U-Con and
  finished 2nd. He didn't design it and says it was built so nothing is good.

## Expert summary
Horton had no faith in a King's Court deck: too many stop cards, and it needs
King's Court, Pooka and a Treasure lined up with almost no support and the
slowest thinning. He judged Beggars and Duchies too strong to ignore, opened
Jack of All Trades/Beggar and planned to turn Jack's Silvers into Changelings
and Beggars. All three players went heavy Beggar, and the game ended on
Coppers, Beggars and Changelings. The winner found room for one King's Court,
used it to gain a second Changeling, then King's Courted two Beggars for two
Provinces. Horton questions his Jack opening and suggests going straight to
Beggar, or even money despite Bandit Fort.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert + result) Beggar-based slog with Duchies (and Beggar Silvers) is the central plan; all three players went for it.
- **R2** [supporting] (result) The game ends on piles (Coppers, Beggars, Changelings), so pile control matters.
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) A full King's Court/Pooka deck is unreliable here: too many stop cards and very slow thinning.
- **R4** [supporting] (result) A single King's Court is still useful as payload in the Beggar deck (King's Court + Beggar for Provinces, or King's Court + Changeling).
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Bandit Fort penalizes Silvers and Golds, so Beggar's Silvers and money have a VP cost.

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Building around King's Court/Pooka as the main deck.
- **T2** (single expert, tentative) Opening Jack of All Trades instead of going straight for Beggar; Horton suspects it was a mistake.

## Disagreements
- Horton is unsure whether straight Beggar or plain money accepting the Bandit Fort penalty would have been better.

## Grader notes
- Low-confidence key: one game, a loss, and a board described as having
  nothing good. Weight R1 and R3 most.
- Conquest (Silvers plus VP tokens) interacts with Bandit Fort and Beggar;
  the source doesn't discuss it. Credit reasoning about it as beyond-key.
- 3-player game.
