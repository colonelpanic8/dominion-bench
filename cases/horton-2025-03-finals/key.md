# Key: Horton 2025-03 Finals Kingdom: Tragic Hero, Bank, Lurker, Way of the Turtle, Alliance

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "March 2025 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/march-2025-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, March 2025. Horton designed and playtested this
  kingdom for the final. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/12E1dAbWrWfyzVCSUhEPvG0ut0CSurOst9er15uUWjeY).

## Expert summary
Horton thinks the best strategy at some point is to give up current turns to
save for one huge turn that effectively wins the game. Way of the Turtle (set
the card aside to play next turn) is the only village, so from very early on
you must use your terminal space well, because it limits how long it takes to
pop off. Tragic Hero plus Bank is very potent payload: Tragic Hero trashes
itself for a Treasure when you reach 8 cards in hand, gaining Banks. Lurker,
helped by Way of the Turtle, lets you add Tragic Heroes to the deck, by gaining
them from the trash directly or via Artisan plays. There are enough tools that
you should rarely waste an Action. Treasure Map can be fun with Way of the
Turtle but isn't particularly good. There is plenty of payload for the big turn
as long as you have enough draw to draw some of the Banks you gain from
trashing Tragic Heroes along the way.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Plan: sacrifice some turns to save up for one huge game-winning turn.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Payload is Tragic Hero plus Bank: Tragic Heroes trash themselves for Banks.
- **R3** [core] (designer) Way of the Turtle is the only village, so terminal space is the limiting factor; avoid wasting Actions from early on.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Lurker (with Way of the Turtle) regains Tragic Heroes from the trash; Artisan also gains them.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Needs enough draw to draw the Banks gained during the turn.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Treasure Map with Way of the Turtle: fun but not good here.
