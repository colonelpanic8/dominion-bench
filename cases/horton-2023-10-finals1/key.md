# Key: Horton 2023-10 Finals Kingdom 1: Horn of Plenty, Overlord, Emissary, Band of Nomads

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "October 2023 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/october-2023-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, 2023-10-07. Horton designed and playtested this
  kingdom for the finals. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/17d822tX5MEwbZLUAaWKnAbqEaaxWVIHy-pzWNZHiW9M)
  (comment: explosive Horn/Band of Nomads turns; villages kept tight so
  Emissary's extra Action matters).

## Expert summary
The idea is a Horn of Plenty megaturn, but the draw is Emissary and Ironmonger,
which makes it hard to have enough Action density to draw consistently. You
want a fairly thin deck that uses Overlord's flexibility to keep turns going,
and you collect a stack of Favors by playing Overlord as Emissary at the right
times. Way of the Rat helps you get many Overlords, so the deck builds very
fast. The big trick is that Band of Nomads is more than emergency draw: once
you have drawn your deck, play a Horn of Plenty to gain another Horn, spend a
Favor for +1 Card to draw it, and play it the same turn. The first player to
reach this position and see it can deny Horns to the opponent, which
effectively ends the game.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Payload is a Horn of Plenty megaturn.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Band of Nomads chain: after drawing your deck, each Horn gains another Horn, and spending a Favor (+1 Card on gaining a $3+ card) draws it to play again.
- **R3** [core] (designer) Taking the Horns this way also denies them to the opponent, which effectively decides the game.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Overlord is the flexible core card (play as Emissary for Favors and draw, or as other $5-or-less Actions to keep turns going); Way of the Rat helps gain many Overlords quickly.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Keep the deck relatively thin; Emissary/Ironmonger draw is unreliable without Action density.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Bank Favors by playing Overlord-as-Emissary at the right times (Emissary gives +2 Favors when it causes a shuffle).

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Treating Band of Nomads only as a way to rescue a stalled turn and missing the Horn chain.
