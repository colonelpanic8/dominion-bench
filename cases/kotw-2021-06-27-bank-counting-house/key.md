# Key: KotW 6/27/2021: Counting House, Travelling Fair, Bank, Tower

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/o9dind/), 2021-06-27, 11 comments.
  EphesosX explained the combo; PathfindingLibrary (strong regular) gave the faster
  setup; Snip3 and quantumhovercraft posted solo goldfish screenshots; mountebanker
  gave the Bank finish.

## Expert summary
Everyone agrees this is the Travelling Fair + Counting House combo. Buy Travelling
Fair (+2 Buys, and you may topdeck cards you gain this turn) and Counting House with
your Coppers in play or discard, then topdeck every gain so you never reshuffle. Each
turn, Counting House pulls every Copper from the discard pile back to hand, and you
buy Travelling Fair, a Counting House to replace the one played, and more Coppers.
PathfindingLibrary: set it up faster by just buying Coppers. Banks finish it: once
buying one won't trigger a shuffle, buy Bank, then several Banks the next turn, then
empty the Provinces (mountebanker). Tower adds 1 VP per Copper if the Copper pile
empties. quantumhovercraft's goldfish finished in 8 turns, and he estimates about
66% to finish by turn 9 uninterrupted on 4/3. The counters are Enchantress, and
Jester running through your deck to force a shuffle, so topdeck padding cards.
Snip3 sees the mirror as a race in which attacking the player who sets up first may
be right.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Identifies the Travelling Fair + Counting House loop: topdeck all gains to avoid reshuffling, and use Counting House to recover every Copper each turn. "it's a very clear TF CH" (Snip3)
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Sets up fast by buying Coppers with the extra Buys. "You can set up the Counting House combo much faster by just buying coppers." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R3** [core] (majority) Finishes with Banks once the Copper count is high, then empties the Provinces. (mountebanker, Snip3)
- **R4** [core] (result) Expects the combo to finish around turn 8-9 uncontested. "Goldfishes in 8 turns" (quantumhovercraft)
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Tower turns an emptied Copper pile into about 1 VP per Copper. (EphesosX)
- **R6** [supporting] (majority) Names Enchantress and Jester as the threats to the loop and pads the deck with topdecked cards. (EphesosX, Snip3)
- **R7** [supporting] (single expert) Horse Traders for early Buys; EphesosX opened Horse Traders/Silver and would add one or two more.

## Traps
- None stated beyond R1: nobody in the thread argued for a plan other than the combo.

## Disagreements
- Whether to attack an opponent who sets up first (Snip3 leans yes; attacks are
  telegraphed, so the defender gets a turn to prepare).

## Grader notes
- The goldfish results are solo; no head-to-head game was reported.
- context/combos.md lists Counting House + Travelling Fair, so a careful analyst
  should spot it.
- Counting House, Farming Village, Horse Traders and Talisman are first-edition cards.
