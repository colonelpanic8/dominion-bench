# Key: Horton 2023-10 Finals Kingdom 2: Broker, Hunting Grounds, Swap, Magpie

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "October 2023 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/october-2023-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, 2023-10-07. Horton designed and playtested this
  kingdom for the finals. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/17d822tX5MEwbZLUAaWKnAbqEaaxWVIHy-pzWNZHiW9M).

## Expert summary
Broker is the only village, and the good draw (Hunting Grounds) and good
payload are all terminal, which makes the board awkward. But the draw and
payload are good enough to be worth going for, and there are plenty of ways to
build a reliable deck that keeps enough "Broker food" (cards to trash to Broker
for +Actions) around. Many Swaps ensure you always have a Broker in hand when
you need it without owning too many, and you can Swap Magpies back to the
Supply and re-gain them the same turn without much trouble.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Builds a draw deck despite Broker being the only village; the terminal draw and payload are good enough.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Broker gives Actions by trashing cards, so the deck needs a steady supply of Broker food.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Many Swaps ensure a Broker is in hand when needed without overloading on Brokers.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Swap can return Magpies to the Supply and re-gain them the same turn.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Hunting Grounds is the good terminal draw.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Concluding the board can't support a draw deck because Broker is the only village.
