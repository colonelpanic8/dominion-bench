# Key: 2026 Online Championship final, game 2: Artificer, Mastermind, Livery, Cursed Village, Colonnade

## Sources
- [Lemonspawn and Sharur, neutral commentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3oj4O-lLaH4), 17:30-38:00.
  Quotes are attributed to "commentator". Spectator sillypotter (a former champion) added a
  pre-game opinion in chat, noted where used.
- Result, read from the end screen: agoo03 (player 1) won 33-3, 13 turns to 12. agoo03's deck:
  6 Artificer, 9 Wayfarer, 8 Chariot Race, 3 Cursed Village, 6 Rope, 1 Mastermind, 1 Abundance,
  4 Silver, 2 Copper, 3 Provinces, 1 Estate, and 14 VP taken from Colonnade. yurikamome's deck:
  3 Mastermind, 1 Livery, 2 Artificer, 4 Rope, 1 Cursed Village, 2 Chariot Race, 1 Wayfarer,
  1 Silver, 4 Copper, no Victory cards.

## Expert summary
The commentators read the board as Artificer plus draw: Cursed Village (draw-to-6) refills the hand
after Artificer discards, Mastermind replays the best Action three times (Livery for Horses, or
Wayfarer), Livery's Horses add draw and Actions, and Rope (bought heavily) and Abundance give +Buy,
with Rope also trashing. They rated Mastermind as the most important card, above Livery and well
above Captain, and dismissed Chariot Race and Road Network as marginal. Both players opened into
Rope and Artificer.

The game went the other way. yurikamome built the Livery/Mastermind deck the commentators
preferred, but it came together slowly and stalled on a turn with Mastermind and no Rope.
agoo03 built a wide Artificer/Wayfarer/Cursed Village deck with one Mastermind and no Livery,
bought several copies of Actions per turn with Rope's +Buy to take 14 VP from Colonnade, and
finished with a trick: Artificer gains a Copper, which makes Wayfarer cost $0, so later Artificers
discarding nothing gain Wayfarers. The commentators said they still didn't believe in skipping
Livery/Mastermind but conceded agoo03's deck "works" and that yurikamome made questionable choices.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) Artificer is central: discard junk to gain $5 cards (later Provinces) onto the deck, with Cursed Village drawing back up to 6. Both players built on Artificer.
- **R2** [core] (consensus + result) Rope is the economy buy: it trashes, draws next turn and gives +Buy. "Efficiently getting there is probably all about just getting a bunch of ropes." (commentator)
- **R3** [core] (consensus) Mastermind is the strongest single card, better than Captain, and Mastermind on Livery or Wayfarer is the big turn. "mastermind is more crucial than livery." (commentator)
- **R4** [supporting] (result) Colonnade is a real VP source: with +Buy, gaining an Action you already have in play during the Buy phase takes 2 VP. agoo03 took 14 VP this way.
- **R5** [supporting] (result + commentator) Wayfarer copies the cost of the last card gained, so gaining a Copper first lets Artificer (discarding nothing) or a $0 buy gain Wayfarers. agoo03 used it to take many Wayfarers.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) If you want both Livery and Mastermind, get Livery first. "If you were going to get a livery and a mastermind, you should have gotten the livery first." (commentator)
- **R7** [supporting] (consensus) Cursed Village is a village and draw here despite its Hex, and pairs with Artificer's discards.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Captain over Mastermind. "I would always buy mastermind over captain" (commentator)
- **T2** (consensus) Road Network as a priority buy; called "pretty marginal".
- **T3** (single expert) Abundance as a main buy rather than an occasional gain with Artificer.

## Disagreements
- Chariot Race: the commentators called it "marginal" filler, but agoo03 bought eight and they gave
  him $, VP and Colonnade targets in his winning deck.
- Livery/Mastermind vs wide Artificer: the commentators and sillypotter ("livery MM are too great")
  favoured the Livery/Mastermind core; agoo03 won 33-3 without Livery. One game, with bad draws for
  yurikamome, so the result is noisy.
