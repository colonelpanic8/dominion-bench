# Key: Making Luck ep. 99/100: Swindler, Counterfeit, Encampment, Margrave, Silos (Shelters)

## Sources
- [Making Luck ep. 99 "Swindler"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2rBM1ZSderQ&t=3695s), 2020-01-31,
  61:35-79:00: Adam Horton and Wandering Winder's first look at the kingdom at the end of a Swindler episode.
- [Making Luck ep. 100](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RQk58SzbZd0&t=258s), 2020-02-28,
  4:18-11:25: review after playing a few games.

## Expert summary
Swindler dominates. Counterfeit is the only trashing, and it can't trash
Curses. A Copper Swindled into a Curse stays forever, while an opponent's
Counterfeit can be Swindled into a Duchy. Encampment is the only village
besides Necropolis and gets Swindled into Estates or Shelter-cost cards.
Gold is effectively Swindler-proof, since it comes back as Gold. Both hosts
open Swindler plus Silver or Oasis (they compute about 91% to hit $5 either
way and call the difference tiny). They get to Counterfeit fast, contest
Encampments, and add at least one Margrave as the main draw. Shelters
usually discourage Swindler, but here you want to hit the opponent's
Necropolis (their only other village). Pure money without Swindler (Gear,
Counterfeit, Guildhall) is rejected, because an unharried opponent sets up
Encampments and Margrave/Haunted Woods.

After play: Swindler made the games a luck-fest. What your Swindler hits
mattered far more than the opening, and neither host often had a second
Swindler working. Counterfeit was good, and Margrave was pretty good. Silos
was fine later, though worse once Margrave attacks start. Plunder beat Gold
in practice even though Gold is Swindler-proof, but it was "much closer
than normal".

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus + result) Swindler is the key card: opens with one Swindler. "You want to open with at least one Swindler." (a host)
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Gets Counterfeit early to trash Coppers before they are Swindled into permanent Curses (Counterfeit can't trash Curses).
- **R3** [supporting] (consensus) Opening partner is Silver or Oasis (difference tiny); not Swindler/Swindler.
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Contests Encampment (the only real village) but expects Swindler to make it hard to keep many.
- **R5** [supporting] (result) At least one Margrave; Counterfeit and Margrave performed well in testing.
- **R6** [supporting] (consensus) Notes Gold is Swindler-proof here, yet Plunder still usually beats Gold.
- **R7** [supporting] (single expert) Shelters: hitting the opponent's Necropolis with Swindler is actively good on this board.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Skipping Swindler for a Gear/Counterfeit/Guildhall money deck: the opponent sets up Encampments and attacks freely.
- **T2** (single expert) Opening Swindler/Swindler (or a Messenger to hand both players Swindlers): too many terminals.
- **T3** (single expert) Opening Silos: fine later, bad once Margrave attacks are flying.

## Disagreements
- Encampment's importance: one host thinks it matters a lot; the other agrees
  but expects to keep only about one, so plays "Swindler money-ish" with a
  single Encampment.
- Guildhall: one host would take it on a $5 hand with two Counterfeits already; the other is doubtful.
- Whether the Peddlers will run out (which would weaken Swindler) was left open.

## Grader notes
- The hosts say the outcome hinged on Swindler hits. Credit any plan built
  on Swindler + Counterfeit with an Encampment/Margrave draw component.
