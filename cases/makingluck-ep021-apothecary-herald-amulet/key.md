## Expert summary
The winning deck, which Adam proposed before playing and which held up over
many games with Wandering Winder, opens Potion for Apothecary. It eventually
trashes its Coppers, but keeps Apothecary because Herald needs it: Herald's
village effect is reliable only with Apothecaries setting up the top of the
deck. The payload is terminal-heavy, so the villages are essential: Amulet
gains Silvers, you draw them the same turn, and Spice Merchant trashes them
for +$2 and +Buy, with Royal Blacksmith as the draw. This deck builds to
double Provinces faster than a single-Province deck can win, which one
host hadn't expected. Get Vampire fairly early even though it doesn't fit neatly:
it gains Heralds, Spice Merchants and late Duchies, attacks, and Bat can
trash junk. Sea Hag isn't an opener (you want Potion). It's a tactical buy
to punish an opponent who didn't build enough control, and its Curses can
even be Bat food. It is needed against a Big Money opponent such as the
client's bot. Contraband and Apprentice don't belong in the best deck.
Gains are scarce (only Amulet adds cards over time), so treat them as
precious.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Opens Potion (Apothecary), not Sea Hag.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Herald is the village, and Apothecary is kept (even after Coppers are gone) to make Herald reliable.
- **R3** [core] (consensus) Payload engine: Amulet gains Silvers, drawn the same turn and trashed by Spice Merchant for +$2 and +Buy; Royal Blacksmith draws.
- **R4** [core] (consensus) The deck builds to double-Province turns and beats a single-Province deck.
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus) Picks up Vampire fairly early, for gaining Heralds, Spice Merchants and Duchies and for its attack.
- **R6** [supporting] (consensus) Sea Hag is tactical: buy it only to punish an opponent who neglected deck control (or a money deck), not as a core card.
- **R7** [supporting] (single expert) Gains are scarce (only Amulet adds cards over time), so Bat can trash Silvers or received Curses profitably.
- **R8** [supporting] (result) Over many games with Wandering Winder, Adam's Apothecary/Herald/Spice Merchant deck was found best; a host lost to the client's Big Money bot when he skipped Sea Hag, and Sea Hag crushes that bot.

## Traps

- **T2** (consensus) Contraband.
- **T3** (consensus) Apprentice in the main deck: useful only in weaker early-lead strategies.
