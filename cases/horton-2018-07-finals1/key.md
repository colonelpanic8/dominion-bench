# Key: Horton Summer 2018 finals 1: Pooka, Inheritance, Miser, Lost Arts

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, Summer 2018 Tournament Summary](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-summer-2018-tournament-summary/):
  the designer's write-up of a 3-player finals kingdom. He says 3P kingdoms get less
  playtesting and he is less confident here.

## Expert summary
Horton built a puzzle-style opening into this board. The only thing he's confident about:
on turn 1, play Cursed Gold with four Coppers ($7) and buy Inheritance. There are many good
Inheritance targets and none is clearly best. Shepherd, Baron and Miser all look attractive,
but he leans toward Village, with Miser a close second. He admits building many bad decks
here. He thinks Shepherd is vastly overrated and isn't convinced by Pooka. All the draw is
dicey, so he doubts a huge deck is right, and he suggests blitzing Misers until each is worth
$8 might be best. The only way to trash Cursed Gold is to Counterfeit it, which gives two
Curses, and nothing trashes Curses.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Turn 1: Cursed Gold plus four Coppers for $7, buying Inheritance.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Notes the draw is unreliable, so a huge engine is doubtful.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Inherits Village (Horton's lean) or Miser.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Considers a Miser-focused deck (Misers worth $8 each).
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Notes that Cursed Gold can only be removed by Counterfeit (gaining two Curses) and Curses can't be trashed.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Building around Shepherd: Horton calls it vastly overrated.
- **T2** (designer) Relying on Pooka: he isn't convinced it works here.

## Disagreements
- Horton himself is unsure: Village vs Miser vs Shepherd/Baron as the Inheritance target.

## Grader notes
- Quality C. R1 is the only point the designer is confident about; weight the rest lightly.
- 3-player game; first-edition Royal Seal; Inheritance pre-errata.
