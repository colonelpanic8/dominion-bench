# Key: Making Luck ep. 100, kingdom 3: Amulet, Treasure Map, Wall, Colony

## Sources
- [Making Luck ep. 100 "Rapid-Fire Kingdom Analysis"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RQk58SzbZd0&t=1810s),
  2020-02-28, 30:10-34:20. Adam Horton and Wandering Winder, first look, no playtesting.

## Expert summary
No real draw, no +Buy, Wall penalizing big decks, and Colonies: everything
points to thinning hard and buying Colonies with a small, strong deck. Both
hosts open double Amulet to trash aggressively (gaining a Silver or two as
needed). Then they build money to hit $11: Treasure Maps once thin (four
Golds), one or more Platinums, and a few Silvers. The goal is about five
Colonies. A Monument or Lighthouse may fit depending on terminal space, and
a $2 hand buys Coin of the Realm. Duplicate, Hoard and the remaining cards
aren't wanted. Hoard is marginal given Wall and Colonies.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Identifies no draw, no +Buy, Wall and Colony as pointing to a thin money deck aiming at Colonies.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Opens Amulet/Amulet to thin aggressively.
- **R3** [core] (consensus) Buys Colonies (about five) as the win condition, with Platinum in the deck.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Treasure Map after thinning is the fastest route to the money ($11).
- **R5** [supporting] (consensus) Keeps the deck small because of Wall.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Duplicate shenanigans (e.g. duplicating Treasure Maps): no time for it.
- **T2** (consensus) Hoard: marginal here because of Wall and Colonies.
- **T3** (consensus) A village/engine build: there's no draw to support one.

## Disagreements
- Minor: how many Silvers to gain with Amulet (one vs two or three) and
  whether a Lighthouse or Monument earns a terminal slot.
