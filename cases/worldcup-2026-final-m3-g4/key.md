# Key: 2026 World Cup final, Minionpawn vs yurikamome, game 4: Blockade, Marchland, Canal, Shelters

## Sources
- [JNails with drsteelhammer, neutral commentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hY-_wubT4nw), 1:21:30-1:41:30.
  Captions don't mark speakers, so quotes are "commentator".
- Match: Dominion World Cup 2026 final, match 3. yurikamome was player 1; Minionpawn won 48-26.

## Expert summary
Eight terminals and no village except Necropolis (and Staff or Orb if Search happens to give
them). The commentator's plan before the opening: hit $7 for Canal (cards cost $1 less), then
play Blockade a lot, since with Canal it gains $5 cards, and rush Marchlands and Duchies plus some
Estates; that beats Provinces. Open Blockade. Asceticism (pay $ to trash that many cards) clears the
Shelters (Hovel, Overgrown Estate). Each player has 5 Favors (from Importer) for
exactly one Mountain Folk use, which could have been saved to spike $7 for Canal. yurikamome
spent his Mountain Folk on turn 1 and opened Blockade/Barge; Minionpawn used his on turn 2 to buy a
Marchland (whose +Buy took a Search with it), which the commentators first mocked. Minionpawn
then added Death Cart, which can trash itself for $5 for double Marchland and gains Ruins.
Blockading Marchland makes the opponent take a Curse if they gain one. yurikamome built
Faithful Hound/Innkeeper draw, which didn't come together. Minionpawn won the Marchland split
6-2 and the game 48-26; the commentators concluded the Marchland line beat the Barge build.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert + result) VP comes from Marchlands, Duchies and Estates gained in quantity rather than Provinces; Marchland is the key pile. "Rush Marchlands and Duchies and probably some Estates and that'll be better than provinces." (commentator)
- **R2** [core] (single expert + result) Blockade is the main gainer; open Blockade.
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Canal at $7 lets Blockade gain $5 cards (Marchland, Duchy) and is worth hitting $7 early.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Asceticism trashes Shelters/Estates early; the one Mountain Folk use (5 Favors) can spike $7.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Blockading a Marchland the opponent wants denies it: they take a Curse if they gain one.
- **R6** [supporting] (result) Winning the Marchland split decides the game (6-2 here); Marchland's on-gain +Buy and discard-for-$ make early Marchland buys affordable.
- **R7** [supporting] (single expert) Ruins are an easy third pile via Death Cart, which gains two at a time.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus + result) A Barge-based draw engine (with Faithful Hound/Innkeeper): it can work with Necropolis but is slower than the Marchland/Duchy rush. "So it turns out that the death cart plan was better than the barge." (commentator)
- **T2** (single expert) Spending the one Mountain Folk on turn 1 for a Barge instead of saving it for a key price.

## Disagreements
- The commentators called Minionpawn's turn-2 Marchland/Search "getting too fancy", then credited
  "the March line open" with winning. Their own line (Canal first, then Blockade gains) was not
  played exactly by either player.
