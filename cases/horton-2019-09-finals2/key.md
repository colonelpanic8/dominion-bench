## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion: Fall 2019 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-fall-2019-tournament-summary/),
  tournament 2019-09-21. Horton designed and playtested this kingdom for the
  2-player finals bracket. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ViQF-GpoQYWCvz7H-Mr_4xKip7v59aUu_A2K9Z_nUd0).

## Expert summary
Capitalism is the only real village. Patron mostly exists to enable it:
with Capitalism, Patrons (+$2) are Treasures, so you play them in the Buy
phase without Actions and bank Villagers for later turns. Smaller synergies
matter: Loan and Journeyman reveal Patron (Coffers); Tragic Hero gains great
cards once Capitalism is active (Grand Market); and Zombie Mason (from
Necromancer) can trash Estates if you draw your deck, Oasis-discard an
Estate, then play Zombie Mason, or more simply play Harbinger to put an
Estate back first. Harbinger matters beyond Museum: you avoid adding Treasures
while Loan is still trashing, you don't want too many Oases, and Harbinger
later helps Loan hit your last Coppers after Capitalism. Horton's line: open
Patron/Loan, Library with the first $5, aim for a Villager or two early (bigger
early turns bank more Villagers). Then one Necromancer and more Patrons, maybe
a second Library, some Oases and Harbingers. Switch from Library to Tragic
Hero for draw and buy Capitalism around then, ideally after 4+ Coppers are
gone; this timing is the tricky decision. After a few Tragic Heroes,
Journeyman becomes the main draw because it reveals Patrons. Missing these
synergies leaves you exposed to Ghost Ship.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Capitalism is the key village effect: Patrons become Treasures, played in the Buy phase for $ and Villagers.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Opens Patron/Loan, with Library as the first $5.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Tragic Hero becomes strong after Capitalism (gains Grand Markets) and later replaces Library as draw.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Journeyman (and Loan) reveal Patrons for Coffers; Journeyman becomes the main draw late.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) One Necromancer; Zombie Mason trashes Estates, set up with Harbinger (or Oasis).
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Times Capitalism after several Coppers are trashed by Loan; delay adding Treasures while Loan trashes.
- **R7** [supporting] (designer) Bank Villagers early for later bigger turns.

- **T2** (designer) Dismissing Harbinger as a Museum-only card.
