# Key: KotW 12/19/2021: Ironmonger, Sleigh, Tracker, Bonfire

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/rkd9b9/), 2021-12-19, 12 comments.
  willowhelmiam gave a full engine plan; PathfindingLibrary (strong regular) gave the
  opening and defended the engine against SignError; shadowlancerr proposed Treasure Map
  money; matress_mozzerella reported a game against a bot.

## Expert summary
willowhelmiam and PathfindingLibrary agree on an engine: Bonfire thins (trash up to 2
cards you have in play), draw comes from Ironmonger and Sleigh, and payload from
Festival and Messenger (willowhelmiam). PathfindingLibrary opens Bonfire/Cobbler and
calls Snowy Village + Tracker + Sleighs reliable draw: Tracker (with its Pouch)
topdecks the Horses Sleigh gains, so you start each turn with a stack of Horses on top
of the deck. willowhelmiam wants to pile the Ironmongers (Baker's Coffers for 2
Ironmongers on 4/3) and Bonfire Coppers while keeping payload up. shadowlancerr
proposed Cobbler/Warehouse into Treasure Maps and Gold. SignError found it plausible
and noted money cedes the Ironmongers. PathfindingLibrary: "The engine here looks very
clear," and Treasure Map could be late engine payload. SignError then worked through
the numbers and concluded the engine holds with enough Ironmongers and Sleighs.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Builds an engine of Ironmonger, Sleigh, Tracker and Snowy Village, thinned with Bonfire. (willowhelmiam, PathfindingLibrary)
- **R2** [core] (single expert) Tracker topdecking Sleigh's Horses makes the draw reliable. "You are topdecking a big stack of Horses each turn. It won't dud." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Opens Bonfire/Cobbler. "Bonfiire/Cobbler." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Contests or piles the Ironmongers early. (willowhelmiam; SignError notes money cedes them)
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Treasure Map is possible late payload in the engine. (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Payload otherwise is thin (Festival, Messenger, Silvers). (willowhelmiam, SignError)

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Treasure Map money as the main plan. "Alt-VP and attacks are not pre-requisites to building." (PathfindingLibrary)

## Disagreements
- shadowlancerr's Treasure Map money (with a Messenger to hand the opponent a useless
  Treasure Map) vs the engine. matress_mozzerella opened Bonfire, then
  Messenger/Treasure Map, and won against a bot after a turn-5 Treasure Map hit; no
  human test.

## Grader notes
- The engine has two independent supporters and survived SignError's numbers check;
