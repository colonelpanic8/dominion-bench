# Key: Making Luck ep. 100, kingdom 5: Pixie, City Quarter, Inn, Wild Hunt, Triumph, Arena (Colony)

## Sources
- [Making Luck ep. 100 "Rapid-Fire Kingdom Analysis"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RQk58SzbZd0&t=2470s),
  2020-02-28, 41:10-53:25. Adam Horton and Wandering Winder, first look, no playtesting.

## Expert summary
Everything is available (trashing, villages, strong draw, attacks), but
payload is awkward: Contraband is the only +Buy. Colonies are so many points
that they dwarf the alternatives, and both hosts are mostly planning a deck
that buys a Colony per turn. Goat covers early thinning, so neither opens
Transmogrify (one would only without Goat). A Mint opening is rejected even
in the unlikely best case. A Mint/Contraband gain-everything deck was
considered and dismissed: it can't outscore Colonies, and Triumph pressure
on Estates hurts it. Werewolf is rarely taken over Wild Hunt.

The hosts then diverge. One wants many Pixies with City Quarter as the
village, hoping for Forest's Gift Boons and backdooring Triumph gains, and
expects to win the Arena split. The other wants Inn and Wild Hunt as the
draw, a few Silvers and $5s, and believes that deck greens better and
reaches $10 every turn. Opening suggestions: Silver/Pixie, or Sculptor/Pixie
on 5/2. One host would open double Silver and let Goat trash an Estate.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (consensus) Recognizes Colonies as the dominant payload; plans to buy Colonies with a drawing deck.
- **R2** [core] (consensus) Notes the payload problem: Contraband is the only reliable +Buy.
- **R3** [supporting] (consensus) Goat handles early thinning, so no Transmogrify or Mint opening.
- **R4** [supporting] (consensus) Wild Hunt is good here and preferred over Werewolf.
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) Inn + Wild Hunt as the main draw, with a few Silvers, greens better.
- **R6** [supporting] (single expert) Many Pixies with City Quarter as the village.

## Traps
- **T1** (consensus) Mint-into-Contrabands mass-gain/Triumph deck: can't outscore Colonies and is vulnerable around Estates/Triumph.
- **T2** (consensus) Opening Mint (e.g. Mint/Pixie on 5/2): "hot garbage" by one host's verdict, and the other agreed.

## Disagreements
- Pixie/City Quarter/Triumph (one host) vs Inn/Wild Hunt with Silvers (the other).
- Whether Triumph would be strong if Colonies weren't in the game (one host yes).

## Grader notes
- Contested key. Score mainly on R1-R4 and the traps; either draw package
  is acceptable.
