# Key: Horton Winter 2019 Table 2: Bandit Fort, Triumph, Sculptor, Courtyard

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion: Winter 2019 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/repost-dominion-winter-2019-tournament-summary/),
  tournament 2019-01-12. Horton designed and playtested this kingdom for a
  2-player tournament (played in the Swiss rounds). [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1__ePuWrM5XpCsVuLMIA3dmGWHKNOoZV5auC1ObWGbbk).

## Expert summary
Bandit Fort punishes Silvers and Golds, and there's no good way to Provinces
without them. Many players accepted the penalty and played Silver/Gold
anyway, and some won that way. The designed alternative is big Triumph turns
despite the lack of decent draw: bank Villagers from Sculptor plays on
earlier turns, then spend them on Courtyards on a turn with a Ghost in play
(from Cemetery's Haunted Mirror) to gain many cards and buy one or two big
Triumphs. It's hard to pilot: keep the right number of Hideouts so the deck
doesn't bloat and so you can clear out the Silvers before the game ends;
on big turns maximize gains with several Market Squares for extra Copper
buys, Estates or Cemeteries for Hideout to trash (gaining Curses), and
enough Sculptors to keep gaining Silvers. Horton thinks two 7-8 VP Triumphs
every two or three turns is achievable.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer + result) Triumph is the main payload: big gain turns score large Triumphs.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Sculptor gains Silvers (and Villagers); banked Villagers fuel a big Courtyard turn.
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) A Ghost (via Haunted Mirror/Cemetery) doubles Courtyard draw on the big turn.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Market Square gives +Buy to gain extra cheap cards (Coppers) on Triumph turns.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Hideout trims bloat and clears Silvers before game end to dodge Bandit Fort, at the cost of Curses.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Notes Bandit Fort makes Silver/Gold money costly; Provinces need another route.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Treating it as a normal Silver/Gold money board without accounting for Bandit Fort's -2 VP per Silver and Gold.

## Disagreements
- Some players won with Silver/Gold despite Bandit Fort. Horton presents the Triumph deck as a viable, harder alternative, not a proven winner.

## Grader notes
- The Triumph line is supported by a few players' success, not a decisive
  head-to-head. A well-argued money plan that prices in Bandit Fort earns
  partial credit on R1.
