# Key: Horton 2025-10 Designed Kingdom 3: Forts, Destrier, Reckless Guide, Exploration

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "October 2025 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/october-2025-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, October 2025. Horton designed and playtested this
  kingdom for the bracket. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1i-xyLMNrixItE8fQi91r3od4uZAdHhdTSi86wCznmnw).

## Expert summary
Horton thinks the Forts pile (Tent, Garrison, Hill Fort, Stronghold) is the
powerhouse, especially Hill Fort and Garrison. A Tent/Exploration opening
should guarantee a path to two Garrisons fairly quickly; then focus on taking
the Hill Forts until they are gone. Once a Reckless Guide or two is in the
deck, you can gain many helpful cards until you are as thin and drawing as
much as you want. If the opponent doesn't contest Hill Forts at once, Ducats
and Hill Fort gains can make Destriers very cheap (Destrier costs $1 less per
card gained this turn) and the game can get out of control, so the Tent opening
matters partly to keep control of that race.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) The Forts pile is the core; Hill Fort and Garrison are the key cards in it.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Opens Tent/Exploration.
- **R3** [core] (designer) Gets two Garrisons fairly early, then contests and empties the Hill Forts.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) One or two Reckless Guides help gain and thin to the desired deck.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Ducats plus Hill Fort gains make Destrier cheap; an uncontested Hill Fort/Destrier deck can run away, so the Hill Forts should be contested.

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Letting the opponent take the Hill Forts uncontested.
