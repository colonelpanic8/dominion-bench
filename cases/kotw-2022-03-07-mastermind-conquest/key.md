# Key: KotW 3/6/2022: Mastermind, Enchantress, Conquest, Delve

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/t8dg9k/), 2022-03-07.
  pancake_stacker12, willowhelmiam, manchu_pitchu and IlstrawberrySeed
  outlined engine or Silver-gaining plans; GonffzCheeze dismissed Big Money;
  Either_Psychology_82 argued Big Money. EphesosX clarified Enchantress.

## Expert summary
Most commenters want to thin fast (Forager, Scrap, Temple) and build a large,
consistent deck with plenty of +Actions (Bandit Camp, Squire, Mastermind),
then score with Conquest: Silver gains from Delve, Scrap and Squire count for
Conquest, so a turn with several Buys can take 30+ VP (pancake_stacker12:
4 Delves and 4 Conquests from $32 is 36 VP, versus 24 for 4 Provinces).
Mastermind triples key Actions, and Coven is decent with Mastermind.
Enchantress only blanks the first Action each turn, so Mastermind still
works through it. The Big Money suggestion was dismissed.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Thin quickly (Forager, Scrap, Temple) and build an engine rather than money. "Trash fast, build enormous and consistent" (pancake_stacker12)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Conquest is the main VP payload, fed by Silver gains (Delve, Scrap, Squire), possibly in multi-Buy megaturns.
- **R3** [supporting] (majority) Mastermind is a key piece, multiplying Coven or other strong Actions.
- **R4** [supporting] (single player) Enchantress only affects the first Action played each turn, so Masterminded cards still work on later plays. (EphesosX)
- **R5** [supporting] (single player) Squire trashed to gain Coven (an Attack); Forager or Scrap does the trashing.
- **R6** [supporting] (single player) Opens Forager/Enchantress (willowhelmiam) or Scrap/Forager (IlstrawberrySeed).

## Traps
- **T1** (majority) Plain Big Money because the attacks look scary. "Big Money gets absolutely destroyed here." (GonffzCheeze)

## Disagreements
- Opening: Forager/Enchantress vs. Scrap/Forager; nobody tested either.
- willowhelmiam expected not to draw the whole deck; pancake_stacker12
  wanted a huge consistent engine.
