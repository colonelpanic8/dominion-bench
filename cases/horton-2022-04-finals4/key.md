# Key: Horton Spring 2022 Finals 4: Develop, Gamble, Way of the Turtle, Monument

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion: Spring 2022 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-spring-2022-tournament-summary/),
  tournament 2022-04-09. Horton designed and playtested this kingdom (30+
  test games) for the 2-player finals bracket. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Yf8ZZyyavqIiKeg_rTFl1x-KuDldt_1dtOoN7c4Pc0s).

## Expert summary
Horton started from Gamble: make it worth spending all your money every turn
on Gambles, with Monument as a Gamble payoff. He then made Develop, Gamble
and Way of the Turtle the core of the big draw, so almost any card you hit
with Gamble is welcome. In practice the decision tree explodes: so many
things are good, and Develop can change your deck drastically in one turn.
After 30+ tests he says he still doesn't know what's best, or whether a
concrete plan is even right; he only knows many options are strong.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Gamble is central: spending most of your money on repeated Gambles (+Buy, play the revealed Action or Treasure) is a main engine.
- **R2** [supporting] (designer) Develop and Way of the Turtle combine with Gamble for big turns (Turtle sets cards aside for next turn).
- **R3** [supporting] (designer) Monument pairs with a Gamble-heavy deck.
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Flexibility: Develop reshapes the deck quickly, so adapt rather than lock into one plan.

## Traps
- None identified by the designer.

## Disagreements
