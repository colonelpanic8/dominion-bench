# Key: U-Con 2018 Set 4: Cultist, Lost Arts, Orchard, Fairgrounds

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion: Michigan Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-michigan-tournament-summary/),
  2018-11-12. Horton played this 3-player preliminary game at U-Con and won.
  He didn't design it, and he credits a very lucky turn 3.

## Expert summary
Horton calls the board perfectly fine apart from Cultist, which dominates it.
He opened Cultist (one opponent did too, the third ignored it), hit $6 on
turn 3 and bought Lost Arts on his Cultist pile. From there he added many
different cards until Magic Lamp (Secret Cave's Heirloom) went off and he
scored several Orchard sets. Later he had enough draw for a good Mint, then
bought Fairgrounds. He notes nobody Embargoed Cultist, which would have
annoyed him, though he would still have taken Curses to reach three
Cultists. The game ended on time with Horton far ahead; he had 19 unique
cards for Fairgrounds.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert + result) Cultist is the key card and should be opened / contested.
- **R2** [supporting] (single expert + result) Lost Arts on Cultist is a strong early buy.
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert + result) Variety payload: Orchard sets, Magic Lamp Wishes, and Fairgrounds reward many different cards.
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Embargo on the Cultist pile is the main counter to a Cultist player.
- **R5** [supporting] (result) Mint is a useful late payload once the deck draws well.

## Traps
- **T1** (result) Ignoring Cultist: the player who did finished behind.

## Disagreements
- None recorded.

## Grader notes
- Horton credits luck (Cultist with six Coppers on turn 3), so the key is weak
  on the full plan. Weight R1 heavily.
- Ruins from Cultist can add differently named cards for Fairgrounds (Horton
  counted his Abandoned Mines toward 19 unique cards).
- 3-player game.
