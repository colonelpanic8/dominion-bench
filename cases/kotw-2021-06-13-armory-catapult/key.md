# Key: KotW 6/13/2021: Recruiter, Fortress, Peasant, Catapult

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/nzgr8p/), 2021-06-13, 8 comments.
  Hasselhof88 gave openings and a plan; PathfindingLibrary and treelorf (strong
  regulars) corrected the Peasant/Teacher part; hochstrd and tallg33s3 added tricks.

## Expert summary
The clearest point is PathfindingLibrary's: Recruiter trashing Fortress (which
returns to hand) gives 4 Villagers each time, essentially unlimited Actions, so the
Teacher +Action token isn't needed. He would put the +Card token on Cavalry, the card
he expects to have most of, and treelorf doubts there is time to reach Teacher at
all. Hasselhof88 opens Catapult/March on 4/3 (Peasant into March/Catapult on 2/5),
wants Watchtower soon against Catapult, and Fortress for Actions; tallg33s3 suspects
Catapult is the trap because Watchtower is so good. Other tricks: Armory gaining
Cavalry, and Watchtower topdecking Horses from Cavalry.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (single expert) Recruiter + Fortress: trashing Fortress returns it to hand and gives Villagers, so Actions are effectively unlimited. "Recruiter + Fortress will leave you with essentially unlimited actions." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R2** [supporting] (single expert) Cavalry is the main draw card (and +Card token target if Peasant goes that far). (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R3** [supporting] (majority) Watchtower is valuable against Catapult's Curses and to topdeck gains such as Horses. (Hasselhof88, hochstrd, tallg33s3)
- **R4** [supporting] (single expert) Opens with Catapult (Catapult/March on 4/3). (Hasselhof88)
- **R5** [supporting] (single expert) One Recruiter is enough. (Hasselhof88)

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Taking the Teacher +Action token, or planning the game around reaching Teacher. "No +action token." (PathfindingLibrary); "I have a hard time imagining that there will be time for teacher here" (treelorf)

## Disagreements
- Catapult: Hasselhof88 opens it; tallg33s3 suspects it's a trap given Watchtower.
- Hasselhof88 found Rocks too slow but suggested Armory gaining Rocks to trash.
