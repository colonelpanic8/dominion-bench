# Key: KotW 8/28/2022: Village Green, Way of the Mole, Remake, Experiment

## Sources
- [r/dominion KotW thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/x0risr/), 2022-08-29.
  SignError called it an engine and explained the Way of the Mole/Village
  Green rules; PathfindingLibrary (strong regular) and ahicks58 gave one-line
  plans.

## Expert summary
An engine board: strong trashing, villages, draw, +Buy, gains and an attack
(SignError). The key trick is Way of the Mole with Village Green: Way of the
Mole discards your hand, and a discarded Village Green can be played (as
itself or as Way of the Mole), so a Mole play can net a fresh 6-card hand and
an extra Action. PathfindingLibrary's plan is "Remake+Experiment into Mole
DtX" (Way of the Mole as draw-to-X), with about one Remake. ahicks58 thinks
whoever wins the Village Green split wins, opening Remake/Experiment or
Old Witch/Hamlet.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (majority) Builds an engine. "this is definitely an Engine game." (SignError)
- **R2** [core] (majority) Way of the Mole + Village Green: discarding a Village Green to Way of the Mole lets it be played, refilling the hand with net +Actions.
- **R3** [supporting] (single expert) Remake (about one copy) and Experiment early, then Way of the Mole as the draw. "Remake+Experiment into Mole DtX." (PathfindingLibrary)
- **R4** [supporting] (single player) The Village Green split is pivotal. (ahicks58)
- **R5** [supporting] (single player) Opens Remake/Experiment, or Old Witch/Hamlet on 5/2. (ahicks58)

## Traps
- **T1** (single expert) Chaining two Village Greens as Way of the Mole on one discard: the second discards the first's draw. Playing the second as a normal Village Green is better. (SignError)
