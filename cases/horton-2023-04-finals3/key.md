# Key: Horton 2023-04 Finals Kingdom 3: Family of Inventors, Seer, Importer, Weaver

## Sources
- [Adam Horton, "Dominion: Spring 2023 Tournament Summary"](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-spring-2023-tournament-summary/),
  Cincinnati tournament, 2023-04-15. Horton designed and playtested this
  kingdom for the finals. [Kingdom sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1bUdgaAKlS6lsndwvGKeABDdWVf9etjaLFIMcRVTcgqU).

## Expert summary
The board uses Family of Inventors (put Favor tokens on piles to make those
cards cost $1 less each) to mess with costs, plus many cards that care about
costs, especially Seer (puts revealed cards costing $2-$4 into your hand).
Importer, the only Liaison, deliberately caps the Favors available. In
playtesting, reducing a card to $1 so Seer can't draw it was effective if you
lost the Seer split badly, and that came up in one of the two finals games.
Horton likes a Seer/Spice Merchant opening (reducing both with Favors), then a
Weaver, then focusing on the Seers until they're gone. After that, payload
comes fast: Weaver gains Silvers, Bandit gains Golds, and depending on draw you
add Barracks or a Marquis to draw payload faster (discard Weavers for payload,
or play Bandit and use Seers to draw it). Sometimes you reduce Gold so Seer can
draw it, if that helps you more than the opponent. Keeping two Favors to
threaten to drop Silver to $1 (so Seer can't draw it) can limit the opponent.

## Rubric
- **R1** [core] (designer) Opens Seer/Spice Merchant, using Favors (Family of Inventors) to cut their costs.
- **R2** [core] (designer) Contest the Seer pile hard; take Seers until they're gone.
- **R3** [core] (designer) Uses cost manipulation with Seer: reduce cards into or out of Seer's $2-$4 window (e.g. Gold down to $4 for yourself; a card to $1 to deny the opponent's Seers).
- **R4** [supporting] (designer) Gets a Weaver early; payload from Weaver Silvers and Bandit Golds.
- **R5** [supporting] (designer) Adds Barracks or Marquis to draw payload faster, depending on draw.
- **R6** [supporting] (designer) Importer is the only Liaison, so Favors are scarce; spend them deliberately (keep two as a threat on Silver).

## Traps
- **T1** (designer) Making a cost change that helps the opponent's Seers more than yours.
