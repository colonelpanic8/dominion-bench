**RESULT: I lost 64–66 to Expert (P2).** The game ended on turn 25 when the opponent bought the last Province.

**My final deck (45 cards):** 7 Province, 6 Duchy, 3 Estate, 1 Tunnel, 1 Curse, 5 Gold, 5 Silver, 11 Copper, 1 Council Room, 1 Jester, 1 Litter, 2 Border Guard.

**How the plan worked:**
- **The money part worked:** I opened Jester/Border Guard on a 5/2 split and added Council Room and Golds. I took Provinces from turn 11, led 45–38 on turn 19, and ended with 7 of the 8 Provinces.
- **The plan's biggest misjudgement was Vineyard:** it rejected Vineyard, but the opponent built a Litter/Border Guard/Market Square deck with Seaway for +Buy and took all 8 Vineyards. Each Vineyard ended up worth about 7 VP, so their score kept growing faster than a one-Buy money deck could. Jester was weak for me: it hit mostly Coppers and gave them just one Curse, at the very end.
- **The losing move was mine:** on turn 25 I bought the second-to-last Province to lead 65–60. That broke the Penultimate Province Rule, and they reached exactly $8 for the last one (their Jester also gave me a Curse that turn). A Duchy would have been safer, though it would have left only one Duchy and a three-pile ending in reach. I should also have started Duchies earlier, or treated Vineyard as a real threat once they bought their first Potion.
