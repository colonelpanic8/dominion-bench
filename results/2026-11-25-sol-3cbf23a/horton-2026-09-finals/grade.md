## Beyond the key
- A Triumph's Estate, gained in the Buy phase, shuts off both River Shrine's Clean-up gain and Exploration that turn: plausible. Both check for gains in the Buy phase, and Triumph gains an Estate.
- Chaining Triumphs needs $5 to clear each prior 5 Debt, leaving only the last outstanding; two Triumphs after five gains score 6 + 7: plausible. You can't buy while in Debt; each Triumph's Estate is itself a gain.
- Change from Estate to Alchemist costs 1 Debt before or after Trade, ignoring the Potion: plausible. Change compares and charges only $.
- Stonemason overpay after Trade: $4 total for Stonemason plus two Hostelries, $5 for two original-$5 Actions: plausible. Stonemason costs $1 and the overpaid amount must match the reduced cost exactly.
- Hut's Curse draws its recipient a card under Footpad's rule, so play Hut before Footpad: plausible. Footpad says "an Action phase", and the gain happens in the attacker's; the discard-to-3 then follows.
- Discarding Weaver to an opponent's Footpad lets you play it and draw the gained Silvers: plausible. Weaver plays when discarded other than in Clean-up, and the gains happen in an Action phase.
- Artificer discarding zero to gain a Copper adds one to each later Triumph; seven discards gain a Province after Trade: plausible. Exactly $0 is Copper; Province costs $7 under Trade.
- Turn 3 Shrine line: take Hostelry in Clean-up and discard two unplayed Coppers for two Horses: plausible. Shrine gains at the start of Clean-up, when unplayed Coppers are still in hand.
- Stonemason on Gold for two Duchies (6 VP): plausible. Duchy costs less than Gold; no source covers it.
- Flourishing Trade starts with five Suns in two players: plausible, per the Rising Sun setup.
- Weaver's lose-track (stop-moving) complications: not raised, so no extra credit.
- Opening 4/3 River Shrine / Silver over Exploration: doubtful. It forgoes the designer's opening and early token accumulation for a Silver of marginal use in a deck aiming to thin and gain.

## Summary
The analysis picks the right main deck (Hostelry, Footpad draw-on-gain, Artificer, Weaver, Flourishing Trade Buys into Triumph) and correctly casts Change/Stonemason Alchemist-Vineyard as the runner-up. But it scores incrementally with pairs of Triumphs from turns 8-10 rather than one turn-7 megaturn with many Triumphs, opens Shrine/Silver instead of Exploration, underweights Weaver, and still pencils in a Potion. A follower would build the right engine but cash it out far too early and too small.
