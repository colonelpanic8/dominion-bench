## Beyond the key
- Buying Sewers (a Project) the turn Hermit converts still lets it convert: plausible. The tournament wording checks "didn't buy any cards", and a Project is not a card.
- Ferry on Old Witch so Hermit can gain it: plausible. On your turns -$2 makes Old Witch $3, within Hermit's "up to $3".
- Leave a Copper in hand so Sewers can trash it when Hermit converts at Clean-up: plausible. This matches the key's "Sewers trashes another Copper" on conversion.
- Old Witch is the board's attack/junker, and its Attack lets the opponent's Diplomat react from 5 and start the next turn with 4 cards: plausible. The reaction draws 2 and discards 3, and Diplomat then draws to 5 (≤5) for +2 Actions. The grader notes credit this.
- At four cards, Diplomat → Bridge → Diplomat → Bridge → Old Witch works: plausible. Each Bridge drops the hand to 4 so the next Diplomat lands on 5.
- Old Witch's trash-a-Curse plus Sewers trashes another card, but only for a Curse already in hand: plausible. The gained Curse goes to the discard pile.
- One Bridge with Ferry puts Hireling in Hermit range, and two Bridges put Outpost, Rogue and Trove there: plausible. $6-2-1 = $3, and $5-2 = $3.
- One Bridge makes trashed Hermits ineligible for Rogue, and two make Provinces eligible: plausible. Rogue needs $3-$6, so Hermit drops to $2 and Province to $6.
- Caravan's next-turn card can turn Diplomat off: plausible. A 6-card hand draws to 7.
- Outpost can draw a fresh Madman if Clean-up reaches a shuffle: plausible. Madman is gained to the discard pile.
- Probability figures (5/12, ~60% for 2 Diplomats in 14, ~57% for 2 Witches in 15): plausible. They match the hypergeometric values (0.604, 0.571).
- 5/2 Old Witch/pass as an equal or faster opening: doubtful. The key says the opening revolves around Hermit + Sewers, and Horton doesn't discuss Witch.

## Summary
The analysis gets the Hermit/Sewers opening (with the old-wording conversion firing Sewers) and Bridge as the payload right, and sees that the Bridge split gets contested. It makes Madman the main village and Old Witch the attack. Diplomat, Horton's preferred village, appears only as a secondary option enabled by attacks or Outpost+Hireling. Outpost, Rogue-for-Hermits and the Treasure Trove money plan are each only half-credited.
