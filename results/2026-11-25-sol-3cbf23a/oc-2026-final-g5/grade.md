## Beyond the key
- Tools can copy itself or a Duration the opponent still has in play: plausible. Tools says "a card anyone has in play", and Tools is in play when it resolves.
- A Villa gained by Tools in the Treasure step goes to hand and returns you to the Action phase: plausible. Villa's on-gain says so if it's your Buy phase.
- On a Villa gain, decline Innovation and play Villa from hand, saving Innovation for a later gain: plausible under the given "Once during each of your turns, when you gain an Action card, you may play it" wording, which the analysis states as an assumption.
- Band of Misfits can play only Villa, Harbor Village or Sentinel here, and Tools can copy the Band but not the played card: plausible. Caravan and Lighthouse are Durations, the $5s cost the same as Band, and the played card stays in the Supply.
- Finish Treasures before buying Borrow: plausible. No Treasures can be played after a buy unless Villa reopens the Action phase.
- Harbor Village's +$1 follows Junk Dealer, Villa, Lighthouse or a Vault that discards for $: plausible. Each gives +$ when played.
- The worked $16 turn (two Villas, Harbor Village into Junk Dealer, Lighthouse, two Golds, Silver, two Coppers = $15 and three Buys): plausible arithmetic, but a thin Gold/Silver payload unlike the Tools-heavy decks the experts built.
- Lighthouse on a 5/2 for economy: doubtful. The board has no attacks, so it is only a $1-now/$1-later card. The expert line wanted Sentinel early.
- Big Money + Vault as a viable rival plan: doubtful. Nobody discussed it, and an engine with Lost City, Villa and Tools should outpace it.

## Summary
The analysis has the right core: thin with Sentinel, contest Lost City hard, and use Villa and Tools to gain and restart. But it opens Villa rather than Caravan, gives Junk Dealer a co-trasher role the commentators rejected, caps Tools at one copy and puts Gold ahead of Innovation. It misses the Vault limit and the Borrow-for-Band opening.
