# Kingdom analysis · OC 2026 Final Game 4

**Build around Flag Bearer, Crop Rotation, and Laboratory.** Take an early Jester, use Ghost Town to get the deck moving, and add Aristocrats as your draw gets good enough to play several together. Jewelled Egg into Ritual is the best early Treasure upgrade against Corsair. Ritual also gives you a way to win a close endgame without taking another Province.

## Board and setup

| Cost | Kingdom piles and landscapes |
|---|---|
| $2 | Jewelled Egg |
| $3 | Aristocrat, Ghost Town |
| $4 | Alley, Flag Bearer, Ritual (Event) |
| $5 | Corsair, First Mate, Jester, Laboratory, Tactician |
| $6 | Crop Rotation (Project) |

Two players start with seven Coppers and three Estates each. The starting splits are $4/$3 and $5/$2 in either order. There are eight Provinces and ten Curses, with standard Estates and Duchies, no Shelters, and no Platinum or Colonies. Jewelled Egg brings in the shuffled 30-card non-Supply Loot pile. Flag Bearer brings in the shared Flag. Ghost Town uses the Night phase, Alley is a Shadow, and Corsair, Ghost Town, and Tactician are Durations. [Kingdom and card text](/tmp/claude-1000/-home-imalison-projects-dominion-arena/f8629424-2e36-48be-8522-e621f2b4ebe7/scratchpad/sol-bench/sb/oc-2026-final-g4/kingdoms/oc-2026-final-g4.md), [Plunder rulebook](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/DomPlunder.pdf)

The first $5 is crowded. Jester can build and attack on the same play, while Laboratory is the safest way to build recurring draw. Thinning is scarce. Ritual usually replaces a card with a Curse, and reliable self-thinning depends on finding Prize Goat. Provinces, Duchies, and Estates score normally. Ritual scores VP tokens while adding a Curse, and Curses subtract a point each. Expect a Province race, with a three-pile finish available if both players lean on the same cheap piles.

## Openings

| Starting split | Play |
|---|---|
| **$4 then $3** | **Flag Bearer**, then **Ghost Town** unless you hit $5, in which case take **Jester**. The Flag draws a sixth card from the reshuffled four Coppers, Estate, and Flag Bearer. Drawing Flag Bearer makes $5 on turn 2, a **1-in-6** chance before interference. On the Ghost Town branch, the five cards left from that shuffle contain Flag Bearer and at least three Coppers, so turn 3 has at least $5 before either extra draw or an attack. |
| **$3 then $4** | **Ghost Town**, then **Jester** at $5 or **Flag Bearer** at $4. Ghost Town is gained to hand and played immediately. Turn 2 starts with four Coppers and an Estate, then draws from the three Coppers and two Estates you discarded on turn 1. That gives **3-in-5** odds of $5 before interference. The accelerated shuffle often makes turn 3 weaker. |
| **$5/$2 in either order** | **Jester + Jewelled Egg**. Convert the Egg on an early $4 hand when you can pay without playing it and have no more urgent Flag or draw buy. Prefer Corsair at $5 if the opponent has already shown a Silver-heavy opening. |

The Flag applies in Clean-up, so an opponent who takes it leaves your already-drawn hand intact. Ghost Town’s extra card and Action arrive at the start of your turn. [Renaissance rulebook](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/Dominion-Renaissance-Rules.pdf), [Nocturne rulebook](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Dominion-Rules-Nocturne.pdf)

## Engine and key interactions

- **Flag + Ghost Town + Crop Rotation.** With all three working, you see nine cards before playing an Action and have eight in hand after discarding a Victory card. Resolve Ghost Town, Corsair, and Tactician draws before deciding on Crop Rotation. The starting Estates make the Project a strong first $6, and greening keeps it supplied. Curses give it no fodder.
- **Aristocrat + Laboratory + First Mate.** The first Aristocrat opens two extra terminal slots, the second draws three, the third makes $3, and the fourth gives three Buys. Four ordinary plays use all your Actions from a one-Action start. First Mate can play the whole batch, including matching copies drawn during it, and leaves the three Actions supplied by the first Aristocrat available. It can also chain Laboratories, each supplying an Action, or refill after sifting with Alleys. First Mate earns its $5 when you already have worthwhile targets. [Plunder rulebook](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/DomPlunder.pdf), [Rising Sun rulebook](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/DomRisingSunRules.pdf)
- **Jewelled Egg + Ritual.** Ritual’s “Gain a Curse. If you do” makes the remaining Curse count a hard limit. Pay $4 from other cards and trash the **unplayed** Egg for a Loot and **+1 net VP**. With two Buys and $6, you can also buy a replacement Egg. The first conversion can happen on turn 3 or 4. With Jester/Egg and two ordinary five-card hands, there’s about a **34%** chance of seeing the Egg alongside $4 from other cards by turn 4, before attacks or extra draw. Keep one Egg in circulation if your Loot gives no +Buy. [Empires rulebook](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Dominion-Rules-Empires.pdf)
- **Jester + the contested piles.** Usually give the opponent the Copper and take the Laboratory, Aristocrat, or other useful hit yourself. Copying Flag Bearer takes the Flag immediately. Copying Ghost Town gives you a Night play that turn. An early Jester hitting a Victory card in a random starting shuffle is roughly a one-in-four event. Its value comes from the $2 and the useful gains as well as the Curses. Copies must come from the Supply, so a Loot hit produces no copy. [Cornucopia & Guilds rulebook](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/CornucopiaGuilds.pdf)
- **First Mate + Tactician.** Play Tactician through First Mate with a card left to discard, then refill to six. You can spend money that turn and still get **+5 Cards, +1 Action, and +1 Buy** next turn. One Tactician can do this at most every other turn. Staff or Orb can also play Tactician in the Buy phase after you make your money, provided you leave a card in hand to discard. This line is strongest when the Aristocrat pile is contested or you already have a Loot that helps line it up. [Plunder rulebook](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/DomPlunder.pdf)

A plausible build is **Flag Bearer → Ghost Town → Crop Rotation → Jester**, then a Laboratory, an Aristocrat, and an Egg. An $8 hand with the Egg can buy your second Laboratory and second Aristocrat together. With those pieces, Flag, Ghost Town, and Crop Rotation see nine cards, two Laboratories bring that to thirteen, and the second Aristocrat brings it to sixteen. That’s most of a roughly 17-card live deck before incoming junk. The draw order matters. With three Aristocrats among 18 cards, nine random looks find two or more only half the time. Laboratories give you a way to keep drawing when the second Aristocrat starts buried.

I’d expect **$8 around turns 5–8**, first near-full draws around **turns 9–11**, and the first credible double Province around **turns 11–13**. Those are build estimates. A turn that plays seven Coppers, Flag Bearer, Jester, the third Aristocrat, and a $3/+Buy Loot makes **$17 and two Buys**. A fourth Aristocrat adds three Buys if you can line it up. More commonly, aim for **$13 and two Buys** for Province–Duchy, or **$12 and two Buys** for Province–Ritual with a spare expensive card.

## Plans considered and best responses

| Plan | Worked line and matchup |
|---|---|
| **Laboratory / Crop Rotation with Aristocrats** | First $6 around turns 3–5 and first $8 around turns 5–8. Aim for at least two Labs, one Ghost Town, and two Aristocrats, then add the third or fourth as draw permits. One Jester plays roughly once per shuffle early and on most near-full turns. The strongest response is to contest Labs and Aristocrats, steal the Flag on useful turns, and start Provinces while your draw still misses. This is my pick because the Labs keep working even when the Flag changes hands or the Aristocrats split poorly. |
| **First Mate / Tactician** | On the Flag–Ghost Town opening, a plausible line is Crop on turn 3, First Mate and Tactician on two of turns 4–7, then a Lab or second Tactician. A Tactician turn sees up to 14 cards before Actions with Flag, Ghost Town, and Crop all active. First $8 is plausible around turns 6–9. One completed loop can fund multiple buys, but the opponent’s best answer is to race Provinces and contest Labs while your two $5 pieces are still lining up. An early Staff, Orb, or Puzzle Box improves this line. |
| **Big Money + Jester, with Loot** | Open Jester/Egg on $5/$2 or use Flag/Ghost Town to get there. One Jester appears in about one-third of ordinary hands in a 15-card deck. Add a second attack only when you have room for it. Against a largely unopposed Lab build, money needs early Provinces and then Duchies or a pile threat before the engine starts scoring twice a turn. Against sustained Corsair, Loot matters much more than Gold. |

## Buy priorities and card sweep

- **Through the second shuffle**, get the first Jester and Flag Bearer, then take Crop Rotation at $6 if you still have your Estates. Take Laboratory at subsequent $5s. Ghost Town is the best $3 while you need help playing Flag Bearer and Jester together. Buy Aristocrats in time to get a pair, or earlier if the opponent is already contesting them. An isolated Aristocrat is mostly an Action boost.
- **Through the third shuffle**, add a second Lab and a source of +Buy. Alley is a good spare $4 once you have cards worth finding. From the deck it swaps a card in hand without using up an Action. From hand it shrinks the hand by one, so it works better before First Mate’s refill. Take First Mate when it has a batch of Labs or Aristocrats to play. Take a second Ghost Town if your terminals still collide on important turns.
- **Adjust the attack to their economy.** Corsair is a priority against repeated Silver and Gold, especially if you can get two copies onto alternating turns. The trashed Treasure still pays that turn. Against Corsair, lead with Silver to protect Gold when you have both, and favor Loot and Aristocrat money for future turns. Shield blocks either attack if it’s already in hand when the attack is played. [Seaside rulebook](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Seaside2nd.pdf), [Plunder rulebook](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/DomPlunder.pdf)
- **Let the Loot change your purchases.** Prize Goat can trash more Eggs for Loot, trash Flag Bearer to take the Flag, or actually remove junk. Hammer gains Aristocrat, Egg, Alley, or Flag Bearer without spending a Buy. Figurehead helps start the draw chain. Endless Chalice can cover +Buy for the rest of the game. Doubloons’ bonus Gold is vulnerable to Corsair, and Spell Scroll can become a Laboratory or a late Ritual target. [Plunder rulebook](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/DomPlunder.pdf)

The local ratings put Laboratory at **+1.6**, Ghost Town **+2.0**, Flag Bearer **+0.1**, Aristocrat **−1.9**, and Ritual **−4.5**. The biggest upgrades here are Flag Bearer for the opening shuffle, Aristocrat once supported by real draw, and Ritual because Egg supplies an unusually profitable early trash and Loot supplies a high-value late one. Tactician and First Mate also play better together than either rating suggests, though the pair still competes with Laboratory and Jester at $5.

## Greening and ending

Start taking Provinces once you can do so without giving up the draw piece your deck still needs. In a mirror, take an early Province more readily. Crop Rotation helps absorb it, and conceding a Province split can force you to find Ritual points later. Track **Aristocrat, Ghost Town, and Laboratory** in the mirror, plus **Curses, Egg, and Duchy** if both players use Ritual and +Buy heavily. With two piles empty, buying the last Curse through Ritual can end the game on your turn.

Count Ritual by **net** points. Trashing a Loot scores **+6**, Gold **+5**, a spare $5 Action **+4**, and Flag Bearer **+3** plus the Flag. An Estate nets **zero**, and a Duchy or Province nets **+1**. At $12 and two Buys, Province plus Ritual on an unplayed Loot is **12 VP**, provided you can make the $12 without that Loot. Once the Curse pile empties, Ritual does nothing. [Empires rulebook](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Dominion-Rules-Empires.pdf)

**What would change the pick.** If the opponent takes most of the Aristocrats, move toward Lab–First Mate with Ghost Town or Tactician for Actions. If they stockpile Silver and Gold, move Corsair up. In multiplayer, Labs and Aristocrats are harder to secure, Jester gets more chances at useful hits, and the Curse pile and other contested piles can fall faster. That favors earlier scoring and fewer purchases that only pay off once the deck is complete.

**Challenge and uncertainty.** The First Mate–Tactician deck has the strongest case against the recommendation. If it starts refilling to six and activating Tactician by turn 8, it can use the extra Buy to build and green before the Lab deck draws through its junk. The main uncertainty is whether the Lab deck gets its second Lab and second Aristocrat into play soon enough under Jester pressure and Flag turnover. If those two Aristocrats still rarely meet by turn 9, build around your reliable draw and start scoring rather than buying toward four. The timing estimates use worked purchase and draw sequences under the supplied two-player setup. Actual timing depends on Flag ownership, Jester hits, and the first Loot.
