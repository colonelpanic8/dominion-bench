## Beyond the key
- Remodel played from Staff or Orb in the Buy phase sees the discounted Peddler: plausible. Staff and Orb are Treasures played in the Buy phase, and Peddler's discount applies "During a player's Buy phase."
- Sword cuts the hand Way of the Squirrel built, but not the cards Grotto or Enchantress draw: plausible. Squirrel's +2 Cards come at the end of your turn, before the opponent's turn, while Grotto and Enchantress draw at the start of your next turn.
- Under Enchantress, Farmhands loses its +2 Actions, and a card Farmhands reserved can absorb the hit as the first Action played: plausible. Enchantress affects the first Action played on that player's turn, and a reserved card is played at the start of that turn.
- On 2/5, Guardian into Gold: plausible. Guardian is gained to hand, played that Night, and its +$1 at the start of turn 2 turns $5 into $6.
- On 5/2, Farmhands sets aside the fifth Copper so turn 2 has $3 for Amulet: plausible. The on-gain effect sets aside a Treasure from hand, and $4 from the other four Coppers pays for Farmhands.
- Ten of the fifteen Loots give +Buy: plausible. The count matches the supplied Loot text: Amphora, Endless Chalice, Jewels, Orb, Prize Goat, Puzzle Box, Sextant, Shield, Staff and Sword.
- Spell Scroll held as a $7 Remodel target, and Hammer gaining a discounted Peddler: plausible. Remodel turns a $7 card into one costing up to $9, which reaches Province, and Hammer can gain a card costing up to $4, which Peddler costs with two Actions in play.
- Fortune as a high-ceiling rival line: doubtful. The key notes the winner ignored Gladiator/Fortune, and the analysis itself keeps it as a contingency.
- Big Money with Amulet as a viable alternative: doubtful. Nothing in the key supports it against a Grotto/Remodel deck with Province reach outside the Buy phase.

## Summary
The analysis has the experts' core right: Grotto for delayed draw, Farmhands as the village, Peddler as payload, and Remodel turning $8 Peddlers into Provinces. Its 4/3 opening of Remodel/Amulet matches the winner's. It misses that Grotto makes early greening cheap and the multi-Remodel endgame, and it opens Farmhands rather than Grotto on 5/2.
