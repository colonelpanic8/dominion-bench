# Kingdom analysis

**Recommendation.** Build a compact Grotto deck with Amulet, Remodel, Farmhands and one early Enchantress. Use Foray for Treasure and extra Buys, then use cheap Peddlers as both economy and future Remodel targets. The first payoff to aim for is **a bought Province plus a Province gained with Remodel**. Fortune can raise the ceiling later if Gladiators clear.

## Board and setup

| Cost | Kingdom piles and landscapes |
|---|---|
| \$2 | Grotto, Guardian, Monastery |
| \$3 | Amulet, Enchantress, Gladiator, Foray (Event) |
| \$4 | Farmhands, Remodel |
| \$0 + Potion | Transmute |
| \$8 | Peddler, reduced by \$2 per Action in play during your Buy phase |
| \$8 + 8 Debt | Fortune, beneath five Gladiators in the same pile |
| — | Way of the Squirrel |

Two players start with **7 Coppers and 3 Estates** each. The natural splits are 4/3 or 3/4 five-sixths of the time and 5/2 or 2/5 one-sixth of the time. Potion is a \$4 Supply Treasure. Foray draws from the shuffled 30-card non-Supply Loot pile. The setup uses Night, Durations, Potion and Debt, with no Shelters, Colonies or Platinum. [Kingdom and supplied card text](/tmp/claude-1000/-home-imalison-projects-dominion-arena/f8629424-2e36-48be-8522-e621f2b4ebe7/scratchpad/sol-bench/sb/oc-2025-final-g2/kingdoms/oc-2025-final-g2.md)

Scoring is ordinary Provinces, Duchies and Estates, with Curses worth −1 VP and no source of VP tokens. Transmute can gain Duchies, and Remodel can gain Provinces. Province depletion is the default ending. A contested Peddler pile makes a three-pile ending plausible. The opening prices are crowded at \$2–4, there is no \$5 Kingdom card, and Gold at \$6 competes with much cheaper ways to build payload.

## Engine and key interactions

- **Grotto, Enchantress and Way of the Squirrel supply delayed draw.** One Grotto can set aside up to four cards, then discard and replace them at the start of your next turn. That gives up to nine cards before playing an Action, or eleven with Enchantress’s delayed draw or one Squirrel play from the previous turn. Farmhands and Peddler cycle a card each. Aim for two Grottos if you want to use one on successive turns, since a Grotto that just paid out is still in play. Set aside cards you can spare after counting this turn’s money and Foray discards. Squirrel draws after the normal cleanup hand, so Sword can cut those extra cards before your turn. Grotto and Enchantress draw after that attack. [Menagerie rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/DominionMenagerie.pdf), [Plunder rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/DomPlunder.pdf)
- **Farmhands supplies the terminal slots.** One regular Farmhands play lets you play two terminals, such as Remodel plus Enchantress. On gain, Farmhands can reserve a terminal from hand and play it next turn without spending an Action. Remodel can turn an Estate into Farmhands and reserve a second terminal immediately. Under Enchantress, lead with a spare Peddler or Grotto when you can. An affected Farmhands loses its extra Action, and a reserved Action can take the hit if it is your first play. An affected Action can also use Squirrel, which gives up the replacement Action along with the replacement card draw. [Empires rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Dominion-Rules-Empires.pdf), [Menagerie rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/DominionMenagerie.pdf)
- **Peddler turns development into points.** Its discount applies **“During a player’s Buy phase.”** With two, three or four Actions in play, it costs \$4, \$2 or \$0. Action Durations still in play count. In the Action phase, Remodel sees Peddler at \$8 and can turn it into a Province. Gold and any Loot still in hand also work as Province targets. A spare Buy can replace the Peddler on the same turn. A Remodel played via Staff or Orb in the Buy phase sees the discounted price, so check the price before attempting that conversion.
- **Foray turns spare hand size into payload.** Budget \$3, a Buy and three different names still in hand *after* playing Treasures. A Copper, Estate and spare Action can do it. Peddler coins and Amulet’s start-of-turn coin help pay while leaving those names available. Ten of the fifteen Loot types can provide +Buy, including Orb in its money mode. Hammer can gain a discounted Peddler in the Buy phase, Figurehead helps sustain large hands, and Prize Goat adds thinning. Spell Scroll can become a Gold and play it, or stay in hand as a \$7 Remodel target. [Plunder rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/DomPlunder.pdf)
- **Amulet and Monastery handle actual thinning.** Amulet makes two choices per normal play, one now and one next turn. Take Silver when you need economy and trash Estates or surplus Copper when you can afford it. Remodel instead replaces one card per play. Copper can become Grotto, Guardian or Monastery, and Estate can become Farmhands or Enchantress. One Monastery can trash twice on a turn with an Amulet Silver gain and a purchase, or with a Loot gain and another gain. It can take Coppers already played for money. Preserve enough spendable Treasure and hand variety for the next Foray.

### Worked build

This is a favorable 4/3 line with legal hands. It shows what the cheap pieces can produce before Fortune enters the game.

| Turn | Play |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Buy Remodel and Amulet. |
| 3 | Draw Remodel, Estate and three Coppers. Remodel Estate into Farmhands, set aside one Copper with the on-gain effect, and buy Grotto with the other two. |
| 4 | The reserved Copper plays for \$1. With Amulet, another Estate and three Coppers, gain a Silver with Amulet and buy Enchantress. |
| 5 | Amulet trashes the remaining Estate from the opening shuffle. Farmhands draws Silver. Grotto sets aside Copper and Silver, then play Enchantress. Amulet, Farmhands, Grotto and Enchantress are all in play, so buy Peddler for \$0. |
| 6 | Grotto and Enchantress give a nine-card hand. In this shuffle it contains six Coppers, two Estates and Remodel. Play three Coppers and discard Copper, Estate and Remodel for a Loot. |
| 7 | If the Loot is Prize Goat and you draw it with Amulet, Peddler and two Coppers, Peddler can draw a third Copper. Amulet’s coin puts you at \$8 and two Buys. Gold plus a second Grotto is a strong building turn. |

A favorable build can first reach most of its deck around **turns 8–10**, with a two-Province turn around **turns 9–12**. Expect uneven turns until you have overlapping draw sources. Two Grottos, an Enchantress and one or two Farmhands are a reasonable target, adjusted for how often you miss the next draw source or run out of Actions. A built turn with two \$3 Loot, Silver, two played Peddlers and an Amulet coin makes **\$11 and at least two Buys** if one Loot supplies +Buy. Remodel a third Peddler, then buy Province plus Foray, for **12 VP and a Loot**. With Fortune, \$8 made before playing it becomes \$16 and at least two Buys.

## Plans considered

| Plan | Opening and pace | What decides it |
|---|---|---|
| **Grotto–Remodel with Foray** | Remodel and Amulet on 4/3. Aim for \$6 around T4–6 and \$8 around T6–8. One Enchantress attacks intermittently, with repeat attacks easier once the deck cycles. | Needs a Remodel, reliable delayed draw and preferably one Loot with +Buy. It can score twice without Fortune. Its weak turn has villages and delayed draw in hand but carries too little into the following turn. |
| **Big Money + Amulet** | Amulet and Silver on 4/3, Amulet and Grotto on 5/2. A T3 hand of Silver and four Coppers can buy Gold. Gold, Silver and three Coppers can reach \$8 on T5–7 if they line up. Take an easy Foray when available. | Needs one Amulet early and enough Treasure to reach \$8 repeatedly. Usually makes no attacks. A single Enchantress is an alternative for draw if the opponent depends heavily on one terminal. This plan can score first, but usually scores one card per turn. |
| **Gladiator into Fortune** | Gladiator and Remodel on 4/3. Gladiator and four Coppers can make \$6–7 on T3–4. Gladiator, Gold and three Coppers can reach \$8 on T5–7. Two Gladiators with Farmhands can uncover Fortune faster. | Needs about two Gladiators, a village if stacking them, and an exposed Fortune at the right time. Buying Fortune while two Gladiators are in play gains two Golds. The purchase costs \$8 up front and leaves up to \$8 of Debt to repay. Matching reveals and poor timing can delay the payoff. [Empires rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Dominion-Rules-Empires.pdf) |

**Against the best responses.** Against the Grotto build, I would contest Peddlers and play Enchantress while starting Provinces as soon as my own deck can sustain them. Buy a Guardian when the attack repeatedly blanks your only important Action. Against Amulet money, I would build the compact Grotto deck. Money can get a Province or two ahead, so take the first credible two-score turn and let that lead dictate how much longer you build.

## Openings and buy priorities

| Natural split | Buy in turn order |
|---|---|
| **4/3** | **Remodel → Amulet.** Both can improve the first shuffle, and Remodel can gain the first Farmhands. |
| **3/4** | **Amulet → Remodel.** Same pieces and priorities. |
| **5/2** | **Farmhands → Amulet.** Play four Coppers on T1 and set aside the fifth with Farmhands. It plays on T2 and raises the natural \$2 to \$3. Prioritize Remodel in the next shuffle. |
| **2/5** | **Grotto → Amulet.** Grotto can set up the next hand or take Enchantress’s hit before Amulet. Guardian → Gold is the faster money opening here, since Guardian’s immediate Night play raises T2 from \$5 to \$6. |

In the **first shuffle**, use Remodel on an Estate for Farmhands or Enchantress, or on Copper for Grotto if the hand needs draw more than economy. Take an early Foray when the hand already meets the condition. In the **next two shuffles**, add another live draw source, buy cheap Peddlers and add a second Farmhands when terminal collision is costing plays. Gold is good once your draw can reach it, and it gives Remodel a guaranteed late target. Start greening when you can repeatedly reach \$8 or gain two scoring cards in one turn. Green earlier in the mirror if the opponent can drain Peddlers and another development pile.

## Remaining cards and endgame

- **Guardian** is the best cheap response to repeated Enchantress or Sword. Remodel can turn Copper into Guardian, which goes straight to hand and can be played that Night.
- **Gladiator** works best with Farmhands and a reveal the opponent is unlikely to match. An early Gladiator can also fund Foray while you keep the rest of your hand. Watch who will have Gladiators in play when Fortune is exposed.
- **Transmute and Potion** are the weakest investment here. Transmute can turn an obsolete Action into a Duchy or an Estate into Gold, and Farmhands can reserve it to save a terminal slot. The Potion buy and subsequent draw cost too much for a job Remodel already does while also reaching Provinces.
- **Ratings check.** Grotto’s high rating fits the board. Remodel and Foray matter more here than their below-average ratings suggest because they supply the two things delayed draw needs to win: a Province gain outside the Buy phase and access to +Buy. Fortune’s modest rating fits the exposed-pile and Debt constraints. [Local card ratings](/tmp/claude-1000/-home-imalison-projects-dominion-arena/f8629424-2e36-48be-8522-e621f2b4ebe7/scratchpad/sol-bench/sb/oc-2025-final-g2/context/card-ratings.md)

**Ending.** Expect Provinces to empty first. Count **Peddler, Farmhands and Grotto** in an engine mirror, with Duchy a possible third pile once both players start scoring. Discounted Peddlers and Loot Buys can remove several cards in one turn. Gladiator/Fortune counts as one pile and is empty only when both halves are gone. Loot depletion never counts. At two Provinces left, check whether either player can buy one and Remodel into the other. With more players, Farmhands and Peddlers are more contested, Enchantress hits more opponents, and Guardian rises in value. [Empires rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Dominion-Rules-Empires.pdf), [Plunder rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/DomPlunder.pdf)

**Challenge and uncertainty.** Fortune is the highest-ceiling rival. If the opponent helps expose it and you can buy it with two Gladiators in play while paying most or all of the Debt, take that opportunity. The recommended build loses when early Enchantress hits delay Remodel, the first Forays arrive late, and the opponent starts scoring while your draw pieces are still coming together. The least certain estimate is how soon the deck can repeat large hands after greening. By the end of the second reshuffle, check whether you are finding a fresh Grotto or Enchantress on most turns and still reaching the money you need. If those plays keep missing, stop adding villages and take the reliable Province turns your Treasures give you. The turn estimates use the supplied card text and illustrate favorable draws.
