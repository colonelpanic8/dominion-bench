## Beyond the key
- Flagship replaying Throne Room lets the second Throne Room resolution pick a different target: plausible. Flagship replays the next non-Command Action; Throne Room is one, and each resolution chooses a card from hand.
- Experiment played via Way of the Owl stays out of the Supply, so Owl can hold Experiments out to protect or threaten the pile ending: plausible. A card played as a Way does none of its own instructions, including "Return this to its pile".
- Throne Room on Experiment gives +4 Cards +2 Actions with the card returning once: plausible. The first play returns it; the second finds it gone and just resolves.
- Anvil can discard a spare Copper to gain an Experiment pair and keep the Buy: plausible. Anvil gains a card up to $4, and Experiment's on-gain gains another.
- Governor's opponent-side remodel lets them trash a Copper for nothing (no $1 card) or turn a $4 into Duchy/Governor: plausible. "Exactly $1 more" for the +1 version; the board has no $1 card.
- Opponent with $8, a Gold in hand and a Governor can take the last two Provinces with one Buy: plausible. Governor's remodel turns Gold ($6) into a Province ($8) alongside the buy.
- Merchant Camp replayed by a carried Flagship gives +4 Actions and $2 and still topdecks as one card: plausible. Its discard-from-play ability is per card.
- Two pending Flagships both replay the same next Action, giving Tactician three plays: plausible. Each says "the next time you play a non-Command Action card".
- Built turn of $16 and two Buys from three Golds, Silver, two Coppers, Camp and a $2 Forager after one Gold upgrade: plausible. 9+2+2+1+2 = 16.
- Merchant Camp and Tactician outperform their low local ratings here: plausible but unsourced; consistent with the designer's Tactician view.
- 4/3 opening Flagship/Experiment ("Flagship plus three Coppers reaches $5"): plausible arithmetically; no source opinion either way.

## Summary
The analysis finds every one of Horton's mechanisms, including the Leprechaun double-Wish count and the replayed-Tactician-into-Owl refill, but recommends a Governor-first replay engine and treats Tactician as an optional late add rather than the deck's centerpiece. It misses the point that Tactician draw frees Governor for Gold gains and remodels. A player following it would play a reasonable Governor engine and probably buy one Tactician at most, which is close to what the finalists did rather than what the designer intended.
