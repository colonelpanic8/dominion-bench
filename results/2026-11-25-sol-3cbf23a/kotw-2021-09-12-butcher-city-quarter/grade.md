## Beyond the key
- Opening splits of 40% $5/$4, 48% $6/$3 and 12% $7/$2: plausible. Counting the 126 ways to deal Cursed Gold's hand gives 51/60/15.
- After a turn-1 Camp, Cursed Gold has a one-in-five chance to miss the turn-2 hand: plausible. Camp goes onto the deck, so turn 2 draws only 4 of the 5 cards left.
- Butcher can't gain City Quarter: plausible. Butcher gains by coin cost, and City Quarter's cost includes 8 Debt.
- One Quarter supports two terminals, two support three: plausible. Each Quarter nets +1 Action.
- In 20 cards, 2 Quarters give about 45% to start in hand and 3 give 60%: plausible (hypergeometric 0.447 and 0.601).
- Merchant Guild counts Curses from Cursed Gold or Ritual gained in the Buy phase, but not Watchman's Night Tunnel gains: plausible. Guild counts only cards gained during the Buy phase.
- A discarded Hound returns after the Clean-up draw, giving a six-card hand: plausible. It goes to hand "at end of turn".
- Fool's Gold makes $5/$9/$13 for 2/3/4 copies; Ritual on Gold gives 6 VP, on Cursed Gold 4 VP: plausible. Both follow from the printed costs and values.
- Tower cancels Curses once their pile empties, and an emptied Tunnel pile gives no Tower points: plausible. Curse is non-Victory and Tunnel is a Victory card.
- Pooka as an early Copper trasher and draw: doubtful. The thread never mentions it, and as a terminal that needs a non-Cursed-Gold Treasure in hand it competes with Butcher for scarce Actions.
- Merchant Guild with cheap gains feeding Butcher's Gold-to-Province: doubtful. The experts rejected Guild because Actions are too scarce.

## Summary
It picks the right shell (Butcher into a City Quarter engine) and notices Tunnel/Gold, Hound and Watchman, but treats Watchman and Tunnel as afterthoughts. It opens without Night Watchman, Butchers Cursed Gold into Gold instead of a second Butcher, and builds the payload on Merchant Guild and Fool's Gold, the two cards the strongest voice warned against.
