## Recommendation

Open **Butcher** and build toward **City Quarter**. Butcher turns starting junk into useful cards and banks money for the first Quarters. Pooka removes Coppers, Faithful Hound helps the next Quarter draw, and Merchant Guild funds the turns that buy and remodel into multiple Provinces. Aim for two Quarters by turns 6–8 and a third before greening heavily.

The main rival is a faster Butcher–Fool’s Gold deck that takes Provinces while threatening a Tower pile-out. Track that threat as soon as the Curse pile gets low. **Tower cancels the −1 VP on every Curse if the Curse pile empties**, so taking the last one can change both players’ scores and trigger the end of the game. [Empires rulebook](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Dominion-Rules-Empires.pdf)

| $5 | Butcher, Embassy, Merchant Guild, Pooka |
| 8 Debt | City Quarter |
| — | Tower **(Landmark)** |

Embassy and Nomad Camp are first-edition Hinterlands cards. The two-player setup uses six Coppers, three Estates, and one Cursed Gold per player. Cursed Gold is an Heirloom outside the Supply. There are no other added piles, Colonies, Platinum, or Shelters. The kingdom uses Coffers, Debt, and the Night phase. It has no opponent-targeted attacks. Cursed Gold and Ritual junk their own players, and Embassy gives the opponent a Silver.

The nominal splits are **$5/$4 (40%)**, **$6/$3 (48%)**, and **$7/$2 (12%)**, in either order. Each assumes you play Cursed Gold and gain a Curse.

| Split | Opening |
| --- | --- |
| **$5/$4** | **Butcher + Nomad Camp.** Camp gives immediate economy and +Buy on the turn after you gain it. If you have four plain Coppers on turn 1, **Silver** is the safer opener. Camp pushes one starter out of the turn-2 hand and has a one-in-five chance of pushing out Cursed Gold, leaving $4. If you buy Camp on turn 1 using Cursed Gold, turn 2 is Camp plus four Coppers for $6, enough for Butcher and an optional second buy of City Quarter, carrying 7 Debt. I’d normally keep the next buy free for Pooka. |

## Engine and buy priorities

**First shuffle after the opening.** Get **one Pooka** and start financing **City Quarter**. Butcher can turn an Estate into Hound for zero Coffers, or a Copper or Curse into Hound for two. Keep the Coffers when they unlock an important buy. Convert Cursed Gold into real Gold for two Coffers when they meet and you can afford the lost immediate $3. That ends the self-junking and supplies a future Province remodel. Pooka handles the Coppers and needs a Treasure in hand to draw four.

**Next shuffle.** Get **Quarter #2**, then **Merchant Guild** and a second Hound as the money permits. One Quarter supports two terminals, two support three, and three support four. The second Quarter lets you play draw, Butcher, and +Buy on the same turn. If you have two Buys, buy the ordinary card first and Quarter last when you intend to carry Debt. With $8 and two Buys you can buy two Quarters, paying off the first and carrying the second, but the next hand has to support that bill. Butcher’s coin-based gain cannot reach City Quarter from an ordinary coin-cost card. [Empires rulebook](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Dominion-Rules-Empires.pdf), [Cornucopia & Guilds rulebook](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/CornucopiaGuilds.pdf)

**A worked fast line.** On a $5-then-$4 opening, buy Butcher then Camp. On turn 3, Camp and three Coppers buy Pooka. On turn 4, Butcher turns an Estate into Hound and keeps both Coffers. Cursed Gold and two Coppers bring the total to $7 for Quarter #1, leaving 1 Debt. If Quarter and Pooka meet on turn 5 and find Camp, Cursed Gold, and enough Copper, $8 gross can clear the old Debt and buy Quarter #2, again leaving 1 Debt. That line takes three Curses and needs a favorable shuffle. It shows how two Quarters can be active by turn 7. I’d budget for the first near-full draw around **turns 8–11**.

**Reliability and payoff.** In a random five-card hand from 20 cards, two Quarters give about a **45%** chance of opening with one, and three give **60%**. Night Watchman can improve the actual next hand, while a carried Hound gives the Quarter an extra Action to reveal. A strong six-card start can see the whole deck with three Quarters revealing three, four, and six Actions in succession, followed by Pooka drawing four. With two Golds, two Fool’s Golds, two remaining Coppers, Camp, and Guild, that turn makes **$16 and three Buys** before Coffers. Alternatively, Butcher can spend its two fresh Coffers to turn one Gold into a Province, leaving **$13** to buy a Province and Duchy for **15 VP total**. Start greening once you can threaten turns like that, or sooner if the opponent is already taking Provinces or can force piles.

## Interactions that matter

- **Merchant Guild + cheap gains + Butcher.** With one Guild in play, two Buy-phase gains bank two Coffers, exactly enough to turn a Gold into a Province on a later Butcher turn. Guild counts Curses gained from Cursed Gold or Ritual during the Buy phase, and buying cheap Hounds or Fool’s Gold can help pay for the next build turn. The Coffers arrive at the end of the Buy phase. Butcher gains during the Action phase and Night Watchman’s Tunnel gains occur at Night. [Cornucopia & Guilds rulebook](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/CornucopiaGuilds.pdf)
- **Embassy or Night Watchman + Faithful Hound.** Each Hound discarded outside Clean-up can return after the next five are drawn. That gives you a six-card hand and an extra Action to reveal to Quarter. Embassy can discard up to three Hounds or Tunnels in total. Watchman can discard them directly from the deck and leave any Quarter it finds on top. One Watchman is worth considering once these targets are in the deck, especially if the next shuffle is about to start. [Nocturne rulebook](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Dominion-Rules-Nocturne.pdf)
- **Embassy or Night Watchman + Tunnel.** Each Tunnel discarded gains one Gold, giving a money deck payload without spending a $6 buy and giving Butcher future Province fodder. Tunnel itself is worth 2 VP and dilutes Quarter hands, so the engine should usually pick up Tunnels late. Embassy’s free Silver for the opponent is a real cost, although receiving one yourself can help pay Debt or feed Butcher. [Hinterlands rulebook](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/Hinterlands2nd.pdf)
- **Fool’s Gold + draw or +Buy.** Two, three, and four copies played together make **$5, $9, and $13**. Butcher can swap Estates into copies for free, while Camp buys them in groups. When the opponent takes a Province, you can trade one from hand for a topdecked Gold. Favor the reaction when you expect to draw copies separately. Keeping the copies together preserves both the payout and any prospective Tower points. [Hinterlands rulebook](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/Hinterlands2nd.pdf)
- **Ritual + surplus expensive cards.** A spare Gold gives six VP tokens for $4 and a Buy, with a −1 VP Curse if the pile stays live. An unplayed Cursed Gold gives four tokens and retires the Heirloom. Check for lost Tower points on the trashed card and compare with Butcher’s Province conversion. Ritual adds a Curse, and the Event stops working once Curses are gone. If Cursed Gold would take the last Curse, sequence your turn around that before counting Ritual points. [Empires rulebook](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Dominion-Rules-Empires.pdf)

The repo ratings back up the priority on Butcher and City Quarter, both at **+3.7**. The board raises the value of Hound and Watchman, both **−3.0**, because their discards help start the next Quarter turn. Pooka at **−2.2** earns its place through early Copper trashing and four-card draw. Guild at **+1.3** improves once you can play it alongside draw and use both Buys.

## Rival plans and matchup

| Plan | Pace and requirements | Main weakness |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **City Quarter engine** | Butcher opening, then Pooka, two Quarters to start, a third for reliability, and Guild or Camp for +Buy. Can generate $6 around turns 3–4 and $8 gross around turns 5–7, with early money going to Debt and components. Look for multi-score turns around turns 10–12. | Missing Quarter in the opening hand strands the terminals. Debt and Curses can compound a bad shuffle. |
| **Butcher–Fool’s Gold money** | Open Butcher with Fool’s Gold, Silver, or Camp as the split allows. Use Estate swaps and +Buy to get three or four Fool’s Golds. A pair plus a Copper hits $6, and three together hit $9. A Cursed Gold–Butcher hand can reach $8 as early as turn 3, with turns 5–7 a more useful target for the first Province. | The engine can contest Fool’s Gold and cash in its own copies for Tower. Drawing them separately makes weak hands. |
| **Embassy money with Tunnel** | Open Embassy with Silver where possible. An Embassy hand holding Cursed Gold, Silver, and three Coppers hits $8 on turns 3–4. Add one or two Tunnels and a Watchman when you can afford a $3 buy, then turn those discards into Gold. | Embassy subsidizes the opponent’s first $5 or Debt payment. Tunnel adds points and economy over multiple shuffles, while the engine can gain several scoring cards at once. |

I’d take the engine against all three. **Against an engine mirror**, secure three Quarters early and keep enough draw to play through your first greens. **Against the best money response**, watch for the Butcher–Fool’s Gold player to take early Provinces and shorten the game through Tower. Contest Fool’s Gold if a pair fits your payload. If an early Debt stumble leaves you with one Quarter around turn 8 and the opponent is already scoring or threatening piles, use Butcher and the economy you have to score immediately. Against Embassy money, spend the donated Silver on build tempo and watch the Tunnel count as well as Provinces.

## Ending and what could change the pick

Expect **Provinces** to end an ordinary race and **three piles** to end a contested one. Curses, Fool’s Gold, Faithful Hound, and Tunnel are the first piles I’d track, with City Quarter and Nomad Camp live in an engine mirror. Tower only awards points for owned non-Victory cards from empty Supply piles. Emptying Fool’s Gold when you own six and the opponent owns four gives you a two-point edge from that pile. Emptying Tunnel gives the usual 2 VP per owned Tunnel and can end the game, with no Tower bonus. On a closing turn, take any ordinary buys first and use the final Buy on a City Quarter if its Debt is affordable to take and the gain clinches the game. [Empires rulebook](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Dominion-Rules-Empires.pdf)

The strongest challenge to the engine is a **Butcher–Fool’s Gold sprint** that uses Camp to buy groups of Treasures, turns a Gold into a Province, and threatens a Curse–Fool’s Gold–Tunnel finish before your third Quarter pays off. It becomes especially credible if that player gets four Fool’s Golds cheaply and you miss Quarter on successive shuffles. With more players, contest for key piles rises and someone else can trigger the ending sooner. I’d green earlier and put more weight on the pile counts.

**Least sure of the timing.** The worked line establishes an early route to two Quarters. The uncertain part is how soon those decks *repeat* a near-full draw under ordinary shuffles. The practical test is whether your next shuffle finds Quarter early enough to play draw, Butcher, and +Buy together. The turn ranges are estimates using the supplied card text and two-player setup.
