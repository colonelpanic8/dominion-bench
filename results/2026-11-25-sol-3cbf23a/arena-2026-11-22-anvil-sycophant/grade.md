## Beyond the key
- Expand a Sycophant into a Duchy and Anvil a new Sycophant: Province + Duchy (9 VP) and 4 Favors on $8 with one Buy: plausible. A $2 Sycophant Expands to a $5 Duchy; Anvil gains a card up to $4; each gain or trash gives +2 Favors.
- Five such loops fund four Mountain Folk activations: plausible. 20 Favors at 5 each.
- Two Golds, two Silvers and Anvil: Expand a Gold into a Province, leaving exactly $8 for a second; with a spare Copper, Anvil adds an Estate for 13 VP: plausible. $9 ≥ Province; 3+4+1 = 8; 6+6+1.
- Gaining an Estate with Anvil while holding Mapmaker lets Mapmaker react mid-Buy-phase, and its Treasures can still be played: plausible. Mapmaker reacts to any Victory gain, and the player is still in the Buy phase before buying.
- Play Temple, then Anvil a Temple to take the VP just added to its pile: plausible. Temple adds 1 VP to its pile; gaining one takes the pile's VP.
- Way of the Horse can return a Temple to reopen an empty pile: plausible. Horse returns the played card to its pile.
- Crucible trashes a Sycophant for $2 and 2 Favors without an Action, and cashes Lucky Coin for $4: plausible. Crucible is a Treasure; it gives the trashed card's cost; Lucky Coin costs $4.
- Diplomat played from a five-card hand leaves six cards and no +Actions: plausible. 4 + 2 = 6 > 5.
- Holding Lost in the Woods, play Fool as a Horse: plausible. Fool does nothing for the State's holder.
- Avoid is a net-zero Buy: plausible. It costs a Buy and gives +1 Buy.
- The Forest's Gift is the only other +Buy: plausible. No Kingdom card or other Boon gives +Buy.
- Anvil reaches every Kingdom pile except Expand: plausible. Every other pile costs $4 or less.
- Will-o'-Wisp can pull Estates: plausible. Estate costs $2.
- Crucible, Anvil, Estate, Copper, Lucky Coin: trash the Estate, Anvil a Temple, and still buy Mapmaker and gain a Silver: plausible. $2 + $1 + $1 = $4, plus Lucky Coin's Silver.

## Summary
The analysis sees Sycophant's Favors and Mountain Folk, and even builds a Favor-earning Expand/Anvil scoring loop. But it makes Blessed Village–Mapmaker the draw engine and spends the opening Anvil's gains on Blessed Village, which is the trap that lost the source game and all three untold self-play games. It never opens Anvil/Anvil into Sycophants for a turn-4 Mountain Folk, so Expand comes off money around turns 5–9 instead of early off Favor draw. Its card-level claims check out against the text.
