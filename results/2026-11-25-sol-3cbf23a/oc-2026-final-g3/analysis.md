# Kingdom analysis

**Build a Bandit Camp and Council Room engine, then use Horn of Plenty to gain Provinces alongside your buys.** Forager and Scrounge get the expensive pieces into your deck early. Open **Forager + Scrounge on 4/3** and **Upgrade + pass on 5/2**. Aim for a first double-Province turn around **turns 8–10**, with an earlier scoring pivot if the mirror threatens to end on piles. ## Board and setup

| Cost | Kingdom piles and landscape |
|---|---|
| \$3 | Forager, Scrounge (Event) |
| \$4 | Walled Village |
| \$5 | Bandit Camp, Council Room, Horn of Plenty, Inn, Patrol, Upgrade |
| 8 Debt | Overlord |
| \$4 + Potion | Golem |

Two players, eight Provinces, no Colonies, Platinum, or Shelters. Golem adds the \$4 Potion pile to the Supply. Bandit Camp adds the non-Supply Spoils pile. Both players start with seven Coppers and three Estates, so the opening splits are 4/3 or 5/2 in either order. Six kingdom piles cost \$5, which makes Scrounge’s \$3 gain especially valuable.

Council Room and Patrol supply terminal draw. Bandit Camp, Walled Village, and Inn supply extra Actions. Forager and Council Room supply +Buy, while Forager, Upgrade, and Scrounge thin one card at a time. Horn, Upgrade, and Bandit Camp gain cards without spending a Buy. There are no Attacks, although each Council Room gives the opponent a card. All scoring comes from Provinces, Duchies, Estates, and the usual −1 VP for a Curse. The main race is to the eight Provinces, with Scrounge making Duchies cheap enough to create a credible three-pile finish.

## Engine

Start with **one Forager, one Bandit Camp, and one Council Room**. Add a second source of draw and enough villages to reach it. A second Camp is the best economy upgrade when you can draw its Spoils. Walled Village is the cheap way to add Actions and a new name. Inn is strongest when its gain can fix the next hand. Patrol is a good second draw card, especially once players start greening.

One favorable 4/3 line shows how the engine can reach its first double. It assumes the opponent leaves your trashed Estates available and gives you no Council Room draws.

| Turn | Worked line |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Buy Forager and use Scrounge to trash an Estate. |
| 3–4 | With Forager, three Coppers, and an Estate, trash the Estate and Scrounge for Bandit Camp. The other hand has four Coppers and an Estate. Scrounge for Council Room using the remaining Estate in the trash. |
| 5 | Play Camp and Council Room, and trash a Copper with Forager. Four Coppers plus Forager’s \$1 buy Upgrade. |
| 6 | Upgrade an Estate into a second Forager. Camp and Council Room reach the original Forager and enough money for \$8 from four Coppers, a Spoils, and Forager. Buy Horn and Scrounge for Inn. Shuffle Inn and the newly gained Forager into the three cards left in your deck. |
| 7 | Inn finds Camp and Council Room. Five distinct Actions, Copper, and Horn reach seven names, so Horn gains Gold. Spoils, Copper, and Forager make \$5 to buy Walled Village. |
| 8 | Walled Village and Camp start the chain into Inn, Council Room, Upgrade, and Forager. Those six Action names plus Copper, Gold, and Horn give nine names. Copper, Gold, Spoils, and Forager make \$8 for a second Province. |

The turn-8 line needs a favorable shuffle. For a sense of the opening-hand risk, a random five-card hand from a 16-card deck with three villages and two terminal draw cards opens with both about **35%** of the time and with neither about **11%** of the time. Village draws, Upgrade, Inn’s gain, and Walled Village’s topdeck bridge some of those hands. Expect near-full draws around turns 8–10 when the build goes well. Budget for repeatable doubles only once you have two live draw cards and can still reach your money and Horn after adding green.

A built turn with two Spoils, Gold, Silver, Copper, and \$1 from Forager makes **\$13**, with **three Buys** from one Council Room and one Forager. At eight names, one Horn adds a Province to the Province and Duchy you can buy. A third playable Spoils raises the same turn to **\$16**, enough for two bought Provinces plus the Horn Province. Each scoring Horn is one-use, so save that ceiling for turns when the points or the ending justify it.

## Key interactions

- **Forager or Upgrade + Scrounge.** Trash an Estate in the Action phase and spend \$3 in the Buy phase to take an Estate and a \$5 piece. Upgrade can turn the original Estate into a Forager or Silver along the way. With two Buys and two Estates in the shared trash, \$6 can gain two \$5 pieces and two Estates. Take the extra bulk when the gains solve draw, Actions, or economy. Later, one Scrounge can gain Duchy plus Estate for **4 VP**. If you just trashed the Estate yourself, the turn nets **3 VP** from that exchange. [Plunder rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/DomPlunder.pdf)
- **Bandit Camp + draw.** Each Camp puts a \$3 Spoils into the discard after its own draw. Play Camp before Council Room or Patrol when the latter can trigger a shuffle and reach the Spoils. The first Copper in the shared trash makes every Forager worth \$1. A Horn trashed for scoring adds another Treasure name for future Forager plays on both sides. [Dark Ages rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Dominion-Rules-DarkAges.pdf)
- **Horn + distinct names.** Five names gain an engine piece, six gain Gold, and eight gain Province. A clean eight is **Bandit Camp, Council Room, Walled Village, Upgrade, Forager, Copper, Gold, Horn**. Play Treasures that stay in play before Horn. Spoils returns immediately, and a card played from the Supply by Overlord stays there, so only Overlord contributes a name. With seven other names, each successive Horn can gain a Province because each counts itself as it resolves. Keep a Copper until you can replace its name reliably. [Cornucopia & Guilds rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/CornucopiaGuilds.pdf), [Empires rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Dominion_Empire_rule.pdf)
- **Inn + deliberate gain order.** An Inn gained by Upgrade, Horn, Scrounge, or a buy can shuffle itself and selected Actions from your discard into your deck. When you can, gain the Action you want first and Inn second. If five or fewer cards remain after the shuffle, you know which cards will be in your next hand. Inn gives a spare Action and sifts two cards, so it can find the first draw card even though playing it shrinks your hand by one. [Hinterlands rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/DomHinterlands-1.pdf)

## Other cards and ratings check

| Card | Best use here |
|---|---|
| **Patrol** | Play it before Council Room when you have the Actions. Patrol pulls green out of the next four cards and orders the rest for Council Room or your next hand. It also gives Horn a new name. |
| **Walled Village** | On a turn with only Walled Village and one other Action, topdeck it to guarantee a village next turn. That is most useful while the engine is still small. [Official promo notes](https://www.riograndegames.com/games/dominion-walled-village-promo/) |
| **Overlord** | A spare Bandit Camp when you need Actions and money, or Council Room when you need draw. It adds its own name for Horn. Consider one once you can absorb the debt, and check whether its best targets are about to empty. |
| **Golem** | It can play Camp and terminal draw in either order, including with no spare Action. It can also discard the Horn or Spoils you wanted to draw. Potion costs a gain and a deck slot, and the deck already has three villages. Skip it by default. [Alchemy rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/Dominion-Alchemy-Rules.pdf) |
| **Upgrade** | Estate becomes Forager or Silver. Silver or Forager can become Walled Village, Walled Village can become any \$5 piece, and a surplus \$5 can become Gold. Upgrade is also clean Copper thinning because there is no \$1 target. [Intrigue rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Dominion-Rules-Intrigue-2nd-Edition.pdf) |

The ratings agree on Horn (+3.1), Upgrade (+3.1), and Forager (+2.7). I’d move **Bandit Camp (0.0), Council Room (−0.9), and Scrounge (−1.0)** up most for this board. Camp provides both terminal slots and renewable money, Council provides the fastest raw draw and another Buy, and Scrounge reaches every crowded \$5 pile while doubling as late scoring. Golem (−3.8) has a real interaction with Camp and draw, but the extra acquisition cost is still hard to justify. ## Plans considered and matchups

| Plan | Race and matchup |
|---|---|
| **Horn engine — recommended** | First \$6+ around turns 5–6, first \$8 around turns 6–8, and first double around turns 8–10. One Horn is enough to threaten a double, while two give you a stronger finish. The best opponent mirrors the build, takes useful parts and any Estates you leave in the trash, and scores as soon as the pile counts favor them. |
| **Compact Bandit Camp engine** | Use the same opening, then spend the first \$8 on a second Camp and a Scrounged Patrol if an Estate is available. Two Spoils, two Coppers, Silver, and Forager make \$11. With two Buys that can take Province plus Scrounged Duchy and Estate for 10 VP. It can green around turns 6–8 and is the best pivot if Horns are scarce or the board is already closing. A healthy Horn mirror can overtake it by gaining a Province without spending the \$8. |
| **Patrol Big Money** | Open Forager/Silver on 4/3. Forager plus Silver and two Coppers can Scrounge for Patrol after trashing an Estate. A Patrol hand with two Silvers and three Coppers hits \$7 around turn 5, and Gold, two Silvers, and Copper hit \$8 after Gold enters the next shuffle, around turns 7–8. Council Room is a faster raw draw option, but giving the engine an extra card helps its weakest stage. Money can get the first Province and exploit an engine stall, but it has less control of the finish. |

The compact engine is the strongest alternative. Its best case is an early \$11 with two Buys and an Estate available, followed by another scoring turn while the Horn player is still adding names. That is a genuine threat. I’d still take Horn when the deck can already reach five or six names, because it builds for free immediately and can add six points to a later turn. The recommended line loses if it buys Horn before it can draw and use it, loads up on Scrounged Estates, or delays scoring into a pile-out. Adding Potion and Golem makes sense only if you can afford both gains and are repeatedly short of playable Actions.

## Openings and buy priorities

- **4/3 or 3/4.** Buy **Forager** with one opening hand and **Scrounge** with the other. If an Estate is already in the trash, turn it into Bandit Camp. Otherwise trash one of your own Estates. If the opponent takes it, your first Forager can often seed another and Scrounge on the same turn. Forager/Silver is the fallback when you want more immediate coin and expect Estates to be contested.
- **5/2 or 2/5.** Buy **Upgrade** on \$5 and **pass** on \$2. The first Estate-to-Forager play gives you a useful card and can supply the Estate for a \$3 Scrounge that same turn. Opening Overlord alone uses all seven opening coins and still leaves one Debt. [Empires debt rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Dominion-Rules-Empires.pdf)
- **Second shuffle.** Get Camp and Council Room online. Use \$3 hands for those pieces when Scrounge is live. Use \$4 for Walled Village if Actions are the bottleneck. Take a direct \$5 buy when you can afford it and would rather keep the Estate out of your deck.
- **Third shuffle.** Add the first Horn once five names are realistic, then use its gains to fill whichever gap persists. Favor a second draw card if you leave money or villages buried. Favor Camp if you draw well and come up short on coin. Start greening once you can take **Province plus another score** or when passing a Province gives the opponent control of the ending.

## Ending and risks

Expect a Province ending unless **Duchy and two contested kingdom piles** get low together. Track Horn of Plenty, Bandit Camp, and whichever of Council Room or Forager the two decks are taking fastest. Scrounge retrieves Estates from the trash, so those gains leave the Estate Supply untouched. With an Estate available, **\$11 and two Buys buy Province plus Scrounge for 10 VP**. Add a Province from Horn and the turn scores **16 VP**. When two Provinces remain, count what the opponent can gain through Horn as well as what they can buy, and check the turn-count tiebreak before leaving the last Province.

My least certain call is **how early to buy the first Horn in a contested 4/3 mirror**. Turn 6 works in the example because Inn makes turn 7 playable and the deck reaches seven names. If your actual hands are still missing terminal draw or a village, take that piece first. With more players, contested piles drain faster, Council Room hands out more cards, and shared Estates can disappear before your next turn. That favors same-turn Estate conversions and an earlier scoring pivot. The timing above comes from worked hands and deck counts, so use the live pile counts and your last shuffle to set the pace.
