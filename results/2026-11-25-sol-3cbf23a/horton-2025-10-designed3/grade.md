## Beyond the key
- Buying the Exploration Project on turn 1 gives a Coffer and a Villager at once: plausible. A Project is not a gained card, so the Buy phase had no gains.
- 5/2 Exploration then Steward, using the Coffer to reach $3: plausible. Exploration costs $4 and the $2 hand plus 1 Coffer makes $3.
- Cabin Boy converts into Garrison early or Stronghold later, only if that Fort is exposed at the start of turn, so a Tent played that turn is too late: plausible. Only the top card of Forts can be gained, and Cabin Boy's choice comes at the start of the turn.
- A Cabin Boy conversion at the start of the turn counts as a gain for Destrier's discount: plausible. Destrier counts every card gained this turn.
- Hill Fort gaining Ducat into hand lets you trash a Copper and play the Ducat for its Buy: plausible. Ducat's on-gain trashes a Copper from hand, and its "put it into your hand" mode puts the Ducat in hand.
- Garrison must be played before Hill Fort to count its gain: plausible. Garrison counts gains "this turn" only after it is played.
- Reckless Guide plays as +2 Cards +2 Actions, and its call is single, after which it returns to its pile at Clean-up: plausible. Reckless doubles played instructions, and the called Guide is in play and is discarded from there.
- Wishing Well draws before revealing, so it consumes a Harbinger topdeck: plausible. That's the order of its card text.
- $13 with three Buys from Garrison, Mystic, Gold, two Silvers and two Ducats: plausible. $2+$2+$3+$4 plus 2 Coffers is 13, and 1+2 Buys is 3.
- Destrier-miss probabilities (29% for a five-card start, 10% for an eight-card start): plausible. C(13,5)/C(16,5)=0.295 and C(13,8)/C(16,8)=0.10.
- Steward is the opening trasher: plausible. It trashes 2 cards per play, but the key doesn't discuss it, and it takes Tent's opening slot.
- Mystic-Destrier and Big Money + Garrison are credible alternatives: doubtful. The key treats a deck without Hill Forts as the trap side of the race.

## Summary
The analysis builds the deck Horton warns can run away: Hill Fort gains plus Ducats making Destrier cheap. It knows the Forts pile order well (Tent rotation, Cabin Boy into exposed Forts). It opens Exploration/Steward instead of Tent/Exploration, settles for one Garrison, and aims for one or two Hill Forts instead of emptying them. Reckless Guide gets an accurate description but stays a side piece.
