## Beyond the key
- Cage plus Market Square: a Victory gain trashes Cage, and each Market Square in hand can be discarded for a Gold: plausible. Market Square reacts to "one of your cards is trashed", and Cage trashes itself on the next Victory gain.
- A Cage that set aside nothing still waits to trigger: plausible. Its trash is a future effect, so it stays in play.
- Border Guard discarding Tunnel gains a Gold, and Lantern can catch two: plausible. The discard is not during Clean-up, and Lantern makes Border Guard discard 2.
- Horn with Way of the Squirrel to start the next turn with 7 cards including the same Border Guard: plausible. Horn tops the Guard during Clean-up, Squirrel draws 2 more at the end of the turn, and played as Squirrel the Guard gives no +Action, so it costs an Action.
- An opposing Jester hitting Tunnel gives its owner a Gold chance as well as a Curse: plausible. Jester's discard is not during Clean-up.
- Seaway's +Buy token also pays when the card is played as Squirrel: plausible. A Way still plays the card, and the token fires "when you play a card from that pile".
- Jester's first hit on an opening deck is about 25% Estate, 58% Copper and 17% one of the two buys: plausible. That is 3, 7 and 2 of 12 cards.
- The payload arithmetic ($16 and 5 Buys after two Litter Debt) and the Vineyard thresholds (4 VP at 12 Actions, and the 11th-to-12th Action worth 3 VP with three Vineyards): plausible. Both check out against the card text.
- Silver/Astrolabe hits $5 on turn 3 or 4 about 81% of the time, against 91% for Silver/Silver: doubtful. The numbers weren't verified, and the analysis may count Astrolabe's second-turn $1 favorably.
- Opening Silver/Astrolabe and buying Gold as core payload: doubtful. Neither player did either. Astrolabe went unused, and the winners' payload was Actions and Jester.

## Summary
The analysis reads the engine pieces correctly: Litter as the village and draw, Cage on Estates, Patrol over Council Room once greening, and cheap Actions. It even spells out the Vineyard math. But it makes Provinces the main plan and expects a Province ending, while the game was a Vineyard race that piled out on Vineyard, Border Guard and Market Square by turn 14. A player following it would likely green on Provinces and lose the Vineyard split to an Action-denser opponent.
