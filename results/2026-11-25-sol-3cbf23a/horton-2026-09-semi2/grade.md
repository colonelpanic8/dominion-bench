## Beyond the key
- Each player starts with 5 Favors (Importer's 4 plus the standard 1), Trappers' Lodge spends them to topdeck gains, and nothing replenishes them: plausible. Importer is the only Liaison, and its only Favor text is the Setup line.
- Souk turn 1 → turn 2 Souk with 2 Coppers and 2 Estates makes $5 for a second Souk that trashes both Estates, giving a guaranteed $6 on turn 3 and $12 with 3 Buys on turn 4: plausible. I checked it step by step against the Souk, Tireless, Trappers' Lodge, Village Green and Squire text, and the arithmetic and deck order work out.
- Ronin bought into the turn-4 shuffle goes to the bottom and can be played from the deck: plausible. This is the Shadow rule, and Ronin's text allows playing it from the deck.
- Order of play: play the non-drawing Actions first and shrink the hand before each Souk (four Souks making $2/$3/$4/$5), and a Copper held back for Souk's trash costs $1 per Souk played: plausible. This follows Souk's "-$1 per card in your hand." It deserves extra credit for handling Souk's tension with Ronin, though its turn-5 line plays Ronin before the Souks.
- Nothing on the board triggers Village Green's discard Reaction, and there is no Attack for a trashed Squire to gain: plausible. The kingdom has no discard effects outside Clean-up and no Attack cards.
- Transmogrify turns an Estate into Importer or a $4 Action into Souk, straight into hand: plausible. The card says "up to $1 more ... into your hand."
- Enlarge turns a $5 Action into Animal Fair, or Fair or Gold into Province, and its second trash is mandatory: plausible. The card says "gain one costing up to $2 more," and "Trash a card" has no "may."
- Trash an Action to buy Animal Fair, and with 2 piles empty a played Fair alone gives 3 Buys ($12 for a Province and the last two Estates): plausible. This follows Animal Fair's text.
- Money alternative (topdeck Gold on turn 3 for $9 on turn 4, then a guaranteed $8+ on turn 5): plausible. Spot-checked against the deck order.
- "Roughly 76% chance" of seeing a second village on turn 5: doubtful but unverified. It depends on the exact shuffle composition, which the analysis doesn't fully state.

## Summary
The analysis gets the hardest rules point right, Tireless timing, and uses Village Green correctly to recover Souks, so it avoids the tournament's misplay. But it builds a Trappers' Lodge Souk rush with Ronin as support. It misses the designer's Armory → Transmogrify/Importer → Coin of the Realm opening and buys Souk, not Ronin, with its first $5s. A follower would play a different, Souk-first deck from the one Horton recommends.
