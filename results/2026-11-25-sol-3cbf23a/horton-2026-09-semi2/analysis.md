# Horton September 2026 semifinal 2

**Choose $5 first and open Tireless Souk.** Trappers’ Lodge lets you play it on turn 2 and buy a second Souk while trashing two Estates. A Squire and Village Green then guarantee **$12 and three Buys on turn 4**. Use that turn to add Ronin and more villages. Take a third Souk early, and take a fourth if you can use its trashing and still support the terminals.

The deck wins by playing several Souks into a small hand. Village Green retrieves the Souks that Tireless puts just beyond your opening hand, Squire clears a card from hand while adding an Action and $1, and Ronin draws on demand after you’ve made room. Animal Fair and Enlarge give the deck a second way to convert that control into points.

## Board and setup

The game has two players, standard 7 Copper and 3 Estate starting decks, and eight Provinces. The tournament lets you arrange a $5/$2 or $4/$3 split. There are no Shelters, Platinum, Colonies, split piles, or non-Supply cards. Importer gives each player **five starting Favors** including the normal Ally Favor. Trappers’ Lodge spends one to topdeck a gain, and nothing replenishes them. Tireless is on Souk. [Kingdom and supplied card text](/tmp/claude-1000/-home-imalison-projects-dominion-arena/f8629424-2e36-48be-8522-e621f2b4ebe7/scratchpad/sol-bench/sb/horton-2026-09-semi2/kingdoms/horton-2026-09-semi2.md), [Allies rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Dominion-Allies-Rules.pdf)

| Cost | Piles | What they contribute |
|---|---|---|
| $2 | Squire, Coin of the Realm | Squire is the first cheap village and can switch to +2 Buys or an early Silver. Coin banks +2 Actions for a later turn and is especially good with a Ronin already in the deck. |
| $3 | Importer | Gains any $5 piece at the start of next turn. Best in the $3-first opening or when you can spare a terminal slot to build. |
| $4 | Village Green, Armory, Transmogrify | Village Green is the main village and bridges Tireless. Armory topdecks cheap engine pieces. Transmogrify can turn an Estate into an Importer or a $4 Action into a Souk directly in hand. |
| $5 | Souk, Ronin, Enlarge | The crowded price point. Souk supplies thinning, money and +Buy. Ronin supplies the only draw that substantially grows the hand. Enlarge upgrades twice and can gain Provinces. |
| $7 | Animal Fair | Flat +$4, extra Buys after piles empty, and an alternate purchase price of an Action from hand. Its printed cost also makes it good Province fodder for Enlarge. |

Province, Duchy and Estate are the scoring routes, with Curses worth −1 VP. There are no attacks or VP tokens, and nothing here triggers Village Green’s discard Reaction or gives Squire an Attack to gain. Souk is the main source of true thinning. Enlarge and Transmogrify usually replace what they trash, while Coin and Transmogrify can wait on the Tavern mat.

## Opening

| Split | Play |
|---|---|
| **$5 → $2** | **Souk, topdeck it.** Turn 2 is Souk, two Coppers and two Estates. Play Souk for $3, add the two Coppers, buy a second Souk, trash both Estates and topdeck the gain. |
| **$3 → $4** | **Importer, topdeck it, then Village Green, topdeck it.** Put the four Coppers first in the second hand. Play Importer on turn 2 and buy Village Green. Turn 3 Importer gains Souk. Trash up to two Estates, topdeck Souk, then draw it with Village Green. This makes $6–$7, enough for a second Souk and, at $7, Squire. |
| **$4 → $3** | **Armory, topdeck it.** Arrange turn 2 so the topdeck pushes out an Estate. Play Armory to topdeck Importer and buy Silver. Play Importer on turn 3 to gain Souk on turn 4. |
| **$2 → $5** | **Squire, topdeck it.** On turn 2, play Squire for $1 and a Silver gain, then use four Coppers to buy and topdeck Souk. This gets a free Silver, but the opening Souk gain has no Estates in hand to trash. |

### The $5-first line through turn 7

Tireless puts a played Souk onto the deck **after** the next hand is drawn. That timing makes the following line work. [Plunder rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/DomPlunder.pdf)

- **Turn 3 is a guaranteed $6.** The second Souk joins three Coppers and the last Estate. Buy **Village Green and Squire** and topdeck both, spending your third and fourth Favors.
- **Turn 4 is a guaranteed $12 and three Buys.** Start with Village Green, Squire, the first Souk and two Coppers. Village Green draws the second Souk. Play Squire for Actions and $1, then the Souks for $4 and $5, then the Coppers. Buy **Ronin, Village Green and Squire** for $11. Spend the last Favor on Village Green. Clean-up forces a shuffle, so Ronin goes to the bottom where it can be played as a Shadow. [Rising Sun rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/DomRisingSunRules.pdf)
- **Turn 5 can buy Ronin and a third Souk while trashing two Coppers.** For a worked draw, start with the guaranteed Village Green and two Coppers, plus Squire and Copper from the shuffle. Play Squire and Village Green, draw one Souk, then play Ronin from the deck to draw the other Souk, the second Squire and the other Village Green. Use the Squire for Actions and set Village Green to next turn. The two Souks make $3 and $4. The Squires make $2, so one Copper pays for the two $5 gains and the other two can be trashed.
- **Turn 6 can buy the fourth Souk in that line.** The delayed Village Green draws one of the two Tireless Souks into a hand of four Coppers and an Estate. Souk makes $2, so three Coppers pay for the gain, leaving the fourth Copper and the Estate to trash.
- **Turn 7 can double Province.** For example, start with the unplayed Souk from turn 6, another Souk, Village Green, Squire and Copper. Village Green draws the Tireless Souk. Squire makes room for Ronin to draw the other Village Green, the other Squire and a Copper. The second Village Green can draw the fourth Souk. With two Coppers left, the four Souks make $2, $3, $4 and $5. Two Squires and two Coppers bring that to **$18 and five Buys**. If the last Village Green instead draws a Copper, the same line makes **$14 with three Souks**, enough for Province and Duchy.

Turns 5–7 are a worked branch. The first four turns require no favorable shuffle. On turn 5, the opening village and Shadow Ronin give roughly a **76% chance of seeing at least one more village** in the first three cards seen from the shuffled non-Shadow cards, enough to play both Souks. The exact $12 turn above needs a better draw than that.

## Engine and key interactions

**Build around two Village Greens, two Squires, two Ronins and three or four Souks.** That is a target for the first few shuffles. A Coin is good insurance if you have seen a hand where Ronin and Souk compete for your only Action. The deck can reach most of its payload by turn 7 on a good draw. I’d plan for more regular big turns around **turns 8–10**, with occasional partial turns when villages bunch together or green cards fill Ronin’s hand.

- **Tireless + Village Green + Ronin.** A delayed Village Green gives a card and two Actions before playing anything, which can recover one Tireless Souk immediately. Ronin can pick up the rest from the deck once Squires, Reserve cards or other terminals have reduced hand size. Use Village Green immediately when it keeps the current turn going. Delay a spare copy when the next turn needs a starter. [Plunder rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/DomPlunder.pdf), [Menagerie rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/DominionMenagerie.pdf), [Rising Sun rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/DomRisingSunRules.pdf)
- **Souk + extra Buys.** Play the non-drawing Actions first, then calculate whether you can leave Coppers unplayed for an on-gain trash. With two Souks still producing money, a Copper left in hand for both costs $2 in Souk output and produces only $1. Treasures played before the purchase are already out of hand, so they can’t be trashed by the gain.
- **Squire or spare gainer + Animal Fair.** An unplayed Armory, spent-purpose Importer or excess Action can pay for a Fair while preserving your coin for another buy. Fair then supplies $4 and clears its own hand slot before Souk. Each Fair gives one Buy per pile already empty **when played**. Keep the Squires you still need as villages. [Menagerie rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Dominion-Menagerie-Rules.pdf)
- **Enlarge + Animal Fair or Gold.** Enlarge can turn a $5 Action into a $7 Fair, or a Fair or Gold into a Province. The second conversion comes at the start of next turn and is mandatory. One conversion plus $16 and two Buys can take three Provinces in a turn, provided the card you trash is accounted for in the $16 calculation. [Plunder rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/DomPlunder.pdf)
- **Importer, Armory and Transmogrify.** Importer into Souk is a free gain and up to two trashes. Armory into Village Green or Squire fixes the next hand and is particularly useful if Village Green can draw the gain immediately. Transmogrify can put a $5 piece directly into hand from a $4 card, but it takes a play and a later call to do so. Coin is the cheaper way to insure a specific future turn against terminal collision. [Adventures rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/DomAdventures2022.pdf)

The local ratings already favor Souk (+4.4). The board-specific changes are **Tireless** (−1.8), which becomes predictable access with Ronin and Village Green, and **Animal Fair** (−1.1), which has cheap Action fodder and improves Souk’s payout. **Squire** (+0.5) matters more here because its $1 and hand reduction help on the same turns that need its Actions. **Armory** (−2.2) has good cheap targets, but is much more valuable in a $4-first opening than after the preferred start. ## Plans and matchups

| Plan | Best line and main weakness |
|---|---|
| **Souk–Ronin engine** | Guaranteed $6 on turn 3 and $12 on turn 4. Two Souks are enough to start. Aim for three or four, at least two dependable village sources, and a second Ronin by the time you green. The weak turn is a hand that spends its only Action on Ronin or fills to seven before finding a playable village. |
| **Souk money, with Ronin as support** | Take Gold on turn 3 and topdeck it. Turn 4 is Souk, Gold and three Coppers for $9. Take Province immediately, or buy Gold and Silver and topdeck the Gold for a guaranteed $8 or more on turn 5. This is the strongest early Province race. A low village count makes it harder to combine Ronin and Souk or reach the engine’s burst turns. |
| **Early Animal Fair into Enlarge** | On turn 4, Village Green can support both Souks while Squire stays in hand. That makes $9 and three Buys. Trash Squire to buy Fair, then buy Ronin and Village Green with the $9. This gives immediate access to stronger flat payload and opens the Province-conversion line. Losing the Squire makes the following shuffle less reliable, so this is more attractive when you have villages to spare or the Souk split is going badly. |

**Against the best response, expect a mirror.** Both players can force the turn-4 start. Village density decides who converts the next shuffle into more gains, and the Souk split decides who can keep thinning while building payload. Contest the third and fourth Souks when the gains also remove junk. If the opponent gets the better split, add Animal Fair and Enlarge and take points while they are still adding villages.

The money runner-up is most dangerous when it shows an early Province and the engine misses a village on successive turns. Its best response to the engine is to keep taking Provinces and pick up Duchies once the engine threatens the last batch. Against money, the engine can spend an extra turn building when it sees a credible two-Province turn ahead. In a mirror, start scoring earlier. A $13 Province-and-Duchy turn can matter more than another build turn once your opponent can take two Provinces or end on piles.

## Buying and ending

After the forced opening, buy **village support before another terminal** if Actions caused the last miss. Buy another Souk when you can leave junk unplayed for it. Buy Ronin when village support exists and you keep missing payload. Add an Animal Fair when you can play it alongside the Souks, or Enlarge when you have clear upgrades and a productive target for its second trash. In the mirror, turn 7 is already a plausible greening turn. Take a Province sooner if the opponent is scoring and the gain leaves your next shuffle functional.

Provinces are the natural ending. **Souk, Village Green and Squire** are the first Kingdom piles I’d count for a three-pile finish. Ronin can replace one if both players contest draw, and Duchy or Estate can become the last pile once players green. Count Armory and Enlarge gains as well as Buys. With two piles already empty, a played Animal Fair starts you at three Buys by itself, enough at $12 to take Province and the last two Estates. Check the lead and the fewer-turns tiebreak before handing back a turn.

**What would change the plan.** A poor Souk split pushes toward Fair and Enlarge. A missed village shuffle under Province pressure pushes toward taking points with the deck you have. At three or four players, the same ten-copy engine piles are shared more widely, so secure your villages and Ronin earlier and track three-pile endings sooner. At five or six players, the four-pile ending applies.

**Least sure of.** The decision closest to flipping is the turn-4 purchase. I favor Ronin, Village Green and Squire because they make the next turn more likely to play both Souks. The early Fair line wins that comparison if it can retain enough village access to play Fair and both Souks on consecutive turns. The turn estimates assume ordinary shuffles after the arranged opening and use the supplied card text and official timing rulings.
