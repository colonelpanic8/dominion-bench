## Beyond the key
- Mandarin 5/2 opening (Mandarin on $5, the 5 Coppers redrawn for Barge, then Lurker on the $2 hand, all before the turn-4 shuffle): plausible. Mandarin's on-gain topdecks the Coppers, so clean-up draws exactly them.
- Gamble into a topdecked Lurker that gains a trashed Mandarin, then Gamble replays each Gold for +$1 net: plausible. Each step follows the card text, and Lurker can trash Mandarin from the Supply first. The math is right too ($3 per Gold minus $2 per Gamble).
- Buying Mandarin to reset Golds "takes five Gold replays just to recoup": doubtful as strategy. The arithmetic only holds inside the Gamble frame, and the expert's uncontested clock ($118 by turn 11) shows Mandarin Gold-recycling is the payload, not a marginal trick.
- Mandarin topdecking Barge, Village Green or Tunnel for Gamble to hit (Durations played in the Buy phase pay off next turn, Tunnel discarded for a Gold): plausible under the card text.
- Two Displaces: Exile Gold for Province, then Exile Province for Gold, which releases the Exiled Golds (+6 VP): plausible by the rules. Exiled cards still score, and gaining a card releases its Exiled copies. Economically it is weak.
- Displace Tunnel into Duchy for +3 VP, and regaining Tunnel releases Exiled Tunnels that each gain a Gold: plausible. Discarding from Exile is a discard outside Clean-up.
- Probabilities (54% / 77% to see one of 2 Village Greens in 5 / 8 of 16 cards) and Silk Road's 32 VP total: correct arithmetic.
- Cardinal discarding the opponent's Tunnel or Village Green triggers them: plausible. It is a downside of Cardinal, which the analysis frames ambiguously.
- Lurker pairs (one trashes a contested Action from the Supply, the other gains it): plausible.

## Summary
The analysis spots Courtier's three-type reveal of Village Green and the Mandarin on-gain Treasure reset, but it never combines them into the expert's Courtier/Mandarin Gold-replay engine. Instead it builds a Barge/Village Green engine that scores through Displace, the gainer the expert called much too slow. Steward thinning and the +Buy sources are right.
