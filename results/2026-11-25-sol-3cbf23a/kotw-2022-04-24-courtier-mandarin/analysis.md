**Build Village Green–Barge with Courtier for payload and Displace for scoring.** Open **Silver–Steward** on 4/3. On a 5/2 split, gain **Mandarin on the $5 hand**, buy **Lurker on the $2 hand**, and use the five Coppers Mandarin puts back to buy **Barge** before your first shuffle.

## Engine and opening

The first useful core is **Steward, Village Green, two Barges and Courtier**. Aim for a second Village Green and a third Barge as you add Displace. Two Lurkers are worth buying when you can line them up to gain contested parts. One can trash a Barge or Village Green from the Supply and the other can gain it on the same turn. A lone Lurker can use Goat while thinning matters or recover an Action already in the shared trash. [Intrigue rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/Dominion-Intrigue-2nd-Edition-Rules.pdf)

- **4/3 or 3/4 — Silver and Steward.** Silver supports the first $5 while Steward trashes. Cardinal–Steward is the aggressive opening, but a collision makes you choose between thinning and attacking. Buy Cardinal later if you have the Actions and their deck has valuable targets.
- **$5 first — Mandarin, Barge, Lurker.** Mandarin puts the five played Coppers back for turn 2. Buy Barge with them, then buy Lurker with the original $2 hand on turn 3.
- **$2 first — Lurker, Mandarin, Barge.** Buy Mandarin on turn 2 and Barge with the same five Coppers on turn 3. Both Mandarin openings start the first shuffled hand on turn 4 with all three gains available. Use Mandarin for $3 when it hits a price point, and use Goat or Displace when the terminal slot becomes more valuable than its payload. [First-edition Hinterlands rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/Freigabe-PDF_Dominion_Hinterlands_US_Rule.pdf)

A favorable but legal **4/3 build** shows what the engine can do. The timing assumes helpful shuffles and no key Cardinal hit.

| Turn | Worked line |
|---|---|
| 3–4 | Steward trashes two Estates and the two remaining Coppers buy Lurker. Silver and four Coppers make $6 on turn 4. Buy Barge. |
| 5–7 | Barge draws to $7 and two Buys on turn 5. Buy Village Green and a second Lurker. On turn 6, Village Green lets the Lurkers acquire Barge #2 and Steward trash two Coppers. The first Barge draws to $7 again on turn 7. Buy Courtier. |
| 8 | Courtier reveals Village Green for +Action, +$3 and a Gold. Village Green and two Barges see 12 of 14 cards. The Lurkers gain Village Green #2. Courtier, Silver and three Coppers make $8, enough for Displace and Silver with the extra Buy. |
| 9 | With two Village Greens and two Barges, reach Displace, the first Gold and both Silvers. Exile the Gold for a Province. Courtier, two Silvers and two Coppers still make $9 for a second Province. |

Treat turn 9 as the fast end of the range. I’d expect the engine to see most of its deck around **turns 8–11**, with the first two-Province turn around **turns 9–12**. A built turn with one Barge due, two Barges played now and two Village Greens sees up to **16 cards**. The Villages give three terminal slots, enough for both live Barges and Displace if Courtier takes +Action. Two Courtiers, two played Golds and two Silvers make **$16 with four Buys** in that setup. A third Gold in hand can become a third Province through Displace if the pile and score warrant it.

**Reliability is the main constraint.** Two Village Greens among 16 drawable cards give only a 54% chance of seeing one in the opening five, rising to 77% with a delayed Barge’s three start-of-turn cards. Bank Village Green when the current hand can spare the Actions, and stagger Barges so one good turn sets up the next. Keep enough Copper or Silver to reach $4–$5 until Courtier’s Golds arrive.

## Key interactions

- **Courtier + Village Green.** Reveal Village Green *before* playing it. Its three types buy +Action, +$3 and either Gold during the build or +Buy when it matters. Multiple Courtiers can reveal the same Village Green. Barge, Cardinal and Tunnel each offer two choices. With an Action to spare, revealing Barge for +$3 and Gold is already strong. [Intrigue rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/Dominion-Intrigue-2nd-Edition-Rules.pdf)
- **Mandarin + Lurker + Gamble.** Mandarin’s play can put a Lurker on top. With a Mandarin in the trash, play your Treasures, then Gamble into Lurker to gain that Mandarin. The gain puts your played Treasures back on top and you keep the money they already made. Each Gold replayed through Gamble nets **+$1**, each Silver breaks even, and each Copper costs $1. For example, Mandarin’s $3 and four Golds make $15. Gamble into Lurker for $2, then replay the four Golds to reach **$17**. With a second Buy from Barge, that takes two Provinces. Buying the reset Mandarin for $5 takes five Gold replays just to recoup the cost. Goat can supply a Mandarin from hand for a Lurker to recover, at the cost of another Action play. [Menagerie rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Dominion-Menagerie-Rules.pdf) · [First-edition Hinterlands rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/Freigabe-PDF_Dominion_Hinterlands_US_Rule.pdf)
- **Mandarin + Gamble also sets up Durations and Reactions.** Topdeck Barge or Village Green, pay $2 to play it in the Buy phase, and take its benefit next turn. Topdeck Tunnel and Gamble to discard it for a Gold. Each line uses a known top card. Gamble refunds the Buy it spends, and Treasures drawn into hand after the first purchase can only be played that turn through a separate effect that tells you to play them. [Menagerie rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Dominion-Menagerie-Rules.pdf) · [First-edition Hinterlands rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/Freigabe-PDF_Dominion_Hinterlands_US_Rule.pdf)
- **Displace builds first and scores later.** Estate becomes Village Green while keeping the Estate point. Silver can become Barge or Courtier. Gold becomes Province. With two Displaces, you can Exile Gold for Province, then Exile an older Province or Duchy for Gold and release your Exiled Golds. That adds **6 VP**, parks an older green card, and restores the Golds to your discard pile. [Menagerie rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Dominion-Menagerie-Rules.pdf)
- **Tunnel, Silk Road and Cardinal.** Displace can turn Tunnel into Duchy for **3 additional VP**, or alternate Tunnel and Silk Road to add one owned Victory card per play. Regaining Tunnel can release earlier copies and gain a Gold for each released Tunnel. Cardinal can also trigger Tunnel or Village Green if it discards them. An Exiled copy can react later when a matching gain releases it. This makes early Cardinal most attractive against decks whose best targets are Barge, Steward, Courtier or Gold. [Menagerie rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Dominion-Menagerie-Rules.pdf)

The [local card ratings](context/card-ratings.md) put Steward, Barge and Village Green among the stronger cards here. The biggest board-specific upgrades are **Courtier**, because Village Green has three types, **Lurker**, because it can acquire any of the contested Actions or thin through Goat, and **Mandarin**, because its gain effect changes the opening and gives Gamble known Treasure hits.

## Buying and ending

Prioritize **thinning and the first Barge** in the first shuffle. In the next, get **Village Green, Barge #2 and Courtier**, using a Lurker pair or Displace to gain whichever piece is hardest to buy. Add a second Village Green or another Barge when turns are stalling. Add **Displace** once you have the Actions, draw and Gold to make it score. Start greening when you can take two scoring cards in a turn, or sooner if the Province split is about to get away from you.

Expect a **Province ending** against money. In an engine mirror, track **Barge and Village Green** as possible empty piles, with **Duchy, Tunnel or Silk Road** as the third. Lurker can remove the last Action from the Supply without spending a Buy. Before ending on piles, count Exiled Victory cards and recalculate Silk Road at the final Victory-card count. With more players, repeated Cardinals and competition for Barges and Villages make a leaner build and earlier scoring more attractive. If Colonies are in play, Platinum both improves the engine’s payload and nets **+$3 per Gamble replay**, making the Mandarin line stronger.

**Challenge and least certain point.** The best rival is a lean Barge deck using early Mandarin gains to concentrate its Treasure, then using Gamble for a decisive double Province. That can win if Cardinal or a bad shuffle delays your second Barge or Village Green. The timing of the engine’s first repeatable two-score turn is my least certain estimate. If you reach turns **8–9** with just one effective Barge and no reliable source of spare Actions, take the available Provinces and lean into Barge money. If you’re already lining up two draw effects and Courtier, finish the engine.
