## Beyond the key
- Four Fairs + House + four Coppers cost $13 and topdeck exactly five cards: plausible. 1+8 Buys minus 4 for the Fairs leaves 5 for House + 4 Coppers ($0).
- Talisman doubles every Copper buy, so two Fairs + House + two Copper buys make a five-card stack for $9: plausible. Copper costs $0 and isn't a Victory card. Fairs are Events and aren't copied.
- Bank totals ($17 with 8 Coppers, $19 with 9, $24 with 7 Coppers + 2 Banks, $41 with 20 Coppers + Bank): plausible. Bank counts every Treasure in play, itself included.
- Horse Traders discards Coppers that Counting House then recovers, given Farming Village's +2 Actions: plausible. Horse Traders discards to the discard pile, which Counting House searches.
- Its Reaction's start-of-turn draw can force a shuffle when nothing is left on deck: plausible. It draws from the deck at the start of the turn.
- Buying Fair doesn't trigger Haunted Woods: plausible. An Event isn't a card gained.
- Jester can discard an opponent's topdecked House or Bank and gain you a copy: plausible. Jester hits the top card of the deck, which is exactly where the loop topdecks.
- Tower offsets Curses once the Curse pile empties: plausible. Curse is a non-Victory card.
- The Copper pile starts at 46: plausible (60 - 14). But a "24-17 split" adds up to only 41 of the 60 Coppers, which would all be owned once the pile empties unless Raze trashes some: doubtful arithmetic.
- The worked 5/2 line (money and Buy counts turn by turn, five Houses used by turn 7): plausible. Each turn's $ and Buys add up from the card text, but it assumes favorable draws.
- The Haunted Woods / Horse Traders Duration engine as an alternative: doubtful. Nobody in the thread argued for it, and the analysis itself ranks it below the combo.

## Summary
