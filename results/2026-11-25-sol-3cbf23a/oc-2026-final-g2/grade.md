## Beyond the key
- Horses make Wayfarer cost $3, so Rope plus Wayfarer costs $7 with Livery active: plausible. Wayfarer costs what the last other card gained cost, and the Horse ($3) is gained after the $4+ card.
- Triple Horse via Mastermind gives 6 cards and 3 Actions though it returns on the first play: plausible. Mastermind's three plays don't need the card to stay in play, and Horse's text has no condition on where it is.
- Mastermind on Mastermind lets the next turn triple three different Actions: plausible. Three plays make three start-of-turn effects.
- Two Abundances waiting and a Race in play turn $6 and one Buy into three Races for 6 VP: plausible. Both Abundances trigger on the first Action gain ($9, two more Buys), and Colonnade pays for each Race bought with Race in play.
- A Duration (Mastermind, Corsair, Captain) still in play from last turn qualifies its own duplicate for Colonnade: plausible. Colonnade checks only for a copy in play.
- Chariot Race played three times by Mastermind compares against the same opposing top card, which stays on top: plausible. The opponent reveals and does not move it.
- "Two ordinary Liveries, Artificer, Rope, and five Coppers make $13 and two Buys": doubtful. With no village only one Livery can be played, since Artificer gives back only the Action it uses.
- Mastermind on Livery with Artificer, Rope and five Coppers makes $16 and two Buys, two Provinces gaining six Horses: plausible. Three Livery effects each give a Horse per $4+ gain.
- Captain can borrow Wayfarer once a gain makes it cost $4 or less, set up by an earlier Mastermind'd Wayfarer gaining Silver: plausible. Wayfarer's cost changes and Captain checks it when it plays.
- Corsair is a matchup buy against Silver/Gold decks, paying off only on later shuffles: plausible. It trashes the Treasure after it was played. Untested here (key's grader note).
- Rope/Silver has about an 82% chance of $5 on turn 3 or 4: plausible. Rope's next-turn draw adds a sixth card; not checked.

## Summary
The analysis recommends the commentators' preferred Livery/Mastermind core with Artificer, Cursed Village and Rope. It also covers Colonnade scoring and the Copper-then-$0-Wayfarer trick that agoo03 won with, and it avoids all three traps. Its main gap is treating Rope as a one- or two-copy support card rather than the bulk economy buy, plus one $13 example that needs a village it doesn't have.
