# Horton September 2026 semifinal 1

**Choose $5/$2 and open Kitsune and Lurker.** Build toward three or four Highways, with Council Room for draw and Kitsune and Shop for money that survives Panic. Use Reckless Transmute when Lurker gives you cheap access. It can turn early Estates into spending power, clear Curses in the middle game, or take the last two Duchies without a Buy.

## Board

| Cost | Kingdom piles |
|---|---|
| $0 + Potion | Transmute, with Reckless |
| $2 | Lurker |
| $3 | Shop |
| $4 | Silk Merchant, Poet, Tools |
| $5 | Council Room, Figurine, Highway, Kitsune |

Prepare is a $3 Event. Panic starts with **five shared Suns**, removed by Kitsune and Poet. Transmute adds the 16-card Potion Supply pile. There are no Colonies, Platinum, Shelters, Heirlooms, split piles, or non-Supply piles. Each player starts with seven Coppers and three Estates and can arrange a $5/$2 or $4/$3 split. [Kingdom setup](kingdoms/horton-2026-09-semi1.md) · [Alchemy rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/Dominion-Alchemy-Rules.pdf)

The board starts as a race to play Kitsune and shifts into a race to play multiple Highways once Panic turns Treasures into one-use cards. Kitsune is the only attack, there is no defense against it, and Reckless Transmute is the only way to trash Curses from your deck. Scoring comes from Provinces, Duchies, Estates, and the Curse differential. Transmute gains Duchies directly, but every point still comes from a card in your deck. Expect a Province finish or a three-pile ending involving Curses and two contested piles.

## Engine

Start with **Kitsune, one Council Room, one Shop, and two Highways**. Council Room is the main draw. Silk Merchant adds smaller draw, a Buy, and a Villager when gained. Kitsune’s +2 Actions supplies one extra terminal slot when played normally, and Shop can play the first copy of a name such as Council Room or Kitsune for free. A second Council Room, Silk Merchants, and Poets give the deck enough draw to keep growing. Two Highways make every non-Potion card priced at $5 or below eligible for Poet’s bonus draw, so play the Highways first when you can. Figurine draws in the Buy phase, where the Actions it finds usually have to wait. [Plunder rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/DomPlunder.pdf)

**A favorable build reaches most of the deck around turns 8–10.** This line assumes cooperative shuffles and that you get the Silk Merchant you trashed. Incoming Curses and an opposing Lurker can change the buys.

| Turn | Worked line |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Buy Kitsune and Lurker. |
| 3–4 | With Kitsune, Lurker, and three Coppers, trash a Silk Merchant for a Coffers and a Villager. Choose +$2 and Curse to buy Council Room. On the four-Copper hand, spend the Coffers for Highway. |
| 5–6 | Recover the Silk Merchant with Lurker before playing Council Room. If Council finds Highway and Kitsune, use a Villager to play Highway first, then Kitsune. Four Coppers and Kitsune make $6, enough for Highway and Shop at reduced prices. On the next $3 hand, a second Silk Merchant trash plus the banked Coffers can buy Kitsune. |
| 7–8 | Shop can play Council Room, then a Villager opens the chain of two Highways and Kitsune. Shop, Kitsune, and three Coppers make $6 with two Buys, enough for a third Highway and third Kitsune. On the next strong shuffle, three Highways, three Kitsunes taking +$2, Shop, and one Copper make $8. With Council Room for a Buy and Panic for two more, that buys a $5 Province, a $2 Highway, and a $1 Poet. |

The turn-eight deck in that line can see about 15 cards with its starting hand, three Highways, Shop, Council Room, and Silk Merchant. That can reach the payload before it reliably reaches every card. For a more stable deck, **four Highways, two Poets, two Council Rooms, one Silk Merchant, one Shop, and three Kitsunes** can see up to 24 cards. Two Kitsunes taking +2 Actions, Shop’s free play, and one Villager cover the terminals. All three Kitsunes can still take +$2. That gives **$7 from Actions and four Buys** before Coffers or Treasures. One Copper or Coffers reaches two $4 Provinces. A fifth Highway makes Provinces $3 and Duchies free, so $9 can take three Provinces plus Duchies if you have the Buys.

The main failure is opening with Victory cards, Curses, or Lurkers and missing the first draw or village. A second Council Room or a well-timed Prepare helps more than another payload card when that keeps happening. Once Panic is active, play only the Treasures you need for the purchase or Buys you want. Each play gives +2 Buys, and a Treasure returned at cleanup stops taking up future draws. An unplayed Treasure stays in your deck. [Rising Sun rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/DomRisingSunRules.pdf)

## Key interactions

- **Lurker + Silk Merchant.** Trashing Silk Merchant from the Supply gives you one Coffers and one Villager immediately. Gaining it from the trash gives another of each. Two Lurkers on the same turn can secure the card and both sets of tokens without spending your terminal slot. With one Lurker, an opponent can claim the Merchant between your turns. The first trash can matter as early as turn 3. [Intrigue rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Dominion-Rules-Intrigue-2nd-Edition.pdf) · [Renaissance rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Dominion-Rules-Renaissance.pdf)
- **Lurker + Reckless Transmute.** One Lurker trashes Transmute from the Supply and another gains it from the shared trash, bypassing Potion. One play resolves twice, against two separate cards if available. Two Estates become two Golds, two Curses disappear for a two-point swing, or two Actions become two Duchies. Trashing a Silk Merchant for a Duchy also pays its tokens and puts it back within Lurker’s reach. Trashing a Treasure gains a fresh Transmute. The played copy returns to its pile at cleanup, so a repeat play needs another gain. A first resolution around turns 6–8 is plausible if the trash is available. Under Panic, the Golds are one-use bursts, and it can be right to keep a Copper for economy rather than feed it to Transmute. [Alchemy rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/Dominion-Alchemy-Rules.pdf) · [Plunder rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/DomPlunder.pdf)
- **Prepare + draw and villages.** For $3, bank unplayed Actions for next turn. A prepared Council Room draws four without spending the normal Action, and a prepared Kitsune choosing +2 Actions leaves three Actions before ordinary play. A stalled hand with both can set up a strong next turn. A prepared Figurine can draw Actions before the Action phase, and prepared Lurkers can put gains into the discard before a prepared Council Room draws through a shuffle. Prepare stays $3 even with Highways in play. [Plunder rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/DomPlunder.pdf) · [Event cost rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Dominion-Rules-Adventures.pdf)

**Other cards.** Tools is strongest when you already play an expensive Action worth copying, usually Highway, Kitsune, or Council Room. It can also copy a played Reckless Transmute. With no Durations here, expect to copy your own cards. Before Panic, Tools can repeat that gain across shuffles. Afterward, it trades itself for one gain and two Buys. Figurine is situational draw, strongest when prepared or when an extra $1 and Buy from discarding an unplayed Action matter. Potion is a fallback if Lurkers keep intercepting Transmute, but it takes an early $4 purchase and a separate Buy, then returns after its first play under Panic. [Plunder rules](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/DomPlunder.pdf)

The ratings check supports prioritizing Silk Merchant (+6.8), Kitsune (+4.5), and Highway (+3.3). Three cards gain particular value here. Lurker (−0.4) has two exceptional trash targets, Council Room (−0.9) supplies the strongest draw available during the Action phase, and Transmute (−6.8) gets two resolutions and a route around Potion. Shop (−1.9) also earns an early copy because it can play a terminal while providing $1 that Panic cannot take away. [Repository card ratings](context/card-ratings.md)

**Against their best plan.** Expect the opponent to open Kitsune/Lurker too, contest Highways, and threaten a three-pile finish once Curses empty. The engine holds up because three Highways still turn $7 and two Buys into a Province and a Duchy. If they lean into Transmute, use Lurker to take a key card out of the shared trash and start scoring as soon as you can do so while building. Against the Duchy rush, the best response is to take Provinces and keep enough Highway and +Buy to end the game before the last big conversion. Taking all eight Duchies gives 24 VP, the same as four Provinces before Estates and Curses. A rush needs the Curse edge, some Provinces of its own, or control of the ending.

## Openings and buy priorities

- **$5/$2, Kitsune and Lurker.** This is the preferred split. Kitsune can curse on turn 3 and either make $2 or gain Silver alongside the attack. Take +$2 when it reaches a key $5. Choose Silver and Curse when the Silver improves the next shuffle and the extra $2 would buy little. Lurker can cash in Silk Merchant immediately or take a valuable Action the opponent has already trashed.
- **$4/$3, Silk Merchant and Shop.** Shop can find and play Silk Merchant, and the on-gain Villager covers a turn when they fail to line up. With both played and four Coppers, that’s $5 before the opening Coffers. Silver provides a stronger immediate money card if Panic looks late, while Shop keeps paying after activation.
- **Turns 3–7.** Prioritize the first Kitsune if you lack one, the first Council Room if you need a draw starter, and the first Highway once you can use its discount. With one Highway and two Buys, $6 buys Highway plus Shop. With two Highways, $6 buys Highway plus Kitsune or Council Room. Buy a second Lurker if there is a concrete Silk Merchant, Transmute, or pile-control play for it. Otherwise add draw or claim a contested Highway.
- **Greening.** In a mirror, take the first cheap Province when you can still add a useful piece or threaten the next scoring turn. Against money, there is more room to build toward two Provinces per turn. If the opponent has already punished a slow shuffle, use cheap Duchies and Transmute to keep a viable pile-out instead of waiting for the perfect Highway stack.

## Ending and what could change the pick

Count **Curses, Silk Merchants, Highways, and Duchies** every turn once either player is scoring. A Lurker can empty a Kingdom Action pile without using a Buy. A loaded Reckless Transmute can take two Duchies without using one. For example, with Curses gone, one Silk Merchant left, and two Duchies left, a Lurker plus a Transmute with two Actions to feed it can end the game for six points before the Buy phase. Check the score and turn-count tiebreak before giving the opponent that line or leaving the last Province within their reach.

The strongest challenge to the recommendation is an early Transmute that converts Estates into Golds, followed by a prepared turn that converts spare Actions into Duchies while the Highway deck is still putting itself together. I would switch toward that line if Highway access looks capped at two and the Duchies and a third pile are already dropping. The Highway plan loses if its first two-Province turn arrives after the opponent has a winning pile-out. With three or four players, more opponents compete for the same ten Highways, Kitsune attacks every opponent, and Panic starts at eight or ten Suns. That makes me less willing to count on a four-Highway split and more willing to take points when the shared piles start falling.

**Least sure of the opening tempo.** The turn-eight Province line shows what the engine can do on a favorable shuffle. Its critical test is whether turns 5–7 produce a second Highway and enough usable draw while both players are cursing and contesting the trash. If those turns mainly produce lone terminals or weak hands, move the scoring window later and give the Duchy line more weight. Timing ranges are worked estimates using the supplied tournament setup and ordinary shuffles after the arranged opening.
