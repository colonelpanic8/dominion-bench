## Plan: Courtier + Mandarin replaying Golds (Barge, Cardinal, Courtier, Displace, Lurker, Mandarin, Silk Road, Steward, Tunnel, Village Green; Gamble, Way of the Goat)

**Recommendation:** the Courtier/Mandarin combo. Courtier gains Golds; Mandarin's on-gain effect puts all Treasures you have in play back on top of your deck, so the same Golds are played again next turn ("put all the Golds in your deck and then play them 5 times per turn"). PathfindingLibrary called this "a very strong kingdom for Mandarin", with "$118 with 8 buys on turn 11 uncontested".

**How the combo runs:**
- **Courtier** reveals a card from hand and gives one choice per type. **Village Green** has three types (Action, Duration, Reaction), so revealing it gives three choices: gain a Gold, +$3, +Buy. Playing Village Green itself "does nothing" here: keep it in hand for Courtiers.
- **Mandarin played** puts a card from your hand onto your deck: topdeck Village Green each turn so it's in hand for the Courtiers next turn.
- **Mandarin gained** (bought) puts your played Treasures on your deck: buy one on big turns so next turn starts with those Golds.
- You still need **+Buy**, from Courtier choices, Barge or Gamble, to spend the money.
- **Thin early with Steward.**

**Build** (the source gives no turn-by-turn order; this fills it in): Steward early for trashing, Courtiers as soon as you hit $5, one Village Green, then Mandarin. From then on, Courtiers gain Golds and Mandarin buys keep the Golds cycling; add +Buy as the money grows.

**Milestones:** the combo is slow to assemble and then very strong; the source's example is a turn-11 turn worth $118. Judge it by when the cycle starts (Courtier + Village Green + Mandarin working together), not by early score.

**Avoid:** Displace as the main gainer ("not economical", "much too slow"); Cardinal ("useless").

**The alternative a second commenter preferred** (untested): thin with Steward, play Tunnel and Village Green with Gamble, and Displace Golds into Provinces, with Silk Road as extra VP.
