## Plan: Vineyards on a deck of cheap Actions (Tunnel, Council Room, Jester, Litter, Patrol, Vineyard, Border Guard, Cage, Astrolabe, Market Square; Way of the Squirrel, Seaway)

**Recommendation:** fill the deck with cheap Actions and score with Vineyards, ending the game on three piles (Vineyard, Border Guard, Market Square). This is what both finalists played in the 2026 Online Championship final; the more Action-dense deck took the Vineyard split and won by turn 14.

**Why it works:** Vineyard costs only a Potion and is worth 1 VP per 3 Action cards you own. Border Guard ($2) and Market Square ($3, +Buy) are cheap Actions that keep the deck playable, and with +Buy you gain several a turn. Each Action raises every Vineyard's value, so the VP grows with the deck. Provinces are a side source of points, not the plan.

**Opening and build:**
- Open with Cage to set your Estates aside (both finalists took two Cages and set aside all their Estates). Cage gives them back when you gain your first Victory card.
- Take Jester early as the payload attack: both finalists took two.
- Litter is the key card: the only village, and it draws. Take many ("you still want like all the Litters"). Mind its Debt.
- Buy Seaway on Market Square, so every Market Square gives +Buy.
- Add Border Guards and Market Squares with every spare Buy. Get Potions (two or three) so you can take Vineyards.
- For more draw while greening, Patrol beats Council Room: it pulls Victory cards out of the way and doesn't give the opponent cards.

**Milestones:** Vineyards start arriving around turns 7–9, early: the winner greened earlier than the commentators expected. The winner's 5 Vineyards were each worth 5 VP. Expect the game to end on piles by about turn 14.

**Endgame:** watch the Vineyard, Border Guard and Market Square piles. With +Buys you can empty them yourself. End it when you're ahead after counting each Vineyard's value, and don't leave the opponent a turn that empties the third pile while ahead.

**Avoid:** Council Room as your main draw once you're greening (it feeds the opponent's hand).

**Open questions from the commentary:** whether a second Cage is worth it, and whether Border Guard plus Tunnel digging for Golds is a better payload. Neither finalist bought Tunnel.
