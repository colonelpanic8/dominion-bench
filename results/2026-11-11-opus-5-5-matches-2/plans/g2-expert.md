## Plan: Sentinel/Caravan into a Tools engine with Lost Cities and Innovation (Villa, Band of Misfits, Junk Dealer, Lost City, Vault, Lighthouse, Sentinel, Caravan, Harbor Village, Tools; Innovation, Borrow)

**Recommendation:** an engine. Open Sentinel/Caravan, then build on Tools gaining copies of what you have in play, Lost Cities and Caravans for draw, Villas for +Buy, and Innovation. Both finalists in the 2026 Online Championship final played this; the commentators' main criticism was that both underplayed Innovation and Lost City.

**Key pieces:**
- **Sentinel** is the trasher; **Caravan** makes sure you see it. Open Sentinel/Caravan on 4/3. On 5/2 an alternative is Borrow for a Band of Misfits played as a second Sentinel.
- **Tools** is the payload and the gainer: it gains a copy of a card you have in play, so it gains Caravans, Lost Cities, Villas and Golds. "Totally want Tools."
- **Draw** comes from Caravan and Lost City. Take Lost Cities aggressively, several in a turn, even though each gives the opponent a card. "Lost City should have piled earlier."
- **Villa** supplies +Buy and returns you to the Action phase when gained; you need a few.
- **Innovation ($6)** is worth it here: with Villa taking you back to the Action phase, a Caravan or Lost City gained in the Buy phase can be played the same turn. The commentators said it would have won the game; one wanted it before a first Gold.
- **Vault:** one or two at most. "Villa Vault is like Market."

**Milestones:** a well-built engine has its first multi-Province turn around turns 8–11. In the final, one player was $2 short of a triple-Province turn late on, which Innovation would have fixed. The game tied.

**Greening:** start when the deck reliably produces two or more Provinces a turn, not before. Use Tools on Gold or Villa for the money and +Buy you need.

**Avoid:** Band of Misfits after the opening; many Vaults instead of Lost Cities (both finalists took three); Junk Dealer as the trasher instead of Sentinel.

**Open questions from the commentary:** how big Innovation's edge is (one said you can win without it), and whether a turn-5 Gold is premature.
