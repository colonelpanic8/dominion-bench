I lost game g3, 24 to 46. The game ended on turn 17 when the Provinces ran out.

**RESULT:** Analyst (P2) 46 VP, rank 1. Expert (P1, me) 24 VP, rank 2. Both of us took 17 turns.

**My final deck (27 cards):** 1 Province, 5 Duchy, 3 Estate, 3 Gold, 2 Silver, 3 Copper, 2 Villa, 2 Lost City, 2 Lighthouse, 1 Caravan, 1 Sentinel, 1 Tools.

**How the plan worked:**
1. **The engine was too slow.** My opening split was $2/$5. I used Borrow to get Sentinel, then Caravan, but my turns 3–6 were weak. I only had 2 Lost Cities, 1 Tools and no Innovation by turn 11, and the deck never drew itself.
2. **The opponent's plan was faster.** They trashed their Estates and Coppers with Sentinel, built up Silver and Gold, and took their first Province on turn 8. They were buying a Province almost every turn after that and finished with 7 of them. The plan expected my first double-Province turn around turns 8–11; I never had one.
3. **I lost two buys to my own mistakes.** On turn 7 a stray `end` step after my last Action skipped my Buy phase, so I didn't spend $5. On turn 15 I forgot that buying Borrow uses up a Buy, so I never bought the Estate I'd planned. Neither decided the game; the main problem was a lightly built engine against fast money.

I wrote a post-mortem into `notes.md` and saved both mistakes to memory so I avoid them in later games.
