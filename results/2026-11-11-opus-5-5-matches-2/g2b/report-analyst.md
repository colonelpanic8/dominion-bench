**RESULT (the Provinces ran out): 1. Analyst 45 VP (18 turns), 2. Expert 22 VP (17 turns).** I won.

**Final deck (20 cards):** 7 Province, 1 Duchy, 2 Gold, 4 Silver, 2 Copper, 2 Lost City, 1 Lighthouse, 1 Sentinel.

**How the plan worked:**
- **The trimmed-money plan held up.** I opened with a 2/5 split and used Borrow to get Sentinel on turn 1 and Lost City on turn 2. Sentinel trashed 5 Coppers and 3 Estates. My first Province came on turn 8, and I had 4 Provinces by turn 13, as the plan's solo line predicted. The opponent built a Villa/Tools/Caravan deck and didn't green until turn 13, so I led 30–7 by turn 14.
- **Sentinel's stacking was the most valuable effect.** It set up the Province turns. At the end it stacked three Silvers and a Copper on top, so I could take a Duchy with Borrow and still have exactly $8 (with Borrow again) for the last Province the next turn.
- **One mistake, now in notes.md:** Borrow's +1 Buy only pays for Borrow itself, as the plan's assumptions said, so it never gives a second purchase. I learned this on turn 11 when Province + Gold bought only the Province, and it cost me a card from the next hand. From then on I used Borrow only for its +$1 when that reached a price.
