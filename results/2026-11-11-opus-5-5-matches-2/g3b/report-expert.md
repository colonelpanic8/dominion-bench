I lost, 30 VP to 38. The game ended on the Provinces running out.

**RESULT:** Analyst (P2) won 38–30 after 15 turns each.

**My final deck (30 cards):** 7 Copper, 2 Silver (one of them Exiled by the opponent's Cardinal), 5 Gold, 3 Courtier, 2 Village Green, 1 Mandarin, 1 Steward, 1 Lurker, 4 Province, 2 Tunnel, 2 Estate.

**How the plan worked:**
1. The Courtier/Mandarin cycle never got going. I only had the pieces (3 Courtiers, 2 Village Greens, Mandarin) by about turn 10, and Mandarin was never played the same turn as a Courtier with Village Green in hand. In practice the deck was a money deck where Courtier gained a Gold most turns. Courtier revealing Village Green paid off only once, on turn 14 (+Action, +$3, +Buy).
2. Two side tricks worked well. Lurker trashed a Courtier from the Supply and later gained it back, which was a free $5 card. Gamble gave extra Buys and plays. Even so, the opponent's plain Silver/Gold deck with Barge took its first Province on turn 8 and stayed ahead the whole game.
3. In the endgame I took the second-to-last Province to tie at 28. I judged the opponent's chance of hitting $8 next turn at roughly 15–20%, but I underrated the Barge they had in play: it gave them 8 cards and an extra Buy. On turn 15 they had $11 and bought the last Province plus a Tunnel, the turn before my $9 hand could have taken it.
