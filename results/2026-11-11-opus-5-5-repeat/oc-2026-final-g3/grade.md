## Beyond the key
- Scrounge's Estate mode makes Duchy + Estate for $3 (4 VP), limited by Estates in the trash. Plausible. Duchy costs $5, which is within "up to $5". The key notes the commentators thought it too slow as a comeback.
- Overlord can't be gained by Scrounge, Horn or Upgrade. Plausible. A $0+8 Debt cost isn't "up to $X" or "exactly $X+1" in coins.
- Upgrade on a Copper gains nothing. Plausible. No card on this board costs exactly $1.
- Horn trashes itself and then counts as a different Treasure in the trash for Forager. Plausible. Forager counts differently named Treasures in the trash, and Horn is a Treasure.
- Re-buying a Horn with Scrounge each turn makes "roughly one extra Province per turn". Doubtful. Scrounge gains a fresh Horn to the discard pile, which needs to be drawn next turn, and it costs an Estate cycle and a Buy each time.
- Spoils and Estates gained from the trash don't drive a three-pile ending. Plausible. Spoils is non-Supply, and an Estate gained from the trash doesn't deplete the Estate pile.
- Patrol clashes with Estates that Scrounge brings back. Plausible. Patrol puts revealed Victory cards into hand.
- Council Room: 2 copies are enough because it feeds the opponent. Doubtful. The opponent's extra card matters little against a megaturn, and agoo03 had 3.
- Golem should be skipped (Potion cost). Plausible, and the grader note credits it.

## Summary
The analysis sees Horn of Plenty and differently named cards as the scoring engine, but turns it into a one-Horn-per-turn engine with Scrounge Duchies. It misses the consensus plan: stack six or seven Horns for one Bridge-like megaturn. It also opens Forager, the trap that cost yurikamome the game, and over-values Bandit Camp against the key's advice.
