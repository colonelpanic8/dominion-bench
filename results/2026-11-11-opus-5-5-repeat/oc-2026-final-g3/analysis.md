Kingdom: Council Room, Horn of Plenty, Inn, Patrol, Upgrade, Overlord, Forager, Walled Village, Golem, Bandit Camp. Event: Scrounge. No Colonies, no Shelters, 2 players.

**Inventory:** The prices bunch up at $5: Council Room, Horn of Plenty, Inn, Patrol, Upgrade and Bandit Camp all cost $5. Walled Village costs $4, Forager $3 and Scrounge $3. Overlord costs 8 Debt and Golem needs a Potion. There is no card at $2. Bandit Camp brings Spoils, a non-Supply card that makes $3 once and goes back to its pile. The starting deck is the normal 7 Copper and 3 Estates, so turns 1–2 split 4/3 or 5/2.

**Board:** This is an engine board with no attacks. Bandit Camp, Walled Village and Inn are the villages. Council Room (+4 Cards, +1 Buy) is the real draw, and Patrol backs it up. Forager, Upgrade and Scrounge's trash option are the trashers. The board turns on Scrounge: once your trashed Estates are in the trash, each Scrounge is "$3 + 1 Buy: gain a card costing up to $5", plus a returned Estate you can trash again. Horn of Plenty becomes a free Province each turn once 8 or more different cards are in play. That is easy here because the engine pieces all have different names. Nothing attacks, so nothing stops either player from building.

**Key interactions:**
- **Forager/Upgrade + Scrounge:** Trash an Estate, then pay $3 with Scrounge to get that Estate back plus a $5 card. That works out to a $5 for $3. Next shuffle, Upgrade turns the returned Estate into a Forager or Silver ($3), or Forager trashes it again. This runs from turn 3 or 4. It is the cheapest way to pick up Bandit Camps, Council Rooms and Horns.
- **Scrounge for Duchies (endgame):** $3 and 1 Buy gets you Duchy + Estate, which is 4 VP. So $6 with 2 Buys is 8 VP, more than a Province. The only limit is how many Estates are in the trash, and both players draw from the same trash. There are only 6 starting Estates, so the contest is real. Trash yours early, and know that it feeds the opponent too.
- **Horn of Plenty + variety:** A typical built turn has Bandit Camp, Walled Village, Council Room, Forager, Upgrade, Spoils, Silver, Copper and Horn in play. That is 9–10 different names, so Horn gains a Province and trashes itself. Scrounge then gets Horn back for $3 (plus an Estate). That is roughly one extra Province per turn for $3 and a Buy, from about turn 11.
- **Forager + its own trashing:** Forager pays $1 per different Treasure in the trash. Copper goes in first, then Horn (after it gains a Victory card) and any Spoils you trash on purpose. That makes Forager a non-terminal $2–3 card with +Buy late in the game.
- **Upgrade's price ladder:** Estate becomes Forager or Silver. Walled Village becomes a $5 card. Any $5 becomes Gold. A Copper gains nothing, because nothing costs exactly $1, so Upgrade works as a clean Copper trasher.

**Other cards:**
- **Bandit Camp:** the best village here. Its Spoils are the engine's money. Scrounge gets it cheaply.
- **Walled Village:** puts itself back on top of your deck when at most one other Action is in play. Early on that pairs it with Forager or Upgrade every turn. Later it is a filler village.
- **Inn:** a village that sifts. Its on-gain shuffles the Actions from your discard pile back into your deck, which is nice when you gain it mid-shuffle with Scrounge or Upgrade. Third choice of village.
- **Council Room:** the main draw and a +Buy source. It gives the opponent a card each time, so 2 copies are enough.
- **Patrol:** backup terminal draw. It pulls Victory cards into your hand, which clashes with Estates that Scrounge brings back. Get it only after Council Room.
- **Overlord:** can play any $5 here (an extra Bandit Camp or Council Room, or an Upgrade). But 8 Debt costs a whole turn of buying, and Scrounge, Horn and Upgrade can't gain it. At most one, late.
- **Golem:** in theory it plays two Actions in a dense engine, but it needs a Potion. Skip it. Rated −3.8, and that fits this board.

**Ratings check:** Horn of Plenty (3.15), Upgrade (3.08) and Forager (2.68) match their central roles. Scrounge rates −0.98, but on this board I value it much higher. That's because Forager, Upgrade and Council Room supply what it needs: Estates in the trash and spare Buys. Council Room (−0.94) and Walled Village (−1.26) are mid-to-weak cards that are needed pieces here.

### Build lines
- **Engine (Forager/Upgrade/Scrounge with Bandit Camp, Council Room and Horn):**
  - T1–2: Silver / Forager.
  - T3: Forager trashes an Estate, then Scrounge for $3 brings back Estate + Bandit Camp.
  - T4: $5 buys Upgrade.
  - T5–8: Forager and Upgrade trash Coppers and Estates, and Upgrade turns Estates into a second Forager. Add Council Room, a second Bandit Camp, Horn and a third trasher, using Scrounge whenever you have exactly $3–4.
  - T9–10: the deck draws most of itself. It has about 3 villages, Council Room, Patrol, 2–3 Foragers, Upgrade, Horn, Silver and 2–3 Coppers.
  - Built turn: 2–3 Spoils ($6–9), Foragers ($2–4), Silver/Copper ($3) and 3–4 Buys gives about $12–15. Horn gains a Province. Buy Province + Scrounge (Duchy + Estate) + Scrounge to get Horn back. That's about 13–16 VP per turn from turn 11 or 12.
- **Council Room–Big Money:**
  - T1–2: Silver / Silver, Council Room on the first $5.
  - First Gold around T5–6, first Province around T7–9, 4 Provinces around T14–15.
  - Peak is 1 Province per turn, sometimes Province + Duchy. Every Council Room play gives the engine player a card.

| Plan | Starts scoring | Can end by | Peak VP/turn | VP ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scrounge/Horn engine | ~T10–11 | ~T15–16 | Province + Horn Province, or Province + 2 Scrounge Duchies (~12–16) | Provinces + Duchies + recycled Estates |
| Council Room–BM | ~T7–8 | ~T19–21 | Province (+Duchy) | about half the Provinces before the engine takes off |
| Scrounge-Duchy "good stuff" (no real draw) | ~T8 | long game | 2× Duchy + Estate | Duchies; limited by Estates in the trash |

**Plans considered:**
- **Engine (described above):** needs Forager on turn 1–2, 2–3 Bandit Camps, 1–2 Council Rooms and Horn by about turn 9. Its weakness is that Council Room is the only big draw, so a hand with no Council Room stalls on +1-card villages.
- **Council Room–BM:** fast early but capped at about 1 Province per turn, and it helps the opponent draw.
- **Scrounge slog (Foragers + Scrounge Duchies, little draw):** steady 2×4 VP turns. It runs out once the trash has no Estates and the 8 Duchies are gone.

**Against their best plan:** If the opponent plays Council Room–BM, they lead around T8–11 with maybe 3 Provinces. From T11 the engine scores 12+ VP per turn, while Horn and Scrounge Duchies take VP without depending on the Province pile. The engine also gets a card from every Council Room they play. Engine favoured.

In the mirror, the Estates in the trash and the 8 Duchies are what you fight over. Whoever starts using Scrounge for Duchies first gains a real edge. In the mirror, start greening a turn earlier than you would against money.

**Challenged:**
- **Overlord-heavy line:** I gave it a second look, with Overlords as extra Bandit Camps and Council Rooms. It doesn't beat Scrounge, which gets real $5s for $3 with no Debt turn. Rejected.
- **How the pick loses:** The engine loses if you overload it with terminal draw (Patrol plus Council Room with no village in hand), or if you hold back on trashing Estates and let the opponent's Scrounges run on Estates you supplied. It also loses if you go Big Money and leave the opponent unchallenged on the Duchy pile.

**Recommendation:** Build the engine around Forager, Upgrade, Scrounge, Bandit Camp, Council Room and Horn of Plenty. Use Scrounge as a cheap $5 gainer while building and as a Duchy machine at the end. It beats Big Money on ceiling and on scoring that doesn't depend on Provinces, and no attack slows it down.

**Open:**
- **4/3:** Silver / Forager. Alternatives are Walled Village / Forager, or Forager / Scrounge (trash an Estate) if you want Estates in the trash at once.
- **5/2:** Upgrade / nothing. Nothing is worth buying at $2. Bandit Camp is an acceptable alternative.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffles 1–2:** Forager, Upgrade, then Bandit Camp (bought, or gained with Scrounge at $3–4). Trash Estates before Coppers.
- **Shuffle 3:** Council Room, a second Bandit Camp, Horn of Plenty and a second Forager. Upgrade the Estates you got back into Foragers.
- **Greening:** once a turn reliably makes about $11 with 3 Buys (around turn 10–11). Each turn take Horn's Province, a Province, and Scrounge Duchies. Keep getting Horn back with Scrounge.

**Ending:** Most likely the Provinces run out, helped by Horn. The Duchy pile will probably empty as well. Spoils and Estates gained from the trash don't count toward a three-pile ending, so that is unlikely. Track how many Estates are in the trash, since that is your Scrounge-Duchy supply.

**Watch out for:**
- Every Estate you trash also feeds the opponent's Scrounge.
- Council Room's extra card helps an opponent who draws.
- Patrol plus Estates brought back by Scrounge clogs your hand.

**Least sure of:** whether the draw (one strong terminal plus +1-card villages) is enough to play the whole deck by turn 10. If test draws often stall without Council Room, add a second Council Room and Patrol earlier, or lean more on Scrounge Duchies (the slog line).

**Assumed:**
- Scrounge's second option needs an Estate already in the trash, and either player's trashed Estates count. Its "up to $5" excludes Debt and Potion costs.
- Horn counts different card names in play, Treasures included, and becomes a Treasure in the trash for Forager after it trashes itself.
- Upgrade on a Copper gains nothing, because no Supply card costs exactly $1.
