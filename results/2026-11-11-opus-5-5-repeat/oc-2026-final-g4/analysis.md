# Kingdom analysis: oc-2026-final-g4 (2 players, no Colonies, no Shelters)

**Board:** There is no real trashing here. Ritual swaps a card for a Curse, so it doesn't thin. Without trashing, the board comes down to whether **Aristocrat** can be stacked into a whole engine. With 4 of them in play you get +3 Actions, +3 Cards, +$3 and +3 Buys. **First Mate** and **Laboratory** help get them into play together. **Flag Bearer's** Flag gives the holder a 6-card hand every turn, so it's worth fighting over in any plan. Two things are still open: whether an engine with no trashing beats Flag-plus-money, and how fast you can gather 4–5 Aristocrats when only Jewelled Egg gives an early +Buy.

**Inventory:**
- **Kingdom piles:**
  - $2: Jewelled Egg
  - $3: Aristocrat, Ghost Town (Night, Duration)
  - $4: Alley (Shadow), Flag Bearer
  - $5: Corsair, First Mate, Jester, Laboratory, Tactician
- **Landscapes:** Crop Rotation (Project, $6) and Ritual (Event, $4).
- **Outside the Supply:** the Loot pile (via Egg), the Flag, and Curses (10).
- **Price points:** $5 is crowded. $2 holds only Egg. Nothing costs more than $5 apart from Crop Rotation.
- **Opening splits:** the starting deck is the usual 7 Copper and 3 Estate, so the splits are 4/3 or 5/2.

**Key interactions:**
- **Aristocrat stack (it works without help).** From 1 Action, Aristocrats 1 to 4 give +3 Actions (2 left), then +3 Cards (1 left), then +$3 (0 left), then +3 Buys. A 5th copy gives +3 Actions again, and an 8-copy stack gives +6 Actions, +6 Cards, +$6 and +6 Buys. The catch is that the copies must be in play in the same turn. You need about 4–5 of the 10 (if both players want them, expect a 5/5 split).
- **First Mate + Aristocrat (and + Lab).**
  - First Mate plays every Aristocrat in hand for one Action. The first one refunds it with +3 Actions. Then First Mate draws until you have 6 cards in hand.
  - Play First Mate after Labs and Aristocrats have emptied your hand, and that redraw is +4 to +6 cards. Any Aristocrats it finds can then be played one at a time with the leftover Actions.
  - With 2+ Labs in hand, First Mate also plays all of them at once.
- **Flag Bearer → Flag.** It's a terminal +$2, but the Flag adds +1 card to every hand. In a deck that can't thin, that's the best draw on the board. The opponent takes the Flag back by gaining a Flag Bearer, and +Buy makes retaking it cheap.
- **Crop Rotation in a deck with no trashing.**
  - Your 3 Estates never leave, so about 60% of hands from a 20-card deck hold a Victory card. That makes it roughly a free +2 cards most turns from about turn 9.
  - It gets better once you start buying Victory cards, which slows the decay from greening.
- **Ritual as late VP.**
  - Ritual trashes from your hand, so the card must stay unplayed. Trashing a spare $5 Action nets +4 VP (+5, −1 for the Curse) for $4 and a Buy. A Gold nets +5.
  - Trashing a Copper nets −1, and an Estate nets only +1 while swapping one junk card for another. Don't use it early.
  - It also drains the Curse pile, which matters for a three-pile ending.
- **Jester + no trashing.** Curses can never be removed here.
  - A Victory card flipped from the top of their deck gives them a Curse (about 25% early).
  - A flipped Copper lets you give them another Copper.
  - Against money, a flipped Gold or Silver gets you a copy. Against an engine, a flipped Aristocrat steals into the contested pile.

**Other cards:**
- **Ghost Town:** It's gained to your hand, so it works the turn you buy it. In effect it's a cantrip that also gives +1 Action at the start of next turn. It's a solid $3 filler, competing with Aristocrat for $3 buys.
- **Alley:** As a Shadow it can be played from your deck, so with Actions left over it's a guaranteed sifter. That makes it a decent $4 filler in the engine. I'm not counting on First Mate being able to play Alleys from the deck.
- **Jewelled Egg:** It's the only early +Buy, which lets you take 2 Aristocrats on a $6 turn. Ritual on an unplayed Egg gives a Loot plus a net +1 VP, which is only a minor late play.
- **Corsair:** It only trashes Silver and Gold. An engine running on Copper, Aristocrats and Loot barely notices it. It's good only against a pure money opponent.
- **Tactician:** It discards Treasures you haven't played yet, there's nothing that rewards discarding, and it clashes with First Mate's draw-up-to-6. Skip it.
- **Ritual (early):** Skip it until the endgame (see above).

**Ratings check (Glicko):**
- **Aristocrat (−1.93) and First Mate (−0.50):** they play above their ratings here, because each supplies what the other needs.
- **Jester (−1.81):** better than its rating, because no one can trash the Curses.
- **Crop Rotation (0.47):** better than its rating, because Estates can't be trashed away.
- **Corsair (−2.75):** I agree it's weak here, but only because engines carry few Silvers and Golds.
- **Ritual (−4.48) and Tactician (−1.88):** I agree. Ritual is only a finisher.
- **Lab (1.55) and Ghost Town (1.96):** I agree they're solid.

### Build lines

**Engine** (Flag + Aristocrat, First Mate, Lab, with Jester)
- **T1–2:** Flag Bearer and Egg. The Flag gives 6-card hands, so the two hands of turns 3–4 cover the whole 12-card deck.
- **T3–4:** Jester and Lab.
- **T5:** $6 with Egg buys 2 Aristocrats.
- **T6:** First Mate.
- **T7:** $7 with Egg buys 2 more Aristocrats.
- **T8:** Aristocrat plus Egg.
- **T9:** Lab, or Crop Rotation.
- **T10–11:** the first big turns.
- **A typical built turn around T12** (deck of about 25):
  - Start with 6–7 cards. Play Labs, then Aristocrats 1 and 2, then First Mate's redraw. That shows about 17 of the 25 cards and about 3.5 Aristocrats.
  - That comes to roughly $11–13 with 2–4 Buys: Province plus Duchy or Ritual (about 9–10 VP).
  - Top end: with 5+ Aristocrats and Crop Rotation, $16+ and Province ×2.

**Flag–Jester–Lab money**
- **T1–2:** Flag Bearer and Silver.
- **T3–4:** Jester and Lab or Silver.
- **T5–6:** Gold or Lab.
- First $8 around T7–8. 4 Provinces around T13–14. One Province per turn at most, plus Duchies.

**Corsair-BM**
- **T1–2:** Corsair/Egg or Silver/Silver.
- Provinces from about T8–9, 4 by about T15. Corsair is close to blank against the engine.

| Plan | Starts scoring | Can end by | Peak VP/turn | VP ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Aristocrat/First Mate engine | ~T10–11 | ~T17 (Provinces, or a three-pile) | Province + Duchy/Ritual, ~9–12 | Provinces + Duchies + Ritual (limited by the Curses) |
| Flag–Jester–Lab money | ~T7–8 | ~T18–20 | ~6 | about half the Provinces before the engine takes over |
| Corsair-BM | ~T8–9 | ~T20 | ~6 | same as above |

**Plans considered:**
1. **Aristocrat/First Mate/Lab engine with Flag and Jester.** Covered by the line above. It needs 4–5 Aristocrats, 1 First Mate (add a second later if you can), 2 Labs, Eggs and the Flag. Its weakness is consistency: with no trashing, it misses the stack on about 1 turn in 3.
2. **Flag–Jester money.** It's fast and simple, and its Jesters steal Golds in the mirror. The weakness is a hard cap of one Province per turn and no answer to the engine's +Buy turns.
3. **Corsair-BM.** It's the strongest X against a money opponent, and nearly useless against the recommended engine.

**Against their best plan:**
- **The pick against Flag–Jester money:**
  - Money leads about 12–18 VP by T11.
  - From there the engine outscores it by about 4–6 VP a turn and catches up around T14–15. Duchies and Ritual decide the endgame.
  - Their Jester junks you permanently. Your Jester steals their Gold and Silver.
  - I give the engine a slight edge, larger if you hold the Flag.
- **The pick against an engine mirror:** the game turns on the Aristocrat split and Flag control.
  - Keep pace on Aristocrats. 5 copies is enough, since the 5th gives +3 Actions again.
  - Use Jester to steal Aristocrats and Curse their Estates.
  - Green a little earlier than usual.
- **The runner-up (money) against the engine:** it wins only if it opens with a big Province lead or the engine gets choked by Curses.

**Challenged:**
- **Re-testing money.** Its best case is that the Flag stays with it and Jester lands early Curses. That makes money roughly even, which is why the engine's edge is only slight.
- **How the pick loses:**
  - The opponent takes 6+ Aristocrats.
  - The Flag keeps changing hands, so you keep spending Buys to retake it.
  - The engine greens too early without Crop Rotation, and the deck chokes.
- **What the opponent can see.** Opening Flag Bearer/Egg shows the plan. If they rush the Aristocrats, switch to money.

**Recommendation:** Build the Aristocrat/First Mate/Lab engine around the Flag, with 1–2 Jesters as the attack. It scores later than money but at about one and a half to two Provinces' worth per turn. It has the +Buys for Duchies, Ritual VP and a three-pile threat, and Corsair can't touch it. Against Jester-money it holds up as long as you hold the Flag and at least 5 Aristocrats.

**Open:**
- **4/3:** Flag Bearer / Jewelled Egg. Egg's Buy is what lets you double up on Aristocrats later; Flag Bearer/Silver is the money alternative.
- **5/2:** Jester / Jewelled Egg. Laboratory / Egg is close.

**Buy priorities:**
1. **Shuffle 2:** Lab and Jester at $5. Retake the Flag if they took it. A second Egg on spare $2.
2. **Shuffles 3–4:**
   - Aristocrats up to 5+ (2 at a time with Eggs).
   - 1 First Mate.
   - A second Lab.
   - Crop Rotation on a $6 turn once the stack is forming.
   - Ghost Town and Alley as filler.
   - Avoid Gold unless nothing else fits.
3. **Green from about T10–11,** on turns of $13+ with 2+ Buys: Province plus Duchy, or Ritual on a spare $5 Action or a Gold.

**Ending:** Most likely the Provinces run out. A three-pile is possible: Aristocrats (contested), Curses (from Jester and Ritual) and Eggs or Ghost Towns. Engine turns with +3 Buys can force that ending when you're ahead, so count the piles from about T14.

**Watch out for:**
- **The opponent contests Aristocrats or the Flag early.** Stop at 5 Aristocrats and lean on Labs and First Mate. If they get 6+, switch to Flag–Jester money.
- **Being cursed.** Every Curse stays. Play Jester at least as often as they do.
- **More players:** Aristocrats get scarcer and Jester and Corsair get stronger, which pushes toward money.

**Least sure of:** whether this engine, with no trashing, really beats Flag–Jester money, or just ties it. The test is the T10–12 turns. If you average under $11 with 2 Buys, the engine is too slow, so green earlier and play it as a money deck with buys.

**Assumed:**
- First Mate can play Aristocrats drawn during its own resolution (for example by the second Aristocrat's +3 Cards). If it can't, the engine is a bit weaker.
- First Mate can't play Alleys from the deck.
- A Shadow can be played from anywhere in the deck.
- Corsair's victim still gets the $ from the trashed Treasure.
- Ritual can't trash Treasures you've already played.
- Loot is random, so the Egg-to-Loot route is valued as roughly $3 plus a Buy.
