## Beyond the key
- Starting splits of 6/3 at 48%, 5/4 at 40% and 7/2 at 12% with Cursed Gold played: plausible. The hypergeometric math over 6 Coppers + Cursed Gold + 3 Estates gives 60/126, 51/126 and 15/126.
- Butcher can't gain City Quarter: plausible. A card with a Debt cost doesn't cost "up to $X".
- Coffers can pay off City Quarter's debt, and with +Buy you can buy City Quarter, pay it off and keep buying: plausible. Coffers become $ in the Buy phase, and debt blocks buying only until it is repaid.
- Ritual on a $5 card or a Gold scores +5 or +6 VP for one Curse, and stops once the Curses run out: plausible. Ritual gains a Curse and scores only "if you do".
- Tower cancels Curses once the Curse pile empties: plausible. Curse isn't a Victory card, and it comes from a Supply pile.
- Night Watchman's discards trigger Tunnel and Faithful Hound: plausible. Discarding from the deck during Night is "other than during Clean-up".
- Nomad Camp bought on T1 is played on T2: plausible. It is gained onto the deck, on top of the 5 cards left for T2.
- Fool's Gold's Reaction turns the opponent's Province into a Gold, which Butcher then turns into a Province: plausible. Butcher gains a card costing up to $8 by trashing a $6 card and spending 2 Coffers.
- Emptying the Fool's Gold or Hound pile with +Buy for Tower VP and a three-pile ending: plausible. Both are 10-card, $2, non-Victory piles.
- The engine benefits from the free Silvers that the opponent's Embassies hand out: doubtful. Silvers are stop cards for City Quarter, which the analysis itself lists as a risk.

## Summary
The analysis gets the shell right: a City Quarter engine around Butcher, a Butcher/Night Watchman opening and Hounds. It also spots the Tunnel/Butcher Gold-to-Province chain. But it sidelines Night Watchman instead of using it to topdeck City Quarter, builds its payload from Merchant Guild, Fool's Gold and Ritual instead of Tunnel Golds, and adds the Fool's Gold the key warns against.
