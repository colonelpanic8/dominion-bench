I used the kingdom-analysis skill and did the full version (steps 1–8). The build lines below are rough averages worked on paper, not simulations.

**Setup:** 2 players, no Colonies, no Shelters. **Starting deck:** 6 Copper, Cursed Gold, 3 Estates. Played, that's $9 per shuffle, but each Cursed Gold play gains you a Curse. Turn 1–2 splits, if you play Cursed Gold: **6/3 (48%)**, **5/4 (40%)**, **7/2 (12%)**. If you don't play it, its hand loses $3.

**Edition note:** Embassy and Nomad Camp are first-edition only (removed from Hinterlands 2nd ed.).

**Price points:** $2 Hound, Fool's Gold · $3 Night Watchman, Tunnel · $4 Nomad Camp, Ritual · $5 Butcher, Embassy, Merchant Guild, Pooka. City Quarter costs 8 Debt, so you can buy it with almost any hand. Nothing in the kingdom costs $6 or more.

---

**Board:** The only village is City Quarter, and it draws only as well as your hand is full of Actions. The draw is Hound, Pooka, Embassy and City Quarter itself. Payload is Coffers (Butcher, Merchant Guild), +Buy (Merchant Guild, Nomad Camp), Fool's Gold stacks, and two unusual scoring sources:
- **Ritual** turns a card in hand into VP equal to its cost, but only while Curses are left.
- **Tower** gives 1 VP per non-Victory card you own from an empty pile, and that includes Curses.

Both players drain the Curse pile with Cursed Gold and Ritual, so a three-pile ending is a real possibility. The open questions:
- Can City Quarter's debt be paid fast enough?
- Who gets the 10 Curses for Ritual?
- Does the Curse pile empty, which makes every Curse worth 0 net?

**Key interactions:**
- **Butcher + Gold, Curses and Cursed Gold:**
  - Butcher gives +2 Coffers. Trashing a Gold and spending those 2 Coffers gains a Province, so every Gold becomes a free Province from about turn 10.
  - Early, trashing Cursed Gold ($4) plus 1 Coffer gains a $5 card, and keeps 1 Coffer.
  - Trashing a Curse plus 2 Coffers turns junk into Fool's Gold or a Hound.
- **Coffers + City Quarter's debt:** Butcher (+2) and Merchant Guild (+1 per card gained in the Buy phase) pay off the 8 Debt. Buy City Quarter on any weak turn and clear it with Coffers. With +Buy, buy City Quarter first, pay it off, then keep buying.
- **City Quarter + an Action-dense deck:** With 3 other Actions in hand, City Quarter gives +2 Actions and +3 Cards. Cheap Actions (Hound at $2, Nomad Camp, Merchant Guild) keep the density up. Pooka drawing 4 for each Copper trashed both draws and thins the deck.
- **Ritual + Tower (the endgame payload):**
  - Ritual on a $5 Action = +5 VP −1 Curse. Ritual on a Gold = +6 −1.
  - If the Curse pile empties, Tower cancels the −1, but Ritual stops working at that point.
  - At $4 per 4–5 VP it beats a Duchy, and it doesn't add a green card to your deck.
  - Ritual on a late Cursed Gold = +4 −1, and removes the card.
- **Discarding Hounds and Tunnels:**
  - Embassy's "discard 3" and Night Watchman's discards from your deck count as "other than during Clean-up".
  - Each Tunnel discarded that way gains a Gold, which Butcher can turn into a Province.
  - Each Hound discarded that way goes back into your hand at end of turn, so next turn starts bigger.
- **Tower + cheap piles:** An engine with 3+ Buys can empty Fool's Gold or Hound (10 cards at $2). Each card you own from that pile is then +1 VP, and the pile counts toward a three-pile ending with Curses.

**Other cards:**
- **Fool's Gold:** best partner is draw (every copy after the first makes $4). Its Reaction gives you a Gold on your deck when the opponent buys a Province, and Butcher can then turn that Gold into a Province.
- **Nomad Camp (1st ed.):** it is gained onto your deck, so if you buy it on turn 1 you play it on turn 2. An Action with +Buy, which keeps City Quarter's hands Action-dense.
- **Night Watchman:** gained to hand and played the same turn, so it fixes your next hand, and it pairs with Tunnel and Hound. A minor card.
- **Embassy (1st ed.):** every one you gain gives the opponent a Silver. Its partners are Tunnel and Hound. Best in money.
- **Pooka:** best with City Quarter. It can't trash Cursed Gold; Butcher or Ritual handle that.

**Ratings check:**
- Butcher (3.71) and City Quarter (3.66) are rated top tier, which matches my view.
- Ritual is rated −4.48, but it is much better here: Tower can cancel its Curse, and two other cards create Gold to trash.
- Hound (−3.0) and Night Watchman (−3.0) rate low but gain value from the discard interactions. I still treat them as support cards.
- Embassy and Nomad Camp are deprecated, with a large uncertainty, so their ratings carry little weight.

**Plans considered:**
1. **City Quarter engine:** Butcher, Pooka, Hounds, Merchant Guild or Nomad Camp, 2–3 City Quarters, then Fool's Gold. It hits $6 around turn 4–5. It draws its deck from about turn 9–10, then has turns of $15+ with 3 Buys: Province plus Ritual, or a Butcher Gold→Province. It needs at least 2 City Quarters (uncontested, but the debt adds up) and at least 4 Actions per 5 cards. The weakness is a stalled turn when City Quarter turns up with Treasures in hand.
2. **Embassy-money with Tunnels and a Hound:** first $6 on turn 3–4 and $8 on about turn 5–6. About 4 Provinces by turn 14–15. Discarded Tunnels become Golds. The weaknesses are no +Buy, a game-length clock it doesn't control, and the Silvers it hands the opponent.
3. **Pooka or Butcher money:** similar speed, a little slower than Embassy. Butcher-money does gain Coffers and turn Golds into Provinces.

### Build lines
- **City Quarter engine (6/3):**
  - T1 Butcher.
  - T2 Night Watchman, played that night to clear Estates off the top of your deck.
  - T3 Butcher: trash an Estate, spend 2 Coffers, gain Nomad Camp onto your deck. Play Cursed Gold and buy Pooka with $5.
  - T4 Nomad Camp, $5: buy City Quarter, pay 5, owe 3.
  - T5 Pay off the 3, then buy Hound with the remainder.
  - T6–8 Add a second City Quarter, Merchant Guild, another Hound and 1–2 Fool's Gold. Butcher trashes Cursed Gold or a Curse; Pooka trashes Coppers.
  - By T9 the deck is about 16 cards with 3–4 Coppers.
  - Built turn (about T11): City Quarter, City Quarter, Hounds and Pooka draw the deck. Nomad Camp $2, Merchant Guild $1, Fool's Gold $1+4, Coppers about $4 = **$16–18 with 3 Buys**. That buys Province + Ritual (about +10 VP), or Province + Duchy, plus 2+ Coffers.
- **Embassy-money:**
  - T1–2 Embassy / Silver (or Tunnel).
  - T3–4 Gold or Silver. A second Embassy around T6.
  - Tunnels pick up Golds through Embassy discards.
  - Provinces from about T6–7, then roughly one every 2 turns.

| Plan | Starts scoring | Can end by | Peak VP/turn | VP ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| City Quarter engine | ~T10 | ~T15–16 (Provinces, or Curses + Fool's Gold + Hound with Tower) | 10–14 | Provinces + Ritual + Tower bonus |
| Embassy-money | ~T7 | ~T21 alone | 6 every ~2 turns | Provinces/Duchies; no control of the ending |

**Against their best plan:** Against Embassy-money, the engine has fallen behind by about 2 Provinces when it comes online around T10. From there it outscores money 2:1. It also gets free Silvers from their Embassies, and its Fool's Gold turns their Provinces into Golds, which Butcher turns into Provinces. It also controls a three-pile ending with Tower bonuses, so I favour the engine. In the mirror, the Curse split for Ritual and the cheap-pile Tower VP decide it, so green earlier.

**Challenged:**
- I re-tested money. Its best line is fast, but with no +Buy it can't stop a Ritual/Butcher engine scoring 10+ VP a turn after T11.
- The pick loses if it buys too many Treasures (Silvers from Embassy, Fool's Gold) and City Quarter stops drawing. It also loses if both Cursed Golds plus early Rituals empty the Curses before the engine can use Ritual. Then its payload drops back to Provinces only.

**Recommendation:** Build the City Quarter engine around Butcher. It holds up because Coffers cover the debt, and its payload doesn't run out: Provinces, Butcher's Gold→Province, Ritual, and Tower piles. It also decides when the game ends.

**Open:**
- **6/3:** Butcher / Night Watchman (Silver is fine too).
- **5/4:** Butcher / Nomad Camp. If the $4 hand comes first, buy Nomad Camp on turn 1 so it lands in your turn-2 hand.
- **7/2:** Butcher / Hound. Fool's Gold is fine too.

**Buy priorities:**
1. Use Butcher to trash Cursed Gold or Estates into $4–5 Actions.
2. Pooka, then City Quarter on weak hands, paid off with Coffers.
3. Hounds and a +Buy card.
4. 1–2 Fool's Gold once the deck draws itself.

Green once you have 3 Buys and $16: Province, plus Ritual on any spare $5 card or Gold, plus Butcher's Gold→Province. Play Cursed Gold when it reaches a key price, since the Curse pile probably empties and Tower then cancels each Curse.

**Ending:** Curses are almost certain to empty. Fool's Gold or Hound can be bought out with +Buy for Tower VP. Provinces are the other route. Count Ritual uses left against the Curses remaining.

**Watch out for:**
- **Treasure overload:** Embassy Silvers and Cursed Gold weaken City Quarter.
- **Debt lock:** don't take on a second City Quarter's debt before you can pay it.
- **Curses running out:** if the opponent spends the Curses on Ritual first, switch to Butcher Golds and Duchies.

**Least sure of:**
- **Engine speed:** whether City Quarter's debt and the self-cursing let it come online by T10 against Embassy-money. Test: play out T1–10 with real shuffles. If it can't draw its deck by T11, switch to Butcher-money with Tunnels.
- **Whether the Curse pile empties:** Tower only cancels Curses if it does.

**Assumed:**
- Night Watchman's discards from the deck trigger Tunnel and Hound.
- Butcher can't gain City Quarter, because its cost includes Debt.
- Ritual does nothing once the Curse pile is empty.
- Curses count as non-Victory for Tower.
- Coffers can pay off Debt.
- With 3+ players there are 20+ Curses, so they empty more slowly. Tower then cancels Curses less reliably, but Ritual stays usable longer.
