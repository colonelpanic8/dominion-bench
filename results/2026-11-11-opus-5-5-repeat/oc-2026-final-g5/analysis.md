**Board:** This kingdom favours an engine. Villa is a village with +Buy. Lost City is a non-terminal draw card, Sentinel is a fast $3 trasher, and Borrow gives a free +Buy every turn. Nothing on the board attacks, so Lighthouse's defence is dead text. The open question is draw: only Lost City (and Caravan, slowly) adds cards without using an Action, so the deck has to be thinned for that to be enough. Tools fills the gap, because it gains cards (Villa above all) without using an Action.

**Key interactions:**
- **Tools + Villa:** Play Villa in the Action phase. Then in the Buy phase, before buying anything, play Tools to gain another Villa. That Villa goes to your hand, gives +1 Action and sends you back to the Action phase. Play it for +2 Actions, +$1 and +Buy, then go back to the Buy phase, where you can still play Treasures because you haven't bought anything. Each Tools play is a free Villa that pays $1 and a Buy straight away. With 2 Tools that's +$2 and 2 Villas a turn, from about turn 9–10.
- **Buying Villa:** Every Villa you buy comes back to your hand to play, so it really costs $3 and refunds a Buy. $7 buys Villa then Tools, and $8 plus Borrow buys Villa then Lost City. This builds the deck much faster than one gain per turn.
- **Tools + Gold, Silver or Tools in play:** Tools can gain Treasure. Late in the game, Tools after a Gold gains a Gold, which adds payload without using a Buy. Tools copying Tools grows the stack.
- **Tools → Lost City + Innovation:** In the Buy phase, the gained Lost City is played for +2 Cards. Any Treasures it draws can still be played, and a later Tools → Villa lets you play the Actions it draws. That's one free draw-2 a turn (or a Caravan or Junk Dealer play) once you have Innovation. The cost is that every Lost City you gain gives the opponent a card.
- **Borrow:** +$1 and +Buy every turn, paid for with one card off your next hand. Early on that mostly turns a $4 hand into a $5 one. Once the deck draws itself, the lost card barely matters.

**Other cards:**
- **Junk Dealer:** A cantrip that trashes a card and gives $1, and it triggers Harbor Village's +$1. A good second trasher.
- **Harbor Village:** A backup village once the Villas run low. It works best ahead of Villa or Junk Dealer.
- **Caravan:** Draws at the start of your turn. It is filler, but a fine card to gain with Innovation.
- **Vault:** Terminal draw that also pays out late. With spare Villa Actions it draws 2 and turns your green cards into $. It also lets the opponent sift (discard 2 to draw 1), which is minor.
- **Band of Misfits:** Plays Villa or Sentinel from the Supply. It's a $5 Villa with no on-gain effect, and it stops working as Villa once that pile is empty. Low priority.
- **Lighthouse:** Your only $2 card, giving $1 now and $1 next turn. No attacks means no defence value. Filler at best.

**Ratings check (Glicko):** These agree with my view: Villa (5.77, top 10), Sentinel (4.53), Lost City (2.59) and Junk Dealer (1.93).
- **Tools** (0.47) is underrated here. Gaining without an Action, plus Villa's on-gain effect, makes it a free Villa every turn.
- **Vault** (−2.9) is better than its rating on this board, as late payload in a deck with plenty of village Actions.
- **Band of Misfits** (−2.24) deserves its low rating.
- **Innovation** (1.38) is about average, which fits.

### Build lines
- **Villa / Lost City / Sentinel engine (4/3):**
  - T1: Villa. T2: Sentinel.
  - T3: Villa + 3 Copper gives $4. Buy a Villa, which comes back to hand; play it for +$1 and a Buy.
  - T4: Sentinel trashes Estate and Copper. $3 buys Silver.
  - T5: Villa, Sentinel and $4 make $5. Buy Lost City; Sentinel trashes 2 more.
  - T6: Buy Villa, plus Borrow.
  - T7: Lost City, 2 Villas and Silver make about $6–7, plus Borrow. Buy Villa, which comes back to hand, then Tools.
  - T8–10: Deck is about 12–13 cards: Villa ×4–5, Lost City ×2–3, Tools ×1–2, Sentinel, Silver, 2–3 Copper. Take Lost City, Villas and a second Tools, plus Innovation on a spare $6.
  - Built turn (~T12): Lost City ×3–4 draws 10–12 cards. Villas give $4, Gold and Silver give $5–8, and Tools → Villa ×2 gives +$2. With Borrow that's $14–17 and 4+ Buys: 2 Provinces, or Province + Duchy, plus a free Villa or Gold.
- **Tools money, the baseline:**
  - T1–2: Silver/Tools, and Borrow to reach $5 or $6.
  - Silver + Tools hands gain a Silver. First Gold around T6, then Gold + Tools hands gain a Gold.
  - First $8 around T7–8, and about 4 Provinces by T15. On its own it would empty the Provinces around T20–21.
- **Lost City money:** Rejected. Every Lost City you buy hands a money opponent a card, and the deck has no payload beyond Treasures.

**Plans considered:**
- **Engine** (the build line above):
  - Needs: 4–5 Villas and 3–4 Lost Cities by about T10.
  - Weakness: draw is only about +1 per Lost City, so the deck must stay thin. The Villa pile is contested.
- **Tools money with Borrow:**
  - $6 by about T6 and $8 by about T7–8.
  - Weakness: it tops out at one Province per turn, and Treasure gains stall once you green.
- **Innovation-centred build:** Buys Innovation early to play a Lost City or Caravan gained each turn.
  - Weakness: $6 at T3–5 delays trashing and Villas. Take it mid-build as a helper instead.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Engine vs money:** Money has about 2–3 Provinces by T12. From T12–13 the engine takes 2 a turn, so it is ahead by about T15. Keep Lost City to about 3–4 copies, since each one feeds the money deck a card. Lean on Villas and Tools gains instead.
- **Engine mirror:** It comes down to the Villa split. Tools-gaining Villas wins that race, so get Tools by T7–8. Green earlier than you would against money, and watch the Penultimate Province Rule.

**Challenged:**
- **Innovation-early, re-tested:** Even with Tools → Lost City lines it is still slower than getting Sentinel and Villa first. It belongs in the mid-game.
- **How the pick loses:** You over-buy Lost Cities and feed the opponent cards, or you under-trash so 2–3 Lost Cities can't draw the deck. You can also lose the Villa split to a faster mirror.

**Recommendation:** Build the Villa / Lost City / Sentinel engine with 2 Tools as Action-free Villa gainers and Borrow as a spare Buy, and add Innovation mid-game. It reaches double Provinces around T12–13, which beats the money clock. Against a mirror, Tools wins the Villa split.

**Open:**
- **4/3:** Villa / Sentinel.
  - Alternative: Borrow on T1 for a $5 Junk Dealer, then Sentinel on T2. That costs a card on T2 and T3.
- **5/2:** Junk Dealer, then Sentinel by using Borrow to turn the $2 turn into $3. That's trashing first, and both trashers can be played in one turn.
  - Lost City instead of Junk Dealer is acceptable, but it gives the opponent's turn 2 a sixth card.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffles 1–2:** Sentinel, then Villa at $4 and Lost City or Junk Dealer at $5. Use Borrow to turn $4 into $5 and to add a Buy. Silver only on $3 hands with nothing else to buy.
- **Shuffle 3:** Tools ×2, then more Villas (aim for 5) and Lost City (aim for 3–4). Innovation on a spare $6.
- **Late:** Gold only through Tools or as a leftover buy.
- **Greening:** Start once you reliably hit $13–16 with 2+ Buys, around T12. Against money, don't green in a panic.

**Ending:** Most likely the Provinces run out. Three piles is possible in a mirror: Villa will empty, and Lost City plus Tools or Duchy could follow. Count the piles from about T14.

**Watch out for:**
- An opponent racing for Villas. If the Villa pile runs short, fall back to Harbor Village.
- Thinning too slowly. With weak draw, Sentinel's 2 trashes a play matter.
- Giving a money opponent cards by buying too many Lost Cities.
- More players: the Villa split gets tighter and each Lost City feeds more opponents. Both favour Tools and Harbor Village.

**Least sure of:** That 3–4 Lost Cities and Tools gains really draw enough of the deck for $16 by T12–13. The worked line rests on average draws. If a careful playout stalls around $10–11, the engine is closer to the Tools-money baseline than it looks, and that gap is the thing to check.

**Assumed:**
- **Treasures after Villa:** Bought or gained in the Buy phase, Villa sends you back to the Action phase. I followed the repo rule that once you buy anything you can't play more Treasures that turn. Tools gains aren't buys, so those lines still work.
- **Innovation on a Villa:** It adds little, because Villa already goes to your hand with +1 Action.
- **Band of Misfits:** It can't play a card from an empty Supply pile.
- **Borrow's −1 Card token:** It removes one card from your next draw, normally the Clean-up hand.
