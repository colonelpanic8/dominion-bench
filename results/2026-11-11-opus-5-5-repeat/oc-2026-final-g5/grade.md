- A bought Villa effectively costs $3 and refunds the Buy: plausible. It returns to hand and plays for +$1 and +1 Buy in the reopened Action phase.
- Lighthouse's defence is dead text: plausible. There are no Attack cards in the kingdom.

- Band of Misfits can't play Villa once the pile is empty: plausible. It plays a card "from the Supply".

## Summary
The main structure is right: Sentinel trashing, Tools gaining Villas/Lost Cities/Gold, Villa for Buys, Lost City for draw. It avoids all three traps. It misses the Caravan opener and the Borrow/Band of Misfits alternative. It pulls the wrong way on the two points the commentators stressed most: it caps Lost City instead of taking it aggressively, and it treats Innovation as a mid-game afterthought.
