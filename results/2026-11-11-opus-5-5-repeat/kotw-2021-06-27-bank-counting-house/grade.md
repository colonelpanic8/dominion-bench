## Beyond the key
- Each Talisman in play gains an extra Copper per Copper bought, so Travelling Fair Buys flood Coppers: plausible. Copper is a non-Victory card costing $4 or less, and each Talisman triggers separately.
- Talisman doubles itself, so its pile drains fast and scores with Tower: plausible. Talisman costs $4 and isn't a Victory card.
- Tower counts the starting Coppers once the Copper pile empties: plausible. Tower counts non-Victory cards "you have from an empty Supply pile", and starting Coppers are Copper-pile cards.
- Travelling Fair is an Event, not a gained card, so it doesn't trigger Haunted Woods: plausible. Buying an Event gains no card.
- Haunted Woods is weak against the flooder because their hand is empty by the time they buy: plausible. Treasures are played before buying, and with topdecking the deck order hardly matters.
- If Jester empties the Curse pile, each Curse scores +1 under Tower: plausible. Curse isn't a Victory card, and it comes from a Supply pile.
- Enchantress blanks a lone Counting House, so play Raze, Settlers or Farming Village first: plausible. Enchantress hits only the first Action played that turn.
- Bustling Village costs $5, so Talisman can't double it and Settlers is a poor third pile: plausible. Talisman covers cards costing $4 or less only.
- About 10 Coppers a turn and about 75 VP with a three-pile ending at T14–16: doubtful. The estimate assumes all 10 Talismans uncontested and ignores that the Coppers dilute the deck between reshuffles when gains aren't topdecked. Either way, a TF/CH goldfish that ends the game around T9 makes it moot.

## Summary
The analysis spots the Counting House + Travelling Fair interaction and the Tower-on-Copper bonus, but builds a slow Talisman/Tower Copper slog and treats Counting House and Bank as add-ons. It never runs the topdeck-everything, never-reshuffle loop that the key's experts goldfished to a T8–9 finish, so it expects the game to end around T14–16 and would likely lose to a player running the actual combo.
