## Beyond the key
- Sun is shared, so both players' River Shrine plays count toward Flourishing Trade: plausible. Omen "+1 Sun" removes from the one Prophecy's tokens.
- Only 8 Triumphs for both players, since the Estate pile is the limit: plausible. Triumph gives VP only "if you did" gain an Estate, and 2-player Estates are 8.
- Witch's Hut discarding 2 Weavers plays both and curses: plausible. Weaver is an Action and reacts to a discard outside Clean-up.
- Artificer's gain goes onto your deck and Footpad's +1 Card draws it straight away: plausible. The gain is in the Action phase, so the topdecked card is drawn.
- River Shrine's Clean-up gain doesn't spoil Exploration: plausible. Exploration checks at the end of the Buy phase, and River Shrine's check looks only at Buy-phase gains.
- Change's Debt mode (+$3) can be fed by unpaid Triumph Debt carried over: plausible. Debt can persist across turns.
- Stonemason on Gold gains "2 Duchies once the Prophecy makes them $4": wrong as framed. Duchy ($5) already costs less than Gold ($6) without the Prophecy, and both drop by $1 with it.
- Footpad's draw also works on gains in the opponent's Action phase (e.g. a Weaver reacting to a Footpad discard): plausible. The rule says "an Action phase", not "your".
- Footpad's attack is weak against Weaver decks: plausible. Discarding down to 3 can play Weavers.
- Prophecy won't fire until about T13–14 if only one player plays Shrines: doubtful. Two early River Shrines (the analysis's own build) remove a Sun on most turns, and the key's line has it firing by about turn 6–7.
- Witch's Hut cursing as a main engine piece and Curses as an ending pile: plausible by card text, but the key doesn't address it.
- 5/2 (Artificer/nothing) is noticeably worse than 4/3: plausible. The key gives no view.
- The Weaver lose-track/Stop-Moving complication isn't raised, so no extra credit.

## Summary
The analysis finds the board's engine: Footpad turning gains into draw, Weaver/Hostelry/Artificer gaining, and Triumph bought with Flourishing Trade's spare-Action Buys. But it dismisses Exploration, the key's opener, and builds slowly, scoring one Triumph per turn from about T10 instead of a single huge megaturn around turn 6–7. It also misses the Change/Stonemason route to Alchemist and Vineyard without Potions.
