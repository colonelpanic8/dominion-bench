## Beyond the key
- Ferry on Old Witch makes Old Witch cost $3, so a 4/3 opens Ferry/Old Witch and every Hermit play can gain another Old Witch: plausible. Ferry's −$2 token applies on your turns, and Hermit gains cards costing up to $3. The key never discusses Old Witch, but its grader notes credit flagging the junker.
- When Old Witch hits you and you trash the Curse, your own Sewers lets you trash another card from your hand: plausible. Sewers triggers on any trash you make other than with Sewers, including on an opponent's turn.
- Buying Ferry or Sewers doesn't stop the Madman exchange: plausible. The 2019 wording checks "if you didn't buy any cards," and Events and Projects aren't cards.
- Madman played from a 5-card hand draws 4: plausible. Four cards remain in hand after it's played, and it gives +1 Card per card in hand.
- The self-trash only fires Sewers if Hermit leaves play before the hand is discarded: plausible caveat. Horton assumes it works; the analysis flags the ordering as uncertain instead of guessing.
- Moving Ferry to Treasure Trove so it costs $3 and Hermit can gain one: wrong. Ferry moves the token to an Action Supply pile, and Treasure Trove is a Treasure.
- Ferry on Bridge makes Bridge cost $2, and $1 with a Bridge in play: plausible. $4 − $2 − $1.
- Bridge is the only +Buy on the board: plausible. No other card or landscape gives +Buy.
- Rogue's attack trashes Silver, Gold or Hermit off the opponent's deck when the trash is empty of $3–$6 cards: plausible from card text.
- Old Witch Curse-slog as the recommended main plan: doubtful. It's a coherent line, but it is the analyst's own and it displaces the Bridge payload that Horton's playtesting centres on.

## Summary
The analysis sees the Hermit/Sewers thinning and the Rogue-regains-Hermits trick, and it avoids the losing Madman megaturn. But it builds an Old Witch Curse-slog that Horton never discusses. It dismisses Bridge as a payload and Diplomat as a village, and it never links Outpost's 3-card turn to Diplomat. That misses the designer's preferred Diplomat/Bridge deck entirely. It also misreads Ferry as able to target Treasure Trove.
