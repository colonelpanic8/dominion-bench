## Beyond the key
- Governor's trash mode turns Gold into Province (exactly $6→$8), Silver into Duchy/Governor, Experiment into Governor, Copper into Estate: plausible. The parenthetical "+$2" applies to the player who played it, and the costs line up.
- Throne Room on Governor nets +1 Action, so it doubles as a village: plausible. Throne Room uses one Action and Governor gives +1 Action on each of its two plays.
- Friendly triggers once per Governor in hand (assumed, but flagged as its least-sure point): doubtful. Friendly is one "at the start of your Clean-up phase" ability that discards "a Friendly card", which reads as one gain per turn. The pile-race timing (empty by T8–9, 5–5 split) depends on this.
- "Forager gives the only real +Buy": doubtful. Tactician gives +1 Buy too, which the analysis admits later.
- Advisor: "none… skip it": doubtful. It's a non-terminal +1 Action card that nets about +2 cards. The grader note counts it as reasonable support.
- A Wish gains a Gold to hand that a Governor can trash for a Province the same turn: plausible. Wish gains a card costing up to $6 to hand.
- Throne Room on Experiment gives +4 Cards even though the first play returns it to the pile: plausible. Throne Room still replays a card that has left play.
- Leprechaun counts Durations left in play from last turn toward 7: plausible. Leprechaun counts the cards you have in play.
- Merchant Camp topdecks itself, so it's in nearly every hand: plausible from its below-the-line text.
- Each Governor mode gifts the opponent its small version (+1 Card, Silver, or a +$1 remodel): plausible from the card text.

## Summary
The analysis builds a sensible Governor/Friendly deck that doubles Governors with Throne Room and Flagship, and it finds the Throne Room–Leprechaun double-Wish payload. But it dismisses Tactician outright, and Tactician is the centerpiece of Horton's design (multiple Tacticians via Throne Room and Way of the Owl). So it repeats the finalists' mistake instead of the designer's intended deck.
