## Beyond the key
- Lurker trashes Silk Merchant from the Supply for +1 Coffers +1 Villager, then gains it back from the trash for another: plausible. Silk Merchant triggers when you gain or trash it, and Lurker does both without using an Action. This is a strong loop the key doesn't mention.
- With 3 Highways in play, Provinces cost $5 and Estates, Silvers and Shops cost $0, and Panic's Buys turn this into big multi-card turns: plausible. Highway's reduction applies to every gain that turn, and Panic gives +2 Buys per Treasure.
- Big Money with Panic stalls after about 2–3 Provinces because Treasures return to their piles: plausible. Every played Treasure leaves the deck once Panic is active. That still leaves Highway-discounted rebuys, so "stalls" may overstate it.
- Tools copies a $5 Action in play without using an Action, but works once after Panic: plausible. Tools is a Treasure, so Panic returns it.
- Shop's +$1 survives Panic and Shop can play Silk Merchant or Council Room: plausible. Shop is an Action, not a Treasure.
- Reckless Transmute on Curses gains nothing: plausible. Curse is not an Action, Treasure or Victory card.
- Poet reveals Lurker, Shop, Copper or Silver: plausible but incomplete. Estates and Curses also cost $3 or less and would go into hand. Excluding Transmute ($0+Potion) is right.
- The Potion is a one-use $4 card under Panic, so Transmute doesn't pay: doubtful as a verdict. It ignores the pre-Panic window and Transmute's own Treasure→Transmute gains. It is the step that leads the analysis into the trap.

## Summary
The analysis reads Panic, Kitsune and Highway well, and finds a real Lurker/Silk Merchant loop the key doesn't mention. But it explicitly rejects Transmute, the card the designer built the board around. So it misses the Transmute-vs-Panic choice and the Transmute/Lurker endgame, and falls into the key's only trap.
