**Board:** This is a Coffers-and-cost-reduction engine on a board where Treasures stop lasting. Panic (a Prophecy) starts with 5 Sun. Poet and Kitsune each remove one when played (+1 Sun), so Panic will probably switch on around turns 6–9. From then on, every Treasure played gives +2 Buys and goes back to its pile in Clean-up. Coppers then leave your deck after one play, and Silver and Gold become one-use money. The lasting economy is:
- Silk Merchant's Coffers and Villagers, fed by Lurker
- Kitsune's +$2, and Shop's +$1
- Highway's cost reduction, which applies to every one of Panic's many Buys

Kitsune is the only junker, and only Transmute trashes Curses. The open question is pile control. Several Highways plus lots of Buys make Estates and cheap Actions free, so a three-pile ending can come in one turn.

**Key interactions:**
- **Lurker + Silk Merchant:** Lurker trashes a Silk Merchant from the Supply, which triggers its "when you trash this" (+1 Coffers, +1 Villager). A later Lurker gains it back from the trash for another +1 Coffers, +1 Villager. So each Lurker play, which costs no Action, gives about +$1 and +1 Action for later. Every second play also adds a Silk Merchant (+2 Cards, +1 Buy). This runs from turn 3–4. The trash is shared, so whatever you trash, the opponent's Lurker can take. Prefer gaining Silk Merchant back over leaving it in the trash.
- **Panic + Highway:** Each Highway in play cuts every purchase by $1 (Events excepted). With 3 Highways out, Provinces cost $5, Silver and Estates cost $0, and Shop costs $0. Panic supplies the Buys at +2 per Treasure played. A built turn of about $10 takes 2 Provinces plus free Estates. Coppers and Silvers bought free come back next turn as +$1/+$2 and +2 Buys each, if you draw them, so the payload keeps growing.
- **Panic as trashing:** each Copper you play leaves your deck. An engine wants this. A money deck is ruined by it, because each Gold works once.
- **Tools + Highway, Kitsune or Silk Merchant in play:** before Panic, Tools is a $4 Treasure that gains a copy of one of your $5 Actions every time you play it, without using an Action. After Panic it works once and goes back to its pile, but Highways make it cheap or free to buy again.
- **Pile-out:** the Curses empty under Kitsune. 2 Highways make Estates and Lurkers free, and 3 make Shop free. Lurker can also trash Action cards straight from the Supply. Three piles can go in one turn of about 15–20 Buys.

**Other cards:**
- **Kitsune:** village plus junker, and it removes Sun. Choose +$2 and Curse early, +2 Actions and Curse inside the engine, and +2 Actions and +$2 once the Curses are gone. Its Silver option is next turn's money.
- **Shop:** lets you play an Action you don't already have in play (Silk Merchant, Council Room). Its +$1 still works after Panic. It's better here than its rating.
- **Council Room:** best partner is Shop. One copy at most: Silk Merchant draws as well, and Council Room makes the opponent draw a card.
- **Poet:** a cantrip that removes Sun. It reveals Lurker, Shop, Copper or Silver, all of which cost $3 or less. Buy it only if you want Panic sooner.
- **Figurine:** good draw before Panic, one use after it. Skip it.
- **Prepare:** saves a hand of terminals and plays them next turn without spending Actions. Use it when you're short on Actions, which Villagers usually cover.
- **Transmute (Reckless):** trashes two cards per play (Estates for 2 Golds, Curses for nothing). But the Potion is a dead $4 card that works once under Panic, Transmute goes back to its pile, and the Golds work once. Skip it. Its only real use is trashing Curses.

**Plans considered:**
- **A. Kitsune / Silk Merchant / Lurker engine, Highway payload, Panic on early.** Curse early, collect Coffers and Villagers, get 3–4 Highways, then take 2 Provinces plus Estates per turn. It needs 2–3 Kitsunes, 3–4 Silk Merchants, 2 Lurkers and 3–4 Highways. Weakness: income can drop when the Coppers vanish before Coffers and Kitsune $ take over.
- **B. Big Money with Council Room or Figurine (baseline), avoiding Omens.** It reaches $8 around turns 6–8. Normally it takes 4 Provinces by turns 14–17, but the Kitsune player switches Panic on around turns 7–9. After that, each Province uses up $8 of Treasure for good, the deck shrinks to green cards, and it's also taking Curses. It doesn't get there.
- **C. Highway pile-out rush.** 2 Kitsunes plus Highways early, then free Coppers as fuel, ending on Curses, Estates and Lurkers around turns 12–13. It scores few VP, so it's really plan A's finishing move, not a plan of its own.

### Build lines
- **A (5/2, average draws):**
  - T1–2: Kitsune / Lurker.
  - T3: Kitsune + 3 Coppers → $5 with +$2, Curse to the opponent. Buy Kitsune #2.
  - T4: Lurker trashes a Silk Merchant from the Supply (+1 Coffers, +1 Villager). Buy Silk Merchant (+1 Coffers, +1 Villager). Now 2 Coffers, 2 Villagers.
  - T5–7: play Kitsune(s) for $2 and a Curse, Villager into Silk Merchant, totals about $5–7 with 2 Buys. Buy Highway, plus Silk Merchant or Lurker with the extra Buy. Use Lurker to regain Silk Merchant. Buy Tools on a $4 hand with a Highway or Kitsune in play.
  - About T7–9: Panic switches on (both players' Kitsunes, plus any Poet). Coppers start leaving the deck.
  - T8–10: Tools and $5 hands bring Highways to 3–4, plus 1 Shop. Leftover Buys pick up free Silvers or Coppers for next turn.
  - **Built turn, about T10–11:** Lurker (+1 Coffers, +1 Villager). 3 Highways. 2 Kitsunes (+2 Actions and +$2 each). Shop (+$1, plays a Silk Merchant). 2–3 Silk Merchants (+Buy each). 2 one-use Treasures (+$2–3, +4 Buys). Spend 3 Coffers. That's about $10 and 8 Buys, which takes 2 Provinces at $5 each plus free Estates or Coppers, about 13–15 VP.
- **B:**
  - T1–2: Silver/Silver, or Council Room/Silver.
  - About T6–8: first Province.
  - After Panic: each $8 hand spends its Treasures for good. It reaches about 2–3 Provinces and then stalls.

**Against their best plan:** The best reply to A is A, a Kitsune mirror.
- **Curses:** the 10 split about 5–5. Getting Kitsunes out first, and playing one every turn, decides who ends up with more.
- **Silk Merchant and the trash:** contested. Don't leave Silk Merchants in the trash for the opponent's Lurker.
- **Who ends it:** the player with more Highways plus Treasure in hand decides when it ends. Count the opponent's Highways and next turn's Treasures every turn. With 2 Highways and about 9 Treasures they can buy out Estates plus Lurkers, so stay ahead on VP or end it yourself first.

Against B, Panic decides it: B has to win before turns 8–9 and can't.

**Challenged:**
- **Transmute, re-tested as the only Curse answer:** trashing two Curses per play doesn't repay a one-use Potion plus the Transmute buy. Rejected.
- **B, re-tested with no Omens:** A alone switches Panic on by about turn 9 (2 Kitsunes played most turns from turn 5), so B's window is too short.
- **How A loses:** too many Curses taken, building too slowly (gaining one card per turn), or being three-piled while behind.

**Recommendation:** Plan A. It gets stronger after Panic, it holds up in the mirror as long as you match the opponent's Kitsune count, and Highways let you decide when the game ends.

**Open:**
- **5/2:** Kitsune / Lurker (recommended, since you can choose your split).
- **4/3:** Silk Merchant / Lurker. Both openings start the Lurker loop on turn 3–4.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffles 1–2:** Kitsune to 2. Silk Merchant at $4. Lurker to 2. Highway at $5 once you have 2 Kitsunes. Tools at $4 when a $5 Action will be in play.
- **Shuffle 3:** Highways to 3–4, Shop ×1, Silk Merchant to 3–4, Council Room 0–1. Don't buy Silver or Gold as long-term economy. After Panic they're only next-turn fuel, best bought free.
- **Green:** when you reliably have 3 Highways and about $10, around turns 10–11. Take free Estates with every spare Buy late.

**Ending:** Most likely three piles: Curses, Estates (free under Highways), and one of Lurker, Shop or Silk Merchant (Lurker can trash cards from the Supply). Otherwise the Provinces run out around turns 14–16.

**Watch out for:**
- The opponent's pile-out turn. Count their Highways plus the Treasures they'll play.
- Income dropping after Panic. If short, take Kitsune's Silver and free Coppers.
- Feeding the opponent's Lurker from the trash.

With more players there's more Sun to remove (8 or 10), Curses split further, Kitsune gets stronger, and Silk Merchant is more contested.

**Least sure of:** How fast plan A recovers its income once Panic removes the Coppers, around turns 7–9. To test it, play out turns 7–10 counting Coffers, Kitsune $ and Shop $ against the $5 needed for each Highway. If it comes up short, delay buying Omens and use Tools and Silk Merchant buys before Panic.

**Assumed:**
- Lurker trashing Silk Merchant from the Supply triggers "when you trash this" (the same principle as Hunting Grounds + Lurker).
- Highway reduces the $ part of every cost, Provinces and Potion included, but not Events.
- Poet's "costs $3 or less" excludes Potion-cost cards.
- Coffers are spent before buying, so Coffers from Silk Merchants bought this turn count toward later turns.
- Cards played through Prepare give Panic's Buys as normal.
- The opponent has no Duration in play for Tools to copy.
