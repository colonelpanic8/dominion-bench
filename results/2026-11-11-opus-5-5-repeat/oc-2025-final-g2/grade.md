## Beyond the key
- Nothing costs $5–7 except Gold, so a 5/2 opening wastes money: plausible. Kingdom costs are $0P–$4 plus Peddler's $8; Loots aren't buyable. (Though Grotto at $2 is the key's answer to the $2 half, not Monastery.)
- Durations from last turn (Amulet, Grotto, Enchantress) count toward Peddler's discount: plausible. They're Action cards still in play during your next turn's Buy phase.
- Hammer can gain a discounted Peddler: plausible. Hammer is played in the Buy phase, where Peddler's reduced cost applies, so with 2+ Actions in play Peddler costs ≤$4.
- Spell Scroll can become a Gold: plausible. Trash a $7 Loot to gain a cheaper card; Gold costs $6.
- Remodel Gold → Province: plausible. $6 + $2 = $8.
- Monastery trashes Coppers after they've paid out, about 2 per turn with Remodel/Amulet/Buy gains: plausible. Monastery can trash "a Copper you have in play", once per card gained this turn.
- Farmhands' on-gain can replay a stuck Remodel next turn: plausible. When gained (e.g. via Remodel), set aside an Action from hand and play it next turn.
- Enchantress's attack is mild against Peddler decks because they lead with a cantrip: plausible. Replacing a Peddler's instructions with +1 Card +1 Action costs only $1.
- Fortune needs 5 Gladiators gone plus $8 and 8 Debt: plausible. Split pile with Fortune underneath; costs $8+8D.
- Transmute's best output is a Duchy from a Peddler: doubtful. Transmute turns an Estate (Victory) into a Gold, which is better than a Duchy early; skipping it is still reasonable.
- Way of the Squirrel's +2 cards arrive after the Clean-up draw, making a 7-card hand: plausible. "+2 Cards at the end of this turn" resolves after Clean-up.

## Summary
The analysis finds the winning engine (Grotto, Farmhands, Peddler, Remodel with Peddler's $8 printed cost, Foray Loots for +Buy, Squirrel on spare Actions) and gets the core Remodel/Peddler interaction exactly right. Its main misses are underweighting Grotto (never opened, treated as a $2 filler) and recommending Monastery on 5/2, the opening both commentators called "simply very bad"; it also doesn't see Grotto letting the deck green early.
