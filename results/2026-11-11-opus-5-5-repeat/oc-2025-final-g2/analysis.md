**Kingdom analysis: oc-2025-final-g2** (2 players, no Colonies, no Shelters)

**Board:** No card here draws this turn. Farmhands and Peddler only replace themselves. What you get instead is a bigger *next* hand: Grotto (up to +4), Enchantress (+2) and Way of the Squirrel (+2 for any Action you play as the Way). The real payload is **Peddler + Remodel**. In the Buy phase Peddler costs $2 less per Action in play, so it becomes free. In the Action phase it still costs $8, so Remodel turns it into a Province. +Buy is the open question: no Kingdom card gives it. It comes only from Loot (via Foray) and from Fortune, which sits under the Gladiators.

**Inventory.** Kingdom: Grotto, Guardian, Monastery ($2); Enchantress, Gladiator/Fortune, Amulet ($3); Farmhands, Remodel ($4); Peddler ($8, discounted); Transmute ($0 + Potion). Landscapes: Foray (Event, $3; uses the Loot pile), Way of the Squirrel. Also in play: Potion, Debt (Fortune) and Night cards. Starting deck is 7 Copper and 3 Estate, so the splits are 4/3 or 5/2. **Nothing costs $5–7 except Gold**, so a 5/2 opening wastes money.

**Key interactions:**
- **Remodel + Peddler:** Peddler's discount only applies in the Buy phase. In the Action phase it costs $8, so Remodel turns it into any card costing up to $10, i.e. a Province. Remodel on Gold also gives a Province. Each Remodel with a target is about one extra Province per turn from around T10.
- **Peddler + Actions in play:** your own Durations from last turn (Amulet, Grotto, Enchantress) stay in play, so they count toward the discount. Add Farmhands and the Peddlers you play, and 4 or more Actions in play is routine by T7–8. From then on every spare Buy gains a free Peddler: +$1 now, a Province later through Remodel.
- **Foray → Loot = the +Buy:** Foray gains a $7 Treasure for $3 and a Buy. Most Loots give +Buy and $3. Hammer gains a cheap Peddler, Doubloons comes with a Gold, and Spell Scroll can become a Gold. The cost is discarding 3 *differently named* cards from hand. Late in the game those come for free: Province, Estate and a spare Copper. This works against heavy thinning, so keep a little variety of junk.
- **Grotto, Squirrel, Enchantress → big next hand:** Grotto (non-terminal) sets aside up to 4 dead cards and replaces them next turn. Each spare Action spent on a Squirrel adds +2 cards next turn. Expect 7–9-card hands, which feed both the Remodels and Foray.
- **Monastery + several gains:** Remodel (a trash and a gain), Amulet's Silver and each Buy all count as gains. Monastery then trashes Coppers *after* they've paid out, about 2 per turn early.

**Other cards:**
- **Farmhands:** the village that makes Remodel ×2–3 and Squirrel plays possible. Its on-gain trick plays a stuck Remodel or Copper next turn for free.
- **Amulet:** the best opener here: it trashes or gains a Silver, and stays in play as an Action for the Peddler discount.
- **Enchantress:** +2 cards next turn and counts as an Action in play. The attack is mild against Peddler decks, because they lead with a cantrip. Take 0–1.
- **Gladiator/Fortune:** Fortune with Gladiators in play gains Golds, which you can Remodel. It's slow and needs 5 Gladiators gone and 8 Debt. It's a backup source of +Buy only.
- **Guardian:** only blocks Enchantress or Sword. Weak.
- **Transmute:** a Potion clogs the deck, and its best output is a Duchy from a Peddler, which Remodel beats. Skip it. Rating agrees (−6.84).

**Ratings check:** Grotto's 3.41 fits its role. I rate Remodel (−0.09) and Foray (−1.52) higher than their ratings because Peddler and Loot give them targets on this board. I agree with the low ratings for Gladiator and Transmute.

**Plans considered:**
1. **Peddler/Remodel engine** (Farmhands, Grotto, Amulet, Foray Loots). Wants 3 Farmhands, 2–3 Remodels and 3+ Peddlers by T10. The weak points are Foray reliability and contesting the Peddler pile.
2. **Money baseline:** Silver/Gold with Amulet, Grotto and Enchantress, plus Foray Loots. There is no terminal draw, so it is weaker than standard BM. It hits $6 around T4–5 and $8 around T7–9.
3. **Gladiator/Fortune money** with Farmhands: 3 Gladiators, then Fortune for 2–3 Golds. The opponent can block the dig by revealing a copy. It's slow, and the Debt delays the next buys.

### Build lines (average draws; estimated, not simulated)
- **Engine:** T1–2 Remodel/Amulet · T3 Remodel Estate→Farmhands, buy Silver · T4 Amulet trashes Estate, buy Farmhands · T5 Amulet gains Silver; 2 Actions in play, so Peddler costs $4: buy it · T6–8 Grotto, Monastery, second Remodel, Peddlers at $0–2, first Foray when the hand holds Estate + Copper + a spare card · T9–10 first Remodel Peddler→Province · **built turn ~T12:** Farmhands, Farmhands, Grotto, Remodel Peddler→Province, Remodel Gold or Peddler→Province, 2 Peddlers, a Loot, Silver. That's ~$8, 2 Buys and 7 Actions in play, so free Peddlers. Total about 2–3 Provinces per turn.
- **Money:** T1–2 Amulet/Silver · Gold from ~T5 · first Province ~T8 · roughly 4 Provinces by T17. With no draw it can't beat the usual BM+X clock.

**Against their best plan:** Engine vs money: money needs about 4 Provinces by T14. It probably has 2–3 by then, while the engine takes 2+ per turn from T11–12, so the engine wins. In the engine mirror, the 10 Peddlers split and Remodel Gold becomes more important. Grab Peddlers early, even at $2–4, and green earlier. Enchantress doesn't change either matchup much.

**Challenged:** Re-testing money with Foray: Loots give money its own +Buy, but without draw it stays near one Province per turn. It loses unless the engine fails to get Foray Loots. The engine loses if it over-thins its junk, so Foray dies and there's no +Buy, and it is stuck at one Peddler and one Remodel per turn. In that case, fall back to Fortune for +Buy.

**Recommendation:** Build the Peddler/Remodel engine. It scales past one Province per turn, and on this board the money deck has no draw to keep pace.

**Open:**
- **4/3:** Remodel / Amulet. Amulet / Monastery is close.
- **5/2:** Remodel / Monastery. Nothing is worth $5, and Monastery pairs with Remodel's trash-and-gain.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffles 1–2:** Farmhands; Remodel turns Estates into Farmhands; a second Amulet or Silver at $3; Grotto or Monastery at $2. Buy Peddlers as soon as they cost $4 or less.
- **Shuffle 3:** Foray whenever the hand allows it, a second and third Remodel, Peddlers at $0–2, and one Gold if you hit $6 with nothing better.
- **Greening:** start once you have 2 Remodels and 3 Peddlers (~T10). Remodel Peddlers and Golds into Provinces. Near the end, Remodel Silver or Farmhands into Duchies and use spare Buys on Estates.

**Ending:** most likely the Provinces run out around T15–17. The Peddler pile can empty in a mirror. That's one pile, so a three-pile ending is unlikely.

**Watch out for:**
- The opponent contesting Peddlers. If so, lean on Remodel Gold (Doubloons, Spell Scroll, Fortune).
- Over-thinning, which kills Foray.
- The Penultimate Province Rule when the opponent's Remodels can take 2 Provinces in one turn.

**Least sure of:** whether Foray fires often enough (about every other turn from T7) to supply the +Buys. Test it by playing T6–10 out with real hands. If it rarely fires, Fortune becomes the main +Buy and the engine slows by 2–3 turns, which brings money back into contention.

**Assumed:**
- Way of the Squirrel's +2 cards come after the Clean-up draw, so the next hand has 7.
- Durations played last turn count for Peddler's discount.
- Peddler costs $8 in the Action phase, so Remodel reaches Province.
- The order of the Loot pile is random and unknown.
- Gaining a Loot counts as a gain for Monastery.
