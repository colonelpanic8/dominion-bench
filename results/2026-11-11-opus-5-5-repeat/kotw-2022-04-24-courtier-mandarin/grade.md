## Beyond the key
- Courtier revealing a Tunnel (Victory–Reaction) gives +$3 and a Gold: plausible. Two types means two different choices.
- Gamble discarding a Tunnel gains a Gold: plausible. Gamble's discard is outside Clean-up, which triggers Tunnel.
- Displace Exiles Tunnels; gaining a Tunnel later lets you discard the Exiled ones, each gaining a Gold: plausible. Discarding from Exile on gain is outside Clean-up.
- Displace a Gold into a Province, and the Exiled Golds come back when you next gain a Gold: plausible under Exile rules.
- An opponent's Cardinal feeds your Tunnels/Village Green (its discard triggers them) and its Exiles come back when you gain a copy: plausible from Cardinal's "discards the rest" plus the Exile rules.
- Gamble's +1 Buy replaces the Buy it uses, so any number of Gambles can come before a final Province: plausible.
- Mandarin bought first, then Gambles replay the topdecked Treasures for about +$1 net per Gold: plausible. Gamble may play a discarded Treasure, and Mandarin's on-gain puts the Treasures in play on top of the deck.
- Every Courtier play is at least Gold-level, since even a Copper or Estate gives one choice: plausible.
- Shelters would change Courtier's math (Overgrown Estate and Necropolis have 2 types): plausible.
- 12 Tunnels with 3+ players, 8 with 2: plausible. These are standard Victory pile sizes.
- Silk Road worth about 3–4 VP in a deck with roughly 15 Victory cards: plausible arithmetic.
- Tunnel/Silk Road/Duchy as the likely three-pile ending: doubtful. It's speculative, and the key's combo deck ends on Provinces long before that.

## Summary
The analysis finds the right half of the engine (Courtier gaining Golds, with multi-type reveals including Village Green) and avoids both traps. But it builds a Tunnel/Gamble Gold flood and treats Mandarin as a minor card, missing the Mandarin loop that replays the Golds every turn and topdecks Village Green for Courtier. A player following it would gain Golds steadily but never reach the explosive turns the key's expert clocked.
