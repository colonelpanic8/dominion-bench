## Beyond the key
- The $0 Wayfarer chains: after one $0 Wayfarer, the next one also costs $0. Plausible. Wayfarer copies the last *other* card gained, and that Wayfarer's own cost resolves back to the Copper. agoo03's 9 Wayfarers are consistent with this.
- Every Abundance in play triggers on the same Action gain, and an untriggered one stays out. Plausible. Each Abundance sets up its own "next time you gain an Action" effect, and Duration cards stay in play until they resolve.
- The T5 line: after Rope + Abundance and buying Copper then Wayfarer, you have "$7 and 2 Buys left". Wrong. Rope's 2 Buys are spent on the Copper and the Wayfarer, and Abundance adds 1, so only 1 Buy is left.
- Captain can play a $0 Wayfarer from the Supply after a Copper gain. Plausible. Wayfarer is then a non-Duration, non-Command Action costing ≤$4. It's a narrow use.
- Mastermind on Wayfarer at start of turn draws 9 and can gain 3 Silvers. Plausible. No card has been gained yet, so Wayfarer's +3 Cards and optional Silver resolve three times.
- Play Chariot Race before any Copper gain, or a revealed Wayfarer costs $0. Plausible. Chariot Race compares current costs, and Wayfarer's cost follows the last gain.
- Corsair is weak because the deck barely uses Silver/Gold. Plausible. Its attack only trashes Silver/Gold, and it was never bought here.
- Chariot Race as a "free" Abundance trigger: a $3 cost refunded by $3 per Abundance. Plausible from card text. agoo03's 8 Chariot Races also served as Colonnade targets.

## Summary
The analysis found the game-winning tricks: Copper making Wayfarer cost $0, Colonnade VP from repeat Action gains, and Mastermind on Wayfarer. Those are what agoo03 actually used. It wraps them in an Abundance-centred deck that the key flags as a trap, though. It also underuses Artificer and Rope, the two cards both players and both commentators built around, and skips Livery entirely.
