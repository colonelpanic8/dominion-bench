# Kingdom analysis: oc-2026-final-g2 (full analysis)

**Board:** This board has strong payload. Rope and Abundance both give +Buy. Abundance gives +$3 and +Buy each time you gain an Action, and Wayfarer copies the cost of the last card you gained. Together these give you a Wayfarer for nothing every turn and turn every Abundance into $3. Colonnade adds 12 VP for buying an Action while a copy of it is in play. The weak spots are Actions and trashing. Cursed Village is the only real village and it gives you a Hex each time you gain one, and Rope trashes only one card per turn.

**Inventory:** Chariot Race $3 · Abundance $4, Rope $4 (both Treasure–Duration) · Cursed Village, Livery, Mastermind, Artificer, Corsair $5 · Captain, Wayfarer $6 · Road Network (Project, $5) · Colonnade (Landmark, 12 VP for 2 players) · Horses (non-Supply, from Livery) · Hexes and States (from Cursed Village). No Colonies, no Shelters. The $5 slot is crowded and nothing is worth buying at $2. Starting deck is 7 Copper and 3 Estates, so the turn 1–2 splits are 4/3 or 5/2.

**Key interactions:**
- **The free-Wayfarer trick.** Gain a Copper, and Wayfarer then costs $0 for the rest of the turn. Each Wayfarer you then gain keeps the next one at $0, because the last *other* card gained is now a $0 Wayfarer.
  - How to get the Copper: buy it with a spare Buy, play Artificer discarding nothing (it gains a $0 card onto your deck), or get it from the Greed Hex.
  - Smaller versions: buying Silver or Chariot Race first makes Wayfarer cost $3. Even a plain money deck with Rope can buy Silver + Wayfarer for $6.
- **Abundance + any Action gain.** Every Abundance in play triggers on the same Action gain, so each one pays +$3 and +1 Buy. With 3 Abundances, a $0 Wayfarer is worth +$9 and +3 Buys. Chariot Race works as the trigger too: it costs $3 and each Abundance refunds $3. An Abundance that doesn't trigger stays in play until it does, so a Delusion Hex only delays it.
- **Colonnade.** Buying a Wayfarer with one in play, or a Chariot Race with one in play, takes 2 VP. With free Wayfarers each turn the 12 VP are gone in about 6 triggers, so contest it from around turn 7.
- **Mastermind + Wayfarer.** At the start of your next turn it plays Wayfarer three times: +9 cards and up to 3 Silvers, without spending an Action. It stands in for the villages this board lacks. Mastermind on Livery gives +$9, and a Horse for each gain costing $4 or more counts three times.
- **Rope.** It trashes one card per turn, which keeps the Coppers bought for the trick under control.

**Other cards:**
- **Cursed Village:** the only +Actions card. Play it early in the turn while your hand is small, since it draws up to 6. Each one you gain gives you a Hex (Plague, Locusts, Misery and Delusion are the bad ones), so buy one or two at most.
- **Livery:** Horses from gaining $4+ cards (Abundance, Province) give non-terminal draw. It's a decent single copy, but it's terminal and competes with Wayfarer for Actions.
- **Captain:** almost nothing here. Its only Supply target is Chariot Race, except right after a Copper gain, when it can play a $0 Wayfarer from the Supply. Skip it, despite its 3.76 rating.
- **Chariot Race:** a cantrip and the cheap Abundance/Colonnade trigger. Play it *before* any Copper gain, or your revealed Wayfarer counts as costing $0.
- **Corsair:** it hits only Silver and Gold, which this deck barely relies on. Consider it only against a Gold-heavy money deck (rating −2.75, which I agree with).
- **Road Network:** +1 card per Victory card the opponent gains. It's late and small, so only buy it with a spare Buy and $5 (rating −2.99, agree).

**Ratings check:**
- I rate Abundance (−0.49) and Wayfarer (1.93) far higher than their ratings because of the cost trick.
- Rope (3.61) and Mastermind (3.33) agree with my view.

### Build lines
- **Abundance–Wayfarer deck (recommended):**
  - T1–2: Rope / Silver.
  - T3: $4 → Abundance (or Artificer at $5).
  - T4: Rope draws a card and trashes an Estate; $5 → Artificer or Mastermind.
  - T5: Rope + Abundance + 3 Copper = $4 with 2 Buys. Buy Copper ($0), then Wayfarer ($0). Abundance adds +$3 and +1 Buy, so you have $7 and 2 Buys left: buy Abundance + Chariot Race.
  - T6–9: every turn, Copper (or Artificer's Copper), then a free Wayfarer, then spend the rest on Abundance, Cursed Village or Mastermind. Target about 3 Abundance, 3–4 Wayfarer, 1–2 Cursed Village, 1 Mastermind, 1 Artificer, 1–2 Rope.
  - Built turn (about T10, ~25 cards): Cursed Village, Chariot Race, Artificer, Wayfarer, Wayfarer, about 16 cards seen.
    - Money: ~6 Copper ($6) + 2 Silver ($4) + Artificer and Chariot Race ($2) + Rope ($1) = $13.
    - Buy Copper, then a $0 Wayfarer: 3 Abundances add +$9 and +3 Buys, and Colonnade gives +2 VP.
    - Result: about $22 with 3 Buys left, so 2 Provinces plus a Duchy or Chariot Race.
- **Wayfarer Big Money (baseline):**
  - T1–2: Rope / Silver.
  - T3–4: at $6 with Rope in play, Silver + Wayfarer ($3); otherwise Gold.
  - Then a second Wayfarer and Gold, and Province at $8.
  - First $8 around T5–7, 4 Provinces around T13–14, all 8 alone around T20.
- **Livery/Horse money:** Livery plus Rope Buys for Horses. Roughly equal to Wayfarer Big Money, with a lower ceiling.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Abundance–Wayfarer | ~T7 (Colonnade), ~T10 (Provinces) | ~T14–15 | 2 Provinces + 2 VP | Provinces + most of Colonnade |
| Wayfarer Big Money | ~T7 | ~T20 | 1 Province | about half the Provinces |

**Plans considered:**
- **Wayfarer Big Money:** reliable, but its ceiling is one Province per turn and it gets almost none of Colonnade.
- **Livery money:** similar speed and a lower ceiling.
- **Abundance–Wayfarer deck:** needs Rope early, 3+ Abundances and the free-Wayfarer habit. Its weaknesses are collisions between terminals, Hex bad luck, and the Coppers it adds.

**Against their best plan:**
- Against Wayfarer Big Money, this deck has about 5 Provinces plus 6–10 Colonnade VP by T13, while the money deck has 4. Corsair is the only attack and it misses Abundance and Rope.
- In the mirror, Colonnade and the Wayfarer pile are split. Get Wayfarers out early and start greening a turn sooner than you would against money.

**Challenged:** I re-tested a heavier deck with 3+ Cursed Villages and lots of draw. The Hexes and the draw-to-6 limit make it worse than relying on Mastermind and treasure payload. My pick loses to Hex bad luck (Misery twice, Plague) or to buying too many terminal Wayfarers. Once you have 4–5 Wayfarers, use Chariot Race as the Abundance trigger instead.

**Recommendation:** Play the Abundance–Wayfarer deck. It reaches double Provinces around T10–11 and takes most of Colonnade. That beats the Big Money clock, and nothing on the board can attack it effectively.

**Open:**
- **4/3:** Rope / Silver.
- **5/2:** Artificer / nothing. Mastermind is the alternative, and no card on this board is worth buying at $2.

**Buy priorities:**
1. Shuffle 2: Abundance at $4, Artificer or Mastermind at $5, and at $6 with Rope in play, Silver + Wayfarer.
2. Shuffles 3–4, every turn: Copper → free Wayfarer → Abundance, Cursed Village or Mastermind.
3. Stop adding Wayfarers at about 4.
4. Green when you reliably have $16 with 3 Buys, around T10–11.

**Ending:** Provinces most likely run out first. The Wayfarer pile can empty by around T12 in a mirror, so watch for a three-pile ending with Wayfarer + Chariot Race + Abundance. Colonnade will be empty by midgame.

**Watch out for:**
1. **Hexes:** Delusion blocks buying Actions for a turn, and Envy cuts Silver to $1.
2. **Terminal collisions:** play Cursed Village first.
3. **Colonnade race:** if the opponent copies the trick, contest Colonnade and the Wayfarer pile earlier.

With 3+ players, Colonnade grows to 6 VP per player, and Corsair and Road Network improve.

**Least sure of:** how fast the deck builds with only one or two villages. If Wayfarers keep colliding, lean on Mastermind and Abundance plus Chariot Race instead.

**Assumed:**
- Every Abundance in play triggers on the same Action gain.
- An Abundance stays in play until it triggers.
- Artificer can discard 0 cards to gain a $0 card.
- Wayfarer's "last other card" includes another copy of Wayfarer, so the $0 chain holds.
