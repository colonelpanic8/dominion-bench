# Kingdom analysis: horton-2026-09-semi2

My recommendation is a deck I'll call the **Souk carousel**. Gain Souks with Importer, and each Souk gain trashes 2 cards. Tireless keeps every played Souk on top of your deck, so they come back into one hand every other turn. Coin of the Realm and Squire supply the Actions to play them all. A built Souk turn makes about $18–23 with 5 Buys, which is two Provinces or more, and greening doesn't weaken it.

**Board:** Kingdom cards: Coin of the Realm $2, Squire $2, Importer $3, Village Green $4, Armory $4, Transmogrify $4, Souk $5, Ronin $5, Enlarge $5, Animal Fair $7. Landscapes: Trappers' Lodge (Ally) and Tireless (Trait, on Souk). No Colonies, no Shelters, 2 players, starting decks can be arranged for 4/3 or 5/2.

- **Scoring:** only Provinces, Duchies and Estates. There are no Attacks, so the game is a pure race, and it will almost certainly end on Provinces.
- **Favors:** Importer gives +4 Favors at setup but no Favors when played, so each player has exactly 5 for the whole game.
- **What the board is about:** Souk is the payload. It pays more the smaller your hand, and Tireless brings each played Souk back to the top of your deck. Ronin's draw-up-to-7 is the obvious engine card, but it works against Souk. The open question was where the Actions come from to play several Souks.

**Key interactions:**
- **Souk + Tireless: Souks come back together every other turn.**
  - A Souk discarded from play goes on top of your deck at the end of turn, after the next hand is drawn. So every Souk played on turn N is in the hand for turn N+2, as long as you draw nothing on turn N+1.
  - Souks never reshuffle, and Provinces never get between them. A Souk turn is just as good after you've bought 5 Provinces.
  - Each Souk played lowers your hand by one, so the Souks pay $3, $4, $5, $6 and $7 in turn.
- **Coin of the Realm and Squire supply the Actions.**
  - Play Coin of the Realm as a Treasure on the turn between Souk turns. On the Souk turn, call it after a Souk for +2 Actions. It doesn't take a hand slot, so it's the best enabler here.
  - Squire gives +$1 and +2 Actions (net +1).
  - Hand of 4 Souks + Squire, with 1 Coin of the Realm on the mat: Squire, then Souk ($4), Souk ($5), call Coin of the Realm, Souk ($6), Souk ($7). That's **$23 and 5 Buys**: Province, Province, Duchy, Coin of the Realm.
  - Hand of 4 Souks + a dead card, with 2 Coins on the mat: $3+4+5+6 = **$18 and 5 Buys**.
  - 5 Souks with 2 Coins on the mat: **$25 and 6 Buys**.
- **Importer gains Souks, and each gain trashes.**
  - Importer gains a card costing up to $5 at the start of your next turn, with your full hand there. Gaining a Souk then trashes up to 2 cards from that hand, usually Estates.
  - Each Importer play is roughly one Souk plus 2 trashes, about once per shuffle.
  - When you buy a Souk, leave Estates or extra Coppers unplayed and trash them.
- **Enlarge as a converter.** Each play works twice: this turn and at the start of next turn.
  - Estate → Importer, Copper → Coin of the Realm or Squire, Silver or Importer → Souk.
  - Late in the game, Gold → Province and Duchy → Gold.
- **Trappers' Lodge:** spend your 5 Favors topdecking early Souks and Importers so they get played immediately. Don't topdeck anything on the turn before a Souk turn: it lands on top of the Souks and pushes one out of the Souk hand.

**Other cards:**
- **Ronin:** works against the plan. Drawing up to 7 makes every Souk worth about $0. Drawing on an off-turn also pulls the Souks into a hand where you can't play them; they get discarded from hand and lose the Tireless loop. Its partners would be villages plus Gold. Skip it in the Souk plan.
- **Village Green:** a backup enabler. Play it for "next turn" on an off-turn to get +2 Actions at the start of the Souk turn, but its extra card costs each Souk $1. Its discard reaction does nothing here: nothing on the board discards except Clean-up.
- **Animal Fair:** +$4, plus a Buy per empty pile. Once the Souk pile is empty it's a decent off-turn card, gained free by trashing a spent Importer or Squire. Little use before that.
- **Transmogrify:** early it turns an Estate into an Importer or Silver in hand. At the start of a Souk turn it can turn the junk card into a Squire. Marginal.
- **Armory:** gains Importer, Coin of the Realm or Squire onto your deck, but it's a terminal and slow. No real partner here.
- **Squire's when-trashed text:** it gains an Attack card, and the Kingdom has none, so it does nothing.

**Ratings check:** Souk is rated +4.36, and I agree. Tireless is −1.75, but here it's what makes the plan work. Importer (+0.71) and Coin of the Realm (−0.41) are better than their ratings here, as the Souk gainer and enabler. Ronin (+2.13) is worse here because it clashes with Souk. Armory (−2.24) and Animal Fair (−1.15) are about as weak as rated.

### Build lines

These are paper estimates with average draws, not simulations.

- **Souk carousel** (4/3 opening, Importer/Silver):
  - T1–2: Importer (topdecked with a Favor, so you play it T2) and Silver.
  - T3–4: Importer gains a Souk (trash 2 Estates, topdeck the Souk). At $5 buy Souk and trash what you didn't play. At $3–4 buy a 2nd Importer or Squire.
  - T5–8: keep Importers gaining Souks, buy Souk at $5, and use spare Buys on Coin of the Realm.
  - By about T8 you have 4 Souks, 2 Importers, 1–2 Squires, 2 Coins of the Realm, and roughly 3 Estates and 2–3 Coppers trashed.
  - First $18+ Souk turn around T9–10, then one every other turn for 2 Provinces (+ Duchy). Off-turns play the Coins and buy a Duchy, Gold or the 5th Souk.
- **Ronin money** (the Big Money + X baseline):
  - Open Ronin/Silver (5/2) or Silver/Silver into Ronin.
  - Ronin is a Shadow card: after each shuffle it sits at the bottom of your deck and can be played from there, so it never collides with itself.
  - Like Library money, it first hits $6 around T5–6 and $8 around T7–8, and has about 4 Provinces by T14–15.
- **Ronin/Squire engine with Gold:** Squire and Coin of the Realm as villages, Ronins refilling to 7, Squire's Buys.
  - Its money depends on Golds still in hand, and it has to buy each one.
  - It probably reaches about $16 only around T12+.

**Plans considered:**
- **Souk carousel:** the line above. It needs 4–5 of the 10 Souks, and the opponent wants them too. Its weakness is a Souk turn where you're short of Actions.
- **Ronin money:** steady, but capped at one Province per turn, and it gets out-scored from about T10.
- **Ronin/Squire engine:** its payload is weak, and it can't use the board's best card.

**Recommendation:** play the Souk carousel. It reaches two-Province turns by about T10, and its Souk turns don't suffer from greening, so it beats Ronin money comfortably.

**Against their best plan:**
- **The real matchup is the mirror.** There are no attacks, so the only fight is over the Souk pile.
  - Get Souks early: buy one at every $5, and point every Importer play at Souk until you have 4–5.
  - Once the Souk pile is empty, Animal Fair becomes a reasonable off-turn card.
  - Player 1 has the tempo edge. Track Provinces closely, because each Souk turn takes 2–3 of them, so the penultimate-Province rule comes up in big chunks.
- **Against Ronin money,** you're behind until about T10, then you overtake them and stay ahead.

**Challenged:**
- **The Ronin engine** fails because each Ronin draws back only as many cards as you've played, and Golds in hand count toward the 7. It has to buy its money; Souk produces money with no Treasure at all.
- **How the carousel loses:**
  - Getting only 3 Souks (a $12–14 turn, about one Province).
  - A Souk turn without Coins on the mat: one Souk gives $3.
  - Drawing cards on an off-turn by mistake, which breaks the loop.
  - The opponent can't do anything to stop you except win the Souk race.

**Open:**
- **4/3:** Importer/Silver, with the T1 buy topdecked. Importer/Importer is close: more Souk gains, but less money and a risk of drawing both together.
- **5/2:** Enlarge/Squire. Enlarge turns Estates into Importers on T3 and T4, and Squire gives Actions or a Silver. Souk/Coin of the Realm is worse, because a T1 Souk has nothing left in hand to trash.
- **If you get to choose:** I'd take 4/3, because it gains a Souk by about T3–4.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Early:** Souk at $5 (trashing unplayed junk), and Importer at $3–4 until you have 2. Gain Souks with every Importer play.
- **Then:** Coin of the Realm with spare Buys (aim for 2–3), and 1–2 Squires.
- **Enlarge:** a reasonable buy at $5 once you have 4 Souks.
- **Greening:** start on the first Souk turn. Souk turns buy Provinces, and off-turns buy Duchies. Keep buying Coins of the Realm so the Actions don't run out.
- **Late:** a spare Importer or Squire becomes a free Animal Fair, and Enlarge turns Gold into Provinces.

**Ending:** on Provinces, around T14–16 in the mirror. The Souk pile will empty, but no other pile is likely to, so a three-pile ending is unlikely.

**Watch out for:**
1. Losing the Souk split.
2. Missing Actions on a Souk turn: buy enough Coins of the Realm.
3. Off-turn draws (Village Green played for now, Ronin) or topdecked gains that push Souks out of the Souk hand.

**What would flip it:** with 3+ players the Souk pile splits 3–4 Souks each, which would push toward Ronin money plus 2–3 Souks. Without the Tireless loop, Souk would only be a strong terminal.

**Least sure of:** how reliable the Souk turns are.
- The plan assumes about 2 Actions on hand per Souk turn from Coins of the Realm played on off-turns. Once Provinces dilute the off-turn hands, Coins of the Realm turn up less often.
- **Test:** count off-turn hands with no Coin of the Realm. If that happens more than about 1 in 4, add Squires or Village Green.

**Assumed:**
- **Tireless timing:** "at end of turn" means after the Clean-up draw. That's why the Souks skip the next hand and the turns alternate.
- **Ronin:** as a Shadow it's placed at the bottom of your deck on a shuffle and can be played from there.
- **Favors:** 5 per player in total, since Importer has no +Favor line.
- **Animal Fair:** its alternative cost still uses a Buy.
- **Squire:** trashing it gains nothing, since the Kingdom has no Attack cards.
- **Not checked:** the turn estimates are averages I worked out by hand, not simulations.
