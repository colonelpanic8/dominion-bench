## Beyond the key
- "Souk carousel": with correct Tireless timing, Souks played on turn N come back together in the hand for turn N+2 if nothing is drawn on N+1: plausible. Tireless sets them aside and puts them on the deck after Clean-up, so the next Clean-up draw takes them.
- Squire + 4 Souks with 1 Coin of the Realm on the mat makes $23 and 5 Buys: plausible. The arithmetic is $1+$4+$5+$6+$7, with Coin of the Realm called for +2 Actions after the second Souk.
- Importer's start-of-turn Souk gain trashes 2 cards from a full hand: plausible. Souk trashes on gain, and Importer gains while the turn's hand is in hand.
- Ronin makes Souks worth about $0, so it is incompatible with them: doubtful. Souk's -$1 per card is counted when Souk is played, so Souks played before Ronin keep their value. Drawing Souks on an off-turn is a real cost, but it's an ordering problem, not a clash.
- Armory has no real partner: doubtful. With deck-stacking, Armory gains onto the deck, which sets up an immediate Transmogrify or Importer. That is the designer's opening.
- 5 Favors per player (1 base + Importer's 4), spent on Trappers' Lodge: plausible.
- Don't topdeck with Trappers' Lodge on the turn before a Souk turn: plausible. The gained card lands on top of the Souks and pushes one out of the next hand.
- Ronin as a Shadow goes to the bottom on a shuffle and can be played from the deck: plausible per Shadow rules.
- Squire's when-trashed text does nothing (no Attacks), Village Green's reaction does nothing (no discard effects), and Animal Fair's alternative cost still needs a Buy: plausible on card text.
- Enlarge Silver/Importer→Souk, Gold→Province, Duchy→Gold: plausible. Each is a gain of up to $2 more.
- Opening 5/2 with Souk is weak because a T1 Souk has no cards left to trash: plausible. Five Coppers are played to reach $5.

## Summary
The analysis gets the Tireless timing right, which avoids the misplay the designer flagged. But it builds the opposite deck: a Souk rush that skips Ronin, the designer's core card. It also dismisses the Armory/Transmogrify/Importer opening. Its Souk carousel arithmetic holds up on card text, but it falls into the "rush Souks" trap and matches almost none of the core rubric.
