## Beyond the key
- Tent puts itself back on your deck, so a Tent you play is in every hand as a permanent +$2: plausible. "When you discard this from play, you may put it onto your deck."
- Pile order is read correctly. Rotation moves every copy of the top card to the bottom, so Hill Fort needs two rotations to surface, and once it is rotated away it takes three to bring back: plausible. The grader notes ask for credit on exactly this.
- A played Reckless Guide stays on the Tavern mat; a called one is discarded from play and returns to its pile: plausible. Reckless returns a card to its pile only when it is discarded from play.
- Calling a Guide discards your hand, which breaks Tent's loop: plausible. A Tent discarded from hand isn't discarded from play.
- Hill Fort gaining a Guide into hand is "the same as a Lab," and "4 Hill Forts ≈ 4 Labs plus up to 4 villages each turn": doubtful. Hill Fort is terminal, so playing the gained Guide needs a spare Action. And each Hill Fort chooses one mode, so it gives the Lab effect or a delayed village, not both.
- Exploration pays out on the turn you buy it: plausible. Buying a Project isn't gaining a card.
- Destrier cost maths (Ducat then Destrier = $7; a second Destrier brings the total to $11): plausible from "$1 less per card you've gained this turn."
- Cabin Boy can trash itself to gain a Stronghold when Stronghold is on top of Forts: plausible. Stronghold is a Duration, and only the top card of Forts can be gained.
- On a 5/2, buying Ducat with the $2 can't trash a Copper: plausible. Both Coppers are already in play, not in hand.
- Skip Harbinger and Wishing Well: plausible. They add little next to Destrier and Guide.

## Summary
The analysis gets the shape right. Forts is the engine, the Hill Forts are fought over, Reckless Guides supply the actions, and Ducat/Hill Fort gains make Destrier cheap. It also understands the pile rotation well. It misses Horton's Garrison emphasis entirely: it rotates past Garrisons and opens Tent/Steward instead of Tent/Exploration. So it gets the Hill Fort race right but not the designer's route into it.
