# Governor + Friendly, Way of the Owl (2 players, no Colonies/Shelters)

**Board:** The board is about Governor. It is a non-terminal card that draws or gains, and here it has Friendly on it. With Friendly, any Governor you hold unplayed turns into two at the end of the turn. Governor's remodel option turns a Gold in hand into a Province, so Gold is effectively Province money. Leprechaun (and late on, Wish) supply that Gold, and Forager supplies +Buy. There are no attacks. The only junk on the board is what Leprechaun's Hexes do to the person playing it. What's still open:
- How quickly the 10-card Governor pile gets split up.
- How much you give away, since every Governor play also helps the opponent.

**Key interactions:**
- **Friendly + Governor:** at Clean-up, discard an unplayed Governor from your hand to gain another. That's one free Governor per turn without using a Buy. In a mirror the pile is gone by about turn 7–9, so the split is decided early.
- **Governor (remodel) + Gold:** trash Gold ($6) and gain a Province ($8). Only one Governor play is needed, so one fully drawn turn with 3 Golds and 3 spare Governor plays makes 3 Provinces. Refill Golds with Governor's Gold option (you gain Gold, the opponent gains Silver) or with Leprechaun.
- **Throne Room / Flagship + Governor:** two Governor plays, plus +2 Actions. This is the board's village, so Merchant Camp is barely needed.
- **Leprechaun + a built turn:** with 7 cards in play (the Governors, Forager, Flagship, Throne Room and Leprechaun itself all count; returned Experiments don't), Leprechaun gives a Gold and a Wish instead of a Hex. Wish then gains a Gold to your hand, and a Governor turns that into a Province in the same turn. Play Leprechaun last. Before your turns reach 7 cards in play it's a Gold plus a Hex, and some Hexes are nasty: Plague, Misery, or Locusts hitting a Copper, which gives you a Curse.
- **Forager + remodel:** each Gold you remodel goes to the trash. Once the trash holds Copper, Gold, Silver and Anvil, Forager is a non-terminal "+$3–4 +Buy, trash a card". That +Buy is how you cash in a big turn.
- **Way of the Owl + Throne Room / Flagship / Leprechaun:** a Throne Room with no Governor to double isn't dead. Play it as "draw until 6 cards".

**Other cards:**
- **Experiment:** Anvil or the opponent's remodel (Estate → $3) gain a pair. They are one-shot draw that gives the opponent nothing. An unplayed one can be remodeled into a Governor. Filler.
- **Anvil:** discard a Copper to gain a $4 card (Throne Room/Flagship) or an Experiment pair. Also adds a Treasure name for Forager. A minor builder.
- **Merchant Camp:** needed only if you run 2 or more terminals (Flagship, Leprechaun, Tactician). Its topdecking just repeats itself. Low.
- **Advisor:** none. The opponent picks the discard, so they'll take your Gold. Governor draws better.
- **Tactician:** none. It discards the Governor you were holding for Friendly, and Forager is the better +Buy. Skip.
- **Way of the Owl:** covered above. Its other use is Owl-playing a Leprechaun that would hit a Hex. That dodges the Hex but also loses the Gold.

**Ratings check:**
- Governor 5.50 is consistent with my view.
- Throne Room 2.73 and Forager 2.68 are consistent too.
- I rate Leprechaun (−1.37) higher here, because Governor's remodel turns its Gold into a Province and engine turns reach 7 in play.
- I rate Friendly (−0.11) much higher here, because it's on the best card on the board.
- I rate Merchant Camp (−2.39) and Tactician (−1.88) low as well.

### Build lines (average draws, solo)

- **Governor engine (5/2):**
  - T1 Governor, T2 pass. Nothing at $2 helps.
  - T3: hand is Gov + $3. Hold Gov, buy **Forager**, Friendly → 2 Gov.
  - T4: $4 → Silver (or Throne Room).
  - T5: Forager trashes an Estate. Play Gov to draw (opponent +1 card), reach $6, buy Gov → 3.
  - T6: hold Gov → 4, and $3 → Experiment pair or Anvil.
  - T7–8: buy or Friendly the 5th–6th Governor. The pile runs out about here. Add Throne Room/Flagship and a second Forager.
  - T8–10: Gold mode to stock 3–4 Golds. Add 1–2 Leprechauns once your turns reach 7 in play.
  - Built turn, about T11, from 5 Gov, Throne Room, Flagship, Forager, 3 Gold, 2 Silver, 4 Copper and 1 Estate (≈18 cards): about 4 Governor plays draw the deck (opponent +4 cards). The remaining 3 plays remodel 3 Golds into 3 Provinces. Then $4 Silver + $4 Copper + $3 Forager = $11 with 2 Buys buys Province + Silver.
  - That's about 4 Provinces in one turn. After that, alternate a Gold-refill turn with a remodel turn, averaging about 2 Provinces per turn.
- **Governor Big Money (baseline):**
  - 5/2: Gov, then Silver/Gold, holding Govs for Friendly up to about 3.
  - Then mostly Gold mode, remodeling a Gold into a Province on turns short of $8.
  - First $8 around T7–8, 4 Provinces around T13–14, about 1 Province per turn with no +Buy.
- **Leprechaun money + Governor:**
  - 4/3: Silver/Leprechaun. A Gold on every Leprechaun play, with Governor turning Golds into Provinces.
  - About a Hex every other turn early. Plague and Misery cost about 2–4 VP over the game.
  - Fast Golds, but it falls behind in the Governor split and has no +Buy.

**Plans considered:**
- **Governor engine:** Friendly-race Governors, Forager for +Buy and thinning, Throne Room/Flagship to double Governor, Golds remodeled into Provinces, Leprechaun/Wish late.
  - Needs: 5+ Governors and 3+ Golds by about T10.
  - Weakness: losing the Governor split, and draw-mode gifts.
- **Governor-BM:** simpler and scores sooner.
  - Weakness: capped at about 1 Province per turn. Gold mode gives the opponent a Silver every play.
- **Leprechaun money:** cheap Golds.
  - Weakness: Hexes, and it gives up the Governor race.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Engine vs. the engine mirror:**
  - The Governor split decides it. A 5/5 split is even, and 6/4 is a large edge.
  - Your draw-mode gifts matter little there, because both decks draw everything.
  - Green a little earlier than usual, since remodel turns empty the Provinces fast.
- **Engine vs. Governor-BM:** the BM player is about 2 Provinces ahead around T10. The engine's 3–4-Province turns from T11 overtake that.
  - Draw-mode gifts are what matters most here: +4 cards puts a money deck on $8.
  - Use remodel mode wherever you can (it gives them almost nothing: a Gold can't become a $7 card) and Gold mode only when needed.
- **Runner-up (Gov-BM) vs. the engine:** it needs about 5 Provinces before T12 and doesn't get them.

**Challenged:**
- **Retested plan:** Governor-BM with Friendly but no support. It's the engine minus Forager and Throne Room/Flagship. Those cards cost $3–4, and +Buy plus doubled Governor plays are what lift the ceiling above 1 Province per turn. So the engine still wins.
- **How the pick loses:**
  - Holding too few Governors for Friendly early, and losing the split at 3–7.
  - Playing Leprechaun before turns reach 7 in play and eating Plague or Misery.
  - Overusing draw mode against a money deck.
- **What the opponent can read:** a 5/2 Governor opening is public. Their counter is to open 5/2 Governor as well, which leads to the mirror.

**Recommendation:** play the Governor engine. Win the Friendly race first, then use Forager, Throne Room/Flagship and Gold-into-Province remodels. Against any plan it holds the board's strongest card, and a mirror comes down to the Governor split, which the opening below protects.

**Open:**
- **5/2 (recommended):** Governor / nothing. Holding it on T3/T4 doubles it with Friendly.
- **4/3:** Silver / Forager, to hit $5 for Governor on T3–4. Throne Room/Forager is the alternative if the opponent isn't contesting Governor.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffles 2–3:** Governor at $5+ (Friendly every turn you can spare one). Forager ×1–2 at $3. Throne Room or Flagship at $4. Silver otherwise.
- **After the Governor pile is gone:** Gold at $6–7, and a Gold-mode Governor play whenever you don't need to draw. Add 1–2 Leprechauns once 7 in play is routine.
- **Greening:** start once a turn can remodel 2+ Golds (about T10–11). Use spare Buys on Duchies late.

**Ending:**
- Provinces run out around T13–16.
- The Governor pile empties early (pile 1). Duchies could become pile 2. Experiment piles refill and Wish isn't in the Supply, so a three-pile ending is unlikely.
- Watch the Penultimate Province Rule: the opponent can take Provinces without having $8. Count their Golds and Governors.

**Watch out for:**
- Losing the Governor split. Don't play your T3/T4 Governor; hold it for Friendly.
- Leprechaun Hexes before turns reach 7 in play.
- Draw-mode gifts to a money opponent. With 3+ players every Governor play gifts each opponent, so Governor and the engine lose some value.

**Least sure of:**
- That 5/2 Governor clearly beats a 4/3 Silver/Forager opening. It rests on Friendly compounding faster than an earlier Forager.
- The T11 "4-Province turn" estimate.
- A test would be to play both openings out against a 5/2 Governor mirror.

**Assumed:**
- Friendly's "discard a Friendly card" means from your hand, the usual ruling, so a Governor you've played can't be used.
- Leprechaun counts itself among the 7 cards in play, and Experiments that returned to their pile don't count.
- Flagship's replay carries over to a later turn if you play no Action after it.
- There is no Supply card at exactly $7, so the opponent's remodel can't upgrade a Gold.
