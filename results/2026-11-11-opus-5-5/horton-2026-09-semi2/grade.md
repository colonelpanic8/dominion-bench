## Beyond the key
- Each player has 5 Favors for the game (1 starting + Importer's +4 at setup), and nothing gives more: plausible. Importer's Favors come only at setup, and it is the only Liaison.
- Squire's on-trash gain does nothing: plausible. There is no Attack in the Supply.
- Souk income formula 7k − kt − k(k−1)/2 for k Souks played with t other cards held: plausible. The arithmetic follows from −$1 per card in hand as each Souk leaves the hand.
- Importer gains Souk at the start of turn with a full hand, so the on-gain trash hits 2 junk cards, while a bought Souk trashes nothing: plausible. It follows from the timing.
- Treasures held in hand cost $1 per Souk played, so Treasure is negative here: plausible. Treasures can't be played during the Action phase.
- Play Ronin first, then the Souks last with about 1 card left: plausible. It handles the Ronin/Souk hand-size tension the grader note asks about.
- Animal Fair bought by trashing a spent Importer or Squire, then converted by Transmogrify ($7→$8) or Enlarge into a Province: plausible. It matches the cost and gain text.
- Transmogrify or Enlarge on Village Green gains a Souk, which trashes 2 more cards: plausible. $4→$5 (to hand) and $4→up to $6 respectively, and it is a gain.
- Ronin, as a Shadow, sits at the bottom after a shuffle and is playable from the deck: plausible. That is the Shadow rule.
- Village Green's reaction does nothing because nothing discards: plausible. No card on the board discards other than Clean-up.
- Trappers' Lodge on the first Importer-gained Souks: plausible. It is a gain, so a Favor puts it on the deck.
- Armory is "slow" and outclassed by Importer: doubtful. Armory gains to the deck the same turn and was the designer's turn-1 play; Importer is a turn later.
- Coin of the Realm is a weaker village than Squire because it clogs the hand: doubtful. The cost per Souk is real, but the designer uses Coin of the Realm as the deck's Actions.

## Summary
It reads Tireless timing correctly and uses Village Green to bring the Souks back, which is the hardest rules point on the board. But it builds a Souk engine with Ronin as an extra rather than the Ronin draw-to-X deck, prioritizes Souks over Ronin, and replaces the designer's Armory/Transmogrify/Importer/Coin of the Realm opening with Importer/Importer.
