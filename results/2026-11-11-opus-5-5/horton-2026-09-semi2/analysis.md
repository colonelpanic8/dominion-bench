# Kingdom analysis: horton-2026-09-semi2

## Recommendation in brief

Build a thin engine around the Tireless Souks. Open Importer/Importer on 4/3. Use the Importers to gain Souks, trash Coppers and Estates hard, add Village Greens, Squires and one Ronin, and start taking 2–3 Provinces a turn from about turn 12.

## Inventory

- **Kingdom:** Squire $2, Coin of the Realm $2, Importer $3, Village Green $4, Armory $4, Transmogrify $4, Souk $5, Ronin $5, Enlarge $5, Animal Fair $7 (or trash an Action card instead of paying).
- **Landscapes:**
  - **Tireless on Souk:** after you play a Souk, it goes back on top of your deck at the end of your turn.
  - **Trappers' Lodge:** spend a Favor to put a gained card on top of your deck.
- **Favors:** Importer gives every player +4 Favors at setup, on top of the 1 everyone starts with, so each player has **5 Favors for the whole game**. Nothing on the board gives more.
- **Setup:** no Colonies, no Shelters, 2 players. Players choose their split, 4/3 or 5/2.
- **Prices:** the key cards are crowded at $4–5. $3 has Importer, which matters. $6+ has only Animal Fair, which you get by trashing an Action card rather than paying.
- **No Attack cards in the Supply.** So when Squire is trashed, its "gain an Attack card" does nothing.

**Ronin Big Money** (the baseline):

- Ronin can be played from your deck, so you get one guaranteed 7-card hand each shuffle.
- Open Ronin plus Squire or nothing (5/2), or Silver/Silver (4/3).
- Gold around T3–5, first Province around T7–8, 4 Provinces by about T14, all 8 alone by about T19–20.
- Adding a Souk or Armory doesn't help much: Treasures held in hand cut Souk's money, and they are all terminals that compete with Ronin.

**Enlarge good-stuff:**

- Open Enlarge/Squire on 5/2.
- Enlarge turns Estates into $4 cards and Silvers into $5 cards. Late on it turns Gold or Animal Fair into Province.
- It is steady, but it gains one card at a time and has no strong payload. It is better as a part of the engine than as a plan of its own.

**Board:** Souk is the payload: +$7 and +Buy, minus $1 for each card left in your hand. Tireless puts each played Souk back on top of your deck, so any draw next turn picks it up again. The board is about building a deck so thin that every Souk is played with an almost empty hand. Every card you still hold, including Coppers and greens, costs $1 per Souk played after it.

**Key interactions:**

- **Tireless Souk + small draw.**
  - Played Souks return to the top of your deck after the next hand has been drawn.
  - Village Green set for "next turn" (+1 Card at the start of the turn), Ronin, or a dead turn with no draw (the next Clean-up draws it) brings them back.
  - Result: one Souk every other turn with no draw, and every Souk every turn with Village Green or Ronin.
  - k Souks with t other cards left in hand make 7k − kt − k(k−1)/2. That is $18 for 3 Souks with t=0, $22 for 4 Souks, and $12 for 3 Souks with t=2.
- **Importer → Souk.**
  - Importer gains Souk at the start of your turn, when your hand is full. Souk's on-gain trash then removes 2 junk cards.
  - Trappers' Lodge (5 Favors) puts the Souk on your deck so it is played next turn instead of a shuffle later.
  - Two Importers mean 2 Souks and 4 cards trashed by about T6. Buying Souk with Coppers trashes nothing, because your hand is empty by then.
- **Treasure is negative here.** A Copper held while 3 Souks are played costs $3 and adds $1. Trash Coppers and don't buy Silver after the opening. Squire (+$1, played from hand) is the right village; Coin of the Realm is not.
- **Animal Fair as free payload.** Trash a spent Importer or spare Squire instead of paying $7. You get +$4 that doesn't depend on hand size.
- **Late conversion.**
  - Transmogrify turns Animal Fair ($7) into a Province in hand.
  - Enlarge turns Animal Fair or Gold into a Province, and Village Green into a Souk.
  - Transmogrify on a Village Green gains a Souk into your hand and trashes 2 more cards.

**Other cards:**

- **Ronin:** a Shadow, so it is always playable from your deck. Its draw-to-7 collects the Souks stacked on top. Play it first, before the cards that shrink your hand.
- **Village Green:** use its "next turn" mode, which draws the top Souk without spending an Action. Its reaction does nothing here, because nothing on the board discards.
- **Coin of the Realm:** a working village, but it sits in hand as a Treasure when the Souks are played. Weaker than Squire here.
- **Squire:** +2 Actions or +2 Buys, and its +$1 comes from playing it from hand. Its on-trash text does nothing (no Attacks).
- **Armory:** gains a Village Green or Squire onto your deck. It is a slow, terminal gainer, and Importer does the same job better.
- **Transmogrify:** turns Estate into Importer, Village Green into Souk, or Animal Fair into Province, each gained to hand. Good secondary trasher.
- **Enlarge:** turns Estate into Village Green, Silver into Souk, Gold into Province. Optional, mostly in the endgame.

**Plans considered:**

- **Tireless Souk engine:** about $8 with 2 Buys on T6, double Provinces from about T11–12. Needs 4+ Souks, 3 Village Greens or Squires, and a thin deck. Weakness: greens in hand cut Souk's money, and the Souk pile is contested.
- **Ronin Big Money:** first Province around T7–8, 4 Provinces by about T14. Needs only 1–2 Ronins. Weakness: one Province per turn at most.
- **Enlarge good-stuff:** steady but slow (see build lines).

**Against their best plan:**

- **Against Ronin Big Money:**
  - By about T12–13 the money deck has about 3 Provinces.
  - The engine then takes 2+ a turn over T12–14 and finishes about 6 Provinces to 3.
  - The engine wins if it is online by T12–13. Ronin Big Money's only route is greening before T10, and it lacks the Buys to pile out.
- **Mirror:**
  - Souk is the key pile, and the 10 cards will split about 5–5.
  - Two Importers gaining Souks on T5–6 is how you win that split.
  - Green earlier than you would against money, and take the Duchies.
  - Souk, Squire and Village Green could empty for a three-pile ending. Each empty pile also gives Animal Fair +1 Buy.

**Challenged:**

- *Ronin Big Money again:* even with a Souk added for Buys, Treasure in hand cancels Souk's money. It stays at one Province a turn and is not competitive with an engine that comes online around T12.
- *How the pick loses:*
  - The deck isn't thinned (Coppers left over), or greening starts too early. Provinces in hand cut 3–4 Souks by $1 each.
  - So green in big bursts and end fast. Use Transmogrify or Enlarge to turn Animal Fairs into Provinces, so greening doesn't depend on Souk money.
  - An opponent who takes 6+ Souks also hurts you.

## Plan, openings and endgame

**Recommendation:** the Tireless Souk engine.

- It beats the money baseline by about 3 turns.
- In a mirror, it gives you the edge on the Souk split.

**Open:**

- **4/3:** Importer / Importer. Your best option; the second Importer could be Transmogrify instead.
- **5/2:** Enlarge / Squire. Enlarge thins Estates into Village Greens or Importers. Souk on T1/T2 trashes nothing.

**Buy priorities:**

1. Souk, gained by Importer or bought, using Trappers' Lodge on the first 2–3 Souks.
2. Village Green and Squire, enough villages for your terminals.
3. One Ronin.
4. Importer #3, at $3.
5. Animal Fair, by trashing a spent Importer.
6. Transmogrify.

Never buy Treasure after the opening. Green once you reliably make $18+ with 3+ Buys (about T11–12), then end it within 3–4 turns.

**Ending:**

- Most likely Provinces, taken 2–3 at a time.
- In a mirror, watch for a three-pile ending: Souk, plus Squire or Village Green.

**Watch out for:**

- **The opponent racing for Souks.** Take a third Importer and your Souks earlier.
- **Greening too early.** It cuts every Souk you play after it.
- **The Favor budget.** Spend all 5 on early Souks and Ronin, not Provinces.

**What would flip it:**

- **3+ players:** fewer Souks for each player, so the Enlarge or Ronin-money plan gains value.
- **The opponent going Ronin Big Money:** keep building; don't panic-green.

**Least sure of:**

- How fast the engine comes online. The ~T11–12 double-Province turn assumes Importers gain Souks on T5–6 and Coppers get trashed fast.
- The test: play T1–12 on paper with bad shuffles, such as both Importers on the same turn, or Importers missing T3–4.

**Assumed:**

- Tireless returns Souk *after* Clean-up's draw, so a played Souk is not in the very next hand.
- Importer gives no Favors except at setup, so each player has 5.
- Squire's on-trash text does nothing, because there is no Attack in the Supply.
- Animal Fair's alternative cost applies only when buying it.
- Ronin, as a Shadow, sits at the bottom of the deck after a shuffle and can be played from there.
- I checked community ratings only after forming this view. Souk (4.36) is the board's top card, and Tireless on it is much stronger than its average rating (−1.75). Armory's low rating (−2.24) matches what it does here.
