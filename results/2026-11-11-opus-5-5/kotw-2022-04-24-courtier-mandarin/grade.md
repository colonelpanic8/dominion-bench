## Beyond the key
- Revealing Village Green to Courtier doesn't use it, so you can play it afterwards: plausible. Courtier only reveals the card, which stays in hand.
- Courtier on Tunnel gives 2 picks: plausible. Tunnel is a Victory–Reaction, so it has two types.
- Mandarin topdecks a Tunnel, then Gamble discards it to gain a Gold, netting $1 and a Gold: plausible. Gamble's discard is "other than during Clean-up", so Tunnel triggers. Mandarin's +$3 minus Gamble's $2 leaves $1, and Gamble's +Buy replaces the Buy it used.
- Gamble on a Village Green or Barge in the Buy phase sets up next turn: plausible. Village Green's reaction plays it when discarded, and both Durations can take the "start of your next turn" option.
- Cardinal backfires because discarding the opponent's Tunnel gains them a Gold and discarding Village Green plays it: plausible. Cardinal's discard isn't during Clean-up, so both reactions trigger.
- Exiled cards still score and count for Silk Road, so Displace upgrades green cards without losing VP: plausible. Exiled cards are still yours at game end. Tunnel ($3) → Duchy and Silk Road ($4) → Gold fit "up to $2 more".
- Lurker played as Way of the Goat gives no +Action: plausible. A Way replaces the card's whole effect.
- Mandarin re-Gambling the topdecked Golds "pays only with 5 or more Golds in play", so Mandarin is mediocre here: doubtful. It judges the on-gain only as Gamble fuel or a one-off repeat. With Courtier's +Buy, gaining a Mandarin every turn replays the whole Gold stack each turn. That is the key's combo, and it clocked $118.
- Tunnel, Silk Road and Duchy make a three-pile ending a real risk: plausible. Tunnel and Silk Road have 8 cards each in 2-player, and both players want them.

## Summary
The analysis gets the frame right: Courtier gains Golds, Village Green is its best reveal, Steward thins, +Buy is built in, and it avoids Displace and Cardinal. It misses that Mandarin is the other half of the engine. Buying a Mandarin every turn topdecks the Golds in play for a replay, and Mandarin's play topdecks Village Green for the next Courtier. So it builds a solid Gold-flood but not the explosive loop the key's strongest voice clocked.
