## Beyond the key
- Witch's Hut discarding a Weaver plus another Action (including a Horse) curses the opponent, so Hut is a real junker here: plausible. Both discarded cards are Actions, and the discarded Weaver still gets played.
- Footpad's attack is weak against Weaver decks because a Weaver discarded to it gets played: plausible. That discard is "other than in Clean-up".
- River Shrine turns that don't play Treasures can gain Hostelry at Clean-up and discard the held Coppers for Horses: plausible. Unplayed Treasures are still in hand at the start of Clean-up.
- Artificer's gain goes onto your deck and Footpad's rule draws it immediately: plausible. The gain happens in the Action phase.
- Stonemason overpaying $4 (or $4 after the Prophecy) gains 2 Hostelries, Weavers, Artificers or Witch's Huts, and Stonemason on Copper trashes it cleanly: plausible. Matches overpay and "costing less than" text.
- Unpaid Triumph Debt turns Change into +$3: plausible. Change's first line.
- Under the Prophecy, Weaver's "up to $4" reaches Gold, and River Shrine "reaches Gold": wrong. Gold drops to $5, still above $4; River Shrine's limit isn't raised. Duchy and the $5 Kingdom cards do come into range.
- Change on a Triumph Estate gains a Province for "6 Debt, 7 Debt before the Prophecy": wrong. The difference is $6 either way ($2→$8, or $1→$7).
- Triumph's VP is capped by the 8 Estates both players share: plausible. In 2 players the Estate pile has 8, and Triumph scores nothing once it's empty.
- Footpad's rule also fires on the opponent's gains during your Action phase: plausible. "When you gain a card in an Action phase" doesn't say whose; it means your Witch's Hut Curses draw the opponent a card, which the analysis doesn't note.
- It doesn't flag the lose-track or Stop-Moving complications with a discarded Weaver, so there is no extra credit under the grader notes.

## Summary
The analysis finds Horton's main engine: Hostelry Horses plus Footpad's draw-on-gain, Weaver played from Artificer or Witch's Hut discards, and Triumph bought with Flourishing Trade's spare Actions. But it badly underestimates the payoff and timing, planning repeated 2–3-Triumph turns from about T12 instead of one ~55 VP turn around T7. It dismisses Exploration, and it misses the Change/Stonemason route to Alchemist and Vineyard, writing Vineyard off as Potion-locked.
