I worked only from files in this directory: the card text in the kingdom file, the repo context and `data/card-glicko`. The build lines are hand estimates with average draws, not simulations.

**Board:** Rope, Cursed Village, Livery, Mastermind, Captain, Wayfarer, Chariot Race, Abundance, Artificer and Corsair, with Road Network (Project) and Colonnade (Landmark). Two players, no Colonies, no Shelters. The board is about turning junk into engine parts and getting more than one Buy. The only real village is Cursed Village, which gives you a Hex each time you gain it. Wayfarer is the main draw, Livery's Horses add one-shot draw, and Artificer + Cursed Village is a strong pair for filtering junk out of your hand. Only Rope and Abundance give +Buy, and Rope is the only trasher, a slow one. Colonnade adds a 12 VP pool of 2 VP tokens, for buying an Action while a copy of it is in play. That rewards decks that buy copies of what they play.

### Inventory
- **$3:** Chariot Race.
- **$4:** Rope and Abundance, both Treasure-Durations.
- **$5:** Cursed Village, Livery, Mastermind, Artificer, Corsair, plus the Road Network Project.
- **$6:** Captain, and Wayfarer at $6 by default (its cost changes, see below).
- **Non-Supply:** Horses, from Livery.
- **Hexes and States:** Cursed Village's Hexes can give Curses (Plague, Locusts), Misery (−2 or −4 VP), and Deluded (you can't buy Actions next Buy phase).
- **Crowded and empty prices:** most of the key cards are at $5. There is nothing useful at $2.
- **Starting deck and splits:** 7 Copper and 3 Estate, so turns 1–2 split 4/3 or 5/2.

**Scoring:** Provinces and Duchies, Colonnade (12 VP), Chariot Race's +1 VP tokens, and minus points from Hexes. Colonnade and Chariot Race VP add no card to your deck, so take them while you're still building. The game most likely ends on Provinces; a three-pile ending is unlikely.

### Key interactions
- **Artificer then Cursed Village:**
  - Play Artificer, discard 3–5 junk cards, and put a $3–5 card on top of your deck.
  - Then play Cursed Village, which draws your nearly empty hand back up to 6, including the card you just gained.
  - Net: about 4 junk cards become 6 fresh cards plus a free engine part, every time the two meet (from about turn 6).
  - Late in the game, discarding 5 or 8 cards to Artificer gains a Duchy or a Province. So green cards and Coppers become fuel.
- **Wayfarer's cost:** Wayfarer costs the same as the last other card gained that turn.
  - With Rope's Buy: buy Chariot Race or Silver ($3), then Wayfarer costs $3. That's $6 for two cards.
  - After Province + Livery: the Horse ($3) is the last gain, so a spare Buy gets Wayfarer for $3.
  - You can also buy a Copper first to make Wayfarer cost $0, but that adds a Copper to your deck.
- **Livery + any gain costing $4 or more:** you get a Horse (+2 Cards +1 Action, one use).
  - Two Liveries played, then Province + Province, gives 4 Horses, so the next turn draws 8 extra cards for free.
  - **Mastermind on Livery** plays it three times: +$9, and 3 Horses per $4+ gain. Mastermind's play happens at the start of the turn and uses no Action.
- **Mastermind on Wayfarer:** +9 Cards and 3 Silvers. Use it when Livery isn't in hand.
- **Captain + Chariot Race:** Captain's only usual target is Chariot Race, so Captain works out as:
  - now: a cantrip (it replaces itself in hand and keeps your Action);
  - at the start of next turn: +1 Card and +1 Action;
  - up to twice: +$1 and +1 VP from Chariot Race.
  - If you gained something this turn before playing it (e.g. with Artificer), Captain can instead play Wayfarer at $4 or less: +3 Cards and a Silver.
- **Colonnade + Chariot Race, Wayfarer or Livery:** "play X, buy X" earns 2 VP and needs no +Buy. A $3 Chariot Race is the cheapest way to trigger it. With Abundance, buying the Action also gives +1 Buy and +$3, so you can chain it.

### Other cards
- **Rope:** pairs with Wayfarer's cost trick and with Colonnade, and it's the only thinning. You want 2 or more.
- **Abundance:** pairs with buying Actions (Colonnade) and with Artificer's gains, which trigger it in the Action phase.
- **Corsair:** pairs with nothing here. It is strong against money decks (it trashes their Silver/Gold), but weak against this engine, whose money is Livery, Abundance and Rope.
- **Road Network:** pairs with an opponent who greens a lot. It works against Cursed Village, though: an extra card in your starting hand means Cursed Village draws one fewer.
- **Captain:** pairs with Chariot Race, and with Wayfarer after a gain that turn.

### Ratings check
- **Captain (+3.76):** I rate it lower here. Its only usual Supply target is Chariot Race.
- **Rope (+3.61):** agree. It's both the +Buy and the trasher.
- **Mastermind (+3.33):** agree, because Livery and Wayfarer are good targets.
- **Wayfarer (+1.93):** I rate it higher here, because +Buy makes it cheap.
- **Corsair (−2.75):** that looks too low against money. Against this engine it's fair.
- **Road Network (−2.99):** mostly agree. It's only worth it against a money deck that greens.
- **Colonnade (−0.41):** I rate it higher here. Cheap duplicates like Chariot Race claim it easily.

### Plans considered
- **A. Money baseline (Rope, Corsair, Wayfarer):**
  - Open Rope/Silver, add one Corsair and one Wayfarer (bought for $3 with Rope's Buy), then Gold and Provinces.
  - Rough line: $6 around turn 5, $8 around turns 7–8, about 4 Provinces by turns 14–15, attacking on about half of turns.
  - Weakness: no draw beyond Wayfarer, and the opponent's Corsair hits it hard.
- **B. Junk-converting engine:**
  - Pieces: Rope, Artificer, Cursed Village, Wayfarer, Livery, Mastermind, Abundance, plus Chariot Race for Colonnade.
  - Payload: Livery $3 each (or $9 under Mastermind), Abundance $3 plus a Buy, Rope $1 plus a Buy. Horses supply the next turn's draw.
  - Needs: 2 Ropes or 1 Rope + 1 Abundance for 2 or more Buys by turn 10; 2 Cursed Villages; 2 Wayfarers; 2 Liveries.
  - Weakness: Hexes (Curses, Misery), only one real village, and weak trashing.
- **C. Captain/Chariot Race good-stuff:** many Captains and Chariot Races. Rejected: at $6 each, Captain mostly plays a $3 cantrip, and the plan has little +Buy.

### Build lines
- **B (engine):**
  - T1–2: Rope / Silver.
  - T3: about $4 → second Rope or Abundance.
  - T4: Rope trashes an Estate; $5 → Artificer.
  - T5–7: Cursed Village; then Chariot Race + Wayfarer ($3 + $3, using Rope's Buy, and +2 VP from Colonnade if a Chariot Race is in play); then Livery.
  - T8–10: Cursed Village #2, Livery #2, Mastermind, Wayfarer #2 (about 19 cards, 1–2 Hex junk cards).
  - Built turn (about T11–12): Cursed Village → Artificer filter → 2 Wayfarers and Horses draw most of the deck. 2 Liveries ($6) + Abundance ($3) + Ropes ($2) + Coppers and Chariot Race (about $5) ≈ $16 with 3–4 Buys. That's Province + Province + 4 Horses, plus 0–2 VP from Chariot Race.
- **A (money):**
  - Rope/Silver, Corsair at the first $5, Wayfarer for $3 alongside Silver at the first $6.
  - Gold at $6–7, Province from about turn 7, about one Province every other turn.

**Against their best plan:**
- **B against A:** money's Corsair barely touches the engine; it only loses the Silvers Wayfarer gains. Money starts greening around turn 7, so a Road Network bought with a spare $5 Buy around turns 8–10 pays off. From about turn 11, double Provinces plus 4–8 VP from Colonnade and Chariot Race overtake money's roughly 3 Provinces by turn 12. I expect the engine to be favoured, but not by a lot.
- **B in a mirror:** Colonnade's 12 VP and the Chariot Race tokens decide it. Buy duplicates early, and green slightly earlier than you would against money.
- **A against B:** money has to be ahead by turn 13 and play Corsair often. It loses if the engine reaches 2 or more Buys on schedule.

**Challenged:**
- **Money plan:** the strongest version of A, Mastermind on Livery with Rope, was retested. It gets $9 plus Horses about one turn in two, which is still a single-Province ceiling, so it stays a money plan and B still beats it.
- **How B loses:**
  - The Hexes go badly: Plague, Locusts, or Deluded on a turn you need to buy Actions.
  - You're slow to get a second Buy.
  - The opponent claims Colonnade first.

**Recommendation:** play B, the engine. It is the only plan here that takes more than one Province per turn, and the opponent's one attack (Corsair) misses its payload.
- **If the opponent opens money:** add one Corsair, plus Road Network once they start greening.
- **Hex risk:** limit yourself to 2 Cursed Villages.

**Open:**
- **4/3:** Rope / Silver. Rope / Chariot Race is fine too if you want Colonnade sooner.
- **5/2:** Artificer / nothing. Livery / nothing is a fallback, and Corsair / nothing if the opponent looks like money.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffles 2–3:** Artificer, Cursed Village, a second Rope (or Abundance), Wayfarer via the $3 trick, Livery.
- **Shuffles 3–4:** second Cursed Village, second Livery, Mastermind, second Wayfarer.
- **Throughout:** take Chariot Race whenever you have a spare $3 Buy. Buy whatever you just played, for Colonnade.
- **Greening:** start once you hit $13 or more with 2 or more Buys, about turn 11. Late in the game, Artificer discarding 8 cards for a Province (or 5 for a Duchy) helps.

**Ending:** Provinces. Chariot Race is the likeliest pile to empty and the Curses could drain from Hexes, but a three-pile ending is unlikely.

**Watch out for:**
- Each Cursed Village gain is a Hex, so plan your buys around Deluded.
- An opponent's Corsair costs your Wayfarer Silvers; that's harmless.
- If the opponent claims Colonnade first, stop counting on it.

**Least sure of:** whether the engine really reaches 2 or more Buys and double Provinces by turns 11–12, given that Rope is the only thinning. If by turn 9 you have only one Buy source, switch to Mastermind on Livery with Rope, playing for single Provinces plus Colonnade.

**Assumed:**
- **Wayfarer's cost:**
  - "Last other card gained" includes non-Supply Horses.
  - Buying a Copper first makes Wayfarer cost $0.
  - A Captain play at the start of a turn sees Wayfarer at $6.
- **Captain + Colonnade:** a card Captain plays from the Supply is not "in play" for Colonnade.
- **Deluded:** if received during your Buy phase, it lasts until your next Buy phase.
- **Abundance:** gains in the Action phase trigger it.
- **Ratings:** the Corsair rating may reflect a different version of the card.
