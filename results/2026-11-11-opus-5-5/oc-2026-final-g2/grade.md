## Beyond the key
- Captain's only usual Supply target is Chariot Race: plausible. Captain needs a non-Duration, non-Command Action costing up to $4 in the Supply. Rope and Abundance are Treasures, Horse is not in the Supply, and Wayfarer qualifies only after a cheaper gain that turn.
- A Captain play at the start of a turn sees Wayfarer at $6: plausible. Nothing has been gained yet that turn, so Wayfarer keeps its printed cost.
- A card Captain plays from the Supply is not in play for Colonnade: plausible. Captain leaves it in the Supply.
- Mastermind on Livery gives +$9 and 3 Horses per $4+ gain: plausible. Each of the three Livery plays sets up its own gain trigger.
- After a Province plus a Livery Horse, Wayfarer costs $3: plausible. The Horse ($3) is the last other card gained.
- Road Network works against your own Cursed Village: plausible. The +1 Card arrives in the opponent's turn, so Cursed Village draws one fewer up to 6.
- Abundance chains Colonnade buys (+1 Buy +$3 on the Action gain): plausible, but once per Abundance played.
- Deluded received in the Buy phase (from buying Cursed Village) lasts to the next Buy phase: plausible. It is returned "at the start of your Buy phase", which has already passed.
- Corsair is weak against this engine because its money is Livery, Rope and Abundance: plausible. It only hits Silvers from Wayfarer, though agoo03's winning deck held 4 Silvers.
- The Corsair rating may reflect a different version of the card: doubtful. Speculation with no basis in the brief.

## Summary
The analysis lands on the commentators' read almost exactly: Artificer plus Cursed Village, Rope for +Buy and trashing, Mastermind on Livery or Wayfarer, Colonnade through cheap duplicates, and Captain and Road Network dismissed. It underweights how many Ropes to buy, and it treats the Copper-first Wayfarer trick as a footnote rather than the engine of agoo03's wide Artificer/Wayfarer win. A player following it would play the Livery/Mastermind deck the commentators favoured, which lost this game.
