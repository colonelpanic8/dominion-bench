- When a played Cage is trashed on gaining a Victory card, each Market Square in hand can be discarded for a Gold: plausible. The trash triggers Market Square's reaction, and it can happen on the same turn.
- Cage can bank up to 4 surplus cards on a Province turn, returning them at end of turn: plausible, but it's a one-shot because Cage trashes itself.
- Council Room played as Squirrel gives the opponent no card, and Litter played as Squirrel adds no Debt: plausible. A Way replaces the card's instructions.
- Jester discarding the opponent's Tunnel gives them a Curse and a Gold: plausible. Tunnel is a Victory card, and the discard happens outside Clean-up.
