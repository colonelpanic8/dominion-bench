## Beyond the key
- The 6/3 48%, 5/4 36%, 7/2 12%, 4/5 5% opening odds with Cursed Gold: plausible. These match the hypergeometric numbers for 6 Copper + Cursed Gold ($3) + 3 Estates.
- Trashing Fool's Gold when the opponent gains a Province puts a Gold on your deck as Butcher fodder for next turn: plausible. The Gold goes onto the deck, so it is in your next hand.
- Ritual on a Gold nets +5 VP (6 VP minus the Curse), and Tower cancels the Curses if that pile empties: plausible. That follows from the card text, though 10 Curses running out in a 2-player game is unlikely. The key lists these as extras.
- Ritual triggers Merchant Guild because it gains a Curse during the Buy phase: plausible. Merchant Guild counts every card gained in that Buy phase.
- Ritual on City Quarter scores 0: plausible. Its cost is $0 plus Debt, and Ritual counts only $.
- Nomad Camp is gained onto your deck, so a turn-1 or turn-2 buy is in the next hand: plausible. The text says it is gained onto the deck.
- A set-aside Hound returns after the Clean-up draw, giving a 6-card hand: plausible. It goes into your hand "at end of turn".
- Pooka can't trash Cursed Gold: correct, as its text says.
- Embassy's Silvers help a money opponent, so take one Embassy: plausible. Each other player gains a Silver when you gain Embassy.
- An opponent stocking Fool's Gold punishes Butcher Provinces: plausible. Each Province gained lets them trash Fool's Gold for a Gold.

## Summary
It gets the payload right (Tunnel Golds through Embassy, Butchered into Provinces) and opens Butcher. But it writes off Night Watchman, which is the key's enabler: it is in the opening, guarantees a turn-3 Butcher, topdecks City Quarter and controls the reshuffle. So it judges the City Quarter engine too slow and settles for a Butcher-money hybrid. It also spends Coffers on Cursed Gold → Gold instead of a second Butcher, and keeps Fool's Gold and Merchant Guild as late additions.
