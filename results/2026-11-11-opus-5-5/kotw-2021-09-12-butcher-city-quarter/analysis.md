# Kingdom analysis: Butcher, City Quarter, Embassy, Faithful Hound, Fool's Gold, Merchant Guild, Night Watchman, Nomad Camp, Pooka, Tunnel · Ritual, Tower (2 players)

**Board:** No card here attacks the other player. The junk comes from yourself: playing Cursed Gold gains you a Curse, and so does buying Ritual. Three supports exist:
- **Gold sources:** Tunnel when discarded by Embassy or Night Watchman, Butcher turning Cursed Gold into Gold, and Fool's Gold's reaction to the opponent gaining a Province.
- **Turning Gold into VP:** Butcher turns a Gold into a Province, and Ritual turns a Gold into about 5 VP.
- **Playing two terminals:** City Quarter works as a village, paid for in Debt instead of $.

The real draw is weak. Faithful Hound adds 1 card to your hand per play and Embassy about 1, so a full draw engine is slow. The open question was whether Butcher, fed with Golds, beats both plain money and a Fool's Gold engine. I think it does.

**Inventory:**
- **Piles by cost:**
  - $0+8 Debt: City Quarter
  - $2: Faithful Hound, Fool's Gold
  - $3: Night Watchman, Tunnel (8 cards, since it's a Victory pile)
  - $4: Nomad Camp
  - $5: Butcher, Embassy, Merchant Guild, Pooka
- **Landscapes:** Ritual (an Event costing $4) and Tower (a Landmark).
- **First-edition cards:** Embassy and Nomad Camp are first-edition only. I used the text given in the kingdom file.
- **Setup:** Pooka's Heirloom replaces one Copper, so each player starts with 6 Copper, Cursed Gold and 3 Estates, worth $9 in total.
- **Turn 1–2 splits:**

| Split | Chance |
| --- | --- |
| 6/3 | 48% |
| 5/4 | 36% |
| 7/2 | 12% |
| 4/5 (the $4 hand holds Cursed Gold) | 5% |

  In each split the Cursed Gold hand is listed first. Playing it adds a Curse.
- **Special rules:** Debt, a Night card, Coffers. There is nothing at $6 or more except Gold and Province.

**Key interactions:**
- **Butcher + Gold → Province.** Butcher gives +2 Coffers, and spending both upgrades a $6 Gold to an $8 Province. So any turn with Butcher and a Gold in hand gains a Province on top of your normal buy. Each Province costs about $6 plus a Butcher play. This starts around turns 8–9.
- **Butcher + Cursed Gold → Gold.** On turn 3 or 4, Butcher trashes Cursed Gold and gains a Gold, using both Coffers. This removes your Curse source and adds Butcher fodder. Trashing a Curse or Estate can gain a Fool's Gold or Hound and keep the Coffers.
- **Tunnel + Embassy, or Tunnel + Night Watchman.** Embassy draws 5 and discards 3, and Night Watchman discards from the top of your deck. Each Tunnel discarded this way gains a Gold. Tunnel is also 2 VP, so it counts as green that pays for itself. This keeps Butcher supplied.
- **Faithful Hound + Embassy, or Hound + Night Watchman.** A Hound you discard comes back to your hand at the end of the turn, so the next hand has 6 cards.
- **Fool's Gold reaction.** When the opponent gains a Province, you can trash a Fool's Gold from hand to gain a Gold onto your deck. That Gold is Butcher fodder on your next turn, which punishes an opponent who buys Provinces.
- **Ritual + Tower + Gold.** Ritual trashes a Gold for 6 VP but gains a Curse, so the net is +5 for $4 and a Buy. If the Curse pile empties, Tower pays +1 for each Curse, which cancels them out. Ritual also triggers Merchant Guild, because it gains a card during the Buy phase. Use it as an endgame VP sink.

**Other cards:**
- **City Quarter:** best with Butcher and Embassy, because they are the Actions it reveals. Revealing two Actions gives a village that draws 2. It isn't a draw engine by itself.
- **Merchant Guild:** pairs with Ritual, Fool's Gold and City Quarter buys, which each give it a Coffer. It is the main +Buy for late double buys.
- **Nomad Camp:** its gain goes onto your deck, so it is guaranteed in your turn-3 hand. It is still a terminal that collides with Butcher, so it's a weak +Buy.
- **Pooka:** trashes a Copper for +4 cards. It's fine early, but late it eats payload. Skip it, or take one copy.
- **Night Watchman:** pairs with Tunnel and Hound. It is a non-terminal sifter but a dead card while you buy. It's an option at $3, not a priority.
- **Tower:** may score the Fool's Gold and Curse piles. It's a tiebreaker-sized bonus.

**Ratings check:**
- Butcher (+3.71) and City Quarter (+3.66) agree with my view.
- Tunnel (−1.71), Night Watchman (−3.0) and Ritual (−4.48) play above their ratings here, because this board has discard outlets, Gold fodder and Tower's offset for Curses.
- Pooka (−2.17) fits, since Cursed Gold is the Treasure it can't trash.
- Embassy's rating is the deprecated first-edition one and carries little weight.

### Build lines
- **B: Butcher–Gold converter (recommended)**
  - T1–2: Butcher / Silver, playing Cursed Gold to reach $5 or $6.
  - T3–4: Butcher trashes Cursed Gold for a Gold, or an Estate or Curse for a Fool's Gold while keeping the Coffers. The other hand buys Embassy at $5 or Gold at $6.
  - T5–7: a second Butcher, Gold at $6–7, City Quarter on a $2–4 hand (paying Debt from spare $), Tunnel at $3–4 once you have Embassy.
  - Around T8: the deck holds 2 Butchers, Embassy, 1 City Quarter, about 2 Golds, 2 Tunnels and some Silver.
  - Built turn: City Quarter reveals Butcher and Embassy for +2 cards. Embassy discards a Tunnel (Gold) and junk. Butcher trashes a Gold for a Province, and you buy with about $8 left: a Province or Gold, which is 1–2 Provinces per turn. Estimate: 4 Provinces by about T12, threatening to end around T15.
- **A: Embassy/Tunnel money (the baseline)**
  - Embassy/Silver, then Gold, a second Embassy and Tunnels.
  - Hits $6 from about T3–5 and $8 from about T6–8.
  - About 4 Provinces by T14–15.
  - Each Embassy you gain gives the opponent a Silver.
- **C: City Quarter / Fool's Gold engine**
  - City Quarters, Hounds, Pooka, Merchant Guild or Nomad Camp, 5+ Fool's Gold, and Tower on the Fool's Gold pile.
  - Thinning is slow (Butcher trashes 1 card per turn, plus Pooka's Coppers), and Curses from Cursed Gold slow it further. Each Hound adds only 1 card.
  - Estimate: 2 Provinces per turn only around T12–13.

| Plan | Starts scoring | Can end by | Peak VP/turn | VP ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B: Butcher–Gold | ~T8 | ~T15 | 2 Provinces (+ Ritual late) | Provinces + Tunnels + Ritual |
| A: Embassy money | ~T7 | ~T17 | 1 Province | about half the Provinces |
| C: Fool's Gold engine | ~T11 | ~T16 | 2 Provinces + Ritual/Duchy | Provinces + Tower on Fool's Gold |

**Against their best plan:**
- **B vs A:** B scores at about the same time and then at about 1.5× the rate. A's Provinces also turn my Fool's Gold into Gold for my Butchers.
- **B vs C:** B leads on Provinces before C comes online. C's Fool's Gold does punish my Butcher Provinces, so B buys few or no Fool's Gold and just races.
- **B mirror:** the Butcher pile has 10, enough for both players. Split the Tunnels and keep City Quarter in mind, because colliding terminals decide who gets 2 Provinces per turn.

**Challenged:**
- I re-tried C with every helper on the board: Night Watchman setting up hands, Hounds returning to hand, Merchant Guild Coffers. Its draw stays at about +1 card per terminal, so it can't outrun B. Adding Fool's Gold and Merchant Guild late to B is the useful part of it.
- How B loses: Butcher misses its Golds. Fewer than about 3 Golds, or Butcher colliding with Embassy and no City Quarter, leaves it as Butcher money at roughly A's speed. An opponent who denies Tunnels slows the Gold supply, but they pay $3 for each Tunnel they take.

**Recommendation:** Plan B, the Butcher–Gold converter. It lets you gain more than one Province per turn without needing real draw. It holds up against both money and the engine, and it switches to Ritual on Golds when the Provinces run low.

**Openings:**

| 7/2 | Butcher / Fool's Gold | Hound is the alternative at $2. |

Play Cursed Gold only when it reaches a price you need.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffles 1–2:** Butcher > Gold at $6–7 > one Embassy at $5.
- **Shuffles 2–3:** a second Butcher, a City Quarter once you have 2+ terminals (with a spare $2–4 hand or a spare Buy), Tunnels at $3–4.
- **Later:** Merchant Guild for +Buy.
- **Greening:**
  - Province at $8 from about T7.
  - Use Butcher on Gold for Provinces every time you can.
  - With 4 or fewer Provinces left: Duchy, or Ritual trashing a spare Gold or terminal (+4/+5 VP net). Tunnels at $3 as filler.

**Ending:**
- Most likely the Provinces run out, around T15–17.
- Curses can empty through Cursed Gold plays and Rituals, which is when Tower cancels them. Fool's Gold can empty in an engine game.
- A three-pile ending (Curses, Fool's Gold, plus Hounds or City Quarter) only happens if both players lean on those piles.

**Watch out for:**
- An opponent stocking Fool's Gold, which punishes each Butcher Province. Keep counting the Fool's Gold they have in hand.
- Debt from City Quarter blocking a $6 Gold turn. Buy City Quarter on weak hands.
- Embassy's Silvers helping a money opponent. Take only one Embassy.

**What would change the plan:**
- With 3+ players, Embassy is worse (each opponent gains a Silver), the Fool's Gold reaction gets better, and the Curse pile becomes 20, so Tower's offset is unlikely.

**Least sure of:** how often Butcher finds a Gold. I assumed 1.2–1.5 Provinces per turn from T9. If paper runs show Butcher with a Gold in under about 50% of turns, B falls back to plain Embassy money (A). In that case, add Night Watchman, more Tunnels, and Gold over Silver.

**Assumed:**
- If you trash with Butcher, the gain is mandatory when possible. Trashing a Copper while spending no Coffers gains a Copper.
- City Quarter can't be gained by "costing up to" effects, and Ritual on it scores 0, because Debt isn't $.
- Tower counts Curses, since they aren't Victory cards and Curse is a Supply pile.
- A Hound set aside returns to hand after the Clean-up draw, giving a 6-card hand.
- First-edition text is used for Embassy and Nomad Camp.
- All speeds are hand-estimated averages, not a careful turn-by-turn simulation.
