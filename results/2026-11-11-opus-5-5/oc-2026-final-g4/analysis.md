# Kingdom analysis: oc-2026-final-g4

**Board:** Nothing on this board removes junk. Ritual trashes one card but gives you a Curse each time, and only a Loot could add real trashing. So Coppers, Estates and any Curses stay in your deck all game. Jester is the only attack that adds junk, and the Curses it gives are permanent here. The board does have some draw: Laboratory, Ghost Town, the Flag (from Flag Bearer), Tactician, and a cute First Mate + Aristocrat pairing. The open questions were whether that draw adds up to an engine (it doesn't) and how much Ritual adds (more than its −4.48 rating suggests).

**Key interactions:**
- **Jewelled Egg + Ritual → Loot.** Keep an Egg in hand (don't play it) and pay $4 for Ritual. You trash the Egg for +2 VP, take a Curse (−1 VP), and gain a $7 Loot such as Doubloons (which also gains a Gold), Endless Chalice or Prize Goat.
  - In effect a $5 hand becomes a Gold-plus treasure, a dead card and +1 VP.
  - It's playable from turn 5–6 on, once per Egg.
- **Ritual as late-game scoring.** Ritual trashes from your hand, not from play, so you give up the card's money that turn.
  - Holding a Gold or Loot on a $7 hand turns $4 into +5 or +6 VP net. That beats a Duchy (3 VP).
  - A terminal that collided and sits unplayed (Jester or Lab, cost $5) gives +4 VP net for $4.
  - Trashing a Province or Estate nets only +1, so don't.
  - Ritual stops working once the Curses run out.
- **First Mate + Aristocrat.** First Mate plays every Aristocrat in your hand without using Actions, then draws you up to 6 cards.
  - First Mate with one Aristocrat: +3 Actions and a 6-card hand.
  - First Mate with four Aristocrats: +3 Actions, +$3, 4 Buys and a 6-card hand.
  - It's real, but it needs a 2-card combination in one hand (see Challenged).
- **Jester + Flag Bearer / Ghost Town.** When the opponent's top card is something worth having, take the copy yourself.
  - A copied Flag Bearer takes the Flag for you.
  - A copied Ghost Town is gained straight to your hand.
  - Against an engine, Jester steals their Labs and Aristocrats.
- **Flag (from Flag Bearer)**: holding it gives you +1 card on every hand, a lot for a money deck. Gaining a Flag Bearer, or trashing one (Ritual: +4 VP net), takes it back. Expect it to change hands.

**Other cards:**
- **Corsair**: it only hits Silver and Gold, so it has no partner here. The opponent can dodge it with Loot, Flag Bearer and Egg as their money. Only worth a copy if they go heavy on Gold.
- **Tactician**: works with the Flag (11-card hands) and with Ritual or a Duchy on its +Buy. Still a dead turn every other turn.
- **Alley**: as a Shadow, it can be played from your deck every shuffle to sift past an Estate. That's a minor plus. Its partner is Crop Rotation, by discarding green cards.
- **Crop Rotation** ($6 Project, no card added to your deck): at the start of a turn, discard a Victory card for +2 Cards. That's +1 card on most turns once you're greening. Curses aren't Victory cards, so it doesn't help with them.
- **Laboratory**: draws without using your Action, so it never collides with Jester. A good $5 once you have two Jesters.
- **Ghost Town**: the best $3. Opening with it gives a 6-card turn 2.

**Rating check:**
- Jester (−1.81) is better here because Curses can't be removed.
- Ritual (−4.48) is much better because of Egg→Loot and late VP.
- Corsair (−2.75): I agree it's weak here.
- Aristocrat (−1.93) and First Mate (−0.50): agree. The combo is too inconsistent to fix them.

### Build lines
- **Jester money (pick):**
  - T1 $3: Ghost Town (played that Night) → T2 has 6 cards, ~$4–5: Flag Bearer (take the Flag), or Jester on $5.
  - T3–4 (6-card hands, ~$4.5 average): Jester, then Silver or Ghost Town.
  - T5–6 (14 cards, about $0.93 per card × 6 ≈ $5.6): Gold, Jester #2.
  - T7–9: first $8. After that, about one Province every 1.5 turns.
  - Egg→Loot on $5 hands where it's available. Crop Rotation once greening.
  - Jester: with 2 copies in about 16 cards it's played around half the turns. Each play gives the opponent about 0.25 Curse and about 0.5 Copper, or gains you a good card.
- **First Mate/Aristocrat engine:**
  - T1–2 Silver/Aristocrat → T3–6 First Mate, Lab, Aristocrats (bought two at a time with Egg's +Buy).
  - By T10 the deck is about 21 cards: 7 Copper, 3 Estates, 4 Aristocrat, 2 First Mate, 2 Lab, Silver, Egg.
  - Chance a hand has First Mate plus an Aristocrat: about 0.43 × 0.65 ≈ 28%, roughly 35–40% with Labs digging.
  - A good turn sees about 11 of 21 cards: about $7–8 with 2–3 Buys.
  - That's a Province most turns, rarely two. It doesn't beat money and has no attack.
- **Corsair money:** the same line with Corsairs instead of Jesters. It's only strong if the opponent keeps buying Silver and Gold.
- **Tactician money:** alternates a dead turn with an 11-card turn (with the Flag) worth ~$13 and 2 Buys, i.e. Province + Ritual or Duchy. The average is about the same as plain money, and it can't play Jester.

**Plans considered:**
- **Jester money with Ghost Town, Flag and Egg/Ritual:**
  - How: first $8 around T7–9; Jester played about half of turns.
  - Needs: 2 Jesters by about T6 and the Flag most turns.
  - Weakness: a Flag war, and a Jester mirror.
- **Corsair money:** same pace; the attack only works on an opponent who relies on Silver and Gold.
- **First Mate/Aristocrat/Lab engine:** about a Province per turn from T11; needs 4 Aristocrats and 2 First Mates; no trashing, and it's easily hit by Jester.
- **Tactician money:** lumpy; no better than the pick.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Jester money mirror**: the Flag, Ghost Town and Labs decide it, because drawing more is the only answer to Curses.
  - Don't let them hold the Flag for long. A $4 Flag Bearer is worth buying to take it back.
  - Ritual uses up the same Curse pile Jester draws from, so don't spend Curses early.
- **Against the engine**: Jester takes its Labs and Aristocrats and adds Curses it can never remove. Money wins the race.
- **Against Corsair**: keep your Silver and Gold count low and lean on Loot, Flag Bearer and Egg. Then their Corsair is mostly a $2 terminal.

**Challenged:**
- **Engine, second attempt:** I added Tactician so the 10-card next hand contains First Mate plus an Aristocrat about 60% of the time. That still costs a dead turn and still peaks around Province + Duchy, with no trashing to thin the deck. Rejected.
- **How the pick loses:** the opponent keeps the Flag and stacks Labs and Ghost Towns, so they draw their way past your Curses and win the Province race. You'd see that from a Flag Bearer/Ghost Town opening on their side. Answer it with your own Flag Bearer and Labs.

**Recommendation:** Jester money supported by draw. Jester is the only attack the opponent can't dodge on this board, and its junk is permanent. Ghost Town, Lab and the Flag give you hand size without adding terminals. Ritual adds scoring that beats a Duchy. The plan holds up whether the opponent builds an engine or plays money.

**Open:**
- **4/3:** Ghost Town / Flag Bearer. Buy Ghost Town on the $3 turn: if it's T1, your turn 2 has 6 cards.
- **5/2:** Jester / Jewelled Egg. The Egg is for Ritual later.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Turns 1–2:** the opening above.
- **Shuffles 2–3, by price:**

| Price | Buy |
| --- | --- |
| $8 | Province, once you have a Gold or a Loot |
| $6–7 | Gold |
| $5 | Jester (up to 2), then Lab. If an Egg is in hand with $4 besides it, Ritual the Egg instead |
| $4 | Flag Bearer if you don't hold the Flag, otherwise Silver |
| $3 | Ghost Town (up to 2), then Silver |
| $2 | Egg (1–2), with a spare Buy |

- **Crop Rotation** at $6–7 once you have 2 Golds or Loots, or when you start greening.
- **Greening:**
  - Duchy at $5–6 once 4 or fewer Provinces are left.
  - On a $7 hand holding a Gold or Loot, Ritual it instead of buying a Duchy.
  - Estates on the last turns.
- **Corsair:** only if the opponent is visibly Gold-heavy, and then it replaces the second Jester.

**Ending:** most likely on Provinces, around T16–18. Curses will probably run out (Jester plus Ritual), which turns Ritual off. A three-pile ending is unlikely, since there's no cheap pile both players drain. With 3+ players, Jester and Corsair get stronger and the Curses run out faster.

**Watch out for:**
- Losing the Flag for long stretches.
- Keep a Curse or two in the pile for your late Rituals if the Jester mirror is draining it.
- Collided Jesters late: Ritual them for VP rather than leaving them dead in hand.

**Least sure of:**
- Whether two Jesters beat Jester + Lab, or pure Lab/Ghost Town money, in this board's mirror. The test would be a money-bot simulation on this board.
- How much Egg→Loot is really worth. It depends on the Loot mix.

**Assumed:**
- Loot is gained from the top of a shuffled pile of the cards listed here, so its exact mix is unknown.
- The Flag adds its card at the Clean-up draw.
- Alley, as a Shadow, can be played from anywhere in your deck.
- A copy of Ghost Town gained via Jester goes to the gainer's hand.
- I didn't settle whether Jester can copy a Loot from the Loot pile.
- Ritual fails once the Curse pile is empty ("If you do").
