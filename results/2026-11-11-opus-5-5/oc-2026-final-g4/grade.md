## Beyond the key
- Ritual on a $7 Gold/Loot nets +5/+6 VP and beats a Duchy late: plausible. Ritual gives +1 VP per $1 of the trashed card's cost, minus 1 VP for the Curse. It does cost that turn's money.
- Ritual on a Flag Bearer is "+4 VP net": wrong. A $4 card gives +4 VP, and the Curse makes it +3 net. The analysis gets this subtraction right everywhere else.
- Trashing an Estate with Ritual "nets only +1": wrong. +2 VP, minus the Estate's 1 VP, minus the Curse is 0. The Province figure (+1) is right.
- Ritual stops once the Curse pile is empty, so save Curses for it in a Jester mirror: plausible. "If you do" gates the trash and the VP.
- First Mate plays every Aristocrat in hand (one: +3 Actions; four: +$3 and +3 Buys), then draws to 6: plausible. That follows from the counts in play on Aristocrat and First Mate's draw-to-6.
- Corsair has no target because the opponent can lean on Loot, Flag Bearer and Egg for money: plausible. Corsair only trashes Silver or Gold.
- A Ghost Town gained through Jester goes to the gainer's hand: plausible. Ghost Town's gain-to-hand clause applies to any gain.
- Tactician with the Flag gives 11-card turns: plausible. That's 5 + 1 (Flag) + 5 (Tactician).
- Crop Rotation can't discard Curses: plausible. Curse is not a Victory card.

## Summary
The analysis identifies the Flag Bearer/Ritual interaction and expects a Flag fight, but treats both as side issues in a Jester-money plan whose Ritual uses are Egg→Loot and late scoring. It never names Ritual-on-Flag-Bearer as the board's main combination, and never weighs what the war costs. Jester, Egg and Crop Rotation are read well, and its plan (Jester, Ghost Town, contested Flag, money) looks much like what the players actually did. It doesn't take the commentators' preferred build seriously.
