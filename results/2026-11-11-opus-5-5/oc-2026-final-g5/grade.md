## Beyond the key
- Borrow turns a 4/3 into $5 / $3–4 because the −1 Card token costs one card from the turn-2 hand: plausible. Borrow gives +$1 and puts the token on your deck, and the token takes one card off your next draw.
- Band of Misfits can only play Villa, Harbor Village or Sentinel: plausible. It must be non-Duration, non-Command, from the Supply, and cost less than $5, which rules out Lighthouse, Caravan and the $5s.
- Tools can copy itself, or Durations still in play (yours or the opponent's Caravan or Lighthouse): plausible. "A card anyone has in play" includes Tools itself and Durations from last turn.
- Tools gains a Villa before you buy, then you play Actions and still play Treasures afterwards: plausible. Villa returns you to the Action phase, and Treasures are only locked out after a buy.
- An Action that Innovation plays after a buy draws Treasures you can't play: plausible as a rule. But it misses that drawn Actions and Villas can still be played, which is the commentators' point.
- Harbor Village adds +$1 to a following Junk Dealer, Villa or Lighthouse: plausible. Each of those gives +$.
- Lighthouse is a $2 filler for spare Buys: plausible. It's non-terminal with +$1 now and next turn. Neither the key nor the players used it.
- Each Lost City or Caravan nets only +1 card, so the deck must stay thin: plausible from card text.
- A three-pile ending is possible on Lost City, Tools and Lighthouse or Villa: doubtful. Tools copying itself drains its pile, but the game ended on Provinces at turn 12.

## Summary
The core is right: a Sentinel-thinned deck whose draw is Lost City, whose payload is Tools, Villa and Gold, with Vault kept to one. That's close to what both players built. It loses points for opening Junk Dealer via Borrow instead of Caravan (the Junk Dealer trap), for dismissing the Band of Misfits-as-Sentinel opening, and for treating Innovation as optional without seeing how Villa makes it work.
