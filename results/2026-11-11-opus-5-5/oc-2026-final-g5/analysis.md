**Kingdom analysis: Villa, Band of Misfits, Junk Dealer, Lost City, Vault, Lighthouse, Sentinel, Caravan, Harbor Village, Tools · Innovation, Borrow** (2 players, no Colonies or Shelters, standard 7 Copper / 3 Estate start)

**Board:** No card on this board attacks. Two strong trashers (Junk Dealer and Sentinel) and plenty of Actions (Villa, Lost City, Harbor Village) make it easy to thin the deck and keep playing. Draw is weak, though: Lost City nets +1 card, Caravan nets +1 spread over two turns, and Vault nets +1 but is terminal. There are free Buys from Villa and Borrow, and Tools adds a free gain each time it's played. This is an attack-free race. The open points are whether a thin deck built on Lost City draws enough to reach two Provinces a turn, and what Tools and Innovation add.

**Key interactions:**
- **Borrow + the opening:** Borrow is a $0 Event, usable once per turn. It gives +1 Buy, and if your −1 Card token isn't already on your deck it gives +$1 and puts the token there. The token takes one card off your next Clean-up draw. That turns $4 into $5 on turn 1. Turn 2 then has 4 of the remaining 5 cards (about $2–3), plus Borrow's $1. So 4/3 effectively becomes $5 / $3–4, which opens Junk Dealer and Sentinel on every split. Later, in a thin deck that draws most of itself, losing a card costs little, so Borrow is close to a free +$1 and +Buy every turn.
- **Tools (a Treasure: gain a copy of a card anyone has in play):**
  - Early, Tools copies itself, so you gain extra Tools.
  - Later, each Tools played after a Gold, Lost City or Villa gains another one free. With 2 Tools that's 2 free Golds or engine pieces a turn from about turn 9.
  - It can also copy Durations still in play from last turn, yours or the opponent's: Lighthouse and Caravan.
- **Tools + Villa:** play Tools before you buy anything and gain a Villa. The Villa goes to your hand, gives +1 Action and sends you back to the Action phase. Play it for +$1 and +Buy, plus any Action you were holding with no Action left. Then play your remaining Treasures, since you haven't bought yet.
- **Tools + Innovation:** Tools gains a Lost City and Innovation plays it immediately, for +2 Cards before you buy. Any Treasures drawn can still be played. That's a free Lost City play every turn. Innovation does little else here: a card bought in the Buy phase and played by Innovation draws Treasures you can no longer play.
- **Harbor Village before a card that gives +$:** Junk Dealer, Villa or Lighthouse each get +$1 more.

**Other cards:**
- **Band of Misfits:** none. It can only play Villa, Harbor Village or Sentinel (Durations and Commands are excluded), and each of those is cheaper to buy outright.
- **Lighthouse:** a $2 filler for the extra Buys from Villa and Borrow. It's non-terminal, gives +$1 now and +$1 next turn, works with Harbor Village, and Tools can copy it next turn. Its attack protection doesn't matter, since nothing here attacks.
- **Caravan:** the $4 draw card for turns without $5. Unlike Lost City it gives the opponent nothing, Tools can copy it while it's in play, and Innovation can play it when gained.
- **Vault:** take one copy late, once villages cover it. It turns drawn Victory cards and spare Coppers into $. Its downside is that it lets the opponent discard 2 cards to draw 1.

**Ratings check (Glicko, after forming the view above):**
- Agrees: Villa (5.77, top tier) and Sentinel (4.53) are core here, and Lost City (2.59) and Junk Dealer (1.93) are solid.
- Tools (0.47): I rate it higher here, because Gold and Lost City are good copy targets and Tools can copy itself.
- Vault (−2.9) and Band of Misfits (−2.24): low ratings are deserved. Vault is a late single copy at most, and Band of Misfits is skipped.
- Innovation (1.38) and Borrow (0.95): both have a real but modest use here (see above).

### Build lines (one solo game each, average draws)

**Thin Lost City deck (the pick):**
- **T1–2:** T1 $4 + Borrow = $5: Junk Dealer. T2 has 4 cards, about $2–3 + Borrow: Sentinel.
- **T3–4:** play Junk Dealer and trash an Estate. Hit about $4 + Borrow = $5: Lost City. Sentinel trashes Estate + Copper. Buy Silver with about $3.
- **T5–6:** deck of about 11 cards (6C, 1E, JD, Sentinel, Lost City, Silver). Lost City and Junk Dealer get you to about 7 cards, about $7–8 with Borrow. Buy Lost City + Lighthouse, then Tools or Villa.
- **T7–8:** deck of about 14, about 4 Coppers. You play about 8–9 cards for $9–11, with 2–3 Buys. Buy Gold + Lighthouse, or Lost City + Villa. Tools now copies Lost City or Gold.
- **T9–10:** Province + an engine piece each turn. Pick up Innovation at an exact $6 if it fits.
- **T11 onward:** about 10–12 of a 16–18 card deck played: Gold×2–3, Villa, Lighthouse, Junk Dealer, Harbor Village and Borrow give $13–17 with 3 Buys. That's Province + Duchy, or two Provinces.
- **Weak spot:** each Lost City only nets +1 card, so the deck must stay thin. Stop trashing Copper at about 3–4.

**Vault–Big Money (baseline):**
- **T1–2:** Silver/Silver (Vault/Silver on 5/2). Vault at the first $5, a second Vault later.
- **Then:** Gold at $6–7, first Province around T7–9. Use Borrow only to turn $7 into $8.
- **Pace:** about 4 Provinces by T15–16.

**Tools–Gold money:**
- **Build:** 2–3 Tools plus Treasure. Each Tools played in a hand with a Gold gains a Gold.
- **Weak spot:** Tools makes no $ itself, so early it copies Silver or Copper. That is slower than the thin deck and has no trashing.

| Plan | Starts scoring | Can end by | Peak VP/turn | VP ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thin Lost City deck (Villa/Tools/Borrow buys) | ~T9 | ~T15–16 | Province + Duchy, sometimes 2 Provinces | Provinces + Duchies |
| Vault–Big Money | ~T8 | ~T21+ (alone) | 1 Province | about half the Provinces |
| Tools–Gold money | ~T9 | ~T19 | 1 Province, sometimes + Duchy | similar to Vault–Big Money |

**Plans considered:**
- **Thin Lost City deck:** Junk Dealer and Sentinel thin the deck, Lost City and Caravan are the draw, Villa and Borrow give Buys, Tools gives free gains. Needs about 4–5 Lost City or Caravan, 2 Tools, 1–2 Villa and 2 Gold by about T10. Its weakness is that the draw is only +1 per card, and every Lost City gained hands the opponent a card.
- **Vault–Big Money:** about 4 Provinces by T15–16. It can't use the Buys and has nothing that scales.
- **Tools–Gold money:** reaches $8 more slowly than the thin deck and has no trashing.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Against money:** with no attacks on the board, money can't slow the thin deck down. From T11 it outscores money by roughly a Duchy a turn. Money leads around T9–11, which is where the scoring curves cross. The Lost City gifts give money about 4–5 extra cards over the game, roughly half a Province, which isn't enough to swing it.
- **In a mirror:** Lost City (10 copies) and Villa are shared piles. Take Lost City early and buy Caravan as overflow draw. Green a turn earlier than you would against money.

**Challenged:**
- **Retesting the rejected plans:** the best version of Vault–Big Money (Borrow to hit $8, a second Vault, Lighthouse fillers) still has no answer to a deck that keeps gaining Golds from Tools and has spare Buys. The ceiling of Tools–Gold is the thin deck's own Tools use, minus the thinning.
- **How the pick loses:** trashing Copper too early, so you stall at $4–5 on T5–7. Or buying Lost Cities without enough $ cards, so you draw everything and still only hit $8.

**Recommendation:** play the thin Lost City deck. Junk Dealer and Sentinel thinning, Lost City and Caravan draw, Buys from Villa and Borrow, and free gains from Tools beat any money plan, and no attack can slow it down. It also holds up in the mirror.

**Open:**
- **4/3:** Borrow → Junk Dealer, then Sentinel (Borrow on T2 if needed).
- **3/4:** Sentinel without Borrow (so T2 keeps 5 cards), then Borrow → Junk Dealer.
- **5/2:** Junk Dealer without Borrow, then Borrow → Sentinel.
- **2/5:** Borrow → Sentinel, then T2 has 4 Coppers + Borrow = $5 → Junk Dealer.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffles 2–3:** Lost City at $5 (aim for 4–5), one Silver early, then Tools ×2 and Villa ×1–2 at $4. Use Caravan at $4 once you have a Villa. Use Lighthouse for spare $2 Buys. Gold at $6–7. Innovation is optional at an exact $6 once 2 Lost Cities are in. One Vault late.
- **Trashing:** remove all Estates and trash Copper down to about 3–4.
- **Use Borrow** almost every turn once the deck is thin.
- **Greening:** Provinces from about T9–10. Add Duchies at 4 Provinces left. Use the third Buy for Estates near the end.

**Ending:** most likely the Provinces run out around T15–17. A three-pile ending is possible late in a mirror, from Lost City, Tools (it copies itself) and Lighthouse or Villa. Count those piles from about T12.

**Watch out for:**
- **Opponent plays money:** don't panic-green before T9. Your growth wins the long game.
- **Opponent contests Lost City:** switch to Caravan for draw, and remember every Lost City they gain gives you a card.
- **3+ players:** each Lost City gain hands a card to every opponent, which favors Caravan. Tools also gets more opponent Durations to copy.

**Least sure of:** how fast a deck whose draw is only +1 per card really reaches $16. If the T7–10 turns stall at $9–11, double Provinces come later than T11. The thin deck would still beat Vault–Big Money, but by less. A paper playtest of T5–10 with real shuffles would show it. I'm also unsure whether Innovation is worth $6 rather than a Gold.

**Assumed:**
- Sentinel is terminal (the text gives no +Action).
- Tools can copy the opponent's Durations still in play.
- Innovation can play an Action gained during the Buy phase.
- Borrow's −1 Card token applies to the Clean-up draw.
- After you buy anything, you can't play more Treasures that turn, even if Villa sends you back to the Action phase.
- The 2-player game ends on the Provinces as normal.
