# Kingdom analysis: oc-2026-final-g3

**Board:** This board has no attacks and no junkers, so it rewards whoever builds the stronger deck. There are three villages (Walled Village, Bandit Camp, Inn), draw (Patrol, Council Room), cheap trashing (Forager, Upgrade, Scrounge) and +Buy (Forager, Council Room). That makes an engine clearly possible. Two pieces stand out:

- **Scrounge** turns any $3 into a $5 card once an Estate is in the trash. Every key card here costs $4–5, so this matters a lot.
- **Horn of Plenty** has many different card names to count here. It gains $5 cards while you build and gains a Province (trashing itself) at the end.

The open question was how fast the engine comes online compared with money. Working it out turn by turn says roughly turn 11–12.

**Key interactions:**
- **Forager or Upgrade trashes Estates, then Scrounge gets them back.** Scrounge costs $3 and a Buy. It takes an Estate from the trash and gains a card costing up to $5. That Estate goes back into the trash on your next Forager or Upgrade play, so the loop repeats. From about turn 3–5, every $3 hand buys an Upgrade, Bandit Camp, Patrol or Horn. The opponent can also take Estates from the shared trash.
- **Scrounge as late payload.** Late on, the "$5 card" can be a **Duchy**, so $3 and one Buy give an Estate plus a Duchy, which is 4 VP. A Province gives 6 VP for $8. With Forager's +Buys, $14 buys a Province plus two Scrounges for 14 VP, while two Provinces cost $16 for 12 VP. The limits are the number of Estates in the trash and the 8 Duchies.
- **Horn of Plenty and variety.** A built turn has about 8–9 different names in play: Bandit Camp, Walled Village, Patrol, Forager, Upgrade, Copper, Silver, Gold and Horn. So each Horn gains a Province and is trashed. Scrounge rebuys a Horn for $3, so $3 now becomes a Province on a later shuffle. Before the deck is built, Horn gains $5 engine parts or a Gold.
- **Forager and different Treasures in the trash.** Copper goes in on turn 3–4. Forager can also trash a Spoils from hand. Played Spoils go back to their pile, but a trashed one stays in the trash. A Horn trashes itself after gaining a Province, and a Silver can be trashed too. With Copper, Spoils and Horn in the trash, each Forager is a cantrip-sized +$3 +Buy that trashes a card, though it draws nothing.
- **Upgrade as a builder.** It turns an Estate into a Forager or Silver, a Forager into a Walled Village, and a Walled Village into a $5 card. A $5 card becomes a Gold. It trashes and builds while staying a cantrip.

**Other cards:**
- **Bandit Camp:** the best village here. Its Spoils are payload, and Forager can trash a Spoils to add a Treasure name.
- **Walled Village:** a cheaper village. It top-decks itself in light decks, which suits money-plus-one-terminal.
- **Inn:** a sifting village. When gained, it shuffles Actions from your discard pile back into your deck, which is useful for mid-turn Scrounge or Horn gains late in a shuffle. It's a filler village.
- **Patrol:** the main draw here, because it doesn't help the opponent. Late in the game it moves Victory cards from the top of your deck into your hand. That clutters the hand but doesn't block the draw.
- **Council Room:** +Buy and +4 Cards, but each play gives the opponent a card. Take at most one, and use Patrol for the rest of the draw.
- **Overlord:** a flexible copy of any $5 Action (Patrol, Bandit Camp or Upgrade). Its 8 Debt blocks buys, so it's a mid-build buy on a turn with spare $, never in the opening. Scrounge and Upgrade can't gain it.
- **Golem:** checked against every card. Plays two Actions from an Action-dense deck, but needs a Potion, which is a dead card here. Skip it.

I rate Scrounge much higher than its rating on this board, because Forager and Upgrade keep feeding Estates into the trash and every key card costs $5 or less. I also rate Patrol higher, because it's the only draw that doesn't help the opponent.

### Plans considered

1. **Engine (Forager, Upgrade and Scrounge, then Bandit Camp, Patrol and Horn).** Build through Scrounge, draw the whole deck from about turn 10–11, then take 2–4 Provinces per turn plus Scrounge Duchies.
   - Needs: 2–3 Foragers, 1–2 Upgrades, 3 villages, 3 Patrols and 2 Horns.
   - Weakness: Horn and Bandit Camp are 10-card piles that both players want. If the opponent mirrors, it's a race for Estates in the trash.
2. **Council Room Big Money (the baseline).** Silver/Silver, Council Room at the first $5, a second Council Room later, Gold at $6–7.
   - Line: Gold about turn 5–6, first Province about turn 7–9, 4 Provinces by about turn 14–15, all 8 by about turn 20.
   - Weakness: one Province per turn at most, and each Council Room play gives the engine a free card.
3. **Money with Scrounge (Forager, Silver, Horn, Scrounge Duchies).** A middle plan: thin lightly, green early with Scrounge Duchies.
   - Faster VP than plan 2.
   - Weakness: its ceiling is still about one Province plus one Duchy per turn.

### Build lines

**Engine (4/3 opening):**
- T1–2: Silver, Forager.
- T3–4: Forager trashes a Copper, then Estates. At $3, Scrounge for Upgrade. At $5, buy Upgrade.
- T5–6: Upgrade turns Estates into Foragers. Scrounge for Bandit Camp or Patrol. Buy Walled Village at $4.
- T7–8: Bandit Camp ×2, Patrol ×2, and the first Horn (it already gains $5 cards).
- T9–10: the deck draws itself.
- Built turn on about T11–12, with about 17 cards:
  - Cards drawn: 5 in hand, plus 3 from Bandit Camps, 1 from Walled Village, 9 from three Patrols and 1 from Upgrade, which covers the whole deck.
  - Money: three Spoils ($9), three Foragers at about $2 each ($6), Gold, Silver and Copper ($6), for about $21 and 4 Buys.
  - Horns: two Horns count about 9 names each, so each gains a Province.
  - Result: 2 Provinces from Horns, plus 2 bought Provinces (or a Province plus a Scrounge Estate and Duchy). That's 20–24 VP in one turn.

**Council Room Big Money:**
- T1–2: Silver, Silver.
- T3–4: Council Room.
- From T5: Gold at $6–7, a second Council Room.
- First Province about T8. 4 Provinces about T14–15.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Engine against Big Money:** money has about 2–3 Provinces when the engine fires on turn 11–12. One or two engine turns take the other 5–6 Provinces, and every Council Room the money player plays speeds the engine up. The engine wins comfortably.
- **Engine mirror:** it comes down to Horns, Spoils and Estates in the trash. Green a turn earlier than you would against money, and use Scrounge Duchies to stay ahead on points.

**Challenged:**
- **Money's best chance:** a fast Duchy line using Scrounge. It still has only about one Province plus one Duchy per turn by turn 12, and it hands the engine Estates in the shared trash.
- **How the engine loses:**
  - It overbuilds Forager (each play costs two cards from hand) and runs short of draw.
  - It lets the opponent take the Horn pile.
  - It forgets that Forager's trash is mandatory and ends up trashing a Province.

### Recommendation

Build the engine: Forager and Upgrade thin the deck, Scrounge turns $3 into $5 parts, Bandit Camp and Patrol are the core, and Horns are the finisher. No plan here can attack it, and money's one-Province-per-turn ceiling is too slow.

**Open:**
- **4/3:** Forager / Silver.
- **5/2:** Upgrade / nothing. Overlord's 8 Debt would block buying on turns 3–4, and Scrounge costs $3.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffle 2:** the first $3 with an Estate in the trash goes to Scrounge for Upgrade, or Bandit Camp if you already have Upgrade. Buy a second Forager at $3 when the trash has no Estate. At $5, buy Upgrade (up to two).
- **Shuffle 3:** Bandit Camp ×2–3 or Walled Village, Patrol ×3, the first Horn once about 5 names are in play, and at most one Council Room.
- **From shuffle 4:** add Gold or Overlord only once the deck draws itself.
- **Greening:** start when Horns reliably count 8 names (about turn 10–12). Then turn every spare $3 and Buy into a Scrounge Duchy, or a Horn rebuy.

**Ending:** Most likely the Provinces run out, sped up by Horns. Duchies (8) can run low through Scrounge. A three-pile ending needs Duchies, Horns and Bandit Camps all to empty, which is unlikely. Spoils come from a non-Supply pile, so they don't count toward it.

**Watch out for:**
- Forager's trash is mandatory, so play it before you have greens in hand, or with junk to trash.
- The opponent taking your trashed Estates with their own Scrounges.
- Council Room drawing for the opponent.
- With 3 or more players: Horn, Bandit Camp and trashed Estates are more contested, Council Room gives away more, and greening should start earlier.

**Least sure of:** The worth of Scrounge, which is much higher here than its −0.98 rating. If Estates rarely sit in the trash (both players race for them), the engine slows to about one $5 card per turn and its first big turn slips to about turn 13–14. The test: count trashed Estates on turns 5–8.

**Assumed:**
- Spoils trashed by Forager stay in the trash and count as a Treasure name.
- Spoils return to their pile when played, so they never count for Horn.
- Cards Overlord plays from the Supply don't count for Horn.
- Scrounge can gain a Duchy, since it costs $5.
- Draws are average throughout the build lines.
