## Beyond the key
- Golem is a skip because Potion is a dead card here: plausible. Potion adds no $, and nothing else on the board costs a Potion. The grader note asks for this.
- A Spoils trashed by Forager stays in the trash and adds a Treasure name: plausible. Spoils returns to its pile only when played, not when trashed from hand.
- Spoils never count for Horn: plausible. A played Spoils returns to its pile at once, so it is not in play when Horn resolves.
- Cards Overlord plays from the Supply don't count for Horn: plausible. They stay in the Supply. Overlord itself still counts as a name.
- Scrounge and Upgrade can't gain Overlord: plausible. A cost of $0 plus 8 Debt is neither "up to $5" nor "exactly $1 more" than a card without Debt.
- Scrounge can gain a Duchy, so $3 and a Buy give an Estate and a Duchy for 4 VP: plausible from card text. It needs an Estate in the trash, and the key says the comeback version was judged infeasible.
- Scrounge rebuys a trashed Horn for $3: plausible, if an Estate is in the trash. Horn costs $5.
- Forager's mandatory trash can cost you a Province: plausible. "Trash a card from your hand" has no "may".
- Upgrade chain Estate → Forager/Silver → Walled Village → $5 → Gold: plausible. Each step is exactly $1 more.
- The opponent can take your trashed Estates with Scrounge: plausible. The trash is shared.
- Inn's on-gain shuffle helps mid-turn Horn or Scrounge gains: plausible from card text.
- The engine beats Council Room Big Money comfortably: plausible. No attacks, and Council Room feeds the engine cards.

## Summary
The analysis sees Horn of Plenty, variety and draw, and builds a sound-looking engine, but around Forager and a Scrounge Estate loop with only 2 Horns. It doesn't build the many-$5s, six-to-seven-Horn megaturn everyone expected and agoo03 won with. It opens the Forager trap that the commentators said cost yurikamome, rejects the Overlord opening, and overvalues Bandit Camp.
