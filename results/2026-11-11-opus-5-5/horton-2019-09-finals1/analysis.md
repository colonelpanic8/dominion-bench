# Kingdom analysis: Hermit, Diplomat, Treasure Trove, Caravan, Bridge, Hireling, Outpost, Rogue, Old Witch, Vassal · Sewers, Ferry

**Board:** This is a cursing game. Old Witch is the only junker, and it is also the best draw card on the board. Ferry is the card that shapes it: Ferry puts your −$2 token on Old Witch, so it costs $3, Hermit can gain it, and every $3 hand can buy one. There is no real village. Diplomat only gives +Actions from a hand of 4 or fewer, and a Madman is used up when you play it. So this is a terminal-limited good-stuff or money game. Each player plays Old Witch as often as possible, cleans up Curses with Hermit (and Sewers), and pays out with Treasure, Hireling, Treasure Trove and a late Bridge.

**Key interactions:**
- **Ferry + Old Witch:** Old Witch costs $3. Opening 4/3 with Ferry then Old Witch puts the Witch into your first shuffle. With 5/2 you also get one Witch, but no Ferry. After that, any $3+ hand adds another Witch, so you can have 2 Witches by turn 3–4 and win more of the 10 Curses.
- **Hermit + Ferry(Old Witch):** Hermit can gain an Old Witch, which makes it a trasher that is also a Workshop for Witches.
- **Hermit + Sewers:** Hermit trashes a Curse or Estate straight from your discard pile, so it doesn't need to collide with it in hand. Sewers then trashes a card from your hand. That is 2 junk cards gone per play, plus a $3 gain.
- **Opponent's Old Witch + your Sewers:** when their Witch hits you and you trash the Curse from your hand, Sewers trashes another card (an Estate or a second Curse). It is a real defence and costs no Action.
- **Hermit → Madman:** if you don't buy anything, Hermit becomes a Madman. Played from a 5-card hand, a Madman gives 8 cards and 2 Actions, which is enough to play Old Witch plus another terminal. It is a useful one-off on a turn when you couldn't buy anything good anyway. It is not something to build around.
- **Bridge in the endgame:** Province $7, Duchy $4, Estate $1, plus +Buy. For example, $9 plus Bridge's +$1 buys Province + Duchy + Estate.

**Other cards:**
- **Hireling:** pairs with the long game the Curses cause. It gives +1 card every turn, including Outpost turns, so it finds Witches more often. It is about as good as Gold early on and better if bought before about turn 9.
- **Treasure Trove:** it is a Treasure, so it never collides with your terminals. In a slog, gaining a Gold every play is good. The Copper hurts little in an already junked deck. It is the default buy at $5 once you have 2 Witches.
- **Outpost:** the 3-card extra turn is the only reliable way to get Diplomat's +2 Actions. It also pairs with Hireling (an extra turn of 4 cards). But it is a terminal competing with the Witches. At most one, late.
- **Diplomat:** pairs with Outpost turns, as above. Its Reaction lets you filter junk out when you are attacked, but it doesn't block the Curse. Weak here.
- **Rogue:** pairs with Hermit, since Hermits that become Madmen go to the trash and Rogue can gain them. Its attack hits Silver, Gold, Trove and Old Witch. It is a fallback terminal worth $2. Low priority.
- **Caravan:** none. It is a non-terminal filler and loses to Silver here.
- **Vassal:** pairs with Old Witch, which it can play off the top of your deck, but it is a terminal and collides. Skip.
- **Ratings check:** the ratings agree on Ferry, Hermit and Old Witch (all top tier). Hireling (−1.5) and Treasure Trove (−1.8) rate low on an average board. I rate them higher here because the Curses make the game long and the board has no draw-to-X or village to compete for those slots. Rogue (−3.3) stays low.

### Plans considered

1. **Old Witch money (baseline):** Ferry/Old Witch, a second Witch at $3, then Silver, Gold and Trove.
   - Line: $6 around T5–7, $8 around T9–11.
   - Plays Old Witch about 55–65% of turns with 2 copies.
   - Weakness: it eats Curses without answering them.
2. **Old Witch good-stuff with Hermit and Sewers (pick):** plan 1, plus 1 Hermit, Sewers, Hireling, Trove and 1 Bridge.
   - Same pace up to T6. Then it sheds about 1–2 junk cards per Hermit play, and Hermit gains Witches or Silvers.
   - Needs: Hermit by about T4, Sewers by about T6.
   - Weakness: 3–4 terminals with no village means some collisions.
3. **Bridge / Diplomat / Madman engine (rejected):** Diplomat and Bridge would keep each other going (Diplomat from a 4-card hand: 5 cards and +2 Actions; Bridge brings the hand back to 4). The problem is getting started. On a normal 5-card turn, Diplomat goes to 6 cards with no Actions left, so the chain only starts on Outpost turns or from a Madman. It also needs a very thin deck while Curses keep arriving.
4. **Outpost money with Old Witch:** more turns means more Witch plays, but Outpost and Old Witch can't both be played in one turn, and Outpost turns of 3–4 cards rarely reach $8 in a cursed deck. It is a variant of plan 1 at best.

### Build lines

**Plan 2:**
- **T1–2 (choose 4/3):** Ferry on Old Witch, then Old Witch at $3. Deck: 7 Copper, 3 Estate, Old Witch (11 cards).
- **T3–4:** Old Witch shows up in about 10 of 11 cases. With +3 cards that hand has about 4.5 Copper (about $5) and the other hand about $2–3.
  - $3+ hand: Old Witch #2.
  - Other $3 hand: Hermit.
  - $5 hand with Old Witch #2 already: Treasure Trove.
  - $2 hand: buy nothing.
  - Deck about 14 cards with 1–2 Curses.
- **T5–6:** one Witch play per turn, about 60% of the time.
  - Buy Sewers on a $3–4 hand, otherwise Silver or Trove.
  - Hermit: trash an Estate or Curse from the discard, Sewers trashes another Estate or Curse from hand, and gain Old Witch #3 only if the Curses are still running; otherwise Silver.
- **T7–10:** buy Hireling at the first $6 (then Gold), and Trove at $5.
  - The Curses split about 5/5 by about T12. Hermit and Sewers keep you at about 2–3 net.
  - First Province around T9–11.
- **T11–20:** Province at $8. Duchy at $5–7 once 4 or fewer Provinces are left. Pick up one Bridge around T12–14 for double buys.
- **Typical built turn:** Hireling plus 5 cards (Gold, Trove, Silver, Copper, Old Witch). Old Witch draws 3, giving about $10: a Province, plus a Gold from Trove. With Bridge: about $10 → Province + Duchy.

| Plan | Starts scoring | Can end by | Peak VP/turn | VP ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Old Witch money | ~T9–10 | ~T20–22 | 1 Province | half the Provinces, opponent −Curses |
| Old Witch + Hermit/Sewers | ~T10–11 | ~T19–21 | Province (+Duchy/Estate with Bridge) | same, with fewer of your own Curses |
| Bridge/Diplomat engine | ~T14+ | unreliable | multi-Province on Outpost turns only | not reached under Curses |

**Against their best plan:**
- The best reply is the same Ferry/Old Witch opening. The Curse split is then decided by Witch density (2–3 Witches) and by Hermit's ability to gain Witches.
- Against plain Old Witch money, plan 2 ends the game about 2–4 VP cleaner, and Sewers turns the opponent's Witch plays into extra trashing for you.
- If the opponent goes Rogue, keep Hermits out of the trash (skip the Madman line) so their Rogues hit and don't gain. Hireling stays in play, so their Rogue can never trash it.

**Challenged:** I re-tested the engine using Outpost as the way to start the Diplomat/Bridge chain. It needs Outpost, at least 2 Diplomats, at least 2 Bridges and a thin deck, all built while taking Curses at $3 a Witch. That is no earlier than T14, by which point plan 2 has about 3 Provinces. Still rejected.

How plan 2 loses:
- Losing the Curse split badly (for example, opening 5/2 without Ferry, or the opponent getting Witch #2 first).
- Over-trashing Copper with Sewers and stalling the economy. Use Sewers on Curses and Estates first, and on Coppers only once you have 2 or more Silver-equivalents.

**Recommendation:** Old Witch good-stuff: Ferry on Old Witch, 2–3 Witches, 1 Hermit plus Sewers to clear junk, Hireling and Trove for economy, 1 Bridge late. It holds up whatever the opponent does: it keeps pace with Witch money in the race and absorbs the Curse war better.

**Open:**
- **4/3 (chosen):** Ferry (Old Witch) / Old Witch.
- **5/2:** Old Witch / nothing. Strictly worse, since it is the same Witch without Ferry, which is why 4/3 is the pick.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffles 2–3:**
  - Old Witch #2 at $3 or more.
  - Hermit at $3.
  - Sewers at $3–4, once Hermit is in the deck.
  - Trove at $5.
  - Hireling at $6, then Gold.
- **Old Witch #3:** only while 4 or more Curses remain.
- **Midgame:**
  - 1 Bridge.
  - Province at $8.
  - Duchy at $5–7 once 4 or fewer Provinces are left.
  - Estates (at $1 with Bridge) in the last 2–3 turns.

**Ending:**
- The Curses run out first, around T12–14.
- After that it is usually a Province ending around T19–22.
- A three-pile ending (Curses + Duchies + Estates) is possible in a long slog, because Bridge makes Duchies and Estates cheap. Count Bridge buys when the Duchy and Estate piles get low.

**Watch out for:**
1. Terminal collision between 3 Witches, Hermit and Bridge. Don't add another terminal beyond that. Outpost, Diplomat, Vassal and Rogue stay out.
2. The opponent's Rogue gaining your trashed Hermits or Silvers.
3. If the opponent skips Old Witch, cut Hermit and Sewers back and go faster to Gold, Trove and Provinces.

**Least sure of:** whether Hermit plus Sewers ($6 and two buys in total) beats just adding Silver/Trove in an Old Witch mirror. It is right if you would otherwise end with 4 or more Curses. The test is to play both lines against a Ferry/Old Witch bot and compare the final VP difference. Next is Hireling vs Gold for the first $6.

**Assumed:**
- The Hermit wording comes from the tournament rule: it is exchanged when you didn't **buy** a card, and the exchange counts as trashing. Gaining a card with Hermit's own effect doesn't stop the exchange.
- I assumed Sewers' trigger on the Hermit→Madman exchange is useless, because your hand is already being discarded in Clean-up. I'm not sure of that timing.
- Ferry's discount applies only on the owner's turn.
- 2 players. With 3 or more, Old Witch and Sewers get stronger and the Curse pile empties sooner.
