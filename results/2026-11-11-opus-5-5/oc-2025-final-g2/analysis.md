**Kingdom analysis: oc-2025-final-g2 (2 players, no Colonies, no Shelters)**

**Board:** No card here draws you more cards this turn: Farmhands and Peddler only replace themselves. The bigger hands all arrive a turn late, from Enchantress, Grotto, Way of the Squirrel and Figurehead. So nobody can draw their whole deck, and there are no explosive engine turns. The board is about two things:
- **Foray as cheap $7 Treasures.** For $3 and three differently named junk cards you gain a Loot, which is a $7 Treasure and usually comes with +Buy. Foray and Loot are the only real +Buy, apart from Fortune.
- **Remodel turning $6+ cards into Provinces.** Gold, Loot and Peddler can all become Provinces. Peddler counts as $8 in the Action phase, because its discount only applies in the Buy phase.

Open questions: whether Fortune is worth its 8 Debt, and how much the Enchantress attack matters.

**Inventory:**
- **Kingdom:** Guardian, Grotto and Monastery at $2; Enchantress, Gladiator (split pile with Fortune, $8 + 8 Debt) and Amulet at $3; Farmhands and Remodel at $4; Peddler at $8, minus $2 per Action in play during the Buy phase; Transmute at a Potion.
- **Landscapes:** Foray (Event, $3; brings the Loot pile) and Way of the Squirrel.
- **Special rules:** Debt, Potion, Night cards, Durations.
- **Prices:** nothing at $5, and nothing between $6 and $8 except Gold.
- **Starting deck:** 7 Copper and 3 Estate, so turns 1–2 split 4/3 or 5/2. A 5/2 can only buy a $4 card plus a $2 card.

**Key interactions:**
- **Foray + junk + Loot with +Buy:** $3 plus 3 different names (e.g. Copper, Estate, Silver) gets a $7 Treasure. Each Loot with +Buy pays for the next Foray, so Province + Foray turns are possible from about turn 9. You need 3 distinct cards still in hand after making $3, so you need $3 from 1–2 cards: Gladiator, Silver, Gold or Loot. Foray can't happen before roughly turn 4–5.
- **Remodel + Gold / Loot / Peddler:** each of these becomes a Province, giving 2 Provinces per turn (one remodelled, one bought) from about turn 10–11.
  - Remodel can't gain Fortune, because its cost includes Debt.
  - Peddler costs $2–4 in the Buy phase when 2–3 Actions are in play (Durations from last turn count). It is then a $1 cantrip and Province fodder.
- **Remodel Estate into Farmhands:** Farmhands' on-gain effect sets aside a terminal from your hand (Gladiator) to play free next turn. Early on this thins Estates and fixes terminal collisions.
- **Gladiator into Fortune:** Gladiator is a terminal +$3 if you reveal a card the opponent can't match; your own Gladiator or a Loot is safest. Each unmatched play trashes a Gladiator from the pile, so Fortunes show up around turn 8–10.
  - Buying Fortune with 2 Gladiators in play also gains 2 Golds, which are Remodel fodder.
  - Fortune then turns a $9 hand into $18 with 2 Buys.
- **Grotto:** it gives +1 Action and parks up to 4 junk cards this turn, then draws that many next turn. That shifts money into one turn so you hit $6 or $8, and makes hands big enough for Foray.

**Other cards:**
- **Amulet:** good partner for Gladiator. Its two Silvers add money and Foray names, or it can trash Estates.
- **Enchantress:** your first Action each turn becomes a cantrip, which stops a Remodel-to-Province or a Gladiator. Its +2 cards next turn feeds Foray. It needs 2 copies to attack every turn.
- **Guardian:** is gained to your hand and blocks Enchantress or Sword. It's worth buying only if the opponent stacks Enchantress.
- **Monastery:** after a Foray-Loot or Province gain, it trashes a Copper you have in play. It's a minor extra.
- **Way of the Squirrel:** turns a dead terminal (a Remodel with no fodder, a late Amulet) into +2 cards next turn.
- **Transmute:** none. Estate into Gold isn't worth adding a Potion to the deck. Remodel already handles Estates, and turns Peddler into a Province rather than Transmute's Duchy.

**Ratings check:** I'm well above the community on Foray (−1.52) and Gladiator (−2.77). Here Loot is the main +Buy, nothing costs $5, and the Gladiator pile drains into Fortune. My view matches Grotto (3.41, top 10%) and Transmute (−6.84).

**Plans considered:**
1. **Gladiator Big Money (the baseline):** first Gold around turn 3–4, first Province around turn 8, about 4 Provinces by turn 16. It peaks at 1 Province per turn.
2. **Loot money with Remodel (recommended):** Gladiator and Amulet economy, Foray for Loot, 1–2 Farmhands, 2 Remodels to turn Gold, Loot and Peddler into Provinces, optional Fortune. Peaks at 2 Provinces per turn. Weakness: the opponent's Enchantress, and hands with too few different names.
3. **Farmhands / Remodel / Peddler engine:** villages with many Remodels and cheap Peddlers. Without same-turn draw, two Remodels plus fodder rarely land in one hand, and fodder comes in at only one per Buy. It ends up as plan 2 with less money.

### Build lines
- **Loot money with Remodel:**
  - T1–2: Gladiator / Amulet.
  - T3: Gladiator plus 3 Copper = $6, buy Gold.
  - T4: Amulet gains a Silver, $2 left: Grotto.
  - T5: Amulet trashes an Estate at turn start. Gladiator makes $3; discard Copper, Estate and Silver to Foray for a Loot.
  - T6: Gold.
  - T7: Remodel.
  - T8–9: deck is about 18 cards worth about $21, so roughly $5.8 per hand and $8 with Grotto's help. First Provinces, plus Province + Foray when you have a Loot's Buy.
  - T10 on: Remodel a Gold or Loot into a Province and buy another, for 2 Provinces (12 VP) on good turns.
- **Fortune branch:** at $8 with 2 Gladiators in play (around T9–10), take Fortune and 2 Golds instead of a Province. Pay the 8 Debt next turn. After that, hands of about $9 double to 2 Provinces.

**Against their best plan:**
- **If they mirror plan 2:** the game turns on Loot draws and the split of the 5 Gladiators. Take 2 Gladiators early, and reveal cards they lack so the pile drains toward Fortune while you hold Gladiators.
- **If they add Enchantress:** play a cantrip or a weak Action first to absorb it. Add 1–2 Guardians only if they keep two Enchantresses cycling.
- **Against pure Big Money:** plan 2 is a Big Money deck that also has Remodel's second Province per turn, so it wins the race.

**Challenged:** I re-tried the engine with Grotto and Way of the Squirrel adding hand size. Each Farmhands plus one Squirrel play nets about +1 card a turn later, which is roughly Laboratory-level draw with a delay. That isn't enough to reliably line up several Remodels with fodder, so the rejection stands. The pick loses if hand composition starves Foray (too few names, too little money) and Enchantress hits the Remodel turns. Then it falls back to Gladiator Big Money with Remodel, which is still at least the baseline.

**Recommendation:** Loot money with Remodel. It's a money deck at heart, so it can't be outpaced by a slower build. Remodel and Fortune give it a second Province per turn that plain Big Money lacks. The Enchantress attack only touches its Remodel turns.

**Open:**
- **4/3:** Gladiator / Amulet. The alternative is Gladiator / Grotto.
- **5/2:** Gladiator (or Amulet) / Grotto. No $4 card is a better opener, so spend the $5 on a $3 card.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffles 1–2:** Gold at $6–7. Foray at $3–7 when you have 3 names in hand. Second Gladiator, Amulet or Grotto at $3–4. One Farmhands, ideally gained by remodelling an Estate.
- **Shuffle 3:** 2 Remodels. Fortune at $8 if 2 Gladiators are in play before about turn 10. Peddler only when it costs $4 or less.
- **Greening:** Provinces at $8 from about turn 8. Remodel Gold or Loot into Provinces from about turn 10. Duchies from 4 Provinces left. Use Remodel on Silver or Farmhands for Duchy or Gold in the endgame.

**Ending:** the Provinces run out, likely around turns 15–17. The Gladiator/Fortune pile can empty, but no third pile is close, and the Loot pile isn't in the Supply. Watch the penultimate Province, since a Remodel plus a buy can take the last two.

**Watch out for:**
- The opponent stacking Enchantress against your Remodel turns: lead with a cheap Action, or add Guardian.
- Over-thinning: you need Copper and Estate as Foray names, and some money.
- A Gladiator match: if the opponent holds a copy of the revealed card, you get only +$2 and no pile trash.

**Least sure of:** whether Fortune is worth $8 plus 8 Debt at turn 9–10 instead of a Province. The test: by turn 10, does your average non-Fortune hand reach about $8 or more? If so, doubling it gives 2 Provinces per Fortune turn and the purchase pays off. If not, skip Fortune and just buy Provinces.

**Assumed:**
- A hand of 3 Coppers can't Foray, because only 2 cards remain to discard.
- Squirrel's +2 Cards come after Clean-up, giving a 7-card next hand.
- Peddler counts as $8 for Remodel in the Action phase.
- Durations still in play from last turn count for Peddler's discount.
- The Loot pile is the 15 listed Loot, shuffled; the pile size wasn't given.
- **Not confirmed:** how Enchantress interacts with choosing a Way. I assumed Enchantress overrides the choice.
- **With more players:** the opponent to your left is more likely to match Gladiator. Enchantress hits every opponent. With 12 Provinces, Remodel's extra Province matters more.
