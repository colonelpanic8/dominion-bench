## Beyond the key
- Panic needs 5 Suns in a 2-player game, and only Poet and Kitsune remove them: plausible. Those are the board's only Omens, and 5 is the 2-player Prophecy count.
- Lurker trashing Silk Merchant from the Supply gives +1 Coffers and +1 Villager, and the next Lurker gains it back for the same again: plausible. Silk Merchant triggers when you trash or gain it, and Lurker does both.
- Highway prices: with 2 in play Province $6, Duchy $3, Estate $0; with 3, Silk Merchant costs $1 and its gain refunds the Coffer: plausible. This is just the cost arithmetic.
- Tools and Figurine become one-shots under Panic but still give +2 Buys: plausible. Both are Treasures.
- Prepare works as a "$3 village" for stuck terminals, with Panic supplying the extra Buy: plausible. Actions it sets aside are played at the start of next turn without using Actions.
- Shop is "a Peddler that plays one different Action for free": doubtful. Shop has no +Action, so it is terminal unless the card it plays gives Actions (Kitsune does).
- Poet is a weak Laboratory that picks up Copper, Estate and Curse, and Transmute doesn't count as "$3 or less": plausible. Copper, Curse and Estate cost $3 or less, and a Potion-cost card doesn't.
- Reckless Transmute trashes two Curses for nothing, or turns two Estates into two Golds: plausible. Each play resolves twice, and Curse matches none of the type clauses.
- Late Transmute Duchies don't matter because Highway already prices Duchy at $3: doubtful. Transmute's Duchy gain needs no Buy and no money, and Lurker can resupply it with Actions.
- Avoid Silver and Gold entirely because Panic makes them one-shots: plausible. Panic returns played Treasures to their piles.

## Summary
The analysis reads Panic and Kitsune well and plans around Highway and the piles, but it builds a Kitsune/Silk Merchant/Lurker engine and rejects Reckless Transmute, the card the designer built the board around. With no Transmute in the plan, the Copper-trashing-versus-Panic decision and the Lurker/Transmute endgame are both missing.
