## Beyond the key
- Abundance + Chariot Race lets you buy Wayfarer at $3: plausible. Gaining Chariot Race (an Action) triggers Abundance's +$3 and +1 Buy, and Wayfarer then costs $3.
- "With Abundance, each Wayfarer you buy refunds $3 and a Buy": wrong. Abundance triggers only "the next time you gain an Action card", once per copy played, not on every Wayfarer.
- Gaining Wayfarer's Silver makes the Wayfarer pile cost $3 for the rest of the turn: plausible. Silver becomes the last other card gained.
- Captain at the start of next turn plays Chariot Race and works as a village: plausible. +1 Action at the start of the turn carries into the Action phase.
- Captain can play Wayfarer from the Supply after Artificer gains a Copper: plausible. Wayfarer then costs $0, which is within Captain's "up to $4".
- Mastermind on Captain plays 3 Chariot Races now and 3 next turn: plausible. Each Captain play has a now part and a next-turn part.
- Livery's Horse resets Wayfarer to $3: plausible. The Horse ($3) becomes the last card gained.
- Horses never count for Colonnade: plausible. Livery can gain one in the Buy phase, but played Horses return to their pile, so no copy is ever in play.
- Poverty, Fear and Haunting mostly do nothing when Cursed Village is gained in the Buy phase: plausible. All three key off hand size, which is small by then. That no longer holds if Artificer gains the Cursed Village during the Action phase.
- Corsair is a weak terminal here and can be skipped: plausible. Its attack only hits Silver/Gold, and the key notes nobody bought it.

## Summary
The analysis builds a Wayfarer/Colonnade/Chariot Race engine around cost-reduction tricks. That looks like agoo03's winning deck and gets the Colonnade and Copper→Wayfarer points. It misses the commentators' core read. It demotes Artificer to support, gets Rope only half right, and ranks Captain and Abundance above Mastermind, which falls into two of the three traps.
