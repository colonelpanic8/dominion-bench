# Kingdom analysis: oc-2026-final-g2

**Board:** Wayfarer is the card this board bends around. Its cost equals the last other card gained this turn, so on any turn you have a gain to spare it costs $0–3. Rope and Abundance give the Buys, Captain and Artificer can set the price up mid-turn, and Colonnade pays 2 VP for every Wayfarer or Chariot Race you buy with a copy in play. The plan is a draw engine of Wayfarers and Cursed Villages that scores Provinces, Colonnade VP and Chariot Race tokens. Three things are still open: the only real village (Cursed Village) gives you a Hex when you gain it, the only trashing is Rope's one card per turn, and there is no +Buy except Rope and Abundance.

**Inventory:** Chariot Race $3 · Rope $4, Abundance $4 (both Treasure–Duration) · Cursed Village, Livery, Mastermind, Artificer, Corsair $5 · Captain $6, Wayfarer $6* (*varies). Road Network is a $5 Project. Colonnade is a Landmark holding 12 VP. Horses are a non-Supply pile, gained through Livery. Hexes come with Cursed Village. There is nothing at $2. The starting deck is the standard 7 Copper and 3 Estates, so the opening splits are 4/3 or 5/2.

**Key interactions:**
- **Cheap Wayfarer (Rope or Abundance):**
  - With Rope's 2 Buys: buy a Copper for $0, then a Wayfarer for $0. With $6, buy Chariot Race ($3) and then Wayfarer ($3) instead.
  - With Abundance in play: buy Chariot Race (an Action, so Abundance gives +$3 and +1 Buy), then Wayfarer for $3. That is two Actions for $3.
  - This works from turn 3. A run of Wayfarers keeps the cheap price: each costs what the one before it cost.
- **Wayfarer's own Silver:** once you play a Wayfarer and take the Silver, the Wayfarer pile costs $3 for the rest of the turn. With Abundance, each Wayfarer you buy refunds $3 and a Buy, enough for the next one. With a Wayfarer in play, every buy also takes 2 VP from Colonnade. From about turn 7 that means 2 Wayfarers and 4 VP on a typical turn.
- **Captain:** at the start of your next turn, Captain plays Chariot Race. That gives +1 Action and +1 Card before your Action phase, so Captain works as a village for that turn. When played after a Silver or Copper has been gained this turn, Captain can play Wayfarer from the Supply instead (+3 Cards, gain a Silver). Artificer can supply that gain: discard nothing, gain a Copper onto your deck, and Wayfarer now costs $0.
- **Mastermind:** at the start of the next turn it plays a card three times. On Wayfarer that is +9 Cards and 3 Silvers. On Captain it is 3 Chariot Races now and 3 more next turn: +6 Actions, about +$3 and +3 VP. On Cursed Village it is +6 Actions.
- **Livery:** gives a Horse for each card costing $4 or more that you gain. Buying a Province with Livery played gives a Horse. The Horse becomes the last card gained ($3), so Wayfarer drops back to $3 afterwards.
- **Buy order matters:** buy Wayfarers and Chariot Races before the Province, unless Livery's Horse resets the price. Play Chariot Races before Wayfarer: a revealed Wayfarer counts as $6 only until you gain a Silver that turn.

**Other cards:**
- **Cursed Village:** draw to 6 works best as the first card played, before Wayfarer. Its Hex lands on you. The bad ones are Plague, Locusts, Misery, Delusion and War. Poverty, Fear and Haunting mostly do nothing when you gain it in your Buy phase. Take 2, maybe 3.
- **Chariot Race:** a cheap Colonnade target and a setup buy for Wayfarer. Its VP tokens add no card to your deck. A high-cost deck (Wayfarer, Cursed Village, Rope) wins the reveal often.
- **Artificer:** sifts, sets up Captain → Wayfarer, and gains a $5 card onto your deck by discarding 5 junk cards. It is a good support card.
- **Corsair:** it does hit Wayfarer decks' Silvers, but the trashed Silver is only a small tax and thins the deck. A weak terminal. Skip it.
- **Road Network:** you draw +1 Card each time the opponent gains a Victory card. It is fine against a money deck greening early, if you have a spare $5 and Buy late. Low priority. (Rated −3.0, and I agree.)

**Ratings check:** Captain 3.8, Rope 3.6 and Mastermind 3.3 all agree with my view. Abundance is rated −0.5 and Chariot Race 0.0. Both are much better here, because they are what makes Wayfarer cheap and they feed Colonnade.

### Build lines
- **Engine (4/3: Rope/Abundance):**
  - T3: Rope plus 3 Copper and an Estate is $4 with 2 Buys: Copper, then Wayfarer for $0.
  - T4: Abundance and $3: Chariot Race, then Wayfarer for $3 with the refund.
  - T5: Rope trashes the Estate. Wayfarer plus $5: Cursed Village.
  - T6: about $5: Livery or a second Cursed Village.
  - T7: Cursed Village, Wayfarer (taking the Silver) and Abundance: 2 Wayfarers at $3 each, +4 VP from Colonnade.
  - T8–9: Province plus a Wayfarer or Captain.
  - T10–11, a typical built turn: Cursed Village → Wayfarer → Cursed Village → Wayfarer → Captain → Livery, giving $16–18, 3 Buys, 2 Provinces, plus 2–4 VP from Colonnade and Chariot Race.
- **Wayfarer-BM floor (Rope/Silver):** T3 Copper plus Wayfarer for $0. Wayfarer's Silvers build the economy. First Province around T7–8, 4 Provinces around T13–14, then one per turn.

**Plans considered:**
1. **Wayfarer engine:** Rope and Abundance for cheap Wayfarers, 2–3 Cursed Villages, 1–2 Captains, Livery as payload, plus Colonnade and Chariot Race.
   - Needs: 2 Buy sources by turn 4, and about 4 Wayfarers by turn 9.
   - Weakness: Hexes, and few Actions until a second village or Captain arrives.
2. **Wayfarer-BM:** its line is above. It is a strong floor, but it can only take one Province per turn and it gives up the Colonnade VP.
3. **Mastermind on Wayfarer or Captain:** a huge single turn, but it needs both cards lined up and has no Buys without Rope. Mastermind fits into plan 1 as a later add.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Mirror:** the Wayfarer and Colonnade split matters. Buy Wayfarers early: the pile is only 10.
- **Against BM:** the engine is ahead on Colonnade VP by turn 8–9 and takes 2 Provinces per turn from turn 10–11. BM has about 3 Provinces then, against the engine's roughly 10 token VP plus Provinces.
- **Corsair:** if they take it, it costs you about one Silver per turn. Tolerable.

**Advocate's case (Mastermind money):** open Livery, then Mastermind. Each Mastermind turn triples Wayfarer for a 13-card hand worth $12 or more, plus Rope for Province + Duchy, from about turn 8. It falls behind around turn 10–11. By then it trails by about 8 Colonnade and Chariot Race VP, and the engine starts taking 2 Provinces per turn while it takes 1 + Duchy. Even so, it is the better money variant.

**Recommendation:** build the Wayfarer engine. It has the highest ceiling, it scores VP that costs no deck slots from turn 7, and its key trick works from turn 3. In a mirror, contest the Wayfarer pile and the Colonnade VP.

**Open:**
- 4/3: Rope / Abundance. Rope / Silver is an acceptable alternative.
- 5/2: Livery / nothing. Livery's +$3 reaches a $6 Wayfarer and gains a Horse with it. Artificer is the alternative to Livery. There is nothing worth buying at $2.

**Buy priorities:**
- First shuffle: cheap Wayfarers on every turn with a spare Buy. A second Rope or Abundance.
- Next: Cursed Village ×2, Livery, Captain ×1–2. Chariot Race as a $3 filler that also takes Colonnade VP.
- Later: Artificer or Mastermind if there is a spare $5.
- Green when the deck makes $16 or more with 2+ Buys, around turn 10. Take Colonnade VP throughout.

**Ending:** most likely the Provinces, around turn 14. The Wayfarer pile can empty fast. A three-pile ending of Wayfarer, Chariot Race and Cursed Village is possible late with lots of Buys, so keep count.

**Watch out for:**
- Bad Hexes (Plague, Locusts, Misery) from Cursed Village: cap it at 2–3 and lean on Captain for Actions.
- Buy order: buy the Province before the Wayfarer and the Wayfarer then costs $8.
- An opponent racing for Colonnade.

**What would flip it:** with 3 or more players, the Hexes still hit only you, Road Network gets better and Colonnade is split more ways. The engine still holds.

**Least sure of:** whether Cursed Village alone gives enough Actions before Captain arrives. If the engine keeps stalling at one terminal, switch to Mastermind on Wayfarer with Rope.

**Assumed:**
- Rulings for Wayfarer's cost:
  - Buying a Copper makes the pile cost $0.
  - A run of Wayfarers keeps the cheap cost.
  - Captain checks Wayfarer's cost at the moment it plays it.
- Abundance stays in play until you gain an Action card.
- A Horse returns to its pile when played, so it never counts for Colonnade.
