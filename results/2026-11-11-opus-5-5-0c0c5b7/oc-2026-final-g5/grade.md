## Beyond the key
- Tools + Villa: each Tools gains a Villa to hand, returns you to the Action phase, and the Villa is played for +2 Actions, +$1, +Buy: plausible. This follows Villa's on-gain text, and the experts used the same back-to-Action trick for Innovation.
- You can't play Treasures after buying, even after Villa returns you to the Action phase: plausible as a conservative assumption, and the analysis flags it. Playing every Tools before the first buy is safe either way.
- Band of Misfits can only play Villa, Harbor Village or Sentinel: plausible. Caravan and Lighthouse are Durations, and the other $5s don't cost less. Calling it "a worse $5 Villa" is doubtful, because it ignores the second-Sentinel use.
- Vault's discard offer only helps the opponent, and Lighthouse's defence is idle: plausible. There are no Attacks in the kingdom.
- Harbor Village's +$1 rider works after Villa, Junk Dealer or Vault: plausible. Each of those can give +$.
- Tools can copy the opponent's Caravan or Lighthouse while they're still in play: plausible. Tools says "a card anyone has in play", and Durations stay out through the opponent's turn.
- Lighthouse as an early $2 non-terminal economy card: plausible from its text, but not discussed by the sources and not used by either player.
- The Junk Dealer + Sentinel trashing pair thins the deck to 3–4 Coppers by T8: plausible on paper, but it runs against the commentators' preference for Sentinel alone (T3).

## Summary
The engine is close to the key: Tools as the gainer copying Villa, Lost City and Gold, Innovation for gain-and-play, few Vaults, and Villas for +Buy. It misses the Sentinel/Caravan opening, adds Junk Dealer as a co-trasher the experts would skip, and warns against the aggressive Lost City buying the commentators said should have emptied the pile. It also dismisses Band of Misfits even as an opening Sentinel copy.
