Here is the full analysis of `kingdoms/oc-2026-final-g5.md`, covering all eight steps of the kingdom-analysis skill.

**Board:** The kingdom piles are Villa ($4), Band of Misfits ($5), Junk Dealer ($5), Lost City ($5), Vault ($5), Lighthouse ($2), Sentinel ($3), Caravan ($4), Harbor Village ($4) and Tools ($4). The landscapes are the Innovation Project ($6) and the Borrow Event ($0). There are no Colonies, no Shelters and no attacks; Vault's discard offer only helps the opponent. The starting deck is the usual 7 Copper and 3 Estate, so the opening splits are 4/3 or 5/2.

This board is for an engine. It has plenty of villages (Villa, Harbor Village, Lost City), strong trashing (Junk Dealer, Sentinel) and +Buy from Villa and Borrow. Tools gains cards without using an Action, and Villa makes that very strong. The open point is draw: the only draw is Lost City (+1 card net), Caravan, and Vault, which is terminal. So the deck has to be thin to draw itself. The only scoring is Provinces, Duchies and Estates.

**Key interactions:**
- **Tools + Villa:** Play Tools in your Buy phase before buying anything. If a Villa is in play, Tools gains a Villa. The Villa goes to your hand, gives +1 Action and sends you back to your Action phase. You play it for +2 Actions, +$1 and +1 Buy, then return to the Buy phase and play the next Tools. So each Tools works out to "+$1, +Buy, and a permanent Villa" for as long as the Villa pile lasts. This works from about turn 7.
- **Tools + Lost City (+ Innovation):** Tools gets you a $5 draw card every turn for a $4 Treasure. With Innovation, the first one each turn is played straight away: +2 Cards, and any Treasures drawn can still be played if you haven't bought yet. Each Lost City you gain gives the opponent +1 card, though.
- **Tools + Gold:** With a Gold in play, Tools gains another Gold. Buy one Gold, then let Tools copy it.
- **Junk Dealer + Sentinel:** A cantrip that trashes one card, plus a trasher that removes up to 2 of the top 5. By about turn 8 the deck is down to roughly 3–4 Coppers.
- **Harbor Village → Villa, Junk Dealer or Vault:** +$1 more per Harbor Village.
- **Borrow:** +$1 and +Buy once per turn, for nothing. The −1 Card token costs almost nothing once you draw your deck. Early on, use it only to reach $5 or $6.

**Other cards:**
- **Caravan:** pairs with the thin deck, giving +1 card at the start of the next turn. It's the draw that doesn't feed the opponent.
- **Lighthouse:** a $2 non-terminal +$1/+$1 that works well before Harbor Village. Its defence does nothing here.
- **Vault (−2.9 in the ratings, but better here):** with spare Actions it's +2 draw, and discarding Provinces and spent junk turns them into $. Take one late at most. Lost City is better at $5.
- **Band of Misfits:** none. It can only play Villa, Harbor Village or Sentinel (Lost City, Vault and Junk Dealer cost $5, not less), so it's a worse $5 Villa.

The ratings agree on Villa (5.8) and Sentinel (4.5). Tools rates 0.47, but Villa raises it a lot on this board.

**Plans considered:**
1. **Villa / Tools / Lost City engine with trashing:** first Province around turn 10, double Provinces from about turn 11. It needs about 4 Villas, 4–5 Lost City or Caravan, 2 Tools and Innovation. Its weakness is thin draw: a bad shuffle stalls it. It also hands cards to the opponent.
2. **Tools-Gold money with Villa/Lost City support:** first $6 around turn 3–4, first $8 around turn 5–6, and 4 Provinces by about turn 13–14. Its weakness is that it stays at one Province per turn.
3. **Plain Big Money + Lost City or Vault:** the floor, with 4 Provinces around turn 15–16.

### Build lines
**Engine** (4/3 split, average draws). Each line shows the hand, what's played and what's bought:
- **T1:** buy Villa.
- **T2:** buy Sentinel.
- **T3:** Villa + 3 Copper + Estate. Villa makes $4, Borrow adds $1, buy Junk Dealer.
- **T4:** you draw only 4 cards (the token). Sentinel trashes Estate and Copper from the top 5. Buy Lighthouse with $2.
- **T5:** Villa and Junk Dealer (trash an Estate) make $5. Buy Lost City.
- **T6:** Lighthouse and Sentinel (trash 2 Coppers) make $4. Buy Tools. The deck is now about 11 cards: 4 Copper, 1 Estate and 6 Actions.
- **T7:** Lighthouse makes $1 at the start of the turn. Lost City, Villa and Junk Dealer draw Tools. Tools gains a Villa, which you play. That gives about $7 and 3 Buys: Gold, plus Borrow.
- **T8:** about $8 and 3 Buys: Lost City + Harbor Village. Tools gains a Gold.
- **T9:** about $11 and 3 Buys: Innovation + Villa. Tools gains a Lost City.
- **T10:** about $13: Province + Gold.
- **Built turn (T11+):** 3 Golds ($9), 4 Villas ($4), Junk Dealer ($1), Harbor Village bonus ($1) and Borrow ($1) make $16 with 5+ Buys, so 2 Provinces. That means about 5 Provinces by turn 12–13.

**Tools-Gold money:**
- **T1–2:** Silver and Tools.
- **T3–4:** Tools copies a Silver; buy Gold or Lost City.
- **T5–8:** Tools copies a Gold about half the time; buy Gold or Province.
- **Result:** about 1 Province per turn from turn 7–8, and 4 Provinces around turn 13–14.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Engine against money:** money has about 2–3 Provinces when the engine's double turns start (turn 11). The engine takes the remaining 5–6 by turn 13–14. Every Lost City you gain gives the money player a card, about 6 cards over the game, or roughly one extra Gold hand. So use Caravan for some draw, and use Tools more on Villas and Golds.
- **Engine mirror:** the Villa pile (10) is contested, and Tools drains it fast. Get your 4–5 early, and green about a turn earlier than you would against money.

**Advocate's case for money:** Open Silver/Tools, add Gold on turn 3–4, and have Tools copy Gold. Two Tools in about 15 cards gain a Gold roughly every other turn, with no Action and no buy spent. Lost City adds non-terminal draw, and Villa makes Borrow's +Buy go further. Its best line has Provinces on turns 7, 9, 10, 11, 12 and 13, which is 5 or 6 by turn 13 if it never misses. Realistically it's 4 by turn 13. The engine pulls ahead on turn 11–12: its two double turns take it from 1 Province to 5 while money goes from 3 to 4. Money only wins if the engine misses a shuffle on turns 11–12, and there are two trashers to prevent that.

**How the pick loses:** By over-buying Lost Cities and flooding a money opponent with cards, or by trashing so many Coppers that it draws everything but has $11 instead of $16. If you see the opponent open Silver/Tools for money, lean on Caravan and Gold for payload rather than Lost City.

**Recommendation:** Build the trash-and-Villa engine, with Tools as the gainer that uses no Action (it copies Villa, Lost City or Gold) and Innovation around turns 8–10. The board has everything an engine needs, and no attacks slow it down.

**Open:**
- **4/3:** Villa / Sentinel. Tools / Sentinel is the alternative if you want Tools early.
- **5/2:** Junk Dealer / Lighthouse.

**Buy priorities:**
- **First shuffle:** trashing (Junk Dealer, a second Sentinel is optional), then Villa.
- **Second shuffle:** Lost City or Caravan for draw, 2 Tools, one Gold for Tools to copy, and Harbor Village if you're short of Actions.
- **Third shuffle:** Innovation, more Villas and Golds.
- **Greening:** start when you reliably reach $16 with 2+ Buys (around turn 10–11). Use Borrow every turn once the deck draws itself.

**Ending:** Provinces most likely, around turns 13–15. A three-pile ending (Villa, Lost City, plus Tools or Lighthouse) is possible in an engine mirror. Count piles once Villa is low.

**Watch out for:**
1. The Villa split in a mirror.
2. Thin draw: if the deck stops drawing itself, add Caravans before payload.
3. With 3 or more players, Lost City feeds everyone and the engine gets relatively slower, so rely more on Caravan and Tools-Gold.

**Least sure of:** The engine's speed. The turn-11 double Province assumes Tools hits a Villa or Gold most turns and the deck draws itself with only about 4 draw cards. If the T7–T9 lines are stalling by turn 9, switch toward the Tools-Gold money plan.

**Assumed:**
- **Treasures after Villa:** After Villa sends you back to the Action phase, I assumed you can't play more Treasures once you've bought anything. That's why every line plays all Tools before the first buy. If Treasures can be played again, it's slightly better.
- **Innovation and Villa together:** you order the two triggers yourself.
- **Band of Misfits:** can't play from an empty pile.
- **Borrow's token:** the −1 Card token applies to your Clean-up draw.
- **Tools:** can copy the opponent's Caravan or Lighthouse while they're still in play on your turn (a minor point).
