## Beyond the key
- Tower makes Curses worth 0 once the Curse pile empties: plausible. Curse isn't a Victory type, so Tower counts it.
- The Curse pile will empty by about T12–15: doubtful. Each player plays Cursed Gold about once a shuffle, and the key's line trashes it on T3, so the 10 Curses may not run out without heavy Ritual use.
- Butcher turns Gold into a Province at no net Coffers cost (+2, spend 2, $6+2): plausible. Estate with 4 Coffers or Cursed Gold with 2 makes a Gold: plausible.
- Butcher on an Estate with 2 Coffers gains a topdecked Nomad Camp: plausible. Nomad Camp costs $4 and is gained onto your deck.
- Ritual cashes a Gold for 6 VP. With Merchant Guild in play, Ritual's Curse gain gives +1 Coffers: plausible. The Curse is gained during the Buy phase.
- Ritual on your own Cursed Gold scores 4 VP and ends its Curse gains: plausible. Cursed Gold costs $4.
- Night Watchman bought in the Buy phase is played the same turn: plausible. It is gained to hand before the Night phase.
- Butcher can't gain City Quarter, and Ritual scores 0 for it: plausible. A Debt cost isn't "up to $X", and its coin cost is $0.
- The opening split odds (6/3 48%, 5/4 40%, 7/2 12%): plausible. They come out to 60/126, 51/126 and 15/126.
- A Fool's Gold + Hound + Curse three-pile threat: plausible by text, with two $2 piles and +Buy available, but minor.
- Embassy is a poor buy because the opponent gets a Silver: doubtful. A one-time Silver for the opponent is cheap against +5 Cards that discards Tunnels for Golds.

## Summary
The analysis reads the Butcher Gold→Province conversion correctly and names the Tunnel→Gold idea, but it lands on the dissenting money-ish plan. It rejects the full City Quarter engine and calls Embassy a poor buy. It misses the key's main tricks: Night Watchman topdecking City Quarter and controlling the shuffle, Cursed Gold Butchered into a second Butcher, and Embassy/Night Watchman discarding Tunnels as the Gold source. It also spends effort on Ritual and Fool's Gold.
