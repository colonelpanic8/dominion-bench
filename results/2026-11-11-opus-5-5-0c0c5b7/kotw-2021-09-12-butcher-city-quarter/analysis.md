# Kingdom analysis: Butcher, City Quarter, Embassy, Faithful Hound, Fool's Gold, Merchant Guild, Night Watchman, Nomad Camp, Pooka, Tunnel, with Ritual and Tower

## 1. Inventory

- **$0 + 8 Debt:** City Quarter. **$2:** Faithful Hound, Fool's Gold. **$3:** Night Watchman, Tunnel. **$4:** Nomad Camp (first edition only), Ritual (Event). **$5:** Butcher, Embassy (first edition only), Merchant Guild, Pooka. Nothing costs more than $5 apart from Gold and Province.
- **Landscapes:** Ritual (Event) and Tower (Landmark).
- **Setup:**
  - No Colonies and no Shelters. There are 10 Curses.
  - Pooka's Heirloom means each player starts with **6 Copper, 1 Cursed Gold and 3 Estates**. That is $9 in total, and playing Cursed Gold gains you a Curse.
- **Special rules in play:** Debt (City Quarter), Coffers (Butcher, Merchant Guild), a Night card (Night Watchman), and Victory tokens (Ritual).
- **Turn 1–2 splits** (playing Cursed Gold):
  - **6/3:** 48%
  - **5/4:** 40%
  - **7/2:** 12%

## 2. First read

1. **Scoring and ending.** The VP sources are:
   - Provinces and Duchies.
   - Tunnels: 2 VP each, 8 in the pile.
   - Ritual tokens. Ritual scores VP equal to the cost of the trashed card: a Gold is worth 6 VP and a $5 Action 5 VP. Neither adds a card to your deck.
   - Tower: 1 VP for each non-Victory card you own from an empty Supply pile.

   The key fact is that **Curses are non-Victory cards**. Once the Curse pile is empty, each Curse is −1 + 1 = 0 VP. Both players playing Cursed Gold, plus any Rituals, will almost surely empty the 10 Curses by about turn 12–15. That also makes Curses one empty pile toward a three-pile ending.
2. **Payload.**
   - Butcher turns Gold into a Province (trash it and spend 2 Coffers, and the play gives back 2). It also turns an Estate into a Gold (4 Coffers).
   - Ritual cashes a Gold for 6 VP at $4.
   - Nomad Camp and Merchant Guild give +Buy. Merchant Guild gives +1 Coffers for the Curse that Ritual gains.
   - Tunnels make Golds when Embassy or Night Watchman discards them.
3. **Draw and Actions.**
   - The only village is City Quarter, and it only draws when the hand is full of Actions.
   - The real draw is City Quarter feeding Faithful Hound, Pooka and Embassy.
   - Each City Quarter costs 8 Debt.
4. **Trashing.**
   - Pooka trashes Coppers.
   - Butcher trashes Estates, Cursed Gold and Curses, and gains something better in return.
   - Ritual trashes one card from hand.
5. **Attacks:** none. Cursed Gold is a self-junker, and Tower makes its Curses free later.
6. **Standout:** the Curse pile is a shared clock. Each Cursed Gold play by either player uses up one future Ritual. So Ritual VP is scarce (maybe 3–5 Rituals in the whole game) and should be taken early.

**What the board is about:** a Gold economy. Butcher and Tunnels make Golds, then Butcher (to Province) and Ritual (to tokens) cash them out, while the Curse pile empties under Tower. Still open: can the City Quarter engine pay its Debt fast enough to beat this?

## Key interactions

- **Butcher + Gold:** trash Gold, spend 2 Coffers, gain a Province. Butcher's own +2 Coffers makes this cost no Coffers, so a Butcher, Gold and $8 hand takes 2 Provinces. Estate with 4 Coffers becomes a Gold, and Cursed Gold with 2 becomes a Gold.
- **Ritual + Gold or a dead $5 card in hand:** +6 or +5 VP for $4 and a Buy. Curses cost nothing once Tower applies. With **Merchant Guild** in play, each Ritual also gives +1 Coffers, so it effectively costs $3. Ritual on your own Cursed Gold scores +4 VP and stops it taking Curses.
- **Tower + Curses:** once the pile is empty, every Curse scores 0. **Play Cursed Gold freely.** Each play also denies the opponent a Ritual.
- **Tunnel + Night Watchman or Embassy:**
  - Night Watchman is non-terminal and is gained straight to hand, so it plays the turn you buy it. It looks at the top 5 cards and discards Tunnels (each gains a Gold) and junk.
  - About 0.5–0.75 Golds per Night Watchman play with 2–3 Tunnels in the deck.
  - Embassy discards Tunnels from hand, but each Embassy you gain gives the opponent a Silver.
- **City Quarter + Faithful Hound or Pooka:**
  - City Quarter draws one card per Action in hand, and Hounds are $2 Actions.
  - A hand of City Quarter, City Quarter, Hound, Hound and Pooka draws 3, then 4 or more.
  - It's real draw, but it needs 3–4 City Quarters, which is 24–32 Debt.
- **Nomad Camp topdecks itself:** buy it on turn 2 and it is guaranteed in your turn 3 hand. Butcher can also gain it onto your deck by trashing an Estate with 2 Coffers.

## Other cards

- **Fool's Gold:**
  - Fodder for Pooka.
  - +1 VP each under Tower if its pile empties.
  - Trash it for a Gold on your deck when the opponent gains a Province.
  - Fool's Gold plus Hound plus Curses is the realistic three-pile.
- **Faithful Hound:** Embassy or Night Watchman discards it back to your hand, giving a 6-card next hand. It's the cheap Action that City Quarter wants.
- **Embassy:** Tunnels and Hounds. Giving the opponent Silvers makes it a poor buy.
- **Pooka:** trashes Coppers for +4 cards, which suits a Butcher deck. Its low rating comes from Cursed Gold, which matters less here thanks to Tower.
- **Ratings check:**
  - Butcher (3.71) and City Quarter (3.66) are the top cards, which matches my view.
  - Ritual (−4.48) is rated badly, but Tower plus a fast-emptying Curse pile makes it much better here.
  - Night Watchman (−3.0) and Hound (−3.0) are low on average, but each has a real partner here (Tunnel; City Quarter).

## Build lines (5/4 or 6/3, average draws)

**Recommended: Butcher Gold economy, light engine.**
- **T1–2:** Butcher / Silver. 1 Curse.
- **T3:** Butcher trashes an Estate and spends 2 Coffers to topdeck a Nomad Camp. $5 buys Pooka.
- **T4:** Nomad Camp plus $4 is $6 with 2 Buys: Gold.
- **T5:** a $4 hand buys City Quarter and pays 4 (4 Debt left).
- **T6:** Pooka trashes a Copper. $6 pays 4 Debt and buys a Hound or Fool's Gold with the rest.
- **T7:** Butcher with 2 saved Coffers trashes an Estate for a Gold. $5 buys Merchant Guild.
- **T8:** $7 buys Gold.
- **T9:** Butcher trashes a Gold for a Province, and $6 buys a Gold.
- **T10 onward:**
  - Province at $8.
  - Butcher Gold to Province whenever you can.
  - Ritual a Gold or Cursed Gold whenever you have a spare Buy and $4, while Curses remain.
- **Typical built turn:** City Quarter, Butcher (Gold to Province), Nomad Camp, Gold, Silver: $9 and 2 Buys. That's 2 Provinces, or a Province plus a Ritual: 12 VP.

**Full City Quarter / Hound / Pooka engine.**
- **T1–2:** Butcher / Silver.
- **T3:** Butcher trashes an Estate to topdeck a Nomad Camp. City Quarter, paying 5.
- **T4:** Nomad Camp, $6: pay the rest of the Debt, buy a Hound.
- **T5–8:** a second City Quarter, more Hounds and Pooka, paying Debt the whole time. Only 1–2 City Quarters by T8.
- **T9–11:** a third and fourth City Quarter.
- **First big turn around T12–13:** about $16 with 3 Buys, for a Province plus 2 Rituals or 2 Provinces: 12–17 VP.

**Floor: Pooka Big Money.** 4 Provinces around T15.

## Plans considered

- **Butcher Gold economy, light engine.**
  - Butcher and Pooka with 1–2 City Quarters bought on weak hands, Nomad Camp and Merchant Guild for Buys, Ritual cash-outs.
  - First $6 on T4, Province from T9.
  - Needs 2 Butchers at most, 1 Pooka, 1–2 Nomad Camps, about 3 Golds.
  - Weakness: it tops out near 2 Provinces per turn.
- **Full City Quarter engine.**
  - Highest ceiling, but 24–32 Debt and a Curse-diluted deck put its first big turn around T12–13.
  - By then the Ritual window has mostly closed.
- **Pooka Big Money:** the floor.
- **Fool's Gold rush:** buy the $2 piles with +Buy, score them through Tower and end on Curses + Fool's Gold + Hound. Too little VP against Provinces.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Against the City Quarter engine,** my plan leads by about 20 VP at T12 (about 3 Provinces plus 1–2 Rituals). The engine has to take 2 Provinces a turn from T13 with only about 4 Provinces left, so it runs out of VP before it catches up.
- **In the mirror,** the Ritual and Curse race decides it. Take Rituals early, and keep playing Cursed Gold to close the window on the opponent.

**Advocate's case for the engine:** City Quarter and Hounds give genuine Lab-village draw, and Debt lets you buy a City Quarter on any hand. It also grows past 2 Provinces per turn while money flattens.
- **Where it falls behind:** both lines reach about T8 with roughly the same economy, but the engine has spent around 16 Debt instead of buying Golds.
- **By T12** it trails by about 18–24 VP, and Rituals left over are few because Cursed Gold plays have emptied the pile.
- It catches up only if the Provinces last until about T18.
- So the light version keeps City Quarter as a weak-hand buy and stops short of the full engine.

## Recommendation

Play the **Butcher Gold economy with a light City Quarter**:
- Butcher makes and converts Golds.
- Pooka thins Coppers.
- Nomad Camp and Merchant Guild supply Buys.
- Take 1–2 City Quarters only on $0–4 hands, so that Butcher, Pooka and Nomad Camp can all be played.
- Spend scarce Rituals early, on Gold, Cursed Gold or dead $5 cards.

This holds up because the opponent's strongest high-ceiling plan only arrives around T13, and the shared Curse clock punishes slow decks.

**Openings:**

| Split | Turn 1 | Turn 2 | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6/3 | Butcher | Silver | Night Watchman on turn 2 can set up your turn 3 hand |
| 5/4 | Butcher | Silver, or Nomad Camp | Nomad Camp bought on turn 2 is guaranteed in your turn 3 hand |
| 7/2 | Butcher | Nothing | Pass on the $2. Gold instead of Butcher is weaker |

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffles 1–2:** Butcher trashes Estates for a topdecked Nomad Camp, a Silver, or a Gold with 4 Coffers. Buy one Pooka.
- **$6–7:** Gold.
- **$3–4:** City Quarter (pay what you can), or Night Watchman.
- **From shuffle 3:**
  - Merchant Guild, and a second Nomad Camp.
  - Use Butcher's Gold-to-Province conversion as soon as you have a Gold and 2 Coffers.
  - Buy Ritual whenever you have a spare $4 and a Buy.
- **Greening:**
  - Provinces from about T9.
  - Duchies at 4 Provinces left.
  - Tunnels or Estates near the end.

**Ending:**
- Most likely on Provinces around T16–18.
- Curses will be empty, so watch Fool's Gold, Hounds and Tunnels for a three-pile when either player has +Buy.

**Watch out for:**
- **A Curse pile that doesn't empty.** Curses are then −1 each, so Ritual is worth 1 less and Cursed Gold plays cost you.
- **An opponent hoarding Fool's Gold.** It sets up a Tower three-pile, so buy one or two yourself to deny it.
- **Embassy gains.** Each one hands the opponent a Silver.

**Least sure of:** that the full City Quarter engine is too slow. If its playtested first big turn comes by about T11, for example with Hounds bought cheaply alongside Merchant Guild Coffers, it beats this plan.

**Assumed:**
- Tower counts Curses, since they aren't Victory cards.
- The Curse that Ritual gains counts as a gain in the Buy phase for Merchant Guild.
- Butcher can't gain City Quarter, because "costing up to $X" excludes Debt costs.
- Ritual scores 0 VP for City Quarter.
- Embassy and Nomad Camp use their first-edition text as given.
