## Beyond the key
- Footpad's below-the-line rule applies even if nobody buys Footpad: plausible. It says "In games using this".
- With Footpad's rule, Artificer's gain onto your deck is drawn at once: plausible. The gain goes onto the deck first, then the gain trigger gives +1 Card.
- Footpad's Coffers let you buy a Hostelry without playing Treasures, so they stay in hand to discard for Horses: plausible. Hostelry's on-gain discards Treasures from hand.
- Stonemason overpay is 3 gains for one Buy ($6 for Stonemason plus 2 Hostelries): plausible. Overpaying $4 gains two Actions costing $4.
- Triumph stacking scores kG + k(k+1)/2: plausible. Each Triumph gains an Estate, which counts toward the next Triumph's total.
- A Weaver the opponent's Footpad attack makes you discard is played for free: plausible from Weaver's Reaction. Whether its gains then draw is doubtful, since it's the opponent's Action phase. The wording "an Action phase" arguably allows it.
- Buying a Project isn't a gain, so it doesn't spoil River Shrine or Exploration: plausible. Projects are bought, not gained.
- Carrying Triumph's Debt turns Change into +$3: plausible from Change's first line. It also stops you buying anything until the Debt is paid.
- Horses don't count for Vineyard: plausible. They return to their pile when played and rarely stay in the deck.
- Stonemason can turn a Silver into 2 Estates to drain the Estates for denial: plausible. Estate costs less than Silver.
- Flourishing Trade doesn't reduce Triumph's Debt cost: plausible. Cost reduction only affects $.
- No rules caveat about Weaver losing track: not raised, so no extra credit.

## Summary
The core engine is right: Footpad-rule draw from gains, Hostelry Horses, Weaver discard-play, and Flourishing Trade Buys for multiple Triumphs. It loses credit for opening River Shrine/Silver instead of Exploration, putting the megaturn about four turns later than testing, and preferring Hut to Artificer. Its clearest error is dismissing Alchemist and Vineyard as Potion-only, missing the Change/Stonemason route the designer built in.
