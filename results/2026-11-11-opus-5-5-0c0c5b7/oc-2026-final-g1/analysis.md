**Board:** There is no real trashing on this board. The only card that leaves your deck is Cage, which trashes itself. Litter is the only village, and it is also non-terminal draw: it works like a Laboratory with an extra +Action, but you take 1 Debt (−$1) each time you play it. Patrol and Council Room are the big terminal draw. Market Square, Astrolabe, Council Room and Seaway all give +Buy. The scoring that stands out is Vineyard: a Potion and $0 buys up to 6–7 VP in an Action-heavy deck. Jester is the only junker, and junk never leaves either deck here. What's still open: how fast a Litter deck runs while carrying its starting cards and paying Debt, and how many Vineyards it can get.

**Key interactions:**
- **Litter/Market Square/Border Guard + Vineyard:** Litter, Market Square and Border Guard are all non-terminal Actions, so they grow the Vineyard count without clogging the deck. With about 15–18 Actions, each Vineyard is 5–6 VP for a Potion and a spare Buy. With one Potion bought around T9–10 and drawn on most turns of a 10–12 card draw, you get about 0.6–0.8 Vineyards per turn from T11, roughly 4–5 Vineyards and 20–25 VP. This is VP a money deck can't match, because its own Vineyards are worth about 0–1 VP.
- **Border Guard + Tunnel:** discarding a revealed card counts as a discard outside Clean-up, so a Tunnel discarded by Border Guard gains a Gold. One Tunnel in a 20-card deck is hit about 10% of the time per Border Guard play (2 cards revealed), or about 15% with Lantern (3 revealed). Too slow to be a Gold engine. It makes Tunnel a fine late green at $3–4 (2 VP plus a chance at a Gold).
- **Way of the Squirrel + Litter:** your last Litter of the turn, played as Squirrel, gives +2 Cards for next turn and **no Debt**. More generally, your last Action each turn can become a 7-card next hand.
- **Jester in a game with no trashing:** a Victory card on top gives a Curse. A Curse on top isn't a Victory card, so you can give them another Curse. A Copper on top gives them another Copper. A Silver, Gold or Action on top gives you a copy. Every play hurts or helps, and none of it can be trashed. Downside: a Tunnel on their deck turns into a Gold plus a Curse for them.

**Other cards:**
- **Patrol:** pairs with all the green and junk here (Vineyards, Tunnels, Estates, Curses). It pulls them into hand, so later draws are cleaner. It's the best terminal draw for this deck.
- **Council Room:** gives Litter's spare Actions +4 Cards and +1 Buy. The card it hands the opponent is a real cost. It is the best X for a money deck.
- **Cage:** on a green turn, set aside up to 4 leftover cards to get a 9-card next hand. Each Market Square still in hand when Cage is trashed gains a Gold. Minor, as a $2 extra Buy.
- **Astrolabe:** early +Buy for cheap Actions. It isn't an Action, so it does nothing for Vineyard. Filler.
- **Seaway:** gains a Market Square or Border Guard, and its +Buy token on Border Guard makes it a mini-Market. It's only worth $5 late, when you have $5 left over.
- **Horn/Lantern:** come free once Border Guards reveal 2 Actions. Lantern sifts better and Horn puts Border Guard back on top of your deck.
- **Ratings:** most of these cards rate low (Jester −1.8, Tunnel −1.7, Council Room −0.9, Patrol −0.5, Litter +0.5). That fits a flat board where the Vineyard/Action count is what's different.

**Plans considered:**
1. **Litter draw deck + Vineyards (pick):**
   - How it works: build with Litter ×3, Patrol ×1–2, Jester ×1–2, Market Squares and a Gold or two. Then each turn take a Province plus a Vineyard.
   - Line: $6 by T6, first $8 by about T9, Province + Vineyard turns from about T11.
   - Needs: about 15 Actions by the end.
   - Weakness: Debt and the starting cards slow it, and the Vineyards can be split in a mirror.
2. **Council Room big money (+1 Jester):**
   - Line: $6 by T3–4, $8 by T5–6, 4 Provinces by T14–15. Jester is played about 1 turn in 3.
   - Weakness: one Province per turn at most, and every Council Room play feeds our deck.
3. **Cheap-Action Vineyard rush:** Potions ×2, Market Square and Border Guard on every Buy, with three-piling Vineyards, Border Guard and Market Square as the threat. Its money can't keep up (see the advocate's case).

### Build lines
- **Litter deck (P1, 4/3):**
  - T1–2: Silver/Silver.
  - T3: $5, Jester.
  - T4: $4, Market Square (or Silver).
  - T5: Litter.
  - T6: Jester plus $4 makes $6, Litter.
  - T7: Patrol.
  - T8: Litter played (−$1), $5, Litter.
  - T9: Litter + Patrol draw about 10 cards, about $8 minus $1–2 Debt. Gold, or Potion plus Market Square.
  - T10: 19–20 cards, 2 Litters + Patrol + Jester draw about 11–12 cards. About $9–10 minus $2 Debt, 2 Buys: Province, or Potion + Market Square.
  - Built turn (T11+): about 13 of 22 cards, $8–11, 2–3 Buys, Potion in hand about 70% of the time. Province + Vineyard (5 VP) is about 11 VP, plus a Tunnel or Border Guard with leftover $.
- **Council Room big money:**
  - T1–2: Silver/Silver.
  - Then: Council Room, then Gold at $6–7 and Province at $8.
  - Result: 4 Provinces by T14–15, all 8 Provinces alone around T19–20.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Pick vs Council Room big money:**
  - The money deck leads until about T12 (roughly 3 Provinces to our 1).
  - From T11 we score about 11 VP per turn to their 6. Jester has junked them with 2–3 Curses or Coppers, and their Council Rooms keep handing us cards.
  - We pull ahead around T14–15 and stay ahead as Provinces run low. Their best counter is a Potion to deny Vineyards, which costs their deck about a turn.
- **In a mirror:** Vineyards split about 4–4 and the Jester fight decides it. Get your Potion one shuffle earlier and keep Patrol for the Curses.
- **Runner-up vs pick:** the money deck needs the Provinces gone by about T15 to win, and it can't do that alone.

**Advocate's case:** The Vineyard rush opens Potion/Market Square and buys cheap Actions on every Buy, aiming for 8 Vineyards at 6–7 VP (about 50 VP) plus a three-pile ending.
- The economy can't support it. With 7 Coppers, 2 Silvers and a dead Potion, a hand makes about $4.
- That gives about 2 cheap gains per turn, and the Potion is drawn only every ~3 turns.
- By T16 it has about 5 Vineyards × 6 = 30 VP and isn't close to three piles. Money has about 5 Provinces (30 VP) plus Duchies at that point and is still scoring.
- It falls behind for good around T13–16. Its useful part, Vineyards bought with spare Buys, is folded into the pick.

**How the pick loses:** If the opponent plays money plus Jester and junks us early before Litter is running, our start slows by 2+ turns. The other risk is that they take 3–4 Vineyards just to deny us. Both are visible from their T1–4 buys. If either happens, buy the Potion earlier and lean on Provinces.

**Recommendation:** Build the Litter draw deck. Use its spare Buys on Market Squares and Border Guards, add one Potion (two if uncontested) around T9–10, and score with Province + Vineyard. Jester is both payload and junker. It beats Council Room big money mainly because Vineyards are VP money can't get, and because Jester's Curses can't be trashed.

**Open:**
- **4/3:** Silver/Silver, so you hit $5 for Jester and Litter. Silver/Market Square is close.
- **5/2:** Jester/Border Guard. Jester is the first $5 so the junking starts early. Border Guard is the only useful $2 at the start.

**Buy priorities:**
- First $5: Jester. Then Litter ×3, Patrol ×1–2, and a second Jester.
- $3–4: Market Square ×2–3, ahead of Silver from shuffle 2.
- Spare $2 with a spare Buy: Border Guard.
- Potion once you draw 10+ cards a turn (about T9–10).
- Green from about T11: Province + Vineyard every turn. Tunnels with $3–4 left over. Duchies when 3–4 Provinces are left.
- Play your last Litter as Squirrel whenever its +2 Actions aren't needed.

**Ending:** Most likely on Provinces around T16–18. Curses can empty in a Jester mirror. Vineyards (8), Market Square and Border Guard make a three-pile possible only in an Action-heavy mirror. Count those piles from T12.

**Watch out for:**
- Opponent Jester early: get Patrol sooner.
- Opponent buying Potions: Vineyards turn into a race. Take them earlier or switch to Duchies.
- With 3+ players: Jester and Patrol get better, Council Room feeds more opponents, and Vineyards split thinner, so lean on Provinces.

**Least sure of:** whether the Litter deck, held back by Debt and the starting cards, really reaches Province + Vineyard turns by T11. My paper line runs about $1–2 short on T9–10. If your play shows it slipping to T13+, a lighter version beats it: Council Room big money with 1–2 Litters, a Jester and a Potion for Vineyards.

**Assumed:**
- Border Guard discarding a revealed Tunnel triggers Tunnel.
- A Curse turned up by Jester isn't a Victory card, so Jester can give a copy of it.
- Litter played as Squirrel creates no Debt.
- Squirrel's and Cage's cards arrive after the Clean-up draw.
- Glicko ratings are from the 20-Jul-2026 snapshot.
