## Beyond the key
- Lurker trashing Silk Merchant from the Supply for +1 Coffers/+1 Villager, then gaining it back from the trash for the same again: plausible. Silk Merchant triggers on both gain and trash, and Lurker does both; a strong loop the key doesn't mention.
- Highway cost table (3 Highways: Province $5, Silk Merchant $1; 4 Highways: Province $4, Silk Merchant $0): plausible. Arithmetic matches Highway's -$1 per copy in play.
- Figurine, Silvers, Golds and Tools are single-use once Panic is on, so money plans collapse: plausible. Panic returns every Treasure discarded from play to its pile.
- Tools can gain a Tools to replace itself after Panic (net +2 Buys per play): plausible. Tools is in play when it resolves and can copy itself.
- Tools can copy a Reckless Transmute while it's in play: plausible. Tools ignores cost and Transmute stays in play until discarded.
- Poet with 2 Highways is "almost a Laboratory": plausible. $5 cards cost $3 then, so Poet's reveal catches most of the deck.
- Prepare lets set-aside terminals all play next turn without spending Actions: plausible. Prepare plays them at start of turn, not as Action-phase plays.
- Panic needs 5 Sun in 2 players (8 for 3): plausible. Matches the Rising Sun Prophecy setup counts.
- Lurker trashing Kitsunes from the Supply for denial, then regaining them: plausible but slow. It works by card text; whether it is worth the plays is unclear.

## Summary
The analysis reads Panic well, finding the Copper self-thinning and the Highway + Panic-Buys payload, and adds a strong Lurker/Silk Merchant loop. But it explicitly dismisses Reckless Transmute, which the designer built the board around, and so misses the Transmute-vs-Panic decision on Coppers and the Lurker/Transmute endgame. A player following it would build a plausible Highway engine but ignore the board's intended centerpiece.
