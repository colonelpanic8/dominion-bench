**Kingdom analysis: Transmute (Reckless), Silk Merchant, Lurker, Council Room, Figurine, Shop, Poet, Highway, Tools, Kitsune · Panic, Prepare** (2 players, no Colonies, no Shelters, you can choose a 5/2 or 4/3 opening)

**Board:** This board is about **Panic**. Panic switches on after 5 Omen plays in total from both players; only Kitsune and Poet are Omens. Kitsune is a village that also curses, so both players will play it from turn 3, and Panic should be on by about **turn 6–8**. From then on every Treasure is single-use: playing it gives +2 Buys, and it goes back to its pile after the turn. That ruins money decks: your Golds vanish. It helps Action decks, because their Coppers clear themselves out and they get lots of Buys. So the money has to come from things that aren't Treasures: Coffers (from Silk Merchant and Lurker), Kitsune's +$2, Shop's +$1, and above all **Highway's cost reduction combined with all those Buys**. What's still open: the only Curse trasher is Transmute, which needs a Potion, and the Highway pile (10) is contested.

**Key interactions:**
- **Lurker + Silk Merchant:** Lurker trashes a Silk Merchant from the Supply, and Silk Merchant's "when you trash this" gives you +1 Coffers and +1 Villager. Your next Lurker takes that Silk Merchant back from the trash, which pays the same again. Each $2 Lurker played gives +1 Action, +1 Villager and +1 Coffers, and every second play also adds a +2 Cards/+Buy card to your deck. This works from turns 3–4.
- **Highway + Panic Buys:** Each Highway in play makes everything $1 cheaper, and each Treasure played gives +2 Buys. This payload multiplies like Bridge does:

  | Highways in play | Province | Estate | Silk Merchant |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | 3 | $5 | $0 | $1, and its on-gain Coffer pays that back, so it's free |
  | 4 | $4 | $0 | $0, so every spare Buy is worth +$1 (+1 Coffers) and +1 Villager, until the pile runs out |

  A built turn takes 2–3 Provinces plus free Estates.
- **Panic thins for you:** About 7 Coppers leave your deck during the first shuffle after Panic, and each one gives +2 Buys on the way out. Kitsune's Silvers and Tools work the same way: one use each, plus 2 Buys.
- **Kitsune with Lurker:** Lurker can trash Kitsunes from the Supply, which denies your opponent their curser and village, and a later Lurker gains them from the trash. That's a $5 card gained for free.
- **Poet + Highway:** Poet puts the revealed top card into your hand if it costs $3 or less. With 2 Highways in play, every $5 card counts, so Poet is almost a Laboratory.

**Other cards:**
- **Council Room:** your +4 Cards. Kitsune and Villagers let you play it. It's the strongest draw here, but the card it gives your opponent helps their engine. Take 1, maybe 2.
- **Tools:** before Panic it gains a copy of your best card in play (Highway, Kitsune, or Silk Merchant for its on-gain bonus). After Panic it can either gain a Tools to replace itself (net +2 Buys a turn) or be used up for one gain. Take 1.
- **Shop:** lets you play a terminal without spending an Action (Shop into Council Room gives +5 Cards and +$1). It's filler at $3.
- **Prepare:** if a hand has terminals you can't play, set it aside and they all play next turn without needing Actions. Treasures in that hand still give +2 Buys under Panic. It's a fallback for when you're short on Actions.
- **Transmute (Reckless):** each play trashes 2 cards. Its only partner here is Curses, since it's the only way to trash them. It can also turn 2 Estates into 2 Golds, but the Golds are single-use after Panic. Late on it can turn 2 spare Actions into 2 Duchies, which is +6 VP, and Tools can copy a Transmute while it's in play. It's a niche late Duchy/Curse tool, not part of the plan.
- **Figurine:** none. It's draw that comes as a Treasure, so Panic makes it single-use.

**Ratings check:**
- **Agree:** Silk Merchant (+6.84) and Kitsune (+4.48) really are the core.
- **Rate higher here:** Lurker (−0.37) is a top piece because of the Silk Merchant loop. Council Room (−0.94) is the best +Cards on the board.
- **Agree with low ratings:** Figurine (−2.95) and Transmute (−6.84). Reckless makes Transmute playable, not good.

**Plans considered:**
1. **Kitsune / Silk Merchant / Highway engine with the Lurker loop (the pick).**
   - Open Kitsune/Lurker. Panic is on around turn 6.
   - About 3 Highways by turn 9–10. First turn with 2+ Provinces around turn 10–11. Plays Kitsune almost every turn from turn 3.
   - Needs 3–4 Highways and 2 Kitsunes. Both piles are contested.
   - Weakness: Curses stay in the deck for good.
2. **BM + Council Room or Kitsune (the floor).** Silver, Silver, then Gold or Council Room; $6 on turn 3–4, $8 around turn 5–6. Its own Kitsune plays (or the opponent's) turn on Panic around turn 7, and then every Gold it played is gone. It gets about 2 Provinces before the deck falls apart. **Dead.**
3. **Potion opening into Transmute.** Estates become Golds around turn 5–6, then Duchies late. Too slow, it doesn't attack, and Panic makes the Golds single-use (see the advocate's case below).

### Build lines
- **Engine (5/2):**
  - **T1–2:** Kitsune, Lurker.
  - **T3:** Kitsune + 3 Coppers. Kitsune for +$2 and a Curse, giving $5. Buy Kitsune #2.
  - **T4:** Lurker + 3 Coppers. Lurker trashes a Silk Merchant from the Supply (+1 Coffers, +1 Villager). Spend $3 + 1 Coffers on a Silk Merchant (+1 Coffers, +1 Villager).
  - **T5:** Kitsune (+2 Actions, Curse), Silk Merchant draws Lurker, Lurker gains the Silk Merchant from the trash (+1 Coffers, +1 Villager). $3 + 2 Coffers: buy Highway.
  - **T6:** Kitsune for $2 and a Curse, giving $5: buy Council Room. Panic switches on around now (about 3 Sun from you, about 3 from the opponent).
  - **T7:** Kitsune (+2 Actions, $2), Highway, Council Room, Silk Merchant, Lurker via a Villager (+1 Coffers). Four Coppers give $4 and +8 Buys, then go back to the Supply. With Highway in play: buy Highway ($4) and Silk Merchant ($3, +1 Coffers).
  - **T8–9:** keep adding Highway, Kitsune, Tools and Silk Merchants. Coppers are mostly gone by turn 9, leaving a deck of about 14 cards plus Curses.
  - **Built turn (about turn 10–11):** 3 Highways, 2 Kitsunes ($4), 2 Lurkers (+2 Coffers), Shop ($1), about 5 banked Coffers, Tools (+2 Buys), Silk Merchant/Council Room Buys. That's about $11 with Provinces at $5: **2 Provinces + 2 free Estates, about 14 VP.**
- **BM + Council Room:**
  - **T1–2:** Silver, Silver.
  - **T3–6:** Council Room, Gold, Gold, Province.
  - **From about T7:** Panic is on. Each Gold or Silver played disappears, and the deck shrinks to green cards and Curses. Stalls at about 2–3 Provinces.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Highway engine | ~T10 | ~T13–15 (Provinces or three piles) | 2–3 Provinces + Estates | Provinces, Estates, Duchies |
| BM + Council Room | ~T6 | never (collapses under Panic) | 1 Province | ~3 Provinces |

**Recommendation:** **Build the Kitsune / Silk Merchant / Highway engine, and run the Lurker + Silk Merchant loop for Villagers and Coffers.** Choose to have Panic on, not to dodge it. It clears out your Coppers and gives you Buys, and money plans can't survive it. Against an opponent building the same deck, the game comes down to the Curse split, the Highway split, and who has the Silk Merchants in the trash. Lurker helps with all three.

**Against their best plan:** The engine mirror is the real matchup.
- **Curse split:** use Kitsune for Curse + $2 or Curse + Actions every time until the Curse pile empties (around turn 12).
- **Highway split:** pick up Highways early; Tools can copy one while it's in play.
- **Denial:** use Lurker to trash Kitsunes and Highways from the Supply once you have enough, then gain them back yourself.
- **Council Room:** keep to 1, because its extra card feeds the opponent's engine.

If the opponent plays money, Panic beats them by turn 8. Just don't hand them a three-pile ending.

**Advocate's case (Potion opening into Transmute):**
- **T1–2:** Potion, Lurker.
- **T3–4:** Transmute.
- **T5–6:** Transmute trashes 2 Estates for 2 Golds.
- **T7+:** Transmute keeps itself going by trashing Coppers, and Tools copies it. Late on, 2 spare Actions become 2 Duchies.

Where it falls behind:
- **Turn 4:** the opponent already has 2 Kitsunes and has given their first Curse, while this plan has no village and no attack.
- **About turn 7:** Panic arrives just as its Golds do, so each Gold is used once.
- **Turn 8:** it is about 3 Curses down, with 1–2 engine pieces against about 6.

It never catches up. The bigger version of the pick is to aim for 4 Highways, at which point Silk Merchant costs $0 and every Buy turns a profit. That is already the late part of the plan.

**How the pick loses:** On Curses and Highways. You end up on the short side of the Curse split (6+ Curses, and nothing but Transmute to trash them), or the opponent gets 6 or more of the 10 Highways. Both show up in your opponent's first four buys.

**Open:**
- **5/2:** Kitsune / Lurker. Recommended: you curse first and push Panic along.
- **4/3:** Silk Merchant / Lurker, or Silk Merchant / Silk Merchant if you spend the first Silk Merchant's Coffer on turn 2. Then use Coffers to reach $5 for Kitsune on turn 3–4.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffles 1–2:** Kitsune ×2, Silk Merchants (spend Coffers on them), Highway, 1 Council Room. Lurker any time you have $2 to spare.
- **Shuffle 3:** Highways up to 3–4, Tools ×1, Shop with leftover money.
- **Greening:** start when 3 Highways reliably land together (about turn 10). Then take 2+ Provinces a turn and use spare Buys on $0 Estates. Use Transmute for Duchies only if Potion money is spare.

**Ending:**
- **Provinces:** Highway turns can take 3 or more Provinces in a turn.
- **Three piles:** a real risk. Likely piles are Curses (about turn 12), Silk Merchant (drained by Lurker and cheap buys), and Estates (free with Highways), or Highway/Kitsune once Lurker starts trashing them.
- **Plan for it:** when you're ahead, end the game on piles with free Estates. When you're behind, stop draining Silk Merchants.

**Watch out for:**
- **The Highway split.** Contest it and use Lurker to deny.
- **Getting the worse half of the Curses.** Never skip a Kitsune Curse early.
- **Panic coming later than expected.** If neither player plays Omens, treat Silver/Gold as normal economy. Poet plays bring Panic sooner.
- **More players:**
  - Panic needs more Sun (8 for 3 players) but there are more Omen plays to supply it.
  - Kitsune's Curses spread across more opponents.
  - Council Room's extra card goes to everyone.
  - Three piles end the game sooner.

**Least sure of:** Whether 5/2 Kitsune/Lurker beats 4/3 Silk Merchant/Silk Merchant. Coffers and Villagers early might be worth more than one extra Curse; a playtest of both openings would settle it. I'm also unsure exactly when Panic switches on, and how much money a deck without Treasures really has before the Highways come together.

**Assumed:**
- **Lurker trashing Silk Merchant from the Supply:** the trashing player gets the "when you trash this" Coffers and Villager. This matches how Dominion Online handles it.
- **Reckless Transmute:** it does two separate trash-and-gain steps, and it returns to its pile after it's played.
- **Tools:** it can copy a Reckless or Panic card while that card is still in play.
- **Prepare:** cards it plays at the start of the next turn don't use Actions.
- **Rules not in the repo context:** the Panic and Reckless interactions are read straight from the card text provided.
