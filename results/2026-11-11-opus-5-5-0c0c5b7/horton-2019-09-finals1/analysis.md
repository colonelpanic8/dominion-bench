# Kingdom analysis: Hermit, Diplomat, Treasure Trove, Caravan, Bridge, Hireling, Outpost, Rogue, Old Witch, Vassal, with Sewers and Ferry

**Board:** This is an Old Witch board, and Ferry makes it much stronger. Ferry moves your −$2 token onto Old Witch, so Witches cost you $3. That puts a Witch in your opening, and it lets Hermit ("gain a card costing up to $3") gain Witches for free. The only junker is Old Witch, and there is no real village. Diplomat gives +2 Actions only if you have 5 or fewer cards in hand after it draws. Madman is a one-shot. So the open questions are two: who wins the Curse split, and whether the Madman + Bridge burst turn is worth building once the Curses run out.

**Inventory:**
- **Prices:** $3 is crowded and important: Hermit, Vassal, Sewers, Ferry, and Old Witch after Ferry. $5 has Old Witch, Treasure Trove, Outpost and Rogue. Hireling is the only card at $6. There is nothing useful at $2 unless Ferry goes on Bridge.
- **Extra piles:** Madman is a non-Supply pile.
- **Durations:** Caravan, Hireling and Outpost.
- **Starting deck:** the standard 7 Copper and 3 Estate. Players arrange their own decks, so you choose the split. Choose 4/3.

**Key interactions:**
- **Ferry + Old Witch:** On turn 1, buy Ferry with $3 or $4 and put the token on Old Witch. On turn 2, buy Old Witch at $3. You have a turn-2 junker, and every later Witch costs $3, or $2 with Bridge in play.
- **Hermit + Ferry'd Old Witch:** Each Hermit play gains a free Old Witch, while Witches are still worth having, and trashes an Estate or Curse from your hand or discard pile. When the Curses are gone, switch the gain to Hermits, so you get more Madmen.
- **Sewers + every trash:** Sewers gives an extra trash from hand each time you trash. The triggers are:
  - your Hermit trashing something;
  - trashing a Curse from your hand when the opponent's Old Witch hits you;
  - under the 2018 wording, Hermit trashing itself when it turns into a Madman.

  So Sewers is effectively about 2 trashes per Hermit, and it partly undoes each enemy Witch play. That makes it much better than its rating of 0.69.
- **Madman + Bridge (the high-ceiling turn):** Madman played from a 5-card hand gives +2 Actions and draws to 8 cards. A second Madman draws about 7 more, which is roughly your whole deck. Every Bridge you then play adds +1 Buy and +$1 and makes Provinces $1 cheaper.
  - Example: 3 Bridges give Provinces at $5 and 4 Buys. A deck with about $13 of Treasure takes 3 Provinces that turn.
  - Hermit gains Hermit, which keeps Madmen coming. Rogue can also gain Hermits back out of the trash.
  - Late in the game, buy Ferry again to move the token to Bridge. Bridges then cost $2, Hermit can gain them, and so can a Bridge-reduced buy.
- **Diplomat + the opponent's Old Witch:** When they play a Witch, reveal Diplomat: draw 2, then discard 3, which can include Curses and Estates. You start your next turn with 4 cards. Diplomat played from that hand is a real village: 3 left + 2 drawn = 5, so you get +2 Actions. Outpost's 3-card turn does the same thing.

**Other cards:**
- **Caravan:** No special partner. It is a non-terminal +1 card now and +1 next turn, which is good filler in a deck short on Actions.
- **Hireling:** Its partner is the lack of Actions. A permanent 6-card hand costs no Action, which is worth a lot in a long game. Get one at your first spare $6.
- **Treasure Trove:** Its partner is the long, cursed game. It is non-terminal and gains a Gold each play. The Copper it also gives is the cost.
- **Outpost:** Pairs with Diplomat, which becomes a village on the 3-card turn, and with Hireling or Caravan for the bonus cards. It is terminal and collides with Old Witch, so it is optional, at most one copy late.
- **Rogue:** Pairs with the trashed Hermits. The trash will rarely be empty, so Rogue usually gives +$2 and gains a Hermit rather than attacking. It is weak.
- **Vassal:** Pairs with Old Witch or Madman flipped off the deck, but it is terminal and unreliable. Skip it.
- **Ratings check** (Ferry 4.14, Bridge 4.07, Hermit 3.68, Old Witch 3.38, Outpost 2.88, Rogue −3.28): my view broadly agrees. Two exceptions:
  - Sewers rates higher here, because the opponent's Old Witch keeps giving it triggers.
  - Diplomat (−1.04) rates a little higher, because of the reaction-then-village trick.

### Plans considered
1. **Old Witch, Ferry and Hermit good-stuff, then Bridge and Madman cash-out (pick).**
   - **How it works:** Win the Curse war with 2–4 Witches, all bought at $3 or gained by Hermit. Thin with Hermit and Sewers. Treasure Trove and Hireling build the money. Bridge gives +Buy, and Madmen give occasional double-terminal turns.
   - **Needs:** Ferry on turn 1–2 is essential, plus at least 2 Hermits.
   - **Weakness:** A Witch mirror is long. Old Witch is terminal and collides with itself.
2. **Hermit, Madman and Bridge burst deck, no Witch (advocate's plan).**
   - **How it works:** Open Hermit and Ferry on Bridge. Hermits gain Hermits and Bridges. Save Madmen for one Bridge turn that takes 3–4 Provinces.
   - **Needs:** About 3 Madmen, 3–4 Bridges and about $12 of Treasure at the same time.
   - **Weakness:** It takes 6–7 Curses from an opponent on plan 1.
3. **BM + Old Witch at full price (the floor).** Open Old Witch on a 5/2 split, add Silver and Gold, play a Witch about one turn in two.
   - **Weakness:** It is strictly worse than plan 1, which gets the same Witch on turn 2 plus cheaper copies and Hermit gains.

### Build lines (paper, average draws)
**Plan 1:**
- **T1–T2:** Ferry (token on Old Witch), then Old Witch at $3.
- **T3–T4:** Old Witch appears on one of these turns about 80% of the time. That turn, Old Witch draws 3 for about $5 and you buy Treasure Trove. The other turn has $3–4: buy Hermit.
- **T5–T6:** Old Witch play: Curse the opponent and buy Silver, or a second Witch at $3. Hermit play: trash an Estate and gain Witch #2 free. Treasure Trove gains a Gold. Buy Sewers on a $3–4 hand.
- **T7–T8:** Witch plays, and one Curse-free Witch collision. On a weak hand with Hermit, play Hermit, gain a Hermit and buy nothing, so it becomes a Madman. Buy Gold or Hireling at $6.
- **T9–T10:** The deck is about 8 Copper, 1 Estate, 3 Witches, Treasure Trove, 2–3 Gold, Silver, Hireling, Madman, 1 Hermit and about 3 Curses received. Hands produce $6–8. You have given out about 5–6 of the Curses.
- **Built turn (about T11+):** With Madman, play Old Witch and Bridge. That gives about $10 and 2 Buys, enough for Province ($7 with Bridge) + Duchy ($4) or Silver. Without Madman, play Old Witch for about $8, a Province.

**Plan 2:**
- **T1–T2:** Hermit, then Ferry on Bridge.
- **T3–T6:** Gain Hermits and Bridges (Bridge costs $2). Take a Curse on most of the opponent's turns from T3 on.
- **T7–T10:** Turn 2–3 Hermits into Madmen. The deck holds about 5 Curses received, partly trashed by Hermit.
- **First burst (about T12–13):** Madman draws to 8, Madman draws the deck, then 3 Bridges for about $13–14 and 4 Buys. That is 2–3 Provinces.

| Plan | Starts scoring | Can end by | Peak VP/turn | VP ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Ferry-Witch, Hermit | ~T10 | ~T17–20 (Provinces, or a three-pile ending) | Province + Duchy | Province/Duchy split, plus the Curse split (~±4 VP) |
| 2. Madman-Bridge burst | ~T12–13 | ~T18+ | 2–3 Provinces once, then rebuild | Reduced by about 6 Curses taken |
| 3. BM + Witch at $5 | ~T10 | ~T18 | Province | Loses the Curse split to plan 1 |

**Against their best plan:**
- **Plan 1 vs plan 1:** The Curse split decides it. Hermit gaining Witches, Sewers, and Diplomat as a sifter and village after each enemy Witch play are the edges. Once the Curses are gone, re-Ferry onto Bridge. Treat the two-Bridge and Madman turns as where your extra Duchies come from.
- **Plan 1 vs plan 2:** Plan 1 gives about 6 Curses. That is −6 VP for them, and their burst turn shrinks from 3–4 Provinces to about 2.

**Advocate's case (plan 2):** Its fastest version is the line above. Madmen are free villages that draw whole decks, and Bridges make Provinces cost $4–5. Hermit trashes the Curses it takes, and Sewers doubles that. Its first burst is about T12–13 for about 3 Provinces.
- **Where it falls behind:** By T8 it has taken about 4 Curses, which is −4 VP. Each Hermit play then spends its trash on a Curse instead of a Copper or Estate, so it keeps less money for the burst. On T12–13, plan 1 already has about 2–3 Provinces plus a 4–6 VP lead from the Curse split. Plan 2's one burst roughly evens the score, and then it has no Madmen and a greener deck.
- **Verdict:** Plan 1 stands, but steal plan 2's Bridge and Madman turn as your cash-out once the Curses are gone.

**Recommendation:** Plan 1, Old Witch via Ferry with Hermit and Sewers thinning, cashing out with Bridge and Madman. It wins or holds the Curse war against any reply, and it keeps the Bridge burst available for the later game.

**Open:**
- **4/3 (choose this one):** Ferry on Old Witch, then Old Witch at $3.
- **5/2 (only if forced):** Old Witch at $5 and nothing with the $2. Buy Ferry with your first spare $3.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffles 1–2:** A second Witch at $3 (or gained by Hermit), Hermit, Sewers and Treasure Trove at $5.
- **Next:** Hireling or Gold at $6, then Caravan or Diplomat at $4, then 1–2 Bridges.
- **Green:** Start once you reliably hit $8, around T10–11. Use Bridge for Province + Duchy.
- **After the Curses run out:** Re-buy Ferry with a spare Buy and $3 to put the token on Bridge, and switch Hermit's gains to Hermits and Bridges.

**Ending:** The Curses will empty. Old Witch (cheap and gained by Hermit) and Duchies can empty too, so a three-pile ending of Curses + Witches + Duchies is realistic in a mirror. Otherwise the Provinces run out around T18–20. Count the piles once the Curses are gone.

**Watch out for:**
1. **Overbuying Witches.** More than 3–4 terminals collide with only one Action a turn. Once the Curses are gone, Witches are just Moat-like +3 cards.
2. **An opponent who goes Sewers + Diplomat.** That blunts your Witch war, so lean harder on Bridge and money.
3. **A big Madman stash on their side.** It signals a burst turn, so check how many Bridges they hold before you take the penultimate Province.

**Least sure of:** Whether the Madman + Bridge burst, started around T9 in the mirror after the Curse war, beats steady Province + Duchy turns. To test it, play out a mirror where one side starts converting Hermits from T8.

**Assumed:**
- Hermit uses the tournament's 2018–2019 wording: it is trashed when exchanged for a Madman. That trash fires Sewers and fills the trash, which turns off Rogue's attack. I'm not sure Sewers can reliably trash from hand when Hermit is trashed during Clean-up, so I didn't count on that trigger.
- Ferry's discount applies when Hermit checks a gain's cost on your turn.
- With one Buy per turn, Ferry and the Ferry'd Witch can't be bought on the same turn.
