## Beyond the key
- Forge on Hunting Grounds + a $2 card gains a Province, plus a Duchy from Hunting Grounds' trigger (9 VP): plausible. $6 + $2 = $8 exactly; the on-trash gain fires.
- Forge on a lone Hunting Grounds (or Hunting Grounds + Coppers) gains a new Hunting Grounds plus a Duchy: plausible. Copper costs $0, so the total stays $6.
- Forge totals on Treasure (Gold + Estate, Silver + Silver + Estate = $8; three Estates = $6): plausible. Arithmetic from printed costs.
- Trader on Hunting Grounds gains 6 Silvers and a Duchy: plausible. Trader gains a Silver per $1 of cost, and Hunting Grounds' trigger fires.
- Trader's reaction can swap each Estate from a Hunting Grounds trigger for a Silver: plausible. Each gained card is a separate "when you gain" with Trader still in hand.
- Trashing an Encampment to Forge "costs nothing" because it returns to its pile anyway: doubtful. Only a played Encampment returns; trashing one from hand forgoes its +2 Cards +2 Actions, which is the cost.
- Mandarin topdecks Hunting Grounds or Forge to pair them next turn: plausible. Its play puts any card from hand on the deck.
- Coin of the Realm on the Tavern mat can't be Forge fodder; in hand it's $2: plausible. Forge trashes only from hand.
- Scrying Pool's +1 Action doesn't make it a village and every Treasure/Victory stops its reveal: plausible. Card text.
- A 3-Forge Scrying Pool turn is about 33 VP: plausible arithmetic (3 Provinces + 3 Duchies + a bought Province), speculative that it's reachable.
- Plunder is reachable only once the Encampments are gone and they keep returning: plausible. Returned Encampments go back on top of the split pile.

## Summary
The analysis gets the core of one of the key's two competitive decks: Forge as thinner and payload, Hunting Grounds as draw and fuel, Coin of the Realm as the village, and the no-+Buy cap. It misses the tested Silver/Silver opening (falling for the Trader/Silver trap, which delays $6) and underrates the Scrying Pool/Encampment deck, missing how its attack sets up Noble Brigand against the Hunting Grounds player's Golds.
