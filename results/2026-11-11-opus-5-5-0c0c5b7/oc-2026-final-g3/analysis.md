**Kingdom analysis: Council Room, Horn of Plenty, Inn, Patrol, Upgrade, Overlord, Forager, Walled Village, Golem, Bandit Camp; Event: Scrounge**

Setup is 2 players, no Colonies and no Shelters. The starting deck is 7 Copper and 3 Estates, so the opening splits are 4/3 or 5/2. Extra rules in play: Debt (Overlord), Potion (Golem), and the non-Supply Spoils pile (Bandit Camp). Most of the good cards cost $5. At $4 there is only Walled Village. At $3 there are Forager and Scrounge. Nothing worth buying costs $2.

---

**Board:** This board supports an engine. It has three villages (Bandit Camp, Walled Village, Inn), two terminal draw cards (Council Room and Patrol), two trashers that don't use up your Action (Upgrade and Forager), and +Buy on Council Room and Forager. There are no attacks. What makes the board unusual is Scrounge. Once Estates are in the trash, $3 and a Buy get you an Estate plus any card costing up to $5. That makes it a cheap way to gain $5 engine pieces early and a cheap way to score late (Estate + Duchy = 4 VP for $3).

**Key interactions:**
- **Trash an Estate (Forager or Upgrade), then Scrounge it back:** Forager trashes an Estate, and its +Buy then pays for Scrounge, which returns that Estate plus a $5 card (Bandit Camp, Council Room, Upgrade). In effect a $3 hand buys a $5 piece, and this works from turn 3. The opponent's trashed Estates are in the same trash, so either player can take them.
- **Upgrade + Scrounge:** Upgrade turns an Estate into a $3 card (Forager or Silver), and Scrounge then brings the Estate back with a $5 card. One Estate pays out twice. The same move feeds the endgame: Upgrade an Estate, then Scrounge for Estate + Duchy.
- **Scrounge as payload:** each spare Buy plus $3 is worth 4 VP (Estate + Duchy). That is 1.33 VP per $, against 0.75 for a Province. In a built turn with $24 and 5 Buys, 2 Provinces + 2 Scrounges come to 20 VP. Two limits: there must be Estates in the trash, and there are 8 Duchies.
- **Horn of Plenty + a mixed engine:** a built turn easily has 8 or more different card names in play (Bandit Camp, Walled Village, Council Room, Patrol, Upgrade, Forager, Horn, Silver, Gold, Copper). So Horn gains a Gold or a $5 piece every turn without using a Buy. At the end it can gain a Province, which trashes the Horn.
- **Forager with Treasures in the trash:** a trashed Copper alone gives +$1. Trashing a Spoils or a Silver later brings each Forager up to +$2 or +$3.
- **Patrol + Upgrade or Scrounge:** Patrol puts the Victory cards it reveals into your hand. Upgrade can then trash those Estates, or Scrounge (trash option) can.

**Other cards:**
- **Inn:** its best partner is +Buy (Council Room or Forager). When you gain Inn mid-game it shuffles the Actions in your discard back into your deck. It is the weakest of the three villages here.
- **Walled Village:** pairs with Council Room early. With at most one other Action in play it goes back on top of your deck, so you start every turn with village + draw. It is the cheap village.
- **Overlord:** acts as any $5 Action, usually Upgrade, Council Room or Bandit Camp. The 8 Debt makes it cost about as much as a Gold. Horn and Scrounge can't gain it, because "costing up to" excludes Debt. Take at most one, late.
- **Golem:** its best partner is a deck full of Actions. It still needs a Potion that does nothing else here, so skip it.
- **Patrol:** pairs with Upgrade (see above). Use it as the second draw card when you'd rather not give the opponent extra cards.

**Ratings check:** Horn (3.15), Upgrade (3.08), Overlord and Forager are rated high, and that fits this board. Scrounge is rated low (−0.98) but is a key card here, because Forager and Upgrade keep putting Estates in the trash. Council Room (−0.94) is weak on an average board. Here it is the only draw card with +Buy. Golem (−3.8) agrees with skipping it.

### Build lines

- **Engine (4/3 opening):**
  - **T1–2:** Silver / Forager.
  - **T3:** Forager, Silver, C, C, E. Trash the Estate. $4 with 2 Buys: Scrounge gains the Estate back plus Bandit Camp.
  - **T4:** $4, buy Silver.
  - **T5:** Bandit Camp (+Spoils), $5, buy Upgrade.
  - **T6:** Forager trashes a Copper (+$1), $5, buy Council Room.
  - **T7:** Forager #2 or Scrounge.
  - **T8:** Bandit Camp → Upgrade (Estate → Forager) → Council Room. About $8 with 3 Buys: Scrounge (Estate + Bandit Camp) and Horn of Plenty.
  - **T9–10:** 2–3 gains per turn: Scrounge loops, Horn gaining Gold or pieces, a second Council Room, Walled Village.
  - **Built turn, about T11:** deck is 3 Bandit Camp, Walled Village, 2 Council Room, Patrol, 2 Upgrade, 2 Forager, Horn, 2 Silver, Gold, 3 Copper, about 3 Spoils. Money: Silvers $4, Gold $3, Copper $3, Spoils $9, Foragers +$4, so about $23 with 5 Buys. Horn gains a Gold (or a Province at the end). That buys 2 Provinces + 2 Scrounges (Estate + Duchy), about 20 VP per turn.
- **Council Room–Big Money:**
  - **T1–2:** Silver / Silver.
  - **T3–4:** Council Room and Silver, Gold on about T5–6.
  - **Progress:** first Province T7–8, 4 Provinces about T14–15.
  - **Weaknesses:** every Council Room it plays gives the engine player an extra card. Scrounge only scores for it if the opponent has trashed Estates.

**Plans considered:**
- **Trash/Scrounge engine:** Forager and Upgrade thin the deck and Scrounge loops Estates into $5 pieces. Bandit Camp is the village, Council Room (with Patrol) the draw, and Spoils, Horn, Golds and Forager $ the payload. First turn with 2 or more Provinces is about T10–11. It needs about 3 villages, 2–3 draw cards and 2 trashers. Weakness: Council Room feeds the opponent, and the Scrounge loop needs Estates in the trash.
- **Council Room–BM:** 4 Provinces around T14–15, with no Scrounge points unless the opponent supplies Estates. This is the floor plan, and it loses the race by 3–4 turns.
- **Horn of Plenty Province rush:** buy many Horns and cash each in for a Province. Each Horn is a one-shot Province that needs 8 names in play, so this is really the engine's endgame and is folded into it.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Engine mirror:** both players fight over the Scrounge Estates in the shared trash and over the 8 Duchies. Green a little earlier than you would against money. Prefer Patrol over a third Council Room so you don't hand the opponent cards.
- **Against Council Room–BM:** the engine is a few turns behind until about T11. It then scores 15–20 VP per turn, while the money deck has about 3 Provinces. Money has no attack to slow the engine, and its Council Rooms help the engine.

**Advocate's case:** Council Room–BM could go Silver/Silver, then Council Room, Gold, and Scrounge for Estate + Duchy using the engine's trashed Estates. It would have 3 Provinces and about 2 Scrounges by T12, about 26 VP. The engine's line has about 8 VP from Scrounge Estates by T10. It then takes about 20 VP on T11 and again on T12, putting it about 15–20 VP ahead by T12. The point where money falls behind is T11. The pick stands.

**Recommendation:** Build the trash/Scrounge engine. The board passes both engine tests (real draw with villages, and payload that scales through +Buy, Horn and Scrounge). No attacks slow it down, and a worked line has it scoring 2 or more Provinces' worth per turn from about T11, well ahead of money.

**Open:**
- **4/3:** Silver / Forager. Turn 3 can be Forager trashing an Estate followed by Scrounge for Estate + a $5 card.
- **5/2:** Upgrade / nothing. Upgrade is a trasher that doesn't use your Action and turns Estates into Foragers. Nothing at $2 is worth buying, and Overlord's Debt that early is bad.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffles 1–2:** Forager or Upgrade first, then Bandit Camp and Council Room. Scrounge whenever an Estate is in the trash and you have $3 and a spare Buy.
- **Shuffle 3:** Horn of Plenty, a second and third village (Walled Village is fine at $4), Patrol or a second Council Room, a second Forager.
- **Afterwards:** add Gold, Spoils and money from Horn gains.
- **Greening:** once you reliably reach about $16 with 3 or more Buys (around T10–11). Use spare Buys on Scrounge for Estate + Duchy, and cash Horn in for a Province on the final turns.

**Ending:** The Province pile should run out around T13–15. The Duchy pile can empty because of Scrounge. A three-pile ending is unlikely: Spoils are outside the Supply, and Estates mostly cycle through the trash.

**Watch out for:**
- **Scrounge needs an Estate in the trash.** Don't trash Estates the opponent can scoop up unless you can use them first, and count the Estates in the trash during the endgame.
- **Council Room feeds the opponent.** Each play gives them a card, so lean on Patrol in a close mirror.
- **Spoils go to your discard pile, not your hand.** Bandit Camp's money arrives in the next shuffle, not the turn you play it.

**What would flip the choice:**
- With 3 or more players, Council Room feeds several opponents and the Estates in the trash are shared more ways.
- An opponent who trashes Estates aggressively speeds up your Scrounges.

**Least sure of:** how fast the Scrounge loop runs once the trash has no Estates left (both players compete for the same ones). If the trash runs dry, the engine falls back on Horn and plain buys, which is still good enough to beat money but slower. The test is to count, in the T8–10 line, how many Estates are in the trash when you need them.

**Assumed:**
- "Costing up to $5" excludes Overlord's Debt cost, so Scrounge and Horn can't gain Overlord.
- You can pay off Overlord's Debt in the same Buy phase you buy it.
- Upgrade on a Copper gains nothing, because no card costs $1.
- Spoils is a Treasure, so a trashed Spoils counts for Forager.
