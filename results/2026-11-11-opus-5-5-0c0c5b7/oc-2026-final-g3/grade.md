## Beyond the key
- Horn and Scrounge can't gain Overlord because "costing up to $5" excludes a Debt cost: plausible. A cost with Debt is not "up to" a coin amount.
- Forager trashing an Estate, then using its +Buy for Scrounge (Estate + a $5) from turn 3: plausible. The hand Forager, Silver, Copper, Copper, Estate makes $4 with 2 Buys, and the Estate is then in the trash.
- Upgrade turns an Estate into a $3 card (Forager or Silver): plausible. Estate costs $2, so Upgrade gains a card costing exactly $3.
- Scrounge for Estate + Duchy is 4 VP per $3, about 20 VP per turn with 2 Provinces + 2 Scrounges: doubtful. Each Scrounge gain needs a separate Estate already in the trash, and putting one there costs a trash action or a Scrounge. Doing it twice a turn every turn is optimistic.
- Upgrade on a Copper gains nothing because no card costs $1: plausible. Nothing in the kingdom or base cards costs exactly $1.
- A trashed Spoils counts for Forager: plausible. Spoils is a Treasure, but a played Spoils returns to its pile, so it only reaches the trash if trashed from hand.
- Walled Village returns to the top of your deck with at most one other Action in play: plausible. That is what the card text says.
- Skip Golem because the Potion does nothing else: plausible. This matches the grader note: Potion cost, and nothing else uses Potion.
- Council Room–BM is the floor and loses by several turns: plausible. There are no attacks and the engine has strong payload, though it wasn't discussed.

## Summary
The analysis sees an engine of differently named $5s and notices Horn's variety count. It treats Horn as a one-off Gold/Province gainer and centers the plan on a Forager/Scrounge Estate loop with 3 Bandit Camps, so it never builds the multi-Horn greening turn everyone agreed on. It also opens Forager, the trap yurikamome lost with.
