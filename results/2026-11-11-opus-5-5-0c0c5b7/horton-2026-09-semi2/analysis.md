# Analysis: Coin of the Realm, Village Green, Souk (Tireless), Ronin, Enlarge, Armory, Animal Fair, Transmogrify, Importer, Squire · Trappers' Lodge · 2 players

**Board:** Souk is the centre of this board. It pays more the smaller your hand is, it trashes 2 cards whenever you gain it, and Tireless brings each played copy back to the top of your deck. Most of the other cards help it: they leave your hand when played (Squire, Transmogrify, Importer, Coin of the Realm), turn junk into parts (Enlarge, Transmogrify, Importer), or give Actions at the start of the turn (Village Green). There is no Attack and no Curse, and every pile is Kingdom-only. The open questions were whether Ronin's draw-to-7 can work alongside Souk (mostly it can't), and how to play several Souks in one turn (Squire, plus Village Green set for next turn).

**Key interactions:**
- **Souk + Tireless + cards that shrink your hand.** Each played Souk goes on top of your deck at end of turn. The last card in a hand makes Souk worth $7, the second-to-last $6, and so on. A typical built turn: Village Green's next-turn half draws a topdecked Souk and gives +2 Actions, then Squire, Squire, and 3 Souks played into hands of 3, 2 and 1 cards. That is $2 + $4 + $5 + $6 = **$17 with 4 Buys**, from about turn 9–10.
- **Importer or Transmogrify → Souk.** Importer gains a Souk at the start of your turn, and Souk's on-gain trash removes 2 cards from that turn's hand. That is thinning plus +$2 for every Souk played that turn. Transmogrify can trash a Village Green to gain a Souk straight into your hand, which then trashes 2 more cards.
- **Enlarge as a converter.** Copper becomes Squire, Estate becomes Village Green or Importer, and Village Green becomes Souk (with another 2-card trash). It does this twice per play, and each trash also shrinks your hand.
- **Trappers' Lodge (5 Favors).** On a 5/2 split, put the turn-1 Souk on top of your deck. It is then in your turn-2 hand, so turn 2 becomes $4–5 with 2 Buys instead of $2.

**Other cards:**
- **Squire:** the best partner for Souk. It gives +$1 and +2 Actions and leaves your hand. Its on-trash effect does nothing here, because the board has no Attack card.
- **Coin of the Realm:** +2 Actions from the Tavern mat without taking a hand slot. But while it sits in hand during your Action phase it costs Souk $1, so it is worse than Squire.
- **Ronin:** draw-to-7 works against Souk, and a hand full of Coppers or green kills Souk's value. It is only good late, in a deck that is almost all Actions. It is also the core of the best money plan.
- **Animal Fair:** you can "buy" it by trashing a spare Squire during your Buy phase, for a terminal +$4. Its +Buy matters only once piles are empty. It is a late option.
- **Armory:** puts Village Green, Squire or Importer on top of your deck. It is terminal and slow, so it is filler.
- **Village Green's Reaction half:** nothing on the board discards it, so it does nothing.

**Ratings check:** Souk is at 4.36 (top 10%), which agrees with my view. Tireless is at −1.75, but on Souk it is a big upgrade, because it guarantees your payload comes back and keeps it out of the shuffle. Armory (−2.24) and Animal Fair (−1.15) are low, and I agree with both.

### Build lines

These are paper lines with average draws. I assume Tireless puts the card on your deck *after* you draw your next hand (see Assumed).

**Souk engine (5/2):**
- **T1:** buy Souk with 5 Coppers and put it on top with a Favor.
- **T2:** hand is Souk + 2 Copper + 2 Estate. Souk gives $3, plus $2 from Copper, so $5 with 2 Buys. Buy Souk #2 and trash both Estates.
- **T3** ($4–6): Importer + Squire, or Souk #3 trashing the last Estate.
- **T4:** hand is Souk + 4 Copper, so $7. Buy Souk + Squire, or Enlarge + Squire.
- **T5:** Importer gains Souk #4, trashing 2 Coppers. Squire → Souk → Souk makes about $9–10 with 3 Buys. Buy Squire + Village Green/Enlarge.
- **T6–8:** Enlarge or Transmogrify Coppers into Squires. Get Souk #5 and a second Village Green. The first Province comes around T7–8.
- **T9–10 onwards:** $16–20 with 3–5 Buys. Two Provinces, or Province + Duchy, per turn.
- **Built turn:** about $17–20, 4–5 Buys, 12+ VP per turn.

**Ronin–Big Money (the floor):**
- 5/2: open Ronin with nothing at $2. 4/3: open Silver/Silver, then Ronin.
- Two Ronins get played from your deck almost every turn, so you have a 7-card hand nearly every turn.
- $6 on T3–4, the first Province around T6–7, 4 Provinces around T12–13, and all 8 alone around T17–18.

**Souk–money** (Souks + Coppers, no villages): Souk + 4 Coppers makes $7 with 2 Buys, but Souks collide with each other. It comes out about even with Ronin–Big Money and is worse than the engine.

| Plan | Starts scoring | Can end by | Peak VP/turn | VP ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Souk engine | ~T7–8 (one Province), T9–10 (two) | ~T13–14 | 2 Provinces or Province + Duchy | All Provinces + Duchies, three-pile threat |
| Ronin–Big Money | ~T6–7 | ~T17–18 alone | 1 Province | About half the Provinces before the engine's big turns arrive |

**Plans considered:**
- **Souk engine** (above). It needs about 5 Souks (the pile is 10, and the opponent wants them too), 3–4 Squires, 1–2 Village Greens, and early Copper trashing. Its weakness is Coppers and green cards in hand, which drain Souk's value.
- **Ronin–Big Money.** A simple and steady floor. It is capped at one Province per turn.
- **Ronin-drawn thin engine** (the bigger version of the pick). Play your Squires down to 0–1 cards, then play Ronin from your deck to pull in the topdecked Souks along with the rest of your Actions. This only works once the deck is almost all Actions.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Engine vs Ronin–Big Money:** the opponent leaves the Souks to you, so you take 6–7. By T13 Ronin–Big Money has about 4 Provinces. The engine has 1 by T8, then two per turn from T9–10, so about 5–6 by T13, with extra Buys for Duchies. The engine is favoured, but this is closer than on a typical engine board.
- **Engine mirror:** splitting the Souks (5/5) decides it. The Souk pile empties early, and the Squire pile may follow, so a three-pile ending (Souk + Squire + Duchy or Village Green) becomes real. Green a little earlier, and count gains.

**Advocate's case (Ronin-drawn thin engine):** Trash hard with Importer, Enlarge and Transmogrify, keep no Treasure, and add 1 Ronin around T7. On a turn where you play Squire, Squire, Transmogrify, then Ronin from your deck into 3 topdecked Souks and 3 Actions, everything gets played before the Souks. That gives about $20+ with 5 Buys. In my paper line it falls behind the pick at T8–9: Coppers are still in the deck, and Ronin pulls them into hand, which costs about $3–4 per turn compared with the Village Green version. It catches up around T11 only if the Coppers are gone by then. So the verdict is: add one Ronin once the deck is down to about 2 junk cards, not before.

**Recommendation:** Build the Tireless Souk engine. Souks are the payload and the trashing. Squires are the main village. Village Green's next-turn half collects topdecked Souks. Importer, Enlarge and Transmogrify thin the deck. Contest the Souk pile, because even if the opponent goes Ronin–Big Money, every Souk you take is one that can't help them.

**Open:**
- **5/2:** Souk on T1, put on top with Trappers' Lodge. That turns T2 into $4–5 with 2 Buys: buy Souk (trash 2 Estates) at $5, or Importer at $4.
- **4/3:** Importer on T1, put on top so it plays on T2. Squire on T2. Importer then gains Souk at the start of T3 and trashes 2 cards.

5/2 is clearly better, and the tournament rule lets you choose it.

**Buy priorities:**
1. Souks up to 4–5, contested.
2. Squires up to 3–4.
3. One Importer, one Enlarge or Transmogrify to clear Coppers.
4. 1–2 Village Greens.

Buy no Silver or Gold. Start greening when you reliably make $16 with 3+ Buys, around T9–10. Late, use Squire's +2 Buys for Duchies and Estates.

**Ending:** Most likely the Provinces run out. A three-pile ending (Souk + Squire + Duchy or Village Green) is a real risk in a mirror. Animal Fair's +Buy grows as piles empty.

**Watch out for:**
- Slow Copper trashing. Each Copper or Province in hand costs $1 for every Souk played after it.
- Losing the Souk split.
- Ronin–Big Money racing you if your build stalls. You need two Provinces per turn by about T11.

**Least sure of:** whether the engine really outpaces Ronin–Big Money. The gap in my lines is only 1–2 Provinces by T13. The test is to play both lines out with real shuffles. If you aren't making $16 by T10, add a Gold-free Ronin, or switch to taking Duchies.

**Assumed:**
- **Tireless timing.** I read it as the card going on your deck *after* the Clean-up draw, as I recall from the Plunder rulings. So a played Souk comes back the turn after next, unless something draws it (Village Green's next-turn half or Ronin). If it actually goes on your deck before the draw, Souks are in every hand and the engine gets even stronger.
- **Starting deck.** I assume players arrange only which turn gets 5/2 or 4/3, not the full order of the deck.
- **Importer.** As printed here, it gives no Favors when played, so each player has just 5 Favors (1 standard + 4 from setup).
- **Squire.** With no Attack cards in the Kingdom, its when-trashed gain does nothing.
- **More players:** with 3+ players the Souk pile is split three or more ways, so each player gets fewer Souks. That makes Ronin–Big Money or Souk–money relatively stronger, and the three-pile ending comes sooner.

**How the pick loses:** The opponent opens Souk on 5/2 as well and takes half the pile, while you stall on Coppers. Or they play Ronin–Big Money and are 4 Provinces ahead before your T10. You can see both coming from their first two buys: if they take Souks early, contest the pile harder and green earlier.
