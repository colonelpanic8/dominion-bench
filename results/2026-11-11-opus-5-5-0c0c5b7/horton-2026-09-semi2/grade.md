## Beyond the key
- Importer gaining a Souk at start of turn trashes 2 cards from that turn's hand, both thinning and raising Souk's payout: plausible. Souk's on-gain trash works on the hand already drawn.
- Transmogrify trashing a Village Green ($4) to gain a Souk ($5) into hand, triggering another 2-card trash: plausible. Within Transmogrify's "up to $1 more" and its gain-to-hand.
- Enlarge converting Copper→Squire, Estate→Village Green/Importer, Village Green→Souk, twice per play: plausible. Enlarge gives +$2 now and next turn.
- 5/2 Souk on T1, topdecked with a Favor, makes T2 Souk + 2 Copper + 2 Estate = $5 with 2 Buys, buying Souk #2 and trashing both Estates: plausible. Souk leaves 4 cards in hand for $3, the Coppers add $2, and the Estates are still in hand when the gained Souk trashes.
- Each player has 5 Favors (1 standard + 4 from Importer setup), and Importer as printed gives none when played: plausible. Matches the rendered card text.
- Built turn of Village Green + 2 Squires + 3 Souks into hands of 3/2/1 cards for $17 and 4 Buys: plausible. The arithmetic and Action count check out, though getting 3 Souks in one hand is hard given Tireless timing.
- Coin of the Realm in hand during the Action phase costs Souk $1: plausible. It's a Treasure, played only in the Buy phase.
- Squire's on-trash gain does nothing (no Attack in the kingdom): plausible. Ronin is an Action–Shadow, not an Attack.
- Village Green's Reaction does nothing because nothing on the board discards it: plausible. No discard effects in the kingdom.
- Ronin–Big Money plays "two Ronins from your deck almost every turn": doubtful. Ronin is terminal, so without villages only one plays per turn.
- Souk wants Squires and other cards that leave the hand played before it, and Ronin played after Squires and before Souks: plausible. This is the order-of-play handling the grader notes credit as an extra.

## Summary
The analysis gets Tireless timing exactly right and handles the order of play between Souk, Squire and Ronin well. But it builds a Souk-rush engine and rates Ronin as a late add-on, the opposite of the designer's Ronin draw deck. It dismisses Armory, so it misses the playtested Armory/Transmogrify/Importer/Coin of the Realm opening, and it falls into rushing Souks.
