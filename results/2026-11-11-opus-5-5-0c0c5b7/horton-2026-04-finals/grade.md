## Beyond the key
- The opponent's remodel can trash a Copper and gain nothing, so it's free thinning for them: plausible. Exactly $1 costs nothing on this board, and the trash is optional ("may").
- Throne Room on Governor is +6 Cards and +1 Action net, and Flagship on Governor nets 0 Actions: plausible. Throne Room costs 1 Action and the two Governor plays give +2. Flagship and Governor cost 1 Action each and the two plays give +2.
- The remodel turns Gold into Province and Silver into Governor or Duchy: plausible. Each is "exactly $2 more" ($6 to $8, $3 to $5).
- Merchant Camp's self-topdeck means you always start the turn with a village: plausible. Its below-the-line text puts it on your deck when you discard it from play.
- A card played as Owl doesn't use its own text, so an Owl'd Experiment stays out: plausible. A Way replaces the card's instructions, and "return this" is part of those instructions.
- Advisor is bad because the opponent will discard your Governor: plausible. The player to your left chooses which card is discarded.
- Throne Room on Tactician does nothing on the second play: plausible as a claim about card text, but incomplete. The second play can be used as Owl to draw back to 6, which is the designer's point. The conclusion "one copy at most" doesn't follow.
- Leprechaun must be exactly the 7th card in play, and the Wish can take a Governor to hand: plausible. Leprechaun counts itself once it's in play, and Wish gains a card costing up to $6 to your hand.
- There are no attacks, and Hexes only hit the player of Leprechaun: plausible. No card here is an Attack, and Leprechaun has its player receive the Hex.

## Summary
The analysis reads Governor/Friendly flow and Throne Room payload well, and its Forager and remodel details are sound. It misses both of the designer's headline tricks. It explicitly dismisses multiple Tacticians, missing that Owl refills the hand for a second Tactician. It also never replays Leprechaun for several Wishes. A player following it would skip the Tactician deck Horton built the board around.
