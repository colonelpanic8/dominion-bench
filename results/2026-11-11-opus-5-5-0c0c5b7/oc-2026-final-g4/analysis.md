# Kingdom analysis: oc-2026-final-g4 (2 players, no Colonies, no Shelters)

**Board:** The only trashing is Ritual, and Ritual gives you a Curse for every card it trashes. So junk is permanent on this board, which makes **Jester** much stronger than usual. Its Curses and Coppers can never be cleaned out, and it can also take copies of the opponent's good cards. Hand size comes cheaply from three places:
- **Crop Rotation**: your Estates never leave the deck, so it fires nearly every turn.
- **The Flag**: from Flag Bearer.
- **First Mate**: plays every same-named Action in your hand for free, then draws you up to 6 cards.

The open questions were what plays several Jesters in one turn, and where +Buy comes from. The answers are First Mate and Aristocrat for the first, and Jewelled Egg plus the 4th Aristocrat for the second.

**Key interactions:**
- **First Mate + Jester:** with First Mate, Jester, Jester, Copper, Copper in hand, First Mate plays both Jesters for +$4 and 2 attacks, then draws 4 cards (back up to 6). It works as a village and a Smithy in one. Each Jester play gives the opponent junk: a Curse about 30% of the time early (more once they green), otherwise usually a Copper. When they reveal Gold, Lab or Jester, you take the copy. From about T7 that is 2–3 junk cards per turn, and none of it can be trashed.
- **Crop Rotation + no trashing:** a 6-card hand from a 12-card deck with 3 Estates holds an Estate about 91% of the time. Discard it for +2 Cards, so most turns you have 7 useful cards without spending an Action. Late in the game, your Provinces feed it instead.
- **Flag Bearer → Flag:** +1 card in every new hand, until the opponent gains a Flag Bearer and takes the Flag back. Buy it T1 and your T2 hand already has 6 cards.
- **Aristocrat as a village:** the first one in play gives +3 Actions, which covers Jester, Jester and Flag Bearer. Later ones in the same turn give +3 Cards, then +$3, then +3 Buys. First Mate can play several Aristocrats from your hand without spending Actions.
- **Ritual as VP payload:** you gain a Curse and trash a card, and score VP equal to the trashed card's cost.
  - Trashing a Lab or First Mate nets about +4 VP for $4. Trashing a Loot nets +6. A Province costs $8 for 6 VP.
  - It only works while Curses remain, and Jester empties that pile too. So it's an endgame extra, not a plan.

**Other cards:**
- **Laboratory:** draw that costs no Action. It finds Jesters and First Mate. Good everywhere.
- **Ghost Town:** a $3 village (you play it at Night, and it gives +1 Card +1 Action next turn). Use it for Jesters when you don't draw First Mate.
- **Jewelled Egg:** the cheap +Buy. A Ritual that trashes an Egg gives you a Loot, which is fine as fodder but nothing more.
- **Corsair:** only good against an opponent who plays Silver or Gold. Against a money opponent, swap one Jester for one. Only one Corsair's attack counts per turn.
- **Alley:** a cheap sifter that can discard Curses. It gets played from your deck once per shuffle. Minor.
- **Tactician:** none. Discarding your hand clashes with First Mate's draw up to 6 and with Crop Rotation. Skip it.

### Build lines

- **Jester engine (pick)**, opening Flag Bearer/Silver:
  - **T1–2:** Flag Bearer (take the Flag), Silver.
  - **T3–4** (6-card hands, about $5–6): Crop Rotation at the first $6, otherwise Jester first. The other $5 hand gets the other one.
  - **T5–6:** Jester #2, then Lab.
  - **T7–8:** First Mate, Lab. With $3–4, buy Aristocrat or Ghost Town.
  - **T9–10:** Jester #3, Lab #3, Egg ×1–2. First Province around T10.
  - **Example built turn, ~T11–12** (deck about 7 Copper, 3 Estate, Silver, Flag Bearer, 3 Jester, 2 Lab, First Mate, Aristocrat, Egg, Ghost Town):
    1. Crop Rotation discards an Estate and draws 2.
    2. Lab, Lab.
    3. Aristocrat: +3 Actions.
    4. First Mate plays 3 Jesters: +$6 and 3 attacks. It then draws up to 6, finding Flag Bearer for +$2.
    5. Treasures bring the total to about **$14 with 2 Buys**: Province plus Lab, or Province plus Duchy. That turn also junked the opponent three times.
- **Jester-BM + Crop Rotation + Flag (floor):**
  - **T1–4:** Flag Bearer, Silver, Crop Rotation, Jester.
  - **T5–7:** Gold, then Gold or Jester #2.
  - **From T8:** about 7 useful cards per hand gives about $8, so a Province most turns.
  - Unhindered, it has 4 Provinces by about T12. Taking 2–3 junk cards per turn from T7, it drops to one Province every other turn after about T10.
- **Aristocrat/First Mate stack (advocate):** open Aristocrat ×2, then First Mate, then Aristocrats on every $3–4. Four Aristocrats chained turn 4 cards into +3 Cards, +$3 and +3 Buys. In a Copper deck that's about $8–10 with 3–4 Buys by T10, spent on Province plus Ritual.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jester engine | ~T10 | ~T15 | 2 Provinces, or Province + Duchy, plus 3 junk to the opponent | Provinces + Duchies + Ritual |
| Jester-BM + Crop Rotation | ~T8 | ~T14 if unattacked, T17+ if junked | 1 Province | about half the Provinces |
| Aristocrat/First Mate stack | ~T10 | ~T16 | Province + Ritual | Provinces + Ritual (limited by Curses) |

**Plans considered:** the three plans in the table, plus two rejected ones:
- **Corsair-BM:** strong only if the opponent goes Silver/Gold money. It does nothing to an engine built on Jester $ and Labs.
- **Egg/Ritual Loot plan:** each Loot costs $6 and 2 Buys, and leaves a Curse in your deck. Roughly break-even with buying Gold, so it isn't a plan.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Their best reply is Jester-BM + Crop Rotation.** It scores first, around T8, but from T7 it eats 2–3 Jester hits per turn. The Curses are permanent, and the Coppers dilute it. When it reveals a Gold, you take a Gold. Its single Jester hits you about once per turn, and Labs and First Mate dig through that. You overtake around T11–12.
- **In a Jester mirror,** the Jester split and the Curse split decide it. Get 3–4 Jesters fast and pick up a 5th by taking copies from their deck.
- **Runner-up (the money plan) against the engine:** it only wins if its early Provinces carry it to the end before the junk bites. That needs the engine to stumble on $5 hands through T6.

**Advocate's case:** the Aristocrat stack is cheap, at $3 a piece. It builds +Buy from the 4th copy, plays its pieces without Actions through First Mate, and can sink spare $ into Ritual VP. It falls behind around **T9–10**:
- Each chain of 4 Aristocrats costs a net card. With 6 Aristocrats in about 20 cards, a hand averages only about 1.5 of them.
- It has no attack. By T10 the Jester deck has given it about 4 junk cards, cutting its average from about $9 to about $7, while the pick reaches about $12–14.
- Its best version is the pick with 1–2 Aristocrats as villages, and that is already in the recommendation.

**Recommendation:** play the **Jester engine**:
- **Payload:** 3–4 Jesters.
- **Playing them together:** First Mate ×1–2, plus 1–2 Aristocrats and a Ghost Town as villages.
- **Draw:** 2–3 Labs.
- **Hand size:** Crop Rotation and the Flag.
- **+Buy:** Eggs and Aristocrats.
- **VP:** Provinces and Duchies, plus Ritual on the last turns while Curses remain.

It holds up because money can't recover from permanent junk, while the engine digs through the opponent's junk and still plays several attacks per turn.

**Open:**
- **5/2:** Jester / Jewelled Egg. That's the earliest attack, and the Egg is your +Buy.
- **4/3:** Flag Bearer / Silver. Buy Flag Bearer on whichever turn has $4; the Flag means 6-card hands for T2 onward.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffles 1–2:** Jester at $5, then Crop Rotation at the first $6, then Jester #2, then Lab, then First Mate. Take Silver at $3–4 early. Once you have 2 or more terminals, take Aristocrat or Ghost Town at $3–4 instead. Egg at $2.
- **Shuffle 3:** Jester #3, Labs up to 3, Eggs ×2. Gold only at $6–7 when no piece is needed.
- **Greening:** start Provinces when hands regularly reach $8 or more with 2 Buys (about T10). Take Duchies once 4 Provinces are left.
- **Ritual:** use it only in the last 1–2 turns, with a spare $4 and a Buy, to trash a Lab, First Mate or Gold for +4–5 VP net.

**Jester choices:**
- **Copper:** give it to the opponent.
- **Silver, Gold or any Action:** take it yourself.
- **Victory card:** they gain a Curse.

**Ending:** most likely the Provinces run out, around T15–17. Curses will empty mid-game in a Jester war, and once they're gone Ritual stops working. A three-pile ending (Curses, Jesters and one more pile) is possible but unlikely. Duchies matter, because junked games slow down.

**Watch out for:**
- The opponent buying Flag Bearer steals the Flag. Retake it with a Flag Bearer (or a late Ritual on one) only when you have a spare $4 and a Buy.
- Losing the Jester split: 10 in the pile, and you want 3–4.
- An opponent on Silver/Gold money: add one Corsair.

**What would flip the choice:**
- **3+ players:** Jester and Corsair both get stronger.
- **An opponent who skips attacks:** money with Crop Rotation becomes closer.

**Least sure of:** whether unjunked Jester-BM + Crop Rotation + Flag reaches 4 Provinces (about T12) before the engine's T11–12 turns take over. Watch the Jester count: if the opponent gets as many Jesters as you, junk evens out and money's head start matters more. If that happens, green one turn earlier.

**Assumed:**
- A Shadow can be played from anywhere in your deck. First Mate plays from hand only, so it can't play Alleys left in the deck. That reading is unconfirmed, and it barely matters here.
- Cards played by First Mate don't use Actions. Aristocrat counts every Aristocrat in play, however it was played.
- Crop Rotation can't discard a Curse, because Curses aren't Victory cards.
- Once Curses run out, a Victory card revealed to Jester does nothing, and Ritual does nothing because no Curse is gained.
- Ritual trashing a Curse scores 0 VP.
- The speed estimates are paper lines with average draws, not simulations.
