## Beyond the key
- Courtier revealing Village Green (Action–Duration–Reaction) gives +Action, +$3 and a Gold: plausible. There are three types, so it takes three different choices.
- Courtier revealing Barge or Cardinal gives +Action and +$3: plausible. Each has two types.
- Gamble discarding Tunnel gains a Gold, and Gamble on Village Green lets you play it: plausible. Gamble's discard isn't Clean-up, so Tunnel's reaction triggers, and Gamble plays Actions directly.
- Durations played via Gamble can choose "next turn": plausible. Barge and Village Green offer the choice on play however they're played.
- Way of the Goat works on a Gamble-played Action: plausible. Gamble "plays" the card, so a Way can replace its effect.
- Displace turns a Gold into a Province: plausible. Gold costs $6, plus $2 is $8.
- Cardinal is weak against Tunnel holders, because the victim Exiles the other card and discards Tunnel for a Gold: doubtful as stated. The victim must Exile a card costing $3–$6, so if the other revealed card is Copper, Estate or Province, Tunnel is the only legal choice and gets Exiled.
- Exiled Golds come back when the player gains another Gold: plausible. Gaining a card lets you discard copies of it from Exile.
- Lurker needs two plays per gain (trash from the Supply, then gain from the trash): plausible from its "choose one".
- "No deck here reliably makes more than $13–15": wrong. Mandarin's first-edition on-gain topdecks every Treasure in play, so a Courtier deck that gains Golds can replay them every turn. The key's source clocked that deck at $118 with 8 Buys on turn 11.
- Mandarin and Silk Road are first-edition only: plausible. Both were removed in Hinterlands' second edition. Not a strategy claim.

## Summary
The analysis spots the Courtier/Village Green three-type trick, but it treats Mandarin as filler and never sees the on-gain Gold-replay combo. That combo is the whole point of the board. It recommends Barge/Courtier money with a conditional Cardinal, which both commenters called useless. A player following it would play a reasonable but far slower deck than the key's plan.
