## Beyond the key
- Old Witch is the board's only junker, and Ferry on it makes it $3 on your turns, so a 4/3 opens Ferry/Old Witch and Hermit can gain more: plausible. Ferry gives -$2 on your turns, and Hermit gains up to $3. The key says Horton never discusses Old Witch; this is a real gap in the key, though whether Old Witch–money beats the Diplomat deck is untested.
- Sewers plus the opponent's Old Witch: trashing the Curse from hand fires Sewers for a second trash, on their turn: plausible. Old Witch lets you trash a Curse from hand, and Sewers fires on any trash other than its own.
- Revealing Diplomat to an opponent's Old Witch leaves a 4-card hand, so your next Diplomat gets +2 Actions: plausible. 5 + 2 − 3 = 4, then 3 + 2 = 5 after playing Diplomat. The grader notes credit this.
- Hireling's extra card turns off Diplomat: plausible. A 6-card hand becomes 7 after Diplomat.
- Sewers and Hermit remove Treasure Trove's Copper: doubtful. Hermit can only trash a non-Treasure, so only the Sewers trigger can take the Copper.
- The solo test used the current Hermit, and the 2019 wording "doesn't alter the plan": doubtful. The 2019 Hermit trashes itself, firing Sewers a third time per Hermit, and leaves a $3 card for Rogue. The +2 Actions Madman is what the key's Bridge lines run on, so the build lines understate the Hermit/Madman plans.
- Outpost collides with Old Witch: plausible. Both are terminal, and there's no reliable village on a normal turn.

## Summary
The analysis reads the board's mechanics well, including every Diplomat enabler, but uses them to reject the Bridge/Diplomat deck Horton picked, and builds an Old Witch–money deck the key never discusses. Hermit + Sewers are support cards rather than the opening. Its Old Witch observations are sound beyond-key points, but a player following it would not play Horton's plan.
