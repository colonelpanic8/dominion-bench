I recommend **Old Witch–money with Ferry on Old Witch**, supported by Hermit and Sewers to trash Curses and Estates. Open **Ferry, then Old Witch at $3**. Both build lines below were played with `dominion solo`. There is no opponent in solo, so neither line counts the Curses an opponent's Old Witch would send you.

**Board:** The only junker is Old Witch, and it is also the best draw card here. Ferry lowers it to $3 on your turns, so an Old Witch fight decides the game. There is cheap trashing (Hermit, Sewers) and +Buy (Bridge), but no village that works on a normal turn. Diplomat only gives Actions if your hand is 5 cards or fewer after its draw, so it fails as the first card played from a 5-card hand. Madman is single-use. Expect a money game with junking that ends on Provinces.

**Key interactions:**
- **Ferry + Old Witch + Hermit:** Old Witch costs $3, so a $3 hand buys it and Hermit can gain it. Opening Ferry then Old Witch puts the junker in your deck by turn 3–4. Hermit adds more copies later.
- **Hermit + Sewers:** each Hermit play trashes 2 cards. In the solo game, turns 8–9 cleared all 3 Estates (one was trashed earlier) and a Copper.
- **Sewers + the opponent's Old Witch:** when they curse you and you trash a Curse from hand, Sewers lets you trash a second card, on their turn.
- **Diplomat reaction + the opponent's Old Witch:** reveal Diplomat to draw 2 and discard 3, dropping Curses and Estates. You start your next turn on 4 cards, so Diplomat gives +2 Actions and you can follow it with Old Witch. It's a real but occasional benefit, since it depends on their attack.
- **Bridge + Diplomat** would chain (each Bridge shrinks your hand so the next Diplomat gets its Actions). But nothing starts the chain on a normal 5-card turn, only Outpost's 3-card turns, Madman, or the reaction above. So there is no engine here.

**Other cards:**
- **Treasure Trove:** Sewers and Hermit remove the Copper it gains. Better than Silver at $5.
- **Caravan:** fine non-terminal filler that helps find Old Witch.
- **Hireling:** a playable alternative to an early Gold, but its extra card breaks Diplomat.
- **Outpost:** extra 3-card turns, the only reliable way to start Diplomat; it collides with Old Witch.
- **Rogue:** the trash fills with $0–2 cards, so it mostly attacks the opponent's Golds; with Old Witch that's two terminals. Skip.
- **Vassal:** no good Actions to flip. Skip.

**Plans considered:**
1. **Old Witch–money (picked):** Ferry/Old Witch, then Hermit, Sewers and Gold, and a 2nd Old Witch through Hermit. Played: Province turns 7, 10 and 11; Curses given on turns 4, 7, 10 and 11. Weakness: in the mirror, the Curse split decides it.
2. **Same deck without Sewers (Gold first):** Provinces turns 8 and 11 (3rd still to come), Curses given on turns 4, 7, 8 and 10. A turn slower, and it cleans up Curses worse.
3. **Bridge/Diplomat/Outpost "engine":** higher ceiling in theory. In practice it gets at most one terminal per normal turn, so the multi-Bridge turn almost never happens. Rejected.

### Build lines (solo, seed 1, 4/3)
- **Old Witch–money with Sewers:**
  - T1 Ferry (on Old Witch), T2 Old Witch, T3 Sewers.
  - T4 Old Witch (draws to $6), Gold. T5 Hermit. T6 Gold.
  - T7 Old Witch, $10, Province.
  - T8–9 Hermit plus Sewers trash 3 Estates and a Copper, and gain a 2nd Old Witch and a Silver.
  - T10 and T11 Old Witch, $11 each, Province each. That's 3 Provinces by T11.
- **Old Witch–money without Sewers:** Ferry, Old Witch, Hermit, then Gold ×4. Province on T8 and T11; T12 hit $7 (Gold).

**Recommendation:** Old Witch–money with Hermit and Sewers. Against its best reply, the same deck, it holds up because:
- Ferry gets your first Old Witch in just as early.
- Hermit plus Sewers removes Curses faster than a deck without them.
- Diplomat's reaction turns their attack into sifting for you.

The engine was rejected because Diplomat can't start a chain from a 5-card hand. The advocate's best version is Outpost turns plus a Madman turn, and it is still building past turn 12. By then, plan 1 already has 3 Provinces and has given 4 Curses, which also stop the engine's Bridge turns.

**Open (you choose your split under the tournament rule):**
- **4/3 (recommended):** Ferry on Old Witch with the $4, then Old Witch with the $3.
- **5/2:** Old Witch, then nothing. Buy Ferry later. This is worse, because 4/3 gets the same Old Witch plus the Ferry token.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffles 1–3:**
  - $3: Hermit and Sewers (1 each), then a 2nd Old Witch.
  - $5: Treasure Trove.
  - $6–7: Gold.
  - $8: Province from about turn 7.
- **Optional:** one Diplomat as defence once the opponent is playing Old Witch, and one Bridge later for Province + Duchy turns.
- **Greening:** Duchies at $5–7 once 4–5 Provinces are left; Estates near the end.

**Ending:** Curses run out around turns 10–13. The game ends on Provinces, likely around turns 15–18 with Curses slowing both decks. A three-pile ending is unlikely.

**Against their best plan:** In the mirror, Curses split roughly 5/5. The edge goes to whoever gets Sewers and Hermit online and plays Old Witch more often. Against a deck that skips the junker, you win comfortably.

**How the pick loses:** By losing the Curse split badly, for example by missing the reshuffle with your Old Witch going second. The fix is a 2nd and 3rd Old Witch at $3 through Hermit or Ferry. Taking Treasure Trove or Gold instead of attacks only works once the Curses are gone.

**Watch out for:**
- The opponent going for more Old Witches than you: match their count.
- Hireling's extra card each turn, which turns off your Diplomats.

**Least sure of:** whether Sewers before Hermit is worth the $3. It only showed as one turn faster in a single seed with no Curses coming in. A second seed played with incoming Curses would settle it.

**Assumed:**
- The solo engine uses the current Hermit wording. The tournament used the 2018 wording, where Hermit is trashed for a Madman. That trash also triggers Sewers and puts a $3 card in the trash, which slightly helps Rogue. Neither change alters the plan.
- Ferry's token works only on your own turns. The opponent can Ferry Old Witch too.
- One misplay in the Sewers line: I bought a Copper instead of a Silver on turn 8, which if anything makes that line look slower than it is.
