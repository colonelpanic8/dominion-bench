I've finished the analysis, based on three solo games on seed 1. **The pick is a money deck that thins with Steward, keeps Tent in hand every turn and draws with Destriers. It isn't a big engine.** That cuts against the repo's lean toward engines, so the evidence for it is laid out below.

---

**Board:** The supply has no attacks and no junkers. Scoring is Provinces, Duchies and Stronghold (2 VP, behind three other Forts). There is little payload: no +Buy except Ducat, and no gainer that reaches $8. Destrier is the only strong draw, and it needs no village. The real villages are weak: Exploration's Villagers, and Reckless Guide, which can only be used once. So the open question was whether anything turns the gain-counting cards (Destrier's discount, Garrison's tokens, Ducat's +Buy) into payload worth more than a Province a turn. Nothing does.

**Inventory:**
- **Cards:** Ratcatcher $2, Ducat $2, Steward $3, Wishing Well $3, Harbinger $3, Guide $3 (Reckless), Cabin Boy $4, Mystic $5, Destrier $6 minus $1 per card gained this turn.
- **Forts pile:** Tent $3, then Garrison $4, Hill Fort $5, Stronghold $6. Only the top card can be bought, and only Tent can rotate the pile.
- **Exploration** (Project, $4).
- **Setup:** no Shelters, no Colonies, 2 players. The tournament rule lets you choose a 4/3 or 5/2 opening.

**Key interactions:**
- **Tent puts itself back on your deck.** It goes on top during Clean-up, then you draw it, so it's in every hand: +$2 every turn without taking up a reshuffle. The catch is that every other terminal (Steward, Garrison, Stronghold) now collides with it. After turn 5, Steward was mostly dead in my line.
- **Ducat makes Destrier cheaper.** Each cheap gain cuts Destrier by $1. With 2 Buys and $7, a Ducat plus a Destrier works. In practice it builds slowly, because Ducat produces no $ (only 1 Coffers when played).
- **Hill Fort can gain a Destrier.** After 2 gains in a turn, Destrier costs $4 and Hill Fort can gain it to hand. Getting there takes 2 Tent rotations plus earlier gains, so it's too slow to matter.
- **Reckless Guide is used up.** Played, it gives +2 Cards +2 Actions (net +1/+1) and goes to the Tavern mat. Once called, it goes back to its pile when discarded. It's a one-shot "Lab-village" for $3.
- **Cabin Boy can become a Stronghold.** Trashing it to "gain a Duration card" has no cost cap, so a $4 card becomes a $6, 2 VP Stronghold once Stronghold is on top of the Forts.

**Other cards:**
- **Steward:** the best trasher here (trash 2 Estates on turn 3–4).
- **Mystic:** a Silver that costs $5, with no good naming target.
- **Harbinger + Mystic or Wishing Well:** Harbinger sets the top card, then you name it. Cute, but negligible.
- **Ratcatcher:** removes Estates without spending an Action. A decent $2 buy.
- **Garrison:** wants turns with several gains, which this deck seldom has.
- **Exploration:** Coffers and Villagers on turns where you buy nothing. Marginal.
- **Ratings agree:** Steward (3.0) and Destrier (2.6) are the board's best-rated cards, and Mystic (−4.0) and Harbinger (−3.9) the worst.

**Plans considered:**
1. **Destrier money (Tent + Steward + 3 Destriers):** played out below. Provinces on turns 10, 11 and 12, and $9 in hand for the 4th on turn 14.
2. **Tent + Big Money (no Destriers):** 4 Golds but only 1 Province by turn 13 on the same shuffle. Some of that was bad luck, but Destriers clearly help.
3. **Gain engine (advocate):** Ducat-heavy thinning, cheap Destriers, Garrison, Mystic, Cabin Boy. It had 3 VP and hands of about $6 on turn 11.

### Build lines (seed 1, 4/3)

- **Destrier money:**
  - Turns 1–2: Silver, Steward.
  - Turn 3: Steward trashes 2 Estates; buy Tent.
  - Turn 4: Cabin Boy.
  - Turn 5: Steward trashes Estate and Copper.
  - Turns 6, 7, 8: Destrier each turn (Tent rotates once, so Garrison is on top).
  - Turn 9: Gold.
  - Turns 10, 11, 12: Province each turn.
  - Turn 13: Duchy.
  - Turn 14: $9, a Province.
  - A built turn: Destrier ×2, Tent and Cabin Boy give $9 with 1 Buy.
- **Advocate engine:**
  - Turns 1–2: Ducat (trash a Copper), Steward.
  - Turn 3: Ducat (trash a Copper).
  - Turn 4: Cabin Boy.
  - Turn 5: Tent.
  - Turn 6: Ducat.
  - Turn 7: Ducat, then Destrier at $5 (spent all Coffers).
  - Turn 8: Garrison.
  - Turn 9: Mystic.
  - Turn 10: Ducat.
  - Turn 11: 3 VP, about $6 a hand. Ducat-thinning strips out the money faster than gains replace it.

**Against their best plan:** In the mirror there are no attacks, so it's a pure race and shuffle luck plus endgame Duchy/Stronghold choices decide it. Against the gain engine, Destrier money is 3 Provinces ahead by turn 12. The engine has no payload that pays out more than about one Province a turn, so it never catches up.

**Advocate's case:** "Destrier gets cheap or free with many gains, Garrison turns those gains into draw, and Ducats supply Buys and Coffers, so you get repeated multi-Province turns." It falls behind on turn 10, when money takes its first Province and the engine is still buying Ducats. By turn 12 it trails 18 VP to 3. Its turns of many gains buy cheap cards, not VP, because nothing here discounts Provinces.

**Recommendation:** Destrier money. Thin with Steward, add Tent (topdecked into every hand), then a Cabin Boy, then 3 Destriers. After that buy Gold and green. Late on, pick up 1 Ducat for the +Buy so $11–13 hands become Province + Duchy. Keep terminals at Tent plus at most one more.

**Open:**
- **4/3:** Silver / Steward (tested), or Cabin Boy / Steward.
- **5/2:** Cabin Boy / Ratcatcher. Untested. Mystic is just a Silver for $5, and Hill Fort isn't buyable yet.

**Buy priorities:**
- **First shuffles:** Steward, Tent (one copy), Cabin Boy.
- **Each $6–7:** Destrier, up to 3. Then Gold at $6–7 and Silver at $3–5.
- **Greening:** Provinces from the first $8, around turn 9–10.
- **Endgame:** Duchy at $5–7 once 5 Provinces are left. Use Tent rotations to bring Stronghold to the top, so $6 buys 2 VP plus +$3, and Cabin Boy can become a Stronghold.

**Ending:** By Provinces. Nothing else drains: Guides go back to their pile, and Ducat or Forts would need heavy buying, so a three-pile ending is very unlikely.

**How the pick loses:** To a better-tuned version of the same deck. That might use Hill Fort's +1 Card +1 Action mode to gain Silver every time, or Exploration's Villagers to play Steward or Garrison alongside Tent. The opponent's Tent rotations can also bury Stronghold.

**Watch out for:**
- Terminal collision with Tent.
- Over-thinning with Ducat, which costs money.
- Rotations by either player that change which Fort is buyable.

**Least sure of:** Whether a Hill Fort or Exploration version beats my plain Destrier money. I didn't test it. I played only one seed per plan, and each line has small misplays: a turn-9 hand of $9 that bought Gold, and a Destrier stranded in hand on turn 10. A second seed and a Hill Fort variant would settle it.

**Assumed:**
- Reckless doubles Guide's on-play text but not its call ability.
- Guide only goes back to its pile after being called.
- The simulator gives Exploration's bonus based on gains during the Buy phase only.
- Buying a Project isn't a gain.
- The 5/2 opening is untested.
