## Beyond the key
- Horn is a one-shot, so the engine "has no repeatable draw" and can't repeat its payoff: wrong as a plan conclusion. Each Horn does trash itself on a Victory gain, but that is exactly why you stack many Horns for one turn. Seven Horns on one turn take most of the Provinces, and the game doesn't need a second big turn.
- "Inn only sifts", and the only non-terminal draw is +1 per village: doubtful. Inn is +2 Cards +2 Actions, a non-terminal village-draw. Gaining it also shuffles your Actions from the discard back into your deck.
- Scrounge's Duchy + Estate for $3, with two Scrounges for 8 VP off Council Room's +Buy: plausible from card text. It is capped by how many Estates are in the trash. The commentators judged a Scrounge-Duchy comeback infeasible in this game.
- Play Horn before Spoils, because Spoils returns to its pile and stops counting as a name: plausible. Spoils returns when played.
- Golem is a skip, with few Actions to find and an expensive Potion: plausible. This matches the grader note's reasoning.
- Upgrade turns an Estate into Silver or Forager (exactly $3): plausible, and correct from card text.
- The opponent can Scrounge Estates you trashed: plausible. The trash is shared.
- Overlord is "too slow here": doubtful. The commentators split on it and agoo03 bought four. As a different $5 Action it also adds a Horn name.
- Walled Village's topdeck helps a light Council Room money deck: plausible. It only triggers with at most one other Action in play.

## Summary
The analysis misses the board's central plan. It rejects the Horn of Plenty megaturn because each Horn trashes itself, and recommends Council Room Big Money with Scrounge. It does read Scrounge's modes and Horn's name-counting correctly, and it avoids both traps. But a player following it would build the opposite of the consensus deck.
