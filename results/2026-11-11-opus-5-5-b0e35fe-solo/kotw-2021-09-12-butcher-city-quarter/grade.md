## Beyond the key
- Tower turns each Curse into 0 VP once the Curse pile is empty, and both players' Cursed Golds drain it: plausible. Curse is non-Victory, and Cursed Gold gains one every play. The grader notes credit this as an extra.
- Ritual on an unplayed Gold for 6 VP beats a Duchy, and Ritual stops once Curses are out: plausible. Gold costs $6, and Ritual trashes only "if you do" gain a Curse. The net is 5 VP until the Curse pile empties.
- Fool's Gold stacking ($1 then $4 each) plus 1 Tower VP per copy if its pile empties: plausible. It's a non-Victory Supply card, though the pile emptying depends on a mirror.
- Butcher "never costs you anything", because it spends its own +2 Coffers: doubtful. The Coffers are banked otherwise, and the key's line saves them for later Butcher plays.
- Butcher targets (Curse→Fool's Gold, Estate→Nomad Camp on deck, Cursed Gold→Gold, Gold→Province): plausible. Each is a +$2 step paid with 2 Coffers, and Nomad Camp is gained onto the deck.
- The opening split is 6/3 48%, 5/4 40%, 7/2 12%, with no 4/3: correct. Hypergeometric over Cursed Gold + 6 Copper + 3 Estate gives 60/51/15 of 126.
- City Quarter is dead without other Actions in hand: correct. It draws per Action revealed from hand, and it is itself in play.
- Fool's Gold's reaction (trash for a Gold on deck when the opponent gains a Province) as a mirror edge: plausible on card text.
- Faithful Hound discarded by Night Watchman or Embassy gives a 6-card next hand: plausible. It returns at end of turn, after the Clean-up draw.
- Embassy gives the opponent a Silver: correct.
- Fool's Gold money beats the engine by 1–2 Provinces at T12: doubtful. It rests on one seed per plan, and the key's solo engine took 8 Provinces by T11.

## Summary
The analysis builds the minority money plan (Fool's Gold, Butcher, Nomad Camp) and argues the key's City Quarter engine down after one weak test line. It does recognize the Tunnel→Gold→Butcher→Province payload and Embassy as the Tunnel outlet. It misses the Night Watchman/City Quarter top-deck role, the Cursed Gold→second Butcher line, and Night Watchman's reshuffle control.
