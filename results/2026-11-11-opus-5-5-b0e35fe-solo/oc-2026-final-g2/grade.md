## Beyond the key
- Captain's only legal target is Chariot Race: wrong as stated. Wayfarer costs the same as the last card gained this turn, so after a cheap gain (Artificer gaining a $≤4 card, for example) Captain can play the $0–$4 Wayfarer from the Supply. On Captain's next-turn play nothing has been gained yet, so Wayfarer is $6 and out of reach. Skipping Captain is still the right call.
- Mastermind replaying a Horse gives all three plays after the Horse returns to its pile: plausible. Throne-style effects don't need the card to stay in play.
- A Livery played by Mastermind counts as in play for Colonnade: plausible. Mastermind plays it from hand, so it is in play that turn.
- Wayfarer costs $3 if its own Silver gain was the last gain: plausible. It reads "the last *other* card gained this turn".
- Chariot Race is best in a Province-heavy deck, since a revealed $8 beats almost anything: plausible from the card text.
- One Corsair is worth considering against a Silver-heavy opponent: plausible but untested, and the grader notes allow it.
- Horses aren't in the Supply, so a three-pile ending is unlikely and the game ends on Provinces: plausible.
- Opening 5/2 with Livery and nothing, rather than Cursed Village (a Hex on turn 1): plausible. No $2 card exists.

## Summary
The analysis builds the Livery/Mastermind/Cursed Village/Wayfarer core the commentators preferred. It gets Mastermind, the Copper→$0 Wayfarer trick, Colonnade and Livery-before-Mastermind right, and avoids every trap. Its big miss is Artificer, which both players built around. It calls Artificer "not needed" and leaves it out of the plan, and it treats Rope as an opener and +Buy source rather than the bulk economy.
