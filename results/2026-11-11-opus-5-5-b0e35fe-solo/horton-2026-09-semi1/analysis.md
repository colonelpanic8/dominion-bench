I played two lines in `dominion solo` (seed 1, against an idle opponent): the Kitsune engine I recommend, and a Transmute plan as the rival. Money gets no game of its own, because Panic wipes it out (see Plans).

**Board:** It is a token engine with a junker in it. Silk Merchant and Lurker hand out Coffers and Villagers. Kitsune is a village, a Curser and +$2 in one card. Council Room and Silk Merchant are the draw, and Highway turns lots of Buys into cheap green. Panic decides the shape of the game. Every Kitsune or Poet play removes a Sun, so the 5 Suns go by about turn 8–9 (turn 9 in my line, with only me playing Kitsunes). After that, every Treasure you play gives +2 Buys and then goes back to its pile. That wipes out money decks and Gold, but it trashes your Coppers for you. There is no +Buy or trashing problem to solve. What's left open is that the draw is all terminal (+2 or +4 cards), so the Villagers have to keep coming.

**Key interactions:**
- **Lurker + Silk Merchant:** Lurker trashes a Silk Merchant from the Supply (+1 Coffers, +1 Villager). The next Lurker gains it back from the trash (+1 Coffers, +1 Villager, plus the card). So each Lurker play is a cantrip that also gives +$1 and +1 Action. This works from turn 3, and in my line it paid for a turn-3 Kitsune.
- **Kitsune + Villagers:** play Kitsune for "+2 Actions + Curse" when you need Actions, and "+$2 + Curse" when the Villagers cover them. You curse every turn either way. The idle opponent took 9 Curses by turn 12.
- **Panic + free Coppers + Highway:** after Panic, each Copper you draw is $1 and +2 Buys, and then it's gone. Coppers cost $0, so buy several each turn as next turn's money. My built turns had 13–16 Buys. Each Highway cuts every price by $1. With two or three Highways in play, Provinces cost $5–6, Duchies $2–3 and Estates $0–1, so one turn takes several green cards. It can also empty a third pile on purpose.

**Other cards:**
- **Council Room:** the biggest draw. Its partner is Villagers. One or two copies; giving the opponent a card is a small cost.
- **Shop:** plays a different Action from your hand, so Shop → Council Room or Shop → Silk Merchant saves a Villager. It is better here than its rating (−1.86).
- **Poet:** a cantrip that also ticks a Sun. It puts Coppers, Lurkers, Shops and Estates into your hand (more with Highway in play). Filler.
- **Tools:** copies anything in play without using an Action, e.g. Kitsune, Highway or Council Room. Under Panic it returns to its pile unless it copies itself. Good before Panic, marginal after.
- **Figurine:** draw that needs no Action, and you can discard a spare Action to it. Under Panic it is used once and goes back. Low priority.
- **Transmute (Reckless, so it trashes two cards):** Copper+Copper gives 2 Transmutes, Estate gives Gold, an Action gives a Duchy. Silk Merchant trashed to it also gives tokens, and Lurker gains it back. That is clever, but Panic turns its Golds into one-shots and the Potion pays for only one buy. Played out, it was slower (see below). At most a late Duchy add-on.
- **Prepare:** keeps a dud hand for a combined turn, or banks a huge Highway turn. Situational.

**Plans considered:**
- **A. Kitsune/Silk Merchant/Lurker engine with Highway payload.** Open Kitsune/Lurker, curse every turn, then about 4 Lurkers, 6+ Silk Merchants, 3 Kitsunes, 1–2 Council Rooms and 2–3 Highways. Solo: first Province on turn 10, 16 VP by turn 12. Weakness: the draw is terminal, so you can run out of Villagers. It is also a curse mirror.
- **B. Transmute engine.** Open Potion/Lurker, have Transmute trash Coppers and Estates, then Actions into Duchies, with Tools copying Transmute. Solo: 2 VP on turn 9, 4 VP on turn 11, still no $8. It is slower to score at every checkpoint, and Panic kills its Golds.
- **C. Big Money + Council Room or Kitsune (the floor).** It is fine until Panic fires. After that, every Silver and Gold it plays leaves the deck. Panic fires whenever the engine player plays Kitsunes, by about turn 9 even alone and earlier in a mirror. Not viable.

### Build lines
- **A (engine):**
  - T1–2: Silk Merchant / Lurker (the 4/3 seed).
  - T3: Lurker trashes a Silk Merchant; buy Kitsune with 2 Coffers.
  - T4: buy Silk Merchant.
  - T5: buy Kitsune.
  - T6: buy Council Room.
  - T7: buy Kitsune and Lurker.
  - T8: buy Silk Merchant and Lurker.
  - T9: Panic fires.
  - T10: 13 Buys and $8; buy Province plus free Coppers.
  - T11: buy 2 Highways.
  - T12: one Highway in play, $8; buy Province and Estate (16 VP).
  - A built turn: about 15 cards played, $8–10, 15+ Buys. With 2–3 Highways that becomes 2 Provinces, or Province + Duchies.
  - My line had misplays: twice I took a Kitsune mode that wasted the +$2. It is about a turn slow.
- **B (Transmute):**
  - T1–2: Potion / Lurker.
  - T3: buy Transmute.
  - T4: buy Kitsune.
  - T6: Transmute trashes Estate and Copper (gains Gold and Transmute).
  - T8: Transmute trashes 2 Coppers (gains 2 Transmutes); buy Tools and Lurker.
  - T10: first Duchy.
  - T11: 4 VP.

**Against their best plan:** The opponent's best plan is the same engine. It is a curse mirror where the 10 Curses split about 5/5. Whoever curses first and more often comes out ahead, so play Kitsune every turn. Silk Merchants are contested too, because Lurker trashes and both of you buy them. In my solo line the Supply was nearly empty by turn 12. Against Transmute (B), A curses it while B is still assembling and wins the race by about 3 turns. Against money (C), A's own Kitsunes switch Panic on and the money deck falls apart.

**Advocate's case (Transmute):** "Reckless doubles a trasher that pays you: Coppers become more Transmutes, Estates become Golds, and late, every Silk Merchant or Lurker becomes a Duchy plus tokens. Tools copies Transmute for free." Played out with the opening that suits it (Potion/Lurker), it was 2 VP on turn 9 against the engine's 3, and 4 VP on turn 11 against 9. By turn 12 the engine was at 16 VP, while the Transmute deck still couldn't reach $8. It falls behind around turn 10, and the gap is 8+ VP by turn 12. Panic is why: its Golds are one-shots, and the Potion goes back to its pile after one use.

**Recommendation:** Build Plan A, the Kitsune/Silk Merchant/Lurker engine with Highway as the payload. It is the only plan that holds up once Panic fires. It curses the opponent the whole game, and the many Buys from Panic plus Highway give it the biggest turn on the board.

**Open (players choose their split):**
- **5/2 (recommended): Kitsune / Lurker.** The Curser lands on turn 3 or 4, and Lurker starts the token loop at once.
- **4/3: Silk Merchant / Lurker.** The tokens fund a turn-3 or turn-4 Kitsune; I bought Kitsune on turn 3 in the solo line.

**Buy priorities:**
- First shuffles: Kitsune (aim for 3), Silk Merchant, Lurker (Lurkers and Silk Merchants roughly evenly).
- Then 1–2 Council Rooms, and a Shop if you are short on Villagers.
- After Panic: 2–3 Highways, plus free Coppers each turn as next turn's money.
- Green from about turn 10, once you can take a Province plus extras. Don't buy Gold or Silver after Panic.

**Ending:** Curses run out (about turn 10–12), and Silk Merchants run low or out about the same time. A third pile then ends the game. Estates at $0–1 under Highway, or Lurkers, can be bought out in one turn by whoever is ahead. Count pile sizes every turn from about turn 10.

**Watch out for:**
1. Losing the curse split. Never skip a Kitsune play.
2. The opponent emptying Curses, Silk Merchants and Estates while ahead.
3. Running out of Villagers. Keep Lurkers playing and add a Shop. With 3+ players, Curses matter more and the piles run out sooner.

**How the pick loses:** It loses the curse split while stuck on terminal draw. The opponent then closes on three piles before your Highway turns arrive. They can see this coming if you open 4/3 without Kitsune.

**Least sure of:** Whether Panic should be held off or rushed. Holding it means taking Kitsune's +2 Actions/+$2 modes over Poet plays and leaving Omens unplayed, which keeps Gold or Transmute economy alive. Rushing it means playing every Omen early so it trashes Coppers sooner. I assumed both players play Kitsune freely, so Panic fires around turn 7–9. Testing this would take a mirror game where one side holds its Omens.

**Assumed:**
- Coffers can be spent after buying in the same Buy phase. The engine allowed it.
- Lurker trashing Silk Merchant from the Supply triggers its tokens. The engine confirmed it.
- The solo games have an idle opponent, so the engine's speed leaves out the Curses it would receive in a mirror. Allow 1–2 turns for that.
- I played one seed per plan.
