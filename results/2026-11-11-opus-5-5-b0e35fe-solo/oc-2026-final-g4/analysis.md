I played four solo lines with `dominion solo`. The recommendation is **Corsair money**, but it's close against an engine that adds Corsair to the deck.

**Board:** There is no real trashing here. Ritual's trash comes with a Curse, and the only other trasher is a random Loot. Provinces are the only Victory pile, and Ritual's VP tokens are the one alternative way to score. The board has a lot of draw: Laboratory, Aristocrat's 2nd play, Tactician, Crop Rotation and the Flag. For villages there are only Aristocrat's 1st play and Ghost Town. The payload is thin: +$2 terminals, one Aristocrat in four giving +$3, and Loots that are slow to get. So the open questions were whether an Aristocrat/Lab engine can get past one Province per turn, and how much the two attacks hurt money.

**Key interactions:**
- **Corsair against Silver/Gold economies:** while Corsair is in play (from your turn to your next), the opponent trashes the first Silver or Gold they play each turn. Two copies keep it in play almost every turn. Against money that is about one Treasure gone each turn the attack is in play, from around turn 5–6. Flag Bearer, Loot and Aristocrat money are untouched by it.
- **Jewelled Egg → Ritual → Loot:** Egg ($2) plus Ritual ($4) gets you a $7 Loot (usually $3 and +Buy) and +2 VP, minus a Curse. Overall that's like a Gold for $6 and two Buys. It's slow, but it can't be hit by Corsair.
- **Ritual as late-game VP:** Ritual on a Gold you keep in hand scores +6 VP −1 Curse = +5 for $4. On a Loot it's +6. That beats a Duchy, and it also turns a Gold that an enemy Corsair would trash into points. Only the 10 Curses limit it, and Jester's Curses come out of the same pile.
- **First Mate + Aristocrat:** First Mate plays every Aristocrat in your hand without using Actions. But four Aristocrats played one after another already pay for themselves, since the 1st gives +3 Actions. Also, the Flag puts you at 6 cards, which makes "draw until 6" do almost nothing. Whoever holds the Flag gets little out of First Mate.

**Other cards:**
- Laboratory: works with anything; it's the best non-terminal in a money deck.
- Flag Bearer: works with itself. Each one gained takes the Flag, so re-buy one at $4 when the opponent has it.
- Ghost Town: works with Corsair and Flag Bearer. It's gained to your hand and acts as a village next turn, so it fixes terminal collision.
- Jester: works with a money opponent who is greening. If their top card is a Victory card they gain a Curse, and if it's a Gold you take a Gold. It's weaker than Corsair.
- Tactician: works with Flag and Crop Rotation (11+ cards with +Buy), but it costs you every other turn. Not here.
- Alley: none. It's a weak sifter, and Crop Rotation already handles the green cards.
- Crop Rotation: works with Estates and Provinces in a money deck that's greening. It's a reasonable $6 buy instead of a third Gold once you have two. I didn't test it.
- Aristocrat: works with Lab draw and Ghost Town (see the engine below).
- Tactician + First Mate: none.

### Build lines (`dominion solo`, no opponent)
- **Lab engine** (seed 1): Flag Bearer/Aristocrat, Labs, First Mate. 1 Province by T9, 2 by T12. With one Buy and weak payload it stalled.
- **Aristocrat engine** (seed 1): Flag Bearer/Aristocrat, Lab T3, then Aristocrats to 4 plus 3 Labs and Ghost Town. It drew its whole deck from T8 but only had $8–11. Provinces on T8, T11 and T12: **21 VP at T12**. Its best turn was 4 Aristocrats, 3 Labs and Ghost Town for $9 and 4 Buys, with nothing worth spending the extra Buys on.
- **Lab/Flag Bearer money** (seed 1): Flag Bearer/Silver, Lab, Gold, Gold. Provinces on T5, T9, T10, T12 and T13: **33 VP at T13**. That's a lucky seed.
- **Corsair money** (seed 2): Flag Bearer/Silver, Corsair T3, Lab, Corsair, Golds. Provinces on T7, T10, T11 and T13: **27 VP at T13**, with Corsair in play for at least 4 opponent turns before T12.

**Plans considered:**
- **Corsair money:** 2 Corsair, Flag Bearer, 1–2 Labs, Gold. First $6 on T3, $8 on T7, Corsair in play about half the time. Its weakness is that the opposing Corsair trashes its Golds.
- **Lab/Flag Bearer money:** the fastest line solo, but it does nothing to the opponent.
- **Aristocrat/Lab engine** (the highest-ceiling plan): it needs 4 Aristocrats and has no payload beyond them. It was about one Province behind money by T12.
- **Tactician money:** rejected; it gives up half its turns.

**Against their best plan:** Corsair money against plain money wins comfortably, because each Gold they lose is a step down in hand quality, and money has no draw to make it back. In the mirror, the player whose Corsairs land more often, or who switches part of their economy to Flag Bearer and Loot, comes out ahead. Against the Aristocrat engine, my Corsair has almost nothing to hit, but the engine is roughly a Province behind and has to spend buys on attacks of its own to catch up.

**Advocate's case:** The best engine is Aristocrat ×4, Lab ×3, Ghost Town and Corsair ×2. Its money comes from Aristocrats, Flag Bearer and Loots (from Egg plus Ritual), so it's immune to Corsair while its own Corsairs hit my Golds. It scores late with Province + Ritual. Solo it reached 21 VP on T12, against 27–33 VP for money on T13. Money's lead is one or two Provinces by T12–13. The engine's Corsairs would cost money about 3–4 Treasures over that stretch, which closes perhaps one Province of the gap. That leaves the engine still about one Province short when the Provinces split, so the pick stands, narrowly.

**Recommendation:** Corsair money, a Big Money + X deck with X = Corsair ×2, plus Flag Bearer and 1–2 Labs, scoring late with Ritual.

**Open:**
- **4/3:** Flag Bearer / Silver (takes the Flag at once).
- **5/2:** Corsair / Jewelled Egg. Corsair on T3 or T4 hits their opening Silver, and the Egg is a Copper with +Buy that becomes a Loot later.

**Buy priorities:**
- **$8:** Province (Gold first if you have none).
- **$6–7:** Gold, but get the 2nd Corsair by about T6.
- **$5:** Corsair until you have 2, then Lab until you have 2.
- **$4:** Flag Bearer whenever the opponent holds the Flag, otherwise Silver.
- **$3:** Silver, or one Ghost Town once you have 3 terminals.
- **$2:** one Jewelled Egg.
- **Greening:** Duchy at $5–7 once 4 or fewer Provinces are left. Late on, Ritual a Gold or Loot you hold in hand (+5 or +6 VP), especially on turns their Corsair would trash it.

**Ending:** The Provinces run out around T16–18. The Curse pile (Ritual plus Jester) is the only other pile that could empty, so a three-pile ending is very unlikely. Use Ritual's +VP to beat the Penultimate Province problem: on an $8 hand with Egg's +Buy, Ritual + Ritual can be worth more than a Province.

**Watch out for:**
- If the opponent goes for the Corsair-proof engine, cut down on Golds. Move toward Flag Bearer, Egg→Loot and Labs, and keep Corsair mainly for its $2.
- Losing the Flag: buy a Flag Bearer back at $4 right away.
- In 3+ player games Corsair and Jester hit everyone, which makes the attack plan stronger.

**How the pick loses:** An opponent who sees you open Corsair can build an economy with no Silver or Gold in it and play their own Corsairs against your Golds. You can spot it if they open Aristocrat or Egg and never buy Silver.

**Least sure of:** The gap between money and the Aristocrat engine with Corsair. It's one solo seed each, and the solo tool can't model Corsair. If a second engine seed reaches 3 Provinces by T10, the engine with Corsairs would be the better pick.

**Assumed:**
- A Treasure trashed by Corsair still produces its $.
- Ritual can trash a card you kept in hand during your Buy phase.
- Loots average about $3 with +Buy.
- Jester's gain choice belongs to the attacker.
- Crop Rotation's value is untested.
