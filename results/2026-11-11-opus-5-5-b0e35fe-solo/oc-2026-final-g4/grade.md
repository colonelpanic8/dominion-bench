## Beyond the key
- Corsair money (2 Corsairs, Gold) is the best plan: doubtful. Corsair only hits Silver and Gold, and the realistic opposing economies here (Flag Bearer, Jester, Aristocrat, Loots) carry few of them; neither the players nor the commentators considered it.
- Corsair trashes the first Silver or Gold an opponent plays each turn while it's in play, and the trashed Treasure still makes its $: plausible. That's the card text and the standard ruling.
- Ritual on a Gold is +5 net (6 VP minus a Curse), and on a Loot +6: plausible. Ritual gives VP equal to cost; Gold costs $6 and Loots $7.
- Ritual can trash a Treasure kept unplayed in hand during the Buy phase: plausible. Ritual trashes "from your hand", and unplayed Treasures stay in hand.
- Holding the Flag makes First Mate's "draw until 6" do almost nothing: doubtful. After First Mate and n copies leave your hand you still draw back 1+n cards; the Flag costs only one card of that draw.
- Tactician is rejected because "it gives up half its turns": doubtful. With the Flag and Crop Rotation its turns reach 11+ cards plus +Buy, and both finalists bought Tacticians.
- A three-pile ending is very unlikely; only Curses can run out: plausible. Ritual and Jester both drain Curses, and nothing else is contested.

## Summary
The analysis spots Egg/Ritual and the value of retaking the Flag, but it misses the board's central Ritual-on-Flag-Bearer combo and builds Corsair money. Neither the players nor the commentators touched that plan, and it replaces Jester as the attack. It also undersells the Lab/Alley/Aristocrat/Ghost Town build the commentators favoured. It at least avoids the Curse-heavy Flag war.
