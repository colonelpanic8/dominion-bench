I played four lines on the same shuffle (seed 1) with `dominion solo`. The board has no real draw and no +Buy in the Kingdom, so no big engine is possible. The pick is a money deck built around Remodel, Amulet and Farmhands, with Remodel turning Gold into Provinces. It had 4 Provinces by turn 12, about one Province ahead of the other three lines, but that rests on a single seed.

**Board:** A money board in disguise. The only cards that make your hand bigger work on the next turn (Way of the Squirrel, Enchantress, Grotto), and nothing in the Kingdom gives +Buy. The only +Buy sources are Fortune, which sits under the Gladiator pile, and most Loots, which come from Foray. So the game is one buy per turn, decided by money density. Remodel adds a second gain on a turn by turning Gold, Loot or Peddler into a Province. Still open going in: was there a big engine I couldn't see? Playing the advocate's line (below) says no.

**Key interactions:**
- **Remodel + Gold, Loot or Peddler:** turns a $6 Gold, $7 Loot or $8 Peddler into a Province, which is a second gain on a turn that has only one Buy. In the test, the turn-12 Remodel of a Gold was the 4th Province.
- **Peddler + Actions in play:** Peddler costs $0 once 4 Actions are in play, and last turn's Amulet, Enchantress or Grotto still count. The catch is that it uses your only Buy. Take it only when you have under $6 and a Remodel is coming.
- **Remodel Estate → Farmhands:** Farmhands' on-gain effect sets aside a card from your hand to play next turn, and that card can be the Amulet or the Gold you can't use this turn.
- **Way of the Squirrel + spare terminals:** a second terminal you can't use becomes +2 cards next turn, so 7-card hands. It is not a real draw engine.
- **Gladiator → Fortune:** each Gladiator play usually removes one Gladiator from the pile. Once the 5 Gladiators are gone, Fortune ($8 plus 8 Debt) doubles your money and adds a Buy, worth 2 Provinces on a $10 hand. In the test it took until turn 12 to pay off.
- **Foray:** $3 for a $7 Loot if you discard 3 differently named cards that you didn't play. With 5-card hands that is rare, and it uses the Buy. It was good once in the test (turn 11, Province, Copper and Enchantress discarded, gained Staff).

**Other cards:**
- Grotto ($2): a non-terminal filler. Set Victory cards aside so they are replaced next turn.
- Enchantress: the only attack. It turns the opponent's first Action each turn into a cantrip, which neuters a lone Remodel or Gladiator. On your side it gives +2 cards next turn.
- Guardian: blocks Enchantress, and Sword if it comes out of the Loot pile. Only worth it if the opponent goes heavy on Enchantress.
- Monastery: there are few gains per turn to trigger it, and this deck wants its Coppers. Skip.
- Transmute: the Potion is a dead $4. Skip; the low rating agrees.

**Plans considered:**
1. **Remodel/Amulet good-stuff** (picked): Amulets, Remodel, 2–3 Farmhands, Gold, Squirrel for spare terminals, Remodel for Provinces late. Needs no scarce piles. Weakness: Enchantress.
2. **Gladiator → Fortune**: drain Gladiators, then Fortune. Opponent can block a Gladiator by revealing a copy, and the 5 Fortunes are shared. Weakness: slow and debt-heavy.
3. **Action-heavy "engine"** (advocate): Amulets, Farmhands, Enchantress, free Peddlers, Foray for Loots. Weakness: no +Buy, so the action density has nothing to cash out on.
4. **BM + Gladiator** (the floor).

**Recommendation:** Play the Remodel/Amulet good-stuff deck. It was about one Province ahead of BM + Gladiator by turn 12 on the same shuffle. The edge comes from Remodel's extra Province gains, Amulet's flexible economy, and Farmhands making a spare terminal playable. Leave Gladiators alone unless the opponent starts draining them. If Fortune opens while 5 or more Provinces remain and you hit $8, one Fortune is a reasonable buy.

**Against their best plan:** Against BM + Gladiator or Enchantress, you have roughly a one-Province edge. Their Enchantress costs you most on turns where your only Action is Remodel, so when you can, play a Grotto, Amulet or Farmhands first to absorb it. Against a Fortune player, nothing is contested except Provinces, and their debt turns give you tempo.

**Advocate's case:** "Actions in play make Peddlers free, Squirrel makes 7-card hands, Foray makes Loots that bring +Buy, and Remodel turns Peddlers into Provinces." Played out, it falls behind from turn 8: free Peddlers keep competing with Gold for the one Buy, and Foray rarely finds 3 different names. It had 3 Provinces to the pick's 4 by T12–13, and only one Loot by turn 13.

**How the pick loses:** To an opponent using 2 Enchantresses, which shuts off your Remodel turns, or to a lucky early Fortune. Both are visible early: watch for Enchantress buys, and for the Gladiator pile shrinking.

**Open:**
- 4/3: Remodel / Amulet (tested). Farmhands / Amulet is also fine.
- 5/2: nothing is worth $5, so take Remodel or Farmhands with the $5, plus Grotto with the $2.

**Buy priorities:**
- Shuffles 1–2: Amulet (trash Estates, gain Silver when short), Farmhands, Gold at $6–7.
- Later: Remodel Estates into Farmhands, Province at $8, and a $0 Peddler only on a hand under $6 with a Remodel coming.
- Greening: Province at $8 from about turn 9. Remodel Gold into Province. Take Duchies, or Remodel Silver into Duchy, once 4 Provinces or fewer remain.

**Ending:** By Provinces, around turns 15–17. A three-pile ending is unrealistic: only Gladiator/Fortune and Peddler could drain, and both slowly.

**Watch out for:**
- Enchantress from the opponent. Answer with a cheap Action to play first, or Guardian.
- The opponent draining Gladiators to reach Fortune. Decide whether you want one of the 5.
- Remodel Province → Province does nothing for the score but empties the pile. Use it to close the game when you're ahead.

**Least sure of:** Whether the edge over BM + Gladiator or a double-Enchantress money deck holds up. It comes from one seed, and the idle opponent never blocked Gladiator and never attacked. A second seed for each plan, with Enchantress's effect added, would settle it.
