## Beyond the key
- Forge trashing Hunting Grounds plus a $2 card gains a Province, and Hunting Grounds' trash trigger adds a Duchy (9 VP): plausible. $6 + $2 = $8 exactly.
- Forge on Hunting Grounds + 2 Coppers gains a new Hunting Grounds + Duchy and thins 2 Coppers: plausible. Coppers cost $0, so the total is $6.
- Trader on an Estate gives 2 Silvers; Trader on Hunting Grounds gives 6 Silvers + Duchy: plausible from card text.
- "The only fast thinning is Trader": doubtful. Trader replaces each Estate with two Silvers, so it doesn't shrink the deck. Forge trashes any number of cards in one play.
- Call Coin of the Realm after Hunting Grounds to get the Action for Forge: plausible. It's called after playing an Action, for +2 Actions.
- Noble Brigand is best against a Silver-heavy opponent: doubtful. It takes at most one Silver or Gold per attack, and a deck full of Silver never takes its Copper penalty. The key's hosts say a Silver flood is the counter.
- Scrying Pool bogs down because nothing cheap thins Coppers: doubtful. Forge trashes Coppers in bulk, and Scrying Pool's draw ignores how many Coppers are in the deck once the Actions are thick.
- Trader's reaction can turn a Noble Brigand Copper into a Silver: plausible, but it needs Trader in hand on the opponent's turn, so it's rare.
- Beggar reacts to both Noble Brigand and Scrying Pool: plausible. Both are Attacks.
- Scholar is worse than Hunting Grounds here: plausible. Both are terminal draw, and Scholar discards your hand.
- Mandarin is a terminal +$3 that collides with Hunting Grounds: plausible.
- Potion counts as $4 for Forge, and Scrying Pool counts as $2: plausible. Forge sums $ costs only.

## Summary
The analysis spots the Forge + Hunting Grounds Province + Duchy trick and that there's no +Buy, but it sidelines Forge as an optional late buy. It opens Trader/Silver instead of Silver/Silver, and it writes off the Scrying Pool/Noble Brigand deck the hosts found competitive. A player following it would play Hunting Grounds money: reasonable, but without the Forge-centred deck the key credits.
