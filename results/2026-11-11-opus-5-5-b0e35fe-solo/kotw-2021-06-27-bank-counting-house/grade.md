- Curse as a free third pile, each Curse scoring −1 +1 = 0 once empty, with Talisman copying it: plausible. Curse is not a Victory card and costs $0, so Tower and Talisman both apply.
- Raze as the third pile to empty: plausible. $2 non-Victory, so Talisman copies it and Tower scores it.
- Jester is minor against a Copper deck: doubtful. Jester discards the top card of the deck, which on this board is the top-decked Counting House the loop depends on.
- Haunted Woods does little against the rush: plausible. Its attack only top-decks the hand left after the first buy, which is near-empty once Treasures are played; it doesn't force a shuffle.
- Horse Traders' reaction draw can force an unwanted reshuffle: plausible. Its +1 Card at the start of the next turn draws from an empty deck; setting it aside is optional, so it's avoidable.
- Lead with Raze or Settlers to absorb Enchantress: plausible. Enchantress replaces only the first Action played with +1 Card +1 Action.
- Settlers/Bustling Village empties only as a whole split pile: plausible.
