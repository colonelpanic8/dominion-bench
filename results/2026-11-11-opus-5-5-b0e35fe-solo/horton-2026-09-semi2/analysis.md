I recommend a **Souk money deck**: two Tireless Souks, one Ronin and a couple of Squires, with Importers and late Enlarges doing the greening. I played one solo game of each plan. The money line reached 4 Provinces by turn 13, faster than an ordinary Big Money + X deck (turn 14–17). The engine line had its big turn on turn 10, then clogged and fell behind.

---

**Board:** A money board in disguise. Souk is the strongest card here (+Buy, +$7 minus $1 per card in hand, trash 2 cards from hand when gained). Tireless sits on the Souk pile, so each Souk goes back on top of your deck at end of turn. The engine pieces exist: Squire, Village Green and Coin of the Realm give Actions, and Ronin draws. But the only draw is Ronin's "draw until 7", which stops working once your hand is full of Treasures and green cards. Souk also wants a small hand, which is the opposite of what Ronin produces. There are no Attacks or junkers, and there is no alternative VP. The game ends when the Provinces run out.

**Key interactions:**
- **Souk + Tireless:** a Souk played on turn N goes on top of the deck after the Clean-up draw. It is in hand on turn N+2, or on turn N+1 if anything draws a card. In a 5-card hand it plays as +$3 with +Buy, and it cannot miss a shuffle.
- **Souk then Ronin:** play Souk while your hand is small, then Ronin refills you to 7. Ronin is a Shadow: after each shuffle it sits at the bottom of your deck and can be played from there. That works out to one guaranteed Ronin play per shuffle. Squire's "+2 Actions" for $2 lets you play Souk and Ronin in the same turn. A typical line: Squire, then Souk with 3 cards left (+$4), then Ronin draws 4.
- **Importer + Souk:** Importer gains a card at the *start* of your next turn, while your full hand is still there. So the Souk's "trash 2 from hand" removes Estates. Late in the game Importer gains a Duchy every play.
- **Trappers' Lodge:** you get only 5 Favors, because Importer's text gives none. Spend them in the first few turns to put gains on top of your deck. A gain topdecked on turn 2 is guaranteed to be in your turn-3 hand.
- **Animal Fair's alternative cost:** when you have a spare Buy, you can trash an Action from hand (an extra Souk, an Importer or a Squire) to gain Animal Fair (+$4) for free.

**Other cards:**
- **Enlarge:** best with Gold. Trashing a Gold gains a Province, and it does this twice (now and next turn), so it is a late-game greening accelerator. I didn't test it.
- **Transmogrify:** a Reserve trasher. Estate becomes Silver or Importer, $4 becomes Souk, and both go to hand. Good in the engine, marginal in money.
- **Village Green:** a village for the engine. Its Reaction does nothing here, because nothing on the board makes you discard.
- **Coin of the Realm:** an engine village only.
- **Armory:** nothing worth gaining at $4 for money. Skip it.
- **Squire:** its "when trashed, gain an Attack" does nothing, because the kingdom has no Attacks.
- **Ratings check:** Souk (+4.36) agrees with my read. Tireless (−1.75) is underrated here because it sits on Souk. I agree with Armory (−2.24) and Animal Fair (−1.15), apart from the free-gain trick.

**Plans considered:**
- **Souk money** (2 Souks, 1 Ronin, 1–2 Squires, Importer, Golds, late Enlarge): first $8 on T8, 4 Provinces by T13. It needs Souk early. Its weakness is the ceiling of about one Province per turn.
- **Souk/Ronin engine** (Importers, Transmogrify, Squires and Village Greens, Ronin, Souks, Animal Fair): first big turn T10. It needs heavy thinning and few Souks. Its weakness is that Ronin's draw stalls on Treasure and green, and Souk loses value in a big hand.
- **Plain Big Money + Ronin** is the floor. Souk beats it, so it was never a contender.

**Recommendation:** Souk money. On this board an engine never reaches the 3-Province turns it would need to catch up. In my lines the engine had about 11–14 VP on turn 11 against the money deck's 19. The two Souks and their +Buy are the payload, Tireless makes them come back every shuffle, and Squire plus Ronin cover the turns with spare Actions.

**Open:**
- **5/2** (allowed by the tournament rule, and my choice): Souk / Squire. Use a Favor to put the second buy on top of your deck, so it is guaranteed in your turn-3 hand.
- **4/3:** Silver / Importer (Importer gains Souk on turn 3–4), or Importer / Importer. Use Favors to put the Souk on top.

**Buy priorities:**
- At $5: a second Souk first (Souk trashes Estates when gained), then one Ronin.
- At $6–7: Gold.
- At $2: a Squire or two, so Souk and Ronin can share a turn.
- From mid-game: one Enlarge, for Gold → Province twice.
- Importers switch to gaining Duchies from about turn 12.
- Province at $8. Duchy at $5–7 once 4 Provinces are left.

**Ending:** The Provinces run out. No pile is in danger and there are no Curses, so a three-pile ending won't happen. In a money mirror it comes down to a Duchy dance, where free Duchies from Importer and Enlarge decide close games.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Mirror:** whoever gets two Souks and a Ronin first is ahead. The rest is Province and Duchy tempo, plus following the Penultimate Province Rule.
- **Against the engine:** money is ahead around turn 11. The engine has to average two Provinces a turn from turn 10, and my line shows it can't.

**Advocate's case:** The engine's strongest version keeps exactly two Souks. It trashes every Copper through Souk gains, Transmogrify and Enlarge, and runs on Squires, Village Greens and one Ronin. Payload comes from Animal Fairs gained free by trashing Squires, and Souk is played early while the hand is small. Its peak is about $20 with 4 Buys, i.e. 2 Provinces. It falls behind on turn 11: money has 19 VP to the engine's ~14 at most. Money then adds about 6 VP per turn, and the engine can't make up a 2-Province gap with alternating good and bad turns.

**How the pick loses:**
- Bad luck with Souk shuffles.
- An opponent who builds that thin two-Souk engine better than I did.
- A deck that gets too thick for Souk, with too many Treasures in hand.

**Watch out for:**
- **Too many Souks.** Tireless puts them all on top of your deck together, and two Souks in one hand cost each other $1. Keep 2, or 3 at most.
- **Ronin before Souk.** Always play Souk while your hand is small, then Ronin.
- **Three or more players.** The game gets shorter and the Provinces are split more ways, which favors money even more.

**Least sure of:** That the engine is too slow. My engine game was a single seed with misplays (the stray Curse and Silver, too many Souks, running out of Actions on T8). To test it, replay a clean two-Souk engine on two seeds. If it averages two Provinces a turn from turn 10, switch to it.

**Assumed:**
- **Tireless timing:** Souk goes on top of the deck after the Clean-up draw. The engine confirmed this.
- **Ronin:** it can be played from anywhere in the deck after a shuffle.
- **Importer:** it gives no Favors beyond setup, so each player has 5 Favors in total.
- **Squire:** its "when trashed" ability does nothing on this board.
- **Starting split in the tests:** the tool can't arrange the starting deck, so the money line used a 4/3 split, not the 5/2 Souk/Squire opening I recommend. The 5/2 line is reasoned on paper only.
- **Untested pieces:** I didn't test Enlarge, or Squire in the money deck. The money line bought neither.
