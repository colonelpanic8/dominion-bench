## Beyond the key
- Importer's start-of-turn gain lands while the new hand is already drawn, so a gained Souk's "trash up to 2" hits Estates in that hand: plausible. Importer's gain is at the start of the next turn, after the Clean-up draw.
- Each player has only 5 Favors (1 from the Ally plus Importer's Setup +4), so Trappers' Lodge topdecks are scarce: plausible. Importer's play text gives no Favors.
- Ronin, a Shadow, sits at the bottom after a shuffle and can be played from the deck, which gives a reliable Ronin play each shuffle: plausible. That is the Shadow rule and Ronin's text.
- Play Souk while the hand is small, then Ronin: plausible, and the key's grader note credits it. Souk loses $1 per card in hand, and Ronin refills to 7.
- Two Souks in one hand cost each other $1: plausible. The second Souk counts as a card in hand when the first resolves.
- Enlarge trashing Gold gains a Province, twice: plausible. $6+$2 = $8, now and next turn.
- Animal Fair gained free by trashing a spare Action when you have a spare Buy: plausible. The alternative cost applies when buying.
- Village Green's Reaction and Squire's on-trash do nothing here: plausible. Nothing discards outside Clean-up, and the kingdom has no Attack cards.
- Armory is not worth buying: doubtful. It topdecks a $4 Transmogrify, Importer or Village Green, which is how the designer opens.
- Souk money reaches 4 Provinces by turn 13 and beats any Ronin engine: doubtful. It rests on one seed per plan with acknowledged engine misplays, and it contradicts the designer's playtesting.

The analysis gets Tireless timing right (the trap the tournament fell into) and has good play-order points (Souk before Ronin). But it recommends a Souk money deck with one Ronin, skips Armory, and rushes Souks with its $5s. The designer's plan is a Ronin draw-to-X deck opened Armory → Transmogrify/Importer → Coin of the Realm, taking Ronin before Souks.
