I ran a solo game for the 5/2 opening and one for the 4/3 opening; the analysis is below.

**Board:** Governor is the strongest card here (Glicko 5.50), and the Friendly trait lets it copy itself. At the start of Clean-up you may discard one Governor from your hand to gain another. You can do this every turn you keep a Governor unplayed, at no cost in $ or Buys. Governor is also a complete engine by itself: it is non-terminal and gives +3 Cards, gains a Gold, or trashes Gold to gain a Province. Every Governor mode also helps the opponent. So the game turns on how the 10 Governors split, which mode you choose, and how the game ends on Provinces. Open questions were whether anything else is worth buying and how much feeding the opponent matters.

**Key interactions:**
- **Governor + Friendly:** one free Governor per turn, every turn you keep one in hand at Clean-up. Keeping it gives the opponent nothing; playing it always gives them something. In the 5/2 solo line I owned 3 Governors by turn 5, 6 by turn 9 and 9 by turn 12. Uncontested, the pile is gone by about turn 10; contested, expect roughly 5–5.
- **Governor's Gold mode, then its trash mode turning Gold into Province ($6 → $8):** a Province gainer that needs no Buy. On 5/2 turn 12 I turned 2 Golds into Provinces and bought 1 with Forager's +Buy: 3 Provinces in one turn. A Governor in Gold mode refills the Golds for the next turn.
- **Throne Room or Flagship + Governor:** a double Governor. Throne Room on a Governor nets +1 Action, so it doubles as a village. Flagship is terminal, so play it as your last Action: it gives +$2 now and doubles your first Governor next turn (+6 Cards on turns 9, 11 and 14).
- **Forager + trashing:** +Buy, and +$ for each differently named Treasure in the trash. Governor puts Silver and Gold there (Silver → Governor and Gold → Province upgrades), so Forager reached +$2 by turn 12.
- **Way of the Owl on a spare Governor as your last Action:** draws up to 6 without giving the opponent any cards. Use it to avoid feeding them through draw mode.

**Other cards:**
- **Throne Room (2.73):** see above; slightly better than Flagship in a deck full of Governors.
- **Experiment (1.72):** opponent's Governor trash mode lets you turn an Estate into 2 Experiments; played as Owl it doesn't return to its pile. Filler.
- **Advisor (−0.51):** a draw card that doesn't feed the opponent. Governor does the job better.
- **Merchant Camp (−2.39):** puts itself back on your deck, so you always start with +2 Actions, which lets Flagship be played early. At most 1 copy.
- **Anvil (−1.22):** gains $4 cards in the Buy phase, but nothing it reaches beats a Governor. Skip.
- **Tactician (−1.88):** its discard happens before Clean-up, so Friendly can't use the Governors it throws away, and Governor already draws. Skip.
- **Leprechaun (−1.37):** a Wish (from exactly 7 cards in play) could gain a Governor to hand, but otherwise you take a Hex: Plague gives you a Curse, Misery costs −2 VP. Skip.

The ratings agree with my reading. The one gap is Friendly (−0.11): on the Governor pile it is the main point of the board.

### Build lines (`dominion solo`, no opponent)
- **Governor engine, 5/2 (seed 1):** T1 Governor, T2 pass · T3 Forager · T4 Flagship (Governor missed turns 3–4) · T5 kept the Governor → Friendly gain · T6 Forager trashed Copper, Silver · T7 Flagship, Governor · T8 Silver · T9 Governor doubled by Flagship, 3 draw plays, $10 with 2 Buys → 2 Governors, plus Friendly (6 Governors) · T10 Gold · T11 drew the deck, trashed an Estate for Flagship, gained a Gold → Province + Governor, plus Friendly · T12 drew the deck, turned 2 Golds into Provinces, $11 → Province (**4 Provinces, 24 VP**) · T13 a $5 dud hand, Duchy · T14 $14 with 2 Buys plus 3 Golds gained (about 5 Provinces).

**Plans considered:**
- **Governor engine** (Governors, 1–2 Foragers, 1–2 Throne Rooms or Flagships). It reached 4 Provinces by turn 12 on both splits. Needs: half or more of the Governor pile. Weakness: draw mode gives the opponent bigger hands.
- **Big Money + X** (Leprechaun or Flagship as X). Expect the usual BM+X pace of 4 Provinces around turns 14–17, and Leprechaun's Hexes slow it further. If you skip Governor, the opponent uses Friendly to take all 10. Rejected.
- **Feeding off their Governors** (skip Governor and live on the cards and Silvers their plays give you). They control the mode: Owl or trash mode gives you little. Rejected.

**Recommendation:** Governor engine, and contest the Governor pile hard. Two turns of 3 Provinces beat a money deck's 1 per turn, and in the mirror the Governor split decides the game. Limit what you give away: keep a Governor for Friendly whenever the pile still has copies, use Gold or trash mode once you have enough draw, and use Owl for the last draw.

**Open:** you may arrange your deck, so **choose 5/2: Governor / nothing.** Nothing at $2 is worth buying. If forced to 4/3: Silver / Forager (or Silver / Silver to reach $5 sooner; my 4/3 line got its first Governor only on turn 6).

**Buy priorities:**
1. Governor at every $5–7, plus Friendly every turn while the pile lasts. On turns 3–5, keeping a Governor beats playing it.
2. One Forager at $3–4. Then Throne Room or Flagship at $4, one or two copies in total.
3. Gold at $6–7 once the pile is gone or you have about 6 Governors.

Turn Golds into Provinces from about turn 11. When drawing your deck, buy Province plus Duchy.

**Ending:** the Governor pile empties first (about turns 9–11). The game ends on Provinces, around turn 14–16. A three-pile ending is unlikely, since Experiments return to their pile and nothing else drains fast. Watch the Penultimate Province Rule, because a turn of 3 Provinces is possible.

**Against their best plan:** in the mirror, the Governor split and turn order decide it. Giving cards away is symmetrical, so the player who makes fewer draw-mode plays (Owl for the last draw, keeping Governors for Friendly) comes out ahead. Against money the engine wins comfortably: money reaches 4 Provinces around turn 15 at best, and turning Golds into Provinces outscores it from turn 11.

**Advocate's case:** money's best case is Silver/Flagship, Gold, then Province, using the Silvers and +1 Cards from the opponent's Governors. Even generously that is BM pace: about 2 Provinces by turn 12. The engine lines had 4 by turn 12 and could take 3 per turn after that. Money is behind by about 2 Provinces at turn 12, and the gap grows.

**How the pick loses:** you get the short end of the Governor split (3–7), or you play draw mode freely and give the opponent 6+ cards a turn. Their Governor counts and the modes they choose are visible each turn.

**Watch out for:**
- The opponent's trash mode lets you upgrade a card by $1. Take Estate → Forager or Experiment, and Silver → Throne Room.
- Their draw-mode plays enlarge your next hand, so plan for it.
- Don't over-trash Copper with Forager: both lines nearly stalled on dud hands (turns 8, 11 and 13).

**Least sure of:** whether 5/2 beats 4/3. Both lines had 4 Provinces by turn 12, because the 5/2 Governor missed turns 3–4. Running more seeds would settle it. The second uncertainty is how much each draw-mode play is worth to the opponent (I estimate about +$1 each); a mirror game is the test.

**Assumed:**
- Friendly works once per Clean-up. The tool offered it only once, but I never had two Governors in hand at Clean-up to test it.
- An opponent's +1 Card from Governor goes to their hand for their next turn.
- The solo games had no opponent and were one seed each. Mistakes in my lines: I gave up 3 VP by trashing a Duchy to Forager on turn 14, and on turn 5 of the 4/3 line I first ended the Action phase with Forager unplayed, then undid it.
