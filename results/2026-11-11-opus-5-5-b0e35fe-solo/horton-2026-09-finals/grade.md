## Beyond the key
- Only 8 Estates in 2-player, so at most 8 scoring Triumphs shared by both players; Estate denial by buying them at $1: plausible. Triumph scores only "if you did" gain an Estate, and Flourishing Trade makes Estate cost $1.
- Five River Shrine plays by either player switch on Flourishing Trade: plausible. River Shrine is the only Omen, and Sun removal counts both players' Omens.
- River Shrine gaining a Hostelry at Clean-up lets you discard unplayed Coppers for Horses: plausible. Hostelry's on-gain ability discards Treasures from hand, and unplayed ones are still there at the start of Clean-up.
- Stonemason under Flourishing Trade costs $1, and overpaying $3 gains 2 Weavers/Hostelries (each $3) for one Buy: plausible. Each counts toward Triumph, but as Buy-phase gains they draw nothing from Footpad.
- Change under Flourishing Trade turns Duchy ($4) into Province ($7) for 3 Debt: plausible from text.
- Change's Debt "blocks Triumph turns": doubtful. Debt can be paid off with $ in the Buy phase. It only costs money, and Change itself gives +$3 while you have Debt.
- Each Vineyard needs a Potion in play, so one per turn: wrong. Stonemason trashing an Alchemist gains 2 Vineyards (0P costs less than 3P), and Change reaches Alchemist from an Estate.
- An opponent gaining a Curse during your Action phase draws them a card: plausible. Footpad says "an Action phase", not "your". This contradicts the analysis's own "Assumed" line ("their own Action phase").
- Artificer discarding 2 cards gains a Silver that Footpad draws at once: plausible only once Flourishing Trade is active (Silver $2). Before that it takes 3 discards.
- Flourishing Trade doesn't reduce Projects or Triumph's Debt: plausible. Events and Projects aren't cards.
- Witch's Hut discarding Weaver plus another Action also Curses the opponent: plausible from text.
- No mention of the lose-track or Stop-Moving rule complications around Weaver, so no extra credit there.

## Summary
The analysis finds Horton's main idea: Footpad-drawn mass gaining with Hostelry Horses and Weavers, and Flourishing Trade's Action-as-Buys spent on several Triumphs. It opens River Shrine without Exploration, and it misses the Change/Stonemason route to Alchemist and Vineyard. Instead it suggests a late Potion, and its megaturn arrives about two turns later than Horton's.
