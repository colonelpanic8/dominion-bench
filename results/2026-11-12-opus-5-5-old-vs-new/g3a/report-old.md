**RESULT:** I lost, 22 VP to 40. Ash (P1) took the last Province on their turn 14, which ended the game on Provinces. I had played 13 turns.

**My final deck (23 cards):** 7 Copper, 3 Silver, 1 Gold, 4 Estate, 2 Duchy, 2 Province, Barge, Cardinal, Courtier, Steward, Village Green.

**The plan I made (in notes.md):** a light engine. Village Green as the village, Barge and Steward for draw, and Steward or Way of the Goat for trashing. Payload would come from Courtier (revealing Village Green gives +$3, a Gold and +Buy) plus Gamble for extra Buys. I would open Steward and Cardinal, add Village Green, Barge, Courtier and Gold, and start buying Provinces once I could reach about $13 with two Buys.

**How it worked:**
1. The engine never came together. By turn 10 I had only one each of Village Green, Barge and Courtier, so my hands were mostly Copper and Silver, and my first Province came on turn 10.
2. Ash opened Courtier and took a Gold with nearly every Courtier play. They had 5 Golds and 3 Provinces by turn 11, and their plain money deck took 6 of the 8 Provinces before my deck could compete.
3. Some tricks worked: Gamble played a Barge for my next turn and a Steward for +$2, and Cardinal exiled two Silvers and a Gold from Ash's deck. The plan was still the wrong choice: I should have bought Courtier early and played for money rather than build the engine.
