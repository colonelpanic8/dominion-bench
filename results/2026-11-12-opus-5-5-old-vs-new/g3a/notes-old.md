# g5 — I am P2 (Birch). Opp: Ash. No Colonies, no Shelters. My split 4/3.

## Analysis
**Board:** Engine-capable: Village Green (village, also plays itself when discarded), Barge (+3 cards +Buy, duration),
Steward (draw/trash). Trashing: Steward, Way of the Goat (any Action -> trash a card), Displace (exile).
Payload: Courtier (reveal multi-type: Village Green = 3 types -> +$3, Gold, +Buy; Tunnel = +$3 + Gold), Gamble (+Buy event $2),
Mandarin. Scoring: Provinces/Duchies, Tunnel 2VP, Silk Road (alt VP per 4 Victory).
**Key interactions:**
- Courtier revealing Village Green: +$3 +Buy + gain Gold (or +Action). Revealing Tunnel: +$3 + Gold.
- Gamble discards top card: Tunnel -> Gold; Village Green -> plays it; Action/Treasure -> play it. Gamble = $2 for +Buy.
- Mandarin gain topdecks played Treasures; then Gamble replays them (Gold net +$1 each).
- Cardinal: exiles opp's $3-6 cards (Silver/Gold/Tunnel); careful: its discard triggers opp's Tunnels/Village Greens.
**Plans:** (a) BM+Barge/Courtier: ~4 Prov by T15. (b) Village Green/Barge engine w/ Steward thinning + Courtier/Gold payload, Gamble/Barge buys: double Provinces ~T13-14. (c) Tunnel/Silk Road slog: slow.
**Recommendation:** (b), light engine. Open Steward + Cardinal (4/3). Then Village Green, Barge, Courtier; Gold at $6-7 early;
Steward trashes Estates/Coppers (keep ~$ economy). Way of the Goat on spare terminals for trashing. Green when $13+ & 2 buys.
Late: Tunnels for spare $3 with extra buys (2VP), Duchies at $5.
**Watch:** opp's Cardinal exiling my Gold/Silver; don't play Cardinal into opp Tunnels. PPR at end.
