# Game g2 — P1 — notes

## Kingdom analysis (quick)
**Board:** Tunnel, Council Room, Jester, Litter, Patrol, Vineyard(P), Border Guard, Cage, Astrolabe, Market Square; Way of the Squirrel, Seaway. No Colonies/Shelters.
No real trashing (only Cage self-trash), so engines are slowed by starting junk. Draw: Council Room, Patrol, Litter (Lab-village with $1 debt per play). +Buy: CR, MS, Astrolabe, Seaway token.
Way of the Squirrel means a terminal that collides can be played as "+2 Cards at end of turn" (a bigger next hand), so 2–3 terminals in a money deck is fine.

**Key interactions:**
- Way of the Squirrel + extra terminals: a colliding CR/Jester/Patrol still adds 2 cards to my next hand.
- Cage = temporary trasher: set aside up to 4 junk cards (Estates/extra Coppers) until my first Victory gain, then they come back into my hand. Cage + Market Square in hand when it's trashed = Gold (marginal).
- Tunnel: 2VP for $3, the cheap late-game green. It becomes a Gold if discarded by Border Guard, or by the opponent's Jester (which gives a Curse too).
- Jester: if the opponent's top card is a Victory card they get a Curse; otherwise I pick who gains a copy (I take Gold/Silver, give them Copper).

**Plans considered:**
- Council Room-BM (+Jester as 2nd terminal, Squirrel for collisions): solid, ~4 Provinces by T14–16.
- Litter/CR/Patrol draw engine with MS/Astrolabe buys: no trashing, Litter debt eats payload, Jester junk hurts it. Too slow.
- Vineyard: needs Potion plus lots of Actions. No.

**Recommendation:** Council Room / Jester Big Money. Use Way of the Squirrel on a second terminal drawn in the same hand.
**Open:** 4/3 → Silver/Silver (or Silver/Astrolabe) · 5/2 → Council Room/Cage (set aside Estates)
**Buy priorities:** $8 Province; $6–7 Gold; $5: first CR, then Jester, then a 2nd CR or Patrol (max 3 terminals); otherwise Gold/Silver. $3–4 Silver.
Green: Duchy at $5–7 once Provinces ≤ 4–5; at $3–4 late, Tunnel (2VP) over Estate.
**Ending:** Provinces. Curses could run out if Jester mirror.
**Watch out for:** opponent's engine; PPR; Jester giving them Golds (I choose who gains: take good cards for me, give Copper to them).
**Least sure of:** Jester vs Patrol as the 2nd terminal.
