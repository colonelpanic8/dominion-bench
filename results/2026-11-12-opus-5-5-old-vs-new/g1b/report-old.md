**RESULT:** Birch (P1, me) 52 VP, rank 1 · Ash 45 VP, rank 2 · 22 turns each · it ended when the Provinces ran out (the opponent bought the last one while still behind).

**My final deck (39 cards):** 5 Province, 3 Duchy, 4 Tunnel, 5 Estate, 5 Gold, 3 Silver, 7 Copper, 1 Council Room, 1 Jester.

**The plan (in notes.md):** play Big Money with Council Room plus Jester. The board has no real trasher, and Litter's $1 debt per play plus Jester's junking make an engine slow, so I ruled one out.
- **Opening:** on a 5/2 split, Council Room and Cage. Cage set an Estate aside until my first Province.
- **Buys:** Gold at $6–7 and Province at $8, with Jester as the second terminal. A colliding terminal could be played as Way of the Squirrel for 2 extra cards next turn.
- **Greening:** once the Provinces were nearly half gone, Duchy at $5–7 and Tunnel (2 VP for $3) at $3–4, while checking the second-to-last-Province risk.

**How it worked:**
1. The money deck was fast. Five Golds and early Provinces on turns 7, 11 and 12 put me ahead of the opponent's Litter/Patrol deck, which kept paying debt and drawing its own green cards.
2. Greening went well. A $6 hand bought two Tunnels (4 VP) instead of a Duchy (3), and I emptied the Duchy and Tunnel piles so the opponent couldn't catch up on points.
3. Jester and Cage only helped a little. Jester was played once and took a Silver, and Cage removed one Estate for a few turns. The win came from consistent money and the endgame, not from the board's interactions.
