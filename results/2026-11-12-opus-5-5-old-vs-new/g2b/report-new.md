**RESULT: Birch (P1) 32 VP, Ash (me, P2) 30 VP. I lost.** The Province pile ran out on Birch's turn 15; I had taken 14 turns.

**My final deck (19 cards):** 4 Province, 2 Duchy, 2 Gold, 1 Silver, 2 Copper, 3 Lost City, 2 Villa, Junk Dealer, Sentinel, Lighthouse.

**The plan:** build an engine. Lost City for draw that doesn't use up Actions, Sentinel and Junk Dealer to trash cards, Villa for +Buy and +$1, and Borrow (an event that gives +$1 now for one card less next turn) to reach key prices. Openers were Junk Dealer and Sentinel, then Gold, Lost Cities, Villas and another Gold, with Provinces from turn 9. Birch built almost the same deck, so it became a mirror match.

**How it worked:**
- The build went to plan. I drew most of my deck from turn 8 and took 4 Provinces between turns 9 and 13. But real +Buy only comes from Villa, so the engine never got past one Province a turn. With no +Buy advantage, Birch moving first decided the race.
- The deciding call was turn 13. I took the second-to-last Province to lead 27–25, judging Birch's next hand too weak to reach $8. That held for one turn. My turn 14 could only reach $7, so I couldn't stretch the lead past 6 points. On turn 15 Birch's Lighthouse, Villa, Gold, two Coppers and Borrow made exactly $8 for the last Province. A Duchy on turn 13 might have been safer.
- Payload was my weak spot. I trashed Coppers aggressively and added only two Golds, so my greened turns 12 and 14 managed only $5–7. One more Gold or a Vault instead of trashing the last Coppers would probably have given me the $8 or more I needed to end the game myself.

One rule I learned during play: Borrow uses a Buy and gives one back, so it adds no extra Buy.
