**RESULT (the Provinces ran out): 1. Birch (me) 32 VP (15 turns), 2. Ash 30 VP (14 turns)**

**My final deck (24 cards):** 4 Province, 2 Duchy, 2 Estate, 3 Lost City, 2 Villa, 1 Sentinel, 1 Lighthouse, 2 Gold, 2 Silver, 3 Copper.

**The plan (in notes.md):** Build a light engine. Sentinel trashes the starting Estates and some Coppers. Lost City is the main draw because it draws and gives +Actions. Villa supplies +Buy and +$1: bought during the Buy phase it goes to hand and can be played straight away. Borrow covers the remaining +Buy and +$1 needs. Money was the payload, with a Gold or two. The plan was to start buying Provinces once the deck reliably made about $13 or more with 2 Buys. I rejected Big Money with Vault as the main plan.

**How it worked:**
1. The opening worked. Borrow got me Sentinel on a $2 hand and Lost City on a $4 hand. Sentinel had removed all 3 Estates and 3 Coppers by turn 9. The opponent copied the plan almost exactly (Lost City, Villa, Borrow, Junk Dealer), so it became a close mirror.
2. On turn 9 I lost a full $9 buy: I sent an "end" step when I was already in the Buy phase, which ended the turn without buying. After that I was behind on Provinces until the endgame.
3. The endgame saved it. With 2 Provinces left I bought two Duchies rather than the second-to-last Province, which would have let the opponent win by buying the last one. When the opponent then took that second-to-last Province, I took the last one with exactly $8 (Lighthouse, Villa and Borrow each added $1) and won by 2.
