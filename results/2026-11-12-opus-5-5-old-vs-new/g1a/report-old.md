**RESULT (the Provinces ran out): 1. Birch 50 VP (24 turns), 2. Ash 47 VP (24 turns).** I won as P2.

**Final deck (46 cards):** 10 Copper, 4 Silver, 3 Gold, 3 Jester, 1 Council Room, 1 Litter, 4 Province, 6 Duchy, 6 Estate, 2 Tunnel, 2 Curse.

**Plan (in notes.md):** The board had no real trashing and only one village (Litter, which costs 1 Debt per play), so I judged an engine too slow. I planned a money deck with Jester and Council Room, plus a Litter so I could play both in one turn. A cheap Cage would park dead green cards out of my deck. Buy rules: Province at $8, Gold at $6–7, Silver at $3–4; Duchies once 4 or fewer Provinces were left, then Estates or Tunnels near the end.

**How it worked:**
- The money deck kept pace with the opponent's Litter/Council Room/Market Square deck (2 Provinces by turn 12). But they pulled ahead with cheap Tunnels and a Cage + Market Square trick that gained them a Gold, leading 47–43 late.
- In the endgame I didn't take the last Province while it would have lost. I bought the last two Tunnels to deny them, and took Duchies to close the gap. On turn 23 I held off an Estate buy that would have let them end the game on piles while ahead.
- The winning move: on turn 23 I played Jester as Way of the Squirrel, which drew 2 extra cards at end of turn. With Council Room that gave $11 and 2 Buys on turn 24, enough for Province plus the last Estate to win 50–47.
