**RESULT: I lost, 47–50.** Birch (P2) won on turn 24 when the Provinces ran out. They bought the last Province plus an Estate. We both took 24 turns.

**My final deck (60 cards):** 9 Copper, 4 Silver, 2 Gold, 2 Council Room, 2 Litter, 2 Market Square, 1 Jester, 4 Province, 2 Duchy, 8 Estate, 6 Tunnel, 3 Curse.

**The plan I made:** There was no real trashing on this board. Draw would come from Council Room, with Litter (+2 Cards, +2 Actions, but each play costs $1) as the village. One early Jester would attack, and Market Square would give extra Buys. I'd open Jester and Cage on my 5/2 hand. In the endgame I'd follow the Penultimate Province Rule (don't buy the second-last Province if that lets them win by taking the last one) and take Duchies and Tunnels instead.

**How it worked:**
- **Good:** Cage plus Market Square gave me a free Gold when I bought my first Province. Double Council Room turns took a Province plus Tunnel, then 3 Tunnels at the Penultimate Province point. Duchies and Estates kept me ahead 47–43 with one Province left.
- **Bad:** Birch copied the build and had more Gold, because my Litters cost $1 every play. My deck was also full of green cards and Jester Curses, so in the last four turns it couldn't make $8, or $4 with two Buys, to end the game on piles first.
- **Deciding turn:** Birch bought nothing on turn 23 so that Way of the Squirrel would give them a 7-card hand. My answer drew Gold and Silver but no +Buy, so I could take only one of the last two Estates. Their big hand reached $13 and took the last Province. Earlier I should have bought Gold instead of my second Litter, and kept Coppers out of my deck, so the endgame hands could reach $8.
