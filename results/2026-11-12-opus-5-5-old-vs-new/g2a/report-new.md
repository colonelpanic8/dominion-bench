I won, 38 VP to 30.

**RESULT (the Provinces ran out): 1. Ash 38 VP (17 turns), 2. Birch 30 VP (16 turns)**

**Final deck (32 cards):** 6 Province, 2 Estate, 5 Lost City, 4 Villa, 4 Gold, 2 Silver, 3 Copper, 2 Tools, 1 Sentinel.

**The plan (in notes.md):** There were no attacks on this board, so I built an engine:
- **Draw:** Lost City, which gives +2 Cards and +2 Actions.
- **Trashing:** Sentinel.
- **Extra Buys:** Villa, and Borrow when one more $ was needed.
- **Money:** Gold.
- **Speed-up:** Tools, which gains a copy of a card in play. I used it to gain Lost Cities and Golds.

I'd buy Provinces once the deck drew most of itself, and buy two in a turn when Villa made that possible. I followed the plan. I skipped Innovation, Vault and Band of Misfits.

**How it worked:**
1. I lost two turns to my own input mistakes. On turn 1 I bought Borrow before playing my Treasures, and it counts as a buy, so they couldn't be played. On turn 7 I sent "end phase" when I was already in the buy phase, which threw away a $7 buy. That left me 6 VP behind the opponent's mirror Lost City engine after turn 11.
2. Tools made up the lost time. Copying Villa during the buy phase sends it to hand and returns me to the Action phase, so each copy gave +$1 and +1 Buy. With Borrow on top, that was enough for two Provinces on turn 13. I took the second-to-last Province only because I worked out that the opponent's current shuffle couldn't reach the $16 and two Buys needed to take both remaining.
3. In the endgame, on turn 16, I used Sentinel to trash a Copper and an Estate (giving up 1 VP) and stacked Gold on top of my deck. That guaranteed $8 on turn 17. The opponent couldn't reach $8 on their turn, and I bought the last Province to win.
