**Board:** This is an Old Witch curse slog with good trashing to defend against it. Ferry puts your −$2 token on the Old Witch pile, so on your turns Old Witch costs $3. You can open it on a 4/3, and Hermit, which gains cards up to $3, can then gain it too. Hermit plus Sewers is fast trashing that can strip Curses as they arrive. The open question was whether any engine exists. The only repeatable village is Diplomat, and it only gives Actions when your hand is small. That makes a money-and-junker game with a lot of trashing.

**Engine:** There isn't a real one; the missing piece is a village you can rely on.
- **Draw:** Old Witch (+3 Cards, terminal), Hireling (+1 card every turn), Caravan (a cantrip plus one card next turn) and Madman (one use each).
- **Actions:** Diplomat gives +2 Actions only if your hand is 5 or fewer after it draws. So you must play two non-drawing cards before it, or play it on a 3-card Outpost turn. Hireling, Caravan and Madman all make hands bigger, which works against it. Madman gives +2 Actions but is used up each time, and you only get one when Hermit's turn gains nothing in the Buy phase.
- **+Buy:** Bridge, which is there for an extra Buy, not a stack of cost cuts. Ten Bridges with no village is no payload.
- **Result:** Expect one terminal per turn, plus the odd turn a Madman lets you play two.

**Key interactions:**
- **Ferry + Old Witch:** you pay $3 for Old Witch on turn 1, and every Old Witch after that costs you $3. The opponent pays $5.
- **Hermit + Ferry'd Old Witch:** each Hermit play gains an Old Witch, which is a free attack piece. If you then gain nothing in the Buy phase, Hermit turns into a Madman. Madman gives +2 Actions and draws about 4, so that turn can play two Old Witches.
- **Hermit + Sewers:** Hermit trashes an Estate or Curse from your discard pile or hand, and Sewers then trashes a Copper or Curse from your hand. That's 2 cards out per Hermit play. Under the tournament's old wording, Hermit's own trash also triggers Sewers. If your hand is still there at that moment, that's a third trash.
- **Old Witch + Sewers, defending:** when you're hit by Old Witch you may trash a Curse from your hand, and Sewers then trashes another card from your hand. This makes Sewers a strong answer to the opponent's curses.
- **Rogue + Hermit:** Hermits that turn into Madmen land in the trash at $3, so Rogue usually works as a terminal +$2 that gains a Hermit back. It recycles Madmen, but slowly.

**Other cards:**
- **Treasure Trove:** a non-terminal Gold gainer, so it uses no Action. In a deck already short of terminal slots it's the money card. The Copper it gains can be fed to Sewers.
- **Hireling:** a permanent extra card each turn is strong in a slog, and it only uses a slot once. It pairs well with Outpost.
- **Bridge:** take one, late, for the Duchy-plus-Estate endgame. Hermit with a Bridge in play can gain $4 cards.
- **Outpost:** an extra turn on 3 cards (4 with Hireling). Its 3-card turn is the one place Diplomat becomes a village. It's playable late but not a priority.
- **Diplomat:** its reaction (draw 2, discard 3 when attacked) gives a little sifting against Old Witch. Otherwise it's a weak terminal draw card.
- **Caravan:** harmless non-terminal filler.
- **Vassal:** none. It's weak, and a random Old Witch it turns over doesn't save it.

**Plans considered:**
1. **Old Witch plus heavy trashing (Hermit, Sewers, Treasure Trove, Hireling).**
   - How it works: Ferry/Old Witch opening. A second Old Witch through Hermit or a $3 buy. Sewers by about turn 5 or 6, then Treasure Trove/Gold, Hireling, and Bridge late.
   - Line: Old Witch plays on about 40% of turns with one copy and over half with two. First $6 around turn 5–7, first $8 around turn 8–10.
   - Needs: Ferry before your first Old Witch, and Sewers before the Curses pile up.
   - Weakness: two Old Witches plus Hermits collide when no Madman is around.
2. **Big Money with Old Witch, no Ferry (5/2 opening).**
   - It's the baseline, but strictly worse than 1. You get one Old Witch on the same schedule, with no discount and no Curse removal.
3. **Hermit/Madman deck with Diplomat or Bridge.**
   - Madmen give one-time Actions, and Bridge gives Buys.
   - Weakness: nothing renews the Actions, and every non-buying turn spent making a Madman costs a gain. It stalls badly under Curses.

**Recommendation:** Plan 1. Old Witch decides this board, and Ferry makes it cheap enough to get and to repeat. Sewers and Hermit keep your deck clean while the opponent's isn't. If the opponent skips Ferry, you win the Curse split. In a mirror, Sewers and Hermit decide who carries fewer Curses.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Mirror (both play Plan 1):** Curses split about 5–5. The edge goes to whoever gets Sewers and Hermit first and plays Old Witch more often, so a cheap second Old Witch matters.
- **Against an Old Witch money deck without Ferry:** you play more Old Witches and clean up faster. You should get 6+ of the 10 Curses into their deck.
- **If I'd picked Plan 2:** it loses both of these matchups.

**Open:**
- **4/3 (pick this; the tournament rule lets you):** Ferry, then Old Witch. Ferry on the $3 turn and Old Witch at $3 on the $4 turn, or the other way round.
- **5/2:** Old Witch and nothing. It's dominated by 4/3, because it adds no discount, so take it only if forced. Then buy Ferry early so later Old Witches are cheap.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffles 2–3:**
  - $3: Sewers, then Hermit, which then gains you Old Witch #2.
  - $5: Treasure Trove.
  - $6–7: Hireling first, then Gold or Treasure Trove.
  - Get one Bridge around turn 8–10.
  - Stop at 2 Old Witches. A third only if Madmen are giving you spare Actions.
  - Once your Old Witch buys are done, buying Ferry again to move the token onto Hireling ($4) or Bridge ($2) is a minor option.
- **Greening:**
  - Provinces at $8 from about turn 9–10.
  - Duchies at $5–7 once about 5 Provinces are left. A slog runs long, around turns 16–20.
  - With Bridge, take Duchy plus Estate.

**Ending:** Curses run out first, by about turn 10–12. After that it's a Province and Duchy race. A three-pile ending (Curses, Hermit, Old Witch) is possible only if both players drain Hermits by gaining Hermits. Watch the Hermit pile.

**Watch out for:**
- Terminal collisions. Stop at about 3 terminals, counting Hireling only once.
- Buying Sewers late. Every turn without it is a turn of Curses you can't clear efficiently.
- An opponent who also Ferries Old Witch. Then your plays per turn and your trashing decide the Curse split, so prioritise a Hermit that gains Old Witch.

**Challenged:**
- **Re-testing Plan 3 with every fix:** Madman can open a turn: from 5 cards it gives +2 Actions and +4 Cards, which lets you play Old Witch plus Diplomat or Bridge. But each Madman costs a non-buying turn and is used once. Diplomat only becomes a village on 3-card Outpost turns. You can't get more than about two big turns out of it, and that loses to steady Curses.
- **How the pick loses:** the opponent wins the Curse split. Likely causes are an unlucky Old Witch draw or delaying Sewers. Their Sewers then turns each of your attacks into extra trashing for them.

**Least sure of:** how much Outpost and Hireling are worth. An Outpost/Hireling deck playing Old Witch on extra turns might outpace the Treasure Trove line. You'd see it if Outpost turns regularly play an Old Witch on a 4-card hand.

**Assumed:**
- I used the tournament's Hermit wording (Hermit is trashed for a Madman, so trash triggers fire). It's unclear whether your hand is still there when Hermit is trashed during Clean-up. That decides whether Sewers gets a third trash.
- Ferry's discount applies to Hermit's "costing up to $3" gain on your turn.
- An opponent's Rogue making you trash a card counts as you trashing it, so your Sewers triggers.
