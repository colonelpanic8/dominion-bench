## Beyond the key
- **Ferry on Old Witch makes it cost $3 on your turns, so 4/3 can open Ferry then Old Witch:** plausible. Ferry gives −$2 on your turns, and Ferry has to come before Old Witch. The key doesn't discuss Old Witch, and the grader notes invite credit for it as the board's junker.
- **Hermit can gain a Ferried Old Witch:** plausible. Old Witch costs $3 on your turn, and Hermit gains a card costing up to $3.
- **Old Witch's Curse trash triggers the victim's Sewers, which trashes a second card:** plausible. Sewers fires on any trash not caused by Sewers, including on another player's turn.
- **An opponent's Rogue trashing your card triggers your Sewers:** plausible. Under Rogue's text you are the one who trashes the card.
- **Diplomat needs two non-drawing cards played before it:** wrong. From 5 cards, one card played first leaves 4. Diplomat then leaves 3, and drawing 2 makes 5, which qualifies.
- **Diplomat's reaction gives some sifting against Old Witch:** plausible. It reacts from 5+ cards. But it misses that the opponent's Attacks also leave Diplomat able to act as a village, which the grader notes call out.
- **With Bridge in play, Hermit can gain $4 cards:** plausible by card text, since Bridge cuts costs everywhere. Both are terminal, though, so it needs a Madman.
- **Hireling makes Outpost turns 4 cards:** plausible. Hireling's +1 Card applies at the start of every turn, the extra turn included.
- **Curses run out by turns 10–12 in the mirror:** plausible with two cheap Old Witches each, but unverified.
- **Madman from a 5-card hand gives +2 Actions and +4 Cards:** plausible. Madman leaves 4 cards in hand when played.

## Summary
The analysis gets the Hermit/Sewers trashing and the Rogue recycling, and adds a credible Ferry/Old Witch point the key doesn't cover. But it dismisses the two cards Horton's plan centres on, Bridge as the payload and Diplomat as the village, and treats Outpost as an afterthought. What's left is roughly Horton's third option, a Treasure Trove money deck, wrapped around Old Witch.
