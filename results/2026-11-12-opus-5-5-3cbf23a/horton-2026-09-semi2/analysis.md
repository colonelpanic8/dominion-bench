**Board:** This is a no-attack engine board. Importer, Enlarge, Transmogrify and Souk's on-gain trashing thin the deck fast. Ronin draws, Village Green, Squire and Coin of the Realm (CotR) give the Actions, and the payload is Souk's +$ and +Buy plus Animal Fairs you get free by trashing an Action. Nothing junks or attacks, so an engine has nothing to fear and can build undisturbed. The open question was whether a draw-to-7 card and a "minus $1 per card in hand" payload work together. They do, because nearly every card here shrinks your hand when played.

**Engine:**
- **Pieces:**
  - Draw: Ronin, 2–3 copies.
  - Actions: Village Green (3+), Squire (+2 Actions mode) and CotR.
  - Thinning: Souk's on-gain trash, Enlarge, Transmogrify.
  - +Buy and payload: Souk (2–3) and Animal Fair.
  - Builders: Importer, plus Armory if you want it.
- **Speed:** with an Enlarge or Importer opening, the deck is close to drawing itself by turn 8–9, and the first turn worth two Provinces comes around turn 9–11.
- **Reliability:** high.
  - Ronin is a Shadow, so it can be played from the deck whenever a copy sits there.
  - Tireless puts every Souk on top of your deck, so the first Ronin or Village Green you play finds them.
  - Village Green's "next turn" mode gives Actions at the start of the turn.
  - The main stall risk is Treasure left in hand, which clogs both Ronin and Souk.
- **Built turn:** example deck of 3 Village Green, 2 Squire, 3 Ronin, 3 Souk, 2 Animal Fair and Importer, with little Treasure.
  - Souks played last, with the hand nearly empty: $5 + $6 + $7.
  - Animal Fairs: $8. Squires: $2.
  - Total about $26–28 with 4–5 Buys. That is 3 Provinces, or 2 Provinces and Duchies.

**Key interactions:**
- **Importer → Souk:** Importer gains Souk at the start of your turn, when your hand is still a full 5 cards, so Souk's trigger trashes 2 Estates or Coppers. That is a $5 card plus trashing 2, for $3, every time Importer is played, from turn 3–4.
- **Trappers' Lodge on turn 1:** paying a Favor to topdeck the turn-1 buy puts it into your turn-2 hand.
  - With the tournament rule you choose the order of the rest of the deck too.
  - Example: Enlarge topdecked turn 1 gives a turn-2 hand of Enlarge + 2 Coppers + 2 Estates. Enlarge turns an Estate into a $4 card on turn 2 and another on turn 3.
  - You have only 5 Favors (1 starting + 4 from Importer's setup), so spend them on opening gains and key early parts.
- **Ronin + Tireless Souk:**
  - Souks always end up on top of your deck.
  - Ronin played from the deck at the start of a turn (it needs an Action) draws exactly those Souks.
  - Every card you then play shrinks your hand, so the next Ronin refills it and the last Souks resolve with 0–2 cards in hand, for $5–7 each.
- **Animal Fair for free:**
  - Keep a spare Squire (or a used-up Importer or Armory) unplayed and trash it to buy Animal Fair, a $7 card that gives +$4, using one of Souk's Buys.
  - Enlarge can also turn Souk into Animal Fair.
  - This converts builders into payload late in the build.
- **Transmogrify:** call it at the start of the turn.
  - Estate becomes Importer or Silver in hand.
  - Copper becomes Squire in hand.
  - A $4 card you no longer need becomes Souk in hand, and that Souk then trashes 2 more cards.
  - Its play is +1 Action, so it never takes a terminal slot.

**Other cards:**
- **Armory:** its best partner is Village Green or Importer, gained onto your deck. It is terminal and slower than Importer, so treat it as optional and later as Animal Fair fodder.
- **CotR:** acts as a village through the Tavern mat, but it sits in hand during your Action phase. That costs $1 on each Souk and a slot under Ronin's 7, so Squire is the better village at $2.
- **Village Green's Reaction:** dead text, because nothing here discards outside Clean-up.
- **Squire's on-trash ability:** dead text, because the board has no Attack cards.
- **Animal Fair's +Buy per empty pile:** a late bonus at most.

**Plans considered:**
- **Thin Souk/Ronin/Animal Fair engine (pick):**
  - Line: thinned by turn 5–6, $8+ with 2 Buys from about turn 7, double Provinces from turn 9–11.
  - Needs: 2–3 Souk, 2–3 Ronin and 3+ Village Green by about turn 8. No pile is scarce.
  - Weakness: Treasure clogging your hand if you under-thin.
- **Ronin-Big Money with Importer (baseline):**
  - Ronin from the deck is Smithy-like draw every turn (+2 cards) and never collides.
  - Line: $6 around turn 4–5, $8 around turn 6–7, 4 Provinces around turn 13–14.
  - Weakness: no +Buy scaling, and Tireless Souk is weak in a money deck.
- **Animal Fair money (Squire coupons into +$4 terminals):** Animal Fairs are terminal and collide without villages, so this is a worse version of the engine.

**Recommendation:** Build the engine. With no attacks it can't be slowed, the thinning is cheap and fast, and Souk's +Buy plus Animal Fair lift a built turn well past one Province per turn. It overtakes Ronin-money's one Province per turn by about turn 11 and keeps pulling ahead.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Against Ronin-money:** the engine is behind by about 2 Provinces at turn 9–10. It then takes 2–3 Provinces a turn and catches up by turn 11–12.
- **In the mirror:** both players build the same thing. Thinning tempo and the opening Favor use decide it. Count Buys late and watch for three piles.

**Open:**
- **5/2 (preferred):** Enlarge (topdecked with a Favor) + Squire. Arrange the turn-2 hand as Enlarge, 2 Coppers and 2 Estates. Enlarge each Estate into Importer or Transmogrify.
- **4/3:** Importer (topdecked turn 1 off the 3 Coppers + 2 Estates hand) + Transmogrify. Importer gains a Souk on turn 3, trashing 2 cards.

**Buy priorities:**
1. Importer at $3–4, Transmogrify at $4, and Souk or Enlarge at $5. Trash Estates, then Coppers.
2. From the second shuffle: Ronin ×2–3, Village Green ×3+, Squires for Actions and as Animal Fair coupons.
3. Once you draw most of the deck: Souk ×3, then trash spare Squires or Importers into Animal Fairs.
4. Skip Gold.
5. Green once a turn reliably makes $16 with 3 Buys, around turn 9–11, and then green fast.

**Ending:** Provinces are the most likely end. In the mirror, Souk, Squire, Importer and Animal Fair can drain, and each empty pile gives Animal Fairs another Buy, so three-piling is possible. Track it.

**Watch out for:**
1. **Copper left in hand:** each one costs every Souk $1 and Ronin a slot. Keep thinning Copper until the deck draws itself.
2. **Too few Actions:** every terminal (Ronin, Souk, Animal Fair, Importer, Enlarge) needs one. Count them before adding payload.
3. **Wasting Favors:** you only have 5, so save them for the opening and key early gains.

**Challenged:**
- **Ronin-money re-tested:** even optimised with Importer gaining Souk (as a +Buy Silver-like card) and Animal Fair via Squire, it stays at about one Province a turn and gets no attack to slow the engine. It loses unless the engine stalls badly.
- **How the pick loses:** you under-thin, so Coppers sit in hand and cut both Souk and Ronin. You over-build past turn 13. Or you lose a mirror three-pile race.

**Least sure of:** whether Souk really pays $5–7 once Ronin has refilled the hand. That depends on playing the Souks last and having trashed most Treasure. Simulating a built turn with 3 Coppers still in the deck would show how much the payload drops.

**Assumed:**
- Importer gives no Favors beyond its setup +4, per the card text shown, so each player has 5 Favors in total.
- The tournament rule lets you set the full order of your starting deck.
- Tireless Souks go on top of the deck after Clean-up has drawn, so they are not in your next 5-card hand but are the first cards your draw reaches next turn.
- Ronin's Shadow ability lets you play it from the draw pile whenever a copy is there and you have an Action.
