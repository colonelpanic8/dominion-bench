## Beyond the key
- Importer gains Souk at the start of turn with a full 5-card hand, so Souk's on-gain effect trashes 2 cards: plausible. Importer's gain happens at the start of turn, after the hand is drawn, and Souk trashes "from your hand".
- Trappers' Lodge topdecks the turn-1 buy into the turn-2 hand, and there are 5 Favors in total (1 starting + 4 from Importer): plausible. The gain onto the deck happens before Clean-up draws, and Importer's text gives no further Favors.
- Transmogrify turns Copper into Squire: wrong. Copper costs $0 and Transmogrify gains a card costing up to $1 more, while Squire costs $2. Estate → Importer, Silver or Coin of the Realm and $4 → Souk are correct.
- Village Green's Reaction is dead text: plausible. No card here discards outside Clean-up, and Tireless discards from play during Clean-up.
- Squire's on-trash ability is dead text: plausible. The kingdom has no Attack cards.
- You can trash a spare Squire, Importer or Armory to get Animal Fair free, and Enlarge can turn Souk ($5) into Animal Fair ($7): plausible. Both follow from the card text.
- Coin of the Realm in hand costs $1 on each Souk and a slot under Ronin's 7: plausible. As a Treasure it sits in hand through the Action phase. The analysis then undervalues the Coin of the Realm Horton aims for, because once on the Tavern mat it no longer takes hand space.
- Played from the deck, Ronin acts as +2 Cards every turn in a money deck and never collides: plausible, but only while a Ronin is in the draw pile, so "every turn" overstates it.
- A built turn makes $26–28, with Souks paying $5/$6/$7 when played last: plausible. It needs ideal sequencing and almost no Treasure, which the analysis flags itself.

## Summary
The analysis gets the Ronin core and the subtle Tireless timing right, and it handles the Souk-versus-hand-size tension well, which the key's grader note credits as an extra. It misses Horton's Armory → Transmogrify → Coin of the Realm opening in favour of Enlarge/Squire. It also prioritises Souk over Ronin at $5 early, which is the key's T2 trap. One Transmogrify claim (Copper → Squire) is illegal.
