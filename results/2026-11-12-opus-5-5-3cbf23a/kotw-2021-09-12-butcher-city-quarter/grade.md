## Beyond the key
- Opening odds 6/3 48%, 5/4 40%, 7/2 12% with Cursed Gold replacing a Copper: plausible. Enumerating 5-card splits of 6 Copper, a $3 Cursed Gold and 3 Estates gives 47.6% / 40.5% / 11.9%.
- Butcher on a Gold plus its own 2 Coffers gains a Province with no Buy: plausible. Gold $6 + 2 Coffers = up to $8.
- Butcher on Cursed Gold ($4) gains a Gold with 2 Coffers, or a Province with 4: plausible. $1 more per Coffers spent.
- Butcher on an Estate with 2 Coffers gains a Nomad Camp onto the deck: plausible. $2 + 2 = $4, and Nomad Camp is gained onto the deck.
- Ritual on a Gold gives +6 VP and a Curse that Tower refunds once the Curse pile is empty: plausible. Curse is a non-Victory card from a Supply pile.
- Ritual does nothing once Curses run out, so late Ritual is a race for the last Curses: plausible. Its trash-and-score is conditional on "if you do" gain a Curse.
- Ritual's Curse gain adds a Merchant Guild Coffers: plausible. Events are bought in the Buy phase, so it is a card gained in it.
- Fool's Gold also reacts to Provinces gained through Butcher: plausible. Its trigger is any Province gain by another player.
- Night Watchman can be played the turn it is bought: plausible. It is gained to hand in the Buy phase, before Night.
- Night Watchman's discards trigger Tunnel and set Faithful Hound aside for the next hand: plausible. Both trigger on discards outside Clean-up.
- Pooka can't trash Cursed Gold: plausible. Its text excludes Cursed Gold.
- Curses, Tunnels and Fool's Gold as a possible three-pile ending: doubtful. Curses empty is likely, but nothing in its plan buys out 8 Tunnels plus Fool's Gold.
- 1 Tunnel in a 13-card deck is found by a turn-3–4 Embassy about 67% of the time: doubtful as a figure (Embassy sees about 9 of the other 12 cards, closer to 75%), but the direction is right.

## Summary
The analysis finds the key's payload exactly (Tunnel Golds from Embassy and Night Watchman, Butchered into Provinces), but recommends building it as a money deck with City Quarter only as a late add-on, the line the thread's money advocates took rather than the playtested engine.
