**Board:** This is a Gold-flow board, and it wants the Gold cashed in for points. Tunnel turns any discard outside Clean-up into a Gold. Embassy discards 3 on every play and Night Watchman discards from the top of your deck, so both trigger it. Butcher turns a Gold into a Province without using a Buy, and late Rituals turn a Gold into 6 VP. The starting Cursed Gold (one Copper replaced, total $9) puts a Curse in both decks each time it is played. That drains the 10-card Curse pile, which matters for two reasons. Once the pile is empty, Tower refunds every Curse (+1 VP each). And Ritual stops working, because it only trashes and scores "if you do" gain a Curse. The open question is whether City Quarter, the only village, is worth its 8 Debt and needs Action-dense hands. I think it is worth adding late, but not worth building around.

**Engine:** The pieces are City Quarter for Actions and draw, and Embassy, Pooka and Faithful Hound for draw. All of the draw is terminal. Pooka and Butcher thin, and Merchant Guild and Nomad Camp give +Buy. Fool's Gold stacks as payload ($1, then $4 per copy), and Butcher, Merchant Guild Coffers and Ritual add more. Every part is here. The catch is cost: a deck that draws itself needs about 3 City Quarters (24 Debt) for 4–5 terminals. Butcher's Coffers can pay that Debt. With the parts at $5 or Debt, I estimate it first draws its deck around turn 11–12, a turn or two behind the usual turn 8–11, with about $13–16 and 2–3 Buys on a built turn. City Quarter only draws for Action cards in hand. Night Watchman can stack the next hand to fix that, but this remains the shakiest part.

**Key interactions:**
- **Embassy + Tunnel:**
  - Embassy shows about 9 cards, and every Tunnel among them can be discarded for a Gold.
  - With 1 Tunnel in a 13-card deck, a turn 3–4 Embassy finds it about 67% of the time. With 2–3 Tunnels, each Embassy play averages about 1–1.5 Golds.
  - This works from turn 3–4. Tunnels are also 2 VP each.
- **Butcher + Gold:**
  - Butcher gives +2 Coffers. Trashing a Gold ($6) and spending those 2 Coffers gains a Province ($8), so the Coffers come out even and no Buy is used.
  - The same turn's Buy can take a second Province. From about turn 7–8, every collision of Butcher and Gold is a Province.
  - Other Butcher targets: Cursed Gold plus 2 Coffers gains a Gold (removes the source of your Curses). Cursed Gold plus all 4 Coffers from two Butchers gains a Province. Estate plus 2 Coffers gains a Nomad Camp on top of your deck.
- **Night Watchman + Tunnel / Faithful Hound:**
  - Night Watchman needs no Action and is gained to hand, so you can play it the night you buy it.
  - Of the top 5 cards, Tunnels become Golds and Hounds go to your hand at end of turn. Junk can be discarded so the next hand is cleaner.
  - This is a second Tunnel trigger that doesn't take a terminal slot.
- **Ritual + Gold/$5 cards + Tower:**
  - For $4 and a Buy, Ritual trashes a Gold for +6 VP, minus 1 for the Curse it adds, which Tower refunds if the pile empties.
  - That beats a Duchy late. Ritual only works while Curses remain, so it is a race for the last Curses.
- **Merchant Guild + Ritual:** The Curse Ritual gains counts as a gain, so it adds +1 Coffers.
- **Fool's Gold reaction:** Each time the opponent gains a Province, by buying it or through Butcher, you can trash a Fool's Gold to put a Gold on top of your deck. That strongly punishes a money opponent.

**Other cards:**
- **Pooka:** Its best partner is Fool's Gold as cheap fuel. Played alone it is a Smithy that trashes a Copper (Pooka–Big Money), and it collides with Embassy. It is rated −2.2 because Cursed Gold can't feed it. That holds here.
- **Nomad Camp (1st ed.):** Pairs with Butcher (an Estate becomes a Nomad Camp on top of your deck). Use it as +Buy.
- **Merchant Guild:** Ritual and buys of $2 Fool's Golds are the gains that feed its Coffers.
- **Faithful Hound:** Pairs with Embassy and Night Watchman, which return it to hand. It is still a terminal, so it is only marginal.
- **City Quarter:** Embassy and Butcher fill hands with Actions for it to count, and Butcher's Coffers pay its Debt. It is rated 3.7, and I rate it lower here because only an 8-Debt village is available.
- **Tower:** Its partner is the Curse pile. Cursed Gold and Ritual drain it, and Tower then makes Curses worth 0 net.
- **Embassy (1st ed., rated −1.6):** Worth much more here because of Tunnel. Each one you gain gives the opponent a Silver, which costs you little.

**Plans considered:**
- **A. Gold flow, cashed by Butcher (recommended):**
  - How it works: Embassy ×2 + Tunnel ×3–4 make Golds, and Night Watchman adds triggers. Butcher ×2 turns Golds into Provinces, Merchant Guild or Nomad Camp adds a Buy, and Ritual takes over the endgame.
  - Line: Gold by turn 3–4, $8 hands from about turn 6. The first Province comes around turn 7–9, then often 2 a turn (one from Butcher plus one bought), for about 4 Provinces by turn 12–13 plus Tunnel VP.
  - Needs: Tunnels. There are only 8 of them and a mirror opponent wants them too.
  - Weakness: two terminal types collide, and you hand the opponent Silvers.
- **B. City Quarter engine:**
  - How it works: City Quarter ×3 with Embassy, Pooka and Butcher, and Fool's Gold and Merchant Guild as payload.
  - Line: first draws its deck around turn 11–12, then takes 2–3 Provinces a turn, or Province + Ritual.
  - Weakness: 24 Debt, and City Quarter draws poorly from Copper-heavy hands.
- **C. Big Money baseline: Pooka–Big Money or Embassy–Big Money:**
  - Line: $6 by turn 3–5, $8 by turn 5–7, 4 Provinces around turn 14–15.
  - Plan A is the same deck with Tunnel and Butcher added on top, so it beats this.

**Recommendation:** Plan A, adding 1–2 City Quarters in the midgame once you hold 4 or more terminals, paid for with Butcher's Coffers. It scores from about turn 8 and turns Golds into Provinces without using Buys. It stays reliable, because a hand that misses the combo is still a money hand with Golds in it. Against a slow engine it can green first and then cash Golds through Ritual to close out.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Against the City Quarter engine:** A is ahead on tempo. You hold 3–4 Provinces before they draw their deck, and late Rituals take the last Curses before they can use them.
- **Against an A mirror:** It turns into a race for Tunnels and Butcher. A couple of Fool's Golds punish their Provinces.
- **If the opponent plays money with Fool's Gold:** Each of your Provinces hands them a Gold. Even so, Butcher's Provinces come faster than their Golds can be used.
- **If I went B:** Opponent's A would lead by 2–3 Provinces when my engine came online. I would need 3-Province turns to catch up, so B loses that matchup.

**Open:**
- **6/3 (48%; the $6 needs Cursed Gold played, which gains a Curse):** Embassy / Tunnel.
- **5/4 (40%):** Embassy / Tunnel. Alternatively Butcher / Tunnel. The Butcher trashes Cursed Gold for a Gold or an Estate for a Nomad Camp.
- **7/2 (12%):** Embassy / Fool's Gold. The Fool's Gold is a reaction to their Provinces and fuel for a Pooka. Buying nothing at $2 is also fine.
- Play Cursed Gold whenever its $3 reaches a meaningful price. Its Curse will probably be refunded through Tower.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffles 1–2:** Butcher at $5 (first one), a second Embassy, Tunnels at $3–4 up to 3–4, Gold at $6–7. Take Night Watchman at $3 once you hold 2 or more Tunnels.
- **Shuffle 3:** Merchant Guild or Nomad Camp for +Buy, a second Butcher, and City Quarter on a weak hand once you have 4 or more terminals.
- **Greening:** Take Provinces at $8 from about turn 7. Butcher every Gold into a Province when they collide. Once 4–5 Provinces are left, Ritual a Gold or a spare $5 card at $4 instead of buying a Duchy, while Curses remain.

**Ending:**
- Most likely the Provinces run out, around turn 13–15.
- Curses will probably empty first, through Cursed Gold plays and Rituals. That turns Ritual off and counts as one pile.
- In a mirror, Tunnels (8 cards) could be the second empty pile. With +Buy a three-pile ending (Curses, Tunnels, Fool's Gold) is possible, so count those piles.

**Watch out for:**
1. The opponent buying Fool's Gold. Expect a Gold on their deck for each of your Province gains, and close the game faster.
2. Curses running out before you can use Ritual. Track the pile.
3. Too many terminals with no City Quarter. Add a City Quarter, or prefer Tunnels and Night Watchman, which use no Action.

**Challenged:** I re-tested Plan B with every fix the board offers. Night Watchman stacking an Action-heavy next hand makes City Quarter draw. Butcher's Coffers pay the Debt. Fool's Gold makes $13+ cheaply, and Ritual scores on spare Buys. Even so, it probably still loses a straight race to A started on the same turn, because of the 24 Debt and the fact that every draw piece is terminal. My pick loses when Butcher misses its Gold, Embassy misses its Tunnels, and A turns into slow money that hands out Silvers. Against a Fool's Gold opponent, my Provinces also feed them Golds.

**Least sure of:** Whether the City Quarter engine actually beats Plan A. Its ceiling (City Quarter + Fool's Gold + Butcher) is higher than my rough count of its build gives it credit for. One way to settle it: write out a full City Quarter build turn by turn and check whether it is taking 2–3 Provinces a turn by turn 10.

**Assumed:**
- Card text as given in the kingdom file. Embassy and Nomad Camp are first-edition only.

- Ritual does nothing once the Curse pile is empty.
