**Kingdom analysis: Barge, Cardinal, Courtier, Displace, Lurker, Mandarin\*, Silk Road\*, Steward, Tunnel, Village Green; Gamble, Way of the Goat**
(\*first edition only. I assumed 2 players, no Colonies and no Shelters, since the file says these are unknown.)

**Board:** Courtier is the centre of this board. Village Green has three types (Action, Duration, Reaction), so revealing it to Courtier gives three choices: +1 Action, +$3 and a Gold. Barge and Cardinal each have two types, so revealing one gives +1 Action and +$3. Several Courtiers can reveal the same card while it stays in hand. The draw is thin: Barge gives +3, Steward +2 and Village Green +1. Thinning is decent: Steward, Way of the Goat on any Action, and Displace. +Buy is plentiful from Barge, Courtier and Gamble. The open question was whether Barge alone draws enough to support an engine. It does, because Courtier makes the payload non-terminal and gains Golds along the way.

**Engine:**
- **Pieces:**
  - Village Green ($4): the village, and the card Courtiers reveal.
  - Barge ($5): the draw, plus +Buy. Use the "next turn" mode for a strong start to the following turn.
  - Courtier ($5): the payload. It is non-terminal whenever Village Green, Barge or Cardinal is in hand.
  - Steward ($3): thinning early, then +2 Cards.
  - Gamble: spare +Buy.
  - Displace: converts Golds into Provinces late.
- **Worked line:**
  - T1–2: Steward/Cardinal.
  - T3–4: Steward trashes 2 cards; buy Village Green or Courtier.
  - T5–8: add about 2 Barges, 2 Courtiers and 2 Village Greens. Each Courtier played with Village Green in hand now gains a Gold as well as giving $3.
  - T9–10: an 18–20 card deck with 3 Barges, 2–3 Village Greens and 3 Courtiers draws most of itself.
  - First double-Province turn around T10–11, which is normal engine speed.
- **Reliability:** the risk is having no Village Green or Barge in hand. Barge's "next turn" mode, and Mandarin putting Village Green back on the deck, both cover this.
- **Built turn:** 3 Courtiers revealing Village Green give $9, +3 Actions and Buys or Golds. Add 2–3 Golds and Steward's $2 and you have $16–20 with 3–4 Buys. That is 2 Provinces plus a Duchy or Tunnel, plus a Province from Displace on a Gold.

**Key interactions:**
- **Courtier + Village Green:** each Courtier gives +1 Action, +$3 and a Gold (switch to +Buy once greening). Play all the Courtiers before Village Green leaves your hand. From about T5, roughly one Gold per Courtier play.
- **Displace + Gold, with Exile reclaim:** Displace Exiles a Gold to gain a Province with no Buy. The next time you gain a Gold (from Courtier or Tunnel), you may discard all your Exiled Golds, which brings them back. In a Courtier deck that is about +1 Province per Displace play late in the game, at almost no lasting cost.
- **Courtier + Tunnel:** a Tunnel in hand is Victory–Reaction, so revealing it gives +$3 and a Gold. Tunnel is the only green card that still pays Courtier, and it is worth 2 VP.
- **Gamble + Tunnel or Village Green:** discarding from the deck triggers both Reactions. On Tunnel, $2 buys a Gold. On Village Green, it plays for +1 Card and +2 Actions next turn. Gamble can also play Barge in the Buy phase for next turn. In a Gold-heavy deck, each Gamble is about +$1 and neutral on Buys.
- **Mandarin:** it puts Village Green or Tunnel back on top. With Gamble that gives a Gold; otherwise it sets up the next turn's Courtiers. Buying Mandarin puts your played Treasures back on your deck, setting up a Province hand for next turn.
- **Way of the Goat:** any Action can trash a card instead. Lurker becomes a $2 one-card trasher, and an early Courtier or Barge with a weak hand can trash an Estate.

**Other cards:**
- **Cardinal:** a two-type reveal for Courtier, and +$2. Its attack is mixed. It Exiles the opponent's Silver, Steward or Gold, but its forced discard triggers their Tunnel (a Gold) and Village Green.
- **Lurker:** best as the Goat trasher. It can also trash Village Greens from the Supply to deny them or to push a three-pile ending.
- **Silk Road:** a late-game alternative to Duchy. With Estates, Provinces, Duchies, Tunnels and itself, it is often worth 3 VP for $4.
- **Steward:** the opener; later +2 Cards.
- **Ratings check:** Courtier (−0.3) is much stronger here than its rating, because this board gives it many multi-type cards to reveal. Mandarin's −3.2 is a first-edition rating and fair outside these loops.

**Plans considered:**
1. **Village Green / Barge / Courtier engine with Golds and Displace.** Line as above: double Provinces from T10–11, with no attack. Needs about 3 Courtiers, 3 Barges and 2–3 Village Greens. The opponent will contest Courtier and Barge, but there are 10 of each. Weakness: draw rests on Barge, and a hand without Village Green or Barge stalls the Courtiers.
2. **Barge-BM, or Courtier/Tunnel BM.** About $6 by T3–4, $8 from around T6. Roughly 4 Provinces by T15–16. Reliable, but each turn takes one card.
3. **Gold-flood money.** Courtier plus Tunnels, with Displace turning Gold into Province. Faster than BM, but terminal collisions cap it and it shares parts with plan 1.

**Recommendation:** the Courtier engine. It holds up because:
- Its payload is non-terminal, so it doesn't collide.
- Even when it stalls, it gains Golds.
- Its multi-Buy finish outpaces money's one Province per turn from about T11.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Against money:** you are about one shuffle behind early and well ahead after T11.
- **Against the same engine:** split the Courtiers and Barges evenly, take Village Greens early, and start greening first. Displace turns are free Provinces in the mirror.
- **Cardinal:** it can Exile your Barge or Courtier. Any gain of that card brings the Exiled copy back, so this is tempo damage only.

**Open:**
- **4/3:** Cardinal/Steward. Village Green/Steward is an alternative.
- **5/2:** Courtier/Lurker (play Lurker as the Goat to trash). Displace/Lurker is close: Displace turns an Estate into a Village Green or Cardinal.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffles 1–2:** Steward trashes Estates and some Copper. Buy one Village Green, then Courtier or Barge at $5. At $6–7, prefer Courtier to Gold, because Courtier makes the Golds.
- **Shuffle 3:** Barges and Courtiers to about 3 each, a 2nd–3rd Village Green, and one Displace.
- **Greening:** start around T9–11, once a turn makes $13+ with 2+ Buys. Take Tunnel or Silk Road over Estate. Use Displace on Golds every turn.

**Ending:** most likely the Provinces run out, around T13–15. Village Green is the most likely Kingdom pile to empty. A three-pile ending would need Lurker to deliberately trash piles from the Supply, so it is unlikely.

**Watch out for:**
- A hand without Village Green or Barge. Keep one Village Green coming back via Mandarin or Barge's "next turn" mode.
- A Cardinal duel exiling Courtiers. Get the Courtiers back with any later gain of Courtier.
- Overbuilding Golds. Past about 4 Golds, take +Buy instead.

**Challenged:**
- **The money plan:** Barge-BM with Courtier, Tunnels and Displace really can reach Province plus Displace turns. But with only one Action, the Courtier and Barge collisions cap it near BM speed.
- **How the engine loses:** it opens with a weak 5/2, or never draws Village Green with its Courtiers. Then it plays like a clumsy BM while a Cardinal opponent Exiles its Silvers.

**Least sure of:** whether Barge-only draw is reliable enough to beat a Gold-flood money deck that adds a few Courtiers. To test it, play out the T6–T12 hands for the engine with 2 versus 3 Barges.

**Assumed:**
- Gamble's discard from the deck counts as "other than during Clean-up", so it triggers Tunnel and Village Green.
- Several Courtiers may each reveal the same card.
- Village Green played by Gamble in the Buy phase takes its "next turn" mode.
- Gaining a Gold may reclaim Exiled Golds.
