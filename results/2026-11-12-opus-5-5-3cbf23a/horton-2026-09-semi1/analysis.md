**Board:** This is a junking engine board, and the Panic Prophecy shapes it. Kitsune is the village, the junker and a source of $ all in one card. Silk Merchant and Council Room are the draw, and both give +Buy. Lurker trashing Silk Merchant from the Supply and later gaining it back is a steady source of Coffers and Villagers. Kitsune and Poet each remove a Sun token, and Panic starts once all 5 are gone. Kitsune's play will remove them in either deck, so expect Panic around turns 7–9. After that every Treasure you play gives +2 Buys and then goes back to its pile. Money decks fall apart, Copper thins itself out, and spare Buys plus Highway cost reduction become the payload. The open questions were how to thin before Panic (Transmute, or Panic itself) and how to make $ once Treasures stop lasting (Kitsune's +$2, Coffers, Highway).

**Engine:**
- **Pieces:**
  - Draw: Silk Merchant (each gain brings a Villager, so it pays for its own Action) and Council Room. Figurine is optional.
  - Actions: Kitsune (+2 Actions), Villagers, and Shop (it plays one other Action without using one).
  - Non-terminal helpers: Highway and Lurker.
  - +Buy: Silk Merchant, Council Room and Panic.
  - Thinning: Panic removing Treasures, plus an optional Transmute.
- **Timeline:** about 6–8 more gains after the opening. The deck should draw itself around turn 9–10.
- **Reliability:** good. Villagers bank between turns, and Council Room's +4 Cards gets through Curses.
- **Built turn (around turn 11):** roughly $12–15 and 8+ Buys. With 2 Highways in play, Province costs $6, Duchy $3, and Estate and Lurker $0. That is 2 Provinces plus free Estates.

**Key interactions:**
- **Lurker + Silk Merchant:** Lurker trashes a Silk Merchant from the Supply (+1 Coffers, +1 Villager). The next Lurker gains it back from the trash (+1 Coffers, +1 Villager, and you get the card). So each $2 non-terminal play is worth about $1 + 1 Action, from turn 3–4 on. The same two-step trick gains any $5 for free (trash a Highway or Kitsune, then gain it back), and it drains the Silk Merchant pile.
- **Panic + Highway:** after Panic, each Treasure played gives +2 Buys. With 2 Highways in play, Estates and Lurkers cost $0, so every spare Buy is a free point or a step toward emptying a pile. With 3 Highways, Silver costs $0, so spare Buys turn into Silvers worth $2 + 2 Buys next turn. From about turn 10.
- **Tools + engine Actions in play:** Tools is a non-terminal gain with no Buy needed. It can gain a Kitsune, Highway or Council Room, or a Silk Merchant (which also gives +1 Coffers, +1 Villager). After Panic, Tools can gain a copy of itself, which keeps it in the deck permanently and gives +2 Buys every play.
- **Reckless Transmute:** each play happens twice.
  - Trashing Curse + Copper gains a Transmute, which replaces the one that returns to its pile. So one Potion buys a trasher that keeps itself going.
  - Trashing Estate + Copper gives a Gold and a Transmute.
  - Late in the game, trashing 2 Silk Merchants gives 2 Duchies, 2 Coffers and 2 Villagers.
- **Kitsune options:** take +2 Actions + Curse while the Curses last, then +2 Actions + $2. Its Silver gains still make $2 + 2 Buys each after Panic.

**Other cards:**
- **Shop:** works with Council Room. Shop plays Council Room for free, adding +1 Card and +$1. A cheap fix for running short of Actions.
- **Figurine:** discards a Kitsune drawn dead in the Buy phase. It's decent draw before Panic but becomes one-shot after, so it ranks below Silk Merchant and Council Room.
- **Poet:** finds Lurker, Shop, Silver and junk, and removes a Sun token. Filler. Take it only if you want Panic sooner.
- **Prepare:** sets aside a hand of terminals, which then play next turn without using Actions (Prepare'd Treasures also get Panic's Buys). A niche rescue for a stalled hand.
- **Potion:** only for Transmute.

**Plans considered:**
- **Kitsune / Silk Merchant / Highway engine:**
  - How it works: Kitsunes junk the opponent and supply Actions, Silk Merchant and Council Room draw, and Lurker plus Tools build the deck without needing Buys.
  - Line: first $5 on turn 3–4 thanks to Coffers. Draws its deck around turn 9–10. Two or more Provinces a turn from about turn 10–11.
  - Needs: 3+ Kitsunes, 2–3 Highways, 2 Silk Merchants, 1 Council Room.
  - Weakness: Curses in the mirror.
- **Big Money + Council Room/Silk Merchant (baseline):**
  - Line: $6 on turn 3–5, 4 Provinces around turn 14–16 if nothing interferes.
  - Weakness: it gets cursed, and once the opponent's Kitsunes turn Panic on, every Gold is used once and gone. It falls apart after Panic.
- **Transmute Duchy/Gold line:**
  - How it works: open Potion and run Transmute as the main plan.
  - Weakness: too slow against Kitsune. It works better as a side buy.

**Recommendation:** The Kitsune/Silk Merchant/Highway engine. It is the only plan that does well after Panic, and Panic will happen. Its $ comes from cards that aren't Treasures (Kitsune's +$2, Coffers, Shop and Highway's cost reduction), so losing Treasures costs it little. Panic's thinning and Buys feed it. Add one Potion → Transmute midgame only if you are holding 3 or more Curses.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Against money:** the engine wins easily. You curse them, and your own Kitsunes switch Panic on and wipe out their Golds.
- **Engine mirror:** decided by the Kitsune split (Curses), the number of Highways, and control of the Silk Merchant pile. Use Lurker to trash Highways from the Supply if they are stacking them, then gain those Highways back yourself.

**Open:**
- **4/3:** Silk Merchant / Lurker. You get 1 Coffers right away and Lurker adds another on turn 3–4, so the first Kitsune is close to certain.
- **5/2:** Kitsune / Lurker.

**Buy priorities:**
- Kitsune up to 3–4 copies.
- Silk Merchant at $4, about 2 copies.
- Then Highway, Council Room ×1–2, Tools ×1–2, and a second Lurker. Shop if Actions are short.
- Spend Coffers on reaching $5s.
- Start greening when one turn makes $13+, around turn 10–11. Spare Buys go on free Estates once 2 Highways are in play.

**Ending:** Provinces most likely end it, around turns 13–15. A three-pile ending is a real threat: Curses, plus Silk Merchants (drained by Lurker), plus Estates or Lurkers bought for $0 with Highway and Panic's Buys. Copper, Silver, Potion and Transmute never empty because Panic or Reckless sends them back. Whoever leads controls the three-pile ending, so count Buys every turn after Panic.

**Watch out for:**
- **Panic too early:** if Panic arrives while your $ is still Copper, your economy stalls. Spend Kitsune's choice on +$2 or Silver early, and keep Coffers banked.
- **Losing the Kitsune split:** it means 6+ Curses for you. That is when to buy a Potion → Transmute to trash Curse + Copper.
- **Silk Merchant pile:** it runs out fast in a mirror. Get to 2 early.

**Challenged:** I looked again at money played with Prepare and Figurine, getting as many Golds as possible before Panic and then cashing them in for one huge Panic-Buy turn. It gets one or two big turns and then has nothing left, while the engine keeps producing. The engine loses if it falls behind on Curses and the opponent goes for three piles (Curses, Silk Merchant, Estates) before turn 12.

**Least sure of:** how much Reckless Transmute is worth. Its rating is −6.8, and I treat it as a good optional trasher on this board. The test: if games with one Potion buy midgame end with fewer Curses and a deck that draws itself sooner, it belongs in the core plan.

**Assumed:**
- Lurker trashing Silk Merchant from the Supply triggers its when-trashed bonus for the Lurker player.
- Reckless doubles the whole Transmute effect, so it trashes two cards and makes two gains.
- Tools can gain a copy of itself while it is in play.
- Highway lowers only the $ part of a cost, not Potion.
- Transmute does not count as "costs $3 or less" for Poet.
- Coffers have to be spent before buying.
- Panic needs 5 Sun tokens removed in a 2-player game.
