## Beyond the key
- Lurker trashing a Silk Merchant from the Supply gives the Lurker player +1 Coffers and +1 Villager, and a second Lurker gains it back for another: plausible. Silk Merchant triggers "When you gain or trash this", whatever the trash came from.
- With 2 Highways in play, Estates and Lurkers cost $0 and Province $6, so Panic's spare Buys become free Estates: plausible. Two Highways take $2 off, and each Treasure played under Panic gives +2 Buys.
- After Panic, Tools gaining a copy of itself keeps a Tools in the deck for good: plausible. The played Tools goes back to its pile, and the gained copy stays in the discard.
- Reckless Transmute on Curse + Copper replaces itself; on Estate + Copper it gains a Gold and a Transmute: plausible. Curse isn't an Action, Treasure or Victory card, so it only trashes.
- Shop plays Council Room for free: plausible. Shop plays an Action from hand with no copy in play.
- Figurine becomes one-shot after Panic: plausible. It's a Treasure, so it goes back to its pile.
- Prepare'd Treasures also get Panic's Buys: plausible. They're played on your next turn and Panic triggers on play.
- Highway doesn't reduce Transmute's Potion cost, and Transmute isn't "$3 or less" for Poet: plausible. Cost reduction only touches $, and a Potion cost isn't covered by a $ comparison.
- Copper, Silver, Potion and Transmute piles never empty because Panic or Reckless refills them: plausible for Treasures after Panic and for Transmute in play. Copies sitting in decks still count against the pile.
- Big Money falls apart after Panic: plausible. Each Gold is played once and goes back to its pile.

## Summary
The analysis reads the board's Panic dynamics well: Kitsune makes Panic inevitable, Treasures leave after one play, and Highway plus spare Buys threaten piles. It also names the central Transmute-versus-Panic thinning question. Where it falls short of the designer is weight. Horton built the board so Reckless Transmute has a lot going for it, but the analysis keeps it as an optional anti-Curse buy behind a Kitsune/Silk Merchant/Highway engine. It also never ties Lurker to Transmute for the endgame.
