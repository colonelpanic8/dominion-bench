**Board:** This board is built around Governor with Friendly. Governor is a non-terminal +3 Cards (or a Gold, or "trash a card and gain one costing exactly $2 more"). Friendly lets every Governor you leave unplayed in hand become another Governor at Clean-up. All the strong draw is non-terminal: Governor, Advisor, Experiment and Way of the Owl. +Buy comes from Forager (non-terminal) and Tactician, and Governor's remodel mode scores without a Buy by turning Gold into Province. The open questions are how to split the 10 Governors in a mirror, and when to hold a Governor instead of playing it.

**Engine:**
- **Draw:** Governor (+3, and the opponent gets +1), Advisor (about +2 non-terminal, but the opponent picks which card you lose), Experiment (a one-shot +2, and you gain two for one), Way of the Owl (draw up to 6 when your hand is nearly empty).
- **Actions:** Governor gives back its own Action. Merchant Camp is a village that can go back on top of your deck, so it turns up every turn. Throne Room on Governor is a net +1 Action. Only Flagship, Tactician, Leprechaun and plays as Owl use up Actions.
- **Thinning:** Forager. Governor's remodel also turns an Estate into a $4 card.
- **+Buy:** Forager and Tactician.
- **Payload:** Gold from Governor's Gold mode. Then Governor's remodel turns each Gold into a Province, and Throne Room or Flagship on a Governor does that twice.
- **Speed:** the deck draws itself around turns 9–10. Two- and three-Province turns start around turns 10–12, and the game ends around turns 13–14.
- **Reliability:** high. Nearly everything is non-terminal, so a turn rarely stalls.

**Key interactions:**
- **Governor + Friendly:** if you hold one spare Governor in hand, Clean-up turns it into another Governor. That is one free $5 card per turn, from turn 5–6 until the pile runs out. Take it on any turn where your other Governors already give you enough draw. It also takes Governors away from the opponent.
- **Governor remodel + Gold:** each play turns Gold ($6) into Province ($8) without a Buy. The opponent gets a weak version: Silver becomes a $4 card, Estate a $3 card, Gold becomes nothing.
  - Built turn: 2 Governors for draw, 3–4 Governors remodelling 3–4 Golds, plus a bought Province with Forager's Buy. That's 3–5 Provinces a turn.
- **Throne Room or Flagship + Governor:** doubles a Governor, giving +6 Cards, 2 Golds or 2 Provinces. Throne Room is net non-terminal on Governor. Flagship is terminal and gives +$2, so play it after a Merchant Camp or a Governor.
- **Anvil + Experiment:** discard a Copper to gain two Experiments, or a $4 piece. That's an extra build gain each turn without a Buy.
- **Owl + Experiment:** played as Owl, the Experiment doesn't go back to its pile, because the Way replaces its text. On a hand of 0–1 cards it draws 5–6.
- **Forager trashing Copper, then Anvil:** each different Treasure in the trash gives +$1 on every later Forager play.

**Other cards:**
- **Tactician:** +Buy and 5 cards at the start of next turn. But it empties your hand, which kills that turn's Friendly gain. A fallback +Buy only.
- **Leprechaun:** terminal Gold gain plus a Hex on yourself. You get a Wish instead only with exactly 7 cards in play. A Wish can gain a Governor to your hand, so it's playable in a controlled engine, but Governor's Gold mode does the same job without the Hex. Mostly skip it.
- **Advisor:** filler non-terminal draw that Anvil can gain.
- **Merchant Camp:** the village that comes back on top of your deck, for the turns you play Flagship, Tactician or Leprechaun.

**Ratings check:**
- Governor (5.5) and Forager (2.7) match my view.
- I rate these above their ratings on this board:
  - Merchant Camp (−2.4): it puts itself back on top, and Throne Room/Flagship give it terminals to fuel.
  - Flagship (1.3): it doubles Governor.
  - Anvil (−1.2): it gains Experiment pairs.
- Friendly (−0.1) is far stronger here than its rating, because it copies the best card on the board.
- I rate these below their ratings: Leprechaun (−1.4) and Tactician (−1.9), because they clash with Governor and Friendly.

**Plans considered:**
- **Governor engine (Friendly copying, Forager, Gold-to-Province remodels):** about 5 Governors by turn 8. The deck draws itself around turn 9–10, and multi-Province turns follow. Needs 5–6 Governors, and the opponent wants them too. Weakness: losing the Governor split.
- **Governor money (2–3 Governors plus Treasure, no Friendly holding):** Gold by turn 5–6, first Province around turn 7–8, about 4 Provinces by turn 13–15. Reliable, but it takes one Province a turn.
- **Leprechaun money:** Gold every turn, but the Hexes (Curses, Bad Omens, Envy, Poverty) wreck it, and its average is worse than Governor money's.

**Recommendation:** Build the Governor engine. Governor is non-terminal and copies itself, so the engine is close to as reliable as money and grows much faster. The opponent's best answer is the same plan, so the game turns on the Governor split. Contest the pile from turn 3.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Engine mirror:** whoever has more Governors when the pile empties wins.
  - Use Friendly on every turn you can afford to.
  - Play Gold mode early, when Silvers help you more than them. Late, their Silver junks a thin engine.
  - Pick Governor modes that give the opponent the least.
- **Against Governor money:** the engine passes them around turn 11 with 3+ Provinces a turn. Their remodels only add $4 cards and Duchies.

**Open:**
- **5/2 (recommended in either seat):** Governor / nothing. Nothing at $2 is worth buying.
- **4/3:** Silver / Anvil, to hit $5 for Governor on turns 3–4.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Turns 3–6:** Governor at $5+, then Forager ($3–4), Throne Room or Flagship at $4. Gain Experiment pairs or Advisors with Anvil only if you opened it. Take Gold at $6–7 only if no Governors are left (otherwise, Governor). Use Friendly whenever you have 2+ Governors.
- **Turns 7–9:**
  - Add a second Forager and 1 Merchant Camp.
  - Use Governor's Gold mode to stock 3–4 Golds.
- **From about turn 10:** switch to remodelling Gold into Province, plus buying Provinces with Forager Buys. Duchies come from Governor remodels of Gold when the opponent remodels, or by buying them late.

**Ending:** Provinces empty, likely by turns 13–15. Governor will empty, but no third pile is close: Experiment goes back to its pile, and Gold has 30 cards. A three-pile ending is unlikely.

**Watch out for:**
- The Governor split. If you are 2+ behind, prioritise Governor gains over everything else.
- Over-holding for Friendly while the deck can't draw yet. Hold only when the turn doesn't need the third Governor play.
- Leprechaun's Hexes if you get tempted by it.

**Challenged:**
- **Governor money:** it wins only if the engine loses the Governor split badly or overbuilds. Its ceiling is one Province plus a remodel per turn.
- **How the pick loses:** the opponent gets 6+ Governors and fires the Gold-to-Province remodels a turn earlier.

**Least sure of:**
- The holding rule for Friendly: hold whenever you have more Governors than the turn needs. Early on, the extra +3 might be worth more.
- **Test:** compare when each version first draws its deck.

**Assumed:**
- Friendly discards from hand, so a played Governor can't be used for it.
- A card played as Owl doesn't do its own text, so the Experiment's "return to pile" doesn't happen.
- Flagship and Throne Room replay an Experiment even after it has gone back to its pile.
- Copper remodelled by Governor must become an Estate, since Estate costs exactly $2. So don't remodel Copper.
