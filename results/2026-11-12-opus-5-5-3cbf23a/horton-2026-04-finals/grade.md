## Beyond the key
- Governor's remodel turns Gold ($6) into Province ($8) without a Buy, and the opponent's version can't turn Gold into anything: plausible. The opponent gains a card costing exactly $1 more, and no $7 card is in the Supply.
- The opponent's version turns Silver into a $4 card and Estate into a $3 card: plausible. They get the "exactly $1 more" version.
- Throne Room on Governor is a net +1 Action: plausible. Throne Room uses one Action, and two Governor plays give +2.
- A card played as Owl doesn't return to its pile, because the Way replaces the card's instructions: plausible. "Return this to its pile" is part of Experiment's instructions.
- Anvil discards a Copper to gain an Experiment, which comes with a second Experiment: plausible. Experiment costs $3 (up to $4), and its on-gain trigger fires.
- Tactician empties your hand, so no Governor is left for that turn's Friendly gain: plausible as card text, since Friendly discards from hand at the start of Clean-up. It is doubtful as a reason to skip Tactician, which the designer built the board around.
- "Every Governor you leave unplayed in hand" becomes another Governor: wrong. Friendly allows one discard per Clean-up, and the analysis itself later says "one free $5 card per turn."
- Throne Room or Flagship still replays an Experiment that has gone back to its pile: plausible. Replaying doesn't require the card to still be in play.
- A Copper remodelled by Governor must become an Estate: plausible. Estate is the only $2 Supply card.
- A Wish can gain a Governor to your hand: plausible. Governor costs $5, and a Wish gains a card costing up to $6.
- No third pile empties, because Experiment returns to its pile: plausible, although Anvil's double gains do drain Experiment.

## Summary
The analysis gets the Governor/Friendly flow and the Throne Room payload right, but it builds a pure Governor engine. It dismisses both Tactician and the Leprechaun-Wish line, two of the designer's three intended ideas. That repeats the finalists' mistake, which Horton called out.
