**Board:** This is an engine board, and almost everything runs through two global effects. First, Footpad's setup rule ("when you gain a card in an Action phase, +1 Card") applies whether or not anyone buys Footpad. It turns every Action-phase gainer into draw: Weaver, Artificer, Change, Stonemason, and the Horses from Hostelry. Second, Flourishing Trade (Provinces at $7, spare Actions usable as Buys) is the board's only real +Buy. It only switches on after five River Shrine plays, and River Shrine is the board's best early card anyway. The open questions were how fast the Prophecy fires and how many Actions the engine has spare. Scoring is Provinces plus Triumph, which pays 1 VP per card gained that turn and can be bought only while Estates last.

**Engine:**
- **Pieces:**
  - Village: Hostelry, plus Villagers from Exploration.
  - Draw: Witch's Hut, Horses, and Weaver/Artificer, which draw what they gain.
  - Thinning: River Shrine, with Change and Stonemason as backups.
  - +Buy: Flourishing Trade once it fires; Stonemason's overpay before then.
  - Payload: Artificer and Change gaining Provinces, $7 Provinces, Triumph.
  - Nothing is missing. Alchemist isn't needed.
- **Worked line:**
  - T1 ($4): Exploration (+1 Coffers, +1 Villager).
  - T2 ($3 + 1 Coffers): River Shrine.
  - T3–4: one turn plays River Shrine. Trash 2 Estates and buy nothing. At Clean-up gain a second River Shrine or a Hostelry, and discard the unplayed Treasures in hand to Hostelry for Horses. Exploration adds +1 Coffers and +1 Villager. The other turn buys Weaver or Footpad, using Coffers.
  - T5–8: River Shrine turns keep trashing, gaining a $4 card and banking Coffers/Villagers. Buy turns add Witch's Hut, Artificer and a second Weaver.
  - Sun: my River Shrine plays reach about 3 by T6 and 5 by T7–8. It's sooner if the opponent also plays River Shrine, and the opponent can't stop it.
  - About T8–10: a deck of roughly 15 cards (4–5 Treasures) draws itself with 2–3 Hostelries, Horses, Villagers and Witch's Hut.
- **Built turn (about T10):** $14–18, plus Buys equal to the spare Actions. That's 2 Provinces at $7 each, an Artificer discarding 7 cards to put a third Province on the deck, and Triumph at about 8–10 VP. Roughly 20+ VP a turn.
- **Reliability:** Good. The main risks are Footpad cutting a hand to 3 before you draw, and Curses.

**Key interactions:**
- **Witch's Hut + Weaver:** Witch's Hut draws 4, then discards 2 Weavers. Both Weavers play for free (+4 Silvers, which draws 4 cards), and the opponent gets a Curse because both discards were Actions. Each Witch's Hut play is worth +8 cards, 4 gains and a Curse.
- **Artificer + Footpad rule:** discard N cards to gain a card costing exactly N onto your deck, and the +1 Card draws it straight away, so the gain lands in hand. Discarding Weavers to Artificer also plays them. Late game: discard 7 (8 before the Prophecy) to gain a Province.
- **River Shrine + Exploration + Hostelry:**
  - A no-buy River Shrine turn trashes 2 cards and gains Hostelry at Clean-up.
  - The Treasures you kept unplayed become Horses (2–3 a turn).
  - Exploration adds +1 Coffers and +1 Villager.
  - Every River Shrine play is also 1 Sun toward Flourishing Trade.
- **Weaver into Hostelry in the Action phase:** discard 3 Treasures for 3 Horses and draw 4 cards. That's 4 gains for Triumph and keeps your hand size.
- **Triumph + gain-heavy turns:** Triumph scores 1 VP per gain this turn, so a turn with 8 gains makes it worth about 9 VP for 5 Debt. Under Flourishing Trade, pay off the 5 Debt and Triumph again in the same turn. It stops when the Estates run out (8, shared).
- **Change + Triumph's Debt:** while you carry Debt, Change is a terminal +$3. Late, it can also trash a Gold to gain a Province (+2 Debt) and draw it.

**Other cards:**
- **Stonemason:** overpay $5 before the Prophecy (or $4 after) to gain 2 Witch's Huts or Artificers. Trashing a Silver gains 2 Estates, which feeds Triumph and drains the Estate pile.
- **Footpad:** +2 Coffers and a hand-size attack. One copy is plenty, and it competes with Witch's Hut for terminal space.
- **Alchemist/Potion:** not needed; Horses already supply non-terminal draw.
- **Vineyard:** late VP option only. A Potion ($3 under the Prophecy) buys about 6-VP Vineyards in a deck with 18+ Actions. Stonemason trashing an Alchemist also gains 2 Vineyards.
- **Ratings check:** Footpad (−0.4), Weaver (0.6), Witch's Hut (0.4) and Exploration (−1.4) are all rated low. Here they're well above that, because of the Footpad rule and River Shrine's no-buy turns.

**Plans considered:**
1. **Engine (River Shrine / Hostelry / Witch's Hut + Weaver / Artificer, then Provinces + Triumph).**
   - Line: draws itself around T8–10; Witch's Hut curses from about T7; 2+ Provinces a turn from about T10.
   - Needs: 5 Sun, 2–3 Hostelries, at least 2 Weavers.
   - Weakness: a Footpad'd hand before the deck is built.
2. **Weaver/Footpad Big Money.**
   - How it works: Weaver gains 2 Silvers and draws 2.
   - Line: Gold around T5–6; 4 Provinces around T13–15. Faster if the opponent fires the Prophecy and Provinces drop to $7.
   - Weakness: one card per turn, Witch's Hut curses, no Triumph.
3. **Triumph/gain slog.**
   - How it works: Weaver and Stonemason gain every turn, Triumph every turn, then three-pile Estates, Curses and a cheap pile.
   - Verdict: the engine already does this better.

**Recommendation:** Build the engine. It has thinning, a village, strong draw, a junker, and a +Buy route that the opponent can't block. Against money, the engine scores twice a turn by T10 and curses along the way.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Engine mirror:** the opponent's River Shrines speed up your Prophecy too. The contest is over Estates (Triumph) and over who gives more Curses: take a second Witch's Hut early and trash Curses with River Shrine.
- **Against money:** the Prophecy makes their Provinces cheaper, so don't idle. Their 4 Provinces arrive around T13. Your 2-Province turns begin around T10.

**Open:**
- **4/3 (preferred):** T1 Exploration, T2 River Shrine using the Coffers. Alternative: River Shrine/Silver.
- **5/2:** Footpad / Stonemason. Weaker, because thinning comes slower.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffles 1–2:** a second River Shrine, Weaver, Hostelry, Footpad (one copy).
- **Shuffle 3:** Witch's Hut, Artificer, a second Weaver, more Hostelries. Stonemason overpay at $7.
- **Greening:** Triumph once you have about 5 gains a turn, and before the opponent takes the Estates. Provinces at $7 from about T9–10.

**Ending:**
- **Provinces:** about T12–14.
- **Three-pile alternative:** Estates (Triumph/Stonemason), Curses, and Hostelry or Weaver.
- Count Estates every turn.

**Watch out for:**
1. Opponent Footpad on turns 3–6.
2. Over-terminaling: Witch's Hut, Weaver, River Shrine, Footpad and Change are all terminals. Hostelries and Villagers have to keep up.
3. The opponent building money while you fire the Prophecy for them.

**Challenged:**
- I re-tested Weaver-BM with $7 Provinces. It's faster than normal money, but it still takes one card a turn, while the engine takes several. The engine has one real way to lose: getting Footpad'd and cursed in shuffles 2–3 so the deck never draws itself. The fix is a second Hostelry and spending the Villagers.
- **Least sure of:** whether Exploration beats Silver as the T1 buy. It depends on how many no-buy turns you get early. If River Shrine collides or keeps missing turns 3–4, Silver was better.

**Assumed:**
- "Use Action plays as Buys" means unused Actions can be spent as Buys.
- The Footpad +1 Card draws a card that Artificer just put on top of your deck.
- Exploration triggers on the turn you buy it.
- At the start of Clean-up your unplayed Treasures are still in hand, so a Hostelry gained by River Shrine can take them.
- Gaining Horses counts toward Triumph.
- With more players: Sun 8+, Estates 12, Curses split.
