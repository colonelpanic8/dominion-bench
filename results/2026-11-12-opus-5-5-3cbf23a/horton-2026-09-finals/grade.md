## Beyond the key
- The Footpad rule is global, whether or not anyone buys Footpad: plausible. The below-the-line text reads "In games using this", with no ownership condition.
- Witch's Hut discarding 2 Weavers plays both (4 Silvers, drawing 4) and curses the opponent: plausible. Both discards are Actions, the Weaver reaction plays them outside Clean-up, and the gains happen in the Action phase.
- Artificer's gain goes onto the deck, and the Footpad +1 Card draws it straight into hand: plausible. The gain is put on the deck first, then the on-gain draw resolves.
- On a no-buy River Shrine turn, the Hostelry gained at the start of Clean-up can discard the unplayed Treasures still in hand for Horses: plausible. Those Treasures are still in hand at that point. A sharp line the key doesn't spell out, though these gains are outside the Action phase, so Footpad draws nothing from them.
- Exploration pays out on the turn you buy it: plausible. Buying a Project isn't a gain.
- Change is a terminal +$3 while carrying Triumph's Debt: plausible. That's the card's text. It only applies if Debt is left unpaid, though, which this plan seldom does.
- Stonemason trashing a Silver gains 2 Estates, which feeds Triumph and drains the Estate pile: plausible. Estate costs less than Silver both before and after the Prophecy.
- Stonemason overpay of $5 ($4 after the Prophecy) gains 2 Witch's Huts or Artificers: plausible. The overpay gains Actions costing exactly the amount overpaid.
- Late Artificer discarding 7 for a $7 Province, drawn at once: plausible but costly. That's 7 cards discarded mid-turn.
- Weaver Big Money gets 4 Provinces around T13–15: doubtful. It's an unsupported estimate, and the key never weighs money.
- Reckless and Stop-Moving complications: not raised. No extra credit from the grader notes.

## Summary
The analysis finds the board's engine: the global Footpad draw, Hostelry Horses, Weaver discard-plays, the Exploration/River Shrine opening, and Flourishing Trade as the +Buy. It even sees that Triumph can be bought repeatedly under the Prophecy. It misses the designer's payoff, one turn-7 megaturn that buys five Triumphs. It plans Witch's Hut-led Province turns from T10 instead, and it reaches the Alchemist/Vineyard fallback through Potion rather than Change.
