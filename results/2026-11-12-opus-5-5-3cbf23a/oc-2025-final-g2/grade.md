## Beyond the key
- Durations still in play (Amulet, Grotto, Enchantress) count toward Peddler's discount on the turn after they're played: plausible. They stay in play until that turn's Clean-up.
- Monastery trashes once per gain, including a Copper already played, and as a Night card it can't collide with Amulet: plausible from card text. The key says it trashed little in practice.
- Farmhands' on-gain can set aside an unplayed Remodel or Enchantress to play next turn without using an Action: plausible.
- Enchantress makes an opponent's lead Peddler lose only $1: plausible. They get +1 Card +1 Action instead of +1 Card +1 Action +$1.
- Way of the Squirrel's +2 Cards come after the Clean-up draw, so they add to the next hand: plausible. They happen "at the end of this turn".
- Grotto adds 4 to the next hand (5 + 4 + 2 Squirrel = 11): plausible. It discards the 4 set-aside cards and draws 4 on top of the normal 5.
- Transmute turns an Estate into a Gold and a Peddler into a Duchy, and Remodel covers it: plausible.
- Fortune gains a Gold per Gladiator in play, and Remodel can turn those Golds into Provinces: plausible. Gold ($6) plus $2 reaches Province.
- Fortune with 3 Loot makes $18+: plausible ($9 doubled), but it ignores Fortune's $8 + 8 debt cost.
- Hammer and Spell Scroll can gain Remodel or Farmhands: plausible. Both gain a card costing $4 or less (cheaper than $7 for Spell Scroll).
- In the engine mirror, the Peddler pile decides the game: doubtful. Remodel also converts Gold, Loot and Provinces, and the analysis says so itself.

## Summary
The midgame plan matches the experts closely: Grotto plus Farmhands for draw, Peddler as payload, several Remodels turning Peddlers into Provinces, Foray for Loot Buys, early greening and Squirrel. The main miss is the opening. It recommends Monastery on both splits, the opening the commentators called "simply very bad" and that lost, instead of opening Grotto.
