**Board:** There's no draw that adds cards this turn. Hands get bigger only on the next turn: Grotto adds up to 4 cards, each Action played as Way of the Squirrel adds 2, and Enchantress adds 2. So the deck draws itself through bigger next hands, not through draw cards. The payoff is Peddler, which is close to free when bought with several Actions in play, and Remodel, which turns a Peddler into a Province. +Buy comes only from Loot (gained with Foray) and Fortune. The main open question is +Buy: you need at least one source by about turn 8.

**Engine:**
- **Pieces:**
  - Farmhands is the village (+1 Card +2 Actions).
  - Grotto and Squirrel plays are the "draw".
  - Amulet, Monastery and Remodel thin the deck.
  - Peddlers are both payload and feed for Remodel.
  - Remodel is the gainer.
  - Loot and Fortune give the Buys.
- **Speed:** roughly 12–14 cards by turn 6–7. A turn that plays about 3 Actions buys Peddlers at $0–2 from turn 7–8. Remodel turns Peddlers into Provinces from about turn 9–10.
- **Reliability:** Grotto and Squirrel cards arrive at the start of the turn and so can't stall. The risk is Remodel and Peddler landing in different hands, so get 2 Remodels.
- **Built turn:** a hand of 11 or more cards (5 + Grotto 4 + Squirrel 2). Play Farmhands ×2, Grotto, then Remodel ×2 on Peddlers for 2 Provinces. That leaves about $8 and 2–3 Buys: one more Province plus free Peddlers to restock. That's about 3 Provinces a turn.

**Key interactions:**
- **Remodel + Peddler:** Peddler's discount applies only in a Buy phase, so in the Action phase it costs $8. Remodel turns it into a Province (up to $10). Buy Peddler for $0–2, then next turn convert it. That's +1 Province per Remodel play, with no Buy and no $, from about turn 9.
- **Durations + Peddler:** Amulet, Grotto and Enchantress stay in play through your next turn, so each counts toward Peddler's discount on two turns. With 4 Actions in play, Peddler costs $0.
- **Grotto + greening:** in the Action phase, set aside up to 4 dead cards (Estates, Provinces, spare Coppers). Next turn they're discarded and 4 fresh cards are drawn. That's +4 hand size and sifting, and it gets better as you green.
- **Monastery + gains:** each gain lets it trash one card, including a Copper you've already played. Gains include the buy, Amulet's Silver, Remodel's gain, Foray's Loot and Hammer's gain. Early that's 2–3 trashes per turn without using an Action.
- **Foray → Loot → Remodel:** spend $3 and discard 3 different-named dead cards (easy from a 9-card or bigger hand) to get a $7 Treasure. Most Loot give $3 + Buy. Later Remodel takes the Loot ($7) to a Province.
- **Farmhands' when-gain:** when you buy Farmhands, set aside an unplayed Remodel or Enchantress from your hand. It plays at the start of next turn without using an Action.
- **Gladiator → Fortune:** Gladiators are +$3, and each one that isn't blocked trashes one from its pile, uncovering Fortune. Fortune doubles your $, gives +Buy, and gains a Gold per Gladiator in play (Remodel can turn those Golds into Provinces). It's a second +Buy source, but slow and debt-heavy.

**Other cards:**
- **Enchantress:** its partner is Peddler, as an Action in play on two turns, plus +2 Cards next turn. The attack is mild: the opponent leads with a Peddler and loses $1. If they have no Peddler, it costs them their Remodel or Grotto.
- **Guardian:** blocks Enchantress and Sword, and its gain lets Monastery trash a card. It's weak. Buy one only if Enchantress is hitting a key Remodel.
- **Transmute:** turns an Estate into a Gold and a Peddler into a Duchy, but needs a $4 Potion that does nothing else. Remodel does the same jobs, so skip it.
- **Way of the Squirrel:** with Farmhands' spare Actions, play surplus Peddlers or a Remodel with no target as Squirrel.
- **Hammer and Spell Scroll** gain Remodel or Farmhands. **Sword** hits a money opponent.

**Plans considered:**
1. **Peddler/Remodel engine** (above). First $6+ around turn 6–7, Provinces from turn 9–10, about 3 Provinces a turn from turn 11. Needs 2 Farmhands, 1–2 Grottos, 2 Remodels, 5 or more Peddlers, and Loot or Fortune for Buys. Weakness: the Peddler pile is shared and each conversion trashes a Peddler.
2. **Money: Gladiator/Enchantress-BM**, going for Fortune. $6 on turns 3–5, $8 around turn 6–8, 4 Provinces by about turn 15–16. Needs 2–3 Gladiators. Weakness: one Province a turn, and the opponent can block Gladiator's bonus by revealing a copy of the same card.
3. **Thin money: Amulet/Monastery into Golds and Loot, plus Remodel Gold→Province.** Better than plan 2, but capped at about 2 Provinces a turn without Peddlers.

**Recommendation:** Plan 1, the engine. It scores 2–3 Provinces a turn from turn 10–11, against money's 4 Provinces by turn 15–16. Remodel needs no Buy, so even without +Buy it beats one Province a turn.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Engine mirror:** the Peddler pile decides it (5 each). Get Peddlers early. Once they run low, turn Golds and Loot into Provinces with Remodel instead. Enchantress is worth one copy.
- **Against money:** you're ahead by about turn 12. Ignore Sword, since Grotto and Squirrel refill your hand.
- **Runner-up (thin money):** loses to the engine on the race.

**Open:**
- **4/3:** Amulet / Monastery. Two thinners that can't collide, since Monastery is a Night card. Remodel/Amulet is also fine.
- **5/2:** Remodel / Monastery. Turn Estates into Farmhands or Silver, and Monastery trashes again for each gain.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffles 1–2:** Silver, Farmhands, Grotto, a second Monastery or Amulet, then Remodel. Trash Estates first, then Coppers.
- **Shuffle 3:** Peddlers whenever they cost $4 or less, a second Remodel, and Foray whenever you have $3 and 3 dead cards with different names. Add 1 Enchantress.
- **Then:** Gold only if you have nothing better.
- **Greening:** start once a turn reliably has Remodel plus a Peddler (about turn 9). Green fast after that: the deck handles green through Grotto, and Remodel gives VP for free.

**Ending:** Provinces run out around turn 13–15. Peddlers may empty too (along with Gladiator/Fortune if both players drain it), but a three-pile ending is unlikely.

**Watch out for:**
- Being out-bought on Peddlers.
- No +Buy by turn 9. Push Foray then.
- Thinning too hard. Keep about $6–8 for Provinces after the conversions.

**Challenged:**
- **Fortune-heavy plan:** Fortune with 3 Loot makes $18 or more and 2 or more Buys. It needs about 5 Gladiator plays first, which is too slow as the main plan, but take one Fortune as a +Buy and payload add-on once it's available.
- **How the engine loses:** Enchantress hits Remodel turns while a Gold/Loot money deck takes Provinces from turn 7. Answer with Guardian, or by playing Peddler first.

**Least sure of:** whether Fortune is a better +Buy source than Foray's Loot, given Fortune's 8 debt. Test: count how often a 9-card hand has 3 different dead names, which Foray needs.

**Assumed:**
- Squirrel's +2 Cards are drawn after the Clean-up draw, so they add to the next hand.
- Durations still in play count toward Peddler's discount.
- Peddler costs $8 when Remodel trashes it in the Action phase (the discount is Buy-phase only).
- Revealing nothing to Gladiator still gives +$1 and trashes one from the pile.
