**Board:** A thin, non-terminal engine board with no attacks. Junk Dealer and Sentinel thin the deck. Lost City is the main draw and also counts as a village. Villa and Borrow give +Buy, and Tools gains a free engine piece every turn. Provinces are the only VP. The open questions are how much Lost City's gift to the opponent costs, and where the payload for double-Province turns comes from. Answers: Villa and Borrow, Junk Dealer's +$1, Vault discarding green cards, and a little Gold.

**Engine:**
- **Pieces:**
  - Junk Dealer ($5) and Sentinel ($3) for thinning.
  - Lost City ($5) as draw. Each one nets +1 card and +1 Action, so it is also the village.
  - Villa ($4) for +Buy and +$1.
  - Borrow ($0) gives a free +Buy and +$1 every turn. Its −1 Card token costs almost nothing once the deck draws itself.
  - Tools ($4) to copy pieces.
  - 1–2 Vaults late, as terminal draw that is also payload.
- **Terminals:** only Sentinel and Vault. Lost City's spare Actions and Villa cover them, so Harbor Village isn't needed.
- **Build:** open with the two trashers. Build turns 3–8 (about 3 Lost City, 2–3 Villa, 1–2 Tools). The deck draws itself around turn 8–9.
- **First double-Province turn:** about turn 10–11. Example: 3 Villa + 2 Junk Dealer + Borrow is $6. Add a Gold or two (+$3–6) and Vault discarding 3–4 green cards (+$3–4) to reach about $16, with 4+ Buys.
- **Reliability:** high. All the draw is non-terminal, there are no attacks, and the thinning is strong.

**Key interactions:**
- **Tools + Villa:** you play Tools in the Buy phase with Villa in play, so it gains a Villa. Villa's on-gain puts it in your hand, gives +1 Action and sends you back to the Action phase. You play it for +2 Actions, +1 Buy and +$1, and then can play any Actions you drew in the Buy phase. Net: each Tools is +$1, +1 Buy and a permanent Villa. It's the best Tools target from about turn 6 until the 10 Villas run out.
- **Tools + Lost City:** gains a free $5 draw piece every turn. Each Lost City you gain (bought or from Tools) makes the opponent draw a card at once. That is about one free Lab play for them per Lost City.
- **Buying Villa:** if you have a second Buy (Borrow), Villa effectively costs $3. Buy it first and you're back in the Action phase with Villa in hand, playing it for +$1 and another Buy.
- **Borrow:** in the opening it turns $4 into $5 or $2 into $3, at the cost of one card the next turn. Once the engine is built it is a free +$1 and +Buy every turn.
- **Vault + greening:** each green card you discard is +$1, so late Provinces fund the next ones.

**Other cards:**
- **Innovation ($6):** its best use is Tools gaining a Lost City in the Buy phase. Innovation plays it and draws 2 cards, which can be Treasures you then play. That is small for $6 once per turn, so skip it or buy it only with spare money.
- **Band of Misfits ($5):** it can only play Villa, Harbor Village or Sentinel. That makes it at best a $5 Villa without the on-gain bonus, so it's only a Villa replacement after the pile empties.
- **Harbor Village ($4):** gives $1 more after Villa, Junk Dealer or Lighthouse. Not needed, because Lost City supplies the Actions.
- **Caravan ($4):** a cantrip that gives +1 card at the start of next turn. It's filler draw that doesn't feed the opponent, and Tools can copy it while it's still in play.
- **Lighthouse ($2):** non-terminal $1 now and $1 next turn. There are no Attacks for it to block. It's a fine $2 buy and also triggers Harbor Village's bonus.

**Plans considered:**
- **Vault Big Money with Borrow:**
  - How: 1–2 Vaults, Borrow when $1 reaches a price.
  - Line: about $6 on turns 3–5, $8 from about turn 6–7, 4 Provinces around turn 15.
  - Needs: nothing contested.
  - Weakness: one Province per turn, never speeds up.
- **Thin Lost City/Villa/Tools engine (pick):** as in the Engine section above.
  - Line: first big turn about turn 10–11, game over about turn 13–14.
  - Needs: 3 or more Lost Cities and 2–3 Villas. Villa is the contested pile in a mirror.
  - Weakness: Lost City gains feed the opponent, and Tools needs the right card in play.
- **Villa/Tools pile rush:** rejected. There are no alternative VP cards and only one cheap pile to drain, so ending on piles doesn't pay.

**Recommendation:** Build the engine. Against money, the 4–6 cards you gift with Lost City are worth roughly half a Province or less to them. An engine taking 2–3 Provinces a turn from turn 10–11 still finishes well before money reaches its 4 Provinces around turn 15.

**Against their best plan:**
- **If they play money:** gain your Lost Cities early, while extra cards are worth little to their weak deck. Get late draw from Caravan and Vault instead.
- **If they mirror the engine:** the Lost City gifts cancel out. The deciding pile is Villa, so take the 2nd and 3rd Villas early and use Tools to copy Villa.

**Open:**
- **$4/$3:** turn 1 Borrow to Junk Dealer. Turn 2 buy Sentinel. Use Borrow on turn 2 only if you have just $2 (that costs a card on turn 3).
- **$3/$4:** Sentinel, then Borrow to Junk Dealer.
- **$5/$2:** Junk Dealer, then Borrow to Sentinel.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Turns 3–6:**
  - At $5: Lost City.
  - At $4: Villa first, then Tools.
  - At $6–7: Lost City plus Lighthouse, or Villa plus something $2–3 using Borrow's Buy.
  - Use Junk Dealer and Sentinel to trash Estates first, then Coppers. Keep about $5 of money in the deck.
- **Turns 6–9:**
  - Get to 3–4 Lost Cities, 3 Villas and 1–2 Tools.
  - Add 1 Gold or 2 Vaults as payload.
  - A second Junk Dealer is fine.
- **Greening:** start once a turn makes $13–16 with 2–3 Buys, about turn 10. Then take Provinces and Duchies with every Buy.

**Ending:** Provinces run out around turn 13–14. Villa is the only pile likely to empty, so there's no realistic three-pile threat.

**Watch out for:**
- **Stacking Lost City buys:** two in one turn hands a money opponent a 7-card turn. Use Tools on Villa or Caravan instead when they're close to $8.
- **Over-trashing:** keep about $5 of Treasure until Villas and Junk Dealers replace it.
- **A Villa race:** if they open Villa or Tools, take Villas earlier.

**Challenged:** I re-tested Vault money with Borrow. Its worked line gives 4 Provinces around turn 15. The engine loses only if it stalls on turns 9–11 or gifts 8+ Lost City cards. Neither is likely with two trashers and non-terminal draw.

**Least sure of:** the turn-10–11 first big turn. If by turn 9 you aren't drawing the deck with $12 or more, switch to Gold and Vaults and green one Province per turn.

**Assumed:**
- I'm not sure you can play Treasures again in the second Buy phase after Villa sends you back. The repo's rules say no Treasures after a buy "that turn". If that holds, play every Treasure before using Tools or buying Villa.
- Vault's discard-for-$ counts as "+$" for Harbor Village's bonus.
- Innovation can play a card in the Buy phase. Drawing there is only useful before your first buy.
