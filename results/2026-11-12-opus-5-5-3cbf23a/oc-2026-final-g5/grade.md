## Beyond the key
- Band of Misfits can play only Villa, Harbor Village or Sentinel: plausible. Caravan and Lighthouse are Durations, Tools is a Treasure, and the other Actions cost $5.
- Tools + Villa nets +$1, +1 Buy and a permanent Villa per Tools: plausible. Villa's on-gain puts it in hand and returns you to the Action phase.
- With Borrow's Buy, Villa "effectively costs $3": plausible. Borrow's +$1 and Villa's own +$1 when replayed offset $2 of its cost against the Buy.
- Borrow is a free +$1 and +Buy every turn once the deck draws itself: plausible. The −1 Card token is removed at the next draw, so the cost is one card per turn.
- Innovation's best use is Tools gaining a Lost City in the Buy phase and drawing Treasures to play: plausible as a line, but it undersells the card (see R4).
- Villa is the only pile likely to empty, so there's no three-pile threat: doubtful. The commentators thought Lost City should pile, and both final decks held 3–5 Lost Cities.
- Lost City gifts to a money opponent are worth about half a Province or less: plausible but unquantified. Each gift is one extra card for them.
- Treasures may not be playable in the second Buy phase after Villa: doubtful. A fresh Buy phase normally allows Treasures again, though the analysis's caution of playing Treasures first costs little.
- Harbor Village's bonus triggers after Villa, Junk Dealer or Lighthouse: plausible. Each gives +$.

## Summary
The analysis gets the engine right: Sentinel thinning, Tools copying Villa and Lost City, Villa's +Buy and gain-and-play, Vaults capped at one or two. But it opens Junk Dealer through Borrow instead of Caravan, which is the trasher line the commentators rejected. It is cautious about Lost City gifts where they wanted Lost City taken aggressively. And it dismisses Innovation, the commentators' main point about this board.
