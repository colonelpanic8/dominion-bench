## Beyond the key
- With Abundance in play, buying a $3 Chariot Race gives +$3 and +Buy, which pays for a $3 Wayfarer with no Rope: plausible. Abundance triggers on the next Action gained, and Wayfarer then copies the $3 cost of the last other card gained.
- With Livery in play, buying a $6 Wayfarer gains a Horse, which makes the next Wayfarer cost $3: plausible. The Horse ($3) is the last other card gained, but this still needs a second Buy.
- Artificer before Cursed Village sees about 9–10 cards and draws the newly gained part in the same turn: plausible. Artificer gains onto the deck, and Cursed Village then draws up to 6 from about 1 card in hand.
- Mastermind on Wayfarer gives +9 cards at the start of the turn without spending an Action: plausible. Mastermind plays the card at start of turn, outside the Action phase's Action count.
- A Wayfarer revealed to Chariot Race after an Artificer gained a Copper costs $0 and loses the reveal: plausible. Wayfarer's cost follows the last other card gained this turn wherever it is.
- Captain can play a Wayfarer from the Supply once a gain earlier in the turn has brought its cost to $4 or less: plausible for the play on the turn Captain is played. It rarely works on the next-turn play, which happens before any gain.
- Gaining a Horse counts as gaining an Action, so it can trigger Abundance: plausible. Horse is an Action card.
- War can trash a Rope or Abundance: plausible. Both cost $4, and War trashes the first $3 or $4 card it reveals.
- Corsair barely touches this engine, whose payload isn't Silver or Gold: plausible, and it matches the grader note's reading of Corsair.
- Chariot Race can't drive a three-pile ending, because Curses only come from Plague and Locusts: plausible.

## Summary
The analysis recommends roughly the deck that won this game: wide Artificer/Cursed Village/Wayfarer with Rope, Colonnade and Chariot Race, with Livery and Mastermind as later payload. It also reads the big Mastermind turns correctly. It undersells Rope as the card to stack, and it misses agoo03's trick of gaining a Copper to get $0 Wayfarers, though it notices the Copper-makes-Wayfarer-$0 effect in passing.
