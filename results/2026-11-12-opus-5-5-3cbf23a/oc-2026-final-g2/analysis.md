# Kingdom analysis: oc-2026-final-g2

**Board:** Two players, no Colonies, no Shelters, normal 7 Copper / 3 Estate start, so the opening split is 4/3 or 5/2. The board has no real trasher (only Rope's one card a turn) and no plain village. What it does have:
- **Wayfarer's cost changes.** It costs the same as the last other card gained this turn, so once you have gained something cheap it is a $3 (or $0) Smithy.
- **Cursed Village draws up to 6** and gives +2 Actions. It is the board's only village, and each copy gained gives you a Hex.
- **+Buy comes only from Treasures.** Rope gives it every play. Abundance gives +Buy and +$3 the next time you gain an Action.

VP comes from Provinces, plus three sources that add no card to the deck: Chariot Race tokens, Colonnade (12 VP in the pool) and Hex-driven minus points (Misery, Plague Curses). The game ends on Provinces.

**Engine:**
- **Pieces:**
  - Draw: Wayfarer ($6, often $3 with a gain first), Cursed Village, Horses (from Livery), and Artificer used as a discard outlet.
  - Actions: Cursed Village, or Mastermind for one tripled terminal.
  - +Buy: Rope and Abundance.
  - Payload: Livery (+$3 and Horses), Chariot Race (+$1 and +1 VP tokens), Silvers from Wayfarer, and Colonnade.
- **Speed:** with the cheap-Wayfarer trick it gains two parts most turns from turn 3–5. It first draws most of its deck around turns 8–10, and a built turn reaches $14–18 with 2–4 Buys from about turn 10–12. That is normal engine speed.
- **Reliability:** moderate. There is no real thinning, and Hexes add a little junk and minus points. Artificer followed by Cursed Village absorbs most of the junk, and Rope removes Estates first.

**Key interactions:**
- **+Buy + Wayfarer:** buy a $3 card (Chariot Race or Silver), and Wayfarer then costs $3. That is $6 for two parts.
  - Abundance does it with $3 in hand. Buying Chariot Race triggers Abundance (+$3, +Buy), and the $3 pays for a Wayfarer.
  - Livery does it too. With Livery in play, buy a $6 Wayfarer, gain a Horse (cost $3), and the next Wayfarer costs $3.
- **Artificer + Cursed Village:** order matters. Play Artificer (+1 Card) and discard 3–4 junk cards. That gains a $3/$4 part onto your deck, which also drops Wayfarer's cost to $3/$4 for the turn. Then Cursed Village refills you from about 1 card to 6. Together that is about 9–10 cards seen, and the new part arrives in the same turn. This is the engine's real draw core, and it sifts out Coppers and Estates.
- **Livery + gains:** each gain costing $4 or more gives a Horse (+2 Cards, +1 Action, non-terminal, the next turn it is drawn). Mastermind played on Livery next turn gives +$9, and every gain costing $4 or more that turn brings 3 Horses.
- **Mastermind + Wayfarer:** +9 cards at the start of the turn without spending your Action, and up to 3 Silvers.
- **Colonnade:** 2 VP each time you buy an Action with a copy of it in play. Easy targets are Chariot Race or Wayfarer bought on spare Buys. The engine player should take 4–6 of the 6 claims, worth 8–12 VP.
- **Chariot Race:** a cantrip worth $1 and 1 VP whenever your revealed card costs more than the opponent's top card. Your deck is full of $4–6 cards, so it wins roughly 50–65% of reveals once built. Warning: if Artificer gained a Copper earlier in the turn, a revealed Wayfarer costs $0 and loses.

**Other cards:**
- **Captain:** plays Chariot Race now and next turn, or a Wayfarer from the Supply after a gain this turn has brought it to $4 or less. Best partner is Mastermind (6 Chariot Races). Fine, but late and low priority.
- **Corsair:** +$2, and it trashes a Silver or Gold from money decks. It barely touches this engine, whose payload is Rope, Abundance and Livery (Wayfarer's Silvers are optional). Best partner is a money plan.
- **Rope:** the opener. It gives +Buy for the Wayfarer trick and trashes Estates.
- **Road Network:** gives cards on the opponent's greening turns. Partner is none; buy it only with a spare $5 Buy late in an engine mirror.
- **Ratings check:** Corsair (−2.8) and Abundance (−0.5) both rate low.
  - Abundance plays far better than its rating here, because of the Wayfarer trick.
  - Corsair's low rating fits, since the opponent's likely engine has little Silver or Gold to trash.

**Plans considered:**

1. **Cursed Village / Wayfarer / Artificer engine** (Rope, Abundance and Livery for payload; Chariot Race and Colonnade for VP).
   - Line: about 2 parts a turn from turn 3; draws its deck around turns 9–10; double Provinces plus tokens from about turn 11.
   - Needs: 2–3 Cursed Villages, 3–4 Wayfarers, 2 Artificers, and 3 or more +Buy Treasures.
   - Weakness: Hexes (Misery −2/−4 VP, Plague Curses, War trashing a Rope or Abundance) and no real thinning.
2. **Wayfarer-BM or Corsair-BM with Rope** (the baseline).
   - Line: $6 around turns 3–5, $8 around turns 5–7, 4 Provinces around turns 14–15.
   - Corsair attacks about every other turn from turn 3–4.
   - Weakness: one card a turn, and it leaves all of Colonnade and the Chariot Race tokens to the engine.
3. **Mastermind / Captain Chariot Race token deck.**
   - It scores tokens, but it is slow ($11 of parts for 6 Chariot Race plays) and shares the engine's draw problems.
   - Best folded into plan 1 as late payload, not built as its own plan.

**Recommendation:** Plan 1, the engine. Cheap Wayfarers and Artificer feeding Cursed Village give it real draw on a board with no plain village. +Buy is plentiful, and the VP that adds no card (Colonnade's 12 and Chariot Race tokens) widens the gap over a one-buy money deck. The opponent's only attack, Corsair, misses this payload.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Against Corsair-BM or Wayfarer-BM:** they reach about 4 Provinces by turn 14–15. You should hold 8+ VP from Colonnade and tokens by turn 11, then take 2 Provinces a turn.
- **Against an engine mirror:** Colonnade and Wayfarers are contested. Take Colonnade VP early, since each claim is a 4 VP swing, and don't over-gain Cursed Villages (Hexes).
- **If you played the runner-up (money) against an engine,** you lose the Colonnade and token race. Corsair barely slows them.

**Open:**
- **4/3:** Rope / Silver. Abundance / Silver is close.
- **5/2:** Livery / nothing. Livery's $3 hands on turns 3–4 hit $6 for a Wayfarer plus Horse. There is no $2 card worth buying.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Shuffles 2–3:**
  - With $6 or more and a +Buy, take Chariot Race plus a $3 Wayfarer.
  - With $5, take Cursed Village first, then Artificer.
  - Add Abundance or a second Rope at $4.
  - Targets by about turn 8: 2–3 Wayfarers, 2 Cursed Villages, 2 Artificers.
- **Then:** a second Livery, Mastermind, Silvers from Wayfarer while the money is still short, and Chariot Race on spare Buys (with one in play, for Colonnade).
- **Green:** once turns reliably reach $13+ with 2 Buys, around turns 10–12.

**Ending:** Provinces run out. Colonnade will empty but ends nothing. Chariot Race could run low but won't make a three-pile ending without Curses (only Plague and Locusts give them). Watch the Penultimate Province Rule against a money opponent's banked Gold turns.

**Watch out for:**
1. **Hex luck.** If an early Hex is Misery or Plague, cap Cursed Villages at 2 and use Mastermind as the second "village".
2. **Opponent opens Corsair.** Rely less on Wayfarer's Silvers and more on Livery and Abundance.
3. **Ordering.** Play Artificer before Cursed Village. Don't play Cursed Village with a full hand.

**Challenged:** I re-tested Corsair/Wayfarer money with Rope's +Buy at its best. It still takes one Province a turn and gives up 8–12 VP of Colonnade and tokens, so on equal Province splits it needs a 2-Province lead it rarely gets. The pick loses if the engine is hit early by War (losing a Rope or Abundance), Plague and Misery, and stalls with no thinning. Money then wins around turn 15.

**Least sure of:** that Artificer plus Cursed Village gives reliable draw without real thinning. The test: if the deck isn't drawing about 80% of itself by turn 10, switch weight toward Horses (Livery) and a Mastermind-tripled Wayfarer.

**Assumed:**
- Wayfarer's changed cost applies in the Supply and when bought.
- A second Wayfarer bought right after the first also costs the same as the earlier $3 card.
- Abundance triggers on an Action gained in the same Buy phase it was played.
- Captain can play a Wayfarer from the Supply when its current cost is $4 or less.
- Gaining a Horse counts as gaining an Action card, so it can trigger Abundance.
- Colonnade counts Actions bought or gained during the Buy phase.
