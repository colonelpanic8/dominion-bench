**Board:** No real trashing, a village that also draws (Litter, paid for in Debt), two terminal draw cards at $5 (Patrol, Council Room) and +Buy almost everywhere. The board's main trick is a Gold factory. Cage trashes itself when you gain a Victory card, and each Market Square still in your hand reacts to that trash by gaining a Gold. Until you start buying Victory cards, Cage also acts as $2 thinning: it holds your Estates out of the deck. The open question was whether a deck with no permanent thinning can draw itself fast enough to run this. It can, by about turn 8–10.

**Setup:** Potion ($4) is in the Supply because of Vineyard. Litter adds Debt when played. Border Guard brings the Lantern and Horn artifacts. Seaway is an Event and Way of the Squirrel is a Way. No Shelters, Colonies or Heirlooms, so the deck is the standard 7 Copper and 3 Estates and the opening splits are 4/3 or 5/2. Nothing in the Kingdom costs $4 (only Silver or Potion), and the $5 slot is crowded: Litter, Patrol, Council Room, Jester, and the Seaway Event.

**Engine:**
- **Pieces:**
  - Litter (+2 Cards +2 Actions, $1 Debt per play) is the village and also draws one net card.
  - Patrol ×2–3 is terminal draw. It also pulls Victory cards into your hand, so they stop clogging later draws.
  - One Council Room adds +4 Cards and a Buy. Keep it to one, because each play draws the opponent a card.
  - Market Square ×3–4 are cantrips with +Buy and are the payload trigger.
  - Cage ×2 early hold the Estates (and spare Coppers) out of the deck. Later, one Cage a turn is the trigger.
  - Astrolabe and Silver or Gold supply early money.
- **When it draws its deck:** a 16-card working deck is likely about turn 10. For example: 3 Litter, 2 Patrol and Council Room add 13 cards to a 5-card hand, while two Cages hold 3 Estates and about 3 Coppers outside the deck.
- **Built turn:** about $10–12 after paying Litter Debt, before the Gold loop starts. Once the loop has run for 2–3 turns it is $18+ with 3+ Buys, which is two Provinces plus a Vineyard or Tunnel. Reliability is moderate: thinning is only temporary, so spare draw matters.

**Key interactions:**
- **Cage + Market Square + any Victory gain:**
  - In the Buy phase, play Cage and keep Market Squares unplayed in hand.
  - Gain a Victory card: a Tunnel ($3, 2 VP), or a Vineyard once you have a Potion. Cage trashes itself, and each Market Square you discard from hand gains a Gold.
  - With 2 spare Buys, also buy a new Cage ($2) so the next turn can do it again.
  - Yield: 2–3 Golds plus 2–4 VP per turn, starting around turn 8–9.
  - Limits: the Cage pile has 10 cards and only about 8 such turns are realistic. Held Market Squares don't give their +Buy, so take Buys from Astrolabe, Council Room or one Market Square you do play.
- **Cage as temporary thinning:** played on turn 3 or 4, it sets aside Estates (and Coppers you don't need) until your first Victory gain. Then they come back into your hand at end of turn, so you lose no draws.
- **Potion + Vineyard:** an engine with 12–15 Action cards makes each Vineyard ($0 plus Potion) worth 4–5 VP. With the deck drawing itself, one Potion gives you a Vineyard every turn. Each Vineyard is also the Victory gain that sets off the Cage loop.
- **Way of the Squirrel:** if your last Action would otherwise play a cantrip or a spare card, play it as the Way. You get +2 Cards at the end of the turn, so the next hand has 7 cards.

**Other cards:**
- **Tunnel:** the cheap Victory gain that triggers Cage. Also gains a Gold when Border Guard (or the opponent's Jester) discards it.
- **Border Guard:** a $2 Action that adds to the Vineyard count. It earns Lantern or Horn in an Action-dense deck, and its discard triggers Tunnel. Filler for spare Buys.
- **Seaway:** gaining a $3 Market Square for $5 is poor. Use it only for spare $, or put the +Buy token on Market Square or Border Guard if Buys are short.
- **Jester:** +$2, and it lets you gain copies of the opponent's Golds or Litters off their top card. Gives Curses when the top card is a Victory card. Worth 0–1 copies, as payload.
- **Council Room:** one copy, for +4 Cards and the Buy.
- **Astrolabe:** early $ and +Buy that helps feed the loop's Buys.

**Ratings check:** nothing on this board rates highly. The top kingdom card is Border Guard at 1.6. Cage (0.1) and Market Square (1.3) are rated as ordinary cards, but here they form the main interaction. Tunnel (−1.7) is better than its rating because it is the loop's trigger.

**Plans considered:**
- **Litter/Patrol engine with the Cage + Market Square Gold loop (recommended).**
  - Line: first $5 on turn 3/4. The deck draws itself around turn 9–10. Golds pile up from about turn 9, and double-Province turns come around turn 11–13.
  - Needs: 3+ Litter, 2+ Patrol, 3–4 Market Square, and Cages in steady supply.
  - Weakness: no permanent thinning, Debt tax, and a slowish build.
- **Council Room Big Money with Cage** (Cage holds the Estates until the first Province).
  - Line: Gold by about turn 5–6, $8 from about turn 7. Four Provinces around turn 14–15.
  - Weakness: capped at one Province a turn, Council Room feeds the opponent, and the Market Square loop rarely lines up in 5-card hands.
- **Jester money:** +$2 plus Curses and stolen Golds. Weaker than Council Room money unless the opponent greens early. One copy is a fine extra in either plan.

**Recommendation:** Build the engine. Its Gold loop costs $5 and two Buys per turn and adds 2–3 Golds and 2–4 VP. Plain money can't match that rate, and it also scores alongside Provinces.

**Against their best plan:** Their best answer is Council Room money with Cage, aiming for 4 Provinces around turn 14–15.
- **With the engine:** once the loop is running (turn 11–13), you take 2 Provinces a turn plus Vineyards and Tunnels and overtake them. If their Jester flips your Golds, they take copies, so hold the Golds you can.
- **With money yourself, in a money mirror:** add one Jester.

**Open:**
- **5/2:** Patrol / Cage.
- **4/3:** Silver / Cage. Astrolabe / Cage is an acceptable alternative.
- On your turn 3 or 4, play Cage first and set aside your Estates.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Building:**
  - $5: Patrol first, then Litter and Patrol alternating, with Litter outnumbering the terminals. One Council Room.
  - $6–7: Gold (1–2), or a $5 card plus Cage/Border Guard.
  - $3–4: Market Square up to 3–4.
  - $2: a second Cage.
- **Don't gain any Victory card** until at least 2 Market Squares and a Cage can be in hand on the same turn.
- **Around turn 8–10:**
  - Buy Potion once the deck mostly draws itself.
  - Start the loop: each turn, a Vineyard or Tunnel, plus a new Cage, while holding Market Squares.
- **Turn 11+:** Provinces, plus Vineyard and Cage with the spare Buys. Add Duchies late.

**Ending:**
- Most likely on Provinces, around turn 13–15.
- Watch Tunnel (8), Vineyard (8) and Cage (10). Together, or with Curses if both sides play Jester, they can make a three-pile ending. Count those piles from the midgame on.

**Watch out for:**
- **Litter Debt:** it must be paid before you can buy, so never play a Litter you can't afford.
- **Mistiming Cage:** an accidental early Victory gain (or any Victory gain) releases all the caged junk at once.
- **Contested piles:** if the opponent copies the loop, Cages and Vineyards run out. Then skip extra Cages and prioritise Vineyards.

**Challenged:**
- **Council Room money, re-tested:** I gave it Cage and a single Market Square. It still tops out at one Province a turn and can't line up the trigger, so the engine stays ahead if the loop starts by about turn 10.
- **How the engine loses:** the build stalls without thinning, and the loop starts at turn 12 or later. Meanwhile, Council Room money plus their Jester hitting your Golds wins the race.

**Least sure of:** that the engine draws itself by turn 9–10 without permanent thinning. To test it, play out the build turn by turn. If double-Province turns come only after turn 13, switch to Council Room money with Cage and Jester.

**Assumed:**
- Cage's set-aside cards come back after the Clean-up draw, adding to the next hand.
- Each Cage's trash is a separate trigger, but each Market Square can react only once, because it is discarded.
- Vineyard counts every Action you own at game end, and gaining a Vineyard counts as a Victory gain for Cage.
