**Board:** This is an engine board built around $5 cards. Forager and Upgrade are non-terminal trashers. Bandit Camp, Walled Village and Inn are villages. Council Room and Patrol are the draw. The payload is unusually varied: Spoils, Forager's $, extra Buys from Council Room and Forager, and Horn of Plenty, which can gain a Province in a turn that plays many different card names. There is no junker, and the only attack-like effect is Council Room handing the opponent a card. The open questions were whether there was enough +Buy (there is), whether Scrounge pays (it does, as a cheap way to score late), and whether Golem or Overlord earns a slot.

**Engine:**
- **Pieces:** Forager for thinning, money and +Buy. Bandit Camp, Walled Village or Inn for Actions. Council Room and Patrol for draw. Horn of Plenty, Spoils and Gold for payload.
- **Build:** open Forager/Silver, then add $5 parts at about one or two per turn from turn 3. Forager's +Buy and Horn's gains speed this up.
- **Speed:** a 16–18 card deck should draw itself from about turn 9–11.
- **Reliability:** two to four villages, all non-terminal, against two or three terminal draw cards. Thinning keeps it reliable. Patrol helps late, because it pulls Victory cards off the top of the deck.
- **Built turn (example):** roughly $15–20 with 4–5 Buys. That is 2 Spoils for $6, 2 Foragers for +$3 each, Gold and Silver for $5, and Buys from 2 Council Rooms and 2 Foragers. On top of that, each Horn of Plenty played with 8 or more different names in play gains a Province without a Buy. That makes 3–4 Provinces a turn possible.

**Key interactions:**
- **Forager + a varied trash:** Forager trashes first and then counts, so trashing your first Copper already gives +$1. Spoils (from Bandit Camp), Silver, a Horn that trashed itself, and the opponent's trashed Treasures each add +$1 more. Forager reaches +$2–3 by about turn 7 and +$3–4 later. The trash is shared, so the opponent's Forager benefits too.
- **Horn of Plenty + different names:** Bandit Camp, Walled Village, Council Room, Patrol, Forager, Horn, Gold, Silver and Overlord are all different names. With 8 in play, Horn gains a Province and trashes itself; that Horn in the trash then adds +$1 to every later Forager. Early, with 5 names, Horn gains a $5 engine part instead.
- **Trashed Estates + Scrounge:** each Estate trashed by Forager or Upgrade lets a later $3 Scrounge gain an Estate plus a card costing up to $5. While building, that is $3 for a Bandit Camp or Council Room; the Estate that comes with it can be trashed again by Forager. In the endgame it is $3 and a Buy for Estate + Duchy, 4 VP. For example, $11 with 3 Buys gets Province + Estate + Duchy, 10 VP, where a lone Province is 6. It is limited by how many Estates are in the trash, and both players draw on the same Estates.
- **Bandit Camp → Spoils:** each Bandit Camp play is a village now and $3 next shuffle, and Spoils is one more Treasure name for Forager.
- **Upgrade:** turns an Estate into a Forager or Silver, a Silver into a Walled Village, and a Horn or Duchy into a Gold. Upgrading a Copper just trashes it, since nothing costs $1.

**Other cards:**
- **Overlord:** its best partner is the Horn (one more name). It copies Council Room or Bandit Camp without taking from those piles. Buy it with a spare Buy in a big turn and pay the 8 Debt from surplus $. Don't open with it.
- **Inn:** its partner is Horn. Gaining Inn mid-turn shuffles your discarded Actions back into your deck, and the discard-2 gets rid of greens. It is the third-choice village.
- **Walled Village:** none special. It is a $4 village, and early on it puts itself back on your deck, which steadies the shuffle.
- **Golem:** none. Buying a Potion is a dead buy in this deck. Skip it.
- **Council Room vs Patrol:** Council Room for its +Buy and +4 Cards. Patrol is the pick once the deck is greening.
- **Ratings check:** Horn (3.1), Upgrade (3.1) and Forager (2.7) agree with my view. I rate Bandit Camp (0.0) and Scrounge (−1.0) much higher here, because Spoils feeds Forager and Scrounge has trashed Estates to work with.

**Plans considered:**
1. **Forager/Horn engine** (recommended). Open Forager/Silver and add a village and draw at $5. It first draws its deck around turn 9–11 and takes 2–4 Provinces a turn from about turn 11–12. Needs: two Foragers, two or three villages, two or three draw cards. Weakness: a slow start if $5 hands are missed on turns 3–4.
2. **Council Room-Big Money.** Hits $6 around turn 5–6 and $8 from about turn 8. Gets 4 Provinces around turn 14–15. Weakness: every Council Room play also feeds the opponent's deck, and it can't keep up with multi-Province turns.
3. **Patrol-BM + Horn + Scrounge Duchies.** A little faster to green, but its ceiling is still about one Province per turn.

**Recommendation:** Build the engine. Forager's thinning and +Buy, two different villages and Horn's free Provinces give a 3–4 Province turn by about turn 12. That beats Big Money's turn 14–15 clock with room to spare. It also holds up in the mirror: Scrounge and Horn add VP without needing extra $ each turn.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Vs Council Room-BM:** each of their Council Rooms gives you a card. You are behind until about turn 10, then you take 2–3 Provinces a turn. Count the Provinces they have taken, and green earlier if they get 4 by turn 11.
- **Engine mirror:** the Bandit Camp and Council Room piles are contested, so fill gaps with Overlord. The trashed Estates are a shared Scrounge supply, so use it before the opponent does. Get the first Horn turns in, and switch to Duchies earlier.
- **If I had picked Patrol-BM:** it loses to the engine by roughly turn 13.

**Open:**
- **4/3:** Forager / Silver. Use Walled Village / Forager only if you already hold enough money.
- **5/2:** Upgrade or Bandit Camp, then nothing at $2. Scrounge costs $3 and there are no $2 cards. Don't open Overlord: its Debt blocks your turn 3–4 buys.

**Buy priorities:**
- **Turns 3–6:** $5 goes on Bandit Camp, then Council Room, then Horn. $3–4 goes on a second Forager or Walled Village. With Estates in the trash, spend spare $3 on Scrounge for a $5 part.
- **Turns 7–10:** a second draw card (Patrol or Council Room), a third village and a Gold, then Overlord with spare Buys.
- **Trashing:** Forager trashes Estates and Coppers aggressively. Keep about $6 of Treasure plus Spoils so the deck still has money.
- **Greening:** start once a turn reliably makes $13+ with 2 Buys or plays Horn with 8 names, around turn 10–12. After that, spend each spare $3 Buy on Scrounge for Estate + Duchy.

**Ending:** most likely on Provinces, around turn 12–15. A three-pile ending is unlikely. Bandit Camp and Council Room might empty in a mirror, but no third pile drains quickly.

**Watch out for:**
1. **Forager has to trash.** Late in the game it will eat a Spoils or a Horn if that is all you hold, so keep an Estate or Copper to feed it.
2. **Scrounge depends on the shared trash.** It only works while Estates are in the trash, and the opponent's Scrounges use up the same ones.
3. **More players:** each Council Room helps several opponents, and Bandit Camp and the Estates in the trash become more contested.

**Challenged:**
- **Council Room-BM** gets its first Province around turn 8 and its 4th around turn 14. The engine still pulls ahead by turn 12 unless it misses $5 on turns 3–4 twice.
- **Golem** plays two Actions for no Action of its own. It still loses to Bandit Camp: it costs a Potion and the Potion turn.
- **How my pick loses:** the opponent takes the Bandit Camp pile and runs Council Room-BM fast, while my own Horns gain Provinces too early and leave the deck thin on payload.

**Least sure of:** whether Horn's turns with 8+ names really come by turn 11. If built turns usually show only 6–7 names, Horn gains Golds, and the plan leans on $ and Buys instead. The test is to count the different names in play on turns 9–10.

**Assumed:**
- **Spoils:** it returns to its pile when played, so it never counts toward Horn's names. Spoils trashed by Forager does stay in the trash and counts for later Foragers.
- **Scrounge:** it needs both a Buy and $3, and its "card costing up to $5" can be a Duchy.
- **Overlord:** its Debt can be paid off in the same Buy phase it is bought.
