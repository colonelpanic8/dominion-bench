## Beyond the key
- Forager trashes before counting, so trashing the first Copper already gives +$1: plausible. The text trashes first, then gives +$1 per differently named Treasure in the trash.
- A Horn that trashed itself adds +$1 to later Foragers, and the opponent's trashed Treasures count too: plausible. Horn is a Treasure, and there is one trash for both players.
- Spoils never counts toward Horn's names, but a Spoils trashed by Forager stays in the trash: plausible. Spoils returns to its pile when played, so it is never in play, while trashing from hand doesn't trigger that.
- Upgrade on a Copper just trashes it, since nothing costs exactly $1: plausible. The $0 cards are Copper, Curse, Spoils and Overlord (plus Debt), and nothing on the board costs $1.
- Province plus Scrounge (Estate + Duchy) for $11 is 10 VP: plausible, but it says 3 Buys where 2 are enough (one for the Province, one for Scrounge).
- Overlord's 8 Debt can be paid in the same Buy phase it is bought: plausible. Debt can be paid off with $ during the Buy phase.
- Overlord copies Council Room or Bandit Camp without taking from those piles: plausible. Overlord leaves the card in the Supply, so contested piles don't stop it.
- Golem is a dead buy here because a Potion is a wasted buy: plausible. This is the credit the grader note invites. A Potion costs a $4 buy, and its only use is Golem.
- Forager will trash a Spoils or Horn late if that is all you hold: plausible. Forager's trash isn't optional.
- An engine beats Council Room-BM by about turn 12: doubtful, and not checkable from card text. Its own engine relies on the Forager opening the experts rejected.

## Summary
The analysis gets Horn's variety mechanic right and builds a deck of different $5 Actions, but it plays Horn as one payload in a Forager/Spoils money engine instead of the 6–7-Horn megaturn everyone agreed on. It opens Forager, the trap that lost yurikamome the game, rejects the Upgrade/Overlord opening the winner used, and prioritises Bandit Camp where the key wants one. A player following it would green over several turns and likely lose the race to a single Horn turn.
