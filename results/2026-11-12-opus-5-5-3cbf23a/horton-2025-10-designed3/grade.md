## Beyond the key
- Destrier cost math (2 Buys, $7: Ducat + Destrier at $5; 3 Buys, $11: Ducat + Destrier $5 + Destrier $4): plausible. Each earlier gain that turn takes $1 off Destrier's cost.
- Leave a Copper unplayed so gaining Ducat can trash it: plausible. Ducat's on-gain trashes a Copper from your hand.
- Hill Fort's Action-phase gains don't spoil Exploration: plausible. Exploration only checks gains during the Buy phase.
- Calling a Reckless Guide isn't doubled: plausible. Reckless doubles the instructions of played cards, and calling isn't playing.
- Garrison draws a card next turn per gain this turn: plausible, from the card text.
- Hill Fort gaining Guide to hand is net "+1 Card +1 Action, like a Lost City" and "the board's only repeatable village": wrong. Hill Fort (−1 Action) and then Reckless Guide (−1 Action, +2 Actions, +2 Cards) leave net 0 Actions and +1 card in hand. That's a Laboratory plus a gain, not a village. A Reckless Guide played from the deck is a one-shot Lost City.
- "Guide returns to its pile, so this repeats every turn": doubtful. A played Guide goes to the Tavern mat and returns to its pile only after it's called and discarded. The repeat comes from Hill Fort gaining a new Guide from the pile.
- "Mystic can name Tent, because you know it is on top": wrong. Tent goes onto your deck during Clean-up, before you draw your next hand, so it's normally drawn rather than left on top.
- Cabin Boy can trash itself to gain Stronghold when Stronghold is on top: plausible. Stronghold is a Duration. It misses that Garrison, also a Duration, is a Cabin Boy target when it's on top.
- Destrier deck draws itself by turn 9–10 and double-Provinces from turn 11–12: doubtful. The deck has no village beyond Exploration and Guide, and the key's experts didn't see Destrier as the base plan.

## Summary
The analysis builds a Steward/Ducat-opened Destrier/Mystic deck and pushes the Forts pile to the sidelines, which is the opposite of Horton's playtested line: Tent/Exploration into two Garrisons, then contest the Hill Forts. It does find the Ducat→cheap Destrier mechanism and the Hill Fort→Guide gain, which drive the runaway deck the key warns about, but it treats them as side options. It also misreads Hill Fort plus Reckless Guide as a village.
