# Analysis: kingdoms/horton-2025-10-designed3.md

**Recommendation: a thin deck of Destriers (+2 Cards +1 Action, so they need no village) and Mystics for money, with Ducat for +Buy.** Open Steward/Ducat on a 4/3. Money decks are the backup plan, and they are weak on this board.

**Board:** This is a Province game with no junking attack. The board is about non-terminal draw (Destrier) and cheap gains that make Destriers cheaper. Each card gained earlier in your own turn takes $1 off the Destrier price, and Ducat brings +Buy, Coffers and the trashing of a Copper when gained. Steward and Ratcatcher trash cards hard. The only real villages are conditional ones:
- Villagers from Exploration, which come only on turns you gain nothing in the Buy phase.
- Guide with the Reckless trait.
- Hill Fort gaining a Guide to your hand, which needs the Forts pile turned to Hill Fort.

The open question was whether a deck with almost no villages can still draw itself. It can, because nearly everything it wants is non-terminal.

**Engine:**
- **Pieces:**
  - Draw: about 6 Destriers.
  - Money that uses no Action: Mystic (+1 Action +$2), Gold, Silver, Ducat's Coffers.
  - +Buy: about 3 Ducats.
  - Thinning: Steward, Ratcatcher and Ducat's on-gain Copper trash.
- **Terminals:** One terminal per turn needs no village, so the single terminal is Steward, or later a Garrison. A few Villagers from Exploration or a Reckless Guide make room for a second one.
- **Speed:** The deck draws itself around turns 9–10. It takes 2 Provinces a turn from about turn 11–12, which matches the reference speed for an engine.
- **Reliability:** Lab-type draw can't collide, and the deck is thin, so it should stall rarely. What can stall it is too few Destriers (fewer than about 5) or greening too early.
- **Built turn:** About 3 Mystics, 2 Golds, a Silver, 3 Ducats and spare Coffers make $16–18 with 4 Buys. That is 2 Provinces, or a Province and a Duchy, each turn.

**Key interactions:**
- **Ducat makes Destrier cheaper.** Destrier costs $6 minus $1 for each card gained earlier that turn. With 2 Buys and $7 you get Ducat ($2) and Destrier ($5). With 3 Buys and $11 you get Ducat ($2), Destrier ($5) and Destrier ($4). Each Destrier you buy cuts the next one by $1 more. This lets you take most of the 10 Destriers by about turn 8–10. In a mirror that split decides the game.
- **Ducat trashes Copper as you build.** Each Ducat gained lets you trash a Copper from your hand. To use this, leave a Copper unplayed: on $3, play two Coppers, buy Ducat and trash the third. Together with Steward (2 cards a play) and Ratcatcher (1 card a shuffle), the deck is down to about 8–10 cards by turn 7.
- **Garrison in the terminal slot.** Garrison gives +$2. On a turn with 4 gains it also draws 4 cards at the start of the next turn, so it is the best late terminal for the one Action you have spare. Getting it means turning the Forts pile past Tent: play Tent and rotate, or buy the 4 Tents out.
- **Hill Fort and Guide.** Hill Fort gains Guide to your hand, then you play Guide with Reckless (+2 Cards +2 Actions). Net, that is +1 Card +1 Action, like a Lost City, plus a gain that makes Destrier cheaper. Guide returns to its pile, so this repeats every turn. It is the board's only repeatable village, but there are only 4 Hill Forts and they need the pile rotated. It is an optional extra.
- **Exploration.** Turns spent trashing with Steward, with no buy, give +1 Coffers and +1 Villager each. Those Villagers later pay for an extra terminal.

**Other cards:**
- **Cabin Boy:** a cantrip, then +$2 next turn. It can also trash itself to gain a Stronghold (2 VP) when Stronghold is on top of the Forts, which works as late VP. It is filler.
- **Stronghold:** 2 VP and either +$3 or +3 Cards next turn. Take it as a late-game VP terminal if the pile has been turned to it.
- **Tent:** +$2 and returns to your deck when discarded, so it is a terminal you play every turn. Its main use is rotating the Forts. Mystic can name it, because you know it is on top.
- **Mystic:** guessing the top card works better once Ratcatcher has thinned the deck. It is the board's best non-terminal money.
- **Wishing Well:** a cantrip that is better in a thin deck. It is a low-value pick.
- **Harbinger:** none. It can set up a Mystic guess, but that isn't worth a buy.

**Plans considered:**
- **Destrier/Mystic/Ducat engine (recommended).**
  - Line: open Steward/Ducat. Build turns 3–8. The deck draws itself from turn 9–10, then takes 2 Provinces a turn from turn 11–12.
  - Needs: 5–6 Destriers and 3 Ducats.
  - Weakness: the Destrier pile is contested.
- **Big Money + Steward or Stronghold.**
  - Line: Steward as a weak Smithy, about 4 Provinces by turn 16–17.
  - Weakness: there is no good terminal draw, and Stronghold first needs the Forts pile turned to it.
- **Hill Fort/Guide village engine with Garrison and Steward as terminals.**
  - Has the higher ceiling, but needs at least 2 rotations of the Forts plus $5 for each Hill Fort, and the opponent can rotate the pile away.

**Recommendation:** Build the Destrier engine. It needs no village, its thinning is excellent, and +Buy comes cheap. It is reliable and beats money by several turns. Add Garrison, or Hill Fort/Guide, only if the Forts pile ends up turned to them along the way.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Against money:** You win the race comfortably. There are no attacks to slow you.
- **Against the same engine:** The game is decided by the Destrier split and by Ducat-plus-Destrier turns. Get to 3 Buys first.
- **If I played money instead:** It loses to the engine by turns 13–14.

**Open:**
- **4/3:** Steward / Ducat, trashing a Copper when you gain Ducat. Ratcatcher is the alternative to Ducat.
- **5/2:** Mystic / Ducat. This is worse: the only $5 is Mystic, and Hill Fort is buried in the Forts pile. Choose 4/3 under the tournament rule.

**Buy priorities:**
1. Shuffle 2: Destrier at $6, or Ducat plus Destrier on $7 with 2 Buys. At $5 take Mystic. At $2–4 take Ratcatcher or Ducat, then Silver.
2. Shuffles 3–4: as many Destriers as each turn allows, using several Buys. Then Mystics and Gold. Add Exploration only on a turn with spare $4 and a spare Buy.
3. Start greening once a turn reliably makes $16 with 2+ Buys, around turn 11.

**Ending:** The Provinces run out. The Destrier, Ducat and Forts piles could also empty, but that is unlikely. Guide returns to its pile after each use, so it won't run out. A three-pile ending is a small risk.

**Watch out for:**
- The opponent out-buying you on Destriers.
- Over-trashing your Copper so you can't afford Destriers.
- If the opponent turns the Forts pile to Hill Fort, consider contesting the Hill Forts.

**Challenged:** I re-tested the Hill Fort/Guide engine. It does give a real repeatable village, but only once 2 rotations and several $5 Hill Forts are in place, which is too slow and the pile is shared. The pick loses if it ends up with only 3–4 Destriers, or if it greens before it draws its deck.

**Least sure of:**
- **How much Mystic adds compared with plain Gold.** Mystic guesses the top card better once the deck is thin. The test is whether Gold-heavy builds hit $16 as often.
- **How much the Hill Fort/Guide village is worth.**

**Assumed:**
- **Reckless Guide:** Played twice, it gives +2 Cards +2 Actions. Its second "put on the Tavern mat" fails, since it is already there. Calling it is not playing it, so the call isn't doubled. It returns to its pile when discarded after being called.
- **Ducat's Copper trash:** It trashes a Copper from your hand, so you need an unplayed Copper there when you gain it.
- **Exploration:** Hill Fort's gains happen in the Action phase, so they don't stop Exploration from paying out.
