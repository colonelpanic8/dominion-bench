**Board:** No thinning and no real trashing. Ritual adds a Curse and trades one card for VP tokens. Prize Goat and Spell Scroll are random Loots. So this is a junk-tolerant draw deck that scores mainly with Provinces. The core is Aristocrat, a cheap card that counts copies in play and acts as village, draw, $ and +Buy in one. Laboratory, First Mate and Tactician add draw, Jewelled Egg adds +Buy, and the Flag adds +1 card to every hand. Two questions decide the board: can a deck with no thinning collect enough Aristocrats together, and how hard does Jester junk a deck that can never trash?

**Engine:**
- **Pieces:**
  - **Aristocrat ($3):** played without help, four copies run themselves. The 1st gives +3 Actions, the 2nd +3 Cards, the 3rd +$3 and the 4th +3 Buys. A 5th needs just one spare Action (Ghost Town or Tactician), and then gives +3 more.
  - **Laboratory:** draw that needs no Actions.
  - **First Mate:** played after your other Actions, it refills your hand to 6, often about +4. It can also play every Jester or Corsair in hand for one Action.
  - **Flag:** +1 card on every hand.
  - **Ghost Town and Corsair:** each gives +1 card at the start of the next turn, and Ghost Town also gives an Action.
  - **Crop Rotation:** +2 cards when you have a Victory card in hand.
  - **+Buy:** Egg, the 4th Aristocrat and Tactician.
- **Missing piece:** thinning.
- **When it works:** with about 5 Aristocrats, 3 Labs, 1–2 First Mates and the Flag in a deck of about 26 cards, it draws most of the deck from about turn 9–10. It is not certain to draw everything.
- **Built turn:** 7 Copper + 2 Jesters + Flag Bearer + 2 Eggs + the 3rd Aristocrat makes about $18–20, with 5–6 Buys. That is 2 Provinces plus a Ritual each turn from about turn 11–12. This is the "no trashing costs a turn or two" pace for an engine.

**Key interactions:**
- **Aristocrat + Jewelled Egg:** $6 and 2 Buys get two Aristocrats in one turn. Aristocrats only work in groups, and the pile of 10 is contested, so buying in pairs from about turn 5 matters.
- **Aristocrat + First Mate / Labs:** the 1st Aristocrat pays for the terminals. Labs find more Aristocrats, and First Mate refills the hand once it has been played down.
- **Ritual as late payload:** Ritual gives +VP equal to the cost of the trashed card, minus the Curse it gains you. With a spare Buy and $4 the net gain is:
  - a Loot: +6
  - a Lab, Jester or First Mate: +4
  - a Flag Bearer: +3, and you also take the Flag
  
  Point for point that is cheaper than a Province ($8 for 6). Use it only from about turn 10, because each Ritual is a Curse in your deck. Ritual on an Estate or Copper gains nothing.
- **Jester versus a deck that can't trash:**
  - A Victory card on top gives them a Curse that stays for good.
  - Treasure or Actions on top: you choose who gains the copy. Take their Gold, Lab or Aristocrat, which also drains the Aristocrat pile your way, or give them a Copper.
  - Two Jesters played per turn from about turn 8 is real payload.
- **Flag Bearer:** whoever gained one most recently holds the Flag. The engine's spare Buys make taking it back cheap ($4), but a one-Buy money deck has to give up its whole turn to do it.

**Other cards:**
- **Corsair:** its attack only trashes Silver and Gold, so it is strong only if the opponent's economy is Silver and Gold. Loots and Action money don't trigger it.
- **Tactician:** weak here. It discards the Coppers you need, so take at most one, and only for its +Buy and +Action next turn.
- **Alley:** a sifter you can play from your deck. Low priority; it discards Estates and Curses.
- **Crop Rotation:** buy it with a spare $6 and Buy around turn 7–9. It gets better as you green.
- **Egg via Ritual:** Ritual on an Egg turns it into a Loot (net +1 VP and a Curse). Only worth it late, with spare Buys.

**Plans considered:**
- **Flag money (baseline):** Flag Bearer, Silver, then Jester ×2 and Gold, with Provinces at $8. The Flag's 6-card hands make it fast: $6 from about turn 3–5, first Province about turn 7–8, 4 Provinces around turn 13–14. Its weakness is one Buy per turn: it can't keep the Flag, and it can't use Ritual.
- **Aristocrat/Lab/Jester engine:** built as above. It needs about 5 Aristocrats by turn 9 and must win the Aristocrat split. Its weaknesses are no thinning, Jester Curses and Coppers clogging it, and the pile split.
- **Corsair money:** only good against an opponent whose economy is Silver and Gold. Otherwise it is worse than Jester money.

**Recommendation:** the Aristocrat/Lab engine with 2 Jesters, the Flag and Eggs, scoring with Provinces and late Rituals. Jester is the attack in both lines, so neither player gets out of the junking. The engine's +Buy lets it keep the Flag, take two Provinces a turn and turn spare $ into Ritual VP, while money is capped at one Province per turn.

**Against their best plan:**
- **Flag/Jester money:** take the Flag back with spare Buys, and use your Jesters to take their Golds. You must have your first double-Province turn by about turn 12.
- **The same engine:** the Aristocrat split decides it, so buy them in pairs early and use Jester to take theirs. Green somewhat earlier than you would against money.

**Open:**
- **4/3, $4 first:** Flag Bearer / Silver. With the Flag from turn 1, turn 2 draws 6 cards and hits $5 one time in six.
- **4/3, $3 first:** Ghost Town / Flag Bearer. Ghost Town is gained to hand and played that night, so turn 2 has $5 three times in five. If turn 2 hits $5, take Jester and get Flag Bearer next.
- **5/2:** Jester / Jewelled Egg.

**Buy priorities:**
1. Hold the Flag throughout.
2. Jester ×2, then Labs ×3, then Aristocrats in pairs using Egg Buys, aiming for 5–6.
3. One First Mate, one Ghost Town, and a second Egg.
4. Crop Rotation with a spare $6.
5. Green at about $13+ with 2+ Buys (around turn 10–11): Provinces first, then Ritual on Loots, Labs or Flag Bearers, then Duchies near the end.

**Ending:** most likely on Provinces. Three piles is possible: Curses (from Jester and Ritual) plus Aristocrats plus one of Labs or Eggs. Count all three once Curses are below 4.

**Watch out for:**
- A 5/5 Aristocrat split or worse. With only 3–4, Aristocrats are just a village plus a Smithy, so lean on Labs and First Mate.
- Heavy Jester Curses on you. Then switch toward Flag money with Provinces and Duchies.
- An opponent whose money is Silver and Gold. Then add a Corsair.

**Challenged:** I re-tested Flag/Jester money. The 6-card hands make it faster than plain money, perhaps 4 Provinces by turn 13. My pick loses if the engine is still stalling on Coppers and Curses on turn 12. In that case take Provinces at $8 and keep Jester attacking.

**Least sure of:** that a deck with no thinning draws reliably enough to beat Flag money. The test: by turn 10 you should have at least 5 Aristocrats, 3 Labs and the Flag, and hit $13 with 2 Buys. If not, switch to money.

**Assumed:**
- Shadow cards (Alley) can be played from anywhere in your deck, per `rules.md`.
- Ritual's +VP counts the trashed card's printed cost, and needs a card in hand to trash.
- Aristocrat counts every Aristocrat in play, in the order played.
- The ratings agree with these calls: Aristocrat −1.9, Jester −1.8, Ritual −4.5 and Tactician −1.9 are all low. I rate Aristocrat and Ritual higher here for the reasons above: Egg's +Buy and First Mate support Aristocrat, and Ritual has late Loots and Labs to trash.
