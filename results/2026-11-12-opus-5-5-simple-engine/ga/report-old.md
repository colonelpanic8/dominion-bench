**RESULT (the Provinces ran out): 1. Birch 43 VP (20 turns), 2. Ash 37 VP (20 turns).** I won as P2.

**Final deck (32 cards):** 4 Province, 6 Duchy, 1 Estate, 3 Gold, 1 Silver, 5 Copper, 2 Market, 2 Festival, 2 Cellar, 1 Bridge, 1 Smithy, 1 Chapel.

**The plan:** No attacks on this board, so I planned a Chapel-thinned engine. Chapel would trash the starting cards, Village and Smithy would draw, and Market, Festival and Bridge would give extra buys and money. Bridge also cuts every cost by $1. I opened Chapel/Festival on my 2/5 split. I expected to take two Provinces a turn from about turn 10, and planned to start buying Victory cards once my $ plus Bridge discounts covered two Provinces.

**How it worked:**
1. I never really built the engine. I bought Golds and Markets and only one Smithy, so the deck played as a thin Chapel-money deck. That reached Provinces early (turns 11–13), but it couldn't keep pace with the opponent's fuller Village/Smithy/Bridge engine. I was behind 27–37 with one Province left.
2. The endgame saved it. I couldn't win by taking the last Province, so I left it alone and used Bridge with several buys to take the remaining Duchies and Estates instead. That brought the score to 37–37 with no cheaper Victory cards left. A tie on their turn would go to me, because they would have taken one more turn.
3. The opponent then missed $8 twice. On turn 20, Festival, Smithy and Market got me to exactly $8 for the last Province. Next time on a board like this I should buy Villages and Smithies earlier instead of a third Gold.
