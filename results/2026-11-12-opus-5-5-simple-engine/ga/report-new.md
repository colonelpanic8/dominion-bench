I lost the game.

**RESULT:** Birch (P2) won 43 to my 37 (Ash, P1). The game ended when the Provinces ran out, after 20 turns each.

**My final deck (35 cards):** 4 Province, 2 Duchy, 7 Estate, 2 Gold, 2 Silver, 3 Copper, 3 Village, 3 Smithy, 2 Festival, 2 Bridge, 1 Market, 1 Chapel, 1 Cellar.

**The plan:** a Chapel engine. Open Silver and Chapel, and trash Estates and Coppers hard. Then add Festivals and Villages for Actions, Smithies for draw, and Bridge and Market for extra Buys and payload. Start buying Provinces once the deck draws itself, and use Bridge's discount to take Province plus Estates in one turn.

**How it worked:**
1. The thinning worked: by turn 10 I had trashed all my starting Estates and 4 Coppers, and the deck drew itself. But I trashed too much money for an engine with so little Treasure. I needed Golds bought late to reach $8, and the Smithy turns often drew Actions with no Actions left to play them. On turn 13 I wasted a turn that way myself: I played Smithy with my last Action, so the Village and Festival it drew were dead.
2. Using Bridge to buy Province plus Estates put me ahead 37–27 by turn 17. But all those green cards (7 Estates, 4 Provinces, 2 Duchies) clogged a deck with no draw left to get through them. The opponent's Gold/Market/Festival money deck stayed steadier.
3. At the end we were tied 37–37 with one Province left and the Estate and Duchy piles empty. My next two hands made only $7 and $3, and the opponent's next hand reached $8 for the last Province. Keeping more Treasure, or adding a Remodel to turn a Gold into a Province, would probably have closed the game out first.
