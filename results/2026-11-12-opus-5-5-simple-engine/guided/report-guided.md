I lost: Opus (P1) won 47–35 when the Provinces ran out, after 25 turns each.

**RESULT:** Opus 47 VP (rank 1), Guided (me, P2) 35 VP (rank 2). Reason: provinces-gone.

**Final deck (44 cards):** 3 Province, 4 Duchy, 6 Estate, 1 Curse, 5 Bridge, 4 Festival, 2 Village, 2 Smithy, 2 Cellar, 1 Chapel, 5 Silver, 4 Copper.

**The plan:** Open Silver and Chapel on my 3/4 split. Trash the Estates and most Coppers, then build an engine: Village and Festival for Actions, Smithy for draw, Bridges as payload. The target turn was 2 Bridges and about $12, enough for 2 Provinces a turn, from around turn 11, which should beat the opponent's Smithy-plus-money deck. I followed the plan; late in the game I switched to taking all the Duchies and Estates so the opponent's cheaper hands couldn't score.

**How it worked:**
1. The trashing came too late. Chapel never drew with my Estates until turn 9, and I trashed only 2 Coppers on turn 3. The deck stayed slow, and the opponent had 2 Provinces and 3 Gold-level hands by turn 10 while I was still building. Trashing all 3 Coppers on turn 3 would probably have helped.
2. The engine never reached a 2-Province turn. Five Bridges in a 35+ card deck, a third of it Victory cards, never came together. My best turns were one Province or Duchy-and-Estate turns.
3. I made two misclicks by reusing option numbers without re-reading the list: an unwanted Copper on turn 12 and a Curse (−1 VP) on turn 18. Neither changed the result, but option numbers shift after every purchase, so each list has to be re-read. The server worked correctly throughout.
