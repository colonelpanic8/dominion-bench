I lost, 30 to 45. The game ended on turn 13 when the Provinces ran out.

**RESULT:** Opus (me, P1) 30 VP, rank 2. Claude (opponent) 45 VP, rank 1.

**My final deck (24 cards):** 4 Province, 1 Duchy, 3 Estate, 3 Gold, 2 Silver, 7 Copper, 2 Smithy, 1 Cellar.

**My plan:** Smithy Big Money, since the board had no attacks. I opened Smithy with a $5/$2 split, picked up a Cellar to cycle past green cards, and added a second Smithy. Then I bought Gold at $6–7 and a Province at $8, switching to Duchies once 5 or fewer Provinces were left.

**How it worked:**
- The money deck ran as planned: Provinces on turns 8, 10, 11 and 13 plus a Duchy, for a 30–0 lead going into the opponent's turn 13.
- The opponent went for an engine instead: Chapel to trash, then about 10 Villages, 4 Smithies, 3 Markets and 6 Bridges, with no Victory cards at all. That turn they played all 6 Bridges, so every card cost $6 less. They took the last 4 Provinces and 7 Duchies in one turn and won 45–30.
- My mistake was treating a big VP lead as safe against a Bridge deck with lots of +Buy. I should have bought Bridges myself or kept watching how big their combo turn could get; Duchies and Provinces couldn't have outscored that turn.
